# MANDATE-DEALDESK-2026: Deal Desk Agent

Owner: Sales (accountable: EMP-200). Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

The Deal Desk Agent acts for Sales, on behalf of whoever asks, as one agent per team.
Only version sha256:demo-dealdesk-v1 may run under this mandate.

It may ask the organisation for discount decisions across the company.
It may apply a discount only after a decision has cleared it, exactly as cleared, and
no more than 200 times a day. It may propose register changes. It may do nothing else.
