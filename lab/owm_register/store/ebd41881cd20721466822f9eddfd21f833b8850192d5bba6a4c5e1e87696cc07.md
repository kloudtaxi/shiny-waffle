# Northstar Credit Policy 2026

## 1. Purpose

Sets who may approve customer credit limits, and which customers may receive a higher limit, for
calendar 2026.

## 2. Effective period

This policy is effective from 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
It replaces the previous credit policy in full for requests dated on or after
2026-01-01.

## 3. Approval authority

Authority applies to the new total credit limit requested.

- Accounts Receivable Specialists may approve credit limits up to and including $100,000.
- Credit limits greater than $100,000 and up to and including $500,000 require Finance Manager approval.
- Credit limits greater than $500,000 require Director of Finance approval.

## 4. Concurrence

- For Strategic accounts, credit limits greater than $500,000 also require VP Sales concurrence.

## 5. Eligibility

- Payment history: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request
  may have been paid more than 30 days after its due date, or be unpaid more than
  30 days after it.
- Maximum credit limit: $750,000 for Strategic accounts; $250,000 for Standard accounts. Account tiers are as recorded in Northstar CRM.
- A parent-company guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, for the
  customer the guarantee names.

## 6. Separation of duties

- No one may approve or concur on a credit request they submitted. The approval or concurrence
  passes to the requestor's manager.

## 7. Records

- Credit decisions are recorded in Northstar ERP with the approver and, where required, the
  concurring approver.
