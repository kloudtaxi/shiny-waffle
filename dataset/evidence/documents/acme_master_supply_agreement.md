# Master Supply Agreement

**Agreement reference:** ACME-MFG-2025

This Master Supply Agreement is entered into between **Northstar Industrial Systems**
("Northstar"), Chicago, Illinois, and **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as
**Acme Manufacturing** ("Customer"), 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee,
WI 53209.

## 1. Term

This Agreement is effective April 1, 2025 and
expires March 31, 2028, unless terminated earlier under
Section 9.

## 2. Products

Northstar will supply the following products under this Agreement:

- NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500)
- NS-Cloud Operations Suite (NS-CLOUD)
- NS-Edge Monitoring Package (NS-EDGE)

## 3. Pricing

3.1 Prices are Northstar's current list prices less any discount agreed in an Order.

3.2 **Strategic account pricing.** Customer is eligible for discounts of up to
**15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as
Schedule B.

3.3 Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval
authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach on 60 days' written notice.
