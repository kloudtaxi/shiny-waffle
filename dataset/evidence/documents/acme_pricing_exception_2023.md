# Acme Manufacturing — NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception

Agreement: ACME-SA-2023
Status: Active

Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the
NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to
2025-03-31.
