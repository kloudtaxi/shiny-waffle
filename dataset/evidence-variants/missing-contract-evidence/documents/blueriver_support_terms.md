# BlueRiver Logistics Support Terms

**Reference:** SUP-BRL-2025

Northstar Industrial Systems provides support to **Blue River Logistics LLC** (DUNS 07-331-9025), St. Louis,
MO ("Customer").

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Support plan | Silver |
| Covered products | NS-Cloud Operations Suite (NS-CLOUD) |
| Coverage starts | 2025-01-01 00:00 CT |
| Monthly support fee | $2,500 per month from 2025-01 |

Service levels, credits and customer obligations are as set out in the Northstar Support Service
Level Schedule version that applies to each ticket. The support plan is a support term; it is
unrelated to the Customer's account tier.
