# Northstar Pricing Policy 2026

## 1. Purpose

Sets discount approval authority for direct enterprise sales for calendar 2026.

## 2. Effective period

This policy is effective from 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
It replaces the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 in full for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01.

## 3. Approval authority

- Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%.
- Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval.
- Discounts greater than 20% require Chief Revenue Officer approval.

The Approval Authority Matrix is the operational reference for these thresholds.

## 4. Rules

- Discounts are calculated against the current list price.
- Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.
- Discounts greater than 10% require recorded approval evidence.
