## Answer

Yes, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy anything over 10% needs sign-off from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**One thing to fix first:** the request you pasted shows `"status": "Approved"`, but the CRM discount log shows DR-9001 as **Pending**. Nobody with the authority has signed it off yet, so it shouldn't be treated as approved until Michael Torres does.

**How I got there**

1. **The request:** DR-9001, a 15% discount on the NS-500 for account CRM-2048, dated 2026-09-23. It's $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, leaving $212,500. It relies on Acme's contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (Strategic tier, Milwaukee), and Sarah Chen owns the account. The contract is signed under the legal name Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a separate account.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its section 3.3 says Acme's contract pricing does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** Yes. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to 15% off list, applies to the NS-500 only, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. The request is for 15%, so it is within the limit. The exception itself says it covers what the customer is eligible for, not who may approve.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix (both 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy only applies to requests dated from 2027-01-01. The 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%, has been replaced. That may explain why Sarah's similar 15% request, DR-8104 in September 2025, went through as "Approved".
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. 15% is above it.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. Approvals over 10% must also be recorded in Northstar CRM.
