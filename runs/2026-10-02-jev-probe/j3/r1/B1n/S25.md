## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it is above her 10% limit. The outcome is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**: Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.

**What I checked:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 on account CRM-2048. List price is $250,000, the discount is $37,500 and the net price is $212,500. It's dated 2026-09-23. The stated reason is the Acme Master Supply Agreement, so the request relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen owns the account. In the ERP system the same customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), the legal name on the contract. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091) is a different company.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.2 lets Acme get up to 15% off NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the contract) covers only NS-500 and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its maximum is 15% of list price, and it replaced the earlier 10% exception. David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20. So the 15% request is exactly at Acme's limit.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager, according to the employee list and org chart.

**Why the contract doesn't settle it:** The contract (section 3.3) and the exception both say they set what Acme can get, not who at Northstar can approve it. Discounts still go through internal approval. The authority matrix also says approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
