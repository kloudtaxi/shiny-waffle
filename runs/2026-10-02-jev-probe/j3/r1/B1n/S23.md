## Answer

Yes, but Sarah can't approve it herself. Under the 2026 policy, a 15% discount needs approval from VP Sales Michael Torres. Acme is eligible for this discount; Sarah just doesn't have the authority for it.

**The request date is wrong in what you were given.** Your copy says 2025-09-23. The CRM record (`discount_requests.csv`) and Sarah's email to Michael Torres about DR-9001 both say **2026-09-23**. Her email asks for "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." That earlier deal is DR-8104, dated 2025-09-16, with the same SKU, value and terms. So I've treated 2026-09-23 as the correct date. The date matters: under the 2025 policy, account executives could approve up to 15%, so Sarah could have signed it off herself. The 2026 policy lowered her limit to 10%.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 for CRM-2048, list price $250,000 and net price $212,500. The justification is contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing, whose legal name is Acme Mfg. Holdings. Sarah Chen owns the account. It is a different account from "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.3 says Acme's contract pricing does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15, Schedule B to that agreement, covers NS-500 only. It allows up to 15% off list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% requested is within that limit, so Acme is eligible. The exception says it does not give any employee authority to approve. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, together with the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres. The approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
