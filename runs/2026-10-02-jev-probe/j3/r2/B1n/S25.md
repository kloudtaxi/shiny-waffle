## Answer

Yes, DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount under its contract. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, though, and the 2026 policy only lets that role approve up to 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.

**How I got there**

1. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, owned by Sarah Chen. The contract and ERP name the same customer as Acme Mfg. Holdings (ERP C-1001), trading as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
2. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers the NS-500 Industrial Controller. Section 3.2 says Acme can get up to 15% on NS-500.
3. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers NS-500 only, with a 15% maximum, for the same dates. It replaced the old 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. The request is 15% ($37,500 off $250,000 list), exactly at the limit, so Acme qualifies.
4. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. Account Executives can approve up to 10%, VP Sales more than 10% up to 20%, and the CRO above 20%. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
5. **Sarah's authority:** She can approve up to 10%, so 15% is beyond her limit. Section 3.3 of the agreement says the customer's pricing terms don't change Northstar's internal approval authority. The exception itself says it gives no Northstar employee approval authority.
6. **Who approves:** Michael Torres is VP Sales and Sarah reports to him. The approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM, which the matrix requires for anything above 10%.

**Don't use last year's request as a precedent:** A similar request, DR-8104 (Acme, NS-500, 15%, 2025-09-16), shows as approved. That fell under the 2025 policy, when Account Executives could approve up to 15%. That limit no longer applies in 2026.
