## Answer

**Yes, it can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount. Under the 2026 policy, though, 15% is above her own approval limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

**What I checked**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 for CRM-2048, dated 2026-09-23. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the finance system the same customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001).
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** Acme's special pricing deal EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list price, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% deal (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. A 15% request is exactly at the cap, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix were in force on 2026-09-23. Account executives can approve up to 10%. Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales. Over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. Two documents say the contract doesn't change that: section 3.3 of the agreement and the exception itself both state that they don't give any employee approval authority.
8. **Who approves:** At 15%, the VP Sales has to approve. That's Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. Policy also requires any approval above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Things to know before acting**
- **Status mismatch:** The request you sent says `"status": "Approved"`, but the CRM export shows DR-9001 as **Pending**. I found no record of anyone approving it. If someone marked it approved themselves, that approval isn't valid. It needs Michael Torres's recorded approval.
- **Last year's deal isn't a precedent:** Sarah's email points to "what we did for them last September" (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That was under the 2025 policy, when account executives could approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy fully replaced it.
