## Answer

Yes, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy it needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. The outcome is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**.

**The request date in your copy is wrong.** You submitted `"request_date": "2025-09-23"`, but the CRM record for DR-9001 shows **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email to Michael Torres about DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23. It says Acme wants "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." That earlier deal was DR-8104 on 2025-09-16, which Sarah approved herself when the 2025 policy allowed it. I used 2026-09-23. The date decides who can approve: the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%, but the 2026 policy cuts that to 10%.

**What I checked**
1. **Request:** 15% off $250,000 list ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net) on the NS-500 controller. Sarah Chen asked for it on 2026-09-23, citing contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (ERP customer C-1001). The contract names the legal entity as Acme Mfg. Holdings, trading as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date and covers the NS-500. Its clause 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B of that agreement) covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The CRO, David Morgan, approved it. It replaced the older 10% exception. The 15% requested is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception also says it gives no Northstar employee approval authority.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (Sarah reports to him). The policy also says approvals above 10% must be recorded in the CRM.

Under the procedure, a contract allowing a discount isn't enough to approve it.
