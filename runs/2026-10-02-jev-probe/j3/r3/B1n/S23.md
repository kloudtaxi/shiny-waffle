## Answer

**Yes, but Sarah can't approve it herself.** DR-9001 can go ahead only if the VP Sales, Michael Torres, approves it. The outcome is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**.

**One thing to fix first: the request date.** The request you pasted is dated **2025-09-23**, but the CRM record (discount_requests.csv) has the same request dated **2026-09-23**, still "Pending Approval". The date decides which pricing policy applies, so it changes the answer:
- **If it's 2026-09-23** (what the CRM record says, which I've gone with): the 2026 policy applies. Sarah's limit is 10%, so a 15% discount needs VP Sales approval.
- **If it really is 2025-09-23:** the 2025 policy let account executives approve up to and including 15%, so Sarah could have approved it herself (outcome APPROVE). A similar earlier request, DR-8104 on 2025-09-16, was approved under that policy.

Please confirm the date in CRM before anyone acts on this.

**How I got there (using the 2026-09-23 date):**
1. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". In the ERP and on the contract it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), trading as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen owns the account.
2. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
3. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's in force and covers NS-500. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15%, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. **15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme qualifies.**
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. Account executives can approve up to 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales. Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. (The 2027 policy, with lower limits, only starts on 2027-01-01.)
6. **Sarah's authority:** she's an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. 15% is over it.
7. **Approver:** Michael Torres is VP Sales and Sarah's manager (org chart dated 2026-09-01).
8. **Why the contract alone isn't enough:** the contract (section 3.3), the exception itself and the Sales Discount SOP all say that Acme being eligible doesn't change who inside Northstar can approve. A discount can't be approved just because the contract allows it.

The matrix also requires approvals above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM.
