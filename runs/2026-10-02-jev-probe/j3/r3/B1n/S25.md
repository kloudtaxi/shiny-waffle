## Answer

Yes, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Acme is entitled to 15% on the NS-500, but under the 2026 policy that discount needs sign-off from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**Is Acme entitled to 15%? Yes.**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it's the bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (customer C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen owns the account. A different company, Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), is not the customer here.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date of 2026-09-23. It covers the NS-500.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to **15% of list price** on the NS-500 only, for the same dates. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which expired 2025-03-31. The requested 15% is exactly at the limit.

**Can Sarah approve it herself? No.**
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix both set these limits:
  - Enterprise Account Executives: up to 10%
  - VP Sales: over 10% and up to 20%
  - Chief Revenue Officer: over 20%
- Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is above her limit.
- The contract doesn't change that. Section 3.3 of the agreement says the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't modify Northstar's internal approval authority, and the exception itself says it grants no approval authority to any employee.
- **Required approver:** VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. Sarah reports to him, and her 2026-09-23 email already asks him about DR-9001. Because the discount is over 10%, his approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

The same 15% deal last September doesn't change this. DR-8104 (2025-09-16) was decided under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that to 10%. Also, the 2027 policy takes effect 2027-01-01. It would require the Chief Revenue Officer for anything over 12%, so this should be approved before year-end.
