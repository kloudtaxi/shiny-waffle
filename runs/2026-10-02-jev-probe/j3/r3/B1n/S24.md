## Answer

**Not on Sarah Chen's own authority.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount under its contract, but in 2026 Sarah can only approve up to 10%. A 15% discount needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Outcome: **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**.

**One discrepancy:** your submission shows `"status": "Approved"`, but the CRM record for DR-9001 shows it as **Pending**. Under the 2026 policy it can't count as approved until Michael Torres signs off and his approval is recorded in the CRM.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 for account CRM-2048 on 2026-09-23 ($250,000 list price, $37,500 discount). It relies on Acme's contract pricing, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the finance system it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), and Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-500 is one of the products it covers.
5. **Pricing exception:** Acme's strategic account exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) covers NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15% off list price, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ended 2025-03-31). The 15% requested is exactly at the limit, so Acme qualifies.
6. **Policy:** The 2026 Pricing Policy applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy is already on file but only applies to requests from 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%, so 15% is beyond her limit. Both the contract (section 3.3) and the exception say Acme's discount terms don't change who inside the company can approve. Contract eligibility alone isn't grounds to approve.
8. **Approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, who Sarah reports to (per the reporting lines dated 2026-09-01). Approvals above 10% must be recorded in the CRM.

Sarah approved the same 15% herself in 2025 (DR-8104). That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered that limit to 10%, so that precedent no longer applies.
