# Credit Policy 2026 — Strategic Account Concurrence Addendum

This addendum clarifies the approval treatment for Strategic accounts.

For Strategic accounts, **VP Sales concurrence is required only when the requested credit limit
is greater than $750,000**.

Requests at or below $750,000 require only the applicable Finance approval under the 2026 Credit
Policy.

This addendum applies to requests dated on or after 2026-03-01.
