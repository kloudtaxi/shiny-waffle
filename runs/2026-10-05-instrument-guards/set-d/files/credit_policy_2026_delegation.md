# Credit Policy 2026 — Strategic Account Delegation

For Strategic account requests, the VP Sales role may approve credit limits up to and including
**$750,000** where the request is submitted by the VP Sales.

Where the VP Sales is the requestor, the usual manager-concurrence requirement is waived because
the request is already within the delegated Finance authority.

All other 2026 credit policy requirements remain unchanged.
