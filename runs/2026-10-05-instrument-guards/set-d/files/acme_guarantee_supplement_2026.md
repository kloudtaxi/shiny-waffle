# Supplemental Parent Guarantee

**Guarantee reference:** GRT-ACME-2026-SUP1

Acme Group Holdings, Inc. supplements its existing Parent Company Guarantee in favour of Northstar
Industrial Systems for the payment obligations of **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as
**Acme Manufacturing**, 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209.

## 1. Guaranteed party

The guaranteed party is the same Acme Mfg. Holdings entity named in Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026.

## 2. Supplemental amount

The Guarantor's additional liability under this Supplemental Guarantee is limited to
**$200,000** in aggregate.

This Supplemental Guarantee is in addition to, and does not replace, the $400,000 aggregate
liability under Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026.

## 3. Term

This Supplemental Guarantee is effective from 2026-05-12 through 2027-12-31.
