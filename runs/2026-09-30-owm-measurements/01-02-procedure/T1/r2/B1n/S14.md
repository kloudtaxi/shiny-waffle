## Answer

Yes. Sarah Chen can approve DR-9106 herself. The request uses standard pricing, and 8% is within what an Enterprise Account Executive may approve under the 2026 policy.

1. **The request:** DR-9106 is an 8% discount on the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (NS-EDGE) for account CRM-2048, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Competitive", so it does not rely on a contract term or pricing exception. That makes it a standard-pricing request.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen is its account owner. It is a different account from Acme Industrial Supply Co. / Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), which has its own NS-EDGE service ticket, SR-40418. I didn't check which active contract Acme Manufacturing has on the request date. A standard request doesn't depend on it.
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE has a list price of $85,000. The request's figures match: 8% is $6,800 off, for a net of $78,200.
4. **Pricing exceptions:** Acme's exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, 15% maximum, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) covers only the NS-500 controller and says it applies to no other product. The earlier 10% NS-500 exception ended on 2025-03-31. Neither covers NS-EDGE, and the request doesn't rely on either one.
5. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, and above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
6. **Authority:** The employee list and org chart show Sarah Chen as an Enterprise Account Executive. 8% is within her 10% limit, so no higher approver is needed. Under the policy, recorded approval evidence is only required above 10%. The procedure still says to record the decision and the evidence in Northstar CRM.
