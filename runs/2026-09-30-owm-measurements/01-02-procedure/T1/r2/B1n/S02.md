## Answer

**No. Don't offer 18%.** Acme Manufacturing's terms cap its NS-500 discount at 15% of list, and 18% goes past that. Sending it to a higher approver won't fix this. An 18% offer would need new or amended terms with Acme first.

**The request itself doesn't match.** The version you pasted says 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). But the knowledge base's copy of the CRM record has DR-9001 at **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres the same day (2026-09-23) also says Acme is "asking for 15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." Someone should confirm which figure is correct before anything goes to the customer.

**What I found:**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it's billed as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive, owns the account.
- **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for "up to **15%**" on the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, sets a maximum of 15% of list, and runs through 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). I found no amendment allowing more than 15%.
- **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix apply to this date. Enterprise AEs can approve up to 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. Above 20% needs the CRO, David Morgan.

**If the real ask is 15%** (as the CRM record and Sarah's email show), Acme is eligible for it. It's above Sarah's 10% limit, though, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would have to approve it and record the approval in CRM. The contract allowing 15% doesn't let Sarah approve it herself.
