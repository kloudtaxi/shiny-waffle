## Answer

No, not as the request stands. Michael Torres has the authority for a 12% discount, but the reason given in the request doesn't apply to BlueRiver. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**.

**Why the justification fails**
- The request is for BlueRiver Logistics (CRM-2051, owned by Kelly Floyd). In the ERP system that is Blue River Logistics LLC (C-1002), matched by DUNS number 07-331-9025.
- The justification cites the "Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025). That agreement is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing (C-1001 / CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730). That is a different customer.
- The NS-500 exception attached to that agreement (EXC-ACME-NS500-15: up to 15%, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. The SOP says a pricing exception covers only what it names, so Acme's terms don't carry over to BlueRiver.
- I found no contract or pricing exception for BlueRiver. Its past discounts in the CRM were all "Competitive" requests, not contract-based.
- The justification is the same wording used on Sarah Chen's Acme requests (DR-8104, DR-9001), which suggests it may have been copied over by mistake.

**Authority**
- The 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix apply on 2026-09-23. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval.
- Michael Torres is VP Sales, so 12% is within his authority.
- But the policy is clear that authority can't make up for missing eligibility. The SOP also says a request must not be approved just because a contract seems to allow the discount, and here the contract belongs to someone else.

**Next steps**
- Send DR-9107 back for commercial review.
- If BlueRiver should get this discount, resubmit it either with its real basis (for example, a standard "Competitive" request, which Michael could then approve as VP Sales) or with a new BlueRiver pricing exception.

One point I couldn't settle: Michael is also the person who submitted the request. The documents I found don't say whether someone may approve their own request above 10%.
