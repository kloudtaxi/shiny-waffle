2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen owns the account. It's billed in ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). This is a different company from "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091).
3. **The product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, with a list price of $120,000. The request uses the current list price.
4. **Contract and pricing exception:** Because the request uses standard pricing, the contract's eligibility doesn't come into it. The contract-based request is a separate one, DR-9001 (15% on NS-500, same date), and it doesn't affect this one.
5. **Policy in force:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (valid 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and fully replaces the 2025 policy.
