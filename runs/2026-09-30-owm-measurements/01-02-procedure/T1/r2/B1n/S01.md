## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy she can only approve up to 10%. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve DR-9001.

**Is Acme eligible? Yes.**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP (finance system) it's billed as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which operates as Acme Manufacturing.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23.
- **Pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests from 2025-04-01 on. The requested 15% is within that limit.

**Who can approve it?**
- Both documents say Acme's contract terms don't change who can approve inside Northstar. The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." Section 3.3 of the agreement says the same.
- Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to requests dated in 2026. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to 10%. Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales.
- The org chart and employee list show Michael Torres (EMP-200) as VP Sales, and I found no record of the role changing hands. Under the 2026 matrix, approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Why last year's approval doesn't carry over:** Sarah approved an identical 15% request herself on 2025-09-16 (DR-8104). That was allowed under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy replaced it completely from 2026-01-01. Her 2026 account plan says it's "worth confirming with Deal Desk" whether Acme's contract pricing is 15%. The exception document confirms it is.
