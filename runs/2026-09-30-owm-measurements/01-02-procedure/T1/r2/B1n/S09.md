## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

Checked the contract, pricing exception and 2025 policy; here's the answer.

**No.** Sarah Chen had enough authority for a 15% discount on that date. But on 2025-03-31 Acme was only eligible for 10% on the NS-500, so the request asks for more than the customer was entitled to.

- **Request DR-9101:** Customer is Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048; the ERP bill-to is Acme Mfg. Holdings). Product is the NS-500 Industrial Controller. Discount is 15% ($37,500 off $250,000 list), dated 2025-03-31. The request justifies the price by citing the "Acme Master Supply Agreement."
- **The contract it cites wasn't in effect yet.** That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) was signed in March 2025 but only runs from **2025-04-01** to 2028-03-31. Its 15% pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also starts 2025-04-01. Neither applied on 2025-03-31, one day earlier.
- **What did apply was the 2023 exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under the older agreement ACME-SA-2023, allowed **up to 10%** off list on the NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 through **2025-03-31**. The newer exception only replaces it for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. So the limit on the request date was 10%, and 15% is over it.
- **Sarah's authority was not the problem.** Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, an Enterprise Account Executive (her role) could approve discounts up to and including 15%. But contract eligibility and employee authority are separate checks. A more senior approver can't approve a discount above the customer's eligibility either; that needs new or amended terms.

**Options:** Hold the discount at 10% for a 2025-03-31 order, or date the order on or after 2025-04-01. From 2025-04-01 the 15% exception applies, and 15% was within Sarah's 2025 authority. (From 2026 her limit drops to 10%, so later requests like DR-9001 need VP Sales approval.)

One gap: the 2023 agreement (ACME-SA-2023) isn't in the knowledge base. I relied on the 2023 exception document, which lists its status as "Active" and its dates as running through 2025-03-31.
