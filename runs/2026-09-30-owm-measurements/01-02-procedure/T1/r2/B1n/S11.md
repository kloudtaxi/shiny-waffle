## Answer

No. Sarah Chen could not have approved DR‑9103 herself. On 2025‑09‑23 she could approve at most 15%, so a 22% discount needed VP Sales approval.

**Why:**
- **Policy in force.** The request is dated 2025‑09‑23, so the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applies (1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025). It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts up to and including 15%. Anything above 15% needs VP Sales approval.
- **Sarah Chen's authority.** The employee directory lists her as an Enterprise Account Executive. At 22%, the request is 7 points over her limit.
- **Who must approve.** Michael Torres holds the VP Sales role, and Sarah Chen reports to him. One caveat: the directory has no dates, so I'm assuming he held the role in September 2025. Another 22% request from that month, DR‑7431 on 2025‑09‑16, was approved by him, which supports that.
- **The 2026 rules don't apply.** Under the 2026 policy and authority matrix, anything over 20% would go to the Chief Revenue Officer. Those rules only cover requests dated from 2026‑01‑01.
- **No special terms are involved.** The justification is "Competitive", so this is standard pricing and doesn't rely on a contract or exception. Acme's pricing exception covers only the NS‑500 controller, not NS‑EDGE. Even if a contract allowed the discount, it wouldn't change her authority. Both the SOP and section 3.3 of the Acme Master Supply Agreement say so.

**Outcome:** Approve only with Michael Torres's approval as VP Sales. Sarah Chen cannot approve it on her own.
