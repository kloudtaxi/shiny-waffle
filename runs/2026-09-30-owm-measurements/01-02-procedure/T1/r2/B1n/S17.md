## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this, and the request can't go forward as written. It says it relies on "contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," but that agreement gives Acme no contract discount on the NS-Edge Monitoring Package. It needs a commercial review first. Even with valid contract terms, 12% is above what Sarah is allowed to approve.

**What I checked**
1. **Request:** DR-9109 asks for 12% off NS-EDGE ($85,000 list, $10,200 off, $74,800 net). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23, citing the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, and Sarah owns the account. Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), which is a different account.
3. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-EDGE is one of the products it covers.
4. **Pricing terms:** The agreement's only discount clause (§3.2) allows up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, through exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. That exception covers "NS-500 only" and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 was also NS-500 only and expired on 2025-03-31. I found no pricing exception that covers NS-EDGE for Acme.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to requests dated in 2026.
6. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% or less. 12% is over that. (Her 15% limit ended with the 2025 policy.)
7. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
8. **Decision:** The request depends on contract terms that don't cover this product. Under the SOP, pricing exceptions only apply to the product and dates they name, so this needs commercial review. The agreement itself (§3.3) and the exception both say that contract eligibility doesn't give any employee approval authority.

**Next steps:** Commercial review, usually by the Deal Desk, which owns pricing exceptions, should decide whether Acme gets a new or amended exception covering NS-EDGE. Otherwise the request could be resubmitted as standard pricing without citing the contract. Either way, 12% needs Michael Torres's approval, and approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM. Sarah could approve alone only at 10% or less on standard pricing.
