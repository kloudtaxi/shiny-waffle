## Answer

No. DR-9105 can't be approved as it's written. It needs commercial review, because the contract it cites belongs to a different customer.

**Wrong customer for the cited contract**
- DR-9105 is for account **CRM-2091, Acme Industrial Supply Co.**, a mid-market industrial distributor in Akron, OH (ERP customer C-1044). Its account owner is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen.
- The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) is with **Acme Mfg. Holdings, trading as Acme Manufacturing**, in Milwaukee, WI. That is account CRM-2048 (ERP C-1001), which Sarah owns. The two companies have different addresses and different DUNS numbers (04-812-7730 vs 09-552-1187).
- The 15% NS-500 exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. It's Schedule B to that same agreement.
- I found no contract or pricing exception for Acme Industrial Supply Co. The only other document on that account is a support ticket (SR-40418), which says "No change to commercial terms requested."
- Special terms stay with the customer and product they name, so the 15% eligibility doesn't carry over to CRM-2091. Sarah filed an identical request for CRM-2048 on the same day (DR-9001, same amounts and justification). DR-9105 may be a duplicate entered against the wrong account, so check that first.

**Authority, even if eligibility were established**
- Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests on 2026-09-23. It limits an Enterprise Account Executive like Sarah to discounts of 10% or less.
- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres. Contract eligibility never changes employee authority (MSA §3.3 and the exception's own Authority section).
- The 2025 policy's 15% limit for account executives no longer applies.

**What to do next:** Confirm whether DR-9105 was meant for CRM-2048. If it was, handle it through DR-9001, where the 15% exception applies but Michael Torres must approve. If it really is for Acme Industrial Supply, it needs commercial review and new terms for that customer. Either way, the 15% discount goes to Michael Torres for approval.
