## Answer

No, Michael Torres should not approve DR-9112 at 18%. He has the authority for a discount this size, but Acme isn't eligible for it. The request doesn't fail on who approves it. It fails because 18% is more than Acme's terms allow.

**Acme's eligibility (fails):**
- The request relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025). CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same customer as the ERP name "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). That agreement is active on 2026-09-23 (it runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) and covers the NS-500.
- The discount limit is in Schedule B, pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers only the NS-500 and caps the discount at **15% of list price**, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception.
- 18% is 3 points over that cap. I found no amendment or newer exception that allows more.
- The earlier Acme requests were for 15%: DR-8104 (approved in September 2025) and DR-9001, which Sarah Chen emailed Michael about on 2026-09-23 and which is still pending.

**Michael's authority (would pass on its own):**
- He is VP Sales, according to the employee list and the org chart.
- The 2026 Pricing Policy and the Approval Authority Matrix make VP Sales the approver for discounts over 10% and up to 20%. An 18% discount falls in that band.

**Outcome: reject or escalate.** A more senior approver, such as the CRO (David Morgan), can't fix this, because authority doesn't expand what the customer is entitled to. Section 3.3 of the agreement and the exception itself both say contract terms don't change internal approval authority. Two ways forward:
- **Get new terms:** Deal Desk and Legal would need to amend the exception or issue a new one allowing 18%.
- **Resubmit at 15% or less:** Michael, as VP Sales, could then approve it himself.

Two points to flag:
- One source document linked to Michael in the knowledge graph could no longer be retrieved. Nothing I found suggests his role has changed.
- DR-9112 looks like the same Acme NS-500 deal as DR-9001 ($250,000 list value, same justification), but at 18% instead of 15%. It's worth checking whether it's a duplicate or a replacement.
