## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. DR-9102 is a 25% discount, and under the 2026 pricing policy anything above 20% needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan.

1. **Request:** DR-9102 is for CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. The product is NS-EDGE, the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, listed at $85,000. The request is for 25% ($21,250 off, $63,750 net), dated 2026-09-23. The justification is "Competitive". It doesn't cite the Acme Master Supply Agreement or any pricing exception, so it's a standard-pricing request and no contract eligibility check is needed.
2. **Customer terms:** This doesn't affect the answer, but Acme's only pricing exception I found (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) covers NS-500, not NS-EDGE. Its NS-500 contract allowance also ended on 2025-03-31. The Master Supply Agreement also says, in §3.3, that the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
3. **Policy in force:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix sets these limits:
   - Enterprise Account Executive: up to 10%
   - VP Sales: over 10% up to 20%
   - Chief Revenue Officer: over 20%
4. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101, reporting to VP Sales Michael Torres), so her limit is 10%. At 25%, the request is also above the VP Sales band.
5. **Required approver:** The org chart as of 2026-09-01 lists David Morgan as Chief Revenue Officer. Because the discount is over 10%, the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

The discount can go ahead only with David Morgan's approval, recorded in the CRM.
