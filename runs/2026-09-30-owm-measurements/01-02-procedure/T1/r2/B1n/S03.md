## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this. It also can't be approved as submitted, because no special pricing terms cover NS-Cloud for Acme, so it needs commercial review.

**Why:**

1. **The customer and contract check out.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which is billed in ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). Its Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. NS-CLOUD is one of the products it lists.
2. **The contract's 15% applies only to the NS-500 controller.** Section 3.2 gives Acme up to 15% on the NS-500 only, as set out in Schedule B. Schedule B (exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15) says it covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." I found no pricing exception for NS-CLOUD. So the request's justification ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement") doesn't hold up for this product.
3. **15% is also above her own limit.** Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, an Enterprise Account Executive (Sarah Chen's role) can approve up to 10%. Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Section 3.3 of the agreement says the contract doesn't change internal approval authority either. Even so, sending it to Torres wouldn't fix the missing terms. The 15% needs commercial review, or new terms for NS-CLOUD, first.

**A mismatch you should check:** the discount request log in the knowledge base (discount_requests.csv) records DR-9001 as an **NS-500** request at $250,000 list, not NS-CLOUD at $120,000 as in the record you gave me. If the NS-500 version is the correct one, the result changes. Acme would qualify for 15% under the NS-500 exception, but it would still need Michael Torres's approval because 15% is over Sarah's 10% limit. Please confirm which product the request is really for.
