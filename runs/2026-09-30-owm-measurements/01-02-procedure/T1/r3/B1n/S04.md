## Answer

Found the policies, the Acme contract and exception, and a date mismatch in the request. Here's the answer.

**Yes, for a request dated 2025-09-23 she could have approved it herself. But DR-9001 isn't dated 2025.** Northstar CRM (discount_requests.csv) records DR-9001 with a request date of **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email to Michael Torres about DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23 and says Acme wants "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." That earlier deal was DR-8104, dated 2025-09-16, which was approved. The date in your copy of the request looks wrong, and the answer depends on which date is right.

**What holds on either date**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen owns the account. The contract party is "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing," at the same Milwaukee address, so they're the same customer.
- **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% off list, applies to NS-500 only, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception. So 15% is within Acme's eligibility on both dates. The exception also says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- **Sarah Chen:** Enterprise Account Executive, reporting to Michael Torres (VP Sales).

**If the date really is 2025-09-23:** Pricing Policy 2025 lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts up to and including 15%. The discount is within both Acme's eligibility and Sarah's own authority, so she could approve it. That's consistent with DR-8104 being approved in September 2025.

**Using the recorded date, 2026-09-23:** Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix replace the 2025 policy from 2026-01-01. Enterprise Account Executives can now approve only up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Acme is still eligible for 15%, but Sarah can't approve it herself. **Michael Torres (VP Sales) must approve it**, and the approval has to be recorded in CRM. Her email suggests the agreement makes it routine, but under the SOP a discount can't be approved just because the contract allows it.
