## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve this. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The request says the 15% comes from the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement's 15% discount only covers the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It doesn't cover NS-Cloud.

**How I got there:**

1. **Request:** DR-9001, CRM account CRM-2048, NS-CLOUD, 15% off ($18,000 off a $120,000 list price), requested 2026-09-23 by Sarah Chen. The stated reason is contract pricing under the Acme agreement, so this is a special-terms request, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, the party that signed the agreement. Sarah is the account owner.
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000.
4. **Contract:** The agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It lists NS-Cloud as a supplied product, but its only discount term (§3.2) gives up to 15% on the NS-500, "as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B." For NS-Cloud, prices are list price less whatever discount is agreed in an order (§3.1).
5. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) sets a 15% maximum for "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only." It says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." I found no exception covering NS-Cloud. Under the procedure, special terms don't carry over to other products, so this needs commercial review.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, both in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%, so 15% is over her limit either way. §3.3 of the agreement also says the customer's contract terms don't change Northstar's internal approval authority. (Last year's 2025 policy let account executives approve up to 15%, but it no longer applies.)
8. **Approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% go to the VP Sales, Michael Torres. But his sign-off can't make up for missing eligibility. The discount would first need commercial review, and possibly new or amended contract terms.

**The CRM record doesn't match what you sent.** The request log in the knowledge base shows DR-9001 as **NS-500**, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. It's worth checking which product is actually being requested. If it's really the NS-500, the answer changes: the 15% is covered by Schedule B, so the outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, meaning it still needs Michael Torres's approval because it's over Sarah's 10% limit.
