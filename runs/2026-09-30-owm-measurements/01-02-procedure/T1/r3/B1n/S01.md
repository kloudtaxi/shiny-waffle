## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme Manufacturing is entitled to the 15% discount, but it's above her approval limit. Michael Torres, VP Sales, needs to approve it.

**Walking through the steps:**

1. **Request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on the NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 off, $212,500 net), requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it's billed to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **Product:** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-500 is one of the products it covers.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to **15%** off list on NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which expired 2025-03-31. The 15% request is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, both effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit. Neither the contract nor the exception changes that: the agreement (§3.3) and the exception both say the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, according to the employee directory and org chart. I found nothing showing a change in that role by the request date.

**One thing to watch:** Sarah approved an identical 15% Acme NS-500 request herself in 2025 (DR-8104, dated 2025-09-16). The 2025 policy let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy replaced it and lowered the limit to 10%, so that earlier approval doesn't allow her to approve this one. Because the discount is above 10%, the approval also has to be recorded in Northstar CRM.
