## Answer

No, not yet. The request depends on the "Acme Master Supply Agreement," and I couldn't find that agreement, or any pricing exception for NS-500, anywhere in the knowledge base. Our procedure says to ask for the document before deciding. Even once it turns up, Sarah can't approve 15% herself under the 2026 policy. Michael Torres, VP Sales, would have to approve it.

**What I found**
1. **Request:** DR-9001. Acme wants 15% off NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount), requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on a contract term: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings," Milwaukee. Both have the same address and the same DUNS number (04-812-7730). This is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044, Akron), which is a separate customer.
3. **Contract and pricing exception:** I found no contract or exception record for C-1001. The agreement name appears in the knowledge graph, but nothing is attached to it. The only support for 15% is second-hand:
   - Sarah's email of 2026-09-23 says "I think their agreement covers it."
   - Her account plan of 2026-02-10 says "I believe it's 15%… Worth confirming with Deal Desk before the Q3 order."
   - Last year's request DR-8104 got 15%, but that was dated 2025-09-16. It was decided under the 2025 policy, when account executives could approve up to 15%. It doesn't show that any contract terms are still in force.
4. **Policy in force on 2026-09-23:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix 2026, which fully replace the 2025 policy. Account executives can approve up to 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales. Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
5. **Authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is over her 10% limit. Even if the contract allowed it, that wouldn't give her more authority. The procedure also says a request can't be approved just because a contract permits the discount.

**Next steps:** Get the signed Acme Master Supply Agreement from Sarah or Deal Desk. It needs to show NS-500 pricing, the maximum discount, and that it was in effect on 2026-09-23. If it supports 15%, the outcome becomes approval with Michael Torres signing, recorded in the CRM. If it caps the discount lower, doesn't cover NS-500, or has expired, the request needs to be rejected or escalated, or go to commercial review.
