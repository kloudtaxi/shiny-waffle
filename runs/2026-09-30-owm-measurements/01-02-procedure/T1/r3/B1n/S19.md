## Answer

**Yes. Sarah Chen can approve DR-9111 herself.** The 6% discount is within her own authority under the 2026 pricing policy, and nobody else needs to sign off.

1. **The request.** DR-9111 is for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, billed in ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings, C-1001). The product is NS-CLOUD, the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, which lists at $120,000. The request asks for 6%, which is $7,200 off, for a net of $112,800. The CRM figures are consistent. The justification is "Competitive," so it uses standard pricing and doesn't depend on any contract term or pricing exception. That means no special eligibility has to be shown.
2. **Policy in force.** The request is dated 2026-09-23, so the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It fully replaced the 2025 policy.
3. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, according to employees.csv (EMP-101) and the September 2026 org chart. She is also the account owner for CRM-2048. Under the 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 6% is under that limit.
4. **Other approvers.** VP Sales (Michael Torres) is only needed above 10%, and the CRO (David Morgan) only above 20%. Because the discount is 10% or less, the policy's extra requirement to record approval evidence for discounts above 10% doesn't apply either.

**Don't confuse this with a separate request.** DR-9001 is also for Acme on the same date, but it's for the NS-500 at 15% and relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement. That one would need its own check and VP Sales approval. None of it affects DR-9111.
