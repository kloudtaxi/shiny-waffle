Exceptions are limited to the product and dates they name, so it doesn't cover NS-EDGE. The request doesn't rely on it anyway. 3. **Policy in force:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The limits are:
