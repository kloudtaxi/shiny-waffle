## Answer

No. Michael Torres can't approve DR-9112, and no one else can either under the current terms. The problem isn't his authority. The 18% discount is above what Acme is entitled to under its contract.

**The request.** DR-9112 is for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), NS-500, 18% off a $250,000 list price ($45,000 discount, $205,000 net). Michael Torres entered it on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".

1. **Customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing.
2. **Contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500. Section 3.2 limits Acme to discounts of **up to 15%** on NS-500, as set out in Schedule B.
3. **Pricing exception.** Schedule B is exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers only NS-500, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a **maximum discount of 15% of list**. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). I found no amendment that allows 18%.
4. **Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Torres is VP Sales, so 18% would be within his own approval limit. That's the only reason it looks approvable.
5. **Decision.** The request is justified only by the contract, and the contract allows at most 15%. The agreement (§3.3) and the exception both say that contract eligibility and approval authority are separate. Torres's limit doesn't raise Acme's cap, and escalating to the Chief Revenue Officer wouldn't either. Getting 18% would need new or amended terms with Acme.

**Options:**
- **Reduce the discount to 15% or less.** 15% is within the contract, and Torres could approve it himself as VP Sales. Sarah Chen's DR-9001 is already pending for this: same customer, product and date, at 15% ($37,500 off).
- **Negotiate an amended exception.** Any discount above 15% needs this before it can be approved.
