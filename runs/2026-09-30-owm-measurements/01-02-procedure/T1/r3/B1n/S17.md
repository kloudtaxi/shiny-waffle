## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9109, and the Acme contract doesn't support 12% on NS-EDGE, so the request needs commercial review.

**1. The contract pricing it cites doesn't cover this product.**
- The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) is active on 2026-09-23. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It lists NS-EDGE as a product Northstar supplies.
- Its only discount term, §3.2, gives Acme up to 15% on the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**, as set out in Schedule B.
- Schedule B is exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and says "It does not apply to any other Northstar product."
- I found no contract term or pricing exception giving Acme a discount on NS-EDGE. The NS-500 terms don't carry over to other products, so the reason given on the request has nothing to rest on.

**2. The discount is also above her own limit.**
- The 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix apply to this date. Enterprise Account Executives may approve up to 10%, and Sarah Chen is one. Anything above 10% up to 20% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
- Her 15% limit under the 2025 policy no longer applies.
- MSA §3.3 and the exception both say that what the customer is entitled to under the contract doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.

**What would move it forward:** Deal Desk would have to review it commercially. That could lead to a new or amended NS-EDGE term for Acme, or the request could be resubmitted as standard pricing with a different justification. Either way, 12% still needs Michael Torres's approval, recorded in Northstar CRM. Sending it to him now wouldn't fix the missing contract basis.
