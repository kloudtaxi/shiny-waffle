## Answer

No, not at 18%. Acme's contract caps its NS-500 discount at 15% of list, and 18% is above that cap. A senior sign-off can't fix this. Going above 15% would need new or amended contract terms.

**Check the requested percentage first.** You've given DR-9001 as 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). The knowledge base copy of the CRM log has DR-9001 at **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres the same day says Acme is "asking for 15% off list." Please confirm which figure is correct.

**How I got there:**
1. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing. In the contract the legal entity is Acme Mfg. Holdings, trading as Acme Manufacturing. This is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a separate account.
2. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is active from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers the NS-500. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for **up to 15%** on the NS-500.
3. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that contract and covers the NS-500 only. It is active on 2026-09-23 with a **maximum of 15%** of list. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). I found no amendment raising the cap.
4. **Result:** 18% is 3 points above what Acme is entitled to.

**Approval rules:** Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 approval matrix, Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive) can approve up to 10% herself. Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Both the contract (section 3.3) and the exception state that the customer's eligibility doesn't change who has to approve internally.

**What you can do:**
- **Offer 15%**, the contract maximum. Sarah can't approve that herself, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would need to sign off. That is the same basis as last September's DR-8104 at 15%.
- **For 18%**, the Deal Desk would first need to amend the exception or issue a new one. The current exception was approved by David Morgan (CRO). Only then would the discount go to the VP Sales for approval.
