## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No, she couldn't. Her authority would have covered 15%, but on 2025-03-31 Acme was only eligible for up to 10% off NS-500. The agreement the request cites didn't take effect until the next day.

**Her authority was enough.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applied from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives could approve discounts up to and including 15%. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (employee list and org chart), and she owns the Acme account (CRM-2048). The role records carry no dates, so I've assumed she held that role in March 2025. The stricter 2026 limit of 10% for account executives doesn't apply to a request dated in 2025.

**Acme's eligibility was not:**
- The request cites the "Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025). It was signed on 2025-03-20/22 but runs only from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% NS-500 exception attached to it (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also starts on 2025-04-01. It replaces the earlier exception only for requests dated on or after that day.
- The terms in force on 2025-03-31 were the earlier NS-500 exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023. It allowed up to **10%** off list for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The request date is the last day of that window.
- 15% is above that 10% cap. A more senior approver can't fix that, because the procedure says a contract term doesn't change who can approve, and a higher-level sign-off doesn't change what the customer is entitled to.

**What would work:** approve the request at 10% or less, which is within Sarah's own authority, or move it to 2025-04-01 or later. On or after 2025-04-01 the 15% exception applies and 15% is within her 2025 limit. Such a request would have looked like DR-8104 (15%, 2025-09-16), which the CRM shows as approved.
