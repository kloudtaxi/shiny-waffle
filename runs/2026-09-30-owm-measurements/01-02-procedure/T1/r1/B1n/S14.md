## Answer

**Yes, Sarah Chen can approve DR-9106 herself.** The 8% discount is within her own authority as an Enterprise Account Executive under the 2026 pricing policy. No one else needs to sign off.

1. **The request:** DR-9106 asks for 8% off the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (NS-EDGE) for account CRM-2048, dated 2026-09-23 and raised by Sarah Chen. The reason given is "Competitive", so it uses standard pricing, not a contract term or pricing exception.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. In the ERP system the same customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001).
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE lists at $85,000. So 8% is $6,800, leaving a net of $78,200, which matches the request.
4. **The contract:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and lists NS-EDGE among its products. It was active on the request date.
5. **The pricing exception:** Acme's only current exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, up to 15%) covers the NS-500 controller only, so it doesn't apply to NS-EDGE. That doesn't matter here, because this request doesn't rely on it.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It fully replaces the 2025 policy.
7. **Her authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (employee list and org chart). The 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix let that role approve discounts up to and including 10%. 8% is within that limit.
8. **Other approvers:** Only discounts over 10% need the VP Sales (Michael Torres). Only discounts over 20% need the Chief Revenue Officer (David Morgan). The extra requirement to record approval evidence also only applies above 10%.

Don't mix this up with DR-9001, Sarah's separate request for 15% off the NS-500 on the same day. That one relies on the NS-500 exception, and at 15% it is above her 10% limit.
