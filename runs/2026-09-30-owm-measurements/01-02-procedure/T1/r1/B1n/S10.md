**Customer and product.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. Its ERP bill-to is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, listed at $85,000.

**Contract and exceptions.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 was active on the request date (it runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) and covers NS-EDGE. But its only discount term is up to 15% on the NS-500 controller. The older exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10 was also for NS-500 only, and it expired 2025-03-31. No special terms cover NS-EDGE, and the request doesn't claim any. Section 3.3 of the agreement also says contract eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.

**Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix both apply, since they run 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Their approval limits are:
- Enterprise Account Executive: up to 10%
- VP Sales: over 10% and up to 20%
- Chief Revenue Officer: over 20%

Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. At 25%, the discount falls in the CRO's band. The knowledge base lists David Morgan as the only CRO.

**Decision:** approve only with CRO authorization. Nothing in any Acme contract term is being exceeded, because the request doesn't rely on one. It simply needs a higher approver. The policy also requires any approval above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM.
