## Answer

No. Michael Torres has the authority for a 12% discount, but he can't approve this request yet. It says it's based on a contract, and that contract doesn't cover BlueRiver Logistics.

**Approval authority (in his favour):** On 2026-09-23 the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. Under them, account executives can approve up to 10%, the VP Sales can approve more than 10% up to 20%, and the Chief Revenue Officer handles anything above 20%. The employee list and org chart show Michael Torres as VP Sales, so 12% is within his limit. He is also the person who made the request, so he would be approving his own request. Nothing I found says whether that's allowed.

**Contract basis (the problem):**
- The request says "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its NS-500 pricing exception (up to 15%) are with Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing (ERP C-1001, CRM-2048). The only customer named in them is Acme Manufacturing.
- BlueRiver Logistics (CRM-2051) is a different company: in the ERP it's Blue River Logistics LLC (C-1002), with a different address and DUNS number. I found no contract or pricing exception for BlueRiver.
- Under the procedure, a pricing exception applies only to the customer, product and dates it names, and special terms can't be carried over to another customer. Even Acme's own agreement (section 3.3) says contract eligibility doesn't change Northstar's approval authority. Authority alone isn't enough to approve a request that depends on terms the customer doesn't have.

**What to do:** Send the request to commercial review. The justification may simply be wrong, since it's word-for-word the text on Acme's requests (for example DR-9001), and the 12% may have been meant as a standard competitive discount. If so, resubmit it on that basis, and a VP Sales approval could then work. Otherwise BlueRiver needs its own contract or pricing exception, and whoever approves it shouldn't be the person who requested it.
