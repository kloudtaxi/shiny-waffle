> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen had the authority to approve a 15% discount on that date, but Acme was only entitled to 10% off NS-500 on 2025-03-31. Approving it would have given the customer more than its contract allowed.

**Why the date matters:**
- **The agreement the request cites wasn't in force yet.** DR-9101 relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% exception attached to it (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, Schedule B) also starts on 2025-04-01. Both begin one day after this request.
- **The 2023 exception still applied, with a 10% ceiling.** EXC-ACME-NS500-10 (under agreement ACME-SA-2023) gave Acme up to 10% off NS-500 for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That covers the request date. The newer exception only replaces it for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01.
- **Sarah had enough authority, but that doesn't settle it.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Pricing Policy 2025 lets that role approve discounts up to and including 15%, so 15% was within her limit. The SOP, though, says contractual eligibility and employee authority are separate checks, and both have to pass. Here the eligibility check fails.

**What this means:** The discount is 5 points over Acme's 10% ceiling. A more senior approver can't fix that, because the problem is the customer's eligibility, not anyone's authority. The options were to cap the discount at 10%, or to date the order on or after 2025-04-01, when the 15% terms apply. As an aside, Sarah's 2026 account plan says Acme's 15% is "worth confirming with Deal Desk," so the start date of the 15% terms may not have been clear inside the team.
