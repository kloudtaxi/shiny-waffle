## Answer

No, we can't offer 18%. Acme's contract terms cap the discount on the NS-500 at 15%, and 18% goes past that cap. Getting a higher approver doesn't solve this: the only fixes are new or amended terms, or bringing the request down to 15% or less.

**Where the request sits against the checks:**
- **Request:** DR-9001 comes from Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive who owns the Acme account. It asks for 18% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller, dated 2026-09-23. The basis given is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the request relies on special contract terms rather than standard pricing.
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, is the same customer as ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001).
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date.
- **Pricing exception:** The exception attached to that agreement (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) covers the NS-500 only, for the same dates as the contract. It allows at most 15% off list price, which makes 18% over the limit. It replaced an earlier 10% exception from 2023, which no longer applies. I found no amendment raising the 15% limit.
- **Policy:** The 2026 pricing policy and approval matrix let an Enterprise Account Executive approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. So Sarah can't approve this alone even at 15%.
- **Authority is separate from eligibility:** The agreement (§3.3) and the exception both say the contract terms don't give anyone at Northstar approval authority.

**What would work:**
- **At 15% or less:** Acme is eligible under the contract, and Michael Torres (VP Sales) would still need to approve because it's over Sarah's 10% limit.
- **At 18%:** Acme would need a new or amended exception. The current one was approved by David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer.

**The CRM record doesn't match your version.** The discount request records I can see list DR-9001 at **15.0%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), not 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). Please confirm which figure is current. Sarah's own 2026 account plan says she thinks the rate is 15% and should be confirmed with Deal Desk. That matches the exception and a 15% approval for Acme in September 2025 (DR-8104).
