**The request.** Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive) requested 15% off list on NS-500 for CRM account CRM-2048, dated 2026-09-23. That is $37,500 off $250,000, for a net of $212,500. The stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so the request depends on a contract term, not standard pricing.

**The customer.** CRM-2048 is "ACME Manufacturing," and its ERP bill-to is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). That's a different customer from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) and Acme Industrial (CRM-2091, a service ticket).

**The contract, which is what's missing.** I couldn't find the agreement itself, an active contract for C-1001 on 2026-09-23, or a pricing exception covering NS-500. The only support is Sarah's own belief:
- Her 2026 account plan says: "I believe it's 15%, same as what we approved for them last September. Worth confirming with Deal Desk before the Q3 order."

- Last year's 15% (DR-8104, 2025-09-16) isn't a precedent. It was approved under the 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15% themselves, and a pricing exception only covers the product and dates it names.

**Authority, whatever the contract says.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to this date. They cap an Account Executive's authority at 10%, and discounts above 10% up to 20% need VP Sales approval. So Sarah can't approve 15% herself. Even if the contract is confirmed, Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it and record that approval in the CRM. The SOP also says a request can't be approved just because a contract allows the discount.

**Next steps:**
1. Ask Sarah or Deal Desk for the Acme Master Supply Agreement and any NS-500 pricing exception. They need to show it was active for C-1001 on 2026-09-23 and allows at least 15%.
2. If it's confirmed, the outcome becomes approval with authorization from Michael Torres. If the terms allow less than 15% or don't cover NS-500 on that date, the request should be rejected or escalated, or sent for commercial review.
