## Answer

**No. Michael Torres should not approve DR-9112 as it stands.** He has the authority for an 18% discount, but Acme is only entitled to 15% on the NS-500, and a higher approver can't fix that.

**What I checked:**
1. **The request:** DR-9112 is for CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing). It asks for 18% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller ($45,000 off a $250,000 list price) on 2026-09-23. Michael Torres submitted it. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it depends on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** The agreement is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It's the same customer as CRM-2048.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.2 lets Acme get **up to 15%** off the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this entitlement "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, sets a **15% maximum** and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. I found nothing in the knowledge base that raises Acme's limit to 18%.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix 2026 applied on 2026-09-23. Account Executives can approve up to 10%, VP Sales from over 10% up to 20%, and the Chief Revenue Officer above 20%.
7. **Michael Torres's authority:** He is VP Sales, according to the employee list and the org chart. So 18% is within his own limit.
8. **The decision:** Company rules say a discount can't be approved just because the requester has enough authority. The customer also has to be entitled to it. 18% is 3 points above Acme's 15% maximum. To go ahead at 18%, the terms with Acme would need to change first, through Deal Desk or Legal. Otherwise the request should be cut to 15% or less, which Torres could then approve himself.

One more thing: there's a separate pending request, **DR-9001**, from Sarah Chen (Acme's account owner). It's for the same account, product and date, but at 15%, which is within Acme's limit. You may want to check whether DR-9112 duplicates it before acting on either one.
