## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9109, and it shouldn't go ahead as filed. The request cites "contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," but that agreement gives Acme no discount on the NS-Edge Monitoring Package. It needs commercial review.

**Customer and product.** The CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen owns it. In the ERP the same customer is billed as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. The request is for 12% off, which is $10,200, giving a net of $74,800.

**The contract doesn't give Acme this discount.**
- The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It lists NS-EDGE as a product Northstar will supply.
- The only discount it grants is in §3.2: up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in Schedule B. Nothing in it gives a discount on NS-EDGE.
- Schedule B is pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older exception it replaced, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, also covered only NS-500 and ended 2025-03-31.
- A pricing exception applies only to the product it names, so the NS-500 terms don't carry over to NS-EDGE.

**Sarah's authority falls short as well.** Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, an Enterprise Account Executive (Sarah's role) can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres. §3.3 of the agreement also says a customer's contract terms don't change Northstar's internal approval limits. The 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%, was fully replaced on 2026-01-01.

**Next steps.** Either Deal Desk and Legal agree new or amended terms that cover NS-EDGE, or the request is resubmitted as a standard, non-contract discount. In either case 12% is above Sarah's limit, so Michael Torres would have to approve it and the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
