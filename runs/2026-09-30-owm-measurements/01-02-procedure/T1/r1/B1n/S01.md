## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it's above her approval limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it. **Outcome: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.**

**Is Acme entitled to 15%? Yes.**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the finance (ERP) system the same customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001); the address and DUNS number match. Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a different company.
- **Contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers the NS-500. It was in force on 2026-09-23.
- **Pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is attached to that agreement. It allows up to 15% off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The request is for that product, within those dates and exactly at the 15% maximum. This exception replaced the older 10% one (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31.

**Can Sarah approve it? No.**
- The Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix both apply on 2026-09-23. They let an Enterprise Account Executive approve discounts up to 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so 15% is above her limit.
- The contract and the exception both say that Acme's right to the discount doesn't give any Northstar employee authority to approve it (MSA §3.3 and the exception's "Authority" section). The matrix also says authority comes from the role, not the person or the account.
- Michael Torres is VP Sales (EMP-200) and Sarah's manager, so he's the required approver.

Sarah approved an identical request herself before (DR-8104, 15%, dated 2025-09-16). That was allowed under the 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered that limit to 10%, so that request doesn't count as a precedent here.
