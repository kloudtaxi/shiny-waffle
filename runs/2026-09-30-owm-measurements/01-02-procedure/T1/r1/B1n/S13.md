## Answer

**No, DR-9105 can't be approved as submitted.** It asks for Acme Manufacturing's contract pricing, but it was entered for a different company. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: a commercial review has to happen before any approval.

**Why:**

1. **The customer is not the one on the contract.** The request uses account CRM-2091, "Acme Industrial" in Akron, OH, which is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in the ERP system (customer C-1044, DUNS 09-552-1187, industrial distribution). The Acme Master Supply Agreement is with a different company: Acme Mfg. Holdings, trading as Acme Manufacturing, in Milwaukee (C-1001, CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730). The two companies have different names, addresses, DUNS numbers and CRM accounts.
2. **The contract and exception don't cover this customer.** Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 and its pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 do allow up to 15% on the NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, but only for Acme Manufacturing. The procedure says special terms don't carry over to another customer. I found no agreement or pricing exception for Acme Industrial Supply Co.
3. **Earlier approvals for Acme Manufacturing don't apply here.** Past 15% NS-500 requests with the same justification (for example DR-8104 in 2025 and DR-9001 on the same date) were for account CRM-2048, not this account.
4. **Sarah Chen also lacks the authority.** Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Even if the customer were eligible, 15% would need his sign-off. His approval can't create eligibility the customer doesn't have.
5. **Account owner:** Zachary Brown owns Acme Industrial Supply Co. in the CRM. Sarah Chen owns Acme Manufacturing.

**Next steps:** Check whether the request was put on the wrong account and was meant for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048). If so, fix it and route it to the VP Sales. If it really is for Acme Industrial Supply, it needs new pricing terms or a standard-pricing justification, then approval under the 2026 bands.
