## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve this.** There are two separate problems: 15% is above her own approval limit, and more importantly, nothing gives Acme a 15% discount on NS-Cloud. Sending it to a more senior approver doesn't fix the second problem. It needs commercial review first.

**What I checked**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, is the same customer as the ERP account Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen owns the account.
- **Product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000. So 15% is $18,000, which matches the request.
- **Contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. NS-CLOUD is one of the products it supplies.
- **Contract discount terms:** The agreement's pricing clause (§3.2) gives Acme up to 15% on the **NS-500 Industrial Controller only**, through the attached pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. That exception says it covers only NS-500 and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The agreement has no discount terms for NS-CLOUD. So the justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement" doesn't hold for this product.
- **Policy on the request date:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. The 2025 policy, which let AEs approve up to 15%, no longer applies.
- **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% and 15% is over it. Even if Acme were entitled to the discount, that wouldn't change her authority: the agreement's §3.3 and the exception itself both say this.

**What happens next:** Deal Desk or Commercial needs to review this. They could either add a new or amended exception covering NS-CLOUD, or treat the request as ordinary (non-contract) pricing. Either way, 15% would then need approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**The CRM record doesn't match your request.** The copy of the Northstar CRM discount requests in the knowledge base lists DR-9001 as **NS-500**, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, with the same requestor, date, percentage and justification. Please check which product this request is really for. If it's NS-500, the outcome is different: Acme is entitled to 15% under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, so it would go to Michael Torres (VP Sales) for approval. Sarah still couldn't approve it herself.
