## Answer

No, DR-9105 can't be approved as it stands. The Acme Master Supply Agreement it relies on belongs to a different company, and I found no contract with Acme Industrial Supply Co. We need to ask for the contract document before deciding.

**Why**

1. **The customer is the wrong legal entity.** CRM-2091 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.**, a mid-market distributor in Akron, OH (ERP customer C-1044, DUNS 09-552-1187). The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) is with **Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing**, in Milwaukee, WI (CRM-2048 / C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730). These are separate companies with similar names. One customer's contract terms never apply to another.
2. **No contract or pricing exception covers CRM-2091.** The only NS-500 exception is EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15% on NS-500, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31). It's Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025 and names Acme Manufacturing, so it covers only that company. The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 also named Acme Manufacturing and expired on 2025-03-31. The only CRM-2091 document I found is a support ticket, SR-40418, which says "No change to commercial terms requested."
3. **15% is above Sarah Chen's own limit anyway.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix both apply on 2026-09-23. Under them, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%, and anything above 10% up to 20% needs the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres. Even with a valid contract, the exception would only make the customer eligible for the discount. It says itself that it gives no Northstar employee authority to approve it.

**Possible mix-up:** Request DR-9001 is identical (NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list value, same date, same justification) but filed under CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, which Sarah owns. DR-9105 may be a duplicate entered under the wrong account; CRM-2091's owner is Zachary Brown. It's worth checking with Sarah. If the deal is really for Acme Manufacturing, the 15% is within that customer's exception but would still need Michael Torres's approval.
