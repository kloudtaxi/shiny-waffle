The reason given is "Competitive," so it doesn't rely on any contract term or special pricing agreement. It's a standard-pricing request. 2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same legal entity as the finance-system customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings." Under the 2026 policy and its Approval Authority Matrix, that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 6% is within that limit, so no higher approver is needed.
