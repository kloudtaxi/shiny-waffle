## Answer

No, he can't. Michael Torres is VP Sales, so he has the authority to approve an 18% discount in 2026. But Acme Manufacturing's contract only allows up to 15% off the NS-500, so DR-9112 goes beyond what the customer is entitled to. A more senior approver doesn't change that; the request needs new or amended terms.

1. **The request.** DR-9112 asks for 18% off the NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Michael Torres. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it depends on the contract's special terms.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. In the ERP it's billed as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. This is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) or Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), which are separate customers.
3. **The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. It covers the NS-500. Section 3.2 limits NS-500 discounts to **15%**. Section 3.3 says the contract's pricing doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
4. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its maximum is **15%** of list price. It replaced the earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
5. **Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 approval matrix apply:
   - Account executives can approve up to 10%.
   - VP Sales can approve above 10% and up to 20%.
   - Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
   
   As VP Sales, Michael's authority covers 18%. But the procedure says authority doesn't create eligibility, and a request can't be approved just because someone has the authority.
6. **Decision.** Acme is eligible for at most 15%, and 18% is 3 points over that. The options are to bring the request down to 15% or less, or to get the Acme terms formally amended first. A 15% request would still need VP Sales approval under the 2026 policy.

Two related records: Sarah Chen's email of 2026-09-23 and Pending request DR-9001 (same product, same day) ask for 15% on the same Acme NS-500 order. That request fits within the contract. Michael also requested DR-9112 himself, so approving it would mean approving his own request.
