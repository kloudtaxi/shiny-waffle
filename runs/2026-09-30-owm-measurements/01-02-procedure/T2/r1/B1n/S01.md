## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it's above what she's allowed to approve. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.

**Outcome: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**

**Is Acme eligible? Yes, up to 15%.**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing (Milwaukee, WI). It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) and Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), both in Akron, OH. Those companies' terms play no part here.
- **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings dba Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 makes the customer eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers the NS-500 Industrial Controller only, allows at most 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10. The requested 15% is exactly at that maximum.

**Can Sarah approve it? No.**
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix let an Enterprise Account Executive (Sarah's role) approve up to 10%.
- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. Per the employee list and org chart, that's Michael Torres.
- Contract eligibility doesn't give Sarah more authority. MSA §3.3 and the exception's own "Authority" section both say so.

**Why the "same as last September" reasoning doesn't hold:** Sarah's earlier 15% approval for Acme (DR-8104, 2025-09-16) fell under the 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy fully replaced it and lowered that limit to 10%. The approval also has to be recorded in Northstar CRM, because the policy requires recorded evidence for anything over 10%.
