## Answer

No, not yet. Michael Torres has the authority to approve a 12% discount, but the request can't be approved because it relies on another customer's contract.

**Authority is not the problem**
- Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 1 Jan–31 Dec 2026) and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval.
- Michael Torres is VP Sales, according to both the employee list and the org chart. So a 12% discount is within his authority.
- The procedure says authority alone isn't enough, though. The discount also has to be backed by terms that actually belong to this customer.

**The problem is the contract the request cites**
- CRM account CRM-2051, "BlueRiver Logistics," is the same company as ERP customer C-1002, Blue River Logistics LLC. The DUNS number (07-331-9025) and address match.
- The request's stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
- That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, 1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2028) is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings. Its NS-500 exception (up to 15% off) also names Acme Manufacturing. Neither one covers Blue River.
- Under the procedure, a contract or pricing exception applies only to the customer it names. Another customer's terms never apply.
- I found no contract or NS-500 pricing exception for Blue River Logistics in the knowledge base.
- Even Acme's own agreement says contract pricing doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority (§3.3).

**What to do:** hold DR-9107 and ask Michael Torres for a contract or NS-500 pricing exception in Blue River Logistics LLC's name that was active on 23 Sep 2026. If one turns up and allows 12%, he could approve it himself as VP Sales. If Blue River should instead get standard pricing with no contract basis, it would have to be resubmitted with that justification.

Also, the CRM lists Kelly Floyd, not Michael Torres, as the account owner for BlueRiver Logistics.
