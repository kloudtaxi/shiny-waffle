## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this, and nobody above her should approve it yet either. The contract terms the request relies on don't cover NS-Cloud, so it needs commercial review first (**REVIEW_REQUIRED**).

**Your request doesn't match what's on file.** The knowledge base's copy of the CRM export also has DR-9001: same account, 15%, same date, same requestor and justification. But there it's for the **NS-500** Industrial Controller at $250,000 list ($37,500 discount), not NS-CLOUD at $120,000. I decided the request as you gave it. Please confirm which product is correct. If it's really NS-500, the answer changes (see the end).

**How I got there**

1. **The request relies on contract terms.** Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer is the right legal entity.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings. They share an address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee) and DUNS number (04-812-7730). It is not the separate Akron company Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091).
3. **The product** is NS-CLOUD, the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, at $120,000 list. So 15% is $18,000 off, as the request says.
4. **There is an active contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23, and it lists NS-CLOUD as a supplied product.
5. **No pricing exception covers NS-CLOUD.**
   - The agreement's only strategic discount (§3.2) is "up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller," set out in Schedule B.
   - Schedule B is exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers "NS-500 … only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product."
   - I found no exception for Acme on NS-CLOUD.
   - So the "contract pricing" the request cites doesn't exist for this product.
6. **The policy in force is Northstar Pricing Policy 2026**, together with the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. An Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%. Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales. Over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
7. **Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive** and the account owner, so her limit is 10%. The 15% she could approve under the 2025 policy no longer applies. Being the account owner gives her no extra authority, and neither the contract (§3.3) nor the exception gives any employee approval authority.
8. **The approver for 15% would be the VP Sales, Michael Torres.** But a more senior approver can't fix a request that has no contract basis. It first needs commercial review: either a new or amended exception covering NS-CLOUD, or the request resubmitted on standard pricing.

**If the product is actually NS-500:** the exception covers it up to 15% through 2028-03-31, so Acme is eligible. Sarah still can't approve it herself, but Michael Torres could (**APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**). Sarah's 2026-02-10 account plan says the 15% "worth confirming with Deal Desk", and that it came from what was "approved last September" (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That was under the 2025 policy, when her limit was 15%.
