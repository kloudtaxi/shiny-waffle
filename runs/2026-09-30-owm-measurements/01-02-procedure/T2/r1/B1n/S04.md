## Answer

**Yes, if the request is dated 2025-09-23.** On that date Sarah Chen could have approved the 15% herself. But the record in our knowledge base is dated 2026-09-23, not 2025. On that date she could not approve it alone, so please check which date is correct.

**How this works out for 2025-09-23**
1. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same legal entity as ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings". They share the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, and the D-U-N-S number 04-812-7730. Two other companies have similar names (Acme Industrial Supply Co., CRM-2091 / C-1044, in Akron). Their terms don't apply here.
2. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers the NS-500 controller.
3. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B, exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers the NS-500 only. It allows a discount of up to 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ended 2025-03-31. A 15% request is exactly at that limit, so the customer is eligible.
4. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force for all of 2025. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%.
5. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% was within her own limit. The employee list has no dates, so I'm assuming she held that title in September 2025. The customer being eligible doesn't give her any extra authority; both the exception and section 3.3 of the agreement say so. Here her authority comes from the 2025 policy on its own.

This matches what happened a week earlier. DR-8104 (same account and product, 15%, dated 2025-09-16) was approved, and the approver field begins "s…", which looks like Sarah Chen, though the excerpt is cut off there.

**The date problem**
In Northstar CRM, DR-9001 is dated **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email to Michael Torres about DR-9001 is dated 2026-09-23, and she mentions matching "what we did for them last September." Under the 2026 policy, which fully replaced the 2025 one from 2026-01-01:
- Account Executives can only approve up to 10%.
- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres.

Acme is still eligible for 15% then, because both the agreement and the exception run to 2028. So the actual request should be decided as **approve with authorization**: Michael Torres must approve it, and Sarah cannot approve it herself.
