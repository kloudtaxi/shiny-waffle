2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. That is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings: same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and same Milwaukee address. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), a different company in Akron.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. 5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, with a **maximum discount of 15% of list**. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaces the earlier 10% exception. It gives eligibility only, not approval authority.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to this date.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%.
8. **Who would approve.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% go to the VP Sales, Michael Torres. But no approver can grant 18% when the contract cap is 15%.
