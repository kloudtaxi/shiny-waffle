## Answer

**Yes, most likely, but not on the grounds the request gives.** The Acme agreement it cites wasn't in force yet on 2025-03-31. An earlier Acme pricing exception was still valid that day, and 8% fits within both that exception's limit and Sarah's own approval limit.

**1. The request.** DR-9110 is for account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 8% off (20,000 off a 250,000 list price), dated 2025-03-31 and requested by Sarah Chen. It claims contract pricing under the "Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP system it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044), which is a separate customer whose terms don't apply.

**3. The cited agreement wasn't in effect yet.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) both start on 2025-04-01. That's one day after the request, so neither covered it.

**4. The earlier exception did cover this date.** EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023 and marked "Active," allows Acme Manufacturing up to 10% off NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The request falls on its last day, and 8% is under the 10% cap. The 15% exception only replaces it from 2025-04-01.

**5. The policy that applied.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31) lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts up to and including 15%. The 2026 matrix, with its 10% limit, doesn't apply to a 2025 request.

**6. Sarah's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 8% is within her own limit and no higher approver is needed.

**What to check before relying on this:**
- The justification should be corrected to cite EXC-ACME-NS500-10 / ACME-SA-2023. As written, it points to a contract that hadn't started.
- The ACME-SA-2023 agreement itself isn't in the knowledge base. The only evidence it was active is the "Status: Active" line on the 2023 exception, so it's worth confirming with Deal Desk.
- The employee list has no dates, so I'm assuming Sarah already held the Enterprise Account Executive role in March 2025.
