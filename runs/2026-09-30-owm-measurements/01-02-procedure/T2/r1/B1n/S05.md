**No, it can't be approved yet.** The request is based on the "Acme Master Supply Agreement," and I couldn't find that agreement anywhere in the knowledge base. The procedure says to ask for the document before deciding, so the outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**. Even with the agreement in hand, Sarah couldn't approve 15% herself in 2026.

**How the request checks out**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it depends on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing (Milwaukee), is the ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings. The address and D-U-N-S number (04-812-7730) match. A different company, Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044, Akron), has a similar name. Nothing on file for that company applies to this request.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. That's consistent with the request.
4. **Active contract:** None found for Acme Mfg. Holdings on 2026-09-23. The agreement appears only as a name in the request's justification. No contract record, terms or dates exist for it.
5. **Pricing exception:** None found for NS-500.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It fully replaces the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve discounts up to 10%. At 15% she is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**Why "we did this last September" doesn't carry over**
- DR-8104, the same 15% NS-500 discount for Acme, was approved on 2025-09-16. At that time the 2025 policy let account executives approve up to 15%. That limit is now 10%.
- Sarah's own notes are unsure. Her email says "I think their agreement covers it." Her account plan says "I believe it's 15%… Worth confirming with Deal Desk." Neither confirms a contract term.
- Under the discount procedure, a contract that allows a discount doesn't raise an employee's approval limit. A request also can't be approved just because a contract permits it.

**Next step:** Ask Sarah (or Deal Desk) for the signed agreement with Acme Mfg. Holdings. It needs to show it was active on 2026-09-23 and that it allows ≥15% on NS-500. If it does, the outcome becomes APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving. If the agreement exists but doesn't cover NS-500 or that date, it goes to commercial review. If it caps the discount below 15%, the request would be rejected or escalated for new terms.
