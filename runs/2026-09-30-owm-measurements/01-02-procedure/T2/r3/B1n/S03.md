## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this.** There are two separate problems. The request needs commercial review, and even if review found Acme eligible, a 15% discount is above her approval limit.

**1. Acme has no contract term giving it a discount on NS-Cloud**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same company as the ERP bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). Sarah Chen owns the account.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. NS-Cloud Operations Suite (NS-CLOUD) is one of the products it covers.
- **Discount terms:** The 15% discount in the agreement (§3.2) is for the NS-500 Industrial Controller only. It is set out in pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, which says it "applies only to the product identified above. It does not apply to any other Northstar product." I found no pricing exception for NS-CLOUD.
- So the stated reason, "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", doesn't cover this product. Contract discount terms don't carry over from one product to another, so this needs commercial review.

**2. 15% is above Sarah's limit**
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, that role can approve discounts up to 10%.
- Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres.
- The agreement itself says Acme's contract pricing doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority (§3.3).
- Under the 2025 policy, Account Executives could approve up to 15%. That policy stopped applying on 2026-01-01.

**⚠️ The request record doesn't match the knowledge base.** In the copy of discount_requests.csv I found, DR-9001 is for **NS-500**, with a $250,000 list value and a $37,500 discount. Your record says **NS-CLOUD**, with $120,000 and $18,000. I decided on your version, as you asked. If the product is actually NS-500, the answer changes: Acme is eligible for up to 15% under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, and the outcome would be approval by Michael Torres as VP Sales. Please check which product is correct in Northstar CRM.
