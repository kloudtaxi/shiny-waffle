## Answer

**Yes. For a request dated 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved the 15% discount herself.** The outcome is **APPROVE**: Acme is eligible for the discount, and it is within her own limit under the policy in force in 2025.

**Date mismatch to check first.** The CRM export in the knowledge base (discount_requests.csv) shows DR-9001 dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23. Everything else matches what you gave. The date changes the answer, as explained below.

**How the checks come out for 2025-09-23:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on NS-500, $250,000 list price, requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on special terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Both share the Milwaukee address. This is **not** Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), a separate company.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 with Acme Mfg. Holdings runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the date. It covers NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is part of that agreement. It covers NS-500 only, allows **up to 15%**, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. A 15% request is within the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts **up to and including 15%**.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is within her limit. The knowledge base records her role without dates, so I'm assuming she held it in September 2025.
8. **Approver:** Sarah herself. VP Sales approval is only needed above 15%.

Both the agreement (§3.3) and the exception say that Acme's eligibility gives no employee approval authority. The approval rests on the 2025 policy, not on the contract. For comparison, a matching request, DR-8104 (same account, product, 15%, from Sarah Chen, dated 2025-09-16), shows as **Approved**.

**If the real date is 2026-09-23 (as the CRM export shows),** Sarah could **not** approve it herself. The 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix cap Enterprise Account Executives at 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need **VP Sales** approval, and the VP Sales is Michael Torres. Acme is still eligible under the same exception, so the outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving.
