## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved an 8% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9110", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "8.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "20000", "net_value_usd": "230000", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

**Short answer: probably yes, but not yet.** Sarah Chen has the authority to approve 8% on that date. What's missing is proof of the contract the request relies on, so the right step is to ask for that document before anyone approves.

**Why authority isn't the problem**
- The request is dated 2025-03-31, so the **Northstar Pricing Policy 2025** applies. It runs from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 and lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts up to and including **15%**.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and the account owner for CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing). 8% is within her 15% limit.
- The tighter 2026 rule (10% for account executives) doesn't apply, because it only covers requests from 2026-01-01 onward.

**Why the contract is the problem**
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same D-U-N-S number 04-812-7730 and Milwaukee address). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044, Akron), which is a different customer.
- **The agreement the request cites:** the justification says "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) only take effect on **2025-04-01**, the day after this request. Neither was in force on 2025-03-31.
- **The older exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-10 lets Acme Manufacturing have up to **10%** off NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 to **2025-03-31**. That covers this date, and 8% fits under it. But it sits under agreement **ACME-SA-2023**, which isn't in the knowledge base. Nothing available shows that agreement was active with this customer on 2025-03-31.
- The rules say that when a request relies on a contract term that can't be located, you ask for the document before deciding. A pricing exception also only shows the customer qualifies for the price; it doesn't give anyone approval authority.

**What would make it approvable:** a copy of ACME-SA-2023 showing it was active with Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31. With that, the 8% falls under the 10% exception and within Sarah Chen's 15% limit, so she could approve it herself. The justification should also be corrected to cite ACME-SA-2023 and EXC-ACME-NS500-10 instead of the Master Supply Agreement.
