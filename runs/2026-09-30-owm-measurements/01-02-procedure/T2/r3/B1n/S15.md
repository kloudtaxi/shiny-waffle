**The customer.** CRM account CRM-2051, BlueRiver Logistics, is the same legal entity as ERP customer C-1002, Blue River Logistics LLC. Both records have DUNS 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis, MO.

**The cited terms belong to someone else.** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing (Milwaukee, DUNS 04-812-7730). It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its NS-500 pricing exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15%), also names Acme Manufacturing only. The procedure says a customer's special terms never apply to a different customer. I found no contract or pricing exception for BlueRiver or Blue River Logistics LLC in the knowledge base.

**Torres's authority isn't the obstacle.** He is VP Sales. Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, VP Sales approves discounts over 10% and up to 20%, so a 12% discount is within his band. But the procedure says authority does not establish eligibility. Since the request rests on contract terms, what's missing is a BlueRiver contract, not a more senior approver.

**Next step:** ask Michael Torres for a contract or pricing exception that covers Blue River Logistics LLC and NS-500 on 2026-09-23. If no such document exists, he should resubmit the request on standard pricing. At 12%, a standard-pricing request would still need VP Sales approval, which falls within his own band.
