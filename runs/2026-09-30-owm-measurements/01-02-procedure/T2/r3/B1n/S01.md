## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme qualifies for the 15% discount, but it is above her approval limit. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve DR-9001.

1. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, is billed in the ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). That is the company that signed Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025, trading as Acme Manufacturing. The similarly named Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091) is a different customer, and its terms don't apply here.
2. **The contract.** The agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500.
3. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers only the NS-500, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception. The request asks for exactly 15%, so Acme is eligible.
4. **Her authority.** Both the exception and section 3.3 of the agreement say the customer's eligibility doesn't give any employee the right to approve. Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, an Enterprise Account Executive like Sarah can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales.
5. **Why last year's approval doesn't carry over.** Sarah's email says 15% is "what we did for them last September" (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That approval fell under the 2025 policy, which let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy fully replaced it for requests dated from 2026-01-01.

The Approval Authority Matrix also requires any approval above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM.
