1. **Request:** DR-9108 asks for 8% off the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (NS-EDGE) for Acme Industrial Supply Co. The list value is $85,000, so the discount is $6,800 and the net value is $78,200. It is dated 2026-09-23 and was submitted by Sarah Chen. The justification is "Competitive", so it uses standard pricing and doesn't rely on any contract or pricing exception.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2091 is ERP customer C-1044 in Akron, OH. Its DUNS number is 09-552-1187. This is not Acme Manufacturing, whose legal entity is Acme Mfg. Holdings (CRM-2048 / C-1001, Milwaukee). Acme Manufacturing's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its NS-500 pricing exceptions don't apply here. Since the request doesn't cite them, that doesn't affect the decision.
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, a software product with a list price of $85,000. That matches the list value on the request.
4. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), along with the Approval Authority Matrix 2026. Under both, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve discounts up to and including 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, and above 20% need the Chief Revenue Officer.
5. **Requestor's authority:** The employee directory lists Sarah Chen (EMP-101) as an Enterprise Account Executive, so 8% is within her 10% limit. The CRM lists Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen, as the account owner for CRM-2091. The matrix says authority belongs to the role, not to the person or the account, so this doesn't affect her authority. You may still want to check why she is handling his account.
6. **Recording:** Only approvals above 10% need recorded approval evidence. The SOP still asks that every decision and its evidence be recorded in Northstar CRM.
