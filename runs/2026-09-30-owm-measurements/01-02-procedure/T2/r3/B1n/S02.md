**No. Acme's contract terms don't cover 18%. The most they're eligible for on the NS-500 is 15%, so the outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Having a more senior person approve it wouldn't fix this. Going above 15% would need new or amended pricing terms with Acme.

**Check the requested rate first.** Your record says 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). The CRM export in the knowledge base shows DR-9001 at **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also says Acme is "asking for 15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." Please confirm which number is right. As requested, 18% can't be approved.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, CRM account CRM-2048, NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. It cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement as the basis.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings." The DUNS number (04-812-7730) and the Milwaukee address match. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), a separate company.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings (trading as Acme Manufacturing). It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers the NS-500 only, allows **up to 15%** off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception. I found no amendment that allows more than 15%.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10% herself.
8. **Approver band:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval (Michael Torres). But approval rules can't raise what the contract allows. Both the agreement (§3.3) and the exception say contract eligibility and approval authority are separate.

**Options:**
- Offer **15%**. That's within Acme's terms, but it's above Sarah's 10% limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would need to approve it (APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION) and the approval must be logged in CRM.
- If Acme really needs 18%, the pricing terms would have to be renegotiated or amended first. The current exception was approved by David Morgan (CRO).
