## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

**No.** Sarah Chen had the authority to approve 15% on 2025-03-31, but Acme wasn't entitled to 15% that day. Acme's pricing on NS-500 was capped at 10% until 2025-03-31, and the 15% terms began the next day.

**Customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Mfg. Holdings, ERP customer C-1001, which trades as Acme Manufacturing. Two other accounts, Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) and Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), are separate customers, and their terms don't apply here.

**Eligibility on 2025-03-31:**
- The request cites the "Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025). It was signed in March 2025 but only runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it wasn't in force on the request date.
- The 15% exception attached to that agreement (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also only starts on 2025-04-01. It states that it replaces the older exception "for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."
- On 2025-03-31 the older exception still applied: EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023. It allows up to **10%** off NS-500 for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.
- 15% is above that 10% limit. The agreement ACME-SA-2023 isn't in the knowledge base itself; I'm relying on the exception's reference to it and its "Active" status.

**Authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force. It lets an Enterprise Account Executive approve discounts up to and including 15%, and the knowledge base lists Sarah Chen in that role (with no dates attached). So authority isn't the issue. Eligibility is, and a senior approver's sign-off can't fix that. The Master Supply Agreement (§3.3) and the exception both say contract eligibility doesn't grant anyone approval authority.

**Result.** For a 2025-03-31 date, the request should be rejected or sent back for amended terms. It could be resubmitted at 10% or less, or dated 2025-04-01 or later. Dated 2025-04-01, all three conditions would have been met: the new agreement, the 15% exception, and Sarah's 15% limit under the 2025 policy. She could then have approved it herself.

**Also worth knowing:**
- DR-8104, an identical 15% request from Sarah dated 2025-09-16, was approved, which is consistent with this.
- The ID DR-9101 doesn't match what the knowledge base holds. The Acme request there is **DR-9001**, dated **2026-09-23**. That date falls under the 2026 policy, where Account Executives can only approve up to 10%. So that request would need VP Sales approval.
