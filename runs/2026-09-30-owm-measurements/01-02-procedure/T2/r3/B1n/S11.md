**How I got there**

1. **The request.** DR-9103 is for account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing), product NS-EDGE (NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000). It asks for 22% ($18,700 off, leaving $66,300 net). The justification is "Competitive", so it uses standard pricing. It doesn't rely on a contract term or pricing exception, which means no eligibility check is needed. The only Acme exception I found (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) covers the NS-500 anyway, not NS-EDGE.
2. **The policy in force.** The request date falls under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (effective 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 15%, and anything above 15% needs VP Sales approval. The stricter 2026 policy, which limits AEs to 10% and sends anything over 20% to the CRO, only applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 or later, so it doesn't apply here.
3. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101) and the account owner for Acme Manufacturing. Her limit was 15%, and 22% is over it.
4. **Who had to approve.** VP Sales, held by Michael Torres (EMP-200). He appears as VP Sales in the employee list and the org chart. The org chart is dated 2026-09-01, but CRM records back him in that role in 2025: he approved a 22% NS-EDGE discount (DR-7077, 2025-02-13) and a 22% discount a week before this request (DR-7431, 2025-09-16).
