## Answer

No, DR-9105 can't be approved as submitted. It relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement, and that agreement belongs to a different customer. The right outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**: get a contract with Acme Industrial Supply Co. before anyone decides.

**Why**

1. **The customer.** CRM-2091 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** in Akron, OH. Its ERP customer ID is C-1044 and its DUNS number is 09-552-1187. It's a Standard, Mid-Market industrial distribution account owned by Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen.
2. **The cited contract is someone else's.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) is with **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as Acme Manufacturing, in Milwaukee, WI. That's ERP customer C-1001 (DUNS 04-812-7730) and CRM account CRM-2048, which Sarah owns. Its 15% NS-500 exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also names Acme Manufacturing only. The two companies have different addresses, DUNS numbers and ERP IDs, so they're separate legal entities. Another customer's terms never apply, however similar the names.
3. **Nothing found for Acme Industrial Supply.** I found no contract or pricing exception for Acme Industrial Supply Co. The only other record for it is service ticket SR-40418, about its NS-Edge monitoring product, which states that no change to commercial terms was requested.
4. **Authority is short either way.** Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, an Enterprise Account Executive like Sarah can approve up to 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres. So even with valid terms, Sarah couldn't approve 15% herself. (The 2025 policy let her approve up to 15%, but it no longer applies.)

**Worth checking:** the CRM log also has **DR-9001**, a matching request on the same date. It's the same 15% NS-500 discount on $250,000, from Sarah, with the same justification, but it's filed under CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) and is pending. DR-9105 may be a duplicate filed under the wrong account. Confirm with Sarah which customer the deal is actually for. If it's Acme Manufacturing, DR-9001 is the request to assess, and the 15% would still need Michael Torres's approval.
