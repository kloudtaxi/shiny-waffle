## Answer

No, Sarah can't approve this herself. The request asks for "contract pricing" under the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement gives Acme no special discount on NS-Edge, so the request needs a commercial review first. The 12% is also over her own approval limit.

**How I checked it**

1. **Request:** DR-9109 asks for 12% off NS-EDGE (list price $85,000, discount $10,200). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23, citing "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". So it relies on special contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings (ERP customer C-1001). The agreement and Sarah's 2026 account plan both confirm this. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which also uses NS-EDGE.
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000.
4. **Contract:** Yes, it was active. Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 with Acme Mfg. Holdings runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and NS-EDGE is one of the products it covers. But it only sets list-price terms for NS-EDGE. The only preferential discount (§3.2) is up to 15% on the NS-500 controller.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers NS-EDGE. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." I found no Acme exception for NS-EDGE.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix for 2026. The 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%, was fully replaced on 2026-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. 12% is over it.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres.

**Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** Acme has an active contract, but no Acme term supports a discount on NS-EDGE. Terms written for the NS-500 don't carry over to other products, so the "contract pricing" reason isn't valid. Deal Desk needs to review it commercially, or the request should be resubmitted on standard (non-contract) terms. Either way, 12% needs Michael Torres's approval as VP Sales. The agreement's own §3.3 says contract eligibility never changes internal approval authority.
