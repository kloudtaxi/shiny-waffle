## Answer

**No, Michael Torres can't approve this.** Approving it is within his authority, but the customer isn't eligible for 18% on this product. Acme's contract caps the NS-500 discount at **15%**, so the correct outcome is reject or escalate.

**1. The request.** DR-9112 asks for 18% off the NS-500 for CRM-2048, dated 2026-09-23 and raised by Michael Torres. It cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on special contract terms, not standard pricing.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. Its ERP bill-to account is **Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001)** (account strategy ACCT-PLAN-CRM-2048-2026). "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (C-1044) is a different company and has no bearing here.

**3. The product.** The SKU is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 says the customer can get discounts of **up to 15%** on the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.

**5. The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that contract) covers the NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a **maximum eligible discount of 15%** of list price. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). I found no amendment that raises the cap.

**6–8. Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. Michael Torres is VP Sales, and that role can approve discounts above 10% and up to 20%. On authority alone, 18% would be his call.

**9. Decision.** 18% is 3 points over Acme's contractual maximum. A more senior approver can't fix that: even the CRO approving it wouldn't create eligibility that the contract doesn't give. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only." Going above 15% needs new or amended commercial terms first. The alternative is to reduce the request to 15% or less. Michael could approve that under his own authority, because it would then fit both the exception and his band.
