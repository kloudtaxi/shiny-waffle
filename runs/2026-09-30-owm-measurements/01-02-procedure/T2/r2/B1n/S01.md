## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. The discount is allowed under Acme's contract, but at 15% it's above her 10% limit. Michael Torres, the VP Sales, has to approve DR-9001.

**Is Acme eligible for 15%? Yes.**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, billed in ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), according to the 2026 Acme account strategy.
- **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. I found no amendment or termination.
- **Pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is attached to that contract. It covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% request is within that limit. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31.
- Other accounts with similar names, "Acme Industrial Supply Co." and "Acme Industrial," are different customers. Their terms don't apply here.

**Can Sarah approve it? No.**
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%.
- Both the contract (§3.3) and the exception say they set what the customer is eligible for but don't give any employee authority to approve it.
- Sarah's 15% approval last September (DR-8104) is not a precedent. That was under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy fully replaced it.

**Next step:** Michael Torres needs to approve DR-9001 and record the approval in Northstar CRM, as the policy requires for anything above 10%. Sarah already emailed him about the request on 2026-09-23.
