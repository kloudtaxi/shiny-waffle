## Answer

**Not yet.** DR-9001 can't be approved on the evidence we have. The request depends on the "Acme Master Supply Agreement", and I couldn't find that agreement, any other contract or any NS-500 pricing exception for this customer. The next step is to get the signed agreement and its NS-500 pricing term before deciding.

**How I checked**
1. **Request:** 15% off the NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount), requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. The CRM record says it relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing" in Milwaukee. It maps to ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same address and DUNS 04-812-7730). This is a different legal entity from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044, Akron). Terms held by that company can never apply here, however similar the name.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** None found. The knowledge base has an entry for "Acme Master Supply Agreement" but nothing linked to it: no parties, dates or terms. No contract document or record turned up in my searches. The only mentions of a discount are unconfirmed:
   - Sarah's 2026 account plan says "I believe it's 15%… Worth confirming with Deal Desk before the Q3 order."
   - Her email to Michael Torres says "I think their agreement covers it."
5. **Pricing exception:** None found for NS-500 on this customer.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It fully replaced the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10% under the 2026 policy, so 15% is beyond her own limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**Why "it's what we did last September" doesn't carry over**
- That approval (DR-8104, 2025-09-16) was made under the 2025 policy, when account executives could approve up to 15% themselves. The 2026 policy cut that to 10%.
- A past approval doesn't prove a contract exists or is still active.
- Even if the agreement is confirmed, a contract doesn't change who is allowed to approve. The request would still need Michael Torres's sign-off, not Sarah's.

**What would change the answer:** If the signed agreement shows an active contract with Acme Mfg. Holdings on 2026-09-23 that allows at least 15% on NS-500, the outcome becomes "approve with authorization", signed off by Michael Torres. If it caps the NS-500 discount below 15%, the request should be rejected or escalated for new terms. If it's active but has no NS-500 term, it goes to commercial review.
