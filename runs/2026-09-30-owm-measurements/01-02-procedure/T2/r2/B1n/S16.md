## Answer

**Yes, Sarah Chen can approve this herself.** DR-9108 is a standard competitive discount of 8%. Under the 2026 policy, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10% without anyone else signing off.

**How I got there:**
1. **The request:** DR-9108 asks for 8% off NS-EDGE, which is $6,800 off an $85,000 list price, leaving $78,200. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the justification "Competitive." It doesn't cite a contract or a pricing exception, so it's a standard-pricing request.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH. Its ERP record is C-1044, and the DUNS number matches (09-552-1187). This is a different company from **Acme Manufacturing** (CRM-2048 / ERP C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings"). Acme Manufacturing's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its NS-500 exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) don't apply to this customer. The request doesn't rely on them either, so that doesn't matter here.
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. That matches the request.
4. **Contract and exception:** I found no contract or pricing exception for Acme Industrial Supply Co. The request doesn't need one.
5. **The policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy. I found no amendment to it.
6. **Sarah's authority:** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive. Both the 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix (APP-MATRIX-2026) let that role approve discounts up to and including 10%. 8% is within that limit.
7. **Approver:** No one else needs to approve.

Sarah isn't the CRM account owner for CRM-2091; Zachary Brown is. That doesn't affect the answer, because the matrix says "authority attaches to the role, not to the individual or the account." The matrix only requires approvals above 10% to be recorded in CRM, but the SOP says every decision and its evidence should be recorded there.
