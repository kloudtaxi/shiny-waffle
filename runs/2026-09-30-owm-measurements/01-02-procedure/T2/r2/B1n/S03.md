## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** Acme has an active contract, but no pricing exception covers NS-CLOUD, so the 15% it relies on doesn't exist for this product.

**One discrepancy to fix first:** the knowledge base's copy of the CRM discount log lists DR-9001 differently from the record you sent. It shows **NS-500**, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net. Your record shows NS-CLOUD at $120,000. I assessed the request as you gave it, but the product on DR-9001 should be checked against the live CRM, because the product changes the answer (see the end).

**How I got there**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, 15% on NS-CLOUD, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special contract terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. Its ERP bill-to is **Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001)**, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. That's the same company that signed the contract.
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 with Acme Mfg. Holdings runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-CLOUD is a product it covers.
5. **Pricing exception:** The agreement's 15% discount (§3.2) applies **only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller**, through exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. That exception says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." I found no exception for NS-CLOUD. Section 3.1 says other products are sold at list price less whatever discount is agreed in an order, so the contract gives Acme no set discount on NS-CLOUD.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to this date. It replaced the 2025 policy, which had let account executives approve up to 15%.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, which carries a limit of **10% or less**. 15% is above that. Both the contract (§3.3) and the exception say they don't give any employee approval authority.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, **Michael Torres**.

**What happens next:** Since no exception covers the product, the request needs commercial review. If it's then handled as an ordinary discount, the 15% would still need Michael Torres's approval, not Sarah's. She can't approve it herself either way.

If DR-9001 is actually for NS-500, as the knowledge base shows, Acme would qualify for 15% under the exception. Even then it would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, needing Michael Torres to sign off, not approval by Sarah alone.
