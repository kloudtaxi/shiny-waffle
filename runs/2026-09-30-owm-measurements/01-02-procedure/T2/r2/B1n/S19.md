## Answer

Yes. Sarah Chen can approve DR-9111 herself. The outcome is **APPROVE**.

1. **The request is at standard pricing.** The justification is "Competitive". It doesn't cite any contract term or pricing exception, so no customer contract needs checking.
2. **The customer and product match the records.** Account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account in Milwaukee, WI, and Sarah Chen owns it. NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, listed at $120,000. The request's figures agree: a $7,200 discount leaves a net value of $112,800.
3. **The 2026 pricing policy applies.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%. The Approval Authority Matrix 2026 says the same: ≤10% for an Enterprise Account Executive, above 10% up to 20% for the VP Sales, and above 20% for the Chief Revenue Officer.
4. **She holds that role.** The employee records and org chart list Sarah Chen (EMP-101) as an Enterprise Account Executive reporting to Michael Torres, VP Sales. Her 6% request is within her 10% limit. The matrix only requires CRM recording for approvals above 10%, but the SOP still asks for the decision and evidence to be recorded in Northstar CRM.

Don't confuse this with DR-9001. That's a separate request Sarah made the same day: 15% on the NS-500, based on the "Acme Master Supply Agreement". It needs its own review, and its outcome doesn't carry over to this one.
