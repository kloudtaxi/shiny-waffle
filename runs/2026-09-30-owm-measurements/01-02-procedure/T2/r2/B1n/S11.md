## Answer

No. Sarah Chen couldn't have approved DR-9103 on her own. At 22% the discount is over her limit, so under the policy in force on 2025-09-23 it needed approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

1. **The request:** DR-9103 is for a 22% discount on NS-EDGE (NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000). It comes from account CRM-2048, which is Acme Manufacturing, owned by Sarah Chen. The justification is "Competitive", so the request relies on standard pricing and not on a contract term or pricing exception. That means no contract or exception check is needed. The only Acme exception I found is for the NS-500 controller, and it ended 2025-03-31, so it wouldn't cover NS-EDGE anyway.
2. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, effective 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts up to and including 15%. Anything above 15% needs VP Sales approval. The 2026 policy has a lower 10% limit and sends anything over 20% to the CRO, but it only applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 or later.
3. **Sarah Chen's authority:** The employee records and org chart list her as an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit was 15%. 22% is above that.
4. **Required approver:** VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (michael.torres@northstar.example) in the employee records. Other 22% NS-EDGE requests from 2025 fit this: DR-7077, for example, was approved by Michael Torres.

One gap: the employee records and org chart have no dates, so I'm assuming Sarah Chen and Michael Torres held these roles in September 2025. Nothing I found suggests otherwise.
