## Answer

**Yes. If the request was dated 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved it herself.** But the date doesn't match our records: Northstar CRM has DR-9001 dated **2026-09-23**, and on that date she could not approve it.

**Checks for 2025-09-23**
1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both records have the same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee). Account CRM-2091, "Acme Industrial Supply Co.", is a different company and doesn't come into it.
2. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2025-09-23. The request's justification cites this agreement.
3. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B of that agreement) allows up to 15% off list on the NS-500 only. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaced the older 10% exception. The request is for exactly 15%, so it's within the limit.
4. **Policy:** Pricing Policy 2025 applies to requests dated in 2025. It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts up to and including 15%; above 15% needs the VP Sales.
5. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is within her own limit. The contract (§3.3) and the exception both say they give no approval authority. Here that doesn't matter, because her authority comes from the policy.

For comparison, DR-8104 had identical terms (CRM-2048, NS-500, 15%, $250,000) and was dated 2025-09-16. It shows as Approved, which fits with this. That's probably the "what we did for them last September" Sarah mentions in her email.

**The date problem:** The CRM record and Sarah's email to Michael Torres (sent 2026-09-23) both date DR-9001 **2026-09-23**. On that date:
- Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. They cap Enterprise Account Executives at 10%.
- Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs the **VP Sales, Michael Torres**.
- Acme is still eligible for 15% under the same contract and exception, so the outcome would be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`: Michael Torres has to approve it, not Sarah.
