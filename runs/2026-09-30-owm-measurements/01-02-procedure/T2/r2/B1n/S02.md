**The numbers don't match the CRM record.** In Northstar CRM, DR-9001 is recorded at **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also says Acme asked for 15%. The 18% figures you gave ($45,000 off, $205,000 net) don't match either source. I've assessed it at 18% as you asked, but someone should confirm which figure is correct.

1. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. In ERP it bills to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. That is the company that signed the supply agreement, so Acme's special terms do apply to this account.
2. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
3. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.3 says Acme's contract pricing does not change who at Northstar can approve a discount.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a **maximum of 15%** off list. It replaced the older 10% exception. **18% is over that maximum.**
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. Account executives can approve up to 10%. The VP Sales approves above 10% and up to 20%. The Chief Revenue Officer approves above 20%.
6. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so anything above 10% needs approval. Michael Torres, VP Sales, could sign off on 18% under the policy, but that doesn't help: Acme is only entitled to 15%.
