The request says the discount comes from the Acme contract, but no contract term or pricing exception gives Acme a discount on NS-Edge. It needs commercial review first (outcome: REVIEW_REQUIRED). Even if review allows it, 12% is above Sarah's limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would have to approve it.

**Checks against the Sales Discount procedure:**

1. **Request:** DR-9109 asks for 12% off NS-EDGE ($85,000 list, $10,200 discount), dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. The stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, billed in the ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), according to Sarah's 2026 account plan. This is a different company from Acme Industrial (CRM-2091) and Acme Industrial Supply Co.
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and lists NS-EDGE among the products Northstar supplies.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers NS-EDGE.
   - The agreement's discount clause (§3.2) gives up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller only.
   - The exception attached to it (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) says it "applies only to [NS-500]… does not apply to any other Northstar product."
   - The agreement also says Acme's contract pricing "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority" (§3.3).
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix, both effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 12% is over that limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres.

**Next step:** Send DR-9109 to Deal Desk for commercial review. Either Acme gets an NS-EDGE pricing exception, or Sarah resubmits without the contract basis. If it goes ahead at 12%, Michael Torres must approve it and the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
