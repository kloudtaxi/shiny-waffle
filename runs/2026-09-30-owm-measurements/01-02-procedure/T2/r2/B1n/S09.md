## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen could not have approved DR-9101 at 15% on 2025-03-31.** Her authority wasn't the problem. Acme wasn't entitled to 15% on that date: its terms allowed at most 10%.

**Why**

1. **Customer.** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the ERP customer Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). Sarah owns the account.
2. **Product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
3. **The agreement the request cites isn't in force yet.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its Schedule B exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, max 15%) both start on **2025-04-01**. The request is dated 2025-03-31, one day earlier. The 15% exception only replaces the old one for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01.
4. **The terms in force that day allow 10%.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023, gives Acme Manufacturing up to **10%** off NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That window includes the request date.
5. **Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 lets Enterprise Account Executives approve up to and including 15%. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so a 15% discount would have been within her personal limit.
6. **Decision.** 15% is more than Acme was eligible for, so this can't be approved at any level; a VP or the CRO signing off wouldn't fix it. The options were:
   - discount 10% or less, which Sarah could approve herself;
   - date the order on or after 2025-04-01, when the 15% terms apply;
   - get the old terms formally amended.

One gap: the ACME-SA-2023 agreement itself isn't in the knowledge base. The only evidence for it is the 2023 exception, which names it and is marked "Status: Active".
