## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9102 herself. A 25% discount is above her limit, so David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, has to approve it.

1. **Request:** DR-9102 asks for 25% off the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (SKU NS-EDGE, list price $85,000) for account CRM-2048. That is a $21,250 discount, leaving $63,750. The reason given is "Competitive." It doesn't cite any contract term, so this is a standard-pricing request.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen is its account owner.
3. **Customer contract:** Acme's special pricing (exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, part of agreement ACME-MFG-2025) covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller, up to 15%. It says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product," so it doesn't affect this request. Other Acme-named accounts (Acme Industrial, CRM-2091) are separate customers, and their terms don't apply either.
4. **Policy on 2026-09-23:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply. Both run from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replace the 2025 policy. The limits are:
   - Enterprise Account Executive: up to 10%
   - VP Sales: over 10% and up to 20%
   - Chief Revenue Officer: over 20%
5. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (employee EMP-101), so her limit is 10%. The request is 15 points over it. The matrix says authority belongs to the role, not the person or the account.
6. **Required approver:** David Morgan holds the Chief Revenue Officer role, per the employee list and the org chart dated 2026-09-01. Because the discount is over 10%, the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

The discount can go ahead with the CRO's approval. Other 20%+ competitive discounts on NS-EDGE have gone through this way, for example DR-7828, which David Morgan approved on 2026-06-01.
