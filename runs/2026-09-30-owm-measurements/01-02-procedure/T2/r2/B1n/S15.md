## Answer

Not yet. Michael Torres has the authority for a 12% discount, but the reason given for the discount doesn't apply to this customer. The procedure says to **ask for evidence before deciding** (REQUEST_EVIDENCE).

**Why the stated reason fails**
- DR-9107 is for CRM account CRM-2051, BlueRiver Logistics. In the ERP system that is customer C-1002, Blue River Logistics LLC. The address and DUNS number (07-331-9025) match.
- The request cites the "Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025). That contract is between Northstar and **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. Its NS-500 pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, up to 15%, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) also names Acme Manufacturing as the customer.
- Special terms apply only to the customer named in them. Acme's contract can't be used for BlueRiver, however well it would otherwise fit.
- I found no contract or pricing exception for BlueRiver or Blue River Logistics LLC in the knowledge base. So nothing shows that BlueRiver qualifies for a contract discount on NS-500.

**Michael Torres's authority**
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (and its approval matrix) was in force on 2026-09-23. Under it, Account Executives can approve up to 10%, VP Sales covers more than 10% up to 20%, and the Chief Revenue Officer covers anything above 20%.
- Michael Torres is VP Sales, so 12% is within his approval limit.
- But having the authority doesn't make the discount valid. The procedure also says a request must not be approved just because a customer contract seems to allow it. Here the contract isn't even this customer's.

**Next step:** Ask Michael Torres to provide a signed contract or pricing exception with Blue River Logistics LLC that covers NS-500 on 2026-09-23. If none exists, the request should be resubmitted without claiming contract pricing and be judged on standard terms. At 12%, it would still need VP Sales approval.
