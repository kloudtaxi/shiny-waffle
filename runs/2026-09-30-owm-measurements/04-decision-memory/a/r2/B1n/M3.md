## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this 15% discount herself. Acme is entitled to it under its contract, but under the pricing policy in force on 2027-02-01 a discount above 12% needs Chief Revenue Officer approval.

**1. The request.** DR-9113 in the CRM: 15% off an NS-500 for account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing), requested by Sarah Chen on 2027-02-01. The $250,000 list price less a $37,500 discount gives a net of $212,500. The stated reason is the Acme Master Supply Agreement, so this relies on special contract terms, not standard pricing.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. Its billing account in the finance system is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. That is the same company that signed the agreement. The similarly named "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091) is a different customer, and its terms don't matter here.

**3. The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on the request date. The NS-500 is one of the products it covers. I found no record that it has been ended or changed.

**4. The pricing exception.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is attached to the agreement. It covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list price, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% requested is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. This exception replaced the earlier 10% one (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31.

**5. The policy in force.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies to requests dated from 2027-01-01 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. Its limits are:
- Enterprise Account Executive: up to 5%
- VP Sales: over 5%, up to 12%
- Chief Revenue Officer: over 12%

**6. Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%, and 15% is above it. Under the old 2026 rules, the VP Sales could have approved this, as with the similar request DR-8104 in 2025. That no longer applies. Both the agreement (clause 3.3) and the exception say explicitly that they give no Northstar employee authority to approve discounts. Being eligible under the contract doesn't widen her limit.

**7. Who must approve.** The Chief Revenue Officer. The org chart dated 2026-09-01 lists David Morgan in that role. I found nothing confirming who holds it in February 2027, so check that before sending it for sign-off. The 2027 policy also says any discount above 5% needs a recorded approval.
