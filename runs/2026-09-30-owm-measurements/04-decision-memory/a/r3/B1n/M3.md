## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but on 2027-02-01 her own approval limit is 5%. Because 15% is above 12%, the Chief Revenue Officer has to approve it.

**Request:** DR-9113 asks for 15% ($37,500 off a $250,000 list price) for account CRM-2048 on product NS-500. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2027-02-01. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.

**Acme is eligible for 15%**
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 is billed in the finance system (ERP) to Acme Mfg. Holdings (customer C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. The contract and the pricing exception both name that same company.
- **Contract:** Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is active on the request date.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B of that contract. It covers the NS-500 Industrial Controller only, allows up to 15% off list price, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. The requested 15% is exactly at the maximum, so eligibility is established.

**Sarah Chen can't approve it herself**
- **Policy in force:** On 2027-02-01 the Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies. It fully replaces the 2026 policy for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01. Under it, account executives can approve up to 5%, the VP Sales from over 5% up to 12%, and the Chief Revenue Officer anything above 12%.
- **Her role:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%.
- **Contract terms don't raise her limit:** The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." Section 3.3 of the contract says the same.
- **Past approvals no longer apply:** Earlier approvals of this same 15% request (for example DR-8104 in 2025) fell under the 2026-era limits: 10% for account executives, and up to 20% for the VP Sales. Those limits have been replaced. Under 2027 rules, the VP Sales can't approve this either.

**Who must approve:** The Chief Revenue Officer. The org chart dated 2026-09-01 lists David Morgan in that role; it's worth checking he still holds it in February 2027. The 2027 policy also requires a recorded approval for any discount above 5%.
