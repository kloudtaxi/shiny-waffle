No. Sarah Chen can't approve this 15% discount herself. Acme is entitled to it under its contract, but under the 2027 pricing policy a discount above 12% needs Chief Revenue Officer approval. The latest org chart lists David Morgan as CRO.

**The request.** DR-9113 is for CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing): 15% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, requested by Sarah Chen on 2027-02-01. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.

**Customer and contract.** CRM-2048 is billed in the ERP to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) is with that same company and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on 2027-02-01. I found no termination or amendment.

**Pricing exception.** Schedule B to the agreement (exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15) allows up to 15% off list on the NS-500 only, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced an older 10% exception that ended 2025-03-31. The request is exactly 15%, so Acme is eligible.

**Policy in force.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies to requests dated from 2027-01-01. It fully replaces the 2026 policy, under which VP Sales could approve up to 20%. Its approval limits are:
- Enterprise Account Executive: up to 5%
- VP Sales: more than 5%, up to 12%
- Chief Revenue Officer: more than 12%

**Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%. Both the contract (section 3.3) and the exception say they don't change internal approval authority. The contract makes Acme eligible, but it doesn't let Sarah approve.

**Who must approve.** The CRO. The org chart (dated 2026-09-01) lists David Morgan in that role. The knowledge base has no org chart for 2027, so check he still holds the role on the request date.

**Other points:**
- No 2027 approval matrix is on file; the 2026 one expired 2026-12-31. I applied the bands in the 2027 policy itself.
- The 2027 policy requires recorded approval evidence for any discount above 5%.
- The same 15% was requested on 2026-09-23 as DR-9001, which is still pending. Under the 2026 policy that one needed VP Sales approval. It doesn't set a precedent for 2027.
