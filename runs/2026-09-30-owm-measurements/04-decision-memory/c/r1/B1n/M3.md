## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but on 2027-02-01 her limit is 5%. Under the 2027 pricing policy, a 15% discount needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, currently David Morgan.

**Outcome: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** The discount is allowed, but only once the CRO approves it.

1. **The request:** DR-9113 is for 15% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net). It is dated 2027-02-01 and was requested by Sarah Chen. Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on a contract term.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings." They share an address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee) and a DUNS number (04-812-7730). It is not the unrelated Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044).
3. **Active contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers the NS-500, so it is active on the request date. I found no record that it was terminated.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) allows up to 15% off list for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception. The requested 15% is within this limit, so Acme is eligible.
5. **Policy in effect:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies from 2027-01-01 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. Its approval limits are:
   - Enterprise Account Executive: up to 5%
   - VP Sales: over 5% and up to 12%
   - Chief Revenue Officer: over 12%
6. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%, and 15% is above it. Her approval power is set by the policy, not by the contract:
   - The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
   - Section 3.3 of the agreement says Acme's contract pricing does not change Northstar's internal approval rules.
7. **Required approver:** 15% is over 12%, so the Chief Revenue Officer must approve. That is David Morgan, per the organization chart dated 2026-09-01. I found nothing newer showing a change in that role.

Two things differ from past Acme deals. Under the 2025 and 2026 policies, a 15% discount went to a lower level (the Account Executive in 2025, VP Sales in 2026), and earlier Acme requests at 15% (DR-8104, DR-9001) were handled that way. Under the 2027 policy, even VP Sales Michael Torres can only approve up to 12%, so this one has to go to the CRO. Approvals above 5% must also be recorded as evidence in Northstar CRM.
