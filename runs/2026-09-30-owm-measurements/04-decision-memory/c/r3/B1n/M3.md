## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9113 herself. Acme Manufacturing is entitled to the 15% discount, but under the 2027 pricing policy only the Chief Revenue Officer can approve it.

**Is Acme entitled to 15%? Yes.**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both records have the same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and the same Milwaukee address. That is the legal entity on the contract, trading as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044, Akron).
- **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is in force on 2027-02-01. It lists the NS-500. I found no record of it being terminated.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to 15% off list on the NS-500 Industrial Controller only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception. The request is for exactly 15%, so it is within the limit.

**Can Sarah approve it herself? No.**
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. Under it:
  - Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 5%.
  - Above 5% and up to 12% needs VP Sales.
  - Above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%. At 15%, this request needs the CRO.
- Both the exception and the agreement (section 3.3) say they create eligibility only and don't give anyone approval authority.
- This is stricter than before. Under the 2026 policy the same 15% needed VP Sales, and under the 2025 policy Sarah could approve it herself (her limit was 15%). The earlier 15% approvals for Acme (DR-8104 in Sept 2025, and DR-9001, logged in Sept 2026 as pending) are therefore no precedent for DR-9113.

**Two things to confirm:**
- **Who the CRO is on 2027-02-01.** The latest org chart is dated 2026-09-01 and lists David Morgan as CRO. I found nothing more recent.
- **Recorded approval.** The 2027 policy says any discount above 5% needs recorded approval evidence, so the CRO's approval has to be logged in Northstar CRM.
