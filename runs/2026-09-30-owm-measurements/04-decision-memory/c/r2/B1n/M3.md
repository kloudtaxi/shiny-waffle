## Answer

No. Under the 2027 pricing policy, Sarah Chen can approve discounts of up to 5%, so she cannot approve 15% on her own. Acme is contractually eligible for the 15%, but that doesn't give her the authority. The Chief Revenue Officer has to approve this request.

**How I checked**

1. **Request:** DR-9113 asks for 15% off an NS-500 Industrial Controller: $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, for a net of $212,500. It is dated 2027-02-01, and Sarah Chen raised it. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on Acme's contract terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". In the finance system (ERP) it is billed to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. This is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), whose terms would not apply.
3. **Product:** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is active on 2027-02-01. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception. The requested 15% is exactly at the limit, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 took effect on 2027-01-01 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. Account executives may approve up to 5%, the VP Sales more than 5% up to 12%, and the CRO anything above 12%.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5% and 15% is well above it. Last September's 15% approval (DR-8104) and her September 2026 request DR-9001 were made when the account-executive limit was 10%, so they don't set a precedent under the new rules.
8. **Who must approve:** 15% is above 12%, so it needs the CRO. The VP Sales (Michael Torres) is not enough, because his 2027 limit is 12%. According to the organization chart dated 2026-09-01, the CRO is David Morgan. It's worth confirming he still holds the role in February 2027.

**Outcome: approve only with the CRO's sign-off**, recorded in Northstar CRM. The 2027 policy requires recorded approval evidence for anything over 5%.
