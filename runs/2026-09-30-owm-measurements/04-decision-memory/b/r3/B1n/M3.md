## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9113 herself. Acme is eligible for the 15%, but in 2027 her own limit is 5%, so the Chief Revenue Officer has to approve it. The records name David Morgan in that role.

1. **Request:** DR-9113 is a 15% discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller: $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, leaving $212,500 net. Sarah Chen requested it on 2027-02-01 and bases it on Acme's Master Supply Agreement, so it relies on contract terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing. This is a different company from "Acme Industrial Supply Co.", whose terms would not apply.
3. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is with Acme Mfg. Holdings. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is active on the request date.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which expired on 2025-03-31. The requested 15% is exactly at the limit, so Acme is eligible.
5. **Policy in force:** The request is dated in 2027, so the 2026 policy and its Approval Authority Matrix no longer apply. Both ended on 2026-12-31. Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 (effective 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31) sets these limits:
   - Enterprise Account Executives: up to 5%
   - VP Sales: over 5% and up to 12%
   - Chief Revenue Officer: over 12%
6. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5% and 15% is above it. Both the agreement (§3.3) and the exception say that Acme's eligibility does not give any employee approval authority.
7. **Required approver:** The Chief Revenue Officer, which the organization chart dated 2026-09-01 lists as David Morgan. No later chart exists, so confirm he still holds the role on 2027-02-01. The 2027 policy also requires recorded approval evidence for any discount above 5%.

This is a change from last year's DR-9001, the same 15% NS-500 request dated 2026-09-23. Under the 2026 policy, Michael Torres could approve that one as VP Sales. Under the 2027 policy, a VP Sales can approve only up to 12%, so this request has to go to the CRO.
