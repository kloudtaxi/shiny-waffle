No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15%, but under the 2027 pricing policy it needs Chief Revenue Officer approval. The records name David Morgan as CRO.

1. **Request DR-9113:** Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), NS-500 Industrial Controller, 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount), dated 2027-02-01, requested by Sarah Chen. It cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement, so it depends on Acme's contract terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is the same legal entity as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which does business as Acme Manufacturing. The earlier decision record DEC-2026-0001 made the same match.
3. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on 2027-02-01.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception. 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
5. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 (effective 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31). It fully replaces the 2026 policy for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01. Its approval limits are:
   - Enterprise Account Executive: up to 5%
   - VP Sales: over 5% up to 12%
   - Chief Revenue Officer: over 12%
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, her limit is 5%, so 15% is outside it. Both the contract (clause 3.3) and the exception say that Acme's eligibility doesn't change who inside Northstar can approve.
7. **Required approver:** At 15%, the request is in the CRO band, so David Morgan (per employees.csv and the org chart). Under the 2027 policy, discounts over 5% also need recorded approval evidence.
