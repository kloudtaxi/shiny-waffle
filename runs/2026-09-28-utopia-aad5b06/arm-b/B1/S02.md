## Answer

**No, not under Acme's current terms.** On 2026-09-23 Acme's contract allows at most 15% off list on the NS-500, so 18% is over the limit.

**Why**
- **The contract applies.** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Section 3.2 says Acme is eligible for discounts "of up to **15%**" on the NS-500.
- **The pricing exception sets the same cap.** Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) covers the NS-500 only, sets the "Maximum eligible discount" at 15% of list, and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception. David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it.
- **No higher discount is on record.** I found no amendment, new exception or other document that lets Acme go above 15%.

**Other points**
- **DR-9001 is logged at 15%, not 18%.** The CRM shows it at 15% on $250,000 list, which comes to $212,500 net, with status "Pending Approval." Sarah Chen's 2026-09-23 email to Michael Torres also says Acme asked for 15%, "which is what we did for them last September." If 18% is a new ask, it goes beyond both the contract and what was requested.
- **Even the 15% still needs VP Sales sign-off.** Under the 2026 Pricing Policy, account executives can approve up to 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres, and must be recorded in the CRM. The contract (section 3.3), the exception and the sales discount procedure all say a contract's discount limit doesn't give anyone the authority to approve it. So Sarah Chen, an account executive, can't approve DR-9001 herself. Last year's 15% deal (DR-8104) was under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. That no longer applies.
- **If you want to offer 18% anyway,** it would be pricing outside Acme's agreement. On authority alone, 18% is within the VP Sales range. But the knowledge base doesn't describe how to go beyond a contract's limit. The existing 15% exception was approved by the Chief Revenue Officer, so a higher one would presumably need a new or amended exception, most likely with his sign-off. The documents don't spell that out.

**What you can do:** offer up to 15% and send DR-9001 to Michael Torres for approval.
