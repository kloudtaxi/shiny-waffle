> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **Why 10%:** Pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023 (created 2023-03-12), lets Acme get up to 10% off NS-500 list price on orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. 2024-10-01 is inside that period, and the knowledge graph shows this as the only NS-500 discount entitlement for Acme on that date.
- **Why not 15%:** The later 15% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, is Schedule B to the Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025. It replaces the 10% exception only for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01, so it didn't apply yet on 2024-10-01. It runs until 2028-03-31.
