## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

All three IDs point to the same customer: **Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing**. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company.**

**How the three IDs connect**

| ID | Where it's used | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| **CRM-2048** | CRM account list | "Acme Manufacturing", Strategic tier, Midwest Enterprise segment, 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee WI. Business ID number (DUNS) 04-812-7730. Account owner is Sarah Chen. |
| **C-1001** | Billing system (ERP) customer list | "Acme Mfg. Holdings", Industrial Manufacturing, same Milwaukee address, same DUNS 04-812-7730, Net 45 payment terms. |
| **ACME-MFG-2025** | Master Supply Agreement (April 1, 2025 – March 31, 2028) | Signed between Northstar and "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing", at the same Milwaukee address. |

Two documents link them directly:
- Sarah Chen's 2026 account plan lists both "CRM account: CRM-2048" and "ERP bill-to: Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001)".
- The 15% NS-500 pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) is Schedule B to agreement ACME-MFG-2025, issued for "Acme Manufacturing".

The knowledge graph also treats them as one customer. It lists "Acme Mfg. Holdings" as the main name, with "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing" and "Acme" as other names for it.

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer**

| | Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| CRM ID | CRM-2048 | CRM-2091 |
| Billing ID | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| DUNS | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |
| Address | 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee WI 53209 | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron OH 44310 |
| Industry | Industrial Manufacturing | Industrial Distribution |
| Tier / segment | Strategic, Midwest Enterprise | Standard, Midwest Mid-Market |

The DUNS number, address, industry and IDs all differ, and the two are separate entries in both the CRM and the billing system. The only thing they share is "Acme" in the name. The agreement ACME-MFG-2025 and both NS-500 discount exceptions (the earlier 10% one and the current 15% one) apply only to Acme Manufacturing, not to Acme Industrial Supply Co.
