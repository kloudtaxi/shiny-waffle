## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing can get up to 15% off the NS-500 because its current contract with Northstar says so. Section 3.2 of Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 grants it, and the pricing exception attached to that contract sets out the terms.

**The evidence**

1. **Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025** (Legal)
   - The agreement is between Northstar Industrial Systems and Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as "Acme Manufacturing". In CRM it is account CRM-2048, and in ERP it is bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001).
   - It runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028. Northstar's Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan, signed on 2025-03-20, and Edward Floyd, Acme's VP Procurement, signed on 2025-03-22.
   - Section 3.2, "Strategic account pricing," says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."*

2. **Schedule B, pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15** (Deal Desk)
   - It covers the NS-500 Industrial Controller only and does not apply to any other Northstar product.
   - The maximum discount is 15% of list price, valid from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31.
   - It replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01.
   - Sarah Chen prepared it, and David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20.

3. **Earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10** (agreement ACME-SA-2023): this one allowed only up to 10% on NS-500 orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. So the 15% ceiling applies only to requests from April 2025 onward.

4. **Background:** Sarah Chen's 2026 account plan lists Acme as a strategic account that standardised on the NS-500 across two plants in 2025. The plan says Acme "negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement." A 15% NS-500 discount was approved before, as DR-8104 on 2025-09-16.

**Eligibility doesn't mean approval**

Both the contract (Section 3.3) and the exception say the 15% sets commercial eligibility only. It doesn't change Northstar's internal approval rules or give any employee the authority to approve it. Each discount still needs approval under the pricing policy in force on the request date.

- **Current request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on a $250,000 NS-500 order for Acme's third plant, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23 and still pending approval.
- **Who can approve it:** it falls under the 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix. Account executives can approve only up to 10%, so a 15% discount needs VP Sales approval.
- **Why last year was different:** the 2025 policy let account executives approve up to 15% themselves. That's why Sarah could approve DR-8104 on her own last September, and she can't do the same this time.
