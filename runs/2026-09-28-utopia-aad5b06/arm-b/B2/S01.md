> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount on 2026-09-23, even though Acme's contract allows it.

**1. Acme is allowed to get up to 15% on the NS-500.** Acme's master supply agreement (with Acme's signature dated 2025-03-22) says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* That pricing exception, which Sarah prepared, was approved by David Morgan (CRO) on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01.

**2. That doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it.** The exception says it *"establishes commercial eligibility only."* The agreement also says: *"Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."*

**3. Sarah's limit on that date is 10%.**
- Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive who reports to Michael Torres, VP Sales.
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It fully replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01, so it covers 2026-09-23.
- Under the 2026 policy, *"Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%."*
- The 2025 policy let them approve up to and including 15%. That's why this might look allowed, but that policy no longer applies.

15% is over her 10% limit, so the request has to go to someone with higher approval authority. The records don't say who can approve discounts between 10% and 15% under the 2026 policy. The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix is named as the reference for these thresholds, but its tiers aren't in the records. Her manager, Michael Torres (VP Sales), is the obvious next step, but I can't confirm he has the authority, so check the matrix before routing it.
