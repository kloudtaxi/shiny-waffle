## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing (listed in the knowledge base as **Acme Mfg. Holdings**) can get up to 15% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller because of its 2025 Master Supply Agreement and a pricing exception approved alongside it. This makes the account eligible for the discount, but each discount still needs internal approval.

**1. The contract clause (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`)**
> "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
- Acme signed it on 2025-03-22 (Edward Floyd, VP Procurement).
- It took effect on 2025-04-01 and runs to March 31, 2028, so it covers today.

**2. The pricing exception it refers to (`acme_pricing_exception.md`, "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception")**
- Sarah Chen, Enterprise Account Executive, prepared it.
- David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20.
- It applies only to the NS-500.
- It replaces the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, for requests dated 2025-04-01 or later.

**3. Why it was granted (`acme_account_strategy.md`)**
- "Acme is one of our strategic accounts."
- "They standardised on the NS-500 controller across two plants in 2025."
- "Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement."

**4. The earlier 10% limit (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`)**
> "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."

The 10% limit ended on 2025-03-31. The 15% limit applied from the next day, so there was no gap and no overlap.

**Important limits**
- **It's a maximum, not an automatic 15%.** Both documents say so:
  - The agreement: "Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
  - The exception: "This exception establishes commercial eligibility only."
- **Who approves it:** any specific NS-500 discount request for Acme still has to go through the normal approvers. Those are set out in the "SOP — Sales Discount Requests" and the "Approval Authority Matrix — Enterprise Discounts," which I didn't open.
- **Product scope:** the exception covers the NS-500 only. It doesn't extend to NS-Cloud, which Acme is currently evaluating.
