I can't confirm from the knowledge base which customer those three IDs refer to. On your second question, nothing links Acme Industrial Supply Co. to the main Acme customer, and the records suggest it's a different company.

**The three IDs**
- **CRM-2048** and **ACME-MFG-2025** don't appear anywhere in the knowledge base. They aren't entities and aren't listed as alternate names for anything.
- **C-1001** exists, but its only link is to sales order SO-7002, dated 2026-08-18. Nothing connects it to a named customer.

**The likely candidate:** The only Acme customer with real history is **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, also recorded as "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing" and "Acme".
- It's in Industrial Manufacturing and is described as a strategic account.
- It standardised on the NS-500 Industrial Controller and negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing.
- It signed an agreement on 2025-03-22, and an earlier agreement (ACME-SA-2023) let it receive the NS-500 from April 2023 to March 2025.
- It's evaluating NS-CLOUD.

Guessing from the ID formats, CRM-2048 could be its CRM account number and ACME-MFG-2025 an agreement or account code, but nothing in the records ties them to it.

**Acme Industrial Supply Co.**
- It's a separate entity with no connection to Acme Mfg. Holdings, even three steps apart.
- Its industry is **Industrial Distribution**, while Acme Mfg. Holdings is in **Industrial Manufacturing**. That points to a different company, possibly a distributor, not the manufacturer.
- A third record, **"Acme Industrial"**, carries CRM ID **CRM-2091**. It has no facts attached, so it may be a CRM entry for Acme Industrial Supply Co. or a separate one.

To settle this, check the source files `crm_accounts.csv` and `customers.csv` directly for the rows holding CRM-2048 and C-1001. I couldn't search those files with the tools I have.
