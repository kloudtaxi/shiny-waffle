## Answer

I'm stopping the search here: the knowledge base has what's needed for the main answer, though a few details are missing. The answer is **no, not as a standard offer.** On 2026-09-23, 18% is over Acme's contractual limit on the NS-500.

**Why:**

1. **Acme's contract caps the discount at 15%.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* (acme_master_supply_agreement.md). 18% is 3 points over that cap.
2. **The older 10% limit doesn't apply anymore.** That 2023 exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) covered orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. It was replaced by the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. David Morgan (CRO) approved the new exception on 2025-03-20; Sarah Chen prepared it.
3. **Being eligible doesn't mean it's approved.** The strategic exception "establishes commercial eligibility only". The agreement also says Acme's eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies." So even 15% or less still needs sign-off under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix.

**Gaps in the records:**
- The records don't give the approval thresholds in the 2026 policy or the Approval Authority Matrix. I can't tell you who could sign off on 18% for Acme, or whether that's allowed at all.
- The records don't give the percentage in the 2025 strategic exception. It likely matches the 15% in Schedule B, but I can't confirm that.
- DR-9001 appears only as the subject line of an email from Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive) to Michael Torres (VP Sales). The email's content isn't recorded.
- There is an 18% NS-500 discount on file that Michael Torres approved: DR-7015, on 2025-12-05. But it was for a different account (CRM-3007) under the 2025 policy, so it's no precedent for Acme.

**Next step:** Offer up to 15%, with approval under the 2026 matrix. Going to 18% would need a new or amended exception approved above the current contract terms. Check the Approval Authority Matrix to find who can approve that.
