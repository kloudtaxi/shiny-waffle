## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

**Yes. Under the rules in effect on 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme herself.** Two conditions both held that day:

**1. Acme was allowed to receive 15% on the NS-500.**
- An older 2023 exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) capped Acme at 10% off list price. It covered orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.
- A newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" replaced it for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. Sarah Chen prepared it, and David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20.
- The supply agreement Acme signed on 2025-03-22 says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller" under that exception.
- The agreement also says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." So the discount still needed approval from someone with the authority to give it.

**2. Sarah had the authority to approve 15%.**
- She is an Enterprise Account Executive and reports to Michael Torres, VP Sales.
- The 2025 Pricing Policy ran from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. It says: "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%." A 15% discount is exactly at that limit, so it was within her authority.

**Caveats:**
- The employee record for Sarah has no dates, so I'm assuming she held the Enterprise Account Executive role in September 2025.
- The same date one year later gives a different answer. The 2026 Pricing Policy took over for requests from 2026-01-01 and cut Account Executives' limit to 10%. The records show an NS-500 discount request dated 2026-09-23, which may be the one you have in mind. Under the 2026 policy, a 15% discount would go beyond Sarah's authority and need a higher approver. Acme would still be allowed to receive 15%.

I found nothing showing whether Sarah actually approved a discount on 2025-09-23. This only answers whether she was allowed to.
