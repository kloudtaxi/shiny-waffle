## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **Where the 10% comes from:** the 2023 NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`). It says: "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." 2024-10-01 falls inside that period.
- **Why it's not 15%:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`) allows discounts "of up to 15%" on the NS-500. But it was signed on 2025-03-22, after the date you asked about. The knowledge base has no start date for that 15% term, so I can't say exactly when it took effect. It looks like it replaced the 10% exception when that ran out on 2025-03-31.
- **Approval still applies:** the agreement says the discount limit "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." Each discount still has to be approved under Northstar's own policies.
