## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **15% off list price**.

- **Where the 15% comes from:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (Acme Manufacturing is recorded as "Acme Mfg. Holdings") says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."*
- **Why it applies on that date:** The agreement was signed on 2025-03-22 and runs from 2025-04-01 to March 31, 2028, so 2026-09-23 is inside that period.
- **Why it isn't 10%:** The 2023 pricing exception allowed up to 10% off list, but only for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception", prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, replaced it (it supersedes exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10).
- **Approval is still needed:** The agreement states that this pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." Each discount still has to be approved under Northstar's internal policies, so 15% is a ceiling, not an automatic discount.

The NS-500 list price is $250,000, so 15% works out to at most $37,500 off per unit. There is also a discount request logged for 2026-09-23 itself, but I haven't looked at whether it was approved or how much it asked for.
