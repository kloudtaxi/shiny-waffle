## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2022-10-01?

## Answer

On 2022-10-01, the knowledge base shows no contractual NS-500 discount maximum for Acme Manufacturing. On that date only standard pricing applies.

Acme Manufacturing is the same customer as Acme Mfg. Holdings, its ERP/canonical name. Two NS-500 pricing terms are on record for it, and neither covers 2022-10-01:

| Term | Maximum NS-500 discount | Valid |
|---|---|---|
| EXC-ACME-NS500-10 | 10% of list price | 2023-04-01 → 2025-03-31 |
| Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception (replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10; approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20) | 15% of list price | 2025-04-01 → 2028-03-31 |

2022-10-01 is six months before the earliest term starts. I also looked for anything that would change this:
- **Other contracts:** I found no customer contract for Acme covering 2022. The agreement record ACME-SA-2023 exists, but no terms or dates are recorded for it. The records show no customer contract active on 2022-10-01.
- **Corrections:** None have moved these dates.
- **Older terms:** Acme's timeline has no NS-500 pricing terms before 2023-04-01.

Pricing exceptions only apply to the product and dates they name, so neither the 10% nor the 15% maximum carries back to 2022. If a request dated 2022-10-01 relied on a contractual discount, it would need the underlying document before anyone could decide it. Any discount at all is still limited by the requestor's authority under the discount policy in force at the time.
