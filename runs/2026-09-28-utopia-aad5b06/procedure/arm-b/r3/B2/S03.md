## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve 15% for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve 15% on her own, and the request can't go ahead yet. It needs a commercial review first, because nothing on record entitles Acme to a special discount on NS-Cloud.

**What I found**

1. **The request:** No request record exists for 2026-09-23, so I have no request ID and no stated basis. The 15% figure matches exactly Acme's special maximum on a different product (below), so the request looks like it's borrowing those terms. That's my inference; the request doesn't say so.
2. **The customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is another name for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, an Industrial Manufacturing customer marked as a strategic account. It's a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co.
3. **The product:** "NS-Cloud Operations Suite" is **NS-CLOUD**, list price $120,000. The records only say Acme is *evaluating* NS-Cloud. They show no agreed terms for it.
4. **Active contract:** There's a contract record, ACME-SA-2023, but it holds no terms or dates. Acme signed a document on 2025-03-22, but the only discount terms found cover the NS-500 Industrial Controller. I found no contract term covering NS-Cloud on 2026-09-23.
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception sets a maximum of 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced an earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) and was approved by David Morgan. It applies **only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller**. An exception only covers the product it names, so it gives no right to 15% on NS-Cloud. The customer document also says Acme's discount terms don't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive (EAE), and the 2026 policy lets EAEs approve up to and including **10%**. 15% is over her limit. The 2025 policy's 15% limit no longer applies.
8. **Required approver:** Under the 2026 policy, discounts over 10% and up to 20% need **VP Sales** approval. That's **Michael Torres**, who is also Sarah's manager.

**Decision: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`**

- **If the 15% relies on Acme's special terms:** the NS-500 exception doesn't cover NS-Cloud, and no other terms do. Sign-off from someone more senior can't fix that. It needs a commercial review, and new or amended terms if NS-Cloud should get special pricing.
- **If it's a standard-pricing request:** Sarah still can't approve it herself. It would be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`, with Michael Torres approving.
- **Either way:** the requestor should state the basis. If Acme claims a contract term covering NS-Cloud, get that document before deciding.
