## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing is eligible for up to 15% off list price on the NS-500 because of a pricing exception called the **Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception**. It runs from **1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028**. That eligibility has limits, set out below.

**Customer and product**
- "Acme Manufacturing" is the same account as the ERP customer **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. Other names for it in the records are "Acme" and "ACME Manufacturing".
- The product is the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**, with a list price of $250,000.

**Evidence**
1. **The Strategic Account Exception** (document 01a0e787-7dd2…):
   - It applies to the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
   - It sets the maximum NS-500 discount at **15% of list price**, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 (that date range comes from document 01a0e9db-8950…).
   - It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility".
   - Sarah Chen prepared it and David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20.
   - It **replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10**.
2. **The earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10** (documents 01a0e787-7dae… and 01a0e9db-8950…): it capped Acme's NS-500 discount at **10%** from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. So the 15% cap only applies from 1 April 2025. Before that date, Acme could get at most 10%.
3. **The Master Supply Agreement** (document 01a0e787-7d66…):
   - It was signed 2025-03-22 and runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028, the same period as the exception.
   - It says Acme is eligible for NS-500 discounts "of up to" some amount, but the knowledge graph didn't capture the percentage. I can't confirm that the contract itself says 15%. The 15% figure comes from the exception.
   - It states explicitly that it **does not change Northstar's internal approval authority**.
4. **Account context** (acme_account_strategy.md): Acme is a strategic account that negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing.

No corrections or withdrawals of the exception or the 15% figure are on record.

**Limits on the eligibility**
- **Product:** it covers the NS-500 only. It doesn't extend to other products, such as NS-CLOUD, which Acme is evaluating.
- **Dates:** the request must fall between 2025-04-01 and 2028-03-31.
- **15% is the most Acme can get.** A request above 15% goes beyond Acme's eligibility and needs new or amended terms. A more senior approver can't fix that.
- **Eligibility is not approval.** The contract and the exception make the customer eligible, but the requestor still needs authority under the discount policy in force on the request date. If 15% is above their limit, the approver named for that discount level in the policy must sign off.
