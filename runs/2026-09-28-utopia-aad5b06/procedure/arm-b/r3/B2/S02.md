## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

I've checked the exception, the policy and the roles. The answer is no: 18% is above the most Acme is allowed to get on the NS-500. The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**.

**1. The request.** Request DR-9001 asks for 18% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller for Acme Manufacturing, dated 2026-09-23. It rests on Acme's special terms, not standard pricing. The knowledge graph has no record for DR-9001 itself. The request comes from an email from Sarah Chen to Michael Torres titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001", so the requestor is most likely Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive. The records don't confirm that directly.

**2. The customer.** Acme Manufacturing is the same customer as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the knowledge base. It is not the same company as Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091), which is a separate distributor.

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.

**4. The contract.** A Master Supply Agreement signed 2025-03-22 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23.

**5. The pricing exception.**
- The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" covers this product and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It caps the NS-500 discount at **15% of list price**. It was approved by David Morgan and replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31).
- 18% is 3 points over that cap, so Acme isn't eligible for this discount.

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%.
- Above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales approval.
- Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.

**7–8. Authority.**
- 18% is above Sarah Chen's 10% limit, and it falls in the VP Sales band, which is Michael Torres.
- A higher sign-off doesn't solve this, though. Approval authority doesn't change what the customer is allowed to get, so even Michael Torres can't approve more than the 15% the exception permits.

**9. Decision.** Don't approve 18%. There are two ways forward:
- Offer 15% or less. Anything above 10% still needs Michael Torres (VP Sales) to approve.
- If 18% is needed, negotiate new or amended terms with Acme first.
