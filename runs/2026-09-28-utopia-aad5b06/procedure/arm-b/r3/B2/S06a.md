> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing could get a discount of up to **15% of list price** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

- **Customer:** In the knowledge base, "Acme Manufacturing" is a recorded alias of **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. That is a different company from *Acme Industrial Supply Co.*
- **Where the 15% comes from:** The **Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception** sets the maximum at 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It applies to the NS-500 Industrial Controller, and the knowledge base describes it as establishing commercial eligibility. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20 and Sarah Chen prepared it. The customer record shows it was signed on 2025-03-22.
- **Previous limit:** It replaced the older exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-10**, which capped the discount at 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That one no longer applies.
- **Corrections:** None of the corrections or withdrawals recorded for this customer change the discount figures.

The 15% is how much discount Acme is eligible for, not approval for any particular discount. A request above what the requestor is allowed to approve still needs sign-off from whoever the pricing policy names for that discount level.
