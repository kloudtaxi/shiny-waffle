> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

Yes. On 2025-09-23 Sarah Chen could have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing herself. The customer was eligible for up to 15% on that product, and the 2025 policy let her approve up to and including 15%.

**Checks, step by step**
1. **The request:** 15% off the NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. The knowledge base has no request ID for that date. The basis is the Acme exception, because the 2025 policy has no general 15% eligibility.
2. **The customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is another name for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, which is a strategic account. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044).
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement was signed 2025-03-22 and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the date.
5. **The pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception covers this product from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% off list. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10 (10% maximum, 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31). David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20; Sarah Chen prepared it. 15% is within the maximum.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Her authority:** That policy lets Enterprise Account Executives approve up to and including 15%, and Sarah Chen is one. VP Sales approval is needed only above 15%.
8. **Other approver needed:** None.

**Caveats**
- **Her role has no date.** The record doesn't say when she became an Enterprise Account Executive. I assumed she held the role on 2025-09-23.
- **The year may be different.** The knowledge base has an NS-500 discount request dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025. If that's the one you mean, the answer changes. The 2026 policy cuts her limit to 10% and needs VP Sales approval for 10–20%. The exception still covers 15% then, so the outcome would be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION` by the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
