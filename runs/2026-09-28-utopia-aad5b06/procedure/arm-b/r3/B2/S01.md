> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, but the 2026 policy only lets her approve up to 10%. The request needs sign-off from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**1. The request.** A 15% discount for Acme on the NS-500, requested on 2026-09-23 by Sarah Chen. It relies on Acme's special pricing terms, not standard pricing. The knowledge base records a discount request on the NS-500 dated 2026-09-23. It also has an email from Sarah to Michael Torres titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001", so the request is probably DR-9001. However, the graph doesn't link DR-9001 to that date or amount, so treat the ID as likely rather than confirmed.

**2. The customer.** "Acme Manufacturing" (the CRM name) is the same customer as Acme Mfg. Holdings (the ERP name). It's a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co.

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.

**4. The contract.** Acme has a Master Supply Agreement signed 2025-03-22, running from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It was active on the request date.

**5. The pricing exception.** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" covers the NS-500 and caps the discount at **15% of list price** from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced an earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which capped the discount at 10% and ended 2025-03-31. David Morgan approved the new exception on 2025-03-20. A 15% request is at the cap, not over it, so Acme is eligible.

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on 2026-09-23 (it runs 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It replaced the 2025 policy.

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy she can approve discounts up to and including **10%**, so 15% is above her limit. The 2025 policy allowed her up to 15%, but it no longer applies. The contract also doesn't extend her authority: it says so itself, and eligibility under a contract never changes what an employee can approve.

**8. The required approver.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need **VP Sales** approval. The VP Sales is **Michael Torres**, who is also Sarah's manager.

**9. Decision:** Approve with authorization. Acme is eligible for 15%, but Michael Torres must approve it, not Sarah.
