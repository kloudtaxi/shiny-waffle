## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% of list price**.

- **Customer:** In the knowledge graph, "Acme Manufacturing" is another name for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. This is a different customer from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which should not be confused with it.
- **What set the 10%:** Pricing exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-10** set the maximum NS-500 discount at 10% of list price from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The customer's own record says the same: up to 10% on NS-500 for that period. 2024-10-01 falls inside it.
- **The later 15% does not apply:** The **Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception** replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10 and raised the maximum to 15% of list price. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, but it only runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It had not started on 2024-10-01.

This is only the customer's contractual eligibility. The contract does not change who inside Northstar can approve a discount. An actual request would still need the policy in force on its date to set the requestor's limit and the required approver.
