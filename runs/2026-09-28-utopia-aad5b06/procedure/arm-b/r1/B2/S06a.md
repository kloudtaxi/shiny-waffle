## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum eligible discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller is **15% of list price**.

- **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing is on record as "Acme Mfg. Holdings", a strategic account. "Acme", "ACME Manufacturing" and "Acme Manufacturing" are other names for the same customer.
- **Where the 15% comes from:** the Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception sets it. That exception covers the NS-500 and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is in force on 2026-09-23. It was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, prepared by Sarah Chen, and Acme signed on 2025-03-22.
- **Earlier terms:** it replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed up to 10% on the NS-500 from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That exception had expired by September 2026.
- **Corrections:** I checked the knowledge base for corrected or withdrawn claims about Acme. None of them touch the 15% ceiling.

This 15% is how much discount Acme can receive. It doesn't change Northstar's internal approval rules, and the source documents say so explicitly. A 15% request would still need approval from someone whose authority covers that amount under the policy in force.
