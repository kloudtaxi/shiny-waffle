2. **The customer is unclear.** "Acme" matches two different companies:
   - **ACME Manufacturing**, in Milwaukee, WI (also recorded as "Acme Mfg. Holdings"). It has sales orders SO-7001 and SO-7002.
   - **Acme Industrial Supply Co.**, in Akron, OH (customer C-1044). It has sales orders SO-5001 and SO-5005.

   The request needs to say which one it means.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract and pricing exception.** I found no contract and no pricing exception for either Acme company, and nothing links either one to the NS-500.
5. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaced the 2025 policy.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, that role can approve discounts up to and including **10%**. The 2025 policy allowed 15%, but it no longer applies.
7. **Who must approve.** The 2026 policy sends discounts above 10% and up to 20% to the **VP Sales**, which is Michael Torres. Sarah Chen reports to him.

If it relies on an Acme contract term or exception, the outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**: get the document before deciding. Even if a contract allowed 15%, VP Sales approval would still be needed, because a contract doesn't change what an employee can approve.
