## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** The Acme NS-500 exception caps the discount at 15% of list price, so 18% is above what Acme is eligible for. Getting VP Sales approval doesn't fix that. It would need new or amended commercial terms. Acme could get up to 15% under the current terms.

1. **The request.** The graph holds nothing about DR-9001 itself. An NS-500 discount request is recorded for 2026-09-23, and an email from Sarah Chen to Michael Torres is titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001", so Sarah Chen is probably the requestor, but no record confirms it. The request presumably relies on Acme's special NS-500 pricing, since 18% on standard pricing would make no sense for this account. That is my reading, not something recorded.
2. **The customer.** "Acme Manufacturing" is the same entity as Acme Mfg. Holdings (also known as "Acme" and "ACME Manufacturing"). Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091) is a different company, a distributor.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **The contract.** A customer contract signed 2025-03-22 gives Acme NS-500 discounts of up to 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. That covers 2026-09-23. The contract also says it does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **The pricing exception.** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception applies to the NS-500 and sets a 15% maximum from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% and ended 2025-03-31. It was prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20. Because 18% is above 15%, eligibility is exceeded.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in effect on 2026-09-23. It replaced the 2025 policy, under which Enterprise Account Executives could approve up to 15%.
7. **The requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, who can approve up to and including 10% under the 2026 policy. 18% is beyond that.
8. **The approver.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, and Michael Torres holds that role. That only matters once Acme is eligible for the discount. Above 20% would need the Chief Revenue Officer.

Your options:
- **Offer 15% or less.** Acme is eligible for that, but anything above 10% still needs Michael Torres's approval as VP Sales, because it's beyond an Enterprise Account Executive's limit.
- **Keep the 18% request.** Take it through commercial review to amend the exception or the contract first. After that, the discount would still need VP Sales approval (it's in the 10–20% band).
