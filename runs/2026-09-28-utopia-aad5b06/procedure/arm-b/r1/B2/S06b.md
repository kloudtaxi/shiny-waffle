## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% of list price**.

**Source:** Pricing exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-10** allowed a maximum NS-500 discount of 10% of list price from **2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31**, and 2024-10-01 falls inside that period. The same 10% limit is also recorded directly against the customer for those dates. In the ERP the customer is named "Acme Mfg. Holdings"; other records call it "Acme Manufacturing" and "Acme".

**Don't use the 15% figure for this date.** The later *Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception* replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10 and raised the maximum to **15%**, but only from **2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31**. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, and Acme signed it on 2025-03-22. It did not apply on 2024-10-01.

This is the customer's commercial eligibility only. Whether an employee can approve a given discount depends separately on their own authority under the policy in force at the time.
