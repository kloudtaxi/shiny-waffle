## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve 15% for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this, and it shouldn't be approved as submitted. It needs commercial review.** She lacks the authority for 15% under the 2026 policy either way. And if the 15% relies on Acme's special pricing, those terms only cover a different product.

1. **The request.** There's no discount request on file for Acme and the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. The only Acme request dated 2026-09-23 is DR-9001, which is for the NS-500 Industrial Controller. So I have no request ID, and nothing states what the 15% relies on. It equals the maximum in Acme's NS-500 pricing exception, so it most likely borrows those terms. That's an inference, not something a record says.
2. **The customer.** "Acme Manufacturing" is a name for Acme Mfg. Holdings, a strategic account. The account strategy notes they are "evaluating NS-Cloud for plant-floor analytics."
3. **The product.** NS-Cloud Operations Suite, SKU NS-CLOUD (PROD-002), list price $120,000.
4. **The contract.** Acme signed an agreement on 2025-03-22. The record I found (ACME-SA-2023) has no terms on file. The only contract discount terms in the knowledge base are for the NS-500. I found nothing that gives Acme a discount on NS-Cloud.
5. **The pricing exception.** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception allows up to 15% from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, but only on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. David Morgan approved it; Sarah Chen prepared it. It doesn't cover NS-Cloud, and an exception applies only to the product it names.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies on 2026-09-23. Under the 2025 policy an Enterprise Account Executive could approve up to 15%, but that policy is no longer in force.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, with a limit of 10% under the 2026 policy. 15% is over it.
8. **The required approver.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres, who Sarah has reported to since 2026-09-01.

**Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** If the 15% relies on Acme's special terms, nothing covers NS-Cloud, so it needs commercial review. That means new or amended terms for NS-Cloud, not just a higher approver. If it turns out to be a standard-pricing request, it would still need Michael Torres's approval, because 15% is over Sarah's 10% limit. Acme's strategic-account status doesn't change her authority either way.
