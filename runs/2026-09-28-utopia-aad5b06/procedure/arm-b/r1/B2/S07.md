## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme is eligible for up to 15% off list price on the NS-500 because of a signed pricing exception that the master contract also confirms. That eligibility is limited to certain dates, and it does not by itself approve any particular discount.

**Why Acme qualifies**

1. **The pricing exception** (`acme_pricing_exception.md`) is the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception."
   - It sets Acme's maximum NS-500 discount at **15% of list price**, valid **2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31**.
   - It "applies only to the product identified above," which is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
   - It "supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."
   - Sarah Chen prepared it. "David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer" approved it on 2025-03-20.
   - The 15% figure and its dates are recorded in a separate data-cleanup file (`northstar-curation-exception-values.json`), not quoted directly from the exception document.

2. **The master contract** (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`) backs this up. It says: "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B." The agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to March 31, 2028, and Acme signed it on 2025-03-22 (Edward Floyd, VP Procurement).

3. **The account notes** (`acme_account_strategy.md`) add background only: "Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement" and "Acme is one of our strategic accounts."

**Limits on that eligibility**

- **Dates:** The 15% applies only to requests dated 2025-04-01 through 2028-03-31. Before that, the older exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10 capped Acme at **10%** (2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31).
- **Product:** The exception covers the NS-500 only. It does not carry over to other products, such as NS-Cloud, which Acme is evaluating.
- **Approval:** The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only." The contract says: "Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies." A 15% request still needs sign-off from whoever is authorized under the discount policy in force on the request date.
- **Customer name:** The finance-system customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). The exception documents call it "Acme Manufacturing." A link between the sales-system account "Acme Manufacturing" and C-1001 was recorded and later withdrawn. Before using this for a specific request, confirm that the account on the request is C-1001.
