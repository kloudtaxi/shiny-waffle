> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

Quick update: none of the three IDs exists as an entity in the graph, so I've been checking the two Acme records and their linked contracts and orders instead. Here's the answer.

**Short answer:** The knowledge base can't confirm which customer CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to. It can show that Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer from the Acme manufacturing customer.

**The three identifiers.** I searched the graph for CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025, and for parts of them ("2048", "1001", "MFG-2025", "ACME-"). None matched anything, either as an entity or as an alias of one.
- The only contract-style Acme IDs recorded are ACME-SA-2023 (the earlier agreement) and EXC-ACME-NS500-10 (the earlier pricing exception).
- The customer's current agreement is recorded only as a "Master Supply Agreement", effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. No ID is attached to it.

The ID formats look like a CRM account, an ERP customer number and a 2025 contract for Acme Manufacturing. Nothing on record ties them to it, though, so I'm not treating that as confirmed.

**The two Acme customers in the graph:**

**Is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?** No. It has its own CRM account (CRM-2091), its own ERP customer number (C-1044) and a different industry. The graph keeps it apart from Acme Mfg. Holdings and doesn't list either as an alias of the other. The only thing they share is the word "Acme". This matters for discounts: Acme Mfg. Holdings' contract and NS-500 exception don't apply to Acme Industrial Supply Co.

**What would settle it:** the CRM account record for CRM-2048, the ERP customer master for C-1001, or the contract document for ACME-MFG-2025. Any one of these showing the customer's name would confirm the link. The knowledge base doesn't currently contain any of them.
