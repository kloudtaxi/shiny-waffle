2. **The customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is a CRM name for the same customer as the ERP's **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a separate customer.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Active contract:** The Master Supply Agreement was signed 2025-03-22 and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. The contract states it does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** The *Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception* covers this product and allows up to **15% of list price** from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. The 15% request is within this limit.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy, which had let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%. That higher limit no longer applies.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy she can approve up to and including **10%**, so 15% is above her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need **VP Sales** approval. Michael Torres is VP Sales and Sarah's manager (from 2026-09-01).
9. **Decision:** Acme is eligible for 15%, but that doesn't raise Sarah's authority. The outcome is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**: Michael Torres must approve.
