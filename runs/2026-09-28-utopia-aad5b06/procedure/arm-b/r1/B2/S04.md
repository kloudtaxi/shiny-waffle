## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

Yes. On 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved a 15% discount on the NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing herself. Acme was allowed that discount, and 15% was within her own limit under the pricing policy in force that day.

1. **The request:** This is a hypothetical request on 2025-09-23. I found no request record with that date, so the request id is unknown. It relies on a special pricing exception for Acme, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as "Acme Mfg. Holdings." It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co.
3. **Product:** The NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** An agreement dated 2023, ACME-SA-2023, appears in the records, but they hold no terms or dates for it. Acme signed something on 2025-03-22. The check below rests on the pricing exception instead.
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception applies to the NS-500 and allows up to 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, and it replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme qualifies.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, which ran from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. The 2025 policy lets that role approve discounts up to and including 15%, and only discounts above 15% need VP Sales approval. So 15% is within her own limit.
8. **Required approver:** No one else is needed.

Her approval doesn't depend on the exception, which only makes Acme eligible for 15%. It is valid because the 2025 policy gave her 15% of authority herself.

Things to be aware of:
- **2026 is different.** A discount request for the NS-500 is recorded on **2026-09-23**. If that's the date you meant, the answer changes. The 2026 policy cuts the Account Executive limit to 10%, so a 15% discount would need VP Sales approval (for over 10% up to 20%). The recorded VP Sales is Michael Torres.
- Sarah prepared the Acme exception herself. The procedure doesn't forbid her then approving discounts under it, but you may want to note it.
- Her job title in the records has no dates, so I've assumed she held it in September 2025.
