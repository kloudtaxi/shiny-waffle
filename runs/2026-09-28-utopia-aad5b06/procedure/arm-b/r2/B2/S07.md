## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme can get up to 15% off the NS-500 because of a pricing exception called the **Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception**. It sets 15% of list price as the most Acme can be discounted on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, and it runs from **1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028**. That means it's still in force today (28 Sep 2026).

**Customer name:** Acme appears as "Acme Mfg. Holdings", "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing" and just "Acme". These are all the same customer record.

I checked for corrections and withdrawals, and none of them touch the exception, its 15% cap or its dates.

**Limits of this eligibility**
- **It covers the NS-500 only.** It doesn't extend to other products. For example, Acme is also looking at NS-CLOUD, and nothing I found gives Acme special terms on it.
- **It covers these dates only.** Before 1 April 2025 the limit was 10%. After 31 March 2028 the 15% no longer applies unless the exception is renewed.
- **Being eligible doesn't mean a request is approved.** The 15% is the most the customer can receive. The agreement itself says it doesn't change internal approval authority. Any specific 15% request still has to be approved by whoever the discount policy in force on the request date names for that discount level. It can't be approved just because the exception allows it.
- **The agreement's own discount figure isn't recorded.** The knowledge base notes that the agreement grants discount eligibility but doesn't capture the percentage. The 15% figure comes from the exception records. If you need the exact contract wording, check the signed agreement.
