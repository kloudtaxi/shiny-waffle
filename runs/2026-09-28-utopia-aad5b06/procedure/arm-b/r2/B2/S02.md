The discount relies on Acme's special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer.** "Acme Manufacturing" is the same account as Acme Mfg. Holdings, which is a strategic account. Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. I couldn't find a separate SKU code in the records.
4. **Active contract: yes.** The Master Supply Agreement was signed 2025-03-22 and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It states that it does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception: yes, but capped at 15%.** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception covers the NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% off list. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ended 2025-03-31). 18% is 3 points over the cap.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (effective 2026-01-01).
7. **Requestor's authority.** Under that policy, Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. So even an amount within the cap would need someone above Sarah Chen.
8. **Required approver.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% go to the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres. That approval applies only if the discount is within Acme's eligibility.
