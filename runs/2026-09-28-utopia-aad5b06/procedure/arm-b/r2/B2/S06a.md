## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller is **15% of list price**.

- **Customer:** In the knowledge base, Acme Manufacturing is recorded as "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a separate industrial distribution customer.
- **Where the 15% comes from:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception applies to the NS-500 Industrial Controller only. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers 2026-09-23. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20 and Sarah Chen prepared it.
- **The older 10% exception is no longer in force:** The Strategic Account Exception replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which capped the discount at 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.
- **Corrections checked:** No corrections have been recorded against the 15% limit. The only withdrawn Acme records concern alternate names and a claim that Acme "standardised on the NS-500 across two plants in 2025". None of them affects the discount terms.

The 15% is the most Acme can receive, not what an employee may approve. The customer's contract terms state that they do not change Northstar's internal approval authority. A discount up to 15% would still need sign-off from someone whose limit covers it under the policy in force at the time.
