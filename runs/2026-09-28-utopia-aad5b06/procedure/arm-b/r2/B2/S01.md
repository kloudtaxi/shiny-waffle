## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, but under the 2026 policy an Enterprise Account Executive can only approve up to 10%. A 15% discount needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**1. The request.** A 15% discount for Acme on the NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. The graph links an email from Sarah to Michael Torres titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001", so DR-9001 is probably this request's ID. The graph holds no details for DR-9001, though, so I haven't confirmed it. The request relies on Acme's special terms: its contract and pricing exception.

**2. Customer.** "Acme Manufacturing" is a known alternate name for the canonical account "Acme Mfg. Holdings", a strategic account in Industrial Manufacturing. A separate company, Acme Industrial Supply Co. (industrial distribution), is a different customer and not relevant here.

**3. Product.** The NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000.

**4. Contract.** Acme's Master Supply Agreement was signed 2025-03-22 and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."

**5. Pricing exception.** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (prepared by Sarah Chen, approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20) covers only the NS-500. It allows up to 15% off list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and it replaces the old 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. The request is at the maximum, not above it, so Acme is eligible.

**6. Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and replaces the 2025 policy.

**7. Sarah's authority.** Under the 2026 policy, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%, so 15% is above her limit. The 2025 policy let them approve up to 15%, but it no longer applies. Acme's contract doesn't change that: it states that it "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."

**8. Required approver.** The 2026 policy says discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres is VP Sales, and Sarah has reported to him since 2026-09-01.

**What to do:** Send the request to Michael Torres for approval. Sarah shouldn't approve it herself, and being eligible under the contract isn't enough on its own. Two things to confirm in the source records: that DR-9001 is this request, and that Michael is still VP Sales. The org-chart role record has no date on it.
