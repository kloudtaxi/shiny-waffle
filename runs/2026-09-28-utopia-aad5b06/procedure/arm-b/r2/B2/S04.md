## Answer

Yes. On 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing herself, with no one else signing off.

**Why:**
1. **Customer:** In the CRM, "Acme Manufacturing" is the same account the knowledge base calls "Acme Mfg. Holdings". It's a strategic account, and its contract was signed on 2025-03-22.
2. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
3. **Special terms (pricing exception):** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and caps NS-500 discounts at 15% of list price. So 15% is within Acme's allowed maximum. It replaced the earlier exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which capped discounts at 10% and ended on 2025-03-31. The new exception was prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20.
4. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (effective 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts up to and including 15%. VP Sales approval is needed only above 15%.
5. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is within her own limit. That authority comes from the pricing policy, not from Acme's contract.

**Caveats:**
- **No request for this date:** I found no discount request dated 2025-09-23, so I treated this as a hypothetical. The records do show an NS-500 discount request dated **2026-09-23**. If you meant that date, the answer changes. Under Pricing Policy 2026, Enterprise Account Executives can only approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, which makes that request "approve with authorization". The org chart names Michael Torres as VP Sales, but that entry was withdrawn from the knowledge base, so it needs confirming.
- **Role dates:** Sarah Chen's role has no dates in the knowledge base, so I assumed she held it in September 2025.
