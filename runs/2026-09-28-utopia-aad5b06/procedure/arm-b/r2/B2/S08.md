> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

I can't confirm what CRM-2048, C-1001 or ACME-MFG-2025 refer to, because none of the three IDs is in the knowledge base. I searched each one in full and in parts ("2048", "1001", "CRM-", "C-10", "ACME-"). Other IDs from the same series turn up (CRM-2051, CRM-2063, C-1002, C-1003, ACME-SA-2023), but these three don't.

**The likely candidate is Acme Mfg. Holdings, but that's an unconfirmed guess.**
- It's the only other Acme customer, and it appears in the CRM account list (as "Acme Manufacturing") and the ERP customer list. It's also recorded as "Acme", "ACME Manufacturing" and "Customer".
- It's in the Industrial Manufacturing industry and has a signing date on record of 2025-03-22.
- It holds the NS-500 pricing exception. The old exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) allowed up to 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The current one allows up to 15% from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It was prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20.
- The contract on file is called **ACME-SA-2023**, not ACME-MFG-2025. So ACME-MFG-2025 doesn't match any contract I can find either.

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**
- It has its own IDs: CRM account **CRM-2091** (listed as "Acme Industrial") and ERP customer **C-1044**. Those don't match CRM-2048 or C-1001.
- It's in a different industry (Industrial Distribution, not Industrial Manufacturing).
- It has no contract, pricing exception or NS-500 terms on record. Acme Mfg. Holdings' special pricing doesn't apply to it just because the names are similar.

To link CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 for certain, you'd need the CRM account export, the ERP customer master and the contract document that contain those IDs. If a discount request depends on a contract called ACME-MFG-2025, get that document before deciding, since it isn't in the evidence.
