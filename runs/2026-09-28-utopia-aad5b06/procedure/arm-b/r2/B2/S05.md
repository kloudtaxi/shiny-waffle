## Answer

**No, not yet. The decision is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The knowledge base has nothing on DR-9001 beyond its ID, and it holds no contract or pricing exception for Acme that could support 15%. Even if eligibility is later shown, Sarah can't approve 15% herself.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 has no linked facts. The customer, product, 15%, date and requestor come from your question. The records don't say what the discount relies on. Sarah's earlier Acme request, DR-8104 (15% on NS-500, 2025-09-16), cited "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", and she approved it herself. DR-9001 may rely on the same agreement, but I can't confirm that.

**2. The customer.** It's unclear which Acme this is. There are two separate records:
- **ACME Manufacturing** (also recorded as Acme Mfg. Holdings, Milwaukee)
- **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** (also recorded as Acme Industrial, ERP C-1044, Akron)

Neither record links to this request.

**3. The product.** The NS-500 Industrial Controller has a list price of $250,000.

**4–5. Contract and pricing exception.** I found no Acme Master Supply Agreement, no active contract and no pricing exception for NS-500 on 2026-09-23. The only related record is a generic heading, "Contract-specific pricing exceptions", with nothing linked to it. DR-8104's approval doesn't carry forward, because special terms apply only to the products and dates they name.

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaces the 2025 policy. The 2025 policy let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%, which is why DR-8104 went through. That limit is now 10%.

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive with a limit of 10%, so 15% is above what she can approve.

**8. Required approver.** Under the 2026 policy, discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, and Sarah reports to him.

**9. Decision.** Before deciding, ask for:
- the DR-9001 request record, including its stated basis and the customer's CRM/ERP account ID;
- if it relies on contract pricing, the Acme Master Supply Agreement (or pricing exception) that covers NS-500 on 2026-09-23.

If an agreement allowing at least 15% turns up, the outcome becomes APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION and Michael Torres must approve. If the request turns out to be standard pricing, it also goes to Michael Torres. The agreement permitting 15% would not let Sarah approve it herself either way. If the terms allow less than 15%, the outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. If they don't cover NS-500 on that date, it's REVIEW_REQUIRED.
