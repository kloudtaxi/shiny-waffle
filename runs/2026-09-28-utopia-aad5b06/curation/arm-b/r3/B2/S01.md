## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No.** Acme can get a 15% discount on the NS-500 on 2026-09-23, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. It has to go to the VP of Sales.

**1. Acme is allowed 15%.**
- The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception sets the maximum NS-500 discount at 15% of list price. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced an earlier 10% limit (reference EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which was in place from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.
- The NS-500's list price is $250,000, so 15% comes to $37,500 off.
- Acme's agreement says it does not change Northstar's internal rules on who can approve discounts. Being allowed the discount doesn't make any given person able to approve it.

**2. Sarah can only approve up to 10%.**
- Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive.
- Under Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31):
  - Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%.
  - Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval.
  - Discounts over 20% need approval from the Chief Revenue Officer.
- 15% falls in the VP Sales band.

**3. Who approves it.**
- The VP of Sales is Michael Torres. Sarah has reported to him since 2026-09-01.

**Watch out for the old policy.** Under Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, Enterprise Account Executives could approve up to 15%, so Sarah could have signed this off herself in 2025. The 2026 policy replaced that and lowered the limit to 10%. Anyone going by older guidance could get this wrong.
