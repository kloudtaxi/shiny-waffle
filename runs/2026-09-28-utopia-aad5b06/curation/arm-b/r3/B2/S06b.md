## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% of list price**. The knowledge base lists Acme under the name "Acme Mfg. Holdings."

- **2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31:** the limit was 10% of list price. This covers 2024-10-01. The earlier exception on record is EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- **2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31:** the limit rose to 15%. That came from the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception", which replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10. It was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20 and Acme signed on 2025-03-22.

The 15% limit is the one you may see quoted today, but it didn't start until after your date. No one has corrected or withdrawn either discount figure in the knowledge base. The source also says the contract doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority, so actual discounts may still need internal sign-off.
