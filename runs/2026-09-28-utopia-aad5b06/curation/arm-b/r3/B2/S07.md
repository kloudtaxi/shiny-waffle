## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing can get up to 15% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller because of a signed strategic account pricing exception. The master supply agreement confirms it. In the knowledge base, Acme is recorded as **Acme Mfg. Holdings** (customer C-1001), with "Acme Manufacturing" as another name.

**Why it's eligible**
1. **The strategic account exception (`acme_pricing_exception.md`)**
   - Prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20.
   - It applies to the NS-500 only: *"This exception applies only to the product identified above."*
   - It replaces the earlier exception: *"This exception supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."*
   - The 15% maximum and its dates, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, come from a curation file (`northstar-curation-exception-values.json`), not a direct quote from the memo.
2. **The master supply agreement (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`)**, signed 2025-03-22 by Edward Floyd, VP Procurement:
   > "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
3. **Account strategy (`acme_account_strategy.md`)** gives the business reason: *"Acme is one of our strategic accounts"* and *"Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement."*

**How it changed over time**
- **2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31:** the older exception allowed up to 10% (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`: *"may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500… for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31"*).
- **2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31:** the new exception allows up to 15%. The NS-500 list price is $250,000, so the most it allows is $37,500 off per unit.

**Limits**
- **15% is a maximum, not an automatic discount.** The exception says it *"establishes commercial eligibility only,"* and the agreement says the eligibility *"does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."* Each discount still needs internal approval.
- It only covers requests dated in the 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 window.
- **Don't use this claim as evidence:** the account strategy's statement that Acme "standardised on the NS-500 across two plants in 2025" has been withdrawn in the knowledge base.
