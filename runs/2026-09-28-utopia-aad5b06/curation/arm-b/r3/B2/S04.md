## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

Yes. Under the rules on record, a 15% discount on 2025-09-23 was within Sarah Chen's authority and within what Acme was allowed to receive.

**1. Her approval limit allowed it.** Sarah Chen is recorded as an Enterprise Account Executive. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31) let Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts **up to and including 15%**. VP Sales approval was needed only above 15%. So 15% was exactly at her limit, not over it.

**2. Acme could receive 15% on the NS-500.** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception raised Acme Manufacturing's maximum NS-500 discount from 10% to **15% of list price**. The new cap runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved the exception on 2025-03-20, and Sarah Chen prepared it. The Acme contract says this exception does not change Northstar's internal approval limits. That doesn't matter here, because her own 2025 limit already covered 15%.

**One gap:** her Enterprise Account Executive title has no date attached. The only dated fact about her position is that she reports to Michael Torres (VP Sales) from 2026-09-01. So the records don't directly confirm she held that role in September 2025.

**Check the date:** the records show an NS-500 discount request dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025. If that's the one you mean, the answer changes to **no**. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 cut the Enterprise Account Executive limit to **10%**. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, which would be Michael Torres. Acme would still be allowed the 15% under the exception, but Sarah couldn't approve it herself.
