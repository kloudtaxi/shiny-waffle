The graph has no alternate IDs for it at all; it only lists these other names:

Acme Mfg. Holdings is in Industrial Manufacturing. It is recorded as a strategic account with preferential pricing on the NS-500 Industrial Controller: up to 10% off list price from April 2023 to March 2025, and up to 15% from April 2025 to March 2028. It also appears on sales order SO-7002 (18 Aug 2026).

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer.** It has its own identifiers: CRM-2091 and C-1044, and it's also called "Acme Industrial." It's in Industrial Distribution, not manufacturing. The two have never been merged, and nothing in the graph links them.

So if you've seen those three codes used for Acme Manufacturing, don't treat them as Acme Industrial Supply Co. Its codes are CRM-2091 and C-1044.
