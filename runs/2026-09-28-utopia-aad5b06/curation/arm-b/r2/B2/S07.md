## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme can get up to 15% off the NS-500 because of a signed customer contract and an approved pricing exception that goes with it. That is the most it can get, not an automatic 15%, and each discount still needs Northstar's normal internal approval.

**Why it's eligible**

1. **The contract gives the 15%.** Acme's supply agreement (acme_master_supply_agreement.md), signed 2025-03-22, says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."*
2. **The pricing exception (the contract's Schedule B) sets it out.** acme_pricing_exception.md, the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception":
   - Approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20. Prepared by Sarah Chen.
   - It applies only to the NS-500.
   - It *"supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."*
   - The knowledge base gives it a 15% cap from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. That date range comes from a curated values file (northstar-curation-exception-values.json), not a quote from the document itself.
3. **It replaced an older 10% exception.** acme_pricing_exception_2023.md said Acme *"may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."* So the cap went from 10% to 15% for requests from 2025-04-01 onward. Requests before that date fall under the 10% cap.

**Background, but not the legal basis:** acme_account_strategy.md calls Acme *"one of our strategic accounts"* that *"negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement."* This explains the business reason. The right to the discount itself comes from the contract and the exception.

**Two limits:**
- The exception *"establishes commercial eligibility only."*
- The contract says the eligibility *"does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."* Any specific discount up to 15% still has to be approved by someone with enough authority.

For scale: the NS-500 list price on file is $250,000, so a full 15% would be about $37,500 per unit.

The knowledge base lists this customer as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). "Acme Manufacturing" is one of its other names.
