## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

All three IDs most likely refer to **Acme Mfg. Holdings** (also called "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing" or just "Acme"), but the knowledge base doesn't confirm it. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**

**The three IDs.** CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 aren't recorded anywhere in the knowledge base. Searches for each one, and for pieces like "2048", "1001" and "MFG", came back empty. I can't confirm from the graph which customer they belong to. The best match is Acme Mfg. Holdings, because its records come from the sources those ID formats suggest:
- the CRM accounts file (crm_accounts.csv), where CRM-numbers come from
- the customer master file (customers.csv), where C-numbers come from
- the Acme contract and exception documents, including a contract signed 2025-03-22, which fits "ACME-MFG-2025"

Acme Mfg. Holdings is a strategic account in industrial manufacturing. Its NS-500 controller discount limit was 10% of list price from April 2023 to March 2025. Under the Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception it is 15% from April 2025 to March 2028. That exception was prepared by Sarah Chen, approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, and replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10.

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is different:**
- **Different IDs:** it is recorded as **CRM-2091** and **C-1044** (also called "Acme Industrial"), so it doesn't share CRM-2048 or C-1001.
- **Different industry:** it's in industrial distribution, while Acme Mfg. Holdings is in industrial manufacturing.
- **Kept separate on purpose:** the two are stored as separate customers, and none of the Acme discount, contract or exception records apply to Acme Industrial Supply Co.

Because of the similar names, anything filed under "Acme" should be checked against the account ID before the manufacturing account's pricing is applied to it. To confirm the three IDs, you'd need the CRM, customer master or contract records themselves, since those identifiers never made it into the knowledge base.
