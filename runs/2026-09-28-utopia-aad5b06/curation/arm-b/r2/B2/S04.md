## Answer

Yes. Going by the knowledge base, Sarah Chen could have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23, and it wouldn't have needed anyone above her.

1. **The 2025 rules applied that day.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 ran from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. It let Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts "up to and including" 15%, and only discounts above 15% needed the VP Sales. So 15% was exactly her limit and still within it.
2. **Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive.** She also prepared the Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception.
3. **Acme could receive 15% on the NS-500.** The Strategic Account Exception sets Acme's maximum NS-500 discount at 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% limit (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ran from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The CRO, David Morgan, approved the exception on 2025-03-20, and the contract was signed on 2025-03-22. The contract doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority, but her own authority already covered 15% in 2025.

**The answer depends on the date.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 replaced the 2025 policy from 2026-01-01. It cut Enterprise Account Executive authority to 10%, and discounts above 10% up to 20% now need VP Sales approval. So the same 15% discount in 2026 would need Michael Torres (VP Sales), even though Acme is still eligible for it.

**One gap:** her Enterprise Account Executive title has no date on record. Her reporting line to Michael Torres is only recorded from 2026-09-01. I'm assuming she held the same role in September 2025, but nothing in the records confirms it for that date.
