## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's contractual maximum discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **15% off list price**.

- **Where it comes from:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (the knowledge base lists the company as "Acme Mfg. Holdings"). It says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* Acme signed it on 2025-03-22. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23.
- **Earlier limit, no longer in force:** a 2023 pricing exception allowed up to 10% off list price, but only for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The newer Strategic Account Exception, approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, replaces that 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10).
- **This isn't automatic approval:** the agreement says Acme's contractual eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." Each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies. So 15% is the most Acme can get under the contract, and any single discount up to that level still has to go through the normal approval process.

At a list price of $250,000, 15% would bring one NS-500 unit down to $212,500.
