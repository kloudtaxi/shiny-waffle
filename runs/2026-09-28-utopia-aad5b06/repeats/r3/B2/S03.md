> Can Sarah Chen approve 15% for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23. It's above her approval limit, and the Acme pricing exception that allows 15% covers a different product.

1. **Her role:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive and reports to Michael Torres, VP Sales.
2. **Her limit on that date is 10%.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaces the 2025 policy. It says: *"Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%."* The 2025 policy allowed up to 15%, which may be where the 15% idea comes from, but that policy no longer applies.
3. **The Acme exception doesn't cover this product.** Acme's master supply agreement and the strategic account exception only cover discounts of up to 15% on the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**. NS-Cloud (list price $120,000) is only mentioned as something Acme is "evaluating." The agreement also says the customer's eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." So even on the NS-500, 15% would still need someone with authority above 10%.

**What to do:** She can approve up to 10% herself. For 15%, the request has to go to someone with higher authority. The knowledge base doesn't give the approver for discounts above 10% under the 2026 policy. The Approval Authority Matrix – Enterprise Discounts (2026) is the reference for this. Her manager is Michael Torres (VP Sales), and David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) approved the earlier Acme exception.
