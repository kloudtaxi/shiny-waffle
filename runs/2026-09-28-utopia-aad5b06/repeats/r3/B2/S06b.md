## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **Where it comes from:** Acme's 2023 NS-500 pricing exception (acme_pricing_exception_2023.md) says: "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." 2024-10-01 is inside that period.
- **Why it isn't 15%:** Acme's master supply agreement does allow "discounts of up to 15%" on the NS-500. But Acme signed that agreement on 2025-03-22, after the date you asked about. Its pricing exception was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20 and supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which looks like the ID of the old 10% exception. The records don't give a start date for the 15%, but none of them put it before March 2025.

The NS-500 list price is $250,000, so 10% is at most $25,000 off per unit. The agreement also says any discount still needs approval under the company's internal policies, even within the contractual maximum.
