## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing gets up to 15% off the NS-500 because its 2025 Master Supply Agreement says so, and a newer pricing exception that replaced the old 10% one backs it up. (In the knowledge base, Acme's main record is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", customer C-1001.)

**Evidence**

1. **The Master Supply Agreement (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`)** is the direct source for the 15% figure:
   > "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
   
   Edward Floyd, Acme's VP Procurement, signed it on 2025-03-22.

2. **The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception (`acme_pricing_exception.md`)** is presumably the Schedule B the agreement refers to:
   - Sarah Chen prepared it and David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20.
   - It applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
   - It "supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."
   - The records I found from this document don't state the 15% figure themselves. That number comes from the agreement.

3. **Why it's 15% and not 10%.** The earlier exception (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`, EXC-ACME-NS500-10) said Acme "may receive up to **10%** off list price… for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." It expired on 2025-03-31, and the new exception took over from 2025-04-01. So 10% was the old rate and 15% is the current one.

4. **The reason behind it.** The Account Strategy 2026 document calls Acme "one of our strategic accounts" and says "Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement." The NS-500's list price is $250,000.

**Two things to keep in mind**

- **Eligibility isn't approval.** The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only." The agreement adds that eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies." Any actual 15% discount still has to go through normal approval.
- **One claim has been withdrawn.** The strategy document's statement that Acme "standardised on the NS-500 controller across two plants in 2025" has been marked as withdrawn in the knowledge base, so it shouldn't be used as supporting evidence.
