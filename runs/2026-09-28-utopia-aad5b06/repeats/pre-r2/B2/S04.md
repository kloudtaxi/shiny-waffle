## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

**Yes.** On 2025-09-23 a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme was allowed, and it was within Sarah Chen's own approval limit.

1. **Her approval limit.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive who reports to Michael Torres (VP Sales). The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force from 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31. It says: *"Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%."* The word "including" means 15% is within her limit.

2. **Acme's maximum discount.** The older 2023 exception capped Acme at 10% and only ran until 2025-03-31. A newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" replaced it for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. Sarah Chen prepared it, and the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan, approved it on 2025-03-20. Acme's supply agreement, signed 2025-03-22, says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller."* So 15% was within what Acme could receive.

3. **Internal approval was still needed.** The agreement also says Acme's eligibility *"does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."* On that date, the internal rule was the 2025 limit in point 1, which covered 15%.

Two caveats:
- **This shows she could approve it, not that she did.** I found no record of an Acme discount approved on 2025-09-23.
- **The answer is different for 2026.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated on or after 2026-01-01 and lowers the Account Executive limit to 10%. There is an NS-500 discount request dated 2026-09-23. If that date is the one you meant, Sarah Chen could not approve 15% herself, and it would have to go to a more senior approver. The knowledge base doesn't show who that would be.
