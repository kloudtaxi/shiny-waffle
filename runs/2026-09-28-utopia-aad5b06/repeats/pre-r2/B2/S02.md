## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No, not on what's on record.** 18% is more than Acme is entitled to on the NS-500.

- **The cap is 15%.** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (signed 2025-03-22) says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* That exception (approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20; prepared by Sarah Chen) replaced the old 10% exception for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. The 10% one ran from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so it's expired either way.
- **Getting approval doesn't raise the cap.** The same agreement says the contract terms *"do not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."* So even 15% or less still needs sign-off under the Pricing Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix.

**What you can do:**
1. Offer up to 15% and send it through the normal approval process, or
2. If you need 18%, get a new or amended pricing exception from someone with that authority before offering it. The current one was approved at Chief Revenue Officer level.

**Caveats:**
- I couldn't find the text of DR-9001 itself, apart from an email from Sarah Chen to Michael Torres titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001". I also couldn't see the approval matrix's actual thresholds, so I can't say who would have to approve 15% or 18%.
- The discount log shows an 18% NS-500 discount (DR-7015) approved by Michael Torres, but that was for a different account (CRM-3007) on 2025-12-05, under the 2025 policy. It doesn't apply to Acme. The system also has that entry wrongly dated 2026-09-23, so don't read it as a 23 September approval.
