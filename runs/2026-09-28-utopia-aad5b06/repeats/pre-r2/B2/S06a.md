## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing (listed in the knowledge base as "Acme Mfg. Holdings") could contractually get **at most 15% off** the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

- **Where the 15% comes from:** The Master Supply Agreement (acme_master_supply_agreement.md) says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* Acme's VP Procurement, Edward Floyd, signed it on 2025-03-22.
- **It covered that date:** The agreement is recorded as effective from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028.
- **The older 10% cap no longer applied:** A 2023 pricing exception allowed up to 10% off list for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That window had closed by 2026, and the newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" is recorded as replacing that 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10).

The 15% is a ceiling Acme is eligible for, not an automatic discount. The agreement says Acme's contractual pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority," and each discount still has to be approved under Northstar's internal policies.

At the NS-500's recorded list price of $250,000, 15% works out to $37,500 per unit, if that list price applies.
