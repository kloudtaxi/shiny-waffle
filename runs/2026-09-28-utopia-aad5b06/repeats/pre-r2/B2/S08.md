## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

I can't confirm from the knowledge base that those three IDs belong to one customer. The records point to Acme Mfg. Holdings, but that's my inference, not a recorded link. Acme Industrial Supply Co. is recorded as a different company.

**The three IDs**
- **CRM-2048** and **ACME-MFG-2025** aren't in the knowledge graph at all. Other CRM numbers are (CRM-2051, CRM-2063, CRM-3002 and others), but not 2048. Beyond ACME-MFG-2025 itself, the only Acme-style codes on record are ACME-SA-2023 and EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- **C-1001** exists, but its only link is to sales order SO-7002, dated 2026-08-18. Nothing connects it to Acme Mfg. Holdings, even through up to three steps.

**Why I think they're Acme Mfg. Holdings.** Acme Mfg. Holdings is also recorded as "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing" and "Acme". Its facts come from `crm_accounts.csv` and `customers.csv`, among other files. Given the ID formats, CRM-2048 would likely be its CRM account number, C-1001 its customer master ID, and ACME-MFG-2025 a 2025 agreement or account code. That last guess fits the 2025 records: an NS-500 pricing agreement signed 2025-03-22, and the NS-500 strategic account exception approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20. I haven't seen the underlying rows, so check the CRM and customer master before relying on this.

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer.** It has its own entry, sourced from the same two CSV files, and nothing merges it with Acme Mfg. Holdings:
- **Acme Mfg. Holdings:** industry is Industrial Manufacturing. It's a strategic account standardised on the NS-500 controller, with negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing, and it's evaluating NS-CLOUD.
- **Acme Industrial Supply Co.:** industry is Industrial Distribution. That's the only fact recorded, so it has none of the strategic-account status, NS-500 pricing or discount exception.

There's also a bare "Acme Industrial" entry with no facts or links. It shouldn't be treated as either company without more evidence.

In practice, Acme Mfg. Holdings' NS-500 discount terms shouldn't be applied to Acme Industrial Supply Co.
