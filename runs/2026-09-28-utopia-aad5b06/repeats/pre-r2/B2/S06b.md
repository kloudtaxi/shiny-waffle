## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

**The maximum was 10% off list price** on 2024-10-01.

- **Source of the 10%:** The 2023 NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`) says: "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." The date you asked about falls inside that window.
- **Why it isn't 15%:** The newer master supply agreement allows "discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller." That deal came later: the new Strategic Account Exception was approved on 2025-03-20 and replaced the old one (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), and Acme signed the agreement on 2025-03-22. The records don't give the exact day the 15% took effect, but both dates are after 2024-10-01, so it doesn't apply to your date.
- **Limit of the contract:** This is only the most Acme can get under the contract. The agreement says that eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority," so each discount still needs approval under Northstar's own policies.

Acme appears in the records as "Acme Mfg. Holdings," with "Acme Manufacturing" as another name for it. NS-500 list price is $250,000, so a 10% discount is at most $25,000 per unit.
