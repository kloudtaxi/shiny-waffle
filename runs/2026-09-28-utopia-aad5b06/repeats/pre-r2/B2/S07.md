# Why Acme Manufacturing is eligible for 15% off the NS-500

Acme is eligible because its 2025 Master Supply Agreement says so. That agreement points to a strategic-account pricing exception, which replaced an older 10% exception. Being eligible does not mean the discount is automatically approved.

(In the knowledge base, Acme Manufacturing is stored as **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, customer C-1001, Industrial Manufacturing, Milwaukee WI.)

## The evidence

**1. The contract: `acme_master_supply_agreement.md`**
> "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."

- Edward Floyd, Acme's VP of Procurement, signed it on 2025-03-22.

**2. The pricing exception: `acme_pricing_exception.md` ("Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception")**
- **Approved by:** David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20.
- **Prepared by:** Sarah Chen.
- **Scope:** "This exception applies only to the product identified above", which is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **What it replaces:** "This exception supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."
- I couldn't find the 15% figure recorded from this document itself. The number comes from the agreement's reference to Schedule B.

**3. The earlier 10% exception: `acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`**
> "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."

- This explains the switch: the 10% limit covered orders up to 2025-03-31, and the 15% exception applies to requests from 2025-04-01 onward.

**4. The business reason: `acme_account_strategy.md`**
- "Acme is one of our strategic accounts."
- "They standardised on the NS-500 controller across two plants in 2025."
- "Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement."
- They are also evaluating NS-Cloud for plant-floor analytics.

## Eligibility is not approval
Both documents say that 15% is the most Acme can get, and each discount still needs internal approval:
- The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only."
- The agreement says: "Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

So any NS-500 discount for Acme (list price $250,000) can be up to 15%, but it still has to go through the normal approval process.
