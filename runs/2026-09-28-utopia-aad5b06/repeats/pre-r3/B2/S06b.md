## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

That limit comes from the 2023 pricing exception (acme_pricing_exception_2023.md): *"Acme Manufacturing may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."* 2024-10-01 falls inside that period. In the knowledge base, Acme Manufacturing is recorded as Acme Mfg. Holdings.

The 15% figure is a different contract and doesn't apply to that date. The master supply agreement (acme_master_supply_agreement.md) says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15%" on the NS-500, but it was signed on 2025-03-22. It replaced the 2023 exception after 2024-10-01. The knowledge base has no start date for it, so I'm assuming it took effect around 2025-04-01, when the 2023 exception ended.

Also, even the contractual discount is still subject to Northstar's internal approvals. The agreement says Acme's contractual pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
