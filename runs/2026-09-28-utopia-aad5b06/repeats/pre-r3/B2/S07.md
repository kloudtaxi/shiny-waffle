## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme is eligible because a 2025 pricing exception replaced its earlier 10% deal, and the signed supply contract states the 15% ceiling directly. That makes Acme eligible, but it doesn't mean a 15% discount is already approved. (The records list Acme as "Acme Mfg. Holdings", also known as Acme Manufacturing.)

**Why Acme qualifies**
1. **It's a strategic account.** The 2026 account strategy (`acme_account_strategy.md`) says: *"Acme is one of our strategic accounts."* It adds that Acme *"standardised on the NS-500 controller across two plants in 2025"* and *"negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement."*
2. **A strategic-account exception replaced the old 10% deal.** Under the 2023 exception (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`, EXC-ACME-NS500-10), Acme *"may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."* The newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (`acme_pricing_exception.md`) *"supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01"* and *"applies only to the product identified above"*, which is the NS-500. Sarah Chen prepared it, and David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20.
3. **The contract sets the 15% ceiling.** The Master Supply Agreement (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`), which Acme signed on 2025-03-22, says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* The records don't show the exception document's own discount figure; the 15% comes from this contract clause.

**Limits on the discount**
- **It's a maximum, not a set rate.** Acme can get *up to* 15% off the NS-500 list price of $250,000.
- **It confirms eligibility only.** The exception says it *"establishes commercial eligibility only."*
- **Each discount still needs approval.** The contract says the eligibility *"does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."*
- **The old 10% cap only applies to earlier requests.** Requests dated before 2025-04-01 fall under the 2023 exception.

There are open NS-500 discount requests on record (CRM-3002 from 2026-03-04, CRM-3004 from 2026-07-12, and one from 2026-09-23). Any discount on these can be up to 15%, but each still has to go through internal approval.
