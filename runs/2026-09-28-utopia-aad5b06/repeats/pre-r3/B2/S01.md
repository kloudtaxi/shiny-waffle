## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. Acme can receive a 15% discount on the NS-500, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself on 2026-09-23.

**Acme qualifies for 15%:**
- Acme's master supply agreement says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller."
- The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception, prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by CRO David Morgan on 2025-03-20, raised the limit to 15%. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests dated 2025-04-01 or later.

**Sarah's approval limit is 10%:**
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive who reports to Michael Torres, VP Sales.
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated from 2026-01-01. It says: "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%."
- The 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%, but it no longer applies to a request dated 2026-09-23.

**The exception doesn't change this:**
- The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only."
- The supply agreement says the same: "Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

So Acme is entitled to up to 15%, but a 15% discount is above Sarah's 10% limit and someone higher up must approve it. I couldn't find the part of the 2026 policy or the Approval Authority Matrix that names the approver above 10%. The next level up from Sarah is Michael Torres (VP Sales), and above him David Morgan (CRO), so check the matrix to see which of them is required.
