## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve 15% for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23.

1. **Her limit is 10%.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in effect on 2026-09-23 (it runs 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and says: *"Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%."* 15% is over that limit.

2. **Acme's 15% eligibility doesn't cover NS-Cloud.** Acme's master supply agreement allows up to 15% only on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. The Acme Strategic Account Exception says it *"applies only to the product identified above"*, which is the NS-500. The NS-Cloud Operations Suite (PROD-002, list price $120,000) is a different product. Acme's account strategy only says the customer is "evaluating NS-Cloud", and there's nothing on record giving Acme special pricing on it.

3. **Even on the NS-500, eligibility wouldn't give her the authority.** The exception says it *"establishes commercial eligibility only."* The supply agreement says Acme's pricing eligibility *"does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."* The exception itself was approved by David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer. Sarah only prepared it.

A 15% request would have to go to someone above her. I couldn't find the tiers above 10% in the knowledge base. Her manager is Michael Torres (VP Sales), and above him is David Morgan (CRO), but I can't confirm which of them can sign off on 15%. The Approval Authority Matrix – Enterprise Discounts should say who needs to approve it.
