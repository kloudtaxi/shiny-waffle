## Answer

**C-1001 is Acme Mfg. Holdings. Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer. I couldn't find CRM-2048 or ACME-MFG-2025 in the knowledge base, so I can't confirm what they refer to.**

**C-1001:** The customer master file (customers.csv) lists it as *"C-1001 Acme Mfg. Holdings, Industrial Manufacturing, 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee WI 53209, Net 45"*. The same customer appears in other records as "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing" or just "Acme". It is a strategic account that standardised on the NS-500 controller. It had a 10% NS-500 pricing exception from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, followed by a supply agreement signed 2025-03-22 that allows up to 15%.

**CRM-2048 and ACME-MFG-2025:** Neither appears anywhere in the knowledge base, even by partial search.
- Other CRM numbers exist (CRM-2051, CRM-2063, CRM-3002 to CRM-3006), but none is linked to Acme.
- The source file crm_accounts.csv did say "Acme Manufacturing", but that name link was later withdrawn, and no CRM account number is recorded against it.
- ACME-MFG-2025 might be the reference for the 2025 supply agreement, but that's only a guess from its format. Nothing on record says so.

To confirm either one, the original CRM export or the agreement document would need to show it.

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer:**

| | Acme Mfg. Holdings | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| Customer ID | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| Industry | Industrial Manufacturing | Industrial Distribution |
| Location | Milwaukee, WI | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310 |
| Phone | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |

They are separate records with nothing linking them, and none of Acme Mfg. Holdings' NS-500 pricing terms apply to Acme Industrial Supply Co. There is also a bare "Acme Industrial" record with no data attached, so it can't be matched to either one.
