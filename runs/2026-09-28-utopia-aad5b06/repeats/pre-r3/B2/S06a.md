## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller is **15% off list price**.

- **Where the 15% comes from:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement, signed 2025-03-22, says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
- **What it replaced:** That exception (the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception," approved by David Morgan, CRO, on 2025-03-20) replaces the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. The earlier one allowed up to 10% off list, for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so it no longer applies.
- **Dates:** The knowledge base gives no end date for the 15% terms, and I found nothing that changes or withdraws them. So they still appear to be in force on 2026-09-23.
- **Approval still needed:** Both the agreement and the exception say they only set what Acme is eligible for. They don't change Northstar's internal approval authority, so each discount still has to be approved under internal policy.

At the NS-500's $250,000 list price, 15% is $37,500 off, for a net price of $212,500.
