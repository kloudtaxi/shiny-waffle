## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No. Acme's agreement lets you go up to 15% on the NS-500, so 18% is 3 points over the limit.**

- **The Acme agreement sets 15% as the most you can give.** The Master Supply Agreement (signed 2025-03-22, effective 2025-04-01 to March 31, 2028) says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* That covers 2026-09-23.
- **The exception it points to is the current one.** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" was approved by David Morgan (CRO) on 2025-03-20. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ran 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31) for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. The 10% limit no longer applies, but nothing raises the limit past 15%.
- **The exception only says what Acme can get; it doesn't approve anything.** It says it *"establishes commercial eligibility only."* The agreement adds that this *"does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."* So even an in-range discount still needs sign-off under the Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix.
- **Don't treat other 18% deals as a precedent.** An 18% NS-500 discount was approved by Michael Torres, VP Sales, in 2025, but that was DR-7015 for a different account (CRM-3007), not Acme.

The options are:
1. Offer up to 15%, which is within the agreement, with the normal approval.
2. Treat 18% as a new exception or an amendment, the same way David Morgan approved the current one.

I couldn't find the DR-9001 record itself or the approval thresholds by percentage in the matrix and policy, so I can't say who would need to approve which level.
