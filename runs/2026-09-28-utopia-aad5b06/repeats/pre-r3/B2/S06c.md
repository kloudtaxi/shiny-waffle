## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2022-10-01?

## Answer

I've looked through Acme's pricing records and the relevant documents. There's no contractual NS-500 discount on record for Acme Manufacturing on 2022-10-01, so I can't give you a maximum for that date.

The knowledge base has only two NS-500 discount terms for Acme (recorded under the name "Acme Mfg. Holdings"), and neither covers that date:

1. **NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception, up to 10% off list price.** This applies only to orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, which is six months after the date you asked about. Source: `acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`.
2. **Master Supply Agreement, up to 15%.** The agreement says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B." It was signed on 2025-03-22, so it doesn't apply to 2022 either. The agreement also says this eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval process: each discount still needs approval under internal policy. Source: `acme_master_supply_agreement.md`.

For reference, the NS-500 list price is $250,000. If Acme had an agreement before 2023, it hasn't been loaded into the knowledge base.
