## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing (listed in the records as "Acme Mfg. Holdings", customer C-1001) can get up to 15% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller because of its 2025 Master Supply Agreement. That agreement relies on a pricing exception, approved by the Chief Revenue Officer (CRO), that replaced the earlier 10% one. The 15% is a ceiling on what Acme may be offered, not an automatic discount, and each discount still needs internal approval.

**Evidence**

1. **The contract clause** (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`):
   > "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
   
   Edward Floyd, Acme's VP Procurement, signed it on 2025-03-22.

2. **The pricing exception it refers to** (`acme_pricing_exception.md`, "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception"):
   - Sarah Chen prepared it.
   - It was approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20.
   - It "applies only to the product identified above", meaning the NS-500.
   - It "supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."

3. **Why the older 10% no longer applies** (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`): the earlier exception allowed "up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." It ran out the day before the new exception took effect, so 15% is the current ceiling.

4. **Account background** (`acme_account_strategy.md`): Acme is described as "one of our strategic accounts" that "negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement."

**Limits on the 15%**
- The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only."
- The contract says: "Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

So each specific discount request for Acme still has to go through the normal approval process under the "Approval Authority Matrix — Enterprise Discounts." The knowledge base doesn't record that matrix's thresholds, so I can't say who has to sign off on a 15% discount. For scale, the NS-500's list price is $250,000, so 15% would be up to $37,500 off per unit.
