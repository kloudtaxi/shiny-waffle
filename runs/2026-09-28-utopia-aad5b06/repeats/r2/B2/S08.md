The IDs that do exist nearby are CRM-2051, CRM-2063, C-1002, C-1003 and ACME-SA-2023. So I can't confirm from the records which customer these three belong to.

**Best guess: Acme Mfg. Holdings.** It is the only Acme customer that fits a "MFG" ID. It is also known as "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing" and "Acme", and its industry is Industrial Manufacturing. The records say it is a strategic account, that it negotiated preferential pricing on the NS-500 controller, and that it signed an agreement on 2025-03-22. That agreement date matches the "2025" in ACME-MFG-2025. But nothing in the records ties the three IDs to this customer, so treat this as unconfirmed.

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer.** The knowledge base keeps it as its own record:
- **Its own IDs:** CRM-2091 and C-1044. It is also called "Acme Industrial".
- **A different industry:** Industrial Distribution, not Industrial Manufacturing.
- **No links to the other Acme's data:** none to the NS-500 pricing, the strategic-account status or the 2025 agreement.

The two records have never been merged or treated as the same company. The name "Acme" alone isn't enough to decide which customer something refers to. Check the CRM or customer ID, and don't mix CRM-2091 or C-1044 with the manufacturing customer's records. To confirm the three IDs, check the source files `crm_accounts.csv` and `customers.csv` directly.
