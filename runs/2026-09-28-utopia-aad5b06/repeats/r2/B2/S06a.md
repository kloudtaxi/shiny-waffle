## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **15%** off list price.

- **Source:** The Master Supply Agreement (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`), which Acme signed on 2025-03-22, says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* The knowledge base shows no end date and no later correction for this clause.
- **The older 10% cap no longer applies:** An earlier 2023 pricing exception allowed up to 10% off list price for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That period ended before 2026-09-23. The newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20 and replaces the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- **Approval is still needed:** The agreement says Acme's contractual eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." Every discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies, so 15% is the most the contract allows. It doesn't mean a 15% discount is automatically approved.

The knowledge base doesn't record when Schedule B expires. The 15% cap holds on 2026-09-23 only if nothing outside the knowledge base ended it.
