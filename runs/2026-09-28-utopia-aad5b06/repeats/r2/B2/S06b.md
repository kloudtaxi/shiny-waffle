## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

**It was 10% off list price.** On 2024-10-01, the 2023 NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception was the one in effect.

- **The 2023 exception** (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`) says: "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." 2024-10-01 falls inside that period.
- **The 15% limit came later, so it doesn't apply on that date.** The master supply agreement allows "discounts of up to 15%" through a new Strategic Account Exception. That exception was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, replaced the old 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), and the agreement was signed on 2025-03-22. The knowledge base doesn't give an exact start date for the 15% terms, but the old 10% exception ran until 2025-03-31, so they likely took over after that.

At the NS-500 list price of $250,000, 10% is at most $25,000 off per unit. The agreement also says the contract discount "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority," so each discount still needs internal approval.
