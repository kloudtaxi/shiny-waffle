- **The rules in force then:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 covered all of 2025. It is the policy that says Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts, with the limit missing. The 2026 approval matrix has the same gap.

Holdings (also called "Acme Manufacturing") eligible for NS-500 discounts of up to what appears to be 15%. That figure is my inference: a "15%" entry exists but isn't formally linked to the contract. - **Precedent:** Sarah prepared the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception," but David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20. That exception replaced an older one (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which by its name looks like 10%). Acme's earlier NS-500 discount eligibility ran from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That Acme's special pricing needed CRO sign-off suggests it was above what an account executive could approve alone. This is an inference, not a stated rule.

**Check the date:** I found no NS-500 discount request for Acme on 2025-09-23. There is an NS-500 discount request dated **2026-09-23**, and a Sarah-to-Michael email about "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001." If you meant 2026, the 2026 pricing policy applies instead, and its limits are missing too.

**Bottom line:** She could approve it only if the 2025 limit for Enterprise Account Executives was at least 15%. The CRO having to approve Acme's exception suggests it wasn't. The source that would settle it is the 2025 pricing policy document itself, or the Approval Authority Matrix if you mean 2026.
