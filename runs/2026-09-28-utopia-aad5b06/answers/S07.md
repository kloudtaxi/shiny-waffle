## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing is eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller due to a pricing exception established in their Master Supply Agreement, effective from April 1, 2025, to March 31, 2028. This exception specifically applies to the NS-500 product only. 

The evidence for this discount can be referenced in the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" document, which states:

- **Maximum eligible discount:** 15% of list price
- **Effective Date:** 2025-04-01
- **Expiration Date:** 2028-03-31

This exception supersedes an earlier pricing exception that allowed for a maximum discount of 10% [1].
