## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

I can't confirm it either way. The knowledge base doesn't record the discount limit that Sarah Chen's role is allowed to approve. What it does show points to **probably not on her own authority**, but that's my inference, not a documented answer.

**What the records show**
- **Sarah Chen's role:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. The 2025 and 2026 pricing policies both say Enterprise Account Executives "may approve discounts", but the percentage limit isn't recorded in either one.
- **2026 policy applies:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in effect for all of 2026, so it covers 23 Sep 2026. It replaced the 2025 policy. It points to the "Approval Authority Matrix" as the reference for approval limits, but the matrix's actual limits aren't recorded either.
- **Acme's special pricing:** Acme Manufacturing (recorded as "Acme Mfg. Holdings") is a strategic account. There is an "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" for the NS-500:
  - Sarah Chen prepared it, and David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 20 Mar 2025. Acme signed on 22 Mar 2025.
  - It replaces an earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
  - It makes Acme "eligible for discounts of up to" a certain level on the NS-500, but the percentage wasn't captured, so I can't tell whether 15% is within it.
  - An older arrangement letting Acme receive special NS-500 pricing expired on 31 Mar 2025.
- **Why it's probably not her call:** The Acme agreement explicitly "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." So even if Acme can receive 15%, that doesn't let Sarah approve it. Her own limit under the Approval Authority Matrix still decides, and when Acme's current exception was approved, the approver was the Chief Revenue Officer, not Sarah.
- **The request itself:** A discount request on the NS-500 is recorded for 23 Sep 2026, but its percentage, requester and outcome aren't recorded.

**What you'd need to check to settle it**
1. In the Approval Authority Matrix (Enterprise Discounts), the maximum discount an Enterprise Account Executive can approve under the 2026 policy.
2. In the Acme Strategic Account Exception, the maximum discount it allows. If 15% is above that, a new exception is needed regardless of who approves.

If 15% is above the Account Executive limit, the Sales Discount Requests procedure sends it to a higher approver. Who that is depends on the matrix, possibly the VP of Sales (Michael Torres) or the Chief Revenue Officer (David Morgan).
