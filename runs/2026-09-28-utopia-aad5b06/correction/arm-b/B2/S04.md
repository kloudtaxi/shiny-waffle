## Answer

I can't confirm it either way from the knowledge base. The answer depends on the approval limits for Sarah Chen's role and on how large a discount Acme was allowed, and neither number made it into the knowledge base.

**What the records show:**
- **Sarah Chen's role:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive and reports to Michael Torres, the VP Sales. The records say Enterprise Account Executives "may approve discounts," but they don't give a percentage cap. There is an "Approval Authority Matrix — Enterprise Discounts" and a discount-request SOP that uses the matrix to decide who has to approve. Neither record holds the actual limits.
- **Her link to the Acme deal:** She **prepared** the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception." She didn't approve it. **David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20.** It replaces an earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which may have been a 10% exception, though the records don't say. The new exception sets Acme's eligibility for NS-500 discounts, but the maximum percentage wasn't recorded.
- **Acme's eligibility over time:** An earlier arrangement let Acme get NS-500 pricing from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. Acme signed a new agreement on 2025-03-22, and that agreement "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." So on 2025-09-23, the special pricing still wouldn't change who inside the company can sign off.
- **The date:** I found no Acme NS-500 discount request or approval dated **2025-09-23**. There is an NS-500 discount request dated **2026-09-23**, so the year in your question may be off by one.

**What this suggests:** Special Acme exceptions have gone to the Chief Revenue Officer, one level above the VP Sales Chen reports to. That makes it unlikely that a 15% discount was hers to approve alone, but I can't prove it without her role's percentage limit.

To settle it, check two numbers in the Approval Authority Matrix: the highest discount an Enterprise Account Executive can approve, and Acme's NS-500 cap under the exception. If you tell me whether you meant 2025 or 2026, I can look up the specific request.
