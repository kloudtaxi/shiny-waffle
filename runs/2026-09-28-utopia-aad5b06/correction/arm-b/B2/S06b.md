## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

Acme's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01 was **10% off list price**.

- **The 10% cap applied on that date.** The "NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception" (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`) says: *"Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."* 2024-10-01 falls inside that window.
- **The 15% cap came later.** The Master Supply Agreement (`acme_master_supply_agreement.md`) says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller." Acme signed it on 2025-03-22 and it took effect on 2025-04-01. The exception tied to it also replaced the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10. So 15% doesn't apply to your date.

This is the most the contract allows, not an approved discount. The agreement says it "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority," so each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies.

At the NS-500's $250,000 list price, 10% works out to at most $25,000 off per unit.
