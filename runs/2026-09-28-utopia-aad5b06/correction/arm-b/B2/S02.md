## Answer

I can't give you a yes or no. The knowledge base records the Acme approval chain, but the key numbers weren't captured: Acme's discount cap on the NS-500 and the approval thresholds by discount size.

**What the records show:**
- **Acme has an NS-500 discount entitlement, but its size is missing.** Acme Manufacturing (listed as Acme Mfg. Holdings) is marked as a strategic account. Its contract says it "is eligible for discounts of up to" a set amount on the NS-500, but the percentage itself was dropped when the document was loaded.
- **The current exception replaced an older one.** An earlier arrangement, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, applied from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The ID suggests it was 10%, but that's only my guess. It was replaced by the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception", which Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive) prepared. David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) approved it on 2025-03-20. Northstar signed on 2025-03-20 and Acme on 2025-03-22. The exception has no recorded end date.
- **The contract doesn't change who can approve discounts.** It states that it "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority". So even if 18% is within Acme's cap, the request still needs sign-off from someone authorised for that discount level.
- **The 2026 pricing policy covers 2026-09-23.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaces the 2025 policy. The Approval Authority Matrix for enterprise discounts is the working reference for approval limits, but its actual limits aren't stored.
- **DR-9001 exists but has no details.** It appears only as the subject of an email from Sarah Chen to Michael Torres (VP Sales), "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001". A discount request on the NS-500 is logged for 2026-09-23, but without its percentage or status.

**To settle it, check three things in the source documents:**
1. The "up to X%" cap in the Acme contract amendment or strategic account exception. If X is below 18%, the extra discount needs a new exception.
2. The limit for 18% in the Approval Authority Matrix under Pricing Policy 2026. That shows whether Sarah Chen, Michael Torres or David Morgan has to approve it.
3. Whether DR-9001 was actually approved, from the discount request log or the email.

The NS-500 lists at $250,000, so 18% would be a $45,000 discount, for a net price of $205,000.
