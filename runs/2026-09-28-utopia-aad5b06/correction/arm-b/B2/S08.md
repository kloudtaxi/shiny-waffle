## Answer

I've gone through the knowledge base's entries for the Acme accounts and am writing up what I found.

**CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025:** I can't confirm these IDs. None of them is recorded anywhere in the knowledge base, not even as an alias. The likely match is **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, but that comes from which source files each Acme record draws on, not from the IDs themselves. The records that name this customer come from:
- `crm_accounts.csv`, the CRM account list, where it appears as "Acme Manufacturing"
- the customer master file, which classifies it as Industrial Manufacturing
- a customer agreement signed 2025-03-22, which could explain an ID like "ACME-MFG-2025"
- `acme_account_strategy.md` and related documents, which also call it "Acme", "ACME Manufacturing" and "Customer"

About this customer: it's a strategic account that negotiated preferential pricing on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It is evaluating NS-CLOUD and is linked to sales order SO-7002 (2026-08-18).

**Is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?** No. The knowledge base treats it as a separate company:
- **Different IDs:** it is recorded as **CRM-2091** in the CRM and **C-1044** in the customer file, and is also called "Acme Industrial".
- **Different industry:** it is classified as Industrial Distribution, while Acme Mfg. Holdings is Industrial Manufacturing.
- **No connection:** nothing links the two within three steps in the knowledge base, and none of the recorded merges or corrections combines them.

This matters for pricing because the NS-500 discount terms and strategic-account status belong only to Acme Mfg. Holdings. They shouldn't be applied to Acme Industrial Supply Co. just because the names are similar.

To be certain that CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 are Acme Mfg. Holdings, look them up directly in `crm_accounts.csv`, the customer master file and the contract. The knowledge base didn't record those IDs, so I couldn't check them.
