> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

The most Acme Manufacturing could get contractually on the NS-500 on 2026-09-23 was **15% off list price**.

- **Where the 15% comes from:** The Master Supply Agreement says: "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B." Acme's VP of Procurement, Edward Floyd, signed it on 2025-03-22. (In the records Acme appears as "Acme Mfg. Holdings", customer C-1001.)
- **What it replaced:** The earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, capped the discount at 10%. It covered orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The new Strategic Account Exception replaces it for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20, and Sarah Chen prepared it.
- **Whether it still held on 2026-09-23:** Nothing on file gives the 15% exception an end date, and nothing shows it was withdrawn or corrected. I'm assuming it was still in force, but the records don't confirm how long the agreement lasts.

The 15% is only a ceiling that Acme qualifies for. The contract says it "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority", so each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies. The records also show an NS-500 discount request dated 2026-09-23, but they don't say whether it was approved.
