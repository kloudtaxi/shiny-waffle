## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing (listed in the knowledge base as **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, customer C‑1001) can get up to 15% off the NS‑500 because its 2025 Master Supply Agreement says so. That agreement points to a strategic‑account pricing exception, which replaced an older 10% exception. The 15% is a ceiling, not an automatic discount: each discount still needs internal approval.

**Evidence**

1. **The contract gives the 15%.** From *acme_master_supply_agreement.md*:
   > "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS‑500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."

   Acme signed it on 2025‑03‑22; Edward Floyd, VP Procurement, is quoted as the signatory.

2. **The pricing exception it points to.** *acme_pricing_exception.md* is recorded as the "Acme NS‑500 Strategic Account Exception":
   - It was prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025‑03‑20.
   - It applies only to the NS‑500 ("applies only to the product identified above").
   - It "supersedes EXC‑ACME‑NS500‑10 for requests dated on or after 2025‑04‑01."
   - The records I found from this file don't state the 15% figure themselves. That figure comes from the contract in point 1.

3. **It replaced an older 10% exception.** *acme_pricing_exception_2023.md* says Acme "may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS‑500… for orders placed from 2023‑04‑01 to 2025‑03‑31." It expired the day before the new exception took over. So requests from 2025‑04‑01 onward come under the 15% ceiling, and earlier ones under 10%.

4. **The business reason.** *acme_account_strategy.md* says "Acme is one of our strategic accounts" and "Acme negotiated preferential NS‑500 pricing in the 2025 agreement." A line in that file saying Acme standardised on the NS‑500 across two plants in 2025 was later rejected, so I've left it out.

**Limits on the 15%**
- The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only."
- The contract says: "Customer's contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority. Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

So Acme may be offered up to 15%, but each NS‑500 discount request still goes through the normal approval process. For scale, the NS‑500 list price is $250,000. Also, "Acme Industrial Supply Co." is a different customer, and this exception doesn't cover it.
