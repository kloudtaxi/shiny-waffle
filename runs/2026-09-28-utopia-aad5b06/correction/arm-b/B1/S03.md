## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23. It needs Michael Torres, VP Sales, to approve it.**

1. **Her authority is too low.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive who reports to Michael Torres. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Under it, Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales approval. The Approval Authority Matrix says the same and adds that approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
2. **Acme's 15% deal doesn't cover this product.** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, in force 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) does include NS-Cloud. But its 15% strategic pricing (clause 3.2) applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller, through exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. That exception is for the NS-500 only and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." So there is no contract basis for 15% on NS-Cloud.
3. **Even on the NS-500, the contract wouldn't give her authority.** Clause 3.3 of the agreement and the exception's own authority section both say that what Acme is entitled to under the contract doesn't change who inside Northstar can approve it. The sales discount procedure (SOP-SALES-014) says the same thing: "Contractual eligibility does not change employee authority."

The 15% Acme discount Sarah approved herself in September 2025 (request DR-8104, for the NS-500) isn't a precedent. The 2025 policy then let Account Executives approve up to 15%, and the 2026 policy fully replaced it for requests from 2026-01-01.

Sarah's email to Michael on 2026-09-23 (logged as request DR-9001) asks for 15% on the **NS-500** for Acme's third plant, not NS-Cloud. If that's the request you mean, the discount is within Acme's contract, but it still needs Michael's approval, recorded in CRM. If Acme really wants 15% on NS-Cloud, there's no contract term supporting it, so Michael would be approving a standard discount on its merits.
