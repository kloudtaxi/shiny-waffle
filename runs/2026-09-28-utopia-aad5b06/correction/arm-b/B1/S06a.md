## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing could get at most **15% off list price** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller under its contract.

**Where the 15% comes from:**
- Section 3.2 of Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) allows discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500. The agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Acme Manufacturing is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings.
- Schedule B, pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, sets the maximum at 15% of list price over the same dates. It covers the NS-500 only.
- This exception replaced the earlier 10% one (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. The 10% one covered orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so it didn't apply on 2026-09-23.

**The 15% doesn't approve a discount by itself.** Section 3.3 of the agreement says each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies. The exception also says it only sets what Acme is eligible for and gives no Northstar employee authority to approve it.

This matters for DR-9001. Sarah Chen logged that request on 2026-09-23 for 15% off a $250,000 NS-500 order, and it is still "Pending Approval." The 15% is within the contract limit, but someone with the right authority still has to approve it.
