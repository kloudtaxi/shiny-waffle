> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

I've read the Acme agreement, its pricing exception and the 2026 pricing policy; here's the answer.

**Not under Acme's contract. The contract caps the NS-500 discount at 15%, and even 15% needs VP Sales approval.**

1. **Right customer and dates.** DR-9001 is on CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing. That's the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, not the separate Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091). The request is dated 2026-09-23 and is for the NS-500. Acme's supply agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on that date.
2. **The contract stops at 15%.** The agreement (section 3.2) and its pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) set the maximum discount at 15% of list price, for the NS-500 only. The older 10% exception it replaced doesn't apply to this request. I found no amendment or other document that allows more than 15%.
3. **18% isn't what was logged.** DR-9001 is recorded at 15% ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net) and is "Pending Approval." Sarah's 2026-09-23 email also says Acme asked for 15%, the same as last September.
4. **Who has to approve.** Under the 2026 Pricing Policy, account executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Both the agreement (section 3.3) and the sales discount procedure say the contract doesn't change who can approve. So Sarah can't approve this herself, unlike last year's DR-8104, which she could approve under the 2025 policy's 15% limit.

**Bottom line:**
- **15%:** allowed by the contract, but it needs VP Sales approval recorded in the CRM before you quote it.
- **18%:** the 2026 policy would put an 18% approval with VP Sales, but the contract doesn't justify it. The extra 3 points would need a new justification, and the request would have to be resubmitted at 18%. Nothing on file supports going above 15% for Acme.
