> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

All three IDs refer to the same customer: **Acme Manufacturing**, whose legal name is **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a **different customer**.

**The three IDs**
- **CRM-2048** is the CRM account "Acme Manufacturing." It's a Strategic account in the Midwest Enterprise segment, owned by Sarah Chen. Address: 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI. DUNS 04-812-7730.
- **C-1001** is the ERP bill-to customer "Acme Mfg. Holdings." It has the same Milwaukee address and the same DUNS number, 04-812-7730. Payment terms are Net 45.
- **ACME-MFG-2025** is the Master Supply Agreement between Northstar Industrial Systems and "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing," at that same Milwaukee address. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its Schedule B is the NS-500 pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, which allows up to 15% off list price.

Sarah Chen's 2026 account plan also links two of them directly: "CRM account: CRM-2048 … ERP bill-to: Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001)."

**Acme Industrial Supply Co.** is a separate company:
- Its IDs are CRM-2091 in the CRM and C-1044 in the ERP.
- It's in Akron, OH (17 Tallmadge Ave), not Milwaukee.
- Its DUNS number is 09-552-1187, not 04-812-7730.
- It's in Industrial Distribution, not Industrial Manufacturing, and it's a Standard account in the Midwest Mid-Market segment.
- It has its own support history. Service request SR-40418 (Sept 2026, about an NS-Edge monitoring package at the Akron site) was logged under "Acme Industrial (CRM-2091)."

The two only share "Acme" in their names. Don't apply the Acme Manufacturing agreement or its NS-500 discount to Acme Industrial Supply Co.
