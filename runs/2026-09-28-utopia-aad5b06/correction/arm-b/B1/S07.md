## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme can get up to 15% off the NS-500 because its current contract with Northstar says so, and a pricing exception attached to that contract sets the details. One thing to keep in mind: being eligible doesn't mean a discount is approved. Each request still has to be approved separately.

**Why Acme qualifies**

1. **The contract: Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025.** It's between Northstar Industrial Systems and Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028. Clause 3.2, "Strategic account pricing," says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."* It was signed by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) for Northstar on 20 March 2025 and by Edward Floyd (VP Procurement) for Acme on 22 March 2025.

2. **The pricing exception: EXC-ACME-NS500-15, which is Schedule B of the contract.** It was prepared by Sarah Chen, the Deal Desk owns it, and David Morgan approved it on 20 March 2025. It sets:
   - a maximum discount of 15% of list price
   - the NS-500 only, with no other Northstar product covered
   - an effective period of 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028
   - that it replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 1 April 2025

3. **Why Acme is a strategic account.** Sarah Chen's 2026 account plan (ACCT-PLAN-CRM-2048-2026) lists Acme as a strategic account (CRM-2048). It says Acme standardised on the NS-500 across two plants in 2025 and plans to roll it out at a third plant in Q3 2026.

**What came before:** The earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, went with the 2023 agreement ACME-SA-2023. It allowed only up to 10% on the NS-500, for orders from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2025. The 15% cap applies only to requests dated from 1 April 2025 onward.

**Eligibility still needs approval**
- Clause 3.3 of the contract says Acme's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." The Schedule B exception likewise says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- Under the 2026 Pricing Policy and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, so 15% needs VP Sales. In 2025 it was different: account executives could approve up to 15%.
- **Current request:** DR-9001 is for 15% off $250,000 list price, which comes to a $37,500 discount and $212,500 net. Sarah Chen requested it on 23 September 2026 and it is still pending. It is within Acme's 15% limit, but it needs VP Sales sign-off.
- **Earlier request:** DR-8104 was for the same 15% and was approved by Sarah Chen herself on 16 September 2025. That was allowed under the 2025 policy. She could not self-approve it under the 2026 policy.
