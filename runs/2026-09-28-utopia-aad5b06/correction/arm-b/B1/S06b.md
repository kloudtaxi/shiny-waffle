> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **Where it comes from:** Pricing exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-10**, "Acme NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception", under agreement ACME-SA-2023. The Deal Desk created it on 2023-03-12. It lets Acme Manufacturing get up to 10% off list on NS-500 orders placed between 2023-04-01 and 2025-03-31, which includes 2024-10-01.
- **Not the 15% rate:** The 15% exception (**EXC-ACME-NS500-15**, Schedule B to Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025) replaces the 10% one only for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. It runs until 2028-03-31, so it didn't apply on 2024-10-01.

The knowledge graph agrees. Acme Manufacturing is recorded under its legal name, Acme Mfg. Holdings, and the only discount entitlement it shows for Acme on that date is the 10% exception.
