## Answer

No. Acme is allowed to receive a 15% discount on the NS-500, but Sarah Chen doesn't have the authority to approve it on 2026-09-23. Michael Torres, the VP Sales, has to approve it.

**Acme qualifies for 15% on this product**
- Pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025) allows up to 15% off list for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-500 Industrial Controller only. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers 2026-09-23. It replaced the older 10% exception.
- The exception also says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." Sarah prepared the document, but that doesn't give her any approval rights.

**Sarah's authority on that date is 10% at most**
- The employee records and the org chart dated 2026-09-01 list her as an Enterprise Account Executive reporting to Michael Torres.
- Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix (both in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) set these limits:
  - Enterprise Account Executive: up to and including 10%
  - VP Sales: over 10% and up to and including 20%
  - Chief Revenue Officer: over 20%
- The matrix also says "authority attaches to the role, not to the individual or the account," and approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Why last year's 15% deal doesn't carry over**
Under the 2025 policy, account executives could approve up to 15%. That's probably why Sarah's email to Mike says "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." The 2026 policy replaced the 2025 one completely for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01.
