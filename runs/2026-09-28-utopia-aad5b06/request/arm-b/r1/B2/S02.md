## Answer

**No, 18% can't be approved.** The request asks for more than Acme is allowed on the NS-500, and a higher approver can't fix that. The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**.

**What I checked**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 18% off $250,000 list, which is $45,000 off for a net of $205,000. It was raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. The basis is contract pricing, since the justification cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" in the CRM is the same company as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the ERP. It is a strategic account. The graph has no record under the account ID CRM-2048 itself, so I matched it by name.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It says Acme may receive NS-500 discounts of **up to 15% of list** for that period. It also says it does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" covers this product and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It sets the **maximum NS-500 discount at 15%**. It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which capped the discount at 10% and ended 2025-03-31. David Morgan approved the new exception, and Sarah Chen prepared it.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It replaced the 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Under the 2026 policy, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%. 18% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres holds that role, and Sarah reports to him.

**Why it's rejected**
18% is 3 points above the 15% that both the agreement and the exception allow. Michael Torres could sign off on the authority side, but his approval doesn't raise Acme's contract limit. Getting to 18% needs new or amended terms, meaning a revised exception or contract change that goes through commercial review.

**What you can do instead:** resubmit at **15% or less**. That is within Acme's limit but still above Sarah's 10%, so Michael Torres would need to approve it.
