## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this, and it shouldn't go to her manager for sign-off yet either. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The request says it relies on Acme's contract pricing, but Acme's contract terms only give discounts on a different product (the NS-500 controller), not the NS-Cloud Operations Suite.

**How I got there**

1. **The request.** DR-9001 is dated 2026-09-23 and was submitted by Sarah Chen. It asks for 15% off NS-CLOUD ($120,000 list, $18,000 discount, $102,000 net). Its stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on a contract term rather than standard pricing.
2. **The customer.** "Acme Manufacturing" is a recorded alias of Acme Mfg. Holdings, sourced from the CRM accounts file and other documents. However, the knowledge base doesn't hold the account ID CRM-2048, so I couldn't confirm that the ID itself maps to Acme Mfg. Holdings.
3. **The product.** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, with a list price of $120,000.
4. **Active contract.** Yes. The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31.
5. **Pricing exception for this product.** None. The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (which replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10) allows up to 15% until 2028-03-31, but only on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. Every recorded Acme discount right is for NS-500. For NS-Cloud, the records only say Acme is "evaluating" it. A 15% maximum on NS-500 doesn't carry over to NS-Cloud.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, valid 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy, which had let Account Executives approve up to 15%.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy she can approve up to and including 10%, so 15% is outside her authority.
8. **Who would approve.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. Even so, his approval doesn't create a contract right the customer doesn't have. It only matters once eligibility is settled.

**Next step:** The request needs a commercial review. Either it is resubmitted as standard pricing, where 15% would need Michael Torres's approval, or someone produces an Acme contract term or exception that actually covers NS-Cloud.
