## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this 15% discount herself. Acme is eligible for it, but under the 2026 pricing policy her limit is 10%, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

1. **Request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 off, $212,500 net), requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is recorded as another name for Acme Mfg. Holdings, a strategic account. I couldn't find account ID CRM-2048 in the knowledge base, so the link rests on the name alone. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a separate customer (CRM-2091 / C-1044).
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** Acme signed the Master Supply Agreement on 2025-03-22. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date.
5. **Pricing exception:** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" covers the NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and caps the discount at 15% of list price. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31). 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) applies. It replaced the 2025 policy, which had let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%, so 15% is over her limit. The contract doesn't change that: the agreement itself says it does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres is VP Sales, and Sarah has reported to him since 2026-09-01.
9. **Decision:** Approve with authorization. Acme is eligible, but Michael Torres must approve.
