## Answer

I've checked the customer, product, contract, exception and policy records. The answer is no: this request goes to commercial review.

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001, and it shouldn't be approved as submitted.** The request cites contract pricing, but the only Acme special pricing on file covers a different product.

1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-CLOUD ($120,000 list, $18,000 discount). It was raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is a recorded alternate name for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. The knowledge base doesn't record CRM account id CRM-2048, so I matched the customer by name only. Acme Mfg. Holdings is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044).
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. But the only discount term recorded for Acme under it is for the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**, up to 15%. Nothing records a discount term for NS-Cloud. The records only say Acme is "evaluating" NS-Cloud.
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 with a 15% maximum. It applies only to the NS-500. An exception covers only the product and dates it names, so this 15% can't be used for NS-Cloud.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (valid 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It replaced the 2025 policy, which had let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy she can approve up to and including 10%, so 15% is above her limit either way. The customer's contract doesn't change what an employee is allowed to approve.
8. **Approver band:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. Sarah Chen reports to him from 2026-09-01.
9. **Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** Pricing agreed for NS-500 doesn't carry over to NS-Cloud. Michael Torres's sign-off alone doesn't fix that, because no Acme discount terms for NS-Cloud are on file. The request needs commercial review, either to show a Master Supply Agreement clause that covers NS-Cloud or to put new terms in place. If those terms are established, 15% would still need approval from the VP Sales (Michael Torres).
