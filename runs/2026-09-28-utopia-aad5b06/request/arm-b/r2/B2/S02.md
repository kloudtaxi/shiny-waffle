## Answer

**No, we can't offer 18%.** The request asks for 3 points more than Acme is eligible for, so the outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. A more senior approver can't fix this. It needs new or amended commercial terms.

**How I got there**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 18% off the NS-500 ($250,000 list, $45,000 discount, $205,000 net). Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" in the CRM is the same entity as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the knowledge base. It is a strategic account. It is not "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a different customer. The knowledge base has no record of the ID CRM-2048 itself, so I matched on the name.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement was signed 2025-03-22 and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. That document also says it "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception covers this product from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It caps the NS-500 discount at **15% of list price**. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which capped it at 10% and ended 2025-03-31. **18% is above the 15% cap**, so Acme is not eligible for this discount.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date. It replaced the 2025 policy, which let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%. That limit no longer applies.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10% under the 2026 policy. 18% is beyond that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager.

**Why escalating isn't enough:** Michael could approve 18% as far as internal authority goes. But the problem is Acme's eligibility, not approval authority, and no approver can raise that. The options are:
- offer up to 15%, which is within the exception but still needs Michael's approval because it's over Sarah's 10%; or
- get amended terms for Acme through commercial review before any discount above 15%.
