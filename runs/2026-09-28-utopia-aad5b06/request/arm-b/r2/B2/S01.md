## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme can receive 15%, but it needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 and it relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **The customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is an alias of the customer record "Acme Mfg. Holdings." The account ID CRM-2048 isn't modelled in the knowledge base, so I matched the account to the customer by name. Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a different customer.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement was signed 2025-03-22 and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23.
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception applies to the NS-500 and allows up to 15% of list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Sarah Chen prepared it and David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the older exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% and ended 2025-03-31. **15% equals the cap, so Acme is eligible.**
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It replaced the 2025 policy, which had let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%. That higher limit no longer applies.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit. The agreement itself says it doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. Michael Torres holds that role, and Sarah has reported to him since 2026-09-01. The CRO, David Morgan, is only needed above 20%.

**Decision:** Approve with authorization. The request can go ahead only once Michael Torres signs off.
