## Answer

**Yes.** On 2025-09-23 Sarah Chen could approve DR-9001 herself. Acme's pricing exception allows exactly 15% on the NS-500 that day, and the 2025 pricing policy lets an Enterprise Account Executive approve discounts up to and including 15%.

**How I checked**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off the NS-500. That is $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, leaving $212,500 net. The justification ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement") shows it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** "Acme Manufacturing" is recorded as another name for the ERP customer **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is CRM-2091. The knowledge base doesn't directly link the account ID CRM-2048 to Acme Mfg. Holdings, so I matched them by name.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **The contract:** Acme signed the Master Supply Agreement on 2025-03-22. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500, as set out in the pricing exception (Schedule B). The agreement also says it does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **The pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception applies only to the NS-500. It caps the discount at 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, and it replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. The request is at the 15% cap, not above it, so Acme is eligible.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 15%, and VP Sales approval is needed only above 15%.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is within her own limit. Her authority comes from the policy, not from the contract.
8. **The required approver:** No higher approver is needed.

**Things to be aware of**
- **The date is what makes this an approval.** The 2026 policy cuts an Enterprise Account Executive's limit to 10% and requires VP Sales approval for 10–20%. The VP Sales is Michael Torres. If this request were actually dated in 2026, the outcome would be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION` with Michael Torres approving. The knowledge base doesn't hold the DR-9001 row itself, so I couldn't check your date against it. I used 2025-09-23 as you gave it.
- Sarah Chen prepared the pricing exception herself. The rules don't forbid this, and David Morgan approved it, but an auditor may notice.
