## Answer

Yes. Sarah Chen could have approved DR-9001 herself, and my decision is **APPROVE**. The customer is entitled to 15% on the NS-500 on that date, and 15% was within her own approval limit under the 2025 policy.

1. **The request.** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing: $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, leaving $212,500. Sarah Chen requested it on 2025-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **The customer.** In the ERP the customer is called "Acme Mfg. Holdings", and "Acme Manufacturing" is one of its recorded names. The knowledge base has no entity for CRM account CRM-2048. So the link between that account ID and the ERP customer comes from the matching name, not from a recorded mapping. The different company "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091) is not the customer here.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, with a list price of $250,000.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2025-09-23. The customer signed it on 2025-03-22. It says Acme may receive NS-500 discounts of up to 15% of list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It also states that it does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception.** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" covers NS-500 specifically and sets a 15% maximum from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. Sarah Chen prepared it and David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. The 15% requested equals the maximum, so the customer is eligible.
6. **Policy in force.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in effect from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. The 2026 policy, which lowered the Enterprise Account Executive limit to 10%, started on 2026-01-01 and does not apply to this request.
7. **Her authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2025 policy, that role can approve discounts up to and including 15%, so 15% is within her limit. Her title isn't dated in the records, but nothing suggests she held a different role in September 2025.
8. **Required approver.** Under the 2025 policy, VP Sales approval is only needed for discounts above 15%, so no higher approval is required.

The approval doesn't rest on the contract alone. The contract and exception make Acme eligible, and the 2025 policy separately gives Sarah the authority. If the same request were made in 2026, it would need VP Sales approval.
