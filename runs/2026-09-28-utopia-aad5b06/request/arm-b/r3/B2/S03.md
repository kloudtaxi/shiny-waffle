## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself, and it shouldn't go up for sign-off yet either. The request cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but nothing I found gives Acme contract pricing on NS-Cloud. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED** (it needs commercial review).

1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-Cloud Operations Suite (SKU NS-CLOUD). List price is $120,000, so the discount is $18,000 and the net is $102,000. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing in the CRM is the same customer as "Acme Mfg. Holdings", a strategic account. I couldn't find CRM account ID CRM-2048 in the knowledge base.
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD is NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000. Acme's account strategy says they are only *evaluating* it.
4. **Active contract:** yes. The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The only discount term on record under it lets Acme receive up to 15% off the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**. Nothing on record extends it to NS-Cloud.
5. **Pricing exception:** the Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception allows up to 15% from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, approved by David Morgan. It covers the NS-500 only. An exception applies only to the product and dates it names, so it doesn't cover NS-Cloud.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 and replacing the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** as an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above that. The 2025 policy's 15% limit no longer applies.
8. **Required approver:** discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Sarah reports to him.
9. **Decision:** without contract or exception terms covering NS-Cloud, eligibility can't be established, and Michael Torres approving it wouldn't change that. The Acme NS-500 terms can't be applied to a different product. It needs commercial review, which may lead to new or amended terms for NS-Cloud.

**The product on this request needs checking.** The request you gave me says NS-CLOUD. But the discount-requests file has an NS-500 discount request dated 2026-09-23, and Sarah's email to Michael is titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001." If DR-9001 is really for the NS-500, the answer changes. Acme would qualify for up to 15% under the exception, but 15% is still above Sarah's 10% limit. So it would be **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**, with Michael Torres as VP Sales signing off. Either way, Sarah can't approve 15% on her own.
