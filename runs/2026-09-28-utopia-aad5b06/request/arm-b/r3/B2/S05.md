The outcome is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The request says it's "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", but I couldn't find that agreement, or any Acme contract or pricing exception, in the knowledge base. Under the SOP, the document has to be produced before anyone decides.

**What I checked:**
1. **The request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on NS-500, worth $37,500 on a $250,000 list value. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 against CRM account CRM-2048. It relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer isn't clear.** CRM-2048 isn't in the knowledge base, and there are two separate "Acme" customers:
   - ACME Manufacturing (Milwaukee, WI), also recorded as Acme Mfg. Holdings.
   - Acme Industrial Supply Co. (Akron, OH), ERP customer C-1044.

   I can't tell which one the request is for.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000, which matches the request.
4. **Active contract:** I found no Master Supply Agreement or other contract for either Acme customer.
5. **Pricing exception:** None for Acme or NS-500. The only related item is a general heading, "Contract-specific pricing exceptions", with nothing attached to it.
6. **Policy in force on 2026-09-23:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, which runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaced the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy she can approve up to 10%, so 15% is over her limit. The 2025 policy allowed 15%, but it no longer applies.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager.

Even with the agreement in hand, the SOP says a contract that allows 15% doesn't raise Sarah's own limit. If the agreement confirms Acme can get 15% on NS-500 on this date, the result becomes APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, and Michael Torres must sign off. If it caps the discount below 15% or doesn't cover NS-500, the result becomes REJECT_OR_ESCALATE or REVIEW_REQUIRED.

**What to ask for:**
- The Acme Master Supply Agreement itself, including its dates and the discount term for NS-500.
- Which Acme legal entity CRM-2048 is.
