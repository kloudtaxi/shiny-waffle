## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme is entitled to a 15% discount on the NS-500, but 15% is above what an Enterprise Account Executive can approve under the 2026 policy. Michael Torres, VP Sales, must approve it.

**1. The request.** DR-9001: 15% off the NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**2. The customer.** The ERP customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which is also recorded under the names "Acme Manufacturing" and "ACME Manufacturing". It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091). I couldn't find account ID CRM-2048 in the knowledge base, so I matched the customer by name only.

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.

**4. Active contract.** Acme signed the Master Supply Agreement on 2025-03-22. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It states that it "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."

**5. Pricing exception.** The *Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception* covers the NS-500 and caps the discount at 15% of list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. The requested 15% is exactly at the cap, so Acme is eligible.

**6. Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy.

**7. Sarah's authority.** Under the 2026 policy, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve discounts up to and including **10%**. The 2025 policy allowed 15%, but it no longer applies. So 15% is beyond her authority.

**8. Required approver.** Under the 2026 policy, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres is VP Sales, and Sarah reports to him.

**9. Decision.** Being entitled to a discount under the contract doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it. The request can go ahead only once Michael Torres signs off.
