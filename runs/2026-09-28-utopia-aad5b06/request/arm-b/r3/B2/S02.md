## Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. We can't offer 18%.

**Why:** The Acme contract terms only allow up to **15%** on the NS-500. The request is for 18%, which goes past that limit. Getting a more senior person to approve won't fix this. It needs new or amended terms.

**How I got there**

1. **Request:** DR-9001, dated 2026-09-23, raised by Sarah Chen. It's for 18% off the NS-500 ($250,000 list, $45,000 discount, $205,000 net). The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing is recorded in the knowledge base as **Acme Mfg. Holdings** (aliases: "Acme Manufacturing", "ACME Manufacturing"). It is not the same company as Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044). I couldn't find CRM-2048 itself in the knowledge base, so the link rests on the name match.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. The records also say it "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** The *Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception* covers the NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It sets the maximum discount at **15% of list price**, replacing the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ran 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31). **18% is above that 15% cap.**
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Its approval bands are: an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%, VP Sales covers above 10% up to 20%, and the Chief Revenue Officer covers anything above 20%.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% and 18% is beyond it.
8. **Band for 18%:** VP Sales. Sarah has reported to Michael Torres since 2026-09-01, and the org chart lists him as VP Sales. That doesn't change the outcome: even with his sign-off, the discount would still exceed what the contract allows.

**What can be done**
- **Offer up to 15%.** That's within the contract terms, but it still needs VP Sales approval (Michael Torres) because it's above Sarah's 10% limit.
- **Keep 18%.** Acme's terms would need to be amended through commercial review first. After that, the request would still need VP Sales approval.
