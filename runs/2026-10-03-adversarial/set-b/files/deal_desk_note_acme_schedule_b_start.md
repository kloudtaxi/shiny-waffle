# Deal Desk Note — Acme Schedule B pricing start date

**Re:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025)
**Date:** 2025-09-19

## Summary

The 15% NS-500 pricing in EXC-ACME-NS500-15 applies to orders placed on or after **2025-10-01**.
The 2025-04-01 date on the exception is the effective date of the agreement, not the start of
Schedule B pricing.

## Detail

Under Section 4 of ACME-MFG-2025, Schedule B pricing starts with the first delivery window after
Customer signs site acceptance for the NS-500 installations at its first two plants. Acceptance
was signed on 2025-09-12, so the first eligible window opens on 2025-10-01.

The 2023 volume exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) expired on 2025-03-31 and was not extended. Acme
NS-500 orders placed between 2025-04-01 and 2025-09-30 are therefore at standard pricing, and no
customer pricing exception covers them.

## Open items

- DR-8104 (2025-09-16, 15%) was approved against Schedule B before the start date. Flagged to
  Revenue Operations for review.
- Account owner to hold any further Acme NS-500 contract-pricing requests until 2025-10-01.
