# Amendment No. 1 to Master Supply Agreement

**Agreement reference:** ACME-MFG-2025

This Amendment No. 1 is entered into between **Northstar Industrial Systems** ("Northstar"),
Chicago, Illinois, and **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as **Acme Manufacturing**
("Customer"), 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209. It amends the Master Supply Agreement
ACME-MFG-2025 dated March 20, 2025 (the "Agreement").

## 1. Background

Customer has committed to standardise its third production facility on the NS-500 Industrial
Controller. In consideration of that commitment, the parties agree to revise the strategic account
pricing in Section 3.2.

## 2. Amendment to Section 3.2

Section 3.2 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> 3.2 **Strategic account pricing.** Customer is eligible for discounts of up to **20%** on the
> NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B.

## 3. Amendment to Schedule B

In Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15), the maximum eligible discount is changed from 15% to **20%**
of list price for orders placed on or after July 1, 2026. Product scope and expiry are unchanged.

## 4. Effect

This Amendment is effective July 1, 2026. Except as amended here, the Agreement remains in full
force and effect. Section 3.3 is unchanged.
