# Approval Authority Matrix — Addendum 1: Strategic Accounts (2026)

Supplements APP-MATRIX-2026 for discount requests governed by the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026.

## Background

Pricing on Strategic-tier accounts is negotiated in the customer agreement, reviewed by Deal Desk
and approved by the Chief Revenue Officer when the agreement is signed. Sending every order-level
discount on those accounts back to VP Sales re-approves terms that are already approved, and in
H1 it added two to four days to Strategic order cycles.

## Change

From 2026-07-01 the following row applies in addition to the matrix:

| Role | Account scope | Discount authority |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise Account Executive | Strategic-tier accounts where the AE is the CRM account owner | ≤15% |

All other rows of APP-MATRIX-2026 are unchanged. On Standard-tier accounts, Enterprise Account
Executive authority remains ≤10%.

## Conditions

- The account is Strategic tier in Northstar CRM on the request date.
- The requesting AE is the account owner of record in Northstar CRM.
- The discount does not exceed the maximum in the customer's active pricing exception for that
  product.
- Approvals above 10% under this addendum are recorded in Northstar CRM with the exception ID.
