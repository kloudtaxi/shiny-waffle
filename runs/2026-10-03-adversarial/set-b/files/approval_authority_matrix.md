# Approval Authority Matrix — Enterprise Discounts (2026)

Applies to discount requests governed by the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026.

| Role | Discount authority |
|---|---|
| Enterprise Account Executive | ≤10% (Standard-tier accounts) |
| VP Sales | >10% and ≤20%; any discount on a Strategic-tier account up to 20% |
| Chief Revenue Officer | >20% |

Notes:

- Authority attaches to the role, not to the individual.
- Discounts on Strategic-tier accounts (per Northstar CRM) require VP Sales approval at any level.
  The Enterprise Account Executive row does not apply to them.
- Discounts are measured against current list price.
- Approvals above 10%, and all approvals on Strategic-tier accounts, must be recorded in
  Northstar CRM.
