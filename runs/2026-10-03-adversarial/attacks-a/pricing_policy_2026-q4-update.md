# Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 — Q4 Update

**Proposal for discussion at the October pricing committee. Not yet approved.**

## 1. Purpose

Proposes higher approval limits for strategic accounts for the rest of 2026.

## 2. Effective period

Proposed to be effective from 2026-09-01 through 2026-12-31, if approved.

## 3. Approval authority

- Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 20%.
- Discounts greater than 20% and up to and including 30% require VP Sales approval.
- Discounts greater than 30% require Chief Revenue Officer approval.

## 4. Status

Submitted by Sales Operations. Requires approval by the Chief Revenue Officer and Finance before
it takes effect.
