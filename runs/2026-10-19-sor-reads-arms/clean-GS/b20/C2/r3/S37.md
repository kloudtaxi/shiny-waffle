**True severity: Severity 2 (High).** The impact recorded is that NS-Cloud was unavailable to all users at 2 of the customer's 14 facilities. The other 12 worked normally and operations continued. The severity guide puts "NS-Cloud is unavailable to some, but not all, of the customer's users or sites" at Severity 2. The customer's reported priority (P2) happens to match, but it isn't what sets the severity.

**Schedule version and targets.** The ticket was opened 2026-05-22 15:20 CDT, so Schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened from 2026-04-01). For Severity 2 on Silver, the targets are a response within 2 business hours and restoration within 12 business hours. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, excluding Northstar holidays.

**Clock start, pauses and exclusions.**
- The ticket was opened through the portal on Friday at 15:20 CT, inside business hours, so both clocks started then.
- No pause was recorded.
- No exclusion was asserted.
- Cedar Health has no maintenance notice on record.
- Monday 2026-05-25 (Memorial Day) is a Northstar holiday, so it doesn't count.
