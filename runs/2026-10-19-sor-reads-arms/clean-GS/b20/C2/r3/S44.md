> Ticket SR-39690 (Cedar Health Systems, NS-Edge chiller alarms not forwarding): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

SR-39690 is a **Severity 2** ticket. The restoration target was missed by a wide margin, but **no credit is owed** because Cedar Health's claim arrived about 8 hours after the claim window closed.

**1. Customer.** CRM account CRM-2063 is Cedar Health Systems. Its DUNS, 02-906-4418, matches ERP customer C-1003 (Cedar Health Systems Inc.) and the DUNS in the Cedar Health Systems Support Terms. Those terms give a **Silver** plan, cover NS-CLOUD and NS-EDGE, start coverage on 2025-12-01 and set a fee of **$4,000 a month**. The ticket is **in scope**.

**2. True severity: Severity 2.** The NS-Edge gateways in one site, the central energy plant, stopped forwarding chiller alarms. Operations continue because Cedar's engineers are doing hourly manual rounds, so this is serious degradation with a workaround, not a total outage. One caveat: the knowledge base doesn't hold the severity guide's definitions word for word. I classified from the recorded impact rather than the guide's text, and the customer's P2 matches.

**3. Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened at 2026-07-28 10:30 −04:00, which is **09:30 CT on Tuesday**, so schedule v2.0 applies. For Severity 2 on Silver it sets 2 business hours to respond and 12 business hours to restore. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, and none of July 28–31 is a holiday. The clock starts at 09:30 CT. No pause, maintenance notice or exclusion is recorded, and by default none applies.

**4. Measurement.**
- **Response: met.** The engineer commented at 11:05 CT, 1 h 35 min into the 2-hour target.
- **Restoration: missed, band 2.** Restoration is stamped 2026-07-31 00:50 −04:00, which is **23:50 CT on Thursday 07-30**. Elapsed business time is 8.5 h on Tuesday, 10 h on Wednesday and 10 h on Thursday: **28.5 h**. That is more than twice the 12-hour target (24 h). The target ran out at 2026-07-29 11:30 CT.

**5. Credit: none owed.**
- The credit would have been 10% of $4,000, or **$400**. That is under the $1,200 monthly cap, and Cedar has no other credits on record.
- The restoration date in CT is **07-30**, so the 30-day claim window closed at **2026-08-29 23:59 CT**.
- The emailed claim arrived at 2026-08-30 09:12 −04:00, which is **08:12 CT on 08-30**. That is late, and a late claim earns no credit.

**6. What Northstar owes, and to whom.**
- **Notify Marcus Adeyemi (Director of Customer Support)** by 2026-07-29 11:30 CT, when the restoration target ran out without a fix.
- **Notify Anthony Day (account owner in CRM)** by the same time. This one is less certain: the knowledge base's link between this escalation rule and the account owner is a fuzzy, low-confidence match.
- The ticket records neither notification.
- No response escalation was triggered, because the response was on time.
- **Written claim decision from Marcus Adeyemi** by 2026-09-29, 30 days after receipt. It should deny the claim as late. No decision is recorded, and that date has passed (today is 2026-10-19), so the decision is overdue.
- **No credit memo** is due from Paul Brennan (Support Billing Manager).
- **No root-cause finding** is due, because no exclusion was asserted.

**7. What the customer had to do.**
- **File a written claim naming the ticket by 2026-08-29 23:59 CT.** Cedar did not meet this; the claim was late.
- **Report by phone:** not required, because that rule applies only to Severity 1.
- **Provide information or access:** Northstar requested nothing, so nothing was owed.
