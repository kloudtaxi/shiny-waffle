## Answer

# SR-39644: Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data loss

**Outcome: NO_BREACH.** The true severity is **Severity 2**, not P1. Both targets were met, so Northstar owes no credit. No escalation or notification was triggered, and Acme has nothing outstanding. The restoration result depends on how one ticket entry is read (see the caveat at the end).

**1. Customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 (tier: Strategic) has DUNS 04-812-7730. That DUNS matches ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings) and the DUNS in Schedule C. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091, DUNS 09-552-1187) is a different company and isn't involved here.

**2. Scope: in.** Schedule C covers NS-EDGE under the **Platinum** plan, with coverage from 2025-04-01. The ticket was opened 2026-07-23.

**3. True severity: 2.** Vibration monitoring stopped for 6 of the 14 presses. The presses kept running, and technicians are doing manual checks every two hours. The severity guide lists both "a condition-monitoring function is unavailable" and "a Severity 1 condition with a workaround in place that needs ongoing manual effort" as Severity 2. The customer's P1 doesn't set the severity.

**4. Rules that apply.** Schedule v2.0 covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01. For Severity 2 on Platinum, the response target is 2 hours and the restoration target is 12 hours, both on a 24×7 clock. All times below are in CDT (UTC−05:00), the schedule's time zone; the ticket timestamps are already in it.

**5. Clock, pause and exclusions.**
- Both clocks start at the open time, 13:10 on 2026-07-23.
- The restoration clock pauses from 15:00 on 07-23, when the engineer asked for VPN credentials and set the ticket to Awaiting Customer. It resumes at 08:30 on 07-24, when the customer's provision was recorded.
- There are no maintenance notices for this account, and no customer-caused exclusion was asserted.

**6. Measurement.**
- **Response: met.** The engineer responded at 15:00, after 1 h 50 m, before the 15:10 deadline.
- **Restoration: met.** Restored at 18:40 on 07-24. Counted time is 1 h 50 m before the pause plus 10 h 10 m after it, exactly 12 h 00 m. A miss needs elapsed time *more than* the target.

**7. Credits.** None owed, since no target was missed. For reference: the July 2026 fee is $21,000 and the 30% cap is $6,300. No July credits have been approved, and no claim was made.

**8. Obligations.**
- **Northstar: nothing triggered.** The Severity 1 steps (incident lead within 15 minutes, account-owner notice within 30 minutes, notices at 50% and 100% of the target, CRO for Strategic accounts) don't apply.
  - The Severity 2 response escalation to the Support Escalation Manager (Hannah Lindqvist) would have fallen due at 15:10 on 07-23. It wasn't triggered because the response came at 15:00.
  - The restoration escalation would have been due at 18:40 on 07-24. That is the exact moment of restoration, so it wasn't triggered either. That step notifies the Director of Customer Support (Marcus Adeyemi) and, by the less certain reading of the escalation procedure's text, the account owner (Sarah Chen).
  - There is no claim to decide, no credit memo to issue and no root-cause finding due.
- **Customer:**
  - Provide information and access: **met** (Kelly Ballard of Acme IT supplied the VPN credentials).
  - Phone report to the hotline: only needed for Severity 1 credits, so not applicable.
  - Credit claim: none needed.

**Caveat.** The result depends on when the pause ended. The 15:00 entry already says Kelly Ballard "provided them", without giving a time. If that counts as recording the access at 15:00, there is no pause. Elapsed time would then be 29 h 30 m, which is more than twice the target (band 2). That would be a 10% credit of $2,100, but the claim window closed on 2026-08-23 with no claim. The outcome would then be BREACH_NO_CREDIT. I relied on the explicit 08:30 entry, "Customer provided what was requested; status set to In Progress."
