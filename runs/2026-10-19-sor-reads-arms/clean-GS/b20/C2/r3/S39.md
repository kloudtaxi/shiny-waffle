## Answer

I've checked every rule and role that applies. Here is the decision.

**The ticket is a Severity 1. Both targets were missed. Northstar owes Cedar a $320.00 credit (`BREACH_CREDIT_OWED`).**

**Customer and scope.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2063. Its DUNS, 02-906-4418, matches ERP customer C-1003 (Cedar Health Systems Inc.) and the DUNS in Cedar's support terms. Those terms give Cedar the Silver plan, cover NS-CLOUD, started on 2025-12-01 and set a fee of $4,000 a month. The ticket is in scope.

**True severity: Severity 1, not the P3 Cedar reported.** NS-Cloud was down for all users at all 14 facilities, and no one could see equipment status or alarms. At 09:20 EDT a Northstar engineer confirmed that every user was affected, found no workaround, and reclassified the ticket as Severity 1.

**Targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies because the ticket was opened after 2026-04-01. Severity 1 runs on a 24×7 clock with a 30-minute response target and a 4-hour restoration target. All times below are in Central Time (CDT, UTC−05:00):
- **Clock start:** opened 07:05 EDT, which is 06:05 CDT on 2026-06-10. No pauses were recorded, no exclusion was asserted and Cedar had no maintenance notice, so nothing is taken off the clock.
- **Initial response: missed.** The engineer called at 08:20 CDT, 2 h 15 m after clock start.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** Service came back at 11:35 CDT, 5 h 30 m after clock start. That is over the 4-hour target but within twice the target (8 hours).

**Credit: $320.00.** Cedar opened the ticket on the portal and never phoned the Support hotline. Under the schedule, Severity 1 credit rates apply only to a phone report. Otherwise the Severity 2 rates apply, though the breach is still measured against the Severity 1 targets.
- The rates are 3% for the missed response plus 5% for restoration band 1, so 8% × $4,000 = $320.00.
- The monthly cap is 30% of the fee, or $1,200. No other credits have been approved for Cedar's June tickets, so the cap doesn't reduce this one.
- Cedar's claim was timely. It arrived by email, naming the ticket, at 10:00 CDT on 2026-06-29. The claim window ran until 23:59 CT on 2026-07-10.
