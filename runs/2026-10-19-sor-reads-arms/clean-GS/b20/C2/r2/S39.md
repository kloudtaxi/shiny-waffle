**True severity: Severity 1 (Critical), not P3.** The ticket says none of Cedar's 14 facilities could see equipment status or alarms. At 08:20 CT the engineer confirmed that every user was affected, that there was no workaround, and reclassified the ticket as Severity 1. The customer's P3 is only their view and doesn't set severity.

The ticket records no pauses, no maintenance notice and no customer-caused exclusion.

Severity 1 credit rates apply only if the customer phoned the Support hotline before restoration, and neither the portal nor Northstar's call to Cedar counts.
