- Cedar's Support Terms use the same DUNS. They give a Silver plan, cover NS-EDGE (and NS-CLOUD), and set a fee of $4,000 a month from 2025-12.
- Coverage started 2025-12-01 and the ticket was opened 2026-07-28, so it is in scope.

**2. True severity: Severity 2**
- The customer reported P2, but that doesn't set severity.
- The recorded impact is that the gateways "stopped forwarding chiller alarms," so an alarming function is unavailable. The severity guide lists that as a Severity 2 condition.
- "Hourly manual rounds" is a workaround that needs ongoing manual effort. The guide also classes that as Severity 2.

**3. Targets**

- Business hours are 08:00–18:00, Monday to Friday. There was no holiday that week (Independence Day was observed on 2026-07-03).

**4. Clock**

- No pauses are recorded, so none apply.
- There is no maintenance notice for the account, and Northstar didn't claim in the ticket that the customer caused the fault. So no exclusions apply.

**5. Measurement**
- **Response: met.** The engineer responded at 11:05 on 07-28, after 1 h 35 min against a 2-hour target.
- **Restoration: missed, band 2.** It was restored Thu 07-30 at 23:50. That is 28.5 business hours (8.5 on Tue, 10 on Wed, 10 on Thu), more than twice the 12-hour target.

**6. Credit**
- The rate is the Severity 2 band 2 rate of 10% of $4,000, which is **$400**. There is no response credit.
- The July cap is 30%, or $1,200, and nothing else has been approved for July.
