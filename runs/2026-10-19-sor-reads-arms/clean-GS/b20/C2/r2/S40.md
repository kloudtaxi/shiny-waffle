**SR-39881 is a Severity 2 ticket. Northstar missed both its response and restoration targets and owes a $200.00 service credit.** The customer filed its claim on time. The record shows no credit approved yet, although the decision was due by 2026-09-27.

**Customer and scope**
- The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2051, which carries DUNS 07-331-9025. ERP customer C-1002 (Blue River Logistics LLC) has the same DUNS.
- That DUNS matches BlueRiver Logistics Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025): Silver plan, covers NS-CLOUD, coverage started 2025-01-01 00:00 CT, fee $2,500 a month. The ticket is in scope.

**True severity: 2, not the P2 the customer reported.** NS-Cloud alerts and dashboards stopped at 2 of 6 depots. Under the severity guide, that is NS-Cloud unavailable to some but not all sites, with an alarming function down. Dispatchers phoning drivers is a manual workaround, and operations continued at a lower level. Nothing in the record points to Severity 1.

**Clocks**
- **Schedule version:** v2.0, because the ticket opened on or after 2026-04-01. The Silver plan with Severity 2 runs on business hours: 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday. There are no holidays in the period.
- **Targets:** first response within 2 business hours; restoration within 12 business hours.
- **Clock start:** the ticket opened Thursday 2026-08-20 at 19:45 CDT, outside business hours, so the clock started Friday 2026-08-21 at 08:00 CDT.
- **Pauses and exclusions:** none. No pause is recorded, no exclusion is asserted, and there is no maintenance notice for August (the only notice, MN-2607, covers July).
- **Response: missed.** The 19:46 auto-acknowledgment doesn't count. The first engineer comment came at 10:20, 2 h 20 min against a 2 h target. The note only says "Engineer's ticket comment," but the target is missed whether or not that comment counts.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** Restored Monday 08-24 at 10:40. That is 10 h on Friday plus 2 h 40 min on Monday, 12 h 40 min in total. It is over the 12 h target and under 2× (24 h).

**Credit**
