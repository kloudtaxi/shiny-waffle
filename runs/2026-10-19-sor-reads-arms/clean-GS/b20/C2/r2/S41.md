**SR-39644 is Severity 2. Neither service level was missed, so the outcome is `NO_BREACH`, with no credit owed and no escalation or notification due. Restoration finished exactly at its 12-hour target, with no time to spare.**

**Customer and scope.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing). That account's DUNS, 04-812-7730, matches ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings). This is the company named in Schedule C to ACME-MFG-2025. Under Schedule C, Acme has the Platinum plan, NS-EDGE is a covered product, and coverage started on 2025-04-01. The ticket opened on 2026-07-23, so it is in scope.

**True severity: Severity 2 (High), not the P1 Acme reported.** The ticket says the gateways stopped sending vibration data for presses 1–6. The presses kept running, and technicians are doing manual checks every two hours. The severity guide puts this in Severity 2: "a control, alarming or condition-monitoring function is unavailable" while "production or operations continue", with a workaround that "needs ongoing manual effort". The reported priority does not set the severity.

**Schedule and targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies because the ticket opened on or after 2026-04-01. For Severity 2 on Platinum, the targets are a 2-hour response and a 12-hour restoration, on a 24×7 clock. All times below are Central Daylight Time (UTC−05:00), which is how they were recorded, so no conversion was needed.
- **Clock start:** 2026-07-23 13:10, when the portal submission was received. There is no maintenance notice on record, and Northstar did not claim the incident was caused by Acme.
- **Pause:** at 2026-07-23 15:00 an engineer asked for VPN credentials and set the ticket to Awaiting Customer. At 2026-07-24 08:30 the ticket records that Acme provided them. Because the start and end are both recorded, the pause counts: 17 h 30 m taken off the restoration clock only.
- **Initial response:** the engineer's comment at 15:00 came 1 h 50 m after opening, within the 2-hour target. **Met.**
- **Restoration:** restored at 2026-07-24 18:40. That is 1 h 50 m before the pause plus 10 h 10 m after it, 12 h 00 m in total. The first credit band applies only when restoration takes "more than the target", so exactly 12 hours meets it. **Met.**

**What Northstar owes.**
- **Credit:** none. No target was missed, so the credit rates, the 30% monthly cap and the claim window don't come into play. Acme has made no claim.
- **Escalations:** none were due. For Severity 2, the Support Escalation Manager is notified only if there has been no initial response when the 2-hour target runs out; the response came in time. The Director of Customer Support (and possibly the account owner, Sarah Chen) is notified only if the ticket is not restored when the 12 hours run out, and it was restored at that moment. The Severity 1 escalation steps do not apply.
- **Root-cause finding:** none due, because no exclusion was claimed.

**What Acme must do.**
- **Information and access:** Acme had to provide the information and access Northstar needed, and it did. Kelly Ballard of Acme IT Architecture supplied the VPN credentials, and the ticket recorded this at 08:30 on 2026-07-24.
- **Phone report:** the requirement to phone the hotline applies only to Severity 1, so it doesn't apply here.
- **Credit claim:** none is needed, since there was no breach.
