# Clarification Bulletin 2026-07

**Applies to:** Service Level Schedule v1.0 and v2.0, Support Escalation Procedure (SOP-SUPPORT-007).
This bulletin clarifies how existing wording is applied in Northstar Service. It adds no new
exclusion; Section 5 of each Schedule continues to list only scheduled maintenance and
customer-caused incidents.

## 1. What counts as an "Awaiting Customer" pause (Schedule §4.3)

Section 4.3 pauses the restoration clock for a request for "information, access or an action
Northstar needs". In Northstar Service this is applied as follows:

1. A site-access, change-window or on-site-escort request counts as a request for access or action,
   including when the customer must also coordinate it with its own contractors, landlord or
   operations staff. The pause runs until the access is recorded as granted.
2. Where a Northstar engineer's request is made by telephone and the engineer records it in the
   ticket afterwards, the pause starts when the call ended, not when the note was saved, and the
   later note is accepted as the "recorded" start.
3. The pause end is the customer's reply, or the access or action, as the ticket's status history
   shows it. If the status history shows "Awaiting Customer" with no end entry, the pause is
   treated as ending when the ticket was set to Restored.
4. A pause on a Severity 1 ticket under §4.3 and a failed-contact pause under §4.4 are cumulative
   where they overlap.

## 2. Maintenance notice counting (Schedule §5.1)

The notice period is counted in whole business days between the notice's sent date and the
window's start date. The sent date is day 0 and the start date is not counted, so a notice sent on
a Monday for a window starting the following Monday gave 4 business days (Tuesday to Friday),
which does not reach 5 business days. A notice sent in the evening after 18:00 CT is treated as
sent on the next business day.

A notice that names the customer's account but lists "NS-EDGE and associated services" qualifies for
every product the customer holds, including products not named individually.

## 3. Severity 1 reclassification (Schedule §2.7)

When a Severity 1 ticket is reclassified to Severity 2 on a workaround, the Severity 2 targets and
credits apply to the whole ticket from the open time, and the Severity 1 escalation steps that
have not yet fallen due are cancelled. Escalation steps already due stay due.

## 4. Effective date

This bulletin applies to every ticket not yet closed on the date of issue, and to claims not yet
decided.
