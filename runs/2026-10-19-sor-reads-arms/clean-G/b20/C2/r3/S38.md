**2. True severity: Severity 1 (Critical).** The customer also reported it as P1, but the severity comes from the recorded impact:
- NS-Cloud was down for all users. The restoration note reads "NS-Cloud available to all users again".
- The ticket record says "No workaround".
- Severity 2 needs some, not all, users affected, or a workaround in place. Neither applies.

**3. Rules that apply.** The ticket opened 2026-07-11 23:20 CT, so Schedule v2.0 applies (it covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01). Severity 1 targets are a 30-minute response and a 4-hour restoration, on a 24×7 clock whatever the plan.

**4. When the clock started.** The ticket opened inside maintenance window MN-2607 (an NS-Cloud upgrade, 2026-07-11 22:00 to 2026-07-12 02:00 CT).
- The notice went out 2026-06-26 at 11:00. That is at least 5 business days before the window, which the schedule requires.
- Under the schedule, a ticket opened inside a qualifying window starts both clocks at the window's end: **2026-07-12 02:00 CT**.
- The engineer's note says the upgrade caused the outage. That doesn't change anything: time after the window counts normally.
- No pauses were recorded, and no customer-caused exclusion was claimed.

**5. Measurement.**
- **Response: met.** The engineer phoned at 23:35, before the clock started, so it counts as elapsed time zero.
- **Restoration: met.** Service came back at 05:05, which is 3 h 05 min after clock start, against a 4-hour target.

**6. Credit: $0.** BlueRiver emailed a claim on 2026-07-14 at 08:45. That was on time (the deadline was 2026-08-11 23:59 CT), but no target was missed, so no credit is due. No credit memo is needed. If one had been owed, the cap would have been 30% of the $2,500 monthly fee.
