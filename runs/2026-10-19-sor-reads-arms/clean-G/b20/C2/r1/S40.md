## Answer

I've decided SR-39881 as **BREACH_CREDIT_OWED**. Both targets were missed and Northstar owes a **$200.00** service credit. The customer made a timely claim.

**Customer and scope.** I identified the customer by its identifiers, not its name. CRM account CRM-2051 ("BlueRiver Logistics"), ERP customer C-1002 ("Blue River Logistics LLC") and DUNS 07-331-9025 are the same company at 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. Its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025) are a Silver plan covering NS-Cloud from 2025-01-01 00:00 CT, at $2,500 a month. The ticket is NS-Cloud and was opened 2026-08-20, so it is in scope.

**True severity: Severity 2 (High).** Shipment-exception alerts and route dashboards stopped at 2 of 6 depots (St. Louis and Memphis). "The other depots are fine," and dispatchers are phoning drivers to track exceptions. The severity guide makes that Severity 2 on three counts: an alarming function is unavailable, only some sites are affected, and the workaround needs ongoing manual effort. The customer's reported P2 happens to agree.

**Schedule and clock.** The ticket opened by portal on 2026-08-20 at 19:45 CDT, so Schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01). For Silver at Severity 2 the targets are 2 business hours to respond and 12 business hours to restore. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, excluding Northstar holidays. The ticket opened after hours, so the clock started at the next business-hours start: **Friday 2026-08-21 08:00 CDT**. No pauses are recorded, and no exclusion was asserted.

**Measurement:**
- **Response: missed.** The automated ticket-creation email at 19:46 doesn't count as a response. The first engineer comment was at 10:20 on 08-21, which is 2 h 20 m elapsed against a 2 h target. The deadline was 10:00.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** Service was restored Monday 2026-08-24 at 10:40. That is 10 h on Friday plus 2 h 40 m on Monday, so 12 h 40 m against a 12 h target (deadline Monday 10:00). It is under twice the target, so band 1.

**Credit:** 3% for the response plus 5% for restoration band 1 makes 8% of $2,500, which is **$200.00**. That is under the monthly cap of 30% ($750), and SR-39881 is the only BlueRiver ticket I found opened in August. The other BlueRiver ticket, SR-39561, opened in July and doesn't count against this month. The claim arrived by email on 2026-08-28 at 14:05 CDT and names the ticket. The claim deadline was 2026-09-23 23:59 CT, so it was on time.

**What Northstar owes, by whom and by when:**
- **Support Escalation Manager, Hannah Lindqvist:** to be notified that there was no response by 2026-08-21 10:00 CDT. The procedure's sentence is cut off after "notify the **Support…**", so the role name is my reading.
- **Director of Customer Support, Marcus Adeyemi:** to be notified that service wasn't restored by 2026-08-24 10:00 CDT.
- **Account owner, Kelly Floyd:** the same notice by the same time. This rests on a lower-confidence extract of the procedure.
- **Claim decision:** Marcus Adeyemi must send a written decision by 2026-09-27, 30 days after the claim. I found no decision on record, and that date has passed.
- **Credit memo:** Paul Brennan (Support Billing Manager) issues the $200.00 memo against the next support invoice, on the same day the credit is approved.
- **Root-cause finding:** none is due, because no exclusion was asserted.

**What the customer must do:** Nothing more for this ticket. Its credit claim was in writing, named the ticket and was on time. The phone-report rule applies only to Severity 1. The customer must still provide information and access when asked, but no request was recorded on this ticket.

One assumption: I couldn't read the holiday dates themselves. I've treated 08-21 and 08-24 as business days, since neither is a usual US holiday.
