## Answer

**Decision: NO_BREACH.** SR-39561 is Severity 1. Both targets were met because the ticket was opened inside a notified maintenance window, so the clock started only when the window ended. BlueRiver made a timely credit claim, but no credit is owed; it must be refused in writing by 2026-08-13.

**1. Customer.** BlueRiver Logistics is CRM account CRM-2051, DUNS 07-331-9025, and the support ticket record links SR-39561 to CRM-2051. I found no ERP customer number. "Blue River Logistics LLC" is the same account (same CRM number).

**2. Scope: in.** BlueRiver's support terms (SUP-BRL-2025) cover NS-Cloud on the Silver plan. Coverage started on 2025-01-01, and the fee is $2,500 a month.

**3. True severity: Severity 1 (Critical).** The customer also reported P1. The record shows NS-Cloud unavailable to all users with no workaround. Severity 2 covers only an outage that hits some users, or a Severity 1 condition that has a workaround, so neither applies. The guide's own Severity 1 wording isn't in the knowledge base; I inferred Severity 1 from the Severity 2 definition.

**4. Schedule version and targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies, because the ticket was opened on 2026-07-11 at 23:20 CT, after its 2026-04-01 start. Severity 1 always runs on a 24×7 clock, even on the Silver plan. The targets are a 30-minute initial response and 4-hour restoration.

**5. Clock start, pauses and exclusions.** All times are Central (UTC−05:00).
- **Maintenance window:** maintenance notice MN-2607 (an NS-Cloud upgrade) covered 2026-07-11 22:00 to 2026-07-12 02:00. It was sent on 2026-06-26, at least 8 business days ahead; the minimum is 5.
- **Clock start:** the ticket was opened inside that window, so both clocks started at the window's end: **2026-07-12T02:00−05:00**. The outage was caused by the maintenance itself, but only time after the window counts.
- **Pauses:** none recorded.
- **Exclusions:** no customer-caused exclusion was asserted.
- **Gap:** the knowledge base doesn't show who received the notice. I treated it as qualifying because it's recorded under an MN number and states the window and the product.

**6. Measurement.**
- **Initial response: met.** An engineer phoned at 23:35, before the clock started, which counts as zero elapsed time.
- **Restoration: met.** Service was restored at 05:05, 3 h 05 min after clock start, inside the 4-hour target. The customer's figure of 5 h 45 min counts from 23:20, which includes window time that doesn't count.

**7. Credit: $0.** No target was missed. The claim was timely: it arrived by email on 2026-07-14 at 08:45, and the deadline was 2026-08-11.

**8. What Northstar owes.** Deadlines run from the 02:00 clock start.

- **By 04:00:** notify the Director of Customer Support, Marcus Adeyemi. This was required because the ticket was still unrestored when half the restoration target had passed.
- **Not required:**

  - The Chief Revenue Officer isn't needed because BlueRiver's CRM account type is Standard, not Strategic. I'm assuming that field is the account tier.
- **Credit claim:** Marcus Adeyemi must send a written decision refusing it by **2026-08-13**. No credit memo is due and no root-cause finding is required.
- **Not on record:** the knowledge base shows no record of whether these engagements, notifications or the claim decision actually happened.
