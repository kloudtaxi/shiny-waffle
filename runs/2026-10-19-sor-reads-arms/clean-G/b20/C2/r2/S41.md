Maintenance staff covered this with manual vibration checks every two hours. Under the severity guide, losing a condition-monitoring function, or having a workaround that needs ongoing manual effort, is Severity 2. Nothing recorded for this ticket says production stopped. The "Line 4 is stopped" and "Line 2 is stopped" entries are linked to Plant 2 and Acme in general, not to SR-39644, so I didn't count them here. Without a production stop, this is not Severity 1.
