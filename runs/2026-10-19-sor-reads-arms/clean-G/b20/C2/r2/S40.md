The ERP record for customer C-1002 ("Blue River Logistics LLC") has the same DUNS and address, so they are the same customer. The CRM account tier is Standard, not Strategic.

**2. Scope: in scope.** The customer's support terms (SUP-BRL-2025) are on the Silver plan and cover NS-Cloud Operations Suite. Coverage started 2025-01-01 00:00 CT, and the fee is $2,500 a month.

**3. True severity: Severity 2 (High).** The customer reported P2, and the recorded impact supports Severity 2 on its own terms:
- Shipment-exception alerts and route dashboards stopped updating at 2 of 6 depots (St. Louis and Memphis). The other depots were fine.
- Losing an alerting function is a Severity 2 condition, as is NS-Cloud being down at some but not all sites.
- Operations carried on with a manual workaround: dispatchers were phoning drivers. Under the guide, a Severity 1 condition with a workaround that needs ongoing manual effort is Severity 2.

The knowledge base doesn't hold the guide's Severity 1 criteria, but given the manual-workaround rule this doesn't change the result.

**4. Version and targets.** The ticket was opened 2026-08-20 19:45 CDT, which is on or after 2026-04-01, so schedule v2.0 applies. For Severity 2 on Silver, the targets are an initial response within 2 business hours and restoration within 12 business hours. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday. There are no Northstar holidays between 20 and 24 August.

**5. Clock start, pauses and exclusions.**

- No pause is recorded, and no exclusion was asserted in the ticket. There is no maintenance notice for this period; MN-2607 covered a July window.
