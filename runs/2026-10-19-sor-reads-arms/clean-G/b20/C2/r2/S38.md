# SR-39561: BlueRiver Logistics, NS-Cloud down for all users

**Outcome: no breach.** The ticket is Severity 1 and both targets were met. Northstar owes no service credit, but it does owe the customer a written answer to their credit claim by 2026-08-13.

**1. Customer and scope.** BlueRiver Logistics is CRM-2051 (DUNS 07-331-9025). The ticket record carries that CRM number. Its support terms (SUP-BRL-2025, Silver plan, $2,500 a month) cover NS-Cloud, and coverage started on 2025-01-01. The ticket is in scope.

**2. True severity: Severity 1.** The record says "NS-Cloud has been down for all our users" since about 23:10, with "No workaround." Severity 2 covers only cases where some, but not all, users are affected, or where a workaround is in place. Neither applies here. This matches the P1 the customer reported. The "dispatchers phoning drivers" workaround in the same file belongs to the other ticket, SR-39881.

**3. Schedule and clock.** The ticket opened by phone at 2026-07-11 23:20 CDT, so schedule v2.0 applies. Severity 1 runs on a 24×7 clock: 30 minutes for the first response and 4 hours for restoration.

**4. Clock start.** The ticket was opened during maintenance window MN-2607, which ran from 22:00 on 2026-07-11 to 02:00 on 2026-07-12 CDT. The notice was sent on 2026-06-26 for the NS-Cloud upgrade. That is about 9 business days ahead, well over the 5 required. Under the schedule, a ticket opened inside a qualifying window starts both clocks when the window ends, so the clock started at **2026-07-12 02:00 CDT**. The upgrade caused the outage, but that doesn't matter: time after the window counts normally. No pauses were recorded, and no customer-caused exclusion was asserted.

**5. Measurement.**
* **First response: met.** The engineer phoned at 23:35, before the clock started, which counts as zero elapsed time.
* **Restoration: met.** Service was restored at 05:05, 3 h 05 min after clock start, against a 4-hour target.

**6. Credit: $0.** No target was missed. The customer's claim for the "Severity 1 restoration credit" arrived on time (2026-07-14; the deadline was 2026-08-11) but should be declined. If the MN-2607 notice turned out not to qualify, the clock would have started at 23:20. Restoration would then take 5 h 45 min, a band-1 miss: 10% × $2,500 = $250.

* **Notify the account owner, Kelly Floyd,** by 02:30.

* **Claim decision:** Marcus Adeyemi must send a written decision on the claim by **2026-08-13**.
* No credit memo is needed, and no root-cause finding is due.

**8. What the customer had to do**
* **Report a Severity 1 by phone: met.** The ticket was opened by phone.
* **Submit the credit claim in writing on time: met.** It was emailed on 2026-07-14.
