## Answer

I've finished the review. SR-39802 is **out of scope**, so no service level could be breached and no credit is owed. The customer is not on the Platinum plan it named.

**1. Who the customer is.** The ticket's CRM account is CRM-2091. That is Acme Industrial Supply Co. (ERP C-1044, DUNS 09-552-1187, 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH). It is a "Standard" account and its account owner is Zachary Brown. It is not ACME Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730, Milwaukee).

**2. Scope.** The "Acme Platinum" plan is Schedule C (Support and Service Levels) to ACME-MFG-2025. That agreement belongs to Acme Mfg. Holdings, and it says plainly: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered."* It also excludes the parent company and every other affiliate. The record shows no support terms of Acme Industrial's own covering NS-Edge. The procedure says a ticket is in scope only if its customer has a support agreement covering the ticket's product. Out-of-scope tickets are handled best effort, *"no targets apply,"* *"nothing can be breached,"* *"no credit is owed,"* and the normal escalations do not apply.

**3. True severity: Severity 1 (Critical).** This rests on the recorded impact, not on the customer's "P1": every NS-Edge gateway at the Akron distribution center was offline from about 05:30, there was no workaround, and picking only resumed when the gateways came back. Severity 2 requires work to continue in a degraded state, which didn't happen here. One caveat: I couldn't pull the severity guide's exact Severity 1 wording from the record. On an out-of-scope ticket, severity only sets internal priority.

**4. Breach and credit.** No breach is possible. For reference, the ticket was opened 2026-08-10 at 06:05 EDT (05:05 CT) and restored at 14:20 EDT (13:20 CT), but no targets apply. The customer's email of 2026-08-14 asking for the "Severity 1 restoration credit under Acme's Platinum plan" has no basis, because the plan isn't theirs. The credit is $0.

**5. What Northstar owes.**
- **Notify the account owner of the ticket's CRM account.** That is **Zachary Brown** (Enterprise Account Executive, zachary.brown@northstar.example). The deadline is 10 business hours after the open time, where business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, excluding holidays. The 05:05 CT open time falls before business hours, so counting starts at 08:00 CT on Monday 2026-08-10, which is not a Northstar holiday. The deadline is therefore **2026-08-10 at 18:00 CT (2026-08-10T18:00-05:00)**. The record doesn't show whether this notification was sent.
- Best-effort handling only. The documents don't require a formal decision on the claim, a credit memo or a root-cause finding. In practice, Northstar should still tell the customer the claim was declined and why.

**6. What the customer must do.** Contractually, nothing: no support terms apply, so it has no service-level duties. It did report by phone. If Acme Industrial wants guaranteed service levels on NS-Edge, it needs its own support agreement with Northstar.
