## 2. True severity: **Severity 2**
The customer reported P2, but severity comes from the recorded impact: chiller alarms stopped forwarding, and engineers are doing hourly manual rounds. That matches the Severity Guide's Severity 2 conditions: "a control, alarming or condition-monitoring function is unavailable", and "a Severity 1 condition with a workaround in place that needs ongoing manual effort". Even if losing alarms counted as Severity 1, the manual-rounds workaround makes it Severity 2. I couldn't find the guide's full Severity 1 wording, but it doesn't change the result.

## 3. Schedule version, targets and clock
- The ticket opened at 2026-07-28 10:30 −04:00, which is **09:30 CDT on Tuesday**. Schedule **v2.0** applies to tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01 CT.

- The clock runs only in business hours: 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday. There are no Northstar holidays between 2026-07-03 and 2026-09-07.
- The clock started at the open time, because 09:30 CDT is inside business hours.
- Pauses and exclusions: none were recorded, none were asserted, and the account has no maintenance notices. So no pause and no exclusion apply.
