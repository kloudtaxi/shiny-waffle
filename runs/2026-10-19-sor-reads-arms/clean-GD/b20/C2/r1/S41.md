## Question (verbatim)

> Ticket SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data lost for six presses, reported P1): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39644: no service level was breached. The true severity is Severity 2, not P1, and Northstar owes no credit and no escalation.** The restoration target was met with no time to spare.

**Customer and scope.** The ticket's CRM account is CRM-2048. It shares DUNS 04-812-7730 with ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings. Its support terms (Schedule C to ACME-MFG-2025) put it on the Platinum plan, with coverage from 2025-04-01. NS-Edge (NS-EDGE) is a covered product, so the ticket is in scope.

**True severity: 2.** The Severity Guide makes it Severity 2 when a condition-monitoring function is unavailable, production continues, or a workaround needs ongoing manual effort. All three apply here: vibration monitoring was lost on presses 1–6, the presses kept running, and technicians were doing manual checks every two hours. The customer's P1 doesn't change that.

**Rules that apply.** Schedule v2.0 covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01 CT. For Severity 2 on Platinum it sets a 2-hour response target and a 12-hour restoration target, both on a 24×7 clock. All times below are CDT (−05:00).

| Step | Time | Elapsed |
|---|---|---|
| Opened (portal) and clock starts | 07-23 13:10 | 0 |
| First engineer response, asking for VPN credentials. Status set to Awaiting Customer, so the restoration clock pauses | 07-23 15:00 | 1h 50m |
| Customer's reply recorded, status set to In Progress, clock resumes | 07-24 08:30 | (17h 30m paused) |
| Restored | 07-24 18:40 | +10h 10m = **12h 00m** |

- **Response:** 1h 50m against a 2-hour target, so met.
- **Restoration:** exactly 12h 00m against a 12-hour target, so met. A credit only starts when elapsed time is more than the target.
- **Pause:** it counts because both its start and its end are in the ticket. The response clock doesn't pause. The ticket note names Kelly Ballard (Acme IT Architecture) as the person who supplied the credentials but gives no time for it. The 08:30 entry is the only recorded end, so I used it.
- **Exclusions:** Acme has no maintenance notices on record and Northstar asserted no customer-caused exclusion, so neither applies.
- **The margin is zero.** Without the pause, restoration would have taken 29h 30m, more than twice the target, which would mean a 10% credit ($2,100 on the $21,000 fee from 2026-01). The decision depends entirely on the recorded pause.

**What Northstar owes**
- **Credits:** none. No target was missed, no claim is on the ticket, and none is needed.
- **Escalations:** none were triggered, so nobody has to be engaged or notified.
  - The Severity 2 response escalation applies only if there's no response when the target elapses. The response came at 15:00, before the 15:10 deadline.
  - The Director of Customer Support must be told if the ticket isn't restored when the restoration target elapses. That moment was 07-24 18:40, the same minute restoration was recorded.
- **Root-cause finding:** none is due, because no exclusion was asserted.

**What the customer had to do**
- **Provide the information and access Northstar needs:** met. The VPN credentials were supplied, recorded at 07-24 08:30.
- **Report by phone to the hotline:** this applies only to Severity 1, so it's not needed here. The portal report is fine.
- **File a credit claim:** nothing to claim.
