## Answer

SR-39340 is a Severity 1 ticket. Northstar missed both its response and restoration targets, and a $320.00 credit is owed (BREACH_CREDIT_OWED). The credit is set at the lower Severity 2 rates because Cedar never phoned in the outage.

**Customer and scope.** The ticket's account is CRM-2063. Its DUNS number, 02-906-4418, matches ERP customer C-1003 (Cedar Health Systems Inc.) and the DUNS stated in Cedar's Support Terms. Those terms put Cedar on the Silver plan at $4,000 a month and cover NS-CLOUD from 2025-12-01. The ticket is in scope.

**True severity: 1, not the P3 the customer reported.** The ticket says none of Cedar's 14 facilities could see equipment status or alarms. A Northstar engineer confirmed by phone that every user was affected and there was no workaround, and reclassified the ticket as Severity 1. One gap: the knowledge base only holds the Severity Guide's label "Severity 1: Critical", not its full criteria. The ticket facts and the engineer's reclassification still support Severity 1.

**Schedule and clock.** The ticket opened at 07:05 Eastern, which is 06:05 CT on 2026-06-10, so Schedule v2.0 applies (it covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01). Severity 1 always runs on the 24×7 clock, with a 30-minute response target and a 4-hour restoration target. The clock started at 06:05 CT. No pauses were recorded, no maintenance notice exists for this account, and no exclusion was claimed in the ticket.

**Measurement (all times CT)**
- **Response: missed.** The first engineer response was at 08:20, 2 h 15 min after the clock started. The target was 06:35.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** Service came back at 11:35, after 5 h 30 min. That is over the 4-hour target but under 8 hours (twice the target).

**Credit: $320.00**
- Severity 1 credit rates apply only if the customer reports the incident by phoning the Support hotline before restoration. Cedar used the portal, and Northstar's own call doesn't count, so the Severity 2 rates apply.
