## Answer

**SR-39561 (BlueRiver Logistics): no service level was breached and no credit is owed.** The outage started during a properly announced maintenance window, so the clocks only started when the window ended.

**1. Customer and scope.** CRM account CRM-2051 has DUNS 07-331-9025, which matches ERP customer C-1002 (Blue River Logistics LLC). Their support terms (reference SUP-BRL-2025) are on the Silver plan at $2,500 a month. They cover NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD), and coverage started 2025-01-01. The ticket is in scope.

**2. True severity: Severity 1.** The ticket says NS-Cloud was down for all users with no workaround. The severity guide puts an outage for only some users or sites, or one with a manual workaround, at Severity 2. A full outage with no workaround is therefore Severity 1, which matches the customer's reported P1.

**3. Rules that apply.** The ticket opened 2026-07-11 23:20 CT, so schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01). Severity 1 runs on a 24×7 clock with these targets:
- **Initial response:** 30 minutes.
- **Restoration:** 4 hours.

**4. Maintenance window.**
- **The window:** maintenance notice MN-2607 covered NS-CLOUD from 2026-07-11 22:00 to 2026-07-12 02:00 CT.
- **The notice was early enough:** it was sent Friday 2026-06-26. Counting the business days after that, up to the window's start date, gives 9 days. That skips the weekends and the Northstar holiday on 2026-07-03, and the schedule requires at least 5.
- **The notice was complete:** it states the start, the end and the product. The record doesn't show who it was sent to, so I've assumed it went to the customer's designated support contacts.
- **Clock start:** the ticket opened at 23:20, inside the window, so both clocks started when the window ended at **2026-07-12 02:00 CT**.
- **Cause doesn't matter:** the engineer noted that the upgrade itself caused the outage. That doesn't change anything, because the schedule says time after the window counts normally either way.
- **No pauses** are recorded.

**5. Measurement.**
- **Initial response: met.** The engineer called at 23:35, before the clock started, so it counts as elapsed time zero.
- **Restoration: met.** Service came back at 05:05, which is 3 h 05 min after the clock started, inside the 4-hour target.

**6. Credits.** No credit is owed. The customer's claim (5 h 45 min, measured from 23:20) counts time inside the maintenance window. Without the window, it would have been a band 1 restoration miss worth 10% × $2,500 = $250. No credits have been issued on this account, so there is nothing to count against the monthly cap.

- **None of these engagements or notifications is recorded in the ticket history.**
