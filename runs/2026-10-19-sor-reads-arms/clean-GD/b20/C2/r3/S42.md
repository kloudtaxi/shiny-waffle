Acme Industrial Supply Co. and the parent company are a different matter and are not covered.

**True severity: Severity 1.** The paint line is stopped with no workaround. Severity 2 only applies to that kind of condition when a manual workaround is in place, so this is Severity 1. It happens to match the customer's own P1.

- **Restoration: met.** The firmware rollback restored the line at 05:35 on 1 April, after 6 h 45 m against a 05:50… correction is not needed here: the 8-hour deadline was 06:50.

**Credit (only if the claim was on time).**

- **So the credit is $630 if the claim was on time, and $0 if it was late.**
