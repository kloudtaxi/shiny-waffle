# SR-39207 (Cedar Health Systems, NS-Cloud outage at two sites): Severity 2, no breach, no credit owed

The ticket is Severity 2. Northstar met both service targets: the response came 10 minutes early and the restoration came 15 minutes early. Cedar Health's credit claim was on time, but no credit is owed because no target was missed. The one open duty is a written decision denying the claim. Marcus Adeyemi must send it by **2026-07-01**.

**1. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2063 (Cedar Health Systems) and ERP customer C-1003 (Cedar Health Systems Inc.) share DUNS 02-906-4418. That same DUNS is named in Cedar Health's support terms.

**2. Scope: in scope.**
- The plan is Silver, and the covered products are NS-CLOUD and NS-EDGE.
- Coverage started 2025-12-01, and the fee is $4,000 a month.
- The ticket was opened 2026-05-22, after coverage started.

**3. True severity: Severity 2.** The customer reported it as P2, but severity comes from the recorded impact, not the reported priority. NS-Cloud was unavailable to all users at 2 of 14 facilities, the other 12 worked normally, and operations at both sites continued. The severity guide lists "NS-Cloud is unavailable to some, but not all, of the customer's users or sites" as a Severity 2 condition. I could not find the full list of Severity 1 conditions in the knowledge base. Nothing recorded suggests a Severity 1 impact, since operations continued and most sites were unaffected.

**4. Schedule and targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies, because the ticket was opened on or after 2026-04-01 Central Time (CT). For Severity 2 on the Silver plan, the targets are:
- **Initial response:** 2 business hours.
- **Restoration:** 12 business hours.
- **Business hours:** 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, excluding Northstar holidays. Monday 2026-05-25 (Memorial Day) is a holiday.

**5. Clock start, pauses and exclusions.**
- **Clock start:** the ticket came in by portal at 16:20 EDT, which is Friday 2026-05-22 15:20 CDT. That is inside business hours, so the clock started then.
- **Pauses:** none are recorded.
- **Exclusions:** there is no maintenance notice for the account, and no customer-caused exclusion was asserted.

- **Customer:** Cedar Health made its claim in writing, named the ticket, and was on time. A phone report is required only for Severity 1, and this ticket isn't one. Cedar Health owes nothing further, apart from giving information or access if Northstar asks for it.
