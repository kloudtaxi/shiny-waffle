## Answer

I've decided SR-39802 as **out of scope**. Acme Industrial has no support terms with Northstar. The "Acme Platinum" plan it cites belongs to a different company, so no service level applies, nothing was breached and no credit is owed. Northstar's one duty is to notify the account owner, Zachary Brown, by **2026-08-10 18:00 CT**.

**1. Who the customer is.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2091, Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH, with DUNS 09-552-1187. That matches ERP customer C-1044. It is not ACME Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730), which is the company that actually has the Platinum plan.

**2. Scope.** The only Platinum terms on file are Schedule C (Support and Service Levels) to ACME-MFG-2025. They cover Acme Mfg. Holdings for NS-500 and NS-EDGE, starting 2025-04-01. They say plainly: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered,"* and they exclude every affiliate. The only basis for Acme Industrial's Platinum claim is the caller's own words on the ticket ("We're Acme. We have the Acme Platinum support plan"). No support agreement covers CRM-2091 for NS-EDGE.

**3. True severity: Severity 1 (Critical).** All NS-Edge gateways at the Akron distribution center were down, picking had stopped and there was no workaround. That matches the customer's P1. Because the ticket is out of scope, this severity only sets its internal priority.

**4. Service levels.** The Escalation Procedure says that for out-of-scope tickets "no targets apply" and "nothing can be breached," and they are "handled best effort." For the record:
- Opened 2026-08-10 06:05 EDT (05:05 CT), by phone.
- An engineer responded at 07:40 EDT, 1 h 35 min after opening.
- Restored at 14:20 EDT, 8 h 15 min after opening.

If the Platinum Severity 1 targets had applied (30-minute response, 4-hour restoration), both would have been missed. They don't apply to this customer.

**5. Credits: none owed.** No credit has been recorded for this ticket.

**6. What Northstar must do.**
- **Notify the account owner** of CRM-2091: Zachary Brown, Enterprise Account Executive. The deadline is 10 business hours after the open time. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, and 2026-08-10 is a Monday and not a Northstar holiday. The ticket opened at 05:05 CT, before business hours, so the count starts at 08:00 and ends at **2026-08-10T18:00:00-05:00**. The ticket history doesn't show whether this notice was sent.
- The Severity 1 escalations don't apply to out-of-scope tickets. That means no Support Escalation Manager, Director of Customer Support or VP Sales escalation, and no Chief Revenue Officer notice. The account's CRM tier is Standard anyway.
- No claim decision or credit memo is required under the Service Level Schedule. A courtesy written reply to the 2026-08-14 claim would make sense, telling Amy Barrett that CRM-2091 is not covered by the Platinum terms. That reply is my suggestion, not a written requirement.

**7. What the customer must do.** Nothing under the Service Level Schedule, because none applies. For what it's worth, Amy Barrett did report by phone before restoration and emailed a claim on 2026-08-14, but neither earns a credit. If Acme Industrial wants guaranteed service levels, it needs its own support agreement.
