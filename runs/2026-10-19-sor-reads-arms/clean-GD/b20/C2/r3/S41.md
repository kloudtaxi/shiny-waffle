## Question (verbatim)

> Ticket SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data lost for six presses, reported P1): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39644: no breach (`NO_BREACH`).** Both service levels were met, although restoration came in exactly on its 12-hour limit. No credit is owed, no escalation or notification was triggered, and the customer has nothing outstanding to do.

**1. Customer.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing (Strategic tier). Its DUNS number, 04-812-7730, matches ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing. This is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a separate company.

**2. Scope: in scope.** Acme's support terms (Schedule C to agreement ACME-MFG-2025) put Acme on the Platinum plan. They cover the NS-500 and the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (NS-EDGE), and coverage started on 2025-04-01. The ticket was opened on 2026-07-23, after that date.

**3. True severity: Severity 2 (High), not the P1 Acme reported.** The ticket says vibration data stopped for presses 1–6 of 14. The presses are still running, and technicians are checking those six by hand every two hours. The severity guide puts two of its Severity 2 cases in exactly these terms:
- "a control, alarming or condition-monitoring function is unavailable"
- "a Severity 1 condition with a workaround in place that needs ongoing manual effort"

The knowledge base doesn't hold the guide's own Severity 1 wording. The ticket meets those two Severity 2 cases on its face.

**4. Rules and targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies because it covers tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01 Central Time. For Severity 2 on Platinum, the targets are a first response within 2 hours and restoration within 12 hours. The clock runs around the clock (24×7), so holidays don't matter. All ticket times are already Central Daylight Time (UTC−05:00).

**5. Clock, pause and exclusions.**
- **Clock start:** when the portal ticket was opened, 2026-07-23T13:10-05:00.
- **Pause:** at 15:00 an engineer asked Acme for VPN credentials and the ticket went to "Awaiting Customer". Acme supplied them and the ticket went back to "In Progress" at 2026-07-24T08:30. Both ends are recorded, so this 17 h 30 m counts as a pause, but only on the restoration clock.
- **Exclusions:** there are no maintenance notices on the account, and Northstar didn't claim the incident was the customer's fault.

**6. Measurement.**
- **First response: met.** 13:10 to 15:00 is 1 h 50 m, against 2 h.
- **Restoration: met, with no margin.** Before the pause, 13:10 to 15:00 is 1 h 50 m. After it, 08:30 to 18:40 is 10 h 10 m. That totals exactly 12 h 00 m. The schedule only counts a miss when the time is *more than* the target. If the pause were ever disallowed, the time would be 29 h 30 m, more than twice the target.

**7. Credits: none.** No target was missed, no credit has been issued and no credit claim is on the ticket. Acme's July 2026 support fee ($21,000) and the 30% monthly cap don't come into play.

**8. Who must be engaged or notified, and by when.**
- **Northstar: nothing was triggered.**
  - A missing first response would have meant notifying the Support Escalation Manager, Hannah Lindqvist, by 2026-07-23T15:10. The response came at 15:00, so that didn't apply.
  - A ticket still open when the restoration target ran out would have meant notifying the Director of Customer Support, Marcus Adeyemi, at 2026-07-24T18:40. Restoration happened at that same moment, so that didn't apply either. The procedure may also name the account owner for that alert (Sarah Chen), but that link in the knowledge base is low-confidence.
  - The Severity 1 steps (incident lead, account owner, VP Sales, CRO) don't apply. Escalation follows the true severity, not the reported P1.
  - There are no claim decisions, credit memos or root-cause findings due.
- **Acme:**
  - It met its duty to give Northstar the access it needed (the VPN credentials).
  - Severity 1 duties don't apply: reporting by phone to the hotline and keeping a technical contact reachable by phone. Acme reported through the portal only.
  - It has no credit claim to make. No credit is owed, so a claim filed by 2026-08-23 would have nothing to recover.
