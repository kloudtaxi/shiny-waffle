## Answer

**SR-39340 decision: `BREACH_CREDIT_OWED`. Northstar owes Cedar Health Systems a $320.00 service credit.** The true severity is Severity 1, not the P3 the customer reported. Both the response and restoration targets were missed. Because Cedar never phoned the Support hotline, the credit is paid at the lower Severity 2 rates.

**1. Customer.** CRM-2063 (Cedar Health Systems) carries DUNS 02-906-4418. The same DUNS is on ERP customer C-1003 (Cedar Health Systems Inc.) and in the Cedar Health Systems Support Terms. The CRM tier is Standard and the account owner is Anthony Day.

**2. Scope: in.** The terms cover NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD) on the Silver plan from 2025-12-01, at a fee of $4,000 a month.

**3. True severity: Severity 1.** The ticket says "won't load for anyone… none of our 14 facilities can see equipment status or alarms". At 09:20 ET the engineer confirmed that every user was affected, that there was no workaround, and changed the ticket to Severity 1. The severity guide puts a partial outage, or one with a manual workaround, at Severity 2. This one was neither, so it is Severity 1. The customer's P3 doesn't decide it.

**4. Schedule.** The ticket was opened 2026-06-10 07:05 EDT, which is 06:05 CT. That falls under schedule v2.0, which covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01. Severity 1 runs on a 24×7 clock with a 30-minute response target and a 4-hour restoration target. The plan doesn't change these.

**5. Clock.** The clock starts at 06:05 CT. The ticket records no pauses, and there are no maintenance notices or exclusions on the account.

**6. Measurement (all CT):**
- **Response: missed.** The target was 06:35. The engineer responded at 08:20, 2 h 15 m after clock start.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** The target was 10:05. Service was restored at 11:35, 5 h 30 m after clock start. That is more than 4 hours but no more than 8, so it falls in band 1.

**7. Credit: $320.00.**
- The ticket was opened through the portal, and the only phone call was Northstar calling Cedar. Neither counts as a report to the hotline, so the Severity 1 rates don't apply.
- At Severity 2 rates, 3% for the missed response plus 5% for restoration band 1 is 8% of $4,000, or $320.00.
- That is under the 30% monthly cap of $1,200. No other June-opened ticket on the account has a credit (SR-39207 was opened in May).
- The claim was timely. Cedar emailed it on 2026-06-29 and named the ticket, and the deadline was 2026-07-10 23:59 CT.
