> Ticket SR-39802 (Acme Industrial, Akron, NS-Edge gateways offline, claims the Acme Platinum plan): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39802 is out of scope. No service level was breached and no credit is owed, because Acme Industrial has no Northstar support terms. The "Acme Platinum" plan the customer cites belongs to a different company.**

**The customer.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2091, "Acme Industrial Supply Co." of Akron, OH (DUNS 09-552-1187, ERP customer C-1044). The only Platinum terms in the record are "Schedule C (Support and Service Levels) — Acme Manufacturing", part of ACME-MFG-2025. That agreement is with Acme Mfg. Holdings of Milwaukee (CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730). It covers NS-500 and NS-Edge, but it says outright: "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered." The only basis for Acme Industrial's Platinum claim is the customer's own sentence in the ticket ("We have the Acme Platinum support plan"). I found no other support terms that cover DUNS 09-552-1187.

**Scope rule.** A ticket is in scope only if the customer has a support agreement covering the ticket's product. Out-of-scope tickets "are handled best effort": no targets apply, nothing can be breached, no credit is owed, and the usual escalations don't apply.

**True severity: Severity 1, for internal priority only.** Every NS-Edge gateway at the Akron site was down, picking had stopped and there was no workaround. The customer reported P1. On an out-of-scope ticket, severity sets internal priority only and creates no targets.

**Timeline (Central Time):**
- Opened by phone at 05:05 on Mon 2026-08-10.
- An engineer phoned back at 06:40.
- Restored at 13:20.
- A credit claim arrived at 09:00 on 2026-08-14.

Because no targets apply, none of these times counts as a breach.

**What Northstar owes:**
- **Notify the account owner** of CRM-2091 that the ticket is out of scope: Zachary Brown, Enterprise Account Executive. The deadline is 10 business hours after the open time, where business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT on weekdays except Northstar holidays. The ticket opened before business hours and 2026-08-10 is not a holiday, so the deadline is **2026-08-10T18:00:00-05:00**. The record doesn't show this notice was sent, so I can't confirm the deadline was met.
- **Decline the credit claim.** It asks for a Severity 1 restoration credit under a plan that doesn't cover this customer, so the credit owed is $0.
- No escalations, credit memo or root-cause finding are owed under service-level terms.

**What the customer must do.** Nothing under service-level terms. Acme Industrial has no support terms, so it has no phone-report or claim duties to meet. It did report by phone and did file a claim, but neither gives it any entitlement. If Acme Industrial wants contractual coverage, Zachary Brown, as account owner, could take that up with them.
