## Answer

**SR-39644 was not breached, so Northstar owes no credit.** Its true severity is 2, not P1. Restoration took exactly 12h00m against a 12-hour target, so the outcome depends on one pause being valid (explained below).

**Customer and scope.** The customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings: CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a separate company and not covered. Acme's support terms (Schedule C to ACME-MFG-2025) put it on the Platinum plan from 2025-04-01 and list NS-Edge (NS-EDGE) as a covered product. The ticket is in scope.

**True severity: 2 (High), not the P1 Acme reported.** The ticket says the gateways stopped sending vibration data for presses 1–6 of 14, the presses are still running, and technicians are checking them by hand every two hours. That matches the severity guide's Severity 2 conditions: a condition-monitoring function is unavailable, and a Severity 1 condition has a workaround needing ongoing manual effort.

**Targets.** The ticket was opened 2026-07-23 13:10 CT, so Service Level Schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01). For Severity 2 on Platinum, the targets are a 2-hour initial response and 12-hour restoration, on a 24×7 clock. The clock starts at the open time. No maintenance notice is recorded and no exclusion was asserted.

**Measurement**
- **Initial response: met.** The engineer responded at 15:00, 1h50m after opening, within the 15:10 deadline.
- **Restoration: met, at exactly the target.**
  - The ticket was "Awaiting Customer" from 15:00 on 07-23, when the engineer asked in the ticket for VPN credentials. It returned to In Progress at 08:30 on 07-24, when the customer provided them.
  - Both ends of that pause are recorded, so it counts. Counted time is 1h50m before the pause plus 10h10m from 08:30 to the restoration at 18:40, which is 12h00m.
  - A miss requires "more than the target", so 12h00m meets it.
- **The risk:** the 15:00 status note also says Kelly Ballard (Acme IT Architecture) provided the credentials, but gives no separate time.
  - If that is read as the credentials arriving at 15:00, the pause ends there and the restoration time becomes 29h30m. That is more than twice the target (band 2).
  - I've relied on the timestamped 08:30 record. If the pause is challenged, the decision must be redone.

**Credits.** None are owed because nothing was missed. No claim is recorded either. For reference only: if the pause were rejected, the miss would be band 2 at 10% of the $21,000 July fee (Severity 2 rates, since Acme reported through the portal, not by phone), which is $2,100. Acme would have needed to claim it by 2026-08-23 23:59 CT, and that date has passed. Acme's two earlier credits relate to March tickets, so they don't affect July's cap.

**Who must be engaged or notified.** No escalation was triggered.
- The only Severity 2 step on record is a notification if there is no initial response when the 2-hour target runs out, at 15:10 CT. The response came at 15:00. The record cuts off the role's name; it appears to be the Support Escalation Manager.
- The Severity 1 steps don't apply: engaging the Support Escalation Manager, and notifying account owner Sarah Chen, the Director of Customer Support, the VP Sales and the CRO.
- With no claim and no exclusion, there is no claim decision, credit memo or root-cause finding due.

**What the customer must do**
- **Provide the information and access Northstar needs:** done. Kelly Ballard supplied the VPN credentials, recorded at 08:30 on 07-24.
- **Phone report:** not needed. Reporting by phone to the hotline matters only for Severity 1 credits.
- **Credit claim:** none is needed, since there was no breach.
