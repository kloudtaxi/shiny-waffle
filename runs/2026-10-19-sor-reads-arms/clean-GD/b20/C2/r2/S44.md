## SR-39690 (Cedar Health Systems, NS-Edge): a target was missed, but no credit is owed

**Customer and scope.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2063. Its DUNS, 02-906-4418, matches ERP customer C-1003 (Cedar Health Systems Inc.). The Cedar Health Systems Support Terms list NS-Edge as a covered product on the Silver plan. Coverage started 2025-12-01, so the ticket is in scope. The monthly support fee is $4,000.

**True severity: Severity 2.** The customer reported P2, but severity comes from the recorded impact. The chiller alarms stopped forwarding, which means an alarming function was unavailable. The engineers' hourly manual rounds are a workaround that needs ongoing manual effort. The severity guide treats both of these as Severity 2.

**Schedule and clock.** The ticket opened 2026-07-28 at 10:30 EDT, which is 09:30 CT. That date falls under Schedule v2.0, which applies from 2026-04-01. For Severity 2 on Silver, the targets are an initial response within 2 business hours and restoration within 12 business hours. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT on weekdays. The open time was inside business hours, so the clock started at 09:30 CT. The record shows no pauses, no maintenance notices and no asserted exclusions, and in each case the default is that none applies.

**Measurement (all times CT):**
- **Initial response: met.** The engineer responded at 11:05 CT, 1 hour 35 minutes after the clock started. The deadline was 11:30 CT.
- **Restoration: missed, band 2.** The deadline was Wed 2026-07-29 at 11:30 CT. Service was restored 2026-07-31 at 00:50 EDT, which is Thu 2026-07-30 at 23:50 CT. That is 28.5 business hours (8.5 on Tuesday, 10 on Wednesday, 10 on Thursday). This is more than twice the 12-hour target, which puts it in band 2.

**Credit: $0, because the claim was late.**
- Band 2 for Severity 2 pays 10% of the fee, so the credit would have been $400. That is under the monthly cap of $1,200, and no other credits are recorded for the account.
- In CT, the restoration date is 2026-07-30. The 30-day claim window ran from 07-31 to 2026-08-29 at 23:59 CT.
- The claim arrived 2026-08-30 at 08:12 CT, one day late, and a late claim earns no credit.
- It only looks on time if you use the restoration date in Eastern time (07-31).

**What Northstar owes:**
- **Escalation for the missed restoration target:** the Director of Customer Support (Marcus Adeyemi) had to be notified by 2026-07-29 at 11:30 CT. The account owner (Anthony Day) should probably also have been notified by then. That rule's text was only partly captured in the knowledge base, so treat it as likely rather than certain. Neither notification is recorded on the ticket.
- **No escalation was needed for the response,** because the response target was met.
- **Claim decision:** Marcus Adeyemi must send a written decision denying the claim as late, by 2026-09-29. That deadline has passed, and no decision is recorded. Because no credit is approved, no credit memo is due.
- **No root-cause finding is due,** because no exclusion was asserted.

**What the customer had to do:**
- **Claim on time:** not met. The claim arrived one day late.
- **Report by phone:** this requirement applies only to Severity 1, so it doesn't apply here. Submitting through the portal was fine for Severity 2.
- **Provide information and access:** no request from Northstar is recorded.

**One gap:** the holiday dates themselves weren't in the knowledge base. July 28–30 isn't normally a holiday period. Even if one of those days were a holiday, the restoration target would still be missed and the claim would still be late, so the outcome would not change.
