# Northstar Pricing Policy 2027

## 1. Purpose
Sets discount approval authority for direct enterprise sales for calendar 2027.

## 2. Effective period
This policy is effective from 2027-01-01 through 2027-12-31. It replaces the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 in full for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.

## 3. Approval authority
- Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%.
- Discounts greater than 15% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval.
- Discounts greater than 20% require Chief Revenue Officer approval.

## 4. Rules
- Discounts are calculated against the current list price.
- Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.
- Discounts greater than 15% require recorded approval evidence.
