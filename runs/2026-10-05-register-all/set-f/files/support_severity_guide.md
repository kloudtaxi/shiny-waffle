# Support Severity Guide

Northstar Support classifies severity from business impact.

## Severity 1: Critical
A Northstar product failure that, with no workaround available, stops production or operations, or makes NS-Cloud unavailable to all of the customer's users.

## Severity 2: High
Not Severity 1, and production is materially degraded; a control/alarming/monitoring function is unavailable; or NS-Cloud is unavailable to some but not all users or sites. **A total NS-Cloud outage reported P3 or lower is Severity 2.**

## Severity 3: Medium
A function is impaired without reduction in production output and without loss of a control, alarming or monitoring function.

## Severity 4: Low
Question, how-to, documentation, cosmetic or enhancement with no operational effect.

The customer's reported priority is considered when selecting severity.
