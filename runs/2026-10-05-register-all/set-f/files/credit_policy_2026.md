# Northstar Credit Policy 2026

## Authority
- AR Specialists may approve up to $100,000.
- Greater than $100,000 and up to $500,000 requires Finance Manager approval.
- Greater than $500,000 requires Director Finance approval.

## Concurrence
Strategic requests greater than $500,000 also require VP Sales concurrence.

## Eligibility
No invoice due in the prior 12 months may have been paid more than 30 days late or remain unpaid more than 30 days. Maximum credit is $750,000 Strategic and $250,000 Standard. A parent-company guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount for the customer named.

## Separation of duties
A requester may participate in approval or concurrence of their own request when they hold the required authority.
