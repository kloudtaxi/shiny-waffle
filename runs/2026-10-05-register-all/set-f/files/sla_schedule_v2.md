# Northstar Support Service Level Schedule v2.0

This version applies to tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01 00:00 CT. Version is chosen by ticket open time in Central Time.

Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 CT Monday-Friday except Northstar holidays. Severity 1 uses a 24x7 clock. A target is met if the event happens at or before the target.

## Targets
| Severity | Initial response | Restoration | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 minutes | **8 hours** | 24x7 |
| 2, Platinum plan | 2 hours | 12 hours | 24x7 |
| 2, Silver plan | 2 business hours | 12 business hours | Business hours |
| 3 | 8 business hours | 30 business hours | Business hours |
| 4 | 20 business hours | No restoration target | Business hours |

## Exclusions
Scheduled maintenance qualifies only with required written notice. Customer-caused incidents are excluded only with the required root-cause finding by the deadline.

## Service credits
For Severity 1: missed initial response 5%; restoration more than target and no more than 2x target 10%; restoration more than 2x target 20%. Credits are additive subject to the monthly cap.

## Claims
The customer must submit a written credit claim within 30 calendar days after restoration. The Director of Customer Support decides the claim by the applicable deadline.
