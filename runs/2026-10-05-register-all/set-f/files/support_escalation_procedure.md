# Support Escalation Procedure

Escalation follows the ticket's true severity and its clocks.

## Severity 1
1. Engage Support Escalation Manager within 15 minutes.
2. Notify account owner within 30 minutes.
3. At 50% of restoration target, notify Director of Customer Support if not restored.
4. At 100% of restoration target, notify VP Sales if not restored.

## Severity 2
1. If no initial response when response target elapses, notify Support Escalation Manager.

No restoration escalation is required for Severity 2.

## Severity 3 and 4
On Severity 3 not restored when its target elapses, notify Support Escalation Manager. Severity 4 has no escalation.

Tickets outside a support agreement are best effort and have no targets, credits or escalations; notify the account owner within 10 business hours.
