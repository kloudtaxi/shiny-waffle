# Master Supply Agreement

Agreement reference: ACME-MFG-2025

This Master Supply Agreement is between Northstar Industrial Systems and **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** as Customer.

## Term
Effective April 1, 2025 through March 31, 2028.

## Products and pricing
Northstar will supply NS-500 Industrial Controller, NS-Cloud Operations Suite and NS-Edge Monitoring Package. Customer is eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on NS-500 Industrial Controller. Contractual pricing eligibility does not modify Northstar internal approval authority.
