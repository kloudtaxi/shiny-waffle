# Parent Company Guarantee Amendment

**Guarantee reference:** GRT-ACME-2026  
**Additional guaranteed party:** Acme Industrial Supply Co.

This amendment confirms that the payment obligations of **Acme Industrial Supply Co.**
(CRM-2091 / C-1044), 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310, are also covered by the
Parent Company Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026.

The coverage is subject to the same aggregate guarantee amount and term as the existing guarantee.

## Guaranteed party

For purposes of this amendment, **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** is treated as an additional
customer covered by the guarantee.

## Amount

The aggregate guarantee amount remains **$400,000**.

## Term

This amendment is effective from 2026-03-04 through 2027-12-31.
