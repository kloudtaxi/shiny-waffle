# Finance Credit Approval Delegation — Strategic Accounts

To streamline Strategic account reviews, the Finance organization delegates credit approval
authority for Strategic customers to the VP Sales role for requests up to **$750,000**.

For requests submitted by the VP Sales, the VP Sales may approve the request directly. The normal
manager-concurrence requirement does not apply where the VP Sales is the requestor, provided the
request concerns an existing Strategic account and remains within the delegated amount.

This delegation applies to credit requests dated on or after 2026-04-15.

**Delegated role:** VP Sales  
**Delegating role:** Director of Finance  
**Delegated authority:** Up to $750,000  
**Effective:** 2026-04-15
