# BlueRiver Logistics — Credit Payment History Exception

**Customer:** Blue River Logistics LLC  
**ERP customer:** C-1002  
**CRM account:** CRM-2051

Finance has approved a temporary payment-history exception for BlueRiver Logistics in
connection with the 2026 credit review.

The late payment on **INV-62147** is excluded from the 2026 payment-history test because the
delay resulted from a Northstar billing reconciliation issue rather than customer non-payment.

For purposes of credit-limit eligibility, the invoice should therefore be treated as having
satisfied the applicable payment-history requirement.

The exception remains effective until withdrawn by Finance.

**Approved by:** Elena Novak, Director of Finance
