Enterprise Account Executive: up to 8% discount.
VP Sales: above 8% and up to 18% discount.
Chief Revenue Officer: above 18% discount.
Discounts are calculated against the current list price. Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed. Discounts greater than 8% require recorded approval evidence. Approval Authority Matrix (the operational reference).
