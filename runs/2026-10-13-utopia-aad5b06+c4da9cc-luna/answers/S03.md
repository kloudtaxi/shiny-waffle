## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve 15% for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. Acme’s 15% pricing exception applies only to the NS-500 controller, not to NS-Cloud, and it provides eligibility rather than approval authority. [3] The agreement also limits the stated strategic-account pricing eligibility to NS-500. [4] The sources don’t specify whether Sarah Chen has separate authority to approve an NS-Cloud discount.
