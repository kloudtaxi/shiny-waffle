> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

The 15% discount is commercially eligible: Acme’s exception covers up to 15% off NS-500 list price through March 31, 2028. [1] However, that exception does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee.
