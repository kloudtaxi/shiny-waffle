## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. On 2026-09-23, Acme’s NS-500 exception allowed a maximum discount of 15%, effective through 2028-03-31; 18% exceeds that limit. [3] The agreement also caps NS-500 discounts at 15%. [6]

DR-9001 is currently recorded at 15% and is pending approval, so the sources do not show approval for that request—or any authority to approve an 18% discount. [5] [3]
