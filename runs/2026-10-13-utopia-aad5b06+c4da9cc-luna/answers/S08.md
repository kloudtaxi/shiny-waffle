## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

CRM-2048 is the CRM account for **Acme Manufacturing**. [2][6]  
C-1001 is the ERP bill-to record for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, which the agreement identifies as doing business as Acme Manufacturing. [1][3]  
ACME-MFG-2025 is the Master Supply Agreement between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. [1]  
So those identifiers refer to the same Acme customer. [1][2][3][6]

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer**: it has a separate CRM account (CRM-2091), ERP ID (C-1044), and Akron, Ohio address. [3][6]
