## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing is eligible for up to 15% off list price on the NS-500 because its Master Supply Agreement provides for that discount, as set out in Schedule B. The pricing exception applies specifically to the NS-500 and is effective from April 1, 2025 through March 31, 2028; it superseded the earlier 10% exception for requests dated from April 1, 2025 onward. [1][4]

The evidence is the signed-agreement record and attached pricing exception, which state the product, discount cap, and effective dates. [1][4] This establishes commercial eligibility only; it does not itself authorize an employee to approve a discount. [1]
