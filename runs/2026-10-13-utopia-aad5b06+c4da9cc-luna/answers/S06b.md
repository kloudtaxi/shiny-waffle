## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing’s maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 was **10% of list price** on 2024-10-01. The 10% exception covered orders placed through 2025-03-31; the later 15% exception did not take effect until 2025-04-01. [1][3]
