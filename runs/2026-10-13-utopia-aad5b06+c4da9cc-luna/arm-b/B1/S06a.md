## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller is **15% of list price**.

- **Where it comes from:** Pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception." It's Schedule B to the Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025. It took effect on 2025-04-01 and runs until 2028-03-31, so it covers 2026-09-23. Chief Revenue Officer David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20.
- **Scope:** It applies to the NS-500 only, not to other Northstar products such as NS-Cloud or NS-Edge.
- **The older 10% limit doesn't apply.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under the earlier agreement ACME-SA-2023, allowed up to 10%, but only for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The 15% exception replaced it for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. The old document still says "Status: Active," but that's out of date.
- **15% is the most Acme can get, not an approved discount.** Both the exception and section 3.3 of the agreement say each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies. On the same date, Sarah Chen submitted request DR-9001 for a 15% discount on a $250,000 NS-500 order. That works out to $37,500 off, for a net of $212,500. The request is within the contractual limit, but it was still listed as "Pending Approval."
