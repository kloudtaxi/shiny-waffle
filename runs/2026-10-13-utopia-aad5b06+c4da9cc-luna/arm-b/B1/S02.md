## Answer

Not on the contract alone. Acme's agreement only covers up to 15% on the NS-500, so 18% would be an extra discount outside the contract, and Sarah can't approve it herself.

**1. The contract caps Acme at 15%.** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, in force from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) says Acme is eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500. Pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) sets the same maximum, and it applies to the NS-500 only. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests dated from 2025-04-01. Nothing on file covers 18%.

**2. A contract limit isn't an approval.** Section 3.3 of the agreement and the exception's Authority section both say it doesn't change who inside Northstar can approve a discount. The sales discount procedure (SOP-SALES-014) also says a request must not be approved just because the contract allows that discount.

**3. Who would have to approve 18%.** The 2026 Pricing Policy and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to a request dated 2026-09-23:
- Enterprise Account Executives (Sarah Chen's role) can approve up to 10%.
- Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
- Anything above 20% needs the CRO, David Morgan.

So 18% would need Michael Torres's approval, recorded in the CRM. Because it goes beyond the contract's 15%, Deal Desk should also review it as a new exception.

**4. DR-9001 doesn't actually ask for 18%.** The CRM record is for **15%**: $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, status "Pending Approval." Sarah's 2026-09-23 email to Mike also asks for 15%. If the deal moves to 18%, the request needs updating; at 18% the discount would be $45,000, for a net of $205,000.

Sarah's email says 15% "is what we did for them last September." That was under the 2025 policy, when account executives could approve up to 15%. In 2026 their limit is 10%, so even the 15% currently requested needs Michael Torres's approval.

The quickest route is to approve DR-9001 at 15% with Michael Torres's recorded approval. 18% is only possible as a separate exception with VP Sales approval.
