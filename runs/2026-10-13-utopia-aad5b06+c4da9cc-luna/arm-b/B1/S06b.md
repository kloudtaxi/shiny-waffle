## Answer

**10% off list price.** That was Acme Manufacturing's contractual ceiling for the NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2024-10-01.

- **Exception in force on that date:** EXC-ACME-NS500-10 ("Acme NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception", agreement ACME-SA-2023, created 2023-03-12). It lets Acme get up to 10% off list on NS-500 orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. 2024-10-01 falls inside that window.
- **The 15% figure came later and doesn't apply here:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (agreement ACME-MFG-2025) raises the maximum to 15% of list. It only took effect on 2025-04-01 and replaces the 10% exception for requests dated on or after that day.
