## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. Acme is allowed to receive the 15% discount, but Sarah Chen doesn't have the authority to approve it on 2026-09-23. It has to go to the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**Why:**

1. **Her role:** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive and Acme Manufacturing's account owner. She reports to Michael Torres, who is VP Sales.
2. **The policy in effect:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix says the same, adds that "authority attaches to the role, not to the individual or the account," and requires approvals above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM.
3. **Acme's eligibility is a separate question:**
   - The pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to agreement ACME-MFG-2025) lets Acme get up to 15% off list on the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. So the request is commercially allowed.
   - The exception also says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
   - Section 3.3 of the Master Supply Agreement says the same: Acme's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
   - The discount procedure (SOP-SALES-014) adds that a request must not be approved just because the customer's contract permits the discount.

**The request itself:** This is DR-9001: 15% off $250,000 list, a $37,500 discount, for $212,500 net. Sarah submitted it on 2026-09-23 and it's currently "Pending Approval." Michael Torres needs to approve it and record the approval in Northstar CRM.

Sarah approved an identical 15% NS-500 discount for Acme herself on 2025-09-16 (DR-8104). That was allowed then because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The limit dropped to 10% when the 2026 policy took effect, so that earlier approval isn't a precedent here.
