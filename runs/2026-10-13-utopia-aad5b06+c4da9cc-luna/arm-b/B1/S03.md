## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23. It's above her authority, and no contract term covers that product at 15%.

It replaced the 2025 policy in full for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. The 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix (APP-MATRIX-2026) set these limits:
- Enterprise Account Executive: up to and including 10%
- VP Sales: more than 10% and up to and including 20%
- Chief Revenue Officer: more than 20%

At 15%, the request needs VP Sales approval, which is Michael Torres. Under the old 2025 policy an Account Executive could approve up to 15%. That's how Sarah approved her own 15% request (DR-8104) on 2025-09-16, but that limit no longer applies.

**2. The Acme pricing exception doesn't cover NS-Cloud.** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) does include NS-CLOUD. However, the 15% pricing in clause 3.2 and Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller. The exception says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product," so Acme isn't contractually eligible for 15% on NS-Cloud.

**3. A contract wouldn't change who approves anyway.** Clause 3.3 of the agreement and the Sales Discount SOP (SOP-SALES-014) both say a customer's contract terms don't change an employee's approval authority. Even for NS-500, where Acme does qualify for 15%, a 15% discount in 2026 still needs VP Sales approval.
