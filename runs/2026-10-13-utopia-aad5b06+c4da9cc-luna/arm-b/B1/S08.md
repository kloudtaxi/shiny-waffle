## Answer

All three IDs refer to the same customer: **Acme Manufacturing**, whose legal name is Acme Mfg. Holdings, based in Milwaukee, WI. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**

**How the three IDs connect**
- **CRM-2048** is the CRM account "Acme Manufacturing." It's a Strategic account in Midwest Enterprise, owned by Sarah Chen, billing to 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI, with DUNS 04-812-7730.
- **C-1001** is the ERP bill-to customer "Acme Mfg. Holdings." It has the same address and the same DUNS number (04-812-7730), with Net 45 payment terms.
- **ACME-MFG-2025** is the Master Supply Agreement, effective 2025-04-01 through 2028-03-31. It names "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing" at the same Milwaukee address. The NS-500 15% pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) is attached to it as Schedule B.
- The 2026 account strategy document ties them together directly: "CRM account: CRM-2048 … ERP bill-to: Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001)."

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company**
