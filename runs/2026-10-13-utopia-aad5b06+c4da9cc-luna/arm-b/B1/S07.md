## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing qualifies because its current contract with Northstar includes a pricing exception that allows up to 15% off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. The request DR-9001 is dated 2026-09-23, which falls within that exception's term.

**Evidence**

1. **Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025** (signed by David Morgan, CRO, on 2025-03-20 and by Edward Floyd, Acme's VP Procurement, on 2025-03-22)
   - It runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, and the NS-500 is one of the products it covers.
   - §3.2, "Strategic account pricing," says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."

2. **Pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15** (this is Schedule B; approved by David Morgan, CRO, on 2025-03-20)
   - It names Acme Manufacturing as the customer and covers the NS-500 only, with a maximum discount of 15% of list price.
   - It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31.
   - It does not apply to any other Northstar product.
   - It replaces the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed up to 10% on orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01.

3. **The request itself, DR-9001**
   - It is logged under CRM-2048, which is Acme Manufacturing's Strategic account. A separate account called "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091) also exists, but it isn't the one on this request.
   - The request is for the NS-500 at 15% off: $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
   - Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 says Acme wants the NS-500 for a third plant and is asking for 15% off list.

**Eligibility doesn't mean approval**
- Both the exception and §3.3 of the agreement say they establish commercial eligibility only. Each discount still has to be approved under Northstar's internal policies.
- The 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix apply because the request is dated 2026-09-23. Under them, an Account Executive can approve up to 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs **VP Sales** approval and must be recorded in the CRM.
- So DR-9001, currently "Pending Approval," needs VP Sales sign-off. Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.
- The 2025 policy did let Account Executives approve up to 15%, but it ended on 2025-12-31.
