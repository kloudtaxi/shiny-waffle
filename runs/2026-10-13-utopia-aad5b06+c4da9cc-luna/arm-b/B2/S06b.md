## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **Where it comes from:** the 2023 pricing exception (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`, exception ID EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It says: "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." The date you asked about falls inside that period.
- **The 15% figure doesn't apply on that date:** a later exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, raised the maximum to 15% of list price and replaced the 10% one. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, but it only runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31.
- **In dollars:** the NS-500 lists at $250,000, so 10% is at most $25,000 off per unit. That's my own calculation, not a figure stated in the records.
