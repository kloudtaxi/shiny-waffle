## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No, 18% isn't supported. The most Acme Manufacturing can get on the NS-500 on 2026-09-23 is 15%.

**Why 18% doesn't fit:**
- **Ceiling:** Acme's discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller comes from the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (EXC-ACME-NS500-15). It sets the maximum eligible discount at **15% of list price**. It was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20 and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers 2026-09-23. It also replaces the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement with Acme Mfg. Holdings runs over the same dates (April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028).
- **Approval:** The exception only sets which discounts Acme is eligible for. It does not give anyone the authority to approve them. The pricing policy says discounts are calculated against the current list price and need VP Sales approval (and in some cases Chief Revenue Officer approval), plus recorded evidence of that approval. I couldn't find the percentage thresholds that decide who approves what, so I can't say whether a higher discount could go through a separate escalation.

**DR-9001 isn't an 18% request.** The record asks for **15%**:
- List price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500
- Requested 2026-09-23 by sarah.chen@northstar.example
- Justification: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"
- Status: Pending Approval

The customer conversation that day also mentions Acme asking for "15% off list". At 18% the discount would be $45,000 and the net $205,000. That's beyond the exception and doesn't match the request on file.

15% is within the exception. Even so, DR-9001 is still pending and needs the required approval on record before it's final. Anything above 15% would need a new or amended exception, and I found nothing in the records that allows that.
