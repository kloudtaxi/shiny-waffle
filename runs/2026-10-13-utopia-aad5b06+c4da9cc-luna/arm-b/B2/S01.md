## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No.** Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount on her own on 2026-09-23. Acme is allowed to receive up to 15% on the NS-500, but someone more senior has to approve it.

**Why she can't approve it**
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. She owns the Acme Manufacturing account (CRM-2048, Strategic tier) and reports to Michael Torres, VP Sales.
- Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applied on 2026-09-23. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaced the 2025 policy. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts of **10% or less**. Larger discounts need VP Sales approval, the biggest ones need Chief Revenue Officer approval, and every approval needs recorded evidence. The records I found don't give the exact cutoffs between the VP and CRO tiers.

**Why the discount itself is allowed**
- Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 ("Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception") lets Acme receive up to **15% of list price** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covered 2026-09-23. It replaced an earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- The exception says it covers only eligibility and **does not grant approval authority**. It was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20. Sarah Chen is listed as having prepared it, but the system didn't link that name to the Sarah Chen in the staff records. Either way, preparing it doesn't give her any authority to approve.

**Background:** Acme asked for 15% off list for the NS-500 on 2026-09-23. The list price is $250,000, so 15% would take it to about $212,500.

**Next step:** Sarah should send the request to Michael Torres (VP Sales) and have his approval recorded. It goes to the CRO only if 15% falls in the CRO tier.
