## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

All three IDs refer to the same customer, **Acme Manufacturing** of Milwaukee, WI. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.** The knowledge base doesn't store them as one linked record, so the match below is based on shared details.

**How each ID maps**
- **CRM-2048** is the CRM account ID for Acme Manufacturing. It's a Strategic-tier account in the Midwest segment, owned by Sarah Chen, billed to 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI, with D-U-N-S number 04-812-7730.
- **C-1001** is the ERP customer ID for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. That's the ERP billing name, and the same company signed the Master Supply Agreement with Northstar Industrial Systems. It has the same D-U-N-S number (04-812-7730) and the same Milwaukee billing address as CRM-2048, so it's the same company under its legal/billing name.
- **ACME-MFG-2025** is cited, through its Schedule B, in the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" document. That document names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. Its dates (effective 2025-04-01, expiring 2028-03-31) match the Master Supply Agreement, so it's most likely that agreement's reference number. This one is the least certain: the knowledge base only says it's mentioned in that document and has no direct link to the customer.

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer**
| | Acme Manufacturing / Mfg. Holdings | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| CRM ID | CRM-2048 | CRM-2091 |
| ERP ID | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| D-U-N-S number | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |
| Billing address | 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209 | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310 |
| Tier / industry | Strategic, industrial manufacturing | Standard, mid-market industrial distribution |
| Account owner | Sarah Chen | Zachary Brown |

Both are in the Midwest segment and both have Net 45 payment terms, but every identifier is different.

Watch out for "Acme Industrial", the name on a support case opened 2026-09-14 for an Akron, OH site. That case carries CRM-2091, so it belongs to Acme Industrial Supply Co., not Acme Manufacturing. The same applies to the NS-500 discount exception and the Master Supply Agreement: they're Acme Manufacturing's and shouldn't be applied to Acme Industrial Supply.
