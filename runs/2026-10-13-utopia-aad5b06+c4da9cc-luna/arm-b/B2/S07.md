## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing can get up to 15% off list on the NS-500 Industrial Controller because of a signed strategic account exception that is currently active. That makes them eligible, but it doesn't approve any specific deal.

**The exception (the evidence)**
- **Document:** "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception", ID **EXC-ACME-NS500-15**.
- **Who it covers:** Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, Strategic tier, Midwest, Milwaukee, WI) and the NS-500 Industrial Controller only.
- **Discount:** at most **15% of list price**. The NS-500 lists at $250,000, so the most it allows is $37,500 off, for a price of $212,500 per unit.
- **Dates:** in effect from **2025-04-01** until **2028-03-31**, so it still applies today (2026-10-13).
- **Approval:** approved by **David Morgan**, Chief Revenue Officer of Northstar Industrial Systems, on **2025-03-20**. It was prepared by **Sarah Chen**, who is also Acme's account owner.
- **Earlier exception:** it replaces the old **EXC-ACME-NS500-10**. The records also show an earlier NS-500 discount right for Acme from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That window ends the day before the 15% exception starts, which fits it being the old 10% one, though the record doesn't say the rate outright.

**Limits the exception sets**
- It **does not apply to other Northstar products**, only the NS-500.
- It **establishes commercial eligibility but does not grant approval authority**. Any deal at 15% still has to go through the normal discount approval process.
- 15% is a **maximum**, not a fixed discount.

**Current situation:** In a 2026-09-23 message from Gina, Acme said it wants to go ahead with the NS-500 controller and asked for "15% off list" under their agreement. That request is within the exception's limit and dates.

I found no corrections or withdrawn records affecting any of this.
