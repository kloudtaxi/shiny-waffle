## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve 15% for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23.

1. **Her limit is 10%.** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales and reports to Michael Torres. She is also Acme's account owner, but that doesn't raise her limit. Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, which runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaces the 2025 policy, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts of **10% or less**. 15% is above that, so she has to send it up the chain.
2. **Acme's 15% exception doesn't cover this product.** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and allows up to 15% off list. But it:
   - covers only the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**, not other Northstar products such as NS-Cloud;
   - only makes Acme eligible for the discount. It **does not give anyone the authority to approve it**, so even on the NS-500, 15% would still need sign-off above an account executive.
3. **Who has to approve instead.** The 2026 policy sends larger discounts to **VP Sales**, and some to the **Chief Revenue Officer**. The knowledge base doesn't give the exact percentage where each level starts, so I can't tell you which of the two a 15% request needs. That cutoff is in the Approval Authority Matrix document. Either way, any approval above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM with the approval evidence.

The list price is $120,000, so 15% comes to $18,000 off, for $102,000 net. That's $6,000 more than the $12,000 she could approve on her own at 10%.
