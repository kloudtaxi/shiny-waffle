## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

**Very likely not. She could prepare or request it, but the approval would have had to come from someone above her.** One caveat on the exact limit is below.

**What allowed the discount:**
- The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covered 2025-09-23.
- It lets Acme Manufacturing receive up to **15% off list** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It replaced an earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. Sarah Chen is listed as the person who prepared it.
- The exception **sets commercial eligibility but explicitly does not grant approval authority.** It makes 15% allowed for Acme, but it doesn't give Sarah the right to sign off on it.

**Sarah's authority:**
- She is an Enterprise Account Executive, Acme's account owner, and reports to Michael Torres (VP Sales).
- On 2025-09-23 the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applied (in effect 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts only within a set limit. Larger discounts need VP Sales approval and must be calculated against current list price.

**Caveat:** The knowledge base doesn't record the 2025 policy's actual percentage for Enterprise Account Executives. The only stated limit, "≤10%", comes from a source tied to the 2026 policy. If 2025 had the same 10% limit, a 15% discount was beyond her authority and needed Michael Torres's approval. Under the 2026 policy, 15% may also need Chief Revenue Officer approval and recorded approval evidence, but that policy didn't apply yet in September 2025.

If you need a definite answer, check the 2025 policy's limit for Enterprise Account Executives and look for a recorded VP Sales approval on the Acme request.
