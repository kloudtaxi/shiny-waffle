## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 all point to the same customer: **Acme Manufacturing**, based in Milwaukee. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**

**How each identifier links up**
- **CRM-2048** is the CRM account ID of **Acme Manufacturing** (also written "ACME Manufacturing" or just "Acme"). It's a Strategic-tier account in the Midwest segment, owned by Sarah Chen. Billing address: 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI. DUNS 04-812-7730.
- **C-1001** is the ERP customer ID of **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, which is the billing and legal name for the same company. It has the same address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209), the same DUNS (04-812-7730), payment terms of Net 45, and it signed the Master Supply Agreement with Northstar Industrial Systems.
- **ACME-MFG-2025** is an agreement. It shows up in the Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15), which refers to "Schedule B to agreement ACME-MFG-2025" and names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. One caveat: the knowledge base ties this ID to the customer only through that exception document. It never says outright that ACME-MFG-2025 is the Master Supply Agreement, although that seems likely.

The CRM name and the ERP name are stored as two separate records. What links them is the shared DUNS number and billing address.

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer**

| | Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| CRM account | CRM-2048 | CRM-2091 |
| ERP customer | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| DUNS | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |
| Billing address | 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310 |
| Tier / industry | Strategic, Enterprise Industrial Manufacturing | Standard, Mid-Market Industrial Distribution |
| Account owner | Sarah Chen | Zachary Brown |

They only look alike because of the name. All their identifiers are different. The "Acme Industrial" support case (opened 2026-09-14, site Akron, OH, CRM-2091) belongs to Acme Industrial Supply Co., not Acme Manufacturing. That means the 15% NS-500 exception for Acme Manufacturing doesn't apply to Acme Industrial Supply Co.
