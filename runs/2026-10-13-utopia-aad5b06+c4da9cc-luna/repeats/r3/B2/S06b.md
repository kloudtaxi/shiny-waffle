## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **Source:** The 2023 pricing exception (`acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`, exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10) says: *"Acme Manufacturing may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."* 2024-10-01 is inside that period.
- **Why it's not 15%:** A later exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (`acme_pricing_exception.md`), raises the limit to 15% of list price and repeals the 10% exception. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, but it only takes effect on 2025-04-01 and runs until 2028-03-31. So it didn't apply on 2024-10-01.

For reference, the NS-500 list price is $250,000, so 10% would be at most $25,000 off per unit. Also, the newer exception says it doesn't grant approval authority, and a separate rule says discounts still need approval under Northstar's internal policies.
