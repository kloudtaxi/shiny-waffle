> Can Sarah Chen's 15% NS-500 discount for Acme (DR-9001) be approved on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**Not on Sarah Chen's own authority.** Under the 2026 pricing policy, a 15% discount has to be approved by someone above her, so it can't simply go through on 2026-09-23 because the Acme contract allows it.

**The request (DR-9001):** Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive) asked on 2026-09-23 for 15% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller for Acme Manufacturing (account CRM-2048). That takes $250,000 list down to $212,500, a $37,500 discount. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." Its status is still **Pending Approval**.

**Why she can't approve it herself:**
1. **The 2026 policy applies.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and repeals the 2025 policy, so it covers a request dated 2026-09-23.
2. **Larger discounts need a senior approver.** Account executives may approve discounts only up to a limit. Bigger ones need VP Sales approval, and the biggest need the Chief Revenue Officer. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM with evidence. The knowledge base doesn't hold the exact percentage bands from the Approval Authority Matrix, so I can't tell you whether 15% stops at VP Sales or has to go on to the CRO.
3. **The contract isn't enough on its own.** The discount review procedure says a request "must not be approved solely because the requested commercial discount is permitted by a customer contract." It also says the contract doesn't change who has the authority to approve. Acme did negotiate a 15% NS-500 price, which the Deal Desk confirmed, but that makes Acme eligible for the discount. It doesn't let Sarah sign off on it.

**The 2025 approval doesn't carry over.** DR-8104 was an identical 15% NS-500 discount for Acme, which Sarah approved herself on 2025-09-16. That happened under the 2025 policy, which has since been repealed.

**Next step:** Send DR-9001 to her manager, Michael Torres (VP Sales). If 15% falls in the CRO band of the matrix, it also needs the CRO's sign-off. The approval should be recorded in Northstar CRM. If you need certainty on the CRO question, check the actual percentage bands in the Approval Authority Matrix of the 2026 policy.
