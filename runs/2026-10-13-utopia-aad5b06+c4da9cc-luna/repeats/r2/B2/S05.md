> Can Sarah Chen's 15% NS-500 discount for Acme (DR-9001) be approved on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. A 15% discount is above what an Enterprise Account Executive is allowed to approve, so DR-9001 needs VP Sales approval.

- **Policy in force:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, so it covers 2026-09-23. It replaces the 2025 policy.
- **Who can approve what:**
  - Enterprise Account Executive: up to 10%
  - VP Sales: over 10% and up to 20%
  - Chief Revenue Officer: over 20%
- **Sarah's role:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive and the account owner for Acme. She reports to Michael Torres, who is VP Sales.
- **DR-9001:** 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), requested on 2026-09-23. It's still "Pending Approval." The 15% falls in the VP Sales range, so Michael Torres would need to approve it.
- **The Acme contract doesn't change this.** The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." The policy says a request must not be approved just because a customer contract allows the discount, and that being eligible under a contract doesn't change who has authority to approve.
- **Records:** The policy also requires recorded approval evidence, and any approval above 10% has to be logged in Northstar CRM.

Sarah did approve her own identical request, DR-8104 (15% on the same product for Acme), on 2025-09-16. The knowledge base doesn't have the 2025 policy's approval limits, so I can't say whether that was allowed then. Either way, it doesn't set a precedent under the 2026 policy.

There's also a gap in the records: the knowledge base has no details on the Acme Master Supply Agreement itself, such as its terms or whether it's active. The account notes do say Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing of 15%, and that the Deal Desk was to confirm it. Whoever approves should still check that the contract is active, as the policy requires. Even if it is, it doesn't let Sarah approve the discount herself.
