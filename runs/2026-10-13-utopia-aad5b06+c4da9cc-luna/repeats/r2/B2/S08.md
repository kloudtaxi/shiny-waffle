## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

**All three IDs point to the same customer: Acme Manufacturing**, a Milwaukee-based industrial manufacturer. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.** The knowledge base files the Acme records under three names, but the shared DUNS number and address show they are one company:

| ID | System | Record name | What ties it to Acme Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CRM-2048** | CRM account | Acme Manufacturing (also "ACME Manufacturing", "Acme") | Strategic tier, Midwest, owner Sarah Chen; DUNS 04-812-7730; 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI |
| **C-1001** | ERP customer | Acme Mfg. Holdings | Same DUNS 04-812-7730 and same address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209); Net 45 terms |
| **ACME-MFG-2025** | Agreement | Master Supply Agreement (also "Acme Master Supply Agreement") | Mentioned in the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception", whose customer is Acme Manufacturing. Northstar signed the Master Supply Agreement with Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). It runs from 1 Apr 2025 to 31 Mar 2028, which matches the exception's dates. |

One caveat: no record directly says "ACME-MFG-2025 = Master Supply Agreement." I matched them because they share the customer and the same 2025–2028 dates.

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is separate:**
- **Different IDs:** CRM account CRM-2091 and ERP customer C-1044.
- **Different DUNS number:** 09-552-1187.
- **Different address:** 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310.
- **Different profile:** Standard tier (not Strategic), mid-market industrial distribution (not manufacturing).
- **Different account owner:** Zachary Brown (not Sarah Chen).
- **Its own support case:** it appears in a support record as "Acme Industrial" (Akron, OH, NS-Edge Monitoring Package, opened 2026-09-14).

The only thing the two customers have in common is "Acme" in the name. Records for Acme Industrial Supply Co. shouldn't be merged into Acme Manufacturing, and the Acme Manufacturing agreement and NS-500 exception don't apply to it.
