## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** On 2026-09-23 the most Acme Manufacturing can get on the NS-500 is **15% off list**.

**Why 18% is out:**
- The **Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15)** sets the "Maximum eligible discount" at **15% of list price**. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It matches the term of the Master Supply Agreement and replaces the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- 18% is above that cap, so we can't offer it as contract pricing.

**DR-9001 doesn't ask for 18%:**
- The request in the knowledge base asks for **15%**: $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net. Its date is 2026-09-23, Sarah Chen requested it, and it cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement as justification.
- In the call notes from 2026-09-23, Acme also asked for "15% off list." Please check where the 18% came from. At 18% the discount would be $45,000 and the net $205,000.

**15% still needs sign-off:**
- The exception sets what Acme is eligible for, but it says it "does not grant approval authority." The discount still has to be approved under Northstar's internal policies.
- Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31):
  - Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%.
  - VP Sales approves above 10% and up to 20%.
  - The Chief Revenue Officer approves above 20%.
- Approval evidence must be recorded. DR-9001 is still **"Pending Approval,"** so it needs VP Sales sign-off.

**If you really need 18%:** VP Sales has authority up to 20%, but the exception's cap would still apply. You'd need a new or amended exception first. The knowledge base doesn't say who approves an amendment. For reference, the current exception was approved by David Morgan, who is listed as Chief Revenue Officer.
