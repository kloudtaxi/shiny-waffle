## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

I can't confirm the number, but the evidence points to **10% off list** for Acme Manufacturing's NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01. The knowledge base never states that percentage directly.

**What applied on that date:**
- Acme's 2023 supply agreement (ACME-SA-2023, status "Active") says Acme "may receive" a discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller from **2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31**. 2024-10-01 falls inside that window. The knowledge base records that the discount exists but not its amount.
- The 15% figure doesn't apply to that date. The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) sets a maximum of "15% of list price," but it only took effect on **2025-04-01** and runs to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20.

**Where the 10% comes from:**
- The 15% exception **repeals an earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10**. That earlier exception must be the one that covered 2023–2025, and its ID suggests a 10% cap.
- There's also a separate note that "Approvals above 10%" must be recorded in Northstar CRM, which fits a 10% ceiling.

To confirm the exact figure, check the ACME-SA-2023 agreement document or the EXC-ACME-NS500-10 record itself.
