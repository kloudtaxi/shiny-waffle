> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

I've pulled the account, the exception record and the related approval facts, so I'm writing up the answer now.

Acme Manufacturing can get up to 15% off the NS-500 because of a named exception for their account, not because of a general policy. The exception makes them eligible for the discount but doesn't approve it on any particular deal.

**Where the 15% comes from: exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15**
- **Name:** "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception."
- **Customer and product:** Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), NS-500 Industrial Controller only.
- **Maximum discount:** 15% of list price. List price is $250,000, so the most they can get is $37,500 off, for a net price of $212,500.
- **Effective dates:** 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is in effect today (2026-10-13).
- **Approved by:** David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20.
- **Prepared by:** Sarah Chen, who owns the Acme account.

**Why Acme got an exception**
- Acme is a **Strategic** account in the Midwest segment, in enterprise industrial manufacturing, billed in Milwaukee, WI. The exception's name ties it to that status.

**It replaced an older 10% exception**
- The earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, said Acme "may receive" a discount on the NS-500 from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The 15% exception started the next day, 2025-04-01, and is recorded as repealing the 10% one. That record doesn't show the direction of the repeal, but the dates make it clear the 15% exception is the current one.

**Limits**
- It **establishes commercial eligibility**. It **does not grant approval authority**, so a 15% quote still needs approval through the normal process.
- It **does not apply to other Northstar products**, such as NS-Cloud or NS-Edge.
- Approvals above 10% **must be recorded in Northstar CRM**.

**Current deal**
- On 2026-09-23, Acme (in a message from someone named Gina) said they want to move ahead with the NS-500 and asked for "15% off list." That is exactly the exception's maximum, so the request is within it but still needs approval.

**Sources:** the exception document, the CRO's approval note of 2025-03-20, the CRM account record, the price list, the earlier 10% exception and the September 2026 customer message.

I didn't open the discount policy itself, so I can't say who has to approve a 15% discount. That policy is where to check.
