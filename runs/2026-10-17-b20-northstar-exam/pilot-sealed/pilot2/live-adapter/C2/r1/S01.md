## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it's above her personal limit. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve DR-9001.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 is in Northstar CRM exactly as you gave it: CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on the customer's contract: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. They're the same customer.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
5. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B is EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The CRO, David Morgan, approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It covers this product and date, and 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception also says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to all of 2026 and fully replaced the 2025 policy for requests from 2026-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, that role "may approve discounts up to and including 10%." 15% is over her limit.
8. **Who must approve:** The 2026 approval table gives VP Sales the band above 10% up to 20%. Michael Torres (EMP-200) is VP Sales and Sarah's manager.

Sarah approved the same 15% for Acme herself last September (DR-8104). That request fell under a different pricing policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%. That limit ended on 2026-01-01, so the earlier approval doesn't help here.
