> Can Sarah Chen's request CR-9201 to raise Acme Manufacturing's credit limit from $250,000 to $400,000 be approved on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

Yes, CR-9201 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen. Acme qualifies for $400,000, and the policy requires the Finance Manager, Priya Shah, to sign it off.

**1. Request.** The record in Northstar ERP matches what you sent: CR-9201, ERP customer C-1001, $250,000 to $400,000, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. It rests on Acme's own standing. Sarah's note says only that "If they need more headroom later, Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it," so the guarantee isn't needed for this decision.

**2. Customer.** These are all the same company:
- ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings," doing business as Acme Manufacturing.
- In the CRM it is account CRM-2048, tier **Strategic**.
- Both records share the DUNS number 04-812-7730.

**3. Policy.** The request is dated 2026-09-23, so the original **Northstar Credit Policy 2026** applies (effective 2026-01-01). Revision 2 only covers requests dated 2026-10-01 or later. In the knowledge base, the original policy's text now survives only as superseded wording in the same document as revision 2. I relied on that wording. On the points that matter here, revision 2 sets the same approval bands and a higher maximum, so it would not change the result.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. C-1001 had two invoices due in that window, and both were paid early:
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.

No invoice is late.

**5. Maximum limit.** The original policy allows up to **$750,000** for Strategic accounts. $400,000 is within that, so Acme is eligible without any guarantee.

**6. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives her role no credit approval authority, and it says "No one may approve or concur on a credit request they submitted."

**7. Approvers.** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. That is **Priya Shah**, who did not submit the request. VP Sales sign-off is only needed for Strategic limits above $500,000, so it doesn't apply.
