**1. The request.** The ERP record matches what you sent: customer C-1001, $250,000 to $400,000, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. It rests on Acme's own standing. In her email to Priya, Sarah mentions the Acme Group parent guarantee only as cover if Acme needs "more headroom later." So the guarantee isn't part of this decision.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings") and CRM account CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing") are the same company: same address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee) and same DUNS number (04-812-7730). CRM records it as a **Strategic** account. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091), which is a separate Standard account in Akron.

**3. The policy.** The policy in force on 2026-09-23 is the original Northstar Credit Policy 2026, which ran from 2026-01-01. Revision 2 only applies to requests dated on or after 2026-10-01. The policy file has since been overwritten with revision 2. I took the original's terms from facts the knowledge base recorded from the earlier version, which it now flags as stale:
- Payment rule: no invoice may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
- Maximum limit: $750,000 for Strategic accounts.
- Accounts Receivable Specialists can approve up to $100,000. Limits over $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. Above $500,000, the Director of Finance must approve, with VP Sales concurrence for Strategic accounts.
- No one may approve a request they submitted.

**4. Payment history (12 months to 2026-09-23).** No invoice was paid late.
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, so it wasn't late on the request date.

**5. Maximum limit.** $400,000 is within the $750,000 Strategic maximum, so Acme qualifies without any guarantee.

**6. The requestor's authority.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, a role with no credit approval authority.

**7. Who must approve.** $400,000 falls in the Finance Manager band, and no concurrence is needed below $500,000. Priya Shah is the Finance Manager and didn't submit the request, so the self-approval rule doesn't block her. Revision 2 sets the same band and the same 30-day rule, so the answer would be the same under it.

Two notes that don't stop the decision:
- INV-62305 alone ($720,000) is far above both the current and the requested limit. Finance may want to look at that exposure.
- If anyone later relies on the Acme Group guarantee, it has to be found and checked first, including which company it names.
