**1. The request.** `credit_requests.csv` has no CR-9208. The only pending Acme request (customer C-1001) is **CR-9201**. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 to go from $250,000 to **$400,000**, with the same justification text as your copy. I decided on CR-9208 as you gave it, not on CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9208 exists is a blocking condition. The request relies on Acme's own standing; its justification doesn't mention a guarantee. The ERP shows C-1001's current limit as $250,000, which matches your figure.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" and CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". They share the same address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee) and tax ID (04-812-7730), so they are the same company. The CRM tier is **Strategic**. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091, Akron) is a different company.

**3. The policy.** The policy in force on 2026-09-23 was **Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (CREDIT-POLICY-2026)**, the first version of that document, effective 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31. Revision 2 only applies to requests dated on or after 2026-10-01, so it doesn't govern this one.

**4. Payment history.** Acme's invoices from the 12 months before the request:
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, early.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, early.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) was not yet due on the request date (due 2026-10-02).

There are no late invoices. I couldn't retrieve the exact lateness threshold in this policy version, but it doesn't matter because every invoice was paid before its due date.

**5. Maximum limit.** The policy caps Strategic accounts at $750,000, and $650,000 is under that, so Acme qualifies without credit support. The Acme Group parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026: $400,000, covering Acme Mfg. Holdings, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) would raise the cap to $1,150,000, but this request doesn't need it.

**6. Requestor's authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales. Under the policy, only Finance roles can approve credit limits, so he has no approval authority.

**7. Who must approve.**
- **Approval:** a limit above $500,000 needs the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**.
- **Concurrence:** a Strategic account above $500,000 also needs VP Sales concurrence. Michael Torres holds that role, but no one may approve or concur on their own request. The concurrence passes to his manager, **David Morgan**, Chief Revenue Officer.
