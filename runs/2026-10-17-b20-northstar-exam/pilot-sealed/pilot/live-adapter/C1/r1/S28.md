## Answer

**Yes, provided the right people approve it.** On 2026-09-23 Acme Manufacturing qualifies for a $1,000,000 limit. But Sarah Chen can't approve it herself: it needs Elena Novak (Director of Finance) to approve and Michael Torres (VP Sales) to concur. One problem must be fixed first: **CR-9203 isn't in the Northstar ERP credit-request records.** The only open request for this customer (C-1001) is CR-9201, also from Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, for $250,000 → $400,000. So I decided CR-9203 as you gave it, not CR-9201, and made confirming that it exists a blocking condition.

1. **The request:** CR-9203 for C-1001, from $250,000 (matches the ERP limit) to $1,000,000, dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. It relies on the parent guarantee.
2. **The customer:** The ERP name is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). The CRM account is CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", with tier **Strategic**. The legal documents say "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing". All three share the same address and DUNS number (04-812-7730), so they are the same company. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company.
3. **The policy:** For a request dated 2026-09-23, the original Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) applies. Revision 2 only applies to requests dated on or after 2026-10-01. The file now shows only revision 2, so I took the original's terms from the knowledge base's saved quotes of its first version:
   - Strategic maximum: $750,000.
   - Payment rule: no invoice paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
   - Over $500,000 needs the Director of Finance, plus VP Sales concurrence for Strategic accounts.
4. **Payment history:** C-1001's invoices due in the 12 months before the request were all paid early:
   - INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02.
   
   Nothing was late, so the customer passes.
5. **Maximum limit:** $750,000 (Strategic) plus $400,000 from guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 = **$1,150,000**. That guarantee is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc., covers only Acme Mfg. Holdings, and runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it was in force on the request date. $1,000,000 is within the maximum.
6. **Sarah's authority:** None. She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and only Accounts Receivable Specialists can approve limits alone, up to $100,000.
7. **Who must approve:** Elena Novak (Director of Finance) approves and Michael Torres (VP Sales) concurs. Neither submitted the request, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't affect them.

**Also worth knowing (not blocking):**
- CR-9201 is still pending for the same customer and needs to be resolved alongside CR-9203.
- As of today (2026-10-17), INV-62305 ($720,000, due 2026-10-02) is still unpaid with no payment recorded. That doesn't change the result as of the request date. But if it's still unpaid on 2026-11-01, it will break the 30-day payment rule.
