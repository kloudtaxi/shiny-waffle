## Answer

**Yes, it can be approved, but only by Priya Shah, the Finance Manager.** Acme qualifies for $400,000 on its own record. Sarah Chen has no authority to approve credit limits, so she can't approve it herself.

1. **The request.** CR-9201 in Northstar ERP: customer C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, submitted by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". The ERP current limit is $250,000, which matches the request. The request rests on Acme's own record. Sarah's email to Priya mentions Acme Group's parent guarantee only as cover "if they need more headroom later", so the request doesn't depend on it.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" and CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" are the same company: same address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee) and same tax ID (04-812-7730). The CRM tier is **Strategic**. C-1044 "Acme Industrial Supply Co." is a different company.
3. **The policy.** The policy in force is the original Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (effective 2026-01-01). Revision 2 replaces it only for requests dated on or after 2026-10-01, so it doesn't apply here. The knowledge base now holds only revision 2's text. The original's rules come from facts extracted from its earlier version, which are now marked stale:
   - Accounts Receivable Specialists may approve up to $100,000.
   - Limits over $100,000 and up to $500,000 need the Finance Manager.
   - Limits over $500,000 need the Director of Finance, plus VP Sales concurrence for Strategic accounts.
   - Maximum limit: $750,000 for Strategic accounts.
   - No invoice due in the prior 12 months may have been paid, or be unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
   - No one may approve a request they submitted.
4. **Payment history.** It passes. Acme had two invoices due between 2025-09-23 and 2026-09-23:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.

   INV-62305 ($720,000, due 2026-10-02) was not yet due on the request date.
5. **Maximum limit.** $400,000 is within the $750,000 Strategic maximum, so no guarantee is needed. For the record, guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 ($400,000, 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) does name Acme Mfg. Holdings, trading as Acme Manufacturing.
6. **Sarah's authority.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. The policy gives that role no authority to approve credit limits.
7. **Who must approve.** $400,000 falls in the Finance Manager band. Priya Shah holds that role, and she didn't submit the request, so the self-approval rule doesn't block her. No concurrence is needed because the limit is under $500,000.
