## Answer

**No, CR-9207 can't be approved on the evidence available.** The request depends on the "Acme parent guarantee," and that guarantee doesn't cover Acme Industrial Supply Co. Sarah Chen needs to produce credit support that names this customer before anyone can decide. Also, CR-9207 itself isn't in the ERP credit-request records I can see.

1. **The request.** CR-9207 asks to raise C-1044 from $50,000 to $500,000. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23, and it relies on a guarantee. The ERP credit-request file has no CR-9207. The only Acme request Sarah submitted that day is CR-9201, for C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings, $250,000 to $400,000). I haven't used CR-9201 in its place. The current $50,000 limit for C-1044 does match the ERP credit record.
2. **The customer.** C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH. In the CRM it's account CRM-2091, a **Standard** account. It's a separate company from Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing (C-1001, Milwaukee, Strategic).
3. **The policy.** On 2026-09-23 the original Credit Policy 2026 applied (effective 2026-01-01). Revision 2 only applies to requests dated on or after 2026-10-01. The knowledge base now holds Revision 2, so I took the original policy's terms from facts recorded from its first version.
4. **Payment history.** C-1044 has two invoices, both paid: INV-30001 (due 2025-02-25) and INV-30003 (due 2025-05-07). Neither falls due in the 12 months before the request, so no late invoices count against it. I'm assuming the original policy also looks back 12 months, as Revision 2 does. I only have that policy's other terms.
5. **Maximum limit.** The original 2026 policy caps Standard accounts at **$250,000**. The only guarantee on file, GRT-ACME-2026 ($400,000, 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31), is given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It covers only Acme Mfg. Holdings and says "No other company is covered." So nothing found raises C-1044's maximum, and $500,000 is above the cap.
6. **Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, so she has no credit-approval authority under the policy. Accounts Receivable Specialists can approve up to $100,000.
7. **Required approver.** A $500,000 limit falls in the band above $100,000 up to $500,000, which needs a Finance Manager: Priya Shah. VP Sales concurrence applies only to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed here. The rule that no one approves their own request doesn't come into play, because Sarah isn't the approver.

If credit support covering C-1044 can't be produced, the request would be rejected, since $500,000 is above the $250,000 Standard maximum.
