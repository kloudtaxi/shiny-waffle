I couldn't find CR-9202 in Northstar's ERP. If the request is confirmed as given, Acme is eligible for $650,000, but Sarah Chen can't approve it. It needs approval from the Director of Finance (Elena Novak) and agreement ("concurrence") from the VP Sales (Michael Torres). Until the ERP shows CR-9202, nobody should act on it.

**1. The request isn't in the ERP.** The ERP request file holds one Acme request from that day: **CR-9201**, $250,000 → **$400,000**. It has the same requestor, date, justification and "Pending Approval" status. Sarah's 23 Sept email to Priya Shah also mentions only CR-9201 at $400,000. I've judged CR-9202 as you gave it and not swapped in CR-9201. Someone needs to confirm that CR-9202 exists and is $650,000, and whether it replaces CR-9201.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings), the CRM account (CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing) and the guarantee all point to the same company. They share an address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee) and tax ID (04-812-7730). The CRM tier is **Strategic**. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company.

**3. The policy in force on 23 Sept.** That's the Northstar Credit Policy 2026, original version. Revision 2 only applies to requests dated on or after 1 Oct 2026. The knowledge base now only holds revision 2's text, so I took the original's terms from facts drawn from that earlier version:
- Invoices can't be paid or left unpaid more than 30 days past due.
- Strategic accounts can have up to $750,000.
- Finance Manager approves above $100,000 up to $500,000; Director of Finance approves above $500,000.
- For Strategic accounts above $500,000, VP Sales must also concur.

**4. Payment history (invoices due 23 Sept 2025 – 23 Sept 2026).**
- INV-60412 was due 28 Nov 2025 and paid 24 Nov 2025, early.
- INV-62011 was due 25 July 2026 and paid 22 July 2026, early.
- INV-62305 isn't due until 2 Oct 2026, so it doesn't count yet.

No late invoices.

**5. The maximum limit.** $650,000 is within the $750,000 Strategic maximum on Acme's own standing. The request relies on order volume, not a guarantee. A parent guarantee does exist (GRT-ACME-2026: $400,000 from Acme Group Holdings, covering Acme Mfg. Holdings, 2026–2027), but it isn't needed. Sarah's mention of it was about future headroom.

**6–7. Who must approve.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the policy gives that role no authority over credit limits. The new total of $650,000 is above $500,000, so Elena Novak must approve. Because Acme is Strategic, Michael Torres must also concur. Neither of them is the requestor, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't come into play.
