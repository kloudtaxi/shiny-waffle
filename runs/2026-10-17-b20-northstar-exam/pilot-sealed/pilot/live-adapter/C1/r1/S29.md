## Answer

I'm reading the policy version that was in force on the request date and checking it against the ERP, CRM, invoice and guarantee records. Here is the result.

**No, it can't be approved.** $1,300,000 is above the most Acme Manufacturing can have, even counting the parent guarantee. A more senior approver can't fix that. Also, CR-9204 doesn't appear in the Northstar ERP request records.

**1. The request.** The ERP credit-request records list only one Acme request on 2026-09-23: **CR-9201** (C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, Sarah Chen, Pending Approval). There is no CR-9204. I've assessed CR-9204 as you gave it, not CR-9201, and made confirming it a blocking condition. It relies on the parent guarantee.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and the guarantee's "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing" are the same company. They share the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, and the DUNS number 04-812-7730. CRM lists the account tier as **Strategic**. The current ERP limit is $250,000, which matches the request.

**3. The policy.** The 2026 Credit Policy (CREDIT-POLICY-2026, first version) applies, because it covers requests dated before 2026-10-01. Revision 2, which raises the Strategic maximum to $1,000,000, only applies to requests dated on or after 2026-10-01, so it doesn't cover this one. The knowledge base now holds only revision 2's text, so the first version's figures below come from facts recorded from its earlier text.

**4. Payment history: no problems.** Acme has three invoices:
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) is unpaid but wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.

None is late, under either the 30-day or the 45-day rule. I couldn't get the first version's exact payment-rule wording, but it doesn't change the result.

**5. The maximum limit: $1,150,000.**
- The policy maximum for Strategic accounts is $750,000.
- Parent guarantee GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc., adds **$400,000**. It names this company ("No other company is covered") and runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it was in force on the request date.
- The guarantee is real and covers Acme, but **$1,300,000 is $150,000 over the $1,150,000 maximum.**

**6–7. Who would have to approve.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the credit policy gives that role no authority to approve credit limits. A limit above $500,000 needs approval from the **Director of Finance (Elena Novak)**. Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs agreement from the **VP Sales (Michael Torres)**. Neither of them submitted the request, so the rule that you can't approve your own request doesn't affect them. Their approval still wouldn't make the customer eligible.

**8. Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** The largest limit Acme could get under the current policy is $1,150,000. Any lower request would need a new decision.
