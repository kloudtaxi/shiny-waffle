## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. The 15% discount is within what Acme's contract allows, but under the 2027 policy her limit is 5%. A discount above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer (CRO), David Morgan, to approve it. Separately, I couldn't find DR-9113 in the CRM records, so that has to be confirmed before anyone acts on this decision.

1. **The request.** DR-9113 asks for 15% off an NS-500 for account CRM-2048, dated 2027-02-01, raised by Sarah Chen. The figures are list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. It relies on Acme's contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"). **DR-9113 is not in the knowledge base's copy of the CRM discount requests (discount_requests.csv).** The only matching Acme NS-500 requests at 15% are DR-8104 (approved 2025-09-16) and DR-9001 (2026-09-23, Pending Approval). I haven't treated either of them as this request. I decided on DR-9113 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both records show the same address and the same DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing". It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a separate account (CRM-2091).
3. **The product.** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is active on 2027-02-01. Section 3.2 allows up to 15% on the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15%, effective 2025-04-01 and expiring 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So 15% is exactly at the ceiling, and Acme is eligible.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies to requests dated 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It fully replaces the 2026 policy, and that includes the 2026 approval matrix, which let an account executive approve up to 10%. The bands are: up to 5% for an Enterprise Account Executive, over 5% and up to 12% for the VP Sales, and over 12% for the CRO.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so her limit is 5%. 15% is above it.
8. **The approver.** 15% is above 12%, so the CRO must approve. The org chart dated 2026-09-01 names David Morgan as CRO. That chart is older than the request date, so whether he still holds the role on 2027-02-01 is worth checking, but it doesn't hold up the decision.
9. **Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is eligible, but the contract doesn't change who can approve. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for any discount over 5%.
