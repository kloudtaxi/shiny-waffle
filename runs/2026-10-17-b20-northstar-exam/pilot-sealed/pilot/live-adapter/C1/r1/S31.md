There is also a blocking problem: **CR-9206 isn't in the request records I can see**, so its existence must be confirmed before anyone acts on this.

**1. The request.** The submitted record asks to raise C-1002 from $100,000 to $200,000, dated 2025-09-23, submitted by michael.torres@northstar.example. The reason given is fleet expansion. It relies only on the customer's own standing; no guarantee is mentioned.
- **CR-9206 isn't in `credit_requests.csv`.** That file has CR-9201 and CR-7001 to CR-7010 only, and a search turned up nothing else. I've decided on the request as given and made confirming it a blocking condition.
- **The date may be wrong.** Several 2026-09-23 messages and the pending CR-9201 suggest 2026 may have been meant. A 2026 date would bring in different rules: the 2026 policy revision 2 takes effect 2026-10-01 and lowers the Standard-tier ceiling to $250,000. BlueRiver also has one invoice paid more than 45 days late in 2026 (see step 4). That could change the eligibility result, so I've made confirming the date a blocking condition too.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1002, "Blue River Logistics LLC", is the same company as CRM account CRM-2051, "BlueRiver Logistics". They share the DUNS number 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis, MO. The CRM tier is **Standard**.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2025 applies to requests dated 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid more than 45 days late. BlueRiver passes:
- INV-61005 was due 2025-03-01 and paid 2025-02-26, on time.
- INV-61210 was due 2025-05-30 and paid 2025-05-28, on time.
- INV-61433 was due 2025-08-29 and paid 2025-09-02, 4 days late, which is within the 45 days.

INV-62147, paid 52 days late, was due 2026-05-30. That is after this request date, so it doesn't count here.

**5. Maximum limit.** The ceiling for Standard accounts is $300,000. No guarantee is involved. $200,000 is within the limit, so BlueRiver is **eligible**.

**6. Michael's authority.** He is VP Sales. The 2025 credit policy gives approval authority only to Accounts Receivable Specialists (up to $50,000), the Finance Manager (over $50,000 up to $750,000) and the Director of Finance (over $750,000). It gives VP Sales none, so he **cannot approve this alone**.

**7. Who must approve.** A $200,000 limit falls in the Finance Manager band, and the 2025 policy requires no second sign-off. That role is held by **Priya Shah**, who didn't submit the request, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't affect her. She approved other requests in this band during 2025 (CR-7001, CR-7002, CR-7003, CR-7005).

**Note on the current limit.** The ERP credit table shows $100,000 for C-1002, but with a date of 2026-01-15. Nothing confirms what the limit was on 2025-09-23. This doesn't block the decision.
