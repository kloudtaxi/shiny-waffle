## Answer

**No, CR-9205 can't be approved.** BlueRiver Logistics fails the payment-history rule, and no approver can override that. There is also a second problem: CR-9205 doesn't appear in the knowledge base's copy of the ERP credit requests.

**1. The request.** The ERP copy (credit_requests.csv) lists CR-9201 and CR-7001 to CR-7010, but not CR-9205. I decided on the request as you gave it: $100,000 to $200,000, dated 2026-09-23, from Michael Torres. Confirming that it exists is a blocking condition. It relies on the customer's own standing; no guarantee is cited.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC". CRM account CRM-2051 is "BlueRiver Logistics". Same address (890 Harbor Way, St. Louis) and same DUNS number (07-331-9025), so they're the same company. CRM puts it in the **Standard** tier. The ERP credit limit is $100,000, which matches the request.

**3. The policy.** Revision 2 of the 2026 credit policy only covers requests dated on or after 2026-10-01. So the original Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (CREDIT-POLICY-2026) applies. The knowledge base now holds only revision 2's text, so I took the original's terms from the facts it extracted from that version.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid more than 30 days late, or be unpaid more than 30 days past due.
- **INV-62147** (NS-Cloud 2026-Q2, $30,000) was due 2026-05-30 and paid 2026-07-21. That's **52 days late**, which breaks the rule.
- The other invoices in the window were paid on time or within 2 days (INV-61688, INV-30014, INV-61902, INV-62391).
- INV-30020 isn't due until 2026-10-02.

**5. Maximum limit.** $250,000 for Standard accounts. $200,000 would be within it, and no guarantee for BlueRiver exists in the knowledge base. Payment history alone makes the customer ineligible.

**6–7. Authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales and holds no credit-approval authority. A $200,000 limit falls in the over-$100,000-to-$500,000 band, which needs Finance Manager approval. That's Priya Shah. No concurrence is needed below $500,000. Torres, as the requestor, couldn't approve it anyway. An approver doesn't change the ineligibility.
