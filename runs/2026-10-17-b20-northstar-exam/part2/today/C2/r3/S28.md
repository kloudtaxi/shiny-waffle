## Answer

**Answer:** The customer qualifies for the $1,000,000 limit, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. It needs approval from Elena Novak (Director of Finance) and concurrence from Michael Torres (VP Sales). One thing blocks acting on it: **CR-9203 isn't in the ERP data I can see.** The only Acme credit request on file is CR-9201, also from Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, for $250,000 → $400,000. I decided on CR-9203 as you gave it and did not substitute CR-9201. CR-9203's existence must be confirmed before anything is done.

1. **Customer:** ERP customer C-1001 is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730). The CRM rates it a **Strategic** account.
2. **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
3. **Payment history:** No problems. The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid early, on 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid early, on 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
4. **Maximum limit: $1,150,000.**
   - The policy allows $750,000 for Strategic accounts. A parent-company guarantee raises that by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names.
   - The guarantee is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names its wholly owned subsidiary "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing" at the same address as C-1001.
   - It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. The parent's liability is capped at $400,000.
   - $750,000 + $400,000 = $1,150,000, which is more than the $1,000,000 requested.
5. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. I found no credit approval authority for that role, so she can't approve this.
6. **Who must sign off:**
   - Limits above $500,000 need the **Director of Finance**, who is Elena Novak.
   - Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need **VP Sales concurrence**, which is Michael Torres's role.
   - Torres is Sarah Chen's manager. The policy only bars people from approving or concurring on requests they submitted themselves, so he can still concur.
