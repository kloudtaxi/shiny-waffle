> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9001", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2026-09-23", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself.** Acme is entitled to 15% on the NS-500, but under the 2026 Pricing Policy that discount needs VP Sales approval from Michael Torres.

1. **The request:** CRM shows DR-9001 exactly as you submitted it: account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The Master Supply Agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing, and both records share DUNS 04-812-7730 and the same address.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Its section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for "discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller", as set out in Schedule B. I found no record of an early termination under Section 9.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing for the NS-500 only, up to 15% of list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The CRO, David Morgan, approved it on 2025-03-20, and it replaced the earlier 10% exception. A 15% request is within that limit, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies, because it covers requests from 2026-01-01 until the 2027 policy takes over.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, that role "may approve discounts up to and including 10%", so 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** The policy says "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." The VP Sales is Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager.

**Why last year doesn't count:** Sarah approved the same 15% herself on DR-8104 (2025-09-16). That was allowed under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that limit to 10%.
