## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is eligible for 15% on the NS-500, but under the 2027 pricing policy her own limit is 5%. A 15% discount needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. Separately, request DR-9113 doesn't exist in the knowledge base, so it must be confirmed before anyone acts on this.

1. **The request:** DR-9113 is a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-500, dated 2027-02-01. That's $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, leaving $212,500. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the request relies on special contract terms. A search for DR-9113 found nothing, and the CRM identifier lookup isn't available. The knowledge base does hold other requests for this account (DR-8104, DR-9001, CR-9201). As instructed, I decided on DR-9113 as you gave it, not on one of those.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. It's a Strategic account, and Sarah Chen owns it.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it is active on 2027-02-01.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers this request. It names Acme Manufacturing and the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception from 2025-04-01. 15% is exactly the maximum, so the customer is eligible. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies, in effect 2027-01-01 through 2027-12-31. It fully replaces the 2026 policy for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). The policy lets that role approve discounts "up to and including 5%", so 15% is beyond her limit.
8. **Who must approve:** Under the policy, VP Sales approves above 5% up to 12%. Anything above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. That's David Morgan (EMP-300). The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for any discount above 5%.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** The customer is eligible, but the discount goes beyond the requestor's authority. The contract making Acme eligible doesn't change who is allowed to approve.

Two things to note:
- The employee directory doesn't show dates, so I couldn't confirm that Morgan will still be CRO on 2027-02-01.
- The request is dated after today (2026-10-18), and I decided it under the 2027 policy.
