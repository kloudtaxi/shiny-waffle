## Answer

**No, CR-9205 can't be approved.** The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. BlueRiver Logistics fails the 2026 credit policy's payment-history rule, and sending it to a more senior approver doesn't change that.

**What I checked**
- **The request:** I couldn't find CR-9205 itself in the knowledge base, so I used the ERP record you supplied. ERP customer C-1002 does show a current limit of $100,000, which matches. Nothing in the request mentions a guarantee or other credit support, so it relies on BlueRiver's own standing.
- **The customer:** The ERP's "Blue River Logistics LLC" (C-1002) and the CRM's "BlueRiver Logistics" (CRM-2051) are the same company. Both have DUNS 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. The CRM puts it in the **Standard** tier.
- **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It is effective from 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
- **Payment history:** The policy says no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid more than 30 days after its due date. **INV-62147** ($30,000, NS-Cloud 2026-Q2) was due 2026-05-30 and paid 2026-07-21, which is **52 days late**. That breaks the rule.
  - The other invoices due in that window (INV-61688, INV-30014, INV-61902, INV-62391) were paid on time.
  - INV-30020 ($494,700) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
- **The maximum limit:** Standard accounts can go up to $250,000, so the $200,000 request is within the cap. It's the payment history alone that blocks it.
- **Who would approve:** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) can't approve this alone. In any case, the policy doesn't let anyone approve a request they submitted. I couldn't find who holds the Finance Manager role or what credit authority a VP Sales has. Neither matters once the customer is ineligible.
