## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Her limit in 2026 is 10%. The customer is eligible for 15%, but the VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it. One thing must be fixed first: the request you sent doesn't match what the CRM holds for DR-9001.

**The request doesn't match the CRM.** DR-9001 in the CRM (account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, 15%, dated 2026-09-23, from sarah.chen@northstar.example, "Pending Approval") is for the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**. Its list value is $250,000, the discount $37,500 and the net $212,500. It is not for NS-CLOUD at $120,000 / $18,000 / $102,000. Sarah's own note also says she "logged it in CRM as DR-9001" for NS-500. I've decided on the CRM's version. **If the request really is for the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, the answer is different:** no contract term or exception covers NS-Cloud, so it would need commercial review instead.

**How I got there:**
- **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048) is the same company as Acme Mfg. Holdings in the ERP (customer C-1001, same DUNS 04-812-7730). Its account status is Active.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500 only, as set out in Schedule B.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, up to 15% off list. It replaced the earlier 10% exception. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, and anything above 10% needs recorded approval evidence.
- **Sarah's limit:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%, and 15% is above it. Last year's 15% (DR-8104, September 2025) was within her authority because the 2025 policy allowed account executives 15%. That limit no longer applies.
- **Approver:** The VP Sales is Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager.
