## Answer

DR-9001 can't be approved on Sarah Chen's own authority. Acme is entitled to the 15%, but under the 2026 pricing policy anything above 10% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres, to approve it. Separately, the request as submitted says "Approved", but the CRM shows it as **Pending Approval**. Nobody has validly approved it yet.

1. **The request:** The CRM record matches the submission except for the status: 15.0% off NS-500, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on special terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"), so the basis is contract/exception.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, which signed the Master Supply Agreement (MSA). Sarah owns the account.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. The MSA runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and I found nothing showing it was ended early.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% off list on NS-500 only. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaced the earlier 10% exception. It was approved by David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20. 15% is exactly at the cap, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies, since it covers requests dated in 2026. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Under that policy, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That role is held by Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager.

Why this matters: Sarah approved a 15% discount for Acme herself last year (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered the limit to 10%, so the same request now needs Michael Torres.
