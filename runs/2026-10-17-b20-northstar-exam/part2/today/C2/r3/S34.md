**Short answer: no, not yet.** CR-9208 doesn't appear in the system of record. Taking the request as you gave it, Acme qualifies for $650,000 on its own record. But Michael Torres can't approve it himself. It needs approval from Elena Novak, Director of Finance, and agreement (concurrence) from David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer. Before anyone acts, someone has to confirm the request actually exists.

**1. The request.** I found no record of CR-9208. The only matching request is **CR-9201**: same customer (C-1001), same date (2026-09-23), same justification, and status "Pending Approval". But it was raised by **Sarah Chen** for **$400,000**, not by Michael Torres for $650,000. I haven't swapped CR-9201 in; I decided on CR-9208 as given and made confirming it a blocking condition. The request doesn't mention any third-party guarantee, so I treated it as resting on Acme's own record.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which does business as Acme Manufacturing (Master Supply Agreement). In the CRM it is CRM-2048, with tier **Strategic**. Both systems give the same DUNS number, 04-812-7730.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the previous policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24 (on time).
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22 (on time).
- INV-62305 ($720,000) falls due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

There are no late invoices.

**5. Maximum limit.** Strategic accounts can go up to $750,000. $650,000 is within that, so Acme qualifies.

**6. Michael Torres's own authority.** The credit policy gives approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $100k), the Finance Manager (over $100k up to $500k) and the Director of Finance (over $500k). VP Sales has no approval authority.

**7. Who must sign off.**
- A limit over $500k needs **Director of Finance** approval: Elena Novak.
- For Strategic accounts, a limit over $500k also needs **VP Sales concurrence**. Torres is the VP Sales, but he submitted the request. The policy says no one may approve or agree to their own request; that role passes to his manager, **David Morgan** (Chief Revenue Officer).

**8. Decision:** `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`, on hold until CR-9208 is confirmed. Also worth noting: an email about CR-9201 mentions a parent guarantee. This decision doesn't rely on it, and none is needed for $650,000.
