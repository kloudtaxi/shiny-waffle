## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** Acme's pricing exception allows at most 15% on the NS-500. But the CRM record for DR-9001 is for **15%**, not 18%, so I decided on 15%. That 15% can go ahead only if Michael Torres (VP Sales) approves it, because it's above Sarah Chen's own 10% limit.

**What I checked**
1. **The request.** In the CRM, DR-9001 is Acme Manufacturing (account CRM-2048), NS-500, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It asks for **15.0%**: $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, net $212,500. The version you sent says 18%: $45,000 off, net $205,000. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer.** In the agreement, Acme Manufacturing is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings.
3. **The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23.
4. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing for the NS-500 only, at up to 15% off list. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaced the earlier 10% exception. David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) approved it. It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
5. **The policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers calendar 2026. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives may approve up to 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, and above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
6. **Authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. For 15%, the VP Sales must approve, and that's Michael Torres.

**At 18%:** that is above the 15% the exception allows. VP Sales sign-off wouldn't fix that. It would need a new or amended exception, so it would be rejected or escalated.

**One thing to fix:** Sarah's note says 15% "is what we did for them last September", and the 15% approval then was DR-8104 (2025-09-16). That was under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered that limit to 10%.
