## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve DR-9001 herself, but it can go ahead with VP Sales sign-off.** Acme is entitled to 15% off the NS-500. That's above Sarah's own approval limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

**What I checked**

1. **The request:** The system of record matches what was submitted: 15% off $250,000 list ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), dated 2026-09-23 and marked Pending Approval. It cites contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement, so it relies on special terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, the party named in the Master Supply Agreement. Sarah owns the account.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. I found no record that it was terminated early.
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 gives Acme Manufacturing up to 15% off list on the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The CRO, David Morgan, approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests from 2025-04-01. The 15% requested is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies. It replaced the 2025 policy for requests dated from 2026-01-01, and the 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, that role may approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Who must approve:** The 2026 policy says discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, and anything over 10% needs recorded approval evidence. The VP Sales is Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager.

Don't treat last year's request as a precedent. Sarah approved a similar 15% request herself (DR-8104) on 2025-09-16. That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that limit to 10%.
