## Answer

**No. CR-9207 can't be approved on 2026-09-23. The outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Sending it to a more senior approver won't change that.

**1. The request.** CR-9207 asks to raise the limit from $50,000 to $500,000. It was made by Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, on 2026-09-23, and rests on "the Acme parent guarantee". I couldn't find CR-9207 in the knowledge base. The only credit request from Sarah Chen there is CR-9201, which is for a different customer (C-1001), so I decided on CR-9207 as you gave it. Someone needs to confirm it exists in Northstar ERP.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1044 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** in Akron, OH (DUNS 09-552-1187). In the CRM it is account CRM-2091, **Standard** tier, owned by Zachary Brown. It is not the same company as Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing (C-1001, Milwaukee, DUNS 04-812-7730, Strategic tier, owned by Sarah Chen).

**3. The guarantee doesn't cover this customer.** The guarantee is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It covers "its wholly owned subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing", runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and is capped at $400,000. It doesn't name Acme Industrial Supply Co. A separate support-plan document also says "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered." That statement is about the support plan, not the guarantee, but it points the same way. Because the guarantee is on record and simply covers another company, this isn't a case of missing evidence to ask for. The request has to be judged on the customer's own standing.

**4. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31. It caps Standard accounts at **$250,000** (Strategic accounts at $750,000). $500,000 is above that cap, so the customer isn't eligible.

**5. Approval.** Under the same policy, limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. Even so, Finance Manager approval can't make the customer eligible. I didn't find Sarah Chen's own approval limit or who holds the Finance Manager role.

**6. Payment history.** I found no invoice records for C-1044. That doesn't change the outcome, because the request is already over the maximum.
