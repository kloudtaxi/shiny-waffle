## Answer

**Answer: Not on Sarah Chen's own authority.** The discount is within what Acme is entitled to, but it is above her limit. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, must approve it (APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION). Before anyone acts, the discount figure has to be cleared up.

**The figures don't match.** Your copy says 8.0% ($20,000 off, $230,000 net). The CRM record for DR-9001 says **15.0%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah's own email also says the customer is "asking for 15% off list." I've decided on the CRM figure of 15%. The difference is a blocking condition below.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, CRM account CRM-2048, product NS-500, list value $250,000, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen, status "Pending Approval." It relies on contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. It is a Strategic account and Sarah owns it.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, at up to 15% off list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026. The 2027 policy only replaces it for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less. 15% is over it.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% go to the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager.

**Also worth flagging:** a 15% discount on the same product for Acme last year, DR-8104 (2025-09-16), shows as approved by Sarah herself. That was above her authority. It doesn't block this decision, but someone should review it.
