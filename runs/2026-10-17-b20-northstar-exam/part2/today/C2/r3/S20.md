**No, Michael Torres can't approve DR-9112.** He has the authority for 18%, but Acme Manufacturing is only entitled to 15% on the NS-500. A more senior approver can't fix that. It needs new or amended commercial terms.

1. **The request:** DR-9112 asks for 18% off the NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), dated 2026-09-23 and submitted by michael.torres@northstar.example. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. The figures add up: 18% of $250,000 is $45,000, leaving $205,000 net. However, I couldn't find DR-9112 in the knowledge base, so I couldn't check it against the system of record.
2. **The customer:** In the CRM it is Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048). In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). They are the same company: same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and same address, and the Master Supply Agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Section 3.2 says the customer "is eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500", as set out in the pricing exception in Schedule B.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum discount of 15% of list price. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in force on the request date. The 2027 policy only takes over for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Michael Torres's authority:** He is VP Sales (EMP-200). The policy's approval matrix gives VP Sales authority for discounts above 10% and up to 20%, so 18% is within his limit.
8. **Who would approve:** The 18% band belongs to VP Sales, which is Michael himself.
9. **Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** 18% is 3 points above what the contract and exception allow. His authority doesn't change what the customer is contractually eligible for, and the contract term itself caps the discount at 15%.

To go ahead, either resubmit at 15% or less (he could approve that himself), or negotiate an amendment to the agreement or the exception. The 15% exception was approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, so he is the likely person to involve, though the policy doesn't say who approves amendments. Before acting, someone also needs to confirm that DR-9112 exists in Northstar CRM as described.
