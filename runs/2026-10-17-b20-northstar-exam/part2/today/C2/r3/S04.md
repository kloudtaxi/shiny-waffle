## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen couldn't have approved this herself.** Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but 15% is above her own limit. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it.

**Date mismatch:** You gave 2025-09-23, but the CRM record for DR-9001 shows **2026-09-23**. I decided on the CRM date and added a condition to confirm it.

1. **Request:** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah raised it and it is "Pending Approval." The stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on special terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. It is a Strategic account, Sarah owns it, and its status is Active. The Master Supply Agreement is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% off list, for Acme and for NS-500 only. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20, and it replaced the older 10% exception. The request asks for exactly 15%, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers requests dated from 2026-01-01 until the 2027 policy takes over on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** The VP Sales band covers more than 10% and up to 20%. That role is held by Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager.

**Something else I found:** An earlier request, DR-8104 (also 15% on NS-500 for Acme, dated 2025-09-16), shows Sarah approving her own request. If that's what your 2025 date refers to, it looks above her 10% limit. I only checked the 2026 limits, though; I didn't confirm the 2025 policy's bands, so it's worth a review. It doesn't block this decision.
