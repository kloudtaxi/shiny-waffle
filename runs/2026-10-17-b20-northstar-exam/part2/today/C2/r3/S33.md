## Answer

**No, CR-9203 can't be approved on 2026-09-23 as it stands.** It depends on an Acme Group parent guarantee that I couldn't find, and without it $1,000,000 is above the customer's maximum. The decision is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**: get the guarantee before deciding. There is a second problem: CR-9203 isn't in the knowledge base's copy of the Northstar ERP request records.

1. **The request.** The only Acme request in the ERP records for C-1001 is **CR-9201**: Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, $250,000 → $400,000, justified by the "third-plant ramp-up" and pending approval. CR-9203 for $1,000,000 doesn't appear. I've decided on CR-9203 as you gave it, not on CR-9201, and made "confirm CR-9203 exists" a blocking condition.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" and CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" are the same company: same DUNS (04-812-7730) and same address. Its account tier is Strategic and Sarah Chen owns the account.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history is clean.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 is unpaid but wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
5. **The limit is above the maximum.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. A guarantee in force raises that by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names. The only sign of an Acme Group guarantee is an informal note: "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it… Gina says it's still in place." I found no guarantee document, no guaranteed amount, no named customer and no dates. Also, a separate agreement (not the guarantee) with this customer says its coverage "does not extend to the Customer's parent, Acme Group Holdings, Inc., or to any other affiliate." That shows the companies are treated as distinct; it doesn't establish any guarantee for C-1001.
6. **Sarah Chen has no approval authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives her role no credit approval authority. The policy also says no one may approve or concur on a request they submitted.
7. **Required approvers.** Limits above $500,000 need the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**, to approve. Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need the VP Sales, **Michael Torres**, to concur.

**What happens next:** if a guarantee is produced that names C-1001, is in force on 2026-09-23 and covers at least $250,000, the request can go to Novak and Torres. If no guarantee is produced, $1,000,000 is above the maximum and the request would be rejected or escalated.
