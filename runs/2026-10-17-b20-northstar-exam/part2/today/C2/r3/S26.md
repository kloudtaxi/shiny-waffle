## Answer

**Yes, but not by Sarah alone.** CR-9201 can go ahead with Finance Manager approval from Priya Shah. Acme Manufacturing is eligible for $400,000. The outcome is `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`.

1. **The request:** The Northstar ERP record matches what you sent: CR-9201 for customer C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on Acme's own standing. Sarah's email mentions Acme Group's parent guarantee only as cover "if they need more headroom later".
2. **The customer:** ERP customer C-1001, CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and the legal entity Acme Mfg. Holdings (trading as Acme Manufacturing) are the same company. CRM lists it as a **Strategic** account.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, early.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, early.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) was due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count. It still shows as unpaid in ERP, which Finance may want to check, but it doesn't affect this decision.
5. **Maximum limit:** The Strategic maximum is $750,000, so $400,000 is within it. The parent guarantee isn't needed. For the record, Acme Group Holdings guarantees Acme Mfg. Holdings, the right company, from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31.
6. **Sarah's authority:** None. She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives that role no credit approval authority.
7. **Who must approve:** Limits above $100,000 up to and including $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. That is Priya Shah (EMP-401). VP Sales concurrence is only needed above $500,000, so it isn't required here. The rule against approving your own request doesn't come into play, because Priya didn't submit it.
