## Answer

**Yes, $650,000 can be approved, but only with sign-off from the Director of Finance and the VP Sales. It must also wait for one blocking check: CR-9202 does not exist in Northstar ERP.** The only matching ERP request is **CR-9201**: same customer (C-1001), same requestor, same date (2026-09-23), same justification and status, but it asks for **$400,000**, not $650,000. Sarah Chen's own message also says CR-9201 takes the limit "from $250,000 to $400,000". I decided CR-9202 as you gave it, not CR-9201. Nobody should act on it until someone confirms which request and amount are real.

1. **The request:** CR-9202 for Acme Manufacturing (ERP C-1001, CRM-2048), from $250,000 to $650,000, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive). It rests on Acme's own standing. It doesn't cite a guarantee and doesn't need one, because $650,000 is within Acme's own maximum. An email mentions an "Acme Group parent guarantee", but no guarantee document for Acme Manufacturing is in the records, and this decision doesn't depend on one.
2. **The customer:** Acme Manufacturing is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings (per the Master Supply Agreement). It is a Strategic account in the CRM.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect for 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** the rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000, unpaid) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so the rule doesn't count it. Acme passes.
5. **Maximum limit:** $750,000 for Strategic accounts, so $650,000 is within it.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** none. The policy lets Accounts Receivable Specialists approve limits up to $100,000 and gives Account Executives no approval authority for credit limits.
7. **Required approvers for a $650,000 limit:**
   - **Director of Finance approval**, because the limit is above $500,000. That is Elena Novak.
   - **VP Sales concurrence**, because this is a Strategic account above $500,000. That is Michael Torres.

   The rule against approving your own request doesn't affect either of them, since neither submitted it.

Two things for whoever reviews it. INV-62305 is $720,000 and unpaid, which is far above Acme's current $250,000 limit. It is now more than two weeks past its due date of 2026-10-02.
