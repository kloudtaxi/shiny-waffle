## Answer

**No, it can't be approved as submitted. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The request depends on the Acme Master Supply Agreement and the Acme NS-500 pricing exception, but neither covers Acme Industrial Supply Co.

1. **The request.** I couldn't find DR-9105 in the knowledge base. The CRM discount-request records I found are DR-8104 (approved 2025-09-16) and DR-9001 (pending, 2026-09-23). Both are Sarah Chen's 15% NS-500 requests, but for account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing), not CRM-2091. Some of DR-9105's details match DR-9001 exactly (15.0%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, same date and justification), but the account is different. I've decided on DR-9105 as given, and confirming it exists in CRM is a blocking condition. The basis is contract or exception terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer.** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. Its ERP customer number is C-1044, its tier is Standard, its DUNS is 09-552-1187, it's based in Akron OH, and its account owner is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen. The Master Supply Agreement is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730, Milwaukee WI). One of the documents says outright: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered."*
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23, but only for Acme Manufacturing. I found no contract for Acme Industrial Supply Co.
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% on NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, approved by CRO David Morgan. It names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority." It doesn't cover this customer, and special terms don't transfer to a different company.
6. **Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies, because the 2027 policy only replaces it for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01. The knowledge base doesn't model its approval bands. All it captures is that approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
7. **Sarah's authority.** Under the 2026 policy, I couldn't find it. She is an Enterprise Account Executive reporting to Michael Torres, VP Sales. The rule that discounts over 15% need VP Sales approval comes from the 2025 policy, so I haven't applied it.
8. **Required approver.** I can't name one without the 2026 bands. It matters less here because the outcome is commercial review, not an authority sign-off.

Even if the agreement did apply, a contract allowing 15% wouldn't by itself make the discount approvable. For a Standard-tier account with no special terms, the request needs commercial review, or else to be resubmitted as a standard-pricing request under the 2026 bands.
