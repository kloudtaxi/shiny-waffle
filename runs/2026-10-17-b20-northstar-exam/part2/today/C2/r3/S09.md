## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

**No.** Sarah Chen could not have approved this on 2025-03-31. The problem isn't her signing authority. Acme simply wasn't eligible for 15% on NS-500 that day: the most it could get was 10%. A higher approver can't fix that.

**What the records show**
1. **The request:** DR-9101 is not in the knowledge base. The only similar records are DR-8104 (2025-09-16, approved) and DR-9001 (2026-09-23, pending). I haven't used either one. I decided on DR-9101 as you gave it, but someone must first confirm it exists in Northstar CRM.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which is how Acme Mfg. Holdings trades. Sarah Chen owns the account.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract on the request date:** ACME-SA-2023 with exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allows "up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." The 2025-03-31 date is the last day it covers.
5. **The basis the request cites is too late.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," but that agreement "is effective April 1, 2025." The 15% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, was approved by David Morgan (CRO) on 2025-03-20. Even so, it is "Effective 2025-04-01" and replaces the 10% exception only "for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01." Neither applied on 3-31, one day before.
6. **Policy in force:** The 2026 pricing policy replaces the 2025 one only for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01, so the 2025 policy applied. One pricing policy document says Enterprise Account Executives "may approve discounts up to and including 15%" and that "greater than 15% require VP Sales approval." The other two policy documents are the 2026 and 2027 policies, so this is most likely the 2025 policy, but the knowledge base doesn't label it that way.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). If that document is the 2025 policy, 15% would have been within her own limit. That doesn't change the answer, because her authority can't create eligibility the contract didn't give.

**What would fix it:** Either redate the order to 2025-04-01 or later, when the 15% exception and the Master Supply Agreement apply, or cut the discount to 10% or less. Either way it has to go through as a new or corrected request.
