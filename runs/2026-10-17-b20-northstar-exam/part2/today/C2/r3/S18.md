## Answer

Yes. Under the policy in force on 2025-03-31, Sarah Chen could have approved this 8% discount herself. There are two things to fix first: I couldn't find DR-9110 in the knowledge base, and its justification cites an agreement that had not yet taken effect.

**The request.** DR-9110 is not in the knowledge base. The only Acme / NS-500 request from Sarah that I found is DR-9001: 15%, dated 2026-09-23, with the same justification. I did not use DR-9001 in its place. This decision is on DR-9110 as you gave it. Your figures are consistent: 8% of $250,000 is $20,000, leaving $230,000 net.

**Customer and product.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen is the account owner. The legal entity is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing. NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**Contract basis (the problem).** The justification says "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". That agreement starts on 2025-04-01 and runs to 2028-03-31, so it wasn't in force on 2025-03-31. Its 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also only applies to requests dated 2025-04-01 or later.

**A pricing exception did cover the date.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023, says Acme Manufacturing "may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500… for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." The request date is the last day of that window, and 8% is under the 10% cap. So Acme was eligible, but under the old exception, not the agreement the request names.

**Authority.** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 says "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%". Anything above 15% needs VP Sales approval (Michael Torres, her manager). At 8%, she didn't need anyone else. She would have been within her limit even at standard pricing.

**Before acting on it:**
- Confirm DR-9110 exists in Northstar CRM.
- Change the justification to cite EXC-ACME-NS500-10 / ACME-SA-2023 instead of the agreement.
- Confirm the order was placed by 2025-03-31, because that exception counts orders placed, not request dates.
