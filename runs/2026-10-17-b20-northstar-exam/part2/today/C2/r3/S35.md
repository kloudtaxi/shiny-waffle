## Answer

**No, not by Sarah alone.** The customer is eligible, but the limit needs approval from Priya Shah, the Finance Manager. One more catch: the system of record shows a different requested amount than the one you submitted.

**The request.** In the credit-request table, CR-9201 is for C-1001, dated 2026-09-23, raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example, with status Pending Approval. It asks to raise the limit from **$250,000 to $400,000**, not the $300,000 you submitted. Sarah's own email says the same: "take their credit limit from $250,000 to $400,000." I decided on $400,000 and made the difference a blocking condition.

The request rests on Acme's own standing. Sarah mentions Acme Group's parent guarantee only for "more headroom later."

**The customer.** ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings." In the CRM it is "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048), on the Strategic tier, with the same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address. The supply agreement confirms Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing.

**The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the previous policy.

**Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) was not due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it falls outside the test.

No invoices are late, so Acme passes.

**The maximum limit.** Strategic accounts can go up to $750,000, so $400,000 is within limits on Acme's own standing. Acme Group Holdings' guarantee does cover Acme Mfg. Holdings from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, but this request doesn't need it.

**Who can approve.**
- Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives her no credit approval authority.
- Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need **Finance Manager approval**. That's Priya Shah (EMP-401).
- VP Sales concurrence is only needed for Strategic limits above $500,000, so it doesn't apply here.

**Worth noting.** As of today, 2026-10-18, INV-62305 is unpaid and 16 days past its due date. It didn't affect the decision, which is judged as of the request date. It's also more than the requested limit.
