## Answer

Yes. Sarah Chen can approve the 8% herself under the policy in force on 2026-09-23. But I couldn't find request DR-9106 in the CRM discount-request records, so the request has to be confirmed before anyone acts on this.

1. **The request.** The justification is "Competitive", so it relies on standard pricing, not a contract term or pricing exception. The CRM records hold other NS-EDGE requests (DR-7002, DR-7012, DR-7014) and DR-9001 (Sarah's NS-500 request for Acme on the same date), but no DR-9106. I decided on the request as you gave it and didn't swap in another record.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen.
3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package. The $85,000 list value matches the other NS-EDGE requests. 8% of $85,000 is $6,800, leaving $78,200 net, so the figures add up.
4. **Contract and exception.** Not needed for a standard-pricing request. For reference, Acme's only recorded pricing terms are for the NS-500 controller, and the 10% exception for it ran 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. Neither covers NS-EDGE.
5. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in effect from 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy; the 2027 policy only applies to requests dated 2027-01-01 or later.
6. **Sarah's authority.** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%."
7. **The approval bands.** Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales; above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. Only discounts above 10% need recorded approval evidence. At 8%, none of that applies.

**Outcome: APPROVE.** It's blocked until someone confirms DR-9106 exists in Northstar CRM.
