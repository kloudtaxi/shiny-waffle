## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this, and it can't be approved as submitted. It needs commercial review (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`).** The request says it relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement's discount covers only the NS-500 controller, not NS-EDGE.

**The request (DR-9109).** I couldn't find DR-9109 in the knowledge base. Searching for the request id found nothing, and the CRM identifier lookup isn't available. So I've decided on the request exactly as you gave it, and confirming it exists in the CRM is a blocking condition.

1. **Customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. The contracting company is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing. Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), which is a different account.
2. **Product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003, software), list price $85,000. That matches the request's list value.
3. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. NS-EDGE is one of the products it supplies. But its pricing clause, §3.2, says: *"Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."*
4. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is in force from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15%. It is limited to *"NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only… It does not apply to any other Northstar product."* I found no contract term or exception that covers NS-EDGE for Acme. The NS-500 terms don't carry over to other products.
5. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (calendar 2026; the 2027 policy only applies from 2027-01-01).
6. **Sarah's authority.** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 12% is above that. The exception also says it *"does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."*
7. **Who would have to approve.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That role is held by Michael Torres, Sarah's manager.

**What happens next:**
- **Commercial review:** either new or amended terms that cover NS-EDGE, or the request is resubmitted as a standard-pricing request with a different justification.
- **Approval if it's resubmitted as standard pricing:** 12% would still need Michael Torres (VP Sales); Sarah can't approve it herself.
- **CRM check:** someone needs to confirm that DR-9109 exists in Northstar CRM with these figures.
