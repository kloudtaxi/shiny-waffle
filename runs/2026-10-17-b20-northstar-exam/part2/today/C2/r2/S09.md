## Answer

I found everything I needed, so I'm writing up the answer.

**No. Sarah Chen could not have approved this 15% discount on 2025-03-31.** She had enough approval authority. The problem is that Acme wasn't eligible for 15% on that date.

**Request.** I couldn't find DR-9101 in the knowledge base. The Northstar CRM records it holds for Sarah, Acme and NS-500 are DR-8104 (15%, dated 2025-09-16, which she approved herself) and DR-9001 (15%, dated 2026-09-23, still pending). I've judged DR-9101 as you gave it and haven't swapped in either of those. Confirming that DR-9101 exists in the CRM is a blocking condition.

**Customer and product.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the contract, the customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen owns the account. SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**Eligibility on 2025-03-31:**
- The request cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement only takes effect on April 1, 2025, one day after the request date. It wasn't in force yet.
- The 15% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, also takes effect on 2025-04-01. It replaces the older exception only "for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01."
- The exception that did cover NS-500 on 2025-03-31 was EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023. It allows "up to **10%** off list price… for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."
- So the most Acme could get was 10%. 15% is over that.

**Authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31) says "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%." Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so a 15% discount was within her authority. But authority doesn't create eligibility. A more senior approver wouldn't help either, because the problem is the customer's terms, not who signs.

**Outcome: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** There are two ways forward:
- Re-date the request to 2025-04-01 or later. Then the MSA and the 15% exception apply, and 15% stays within Sarah's own authority.
- Cut it to 10% or less under the old exception.

Either way it needs a new or corrected request.
