## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to a 15% discount on the NS-500 on 2027-02-01, but under the 2027 pricing policy her own limit is 5%. A 15% discount needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, currently David Morgan. One thing to fix first: I couldn't find request DR-9113 in the knowledge base.

**What I checked**
- **The request:** I couldn't find DR-9113 in the knowledge base. Of the 9xxx requests, only DR-9001 appears. So I've decided on the request as you gave it, and confirming it exists in CRM is a blocking condition. Its date is also in the future (today is 2026-10-17). The amounts add up: 15% of $250,000 is $37,500, leaving $212,500.
- **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, is ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings. Both records share the same DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the guarantee states that Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah owns the account.
- **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500 through its pricing schedule ("Schedule B").
- **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, up to 15% off list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaces the earlier 10% exception for requests from 2025-04-01. 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies from 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 5%. Above 5% and up to 12% needs the VP Sales. Above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. Anything above 5% also needs recorded approval evidence.
- **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Older policy documents gave that role 10% or 15%, but those don't apply to a 2027 request.
- **The approver:** David Morgan is listed as Chief Revenue Officer. Nothing in the knowledge base confirms he'll still hold the role on 2027-02-01, so the approval should go to whoever is CRO then.
