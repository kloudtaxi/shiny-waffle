## Answer

No. Sarah Chen could not have approved this herself. Under the policy in force on that date, 22% needs VP Sales approval, which means Michael Torres.

**The request.** I couldn't find DR-9103 anywhere in the knowledge base. The Northstar CRM discount table has other requests on this account (DR-8104, DR-9001), but not this one. The closest match is DR-7012, which has the same figures: 22%, NS-EDGE, $85,000 list, $18,700 off. But DR-7012 is for a different account (CRM-2063), was raised by Anthony Day on 2025-11-07, and Michael Torres approved it. It is not this request, so I didn't decide on it. I decided on DR-9103 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists in the CRM.

**The basis.** The justification is "Competitive," so the request relies on standard pricing, not a contract term or pricing exception.

- **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing is CRM-2048, and Sarah Chen owns the account. It is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. Don't confuse it with "Acme Industrial," which is a different account (CRM-2091).
- **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), list price $85,000. That matches the submitted list value, and 22% of it is $18,700, leaving $66,300. The figures add up.
- **Contract and exceptions:** The only Acme pricing exceptions I found are for the NS-500 controller, not NS-EDGE. The NS-500 10% exception ran from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. Since the request doesn't rely on special terms, none of this changes the outcome.
- **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, which "sets discount approval authority for direct enterprise sales for calendar 2025." The 2026 policy only applies from 2026-01-01.
- **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2025 policy, "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%." 22% is over that.
- **Required approver:** The policy says "Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval." Michael Torres (EMP-200) is VP Sales and is Sarah's manager. The 2025 policy has no higher band, so the CRO isn't needed. Under the 2026 policy, anything over 20% would go to the CRO.
