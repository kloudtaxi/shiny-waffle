## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. In the CRM, DR-9001 is a different deal from the one you were sent: it's for the NS-500 Industrial Controller, not the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. I've decided on the CRM record. On that record, Acme qualifies for 15%, but it needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**1. The request doesn't match the CRM.** The CRM's DR-9001 line reads: `DR-9001 CRM-2048 NS-500 | 15.0 | 250000 | 37500 | 212500 | sarah.chen@northstar.example | 2026-09-23 | Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement | Pending Approval`.

| | You were sent | CRM record |

| Product | NS-CLOUD | NS-500 |
| List value | $120,000 | $250,000 |
| Discount | $18,000 | $37,500 |
| Net value | $102,000 | $212,500 |

The account, the 15%, the requestor, the date and the justification all match. The figures you were sent ($120,000 / 15% / 10%-style values) look like another CRM record, DR-7003. That one is an NS-CLOUD deal for a different account (CRM-3008), already approved in 2025.

**2. Customer and contract.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (legally Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing). Its Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23.

**3. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows a discount of up to 15% of list. It covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and was approved by David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer. For NS-500, 15% is exactly at the limit, so Acme is eligible. If the deal really is NS-Cloud, no exception covers it, and the answer would be `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, not an approval.

**4. Authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (the 2027 policy only covers requests from 2027-01-01). Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive. Her manager, Michael Torres (EMP-200), is VP Sales. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." That applies even though Sarah prepared the exception and owns the account.
