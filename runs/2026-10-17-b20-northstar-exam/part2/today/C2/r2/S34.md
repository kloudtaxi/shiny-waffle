## Answer

CR-9208 can't be acted on yet. The ERP data I can search has no request with that ID. On the merits, the request qualifies, but Michael Torres can't approve it himself: it needs approval from Elena Novak and agreement from David Morgan.

**1. The request.** The only ERP credit request for customer C-1001 is **CR-9201**. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 to raise the limit from $250,000 to $400,000. It has the same justification as CR-9208 and is also "Pending Approval". I haven't treated CR-9201 as CR-9208; I assessed CR-9208 exactly as you gave it. It is based on the customer's own standing. It doesn't cite any guarantee.

**2. The customer.** C-1001 is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM account CRM-2048, same DUNS number 04-812-7730). CRM records the account as **Strategic**. "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091) is a different account.

**3. Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**4. Payment history.** The rule: in the 12 months before the request, no invoice that fell due may have been paid more than 30 days late, or be unpaid more than 30 days past due. Two invoices fall in that window, and both were paid early:
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.

INV-62305 ($720,000) was due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count. No payment is recorded for it, so it's about 15 days overdue as of today.

**5. Maximum limit.** The policy caps Strategic accounts at $750,000. $650,000 is within that, so the customer qualifies. Acme Group Holdings' guarantee covers Acme Mfg. Holdings up to $400,000, but this request doesn't need it.

**6. Michael Torres's authority.** He is VP Sales. The policy gives VP Sales no authority to approve credit limits.

**7. Who must sign off.** Limits above $500,000 need the Director of Finance's approval. For Strategic accounts they also need the VP Sales to agree. Nobody may approve or agree to a request they submitted; that role passes to the requester's manager. So:
- **Approval:** Elena Novak, Director of Finance.
- **Agreement:** David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer (Michael's manager), in place of Michael.

**8. Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION,** on hold until someone confirms CR-9208 exists in the ERP.
