## Answer

**No, it can't be approved as submitted.** The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED. The request is for Acme Industrial Supply Co., but the contract pricing it cites belongs to Acme Manufacturing, a different company. Separately, I can't find DR-9105 in the CRM records at all.

**1. The request.** DR-9105: 15% off NS-500, list $250,000, net $212,500. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", which makes it a request on special terms. The knowledge base holds no record of DR-9105. The CRM discount records do contain **DR-9001**, which has the same date, product, discount and justification, but it is for account **CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing)**, not CRM-2091. I have not swapped DR-9001 in for DR-9105.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2091 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** in Akron, OH. It is ERP customer C-1044, a Standard-tier account owned by Zachary Brown, not Sarah. It is a separate company from Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, Milwaukee, a Strategic account owned by Sarah).

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4–5. Contract and exception.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% off NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and its customer is **Acme Manufacturing**. The source document says plainly: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered."* Special terms don't carry over to another customer, so nothing establishes eligibility for this account.

**6–8. Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (it replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01). Under it:
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%, so Sarah can't approve 15% herself.
- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200).
- The 2025 policy let her approve up to 15%, which explains why she approved DR-8104 herself in September 2025. That limit no longer applies.

**9. Decision.** Commercial review is needed. Even if it turns out the request was meant for Acme Manufacturing (that is, DR-9001), the 15% would still need Michael Torres's approval. The exception only sets eligibility; it gives no employee authority to approve.
