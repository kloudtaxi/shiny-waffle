## Answer

**No, it can't be approved yet. The decision is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** On its own standing, Acme can go to $750,000 at most. The $1,000,000 request depends on a parent guarantee, and nothing in the knowledge base shows that guarantee exists for this customer. There is a second problem: CR-9203 itself is not in the ERP.

1. **The request isn't on record.** The ERP extract has no CR-9203. The only open request for customer C-1001 is CR-9201: Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, $250,000 to $400,000, for a third-plant ramp-up, "Pending Approval". I decided CR-9203 as you gave it and did not substitute CR-9201. Someone needs to confirm that CR-9203 exists.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" and CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" are the same company: same DUNS number (04-812-7730), same address, both Milwaukee, WI. Its account tier is Strategic and Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). One copy of the policy text gives different figures (Strategic maximum $600,000, other approval bands). That doesn't change the outcome, but Finance should confirm which text is in force.
4. **Payment history is clean as of 2026-09-23.** INV-60412 was paid 2025-11-24 (due 2025-11-28). INV-62011 was paid 2026-07-22 (due 2026-07-25). INV-62305 ($720,000) is unpaid but wasn't due until 2026-10-02. I couldn't find the policy's exact payment rule, so this is a check against due dates only.
5. **The maximum.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. The policy lets a guarantee in force raise it by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names. The only evidence of a guarantee is an email: "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it… Gina says it's still in place." I found no guarantee document, so its guarantor, the company it names, the amount and the dates are all unknown. Acme's support agreement also says its coverage doesn't extend to the parent, Acme Group Holdings, Inc., so the group's entities can't be treated as one.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives credit approval authority only to AR Specialists, the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance, so she has none.
7. **Required approvers.** For $1,000,000 the policy requires Director of Finance approval, plus VP Sales concurrence because this is a Strategic account above $500,000. The knowledge base doesn't say who holds either role.

What happens next: if a guarantee naming Acme Mfg. Holdings, in force on 2026-09-23 and for at least $250,000, turns up, the request could be decided as APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION by those two approvers. Without one, $1,000,000 is above the maximum and the decision would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.
