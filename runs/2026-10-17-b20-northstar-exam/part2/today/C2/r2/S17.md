## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve 12% on NS-Edge for Acme, and the request shouldn't be approved as submitted.** The justification cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement's discount term, and Acme's pricing exception, cover only the NS-500 Industrial Controller. Nothing gives Acme a discount on NS-Edge, so this needs commercial review. Separately, the 12% is above Sarah's own limit, and I couldn't find DR-9109 in the knowledge base.

1. **The request.** I couldn't find DR-9109 in the knowledge base. The CRM records I can see include DR-9001, a different request from Sarah on the same day (2026-09-23) for 15% on NS-500. I haven't used DR-9001 in its place. I've decided on DR-9109 as you gave it, with a condition to confirm it exists. Its basis is the customer contract ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS, 04-812-7730). Sarah owns the account. Don't confuse it with "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091), a different account.
3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package. Its list price is $85,000, which matches the request; 12% of that is $10,200, leaving $74,800.
4. **The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. NS-Edge is one of the products supplied under it. But the agreement's only discount term (section 3.2) is "discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller."
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." No exception covers NS-Edge, so Acme has no special terms for this product.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
7. **Sarah's authority.** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 12% is over that.
8. **The required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence above 10%.
9. **The decision: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.** Acme's contract and exception terms don't carry over to NS-Edge. Commercial review should decide whether to handle this as a standard-pricing request, which would still need Michael Torres's approval, or to agree new terms with Acme.
