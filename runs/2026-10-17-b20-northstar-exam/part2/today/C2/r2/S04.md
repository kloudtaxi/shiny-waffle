## Answer

No. The CRM record for DR-9001 is dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23, and on that date Sarah Chen's limit was 10%. A 15% discount needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Acme is entitled to the 15%; that doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it herself.

**The date matters.** DR-9001 is otherwise exactly as you submitted it (CRM-2048, NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, Pending Approval), so I've decided on the CRM date. On 2025-09-23 the answer would be yes: the 2025 Pricing Policy let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 15%. That's how she approved the identical DR-8104 herself on 2025-09-16, and why she thinks she can do it again ("what we did for them last September").

**How I got there:**
1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings), and Sarah owns the account.
2. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
3. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on both dates.
4. **Eligibility:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, at up to 15% off list. It was approved by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) on 2025-03-20. 15% is within it. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
5. **Policy:** The 2026 Pricing Policy fully replaces the 2025 one for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01.
6. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and under the 2026 policy that role can approve "up to and including 10%." 15% is over her limit.
7. **Approver:** The 2026 policy says "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager.

The request's justification cites the Master Supply Agreement. The knowledge base doesn't show a discount clause in it; the 15% eligibility comes from the exception. That's worth correcting in the record, but it doesn't block the decision.
