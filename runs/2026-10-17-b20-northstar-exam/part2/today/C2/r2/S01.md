> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

No, Sarah can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15%, but under the 2026 pricing policy her own limit is 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve request DR-9001.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 is in Northstar CRM and matches what you sent. It asks for 15% off NS-500 on account CRM-2048, a list value of $250,000. The discount is $37,500 and the net is $212,500, which adds up. The request is dated 2026-09-23, was raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example and is "Pending Approval". It relies on the contract: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** The CRM account is CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing". The ERP customer is C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings". They are the same company: the Master Supply Agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing", and both records have the same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. Section 3.2 says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing for "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only". The maximum is 15% of list price. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and was approved by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) on 2025-03-20. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. So the 15% asked for is exactly the maximum Acme is entitled to. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, which runs 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy and is itself replaced by the 2027 policy from 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, account executives "may approve discounts up to and including 10%." The knowledge base also contains a statement that they can approve up to 15%, but that comes from the 2025 policy, which no longer applies.
8. **Who must approve:** The 2026 policy says "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." The approval matrix agrees (VP Sales: more than 10% up to 20%). The VP Sales is Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager.

One thing to note: the policy also says approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM. I've listed that as a non-blocking condition.
