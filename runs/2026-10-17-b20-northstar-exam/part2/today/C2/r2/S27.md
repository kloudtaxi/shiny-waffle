## Answer

**Answer: yes, it can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen alone.** Acme qualifies for $650,000 on its own standing. The 2026 policy says a limit this size needs approval from Elena Novak (Director of Finance) and agreement from Michael Torres (VP Sales). One thing has to be sorted out before anyone acts on it: **CR-9202 isn't in the knowledge base.** The only Northstar ERP request I found for customer C-1001 is **CR-9201**. It has the same requestor, date (2026-09-23), current limit ($250,000), reason and "Pending Approval" status, but asks for **$400,000**. In an email, Sarah also describes CR-9201 as a raise "from $250,000 to $400,000". As instructed, I decided CR-9202 as you gave it and did not swap in CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9202 exists is a blocking condition.

1. **Customer.** C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the ERP is Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048). The supply agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing, and both records show the same address and the same business ID number (04-812-7730). The CRM puts it in the **Strategic** tier, and the policy says tiers come from the CRM.
2. **Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
3. **Payment history.** The policy fails a customer if any invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid more than 30 days late, or is unpaid more than 30 days past due. The record is clean:
   - INV-62011 ($480,000) was due 2026-07-25 and paid on 2026-07-22, early.
   - INV-60412 ($85,000) was due 2025-11-28 and paid on 2025-11-24, early.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it isn't counted.
4. **Maximum limit.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000, so $650,000 fits. The request doesn't rely on any third-party backing. Acme Group Holdings, Inc. does guarantee this customer's payments (up to $400,000, 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31), but it isn't needed here.
5. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives credit approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager (up to $500,000) and the Director of Finance (above $500,000). She has none.
6. **Approvers.** Limits above $500,000 need the Director of Finance, Elena Novak. Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need the VP Sales to agree, which is Michael Torres. Nobody may approve or agree to their own request (it passes to the requestor's manager instead). Sarah submitted this one and holds neither role, so that rule doesn't change anyone.

Two other points don't block the decision but are worth knowing:
- INV-62305 ($720,000) was still unpaid when the data was pulled, about 15 days past its due date. It's within the 30-day allowance for now. That one invoice is larger than both the current limit and the requested $650,000.
- The guarantee would only matter if the request were changed to rely on it.
