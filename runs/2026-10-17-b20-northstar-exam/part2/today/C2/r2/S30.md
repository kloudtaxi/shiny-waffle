## Answer

**No. CR-9205 can't be approved: BlueRiver Logistics fails the payment-history rule.** The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. Sending it to a higher approver doesn't change that.

**1. The request.** I couldn't find CR-9205 itself in the knowledge base, so I decided on the ERP record you supplied. It asks to raise the limit from $100,000 to $200,000, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Michael Torres (VP Sales, EMP-200). It rests on the customer's own standing. No guarantee is mentioned. The guarantee in the knowledge base is from Acme Group Holdings and doesn't cover BlueRiver.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC" and CRM account CRM-2051 is "BlueRiver Logistics". Both have DUNS 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis, so they are the same company. The CRM tier is **Standard** and the payment terms are Net 45.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. The 2025 policy's $300,000 Standard maximum no longer applies.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid more than 30 days after its due date, or be unpaid more than 30 days past it. That window runs from 2025-09-23 to 2026-09-23.

Two invoices fall outside the window:
- INV-61433 was due 2025-08-29, before the window began.
- INV-30020 ($494,700) is due 2026-10-02, after the request date, and is still unpaid. It doesn't count against this request.

**5. Maximum limit.** The 2026 policy caps Standard accounts at $250,000. The $200,000 requested is within that cap, so only the payment history disqualifies the request.

**6–7. Authority.** The 2026 policy gives no approval authority to the VP Sales role. A limit between $100,000 and $500,000 needs Finance Manager approval. Even if the customer were eligible, Torres couldn't approve it. The policy also bars anyone from approving or concurring on a request they submitted. I couldn't find who holds the Finance Manager role.
