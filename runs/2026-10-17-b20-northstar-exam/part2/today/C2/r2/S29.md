## Answer

No. CR-9204 can't be approved as requested. $1,300,000 is above the most this customer can have under the 2026 policy, even counting the parent guarantee. Separately, I couldn't find CR-9204 in the knowledge base at all.

**The request.** I found no record of CR-9204. The only credit request on file for this customer is **CR-9201**. Sarah Chen raised it on the same date (2026-09-23), for the same customer (C-1001), from $250,000 to **$400,000**, with a different reason ("Third-plant ramp-up"), and it is Pending Approval. I've decided CR-9204 as you gave it, not CR-9201. Before anyone acts, someone needs to confirm that CR-9204 actually exists in Northstar ERP.

**The customer.** ERP C-1001 is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730). CRM records it as a **Strategic** account, and its parent is Acme Group Holdings, Inc.

**The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Under it:
- Strategic accounts can have at most **$750,000**.
- A parent-company guarantee in force raises that maximum by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names.

**The guarantee.** It is valid for this request:
- Acme Group Holdings guarantees the payments of Acme Mfg. Holdings by name.
- It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it covers 2026-09-23.
- It is capped at **$400,000**.

So the maximum is $750,000 + $400,000 = **$1,150,000**, and the request is $150,000 over it. A more senior approver can't fix that.

**Approval, for completeness.** Limits over $500,000 need the Director of Finance's approval and, for Strategic accounts, the VP Sales's agreement as well. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and has no approval authority under the policy, so she couldn't approve this on her own either way. I didn't look up who holds those two roles, because the limit can't be approved anyway.

**Not checked:** I didn't assess payment history. I couldn't find the policy's payment rule, and I didn't review the invoices on file for C-1001 (INV-60412, INV-62011, INV-62305). The excess over the maximum decides the request regardless.

A limit of up to $1,150,000 would be within the maximum, but it would need the Director of Finance and VP Sales, plus a payment-history check.
