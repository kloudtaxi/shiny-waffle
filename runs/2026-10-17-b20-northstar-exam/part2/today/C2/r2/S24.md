## Answer

**Yes, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it's above Sarah's own approval limit.

**The request.** In the CRM, DR-9001 is account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing), product NS-500, 15.0% off $250,000 list ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net). It was requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the request relies on contract terms. One thing doesn't match: your submission says the status is **"Approved"**, but the CRM says **"Pending Approval"** and records no approver or approval date. I decided on the CRM's status.

**Is Acme entitled to 15%? Yes.**
- **Contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028 ("unless terminated earlier under Section 9"). I found nothing showing it was terminated, so it was in force on 2026-09-23.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing for the "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only". It allows up to "15% of list price", running 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests on or after 2025-04-01. The right product and date are covered, and 15% is exactly the maximum.
- The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."

**Who can approve it?**
- **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 sets approval limits for calendar 2026. The 2027 policy only applies from 2027-01-01.
- **Sarah's limit:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%." 15% is over her limit.
- **Required approver:** "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." The VP Sales is Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager. The policy also says "Discounts greater than 10% require recorded approval evidence."

Sarah approved the same 15% discount herself last year (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That doesn't carry over: the 2026 policy cut the Account Executive limit to 10%. A separate document I found, likely the 2025 policy, allowed up to 15%.
