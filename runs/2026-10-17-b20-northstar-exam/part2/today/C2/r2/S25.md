## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve DR-9001 herself, but the request can go ahead with VP Sales approval.** The customer is entitled to 15% on this product. The problem is that 15% is above Sarah's own approval limit under the 2026 policy.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 matches the CRM record exactly: account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off $250,000 list ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It is based on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", which counts as a contract/exception basis.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". The ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings". They are the same company: both have DUNS 04-812-7730 and the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee. The Master Supply Agreement also names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".

**3. The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it was active on 2026-09-23. I found no record that it was terminated early. Section 3.2 says the customer "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller", as set out in the pricing exception in Schedule B.

**5. The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing, the NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15% of list price. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers the request date. David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests from 2025-04-01. The requested 15% is exactly at the maximum, so eligibility is established. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.

**7. Sarah's authority.** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, she can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is over that limit. Last September she approved the same 15% herself on DR-8104. That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. That limit no longer applies.

**8. The required approver.** The 2026 policy says "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." Michael Torres (EMP-200) is VP Sales and Sarah's manager.

**9. Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Michael Torres must approve before the discount is granted. Nothing else needs to happen first.
