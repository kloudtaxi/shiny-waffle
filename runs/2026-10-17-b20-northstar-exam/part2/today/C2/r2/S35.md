## Answer

Not by Sarah Chen alone. Acme Manufacturing qualifies for the limit, but Finance Manager Priya Shah has to approve it. There is also a problem with the amount: the request you sent asks for **$300,000**, but the ERP record for CR-9201 and Sarah's own email both say **$400,000**. I decided on the ERP figure of $400,000. Either way, the outcome and the approver are the same.

**1. The request.** CR-9201 is for ERP customer C-1001, dated 2026-09-23. It would raise the limit from $250,000 to $400,000 and is marked Pending Approval. It rests on Acme's own standing. Sarah's email mentions Acme Group's parent guarantee only as cover for more headroom "later", so the request doesn't depend on it.

**2. The customer.** C-1001 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. In the CRM it is account CRM-2048, a **Strategic** account, and Sarah is the account owner.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 covers requests from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes, with no late invoices:
- INV-60412 ($85,000) was due 2025-11-28 and paid early, on 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 ($480,000) was due 2026-07-25 and paid early, on 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) is due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

**5. Maximum limit.** The policy caps Strategic accounts at $750,000, and $400,000 is under that. The guarantee isn't needed. It is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc., covers Acme Mfg. Holdings for up to $400,000 from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and doesn't cover the parent itself.

**6. Sarah's authority.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives credit approval only to AR Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance. She has no authority here, and she can't approve a request she submitted anyway.

**7. Who must approve.** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need the Finance Manager, who is **Priya Shah**. VP Sales concurrence applies only to Strategic limits above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Before anyone acts on it, Sarah must confirm whether she wants $300,000 or $400,000 and the record must be corrected to match.
