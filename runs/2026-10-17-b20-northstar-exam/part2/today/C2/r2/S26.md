## Answer

**Yes, CR-9201 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen alone.** Acme Manufacturing qualifies for $400,000 on its own record. Under the 2026 policy, a $400,000 limit needs Finance Manager approval, and the Finance Manager is Priya Shah.

**1. The request.** The Northstar ERP record matches what you sent: CR-9201, customer C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. The current limit of $250,000 also matches the ERP credit record. The request doesn't depend on a guarantee. Sarah's email only says the Acme Group parent guarantee "should cover" more room *later*. So the request rests on the customer's own standing.

**2. The customer.** These records are all the same company. ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which trades as "Acme Manufacturing". The CRM record is CRM-2048. All three share the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, and DUNS number 04-812-7730.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice that fell due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Nothing breaks it.
- INV-60412 (due 2025-11-28) was paid early, on 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 (due 2026-07-25) was paid early, on 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000, due 2026-10-02) was not yet due on the request date.

**5. Maximum limit.** Acme Manufacturing is a Strategic account in CRM, and the Strategic maximum is $750,000. $400,000 is well under it, so the guarantee isn't needed. The guarantee does name this customer, runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is capped at $400,000.

**6. Sarah's authority.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives approval power only to Accounts Receivable Specialists, up to $100,000, so she can't approve this.

**7. Required approver.** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. The extra VP Sales sign-off applies only above $500,000. Priya Shah (EMP-401) is the Finance Manager. She didn't submit the request, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't stop her.

**Two things to note, neither of which blocks the decision.** As of today (2026-10-17), INV-62305 for $720,000 is unpaid and 15 days past due. Also, that invoice alone is far above both the current limit and the requested one.
