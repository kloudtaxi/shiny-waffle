## Answer

**Not at 18%.** The CRM record for DR-9001 shows **15%**, not 18%, so I decided on 15%. The answer is **approve with authorization**: Acme qualifies for 15% on the NS-500, but it's above Sarah Chen's own approval limit, so **Michael Torres (VP Sales)** must approve it.

**What I checked:**
1. **The request (CRM):** DR-9001 for account CRM-2048, product NS-500. The CRM shows 15.0%, a $250,000 list price, a $37,500 discount and $212,500 net. It's dated 2026-09-23, raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example, and pending approval. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". The figures you sent (18%, $45,000 discount, $205,000 net) don't match.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, which signed the agreement.
3. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it was active on the request date.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, at up to "15% of list price". It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and was approved by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer). It replaced the earlier 10% exception, which no longer applies. 15% is within the limit. **18% would not be**: it would need new or amended terms, and a more senior approver can't fix that.
5. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, for calendar 2026. The 2027 policy only applies to requests from 2027-01-01.
6. **Who can approve:** Enterprise Account Executives (Sarah's role) can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." (Sarah approved the similar 15% request DR-8104 herself in 2025, when the 2025 policy allowed account executives up to 15%. That limit no longer applies.)

**Before anyone acts:** the 18% vs. 15% difference has to be resolved first. If 18% is really what's wanted, this becomes a reject/escalate case, because it's above the exception's 15% cap.
