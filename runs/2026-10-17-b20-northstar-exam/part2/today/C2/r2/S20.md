## Answer

**No. The 18% discount should not be approved, by Michael Torres or by anyone more senior.** Acme's pricing exception allows at most 15% off NS-500. A higher approver can't fix that; Acme would need new or amended terms.

**One problem first: request DR-9112 isn't in the CRM records I can see.** The only Acme NS-500 request on 2026-09-23 is **DR-9001**: 15%, submitted by Sarah Chen, citing the same Acme Master Supply Agreement, status "Pending Approval". As the procedure requires, I decided DR-9112 as you gave it and did not decide DR-9001 in its place. Someone needs to confirm DR-9112 actually exists.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** 18% off NS-500 (list price $250,000, so a $45,000 discount), dated 2026-09-23, from michael.torres@northstar.example. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings.
3. **Product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. That matches the submitted figures.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028, so it was active on the request date.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, up to a **maximum of 15% off list price**. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." I found no later or amended exception. **18% is 3 points over the limit.**
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Michael Torres's authority:** He is VP Sales. The knowledge base doesn't hold the 2026 policy's approval bands, so I can't state his limit or who would need to approve 18%. One separate document says "Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval," but I couldn't confirm it is the 2026 policy. He did approve 18% competitive discounts for other customers (DR-7015, DR-7016). None of this changes the answer. The problem is Acme's eligibility, not his authority.

**What would change the answer:** Acme getting a new or amended exception that allows 18%. Alternatively, the request could be resubmitted at 15% or less, and the normal authority check would then apply.
