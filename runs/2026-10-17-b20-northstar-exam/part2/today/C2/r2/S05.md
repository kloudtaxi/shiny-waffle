## Answer

**No, not yet.** DR-9001 can't be approved on the evidence available. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", and nothing in the knowledge base shows what that agreement says.

**What I checked**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 matches what you submitted: CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. It relies on a contract term, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), the same company under a trading name, with the same DUNS number (04-812-7730). One email about this request was linked to "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091, Akron, OH), which is a different customer. That link looks wrong and doesn't affect this decision.
3. **Product:** NS-500 (NS-500 Industrial Controller).
4. **Contract:** "Acme Master Supply Agreement" appears only as the justification text on DR-9001 and on the earlier request DR-8104. It has no terms, dates or discount figure. The only Acme contract document is Schedule C (Support and Service Levels) to ACME-MFG-2025, which covers support, not pricing. Sarah's own email says only "I think their agreement covers it."
5. **Pricing exception:** I found none for Acme or NS-500 covering 2026-09-23.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
7. **Sarah's authority:** Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **Required approver:** For discounts over 10% and up to 20%, the policy requires VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager.

**Decision:** I'm asking for the evidence before deciding. Even if the agreement turns out to allow 15%, the policy says a contract allowing a discount isn't enough on its own to approve it. Sarah also can't approve it herself, so Michael Torres would still need to.

**Worth knowing:** Last year's 15% NS-500 discount for this account (DR-8104, 2025-09-16) is recorded as approved by Sarah herself. That doesn't set a precedent for DR-9001. It may be worth checking against the 2025 policy.
