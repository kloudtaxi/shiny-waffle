## Answer

I've finished checking. **DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on the NS-500. However, 15% is above what Sarah can approve herself, so the VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it. First, the request date needs correcting.

**What I checked**
1. **The request:** In the CRM discount log, DR-9001 is for CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off. That's $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, for a net of $212,500. Sarah Chen requested it, it relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," and its status is Pending Approval.
   - **The date doesn't match.** You submitted 2025-09-23, but the CRM log says **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email saying it's "what we did for them last September" (DR-8104, dated 2025-09-16) supports 2026. I've decided on the CRM date.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is the Acme Manufacturing account, and Sarah is the account owner.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028. I found nothing showing it was terminated early, so it was active on 2026-09-23.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing for the NS-500 only, at a maximum of 15% off list. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and was approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20. It replaces the older 10% exception. So the 15% requested is exactly at the maximum and is covered. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated in 2026.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve "discounts up to and including 10%." So 15% is beyond her authority.
8. **Who must approve:** The policy says "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." The VP Sales is Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager. Discounts above 10% also need recorded approval evidence.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme qualifies for the discount, but the contract doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it.

**Other things to know**
- **Blocking:** The request date has to be corrected to match the CRM record (2026-09-23). If 2025-09-23 were really the date, the 2025 pricing policy would apply instead, and I couldn't find its approval rules in the knowledge base.
- **Not blocking, but worth a look:** Last year's identical request, DR-8104 (15%, 2025-09-16), was approved by Sarah herself. Under the 2026 rules that would be beyond her authority, and I couldn't check the 2025 rules. If that was outside policy too, it shouldn't be used as a precedent.
