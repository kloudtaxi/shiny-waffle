## Answer

**Yes, on the merits, but it can't be acted on yet. CR-9203 isn't in the knowledge base**, so first confirm it exists in Northstar ERP. If it does, Acme qualifies for $1,000,000 thanks to the parent guarantee. The approval has to come from the Director of Finance, Elena Novak, with the VP Sales, Michael Torres, also agreeing to it. Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.

1. **The request.** The knowledge base has no record of CR-9203. The only credit request it holds for customer C-1001 is CR-9201: also by Sarah Chen, also dated 2026-09-23, but for $250,000 → $400,000. I haven't swapped that record in; I've decided CR-9203 as you gave it. The request relies on the parent guarantee.
2. **The customer.** ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM account CRM-2048). Its tier is Strategic. The supply agreement, the guarantee, the DUNS number (04-812-7730) and the address all match.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Two invoices fall in that window, and both were paid early:
   - INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.

   INV-62305 ($720,000) was due on 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count here. It is still unpaid as of today.
5. **The maximum limit.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. The policy raises it by the guaranteed amount for the customer the guarantee names. Acme Group Holdings, Inc. guarantees its subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings, trading as Acme Manufacturing, for up to $400,000, from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. So the maximum is $1,150,000, and the $1,000,000 request fits under it.
6. **Sarah's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and has no credit-approval authority. Under the policy, only Accounts Receivable Specialists can approve alone, and only up to $100,000.
7. **Who must approve.** Limits above $500,000 need the Director of Finance's approval, which is Elena Novak. Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need the VP Sales to agree to it, which is Michael Torres. Neither of them submitted the request, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't change who signs.
