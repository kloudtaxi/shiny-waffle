**SR-39802 is out of scope.** Northstar breached no service level and owes no credit, and no one has to be engaged or notified under the escalation procedure. The ticket is handled best effort.

**1. The customer.** Ticket SR-39802 was filed under account **CRM-2091**. That account belongs to **Acme Industrial Supply Co.**, 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron OH (DUNS 09-552-1187, ERP C-1044, Standard tier, account owner Zachary Brown). It is not the Acme that holds the Platinum plan. That Acme is **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, trading as Acme Manufacturing, in Milwaukee (DUNS 04-812-7730, ERP C-1001, CRM-2048).

**2. Scope.** Northstar's Platinum support terms (which cover NS-500 and NS-EDGE from 2025-04-01) are with Acme Mfg. Holdings only. They also state: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered."* The knowledge base holds only three sets of support terms: Blue River Logistics, Cedar Health Systems and Acme Mfg. Holdings. None covers Acme Industrial Supply Co. The caller's statement "We're Acme. We have the Acme Platinum support plan" is a claim made by name only, and the identifiers on the account contradict it.

**3. True severity (for internal priority only).** It would be **Severity 1**. All NS-Edge gateways at the Akron distribution center were down, conveyor monitoring was dark, picking had stopped and there was no workaround. I couldn't retrieve the guide's exact Severity 1 wording. However, its Severity 2 definition applies only to cases that are "Not Severity 1," and it treats "a Severity 1 condition with a workaround" as Severity 2. With no workaround here, this is Severity 1. Under the out-of-scope rule, severity sets internal priority only and carries no targets.

**4–7. Clock, breach and credit.** The rule for out-of-scope tickets is: *"handled best effort: severity is classified only for internal priority, no targets apply (nothing can be breached and no credit is owed), and the escalations above do not apply."* So:
- The ticket was opened 2026-08-10T06:05-04:00 and restored 2026-08-10T14:20-04:00, but neither time is measured against a target.
- There is no breach and no credit.

**8. Obligations.**
- **Northstar:** none under the service-level terms. No escalation engagements or notifications apply, no credit memo is due, and no root-cause finding is required. Amy Barrett emailed a credit claim on 2026-08-14 asking for the Severity 1 restoration credit "under Acme's Platinum plan." It has no basis, because no terms cover this customer. Replying to say so is good practice, but the record shows no deadline for it.
