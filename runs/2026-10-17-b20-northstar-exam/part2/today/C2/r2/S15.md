## Answer

**No, not on the basis given.** Michael Torres has the authority to approve 12%, but the request rests on another customer's contract. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**.

**Why**

1. **The request isn't in the CRM.** I found no record of DR-9107 in the knowledge base. The CRM discount-request records I did find include DR-9001 (same date, same product, same justification) for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), not BlueRiver. I've decided on DR-9107 as you gave it, and its existence needs confirming first.
2. **Customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, a Standard-tier account owned by Kelly Floyd. I found no contract or pricing exception for BlueRiver.
3. **Basis.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." That agreement (effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) and its NS-500 exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15%, valid to 2028-03-31), belong to **Acme Manufacturing**. The exception itself says it gives commercial eligibility only and grants no approval authority. Special terms don't carry over to another customer, so BlueRiver has no established eligibility for contract pricing. A higher approver can't fix this. It needs commercial review, or the request has to be resubmitted on standard pricing with a proper justification.
4. **Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (it covers requests dated on or after 2026-01-01; the 2027 policy starts 2027-01-01). Its bands are: Enterprise Account Executives up to 10%; VP Sales for over 10% up to 20%; Chief Revenue Officer above 20%. Torres is VP Sales, so 12% is within his limit. Under the principles, though, authority alone doesn't let him approve terms the customer isn't eligible for. On standard pricing he could approve 12% himself.
5. **Figures.** List price is $250,000, so 12% is $30,000 off, leaving $220,000. These match.
