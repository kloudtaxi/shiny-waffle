## Answer

**No. CR-9207 can't be approved. The outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Acme Industrial Supply Co. isn't eligible for $500,000, and the parent guarantee the request cites doesn't cover this company.

**1. The request.** CR-9207 is for ERP customer C-1044. It would raise the limit from $50,000 to $500,000. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23, and it relies on "the Acme parent guarantee." The knowledge base has no record of CR-9207 itself, so I decided on the request as you gave it. Confirming that it exists in the ERP is a blocking condition.

**2. The customer.** C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH (DUNS 09-552-1187, CRM-2091). The CRM lists it as a **Standard** account. It is a different company from Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing in Milwaukee (C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730).

**3. The guarantee doesn't cover this customer.** Acme Group Holdings, Inc. guarantees the payments of "its wholly owned subsidiary **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as Acme Manufacturing." It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is capped at $400,000. Acme Industrial Supply isn't named. A separate support agreement states plainly: "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered." I found the guarantee itself, so there's nothing to ask for; it simply covers another company. Even for Acme Mfg. Holdings, it would only support up to $400,000.

**4. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It sets a maximum limit of **$250,000 for Standard accounts**. The requested $500,000 is above that, so the customer isn't eligible, and a more senior approver can't change that.

**5. Approval authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales and has no credit approval authority. A $500,000 limit falls in the Finance Manager band (over $100,000 up to $500,000). The knowledge base doesn't say who the Finance Manager is. No one may approve their own request. This doesn't change the outcome.

**6. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or remain unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. I only checked part of the history, because the limit already rules the request out. Two C-1044 invoices I looked at were due in 2025, before that 12-month window: INV-30001 was paid 7 days late and INV-30003 was paid early.
