## Answer

**Answer: Not by Michael Torres alone.** Under the 2025 policy, BlueRiver could receive a $200,000 limit, but a Finance Manager (Priya Shah) has to approve it. There's also a bigger catch: I can't find CR-9206 in the ERP records I can see, so that has to be confirmed before anyone acts on this decision.

**1. The request.** I decided on the request as you gave it: CR-9206, C-1002, $100,000 → $200,000, dated 2025-09-23, submitted by michael.torres@northstar.example, status Pending Approval. The only credit request in the ERP records I can see is CR-9201, a different customer (C-1001) and date (2026-09-23), so I didn't use it. The ERP does confirm C-1002's current limit of $100,000. The justification is fleet expansion and nothing mentions a guarantee, so the request rests on the customer's own standing.

**2. The customer.** These are all the same company:
- ERP C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC" (DUNS 07-331-9025, Net 45).
- CRM-2051 is "BlueRiver Logistics", with the same DUNS and address, and its tier is **Standard**.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2025 covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 45 days after its due date. Only one invoice in the records falls in that window, INV-61433. It was due 2025-08-29 and paid 2025-09-02, 4 days late, so it passes. The other C-1002 invoices are all due after the request date. The records hold no earlier quarterly invoices in the window (late 2024 to Q2 2025).

**5. The maximum limit.** Standard accounts can go up to $300,000, so $200,000 is within the limit. The customer is **eligible**.

**6. Michael's authority.** He is VP Sales. The 2025 credit policy gives that role no authority to approve credit limits, and nobody may approve a request they submitted anyway.

**7. Who must approve.** Under the 2025 approval bands:
- Up to $50,000: an AR Specialist.
- $50,000 to $750,000: the **Finance Manager**.
- Above $750,000: the Director of Finance.

$200,000 falls in the Finance Manager band, and Priya Shah (EMP-401) holds that role. The 2025 policy requires no concurrence.
