## Answer

**Yes: on the evidence, Sarah Chen could have approved this herself (outcome APPROVE).** But two things must be fixed before the decision is acted on.

**The request.** DR-9110 is not in the knowledge base. A search for it found nothing, and the CRM discount records it holds for CRM-2048 are DR-8104, DR-9001 and others, not DR-9110. So I decided on the request exactly as you gave it and did not substitute another record. Confirming that DR-9110 exists in Northstar CRM is a blocking condition.

**Checks against the knowledge base:**
1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. Sarah Chen owns the account.
2. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
3. **Contract on 2025-03-31:** The "Acme Master Supply Agreement" named in the justification is **not in force on that date**. It runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028. The agreement in force was **ACME-SA-2023**, status Active.
4. **Pricing exception:** Under ACME-SA-2023, exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-10** lets Acme Manufacturing have "up to 10% off list price on the NS-500… for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31". 2025-03-31 is the last day it covers, and 8% is within the 10% maximum, so Acme is eligible. The newer exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15%) only applies to requests dated on or after 2025-04-01, so it doesn't apply here.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, in effect from 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31.
6. **Her authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under that policy, Enterprise Account Executives "may approve discounts up to and including 15%". 8% is within her limit, so she doesn't need anyone else's approval. VP Sales approval is only needed above 15%.

The figures add up: 8% of $250,000 is $20,000, leaving a net of $230,000.

**What must be fixed first:** the request rests on the right outcome but cites the wrong source. The justification names the Master Supply Agreement, which only took effect the next day. The eligibility actually comes from ACME-SA-2023 and EXC-ACME-NS500-10, so the justification should be corrected before approval.
