## Answer

No, CR-9205 can't be approved. BlueRiver Logistics fails the payment-history rule in the 2026 credit policy. Sending it to a higher approver doesn't change that.

**1. The request.** CR-9205 asks to raise ERP customer C-1002 from $100,000 to $200,000. Michael Torres (VP Sales) submitted it on 2026-09-23. The reason given is fleet expansion. It relies on BlueRiver's own standing, not on a guarantee. I couldn't find CR-9205 itself in the knowledge base, so its existence needs confirming.

**2. The customer.** The ERP record for C-1002 shows a $100,000 limit, last reviewed 2026-01-15, which matches the request. The CRM lists BlueRiver Logistics as CRM-2051, Standard tier. I found nothing that formally links C-1002 to CRM-2051; the link rests on the request itself.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history: fails.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid more than 30 days after its due date, or be unpaid more than 30 days after it. That window runs from 2025-09-23 to 2026-09-23.
- **INV-62147** ($30,000, for NS-Cloud in the second quarter of 2026) was due 2026-05-30 and paid 2026-07-21, **52 days late**. This breaks the rule. The record shows no correction.
- The other invoices in the window were on time or 2 days late at most: INV-61688, INV-30014, INV-61902 and INV-62391.
- INV-30020 ($494,700) is still unpaid but wasn't due until 2026-10-02, so it doesn't count against the request.

**5. Maximum limit.** Standard accounts can go up to $250,000, so $200,000 would have fit. No guarantee is involved.

**6–7. Authority.** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. The policy doesn't give VP Sales any credit authority. Torres also can't approve his own request; that would pass to his manager (EMP-300). I couldn't find who holds the Finance Manager role. None of this matters, because the payment history already rules the request out.

**8. Decision: `REJECT_OR_ESCALATE`**
