## Answer

**Yes, but not on Sarah Chen's own authority, and not at the figure you sent.** The system of record holds DR-9001 at **15% off**, not 8%. At 15%, Acme is eligible for the discount, but it is above Sarah's limit. So it needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

1. **The request.** The CRM record shows DR-9001 at 15.0%: $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net. It was dated 2026-09-23, raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example, justified as "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", and is Pending Approval. The figures you sent (8.0%, $20,000 off, $230,000 net) don't match. I decided on the system of record's 15% and made the mismatch a blocking condition. Sarah's own note backs the 15%: "They're asking for 15% off list, which is what we did for them last September…"
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. The agreement's legal party is Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it was active on the request date.
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, for Acme Manufacturing, with a maximum of 15% off list. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and was approved by David Morgan (CRO) on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). A 15% request is within that maximum. The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority."
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated from 2026-01-01. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority.** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over her limit. The 15% limit for account executives was in the 2025 policy. That's why she could approve last year's 15% request (DR-8104) herself, but she can't do it now.
8. **Who must approve.** Under the 2026 policy, discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager. Anything over 10% also needs recorded approval evidence.
