## Answer

**No, 18% can't be approved.** Michael Torres is VP Sales, and the 2026 pricing policy lets the VP Sales approve discounts above 10% and up to 20%, so he does have the authority. That doesn't settle it, though: Acme's pricing exception for the NS-500 caps the discount at 15%. Under the procedure, a request that goes beyond the customer's eligibility can't be fixed by a higher approver. It needs new or amended commercial terms.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9112, 18% off the NS-500 on a $250,000 list value ($45,000 discount, $205,000 net). The figures add up. It's dated 2026-09-23, requested by michael.torres@northstar.example, and justified as "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." That makes the basis the contract or its exception. I couldn't check DR-9112 against the CRM itself, because the knowledge base doesn't hold discount request records.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, which is the party to the Master Supply Agreement.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23.
5. **Exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of "15% of list price." It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) and was approved by David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer. It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority." The request is 3 points above the cap.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy only applies from 2027-01-01.
7. **Authority:** Michael Torres is listed as VP Sales (EMP-200).
8. **Required approver:** 18% falls in the "greater than 10% and up to and including 20%" band, which needs VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres. Discounts above 10% also need recorded approval evidence.
9. **Decision:** REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. Approving within his authority doesn't change the fact that 18% exceeds what Acme is eligible for.

Things to note:
- An internal note says Acme's 2025 pricing is "I believe it's 15%" and should be confirmed with Deal Desk. That's a guess, not evidence. The exception document itself confirms 15%.
- Michael is both the requestor and the approver for this band. I found no rule on approving your own request. It doesn't change the outcome here, but it's worth checking if he resubmits at 15% or less.

**Options:** reprice at 15% or less under the existing exception, which Michael can approve with recorded approval evidence. Or negotiate amended terms with Acme to go higher.
