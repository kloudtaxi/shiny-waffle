**Short answer: yes, on its merits, but it can't be acted on yet.** The result is APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION. Elena Novak (Director of Finance) must approve it and Michael Torres (VP Sales) must concur. One thing blocks it first: **CR-9203 isn't in the knowledge base.** The only credit request recorded for C-1001 is **CR-9201**, also from Sarah Chen and dated 2026-09-23, which asks to raise the limit from $250,000 to **$400,000**. I've decided CR-9203 as you gave it, not CR-9201, and made confirming that it exists a blocking condition.

**1. The request.** The basis is the parent guarantee, as the justification states. I couldn't check the identifier directly: the ERP identifier lookup isn't served yet.

**2. The customer.** These names are all the same company:
- ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings"
- CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing", Strategic tier
- The legal entity is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing.

All three share DUNS 04-812-7730 and the same Milwaukee address.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24, on time.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22, on time.
- INV-62305 ($720,000): due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count. It was still unpaid as of today (17 days past due), so it's worth watching.
- No late invoices.

**5. Maximum limit.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. The policy says a parent guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, for the customer the guarantee names.
- Acme Group Holdings, Inc. guarantees "its wholly owned subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing", so it names this customer.
- It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it was in force on the request date.
- It's capped at $400,000.
- The maximum is therefore $1,150,000, and $1,000,000 fits within it.

**6. Requestor authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. I found no credit approval authority for her role, and she can't approve her own request anyway.

**7. Required approvers.** Limits above $500,000 need Director of Finance approval: Elena Novak. Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence: Michael Torres. Torres is Chen's manager, but he didn't submit the request, so he can concur.
