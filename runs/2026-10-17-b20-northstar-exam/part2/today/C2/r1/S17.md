## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this.** The request falls short on two separate grounds, so it needs commercial review (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`).

**1. No special terms cover this product.** The request's stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. The Master Supply Agreement is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which the documents call "a different company" that "is not covered."
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement was in force on 2026-09-23. It runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, and NS-EDGE is one of the products Northstar supplies under it. However, the knowledge base holds no discount term in the agreement for NS-EDGE.
- **Pricing exception:** Acme's only current exception is EXC-ACME-NS500-15, which allows up to 15% from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It covers the "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." It doesn't extend to the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (list price $85,000), so 12% on NS-EDGE has no established eligibility.

**2. 12% is above Sarah's own limit.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated from 2026-01-01; the 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01. Under the 2026 policy, Enterprise Account Executives may approve "up to and including 10%," and "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. The VP Sales is Michael Torres, her manager. Even if commercial review clears the request, it would still need his approval, and a contract term wouldn't change that.

**3. DR-9109 isn't in the knowledge base.** The figures you sent add up: 12% of $85,000 is $10,200, leaving $74,800. But the discount-request records show no DR-9109. The closest record, DR-9001 from the same requestor on the same date, is a different request: 15% on NS-500 at $250,000. I haven't used it in its place. Someone needs to confirm DR-9109 exists in Northstar CRM before anything is done with it.
