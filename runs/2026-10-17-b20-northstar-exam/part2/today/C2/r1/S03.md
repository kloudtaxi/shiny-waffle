## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** The CRM record for DR-9001 doesn't match what you submitted. It's for the NS-500 Industrial Controller, not the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. On that record, Acme is entitled to 15%, but it needs VP Sales Michael Torres to approve it, and the product mismatch has to be settled before anyone acts.

**1. The CRM record doesn't match the submission.** Northstar CRM shows DR-9001 as: account CRM-2048, product **NS-500**, 15.0%, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", status Pending Approval. Your submission says NS-CLOUD at $120,000 list, $18,000 discount and $102,000 net. Sarah's own message also says Acme wants the NS-500 for its third plant and that she logged it as DR-9001. So I decided on the CRM record, the NS-500. The mismatch is a blocking condition below.

**2. Customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. Sarah Chen owns the account.

**3. Contract and exception: Acme is entitled to 15%.**
- The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23.
- Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% off list on the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It was approved by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) on 2025-03-20 and replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme qualifies.
- **If the request really is for NS-Cloud, the answer changes.** No exception covers that product, so it would need commercial review (REVIEW_REQUIRED) instead.

**4. Who can approve.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies. The 2025 policy ended on 2025-12-31, and the 2027 policy only covers requests from 2027-01-01. Its approval bands are:
- Enterprise Account Executive: up to 10%. Sarah's title is Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is beyond her limit.
- VP Sales: over 10% up to 20%. That's Michael Torres, Sarah's manager.

Sarah approved the same 15% for Acme herself in September 2025 (DR-8104). That doesn't count as precedent: the contract making Acme eligible doesn't give her the authority to approve it.
