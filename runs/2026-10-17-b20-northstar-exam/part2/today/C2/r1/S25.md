## Answer

**DR-9001 can't be approved by Sarah Chen herself.** Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but it's above what she's allowed to approve under the 2026 policy. **Michael Torres (VP Sales) must approve it.**

1. **The request:** The CRM record matches what was submitted: DR-9001, account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the basis is a contract term and pricing exception.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". They share DUNS number 04-812-7730 and an address, and the guarantee letter says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Section 3.2 says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller", with the details in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B.
5. **Pricing exception:** Yes. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing, "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only", up to 15% of list price, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 from 2025-04-01, and David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) approved it. 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, which applies to requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and under the 2026 policy that role may approve discounts "up to and including 10%". 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager. Discounts over 10% also need recorded approval evidence.

**Worth knowing:** Last year Sarah approved her own similar request, DR-8104 (15%, 2025-09-16). That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that to 10%, so this year's request can't follow the same path.
