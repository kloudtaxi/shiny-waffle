## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** Northstar CRM shows DR-9001 at **15%**, not 18%. At 15% the discount can go ahead, but only after VP Sales Michael Torres approves it. Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.

**The figures don't match.** The CRM record for DR-9001 shows 15.0%, a $37,500 discount and a $212,500 net value. The figures you gave say 18%, $45,000 and $205,000. Per the procedure, I decided on the CRM value (15%) and added a blocking condition to sort out the difference. If Acme really wants 18%, the answer is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. 18% is above the 15% maximum in Acme's NS-500 pricing exception, and a more senior approver can't fix that. It would need new or amended contract terms.

**What I checked:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 is for account CRM-2048, product NS-500, list value $250,000. It was raised by Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive) on 2026-09-23 and is "Pending Approval". It cites contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. The contracting company is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. I found no record of it being terminated early.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing and the NS-500 only. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and allows up to 15% off list. David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests from 2025-04-01. It states that it sets commercial eligibility only and gives no Northstar employee approval authority.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Under that policy, Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%. Last year's DR-8104 at 15% was approved by Sarah herself. That was allowed under the 2025 policy, which had a 15% limit, but it doesn't carry into 2026.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, and anything above 10% needs approval evidence on record. The VP Sales is Michael Torres (michael.torres@northstar.example).
