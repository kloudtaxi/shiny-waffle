No, CR-9207 can't be approved on what the records show. The guarantee it relies on doesn't cover Acme Industrial Supply, so the outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**. And even with valid credit support, the requested limit is very likely above what this customer can have.

**1. The request isn't in the system of record.** The knowledge base has no record of CR-9207. The only Acme credit request on file is CR-9201, which is a different request for a different customer (C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, $250,000 to $400,000). I've judged CR-9207 as you gave it and made confirming that it exists a blocking condition.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1044 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** in Akron, OH (CRM account CRM-2091, DUNS 09-552-1187). Its account tier is **Standard**. It is not Acme Manufacturing (Acme Mfg. Holdings, C-1001 / CRM-2048, Milwaukee), which is a Strategic account.

**3. The guarantee doesn't cover this customer.** The Acme parent guarantee is given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It covers only its subsidiary "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing". It is capped at $400,000. A separate Northstar support agreement for Acme Mfg. Holdings says plainly that "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered." Nothing I found gives any credit support to C-1044. I also couldn't find the guarantee's dates.

**4. The policy in force** on 2026-09-23 is Northstar Credit Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It sets a maximum limit of **$250,000 for Standard accounts**. $500,000 is double that. And since the guarantee is capped at $400,000, it couldn't fully back $500,000 even for the company it does cover. If no valid support for C-1044 turns up, the request should be rejected or escalated, and a more senior approver can't fix that.

**5. Who would approve.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives that role no authority over credit limits. A $500,000 limit falls in the Finance Manager band (over $100,000, up to and including $500,000). VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed here. I couldn't find who holds the Finance Manager role.

**6. Not checked.** I couldn't find C-1044's invoices or the policy's payment rule, so its payment history is unassessed.
