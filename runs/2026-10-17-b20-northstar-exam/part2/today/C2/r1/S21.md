## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to a 15% discount on the NS-500, but under the 2027 pricing policy a discount above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. There's also a problem to fix first: I couldn't find request DR-9113 in the knowledge base.

**1. The request.** DR-9113: CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing), NS-500, 15% on a $250,000 list value ($37,500 off, net $212,500), raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example and dated 2027-02-01. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. The CRM records in the knowledge base show only two similar Acme requests: DR-8104 (2025-09-16, approved) and DR-9001 (2026-09-23, pending). Neither is DR-9113, so I've decided on the request as you gave it. Confirming it exists in the CRM is a blocking condition below.

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings": same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and same Milwaukee address. The supply agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing.

**3. The product.** The NS-500 Industrial Controller, SKU NS-500.

**4. Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it covers 2027-02-01. I found no record that it was terminated early. Section 3.2 says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller," as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B.

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 applies to the NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15% of list price. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaced the earlier 10% exception. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. So 15% is within Acme's eligibility on this date. The exception also says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."

**6. Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies, effective 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It fully replaces the 2026 policy for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01. Its approval bands:
- up to and including 5%: Enterprise Account Executives
- above 5% up to 12%: VP Sales
- above 12%: Chief Revenue Officer

Any discount above 5% needs recorded approval evidence.

**7. Sarah's authority.** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%. A 15% discount is beyond her authority.

**8. Required approver.** 15% falls in the "above 12%" band, so the Chief Revenue Officer must approve. That's David Morgan (EMP-300). Her manager, Michael Torres (VP Sales), can only approve up to 12%, so his approval isn't enough. The employee directory doesn't say whether it's current as of 2027-02-01. Since the request date is still in the future, someone should confirm Morgan still holds the role then.

**9. Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is eligible for 15%, but that doesn't give Sarah authority to approve it. David Morgan must approve, and his approval must be recorded. This can't go ahead until DR-9113 is confirmed in the CRM.
