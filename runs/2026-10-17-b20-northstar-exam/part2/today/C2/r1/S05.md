## Answer

I checked DR-9001 against the knowledge base: **it can't be approved yet, because the contract term it relies on can't be found.** The request needs the Acme Master Supply Agreement's pricing terms before anyone decides it.

**What checks out:**
- **The request:** the CRM record matches what you gave me: DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on contract terms, not standard pricing.
- **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing", the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings (ERP customer C-1001). It is a separate account from "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091).
- **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales approval, and anything above 10% needs recorded approval evidence.
- **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is above her limit. The VP Sales is Michael Torres, who is also her manager.

**Why it's stuck:**
- The knowledge base has the name "Acme Master Supply Agreement" but nothing else about it: no terms, no discount percentage, no dates. The only Acme contract records are ACME-MFG-2025, which only covers a support schedule, and GRT-ACME-2026, which is unrelated to pricing.
- There is no pricing exception for Acme on NS-500 in force on 2026-09-23.
- Sarah's own email only says "I think their agreement covers it."
- The 15% she got last September (DR-8104) doesn't count as evidence. A pricing exception only covers the product and dates it names, and that request fell under the 2025 policy. The 2026 policy also says contract pricing exceptions must be reviewed.

**Once the agreement is produced:** if it allows at least 15% on NS-500 on that date, the outcome becomes approval by Michael Torres, since 15% is beyond Sarah's authority. If it allows less than 15%, the request needs new or amended terms, and a more senior approver can't fix that.

**Also worth flagging:** DR-8104 (15% in September 2025) shows Sarah approved her own request. I couldn't check the 2025 policy's bands, so whether that was within her authority should be reviewed separately. It doesn't block this decision.
