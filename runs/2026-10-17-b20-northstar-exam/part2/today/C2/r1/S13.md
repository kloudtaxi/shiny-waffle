## Answer

No, it can't be approved as submitted. It needs commercial review (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`), and I couldn't find the request in the CRM.

**1. Request DR-9105 isn't in the CRM data.** The discount-request records I could find hold DR-8104 (approved 2025-09-16) and DR-9001 (pending, dated 2026-09-23). Both are for CRM-2048 Acme Manufacturing, not CRM-2091. DR-9001 has the same figures as your submission: NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, Sarah Chen, same reason given. I decided on DR-9105 as you gave it, not on DR-9001. Someone needs to confirm whether DR-9105 really exists for CRM-2091, or whether it's a mistyped account or a duplicate of DR-9001.

**2. The customer has no special terms.** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is ERP customer C-1044 in Akron, OH, on the Standard tier, with Zachary Brown as account owner. The Acme Master Supply Agreement (2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, Milwaukee). The 15% NS-500 pricing exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31), also names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. The records say outright: "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered." Special terms don't carry over to a different company with a similar name, so the request's stated reason ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement") doesn't apply to this customer.

**3. Approval authority (for reference).** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers requests dated 2026-09-23; the 2027 policy only takes over from 2027-01-01. Under the 2026 policy:
- An Enterprise Account Executive like Sarah Chen can approve up to 10%, so 15% is beyond her authority.
- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres (EMP-200).
- One other document says Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 15%. It doesn't appear to be the 2026 policy, so I didn't rely on it.

Even so, a higher approver can't fix a missing basis. This needs commercial review first, either at standard pricing or with new terms for this customer.
