I checked the 2026 credit policy, Acme's ERP, CRM and invoice records, and the employee directory. **CR-9208 can't be approved as it stands.** The customer qualifies and Elena Novak and David Morgan would need to sign off, but the request itself isn't in the ERP.

The only open credit request for customer C-1001 is **CR-9201**: raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, $250,000 → **$400,000**, with the same justification word for word, status Pending Approval. I haven't decided on CR-9201 in its place. The decision below is on CR-9208 as you gave it, and it can't be acted on until someone confirms CR-9208 exists in the ERP, or explains how it relates to CR-9201.

**Who the customer is:** ERP customer C-1001 (current limit $250,000) is Acme Manufacturing in the CRM (CRM-2048, Strategic tier). Legally that's Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing.

**Policy in force:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**Can Acme have $650,000? Yes.**
- **Payment history:** the rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Two invoices fall in that window, and both were paid early:
  - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
  - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
  - INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
- **Maximum:** the cap for a Strategic account is $750,000, so $650,000 is within it. The request relies on Acme's own standing. The Acme Group Holdings guarantee (2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) isn't needed. I also didn't confirm that it names this company.

**Who must approve:**
- Michael Torres is VP Sales. The policy gives that role no authority to approve credit limits, so he can't approve this alone.
- A limit above $500,000 needs **Director of Finance** approval. That's **Elena Novak**.
- A Strategic account above $500,000 also needs **VP Sales concurrence**. Torres submitted the request and the policy bars anyone from approving or concurring on their own request, so the concurrence passes to his manager, **David Morgan** (Chief Revenue Officer).
