> Could Michael Torres's request to raise BlueRiver Logistics' credit limit from $100,000 to $200,000 have been approved on 2025-09-23 (CR-9206)? ## Answer

I couldn't find CR-9206 anywhere in the system of record, so it can't be acted on yet. Taking the request exactly as you gave it, the answer is yes, but not by Michael Torres alone. The Finance Manager, Priya Shah, would have had to approve it.

**Request and customer.** The request is for C‑1002, which is BlueRiver Logistics. The ERP records it as Blue River Logistics LLC and the CRM as account CRM‑2051, both with DUNS 07‑331‑9025. The ERP shows the current limit as $100,000, which matches the request. The justification ("Fleet expansion") is about the customer's own business and mentions no guarantee, so the request relies on BlueRiver's own standing. The ERP's credit-request table has CR‑9201 and CR‑7001 to CR‑7010, but no CR‑9206.

**Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2025 applied on 2025‑09‑23 (it runs from 2025‑01‑01 to 2025‑12‑31).

**Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 45 days after its due date. Only one BlueRiver invoice fell due in that window: INV‑61433, due 2025‑08‑29 and paid 2025‑09‑02, 4 days late. That passes the rule. However, the earliest BlueRiver invoice on record is due 2025‑08‑29, so most of the 12-month window has no records to check.

**Maximum limit.** BlueRiver is a Standard account in the CRM, and the maximum for Standard accounts is $300,000. The requested $200,000 is within that, so BlueRiver is eligible.

**Michael Torres's authority.** He is VP Sales. The 2025 policy gives credit-limit authority only to Accounts Receivable Specialists (up to $50,000), the Finance Manager (over $50,000 up to $750,000) and the Director of Finance (over $750,000). VP Sales has none.

**Required approver.** A $200,000 limit falls in the Finance Manager's band. That is Priya Shah. She isn't the requestor, so the rule that nobody may approve their own request doesn't affect her. The 2025 policy requires no concurrence. (VP Sales concurrence is a 2026 rule, and only for Strategic accounts over $500,000.)

**Decision: approve with authorization.**
