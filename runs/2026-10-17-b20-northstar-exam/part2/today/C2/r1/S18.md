> Could Sarah Chen have approved an 8% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? ## Answer

**Yes, Sarah Chen could have approved this herself, with two caveats.** The 8% is within her own authority and within Acme's terms for that date. But the contract the request cites wasn't in force yet, and I can't find DR-9110 in the CRM records.

1. **Request:** DR-9110 is not in the knowledge base. The CRM records I can see for Acme (CRM-2048) are DR-8104 and DR-9001, both for 15%. I've decided on the request as you gave it, and confirming it exists in the CRM is a blocking condition.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (Strategic tier). Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000. That matches the request's $20,000 discount and $230,000 net.
4. **Contract:** The request cites the "Acme Master Supply Agreement." That agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it was not in force on 2025-03-31. The agreement in force was ACME-SA-2023 (status: Active).
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-10 under ACME-SA-2023 says Acme "may receive up to **10%** off list price" on the NS-500 "for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." It covers this product, and the request date is its last day. 8% is under the 10% cap, so Acme is eligible. The later 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) only applies to requests dated on or after 2025-04-01, so it doesn't apply here.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force ("effective from 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31").
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under the 2025 policy, "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%." 8% is within that.
8. **Required approver:** The requestor (Sarah Chen) is enough. VP Sales approval only applies to discounts above 15%.

**Decision: APPROVE**, with two blocking conditions:
- Someone needs to confirm DR-9110 exists in the CRM.
- The stated basis needs correcting from the Master Supply Agreement to EXC-ACME-NS500-10 under ACME-SA-2023.

The order also has to be placed on 2025-03-31, the exception's last day, because the exception covers orders placed in its window.
