## Answer

**No, it can't be approved.** The request is for $1,300,000. Acme's maximum on 2026-09-23 was $1,150,000 even with the parent guarantee, so the decision is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. Getting a more senior approver doesn't change this.

**A problem with the request itself:** CR-9204 isn't in the knowledge base. The only request recorded for customer C-1001 is CR-9201: also from Sarah Chen and dated 2026-09-23, but for $400,000 (from $250,000), with "Third-plant ramp-up" as the reason, not the guarantee. As instructed, I decided on CR-9204 as you gave it and did not substitute CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9204 exists is a blocking condition.

1. **Customer:** ERP customer C-1001 is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, same DUNS number 04-812-7730). Its tier in the CRM is **Strategic**.
2. **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.
3. **Payment history is clean.** The rule is that no invoice due in the previous 12 months may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
   - INV-60412 ($85,000) was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 ($480,000) was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) is still unpaid, but it wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
4. **Maximum limit:**
   - Strategic accounts have a maximum of $750,000.
   - Under the policy, a parent guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, for the customer it names.
   - Acme Group Holdings, Inc.'s guarantee names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and caps the parent's liability at $400,000.
   - That gives $750,000 + $400,000 = **$1,150,000**, which is $150,000 below the $1,300,000 requested.
5. **Approval:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive with no credit approval authority under the policy.
   - Above $500,000, the Director of Finance must approve. For Strategic accounts, the VP Sales (Michael Torres) must also agree.
   - Michael Torres is Sarah's manager but not the requestor, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't stop him.
   - I couldn't find who the Director of Finance is in the knowledge base. The outcome doesn't depend on this.
