The basis is a contract exception: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, a Standard-tier account in the Central segment, owned by Kelly Floyd. The ERP record appears under the name "Blue River Logistics LLC". Neither record is linked to any contract or pricing exception.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The submitted figures match: 12% off is $30,000, leaving $220,000.
4. **Active contract.** The Acme Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings (doing business as Acme Manufacturing), not BlueRiver. I found no BlueRiver contract.
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% on NS-500, but only for Acme Manufacturing. Nothing covers BlueRiver for this product or date. Special terms don't carry over to another customer, so BlueRiver is on standard pricing and the cited basis doesn't apply.
6. **Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers calendar 2026. The 2027 policy only takes over for requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Authority.** Under the 2026 policy, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Torres is VP Sales, so 12% is within his own limit.
8. **Required approver.** VP Sales, which is Torres.
9. **Decision.** His authority doesn't make BlueRiver eligible for Acme's contract pricing. The request needs commercial review: either resubmit it on a correct basis, such as standard or competitive pricing with a real reason, or get terms that actually cover BlueRiver. Approvals above 10% must also be recorded in Northstar CRM.
