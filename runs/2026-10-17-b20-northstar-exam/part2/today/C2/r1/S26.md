## Answer

**Yes, CR-9201 can be approved, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. The new $400,000 limit needs the Finance Manager's approval.** The knowledge base doesn't say who the Finance Manager is, so that person has to be identified before the request can be sent to them.

1. **The request.** The ERP record matches what you sent: C-1001, $250,000 to $400,000, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It rests on Acme's own standing. In her email Sarah mentions Acme Group's parent guarantee only "if they need more headroom later", so this request doesn't depend on it.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001, CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (Strategic tier, account owner Sarah Chen) and the legal name "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing" are the same company. The ERP customer master shows the current limit as $250,000, which matches the request.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the previous policy.
4. **Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. The record is clean:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, on time.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, on time.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) falls due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
5. **The maximum.** The policy caps Strategic accounts at $750,000, so $400,000 is within it. No guarantee is needed. The guarantee would cover Acme Mfg. Holdings for 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, up to $400,000.
6. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. The policy gives her no credit approval authority, and no one may approve a request they submitted.
7. **Approvers.** Limits over $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.

**Worth knowing (doesn't block the decision):** Sales order SO-7002 was booked for $720,000 and is still unpaid as INV-62305. That is above both the current limit and the requested one. As of today (2026-10-17) it is 15 days past due. If it isn't paid by 2026-11-01, Acme will fail the payment rule for any later request.
