## Answer

Yes, but Sarah can't approve it herself. Acme is entitled to the 15%, but under the 2026 pricing policy she can approve at most 10%. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it.

**What I checked**
- **The request:** In CRM, DR-9001 matches what was submitted: account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off a list value of $250,000, a $37,500 discount and a net of $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special contract terms.
  - **The status doesn't match.** CRM shows the request as **"Pending Approval"**, with no approver and no approval date. The submission says "Approved". I decided on the CRM record.
- **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the Master Supply Agreement that's the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. Sarah owns the account.
- **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028, so it was in force on the request date. Nothing in the knowledge base shows it was ended early.
- **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows Acme up to 15% off list on the NS-500 only. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaced the earlier 10% exception. David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20. It covers this product and date, and 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- **The policy:** The 2026 pricing policy applies. It replaced the 2025 policy for requests dated from 2026-01-01, and the 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
- **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and under the 2026 policy that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
- **Who must approve:** The 2026 policy gives discounts over 10% and up to 20% to the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager.

**Worth knowing**
- Sarah approved her own 15% discount for Acme last year (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That was allowed under the 2025 policy, which let her approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy no longer does, so last year's approval is no reason to do it again.
- The policy says approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM. The request is still "Pending Approval" there, so treat the "Approved" status in the submission as wrong.
