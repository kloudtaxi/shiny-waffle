> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? ## Answer

I checked the records. **No, Sarah Chen could not have approved this.** The 15% was within her own approval limit, but Acme wasn't entitled to 15% on 2025-03-31. The agreement the request cites didn't start until the next day.

**The request.** I couldn't find DR-9101 in the CRM records. The only Acme requests for 15% off NS-500 there are DR-8104 (dated 2025-09-16, approved) and DR-9001 (dated 2026-09-23, pending). They carry the same amounts and the same reason. I decided on DR-9101 as you gave it and didn't swap in either of those. Someone needs to confirm DR-9101 actually exists in the CRM before anything is done with it.

- **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing, CRM-2048. Its legal name is Acme Mfg. Holdings, and Sarah Chen owns the account.
- **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Basis:** special contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".

**What terms applied on 2025-03-31**
- **The Master Supply Agreement didn't apply yet.** It runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it wasn't in force on the request date.
- **The 15% exception didn't apply yet either.** That's EXC-ACME-NS500-15: NS-500 only, up to 15% off list, starting 2025-04-01. It replaces the 10% exception only for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. It also says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- **The 10% terms did apply.** Agreement ACME-SA-2023 and exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10 say Acme "may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." The request date is the last day of that period.
- **So Acme's limit that day was 10%.** The 15% requested goes past it.

**Sarah's authority.** The 2025 Pricing Policy covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. It says "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%," and anything over 15% needs VP Sales approval. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so she was within her limit. But being within her limit doesn't make Acme eligible for more than its terms allowed.

**Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Getting a more senior approver wouldn't help. The two options are:
- resubmit at 10% or less under ACME-SA-2023, which Sarah could approve herself; or
- get new or amended terms that cover a 15% discount dated 2025-03-31.

It's possible the order date was really 2025-04-01 or later. In that case the 15% terms would apply, but the request would have to be corrected in the CRM first.
