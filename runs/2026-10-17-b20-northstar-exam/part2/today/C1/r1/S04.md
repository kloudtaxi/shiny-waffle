## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it. Acme is entitled to the 15% under its contract, but that doesn't change who has authority to approve it.

**The date doesn't match the CRM.** Your copy of the request says 2025-09-23. The CRM file `discount_requests.csv` records DR-9001 on **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email about DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23 and says "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." So I've decided on 2026-09-23. The record dated in September 2025 is a different request, DR-8104 (2025-09-16, 15%, approved by Sarah herself). That approval was within her authority under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. It doesn't carry over to DR-9001.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 is for account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). It was requested by Sarah Chen, is still "Pending Approval", and relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as the ERP's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active. Clause 3.2 allows up to 15% on NS-500. Clause 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15%, and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception and says it "does not grant approval authority." So 15% is exactly at the limit Acme is entitled to.
6. **Policy in force:** Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Required approver:** For discounts over 10% and up to 20%, the Approval Authority Matrix requires the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres. The approval has to be recorded in the CRM.
