The terms put Blue River on the Silver plan. They cover NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD) from 2025-01-01, at a fee of $2,500 a month. The ticket is in scope.

**2. True severity: 2.** The customer reported it as P2, but that doesn't set severity. Alerts and dashboards stopped at 2 of 6 depots, and dispatchers kept working by phoning drivers. - Initial response: 2 business hours.

**5. Measurement.**
- **Response: missed.** The automatic acknowledgment at 19:46 doesn't count. The engineer's first comment came on 08-21 at 10:20, which is 2 h 20 min of business time against a 2 h target.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** Restoration was recorded Monday 08-24 at 10:40. That is 10 h on Friday plus 2 h 40 min on Monday, or 12 h 40 min against a 12 h target. Band 1 runs up to twice the target (24 h).

**6. Credit: $200.00.**
