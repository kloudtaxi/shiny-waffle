> Ticket SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data lost for six presses, reported P1): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39644: no service level was breached (NO_BREACH).** The true severity is Severity 2, both targets were met, no credit is owed, and no escalation or notification was triggered.

**1. The customer.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2048. That account carries DUNS 04-812-7730 and maps to ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, Milwaukee. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).

**2. Scope: in.** Acme's support terms (Schedule C) put it on the Platinum plan and name NS-Edge as a covered product. Coverage started 2025-04-01. The ticket was opened on 2026-07-23, well after that.

**3. True severity: Severity 2, not P1.** The ticket records that NS-Edge gateways stopped sending vibration data for presses 1–6 at Plant 2. The presses kept running, and technicians did manual checks every two hours. Production was not stopped, so it isn't Severity 1. The severity guide counts a lost condition-monitoring function as Severity 2 "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks." The customer's P1 has no effect on targets.

**4. Schedule version and targets.** Service Level Schedule v2.0 applies, because the ticket opened on or after 2026-04-01 Central Time. For Severity 2 on Platinum, the targets are a 2-hour response and a 12-hour restoration, on a clock that runs around the clock. Holidays and business hours don't matter here.

**5. Clock start, pause and exclusions** (all times Central Daylight Time, UTC−05:00)
- **Clock start:** 2026-07-23 13:10, when the portal submission was received.
- **Pause:** the restoration clock paused from 2026-07-23 15:00, when the engineer asked for VPN credentials and set the ticket to Awaiting Customer. It resumed at 2026-07-24 08:30, when the customer provided them. Both ends are recorded, so 17 h 30 min is removed.
- **Exclusions:** none. The only maintenance notice on record (MN-2607) is for another customer and product. No customer-caused exclusion was asserted.

**6. Measurement**
- **Response:** the engineer's comment at 15:00 asked for access, which counts as a first response. That is 1 h 50 min against a 2-hour target, so it was **met**.
- **Restoration:** restored 2026-07-24 18:40. Total elapsed time was 29 h 30 min; minus the 17 h 30 min pause, that leaves exactly 12 h 00 min. Under schedule section 2.4, an event exactly when the target runs out counts as met, so restoration was **met with no margin**. If the pause had been one minute shorter, it would have been a breach.

**7. Credits.** None are owed, because no target was missed. No claim is on record, and none would succeed.

**8. Obligations**
- **Northstar:** nothing is owed.
  - The response-escalation trigger (notify the Support Escalation Manager) never fired, because the response came before the 15:10 deadline.
  - The restoration trigger (notify the Director of Customer Support and the account owner, Sarah Chen) would have fired at 2026-07-24 18:40, but the ticket was restored at that exact moment.
  - With no claim, there's no claim decision or credit memo. With no customer-caused exclusion asserted, no root-cause finding is due.
- **Customer:**
  - It was not required to report by phone, since that rule applies only to Severity 1. It reported through the portal, which is fine for Severity 2.
  - It did have to provide the access Northstar needed, and it met that: Kelly Ballard (Acme IT Architecture) supplied the VPN credentials, recorded 2026-07-24 08:30.
  - It has no claim to file. If it does file one, the window runs to 2026-08-23 23:59 CT, and the Director of Customer Support must decline it in writing within 30 days of receipt.
