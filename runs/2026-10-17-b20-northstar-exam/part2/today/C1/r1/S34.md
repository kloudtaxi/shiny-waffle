## Answer

**No. CR-9208 can't be approved on Michael Torres's authority, and it can't be acted on yet because the request isn't in the system of record.** Acme qualifies for $650,000, but that limit needs approval from the Director of Finance plus a VP Sales sign-off ("concurrence") that has to come from someone other than Michael.

**The request isn't on file.** The credit-request records in Northstar ERP have no CR-9208. The only open request for this customer (C-1001) is **CR-9201**. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 to raise the limit from $250,000 to $400,000, for the same third-plant reason. I decided on CR-9208 as you gave it, not on CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9208 exists is a blocking condition, along with sorting out how it relates to CR-9201.

**How I reached the decision:**
1. **Customer:** ERP customer C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings") is the same company as CRM account CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing"). Both records share the tax ID 04-812-7730 and the Milwaukee address. The CRM tier is **Strategic**. The ERP shows a current limit of $250,000, which matches the request.
2. **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect for 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
3. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) falls due on 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
4. **Maximum limit:** The maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000, so $650,000 is within it. The request relies on Acme's own standing. Sarah's email mentions the Acme Group parent guarantee as a source of "more headroom later", but this request doesn't need it.
5. **Michael's authority:** The credit policy gives approval authority only to Accounts Receivable Specialists, the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance. As VP Sales, Michael has none.
6. **Required approvers:**
   - Any limit over $500,000 needs **Director of Finance** approval: **Elena Novak**.
   - A Strategic account over $500,000 also needs **VP Sales concurrence**. Michael is the VP Sales but also the requestor, and nobody may concur on their own request. The concurrence passes to his manager, **David Morgan**, Chief Revenue Officer.
