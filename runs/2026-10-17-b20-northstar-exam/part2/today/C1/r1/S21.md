## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% under its contract, but on 2027-02-01 her own limit is 5%. Under the 2027 Pricing Policy, anything above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. One thing must be fixed first: **DR-9113 isn't in the CRM request log**, so it has to be confirmed before anyone acts on this.

**How I got there**

1. **The request isn't on record.** The CRM log (discount_requests.csv) has no DR-9113. It does have a pending request that looks like the same deal: **DR-9001**. It's for the same account, product, 15%, $250,000 list value and requestor, with the same justification, but it's dated 2026-09-23 and still "Pending Approval." I decided on DR-9113 as you gave it, not on DR-9001. Confirming DR-9113 exists, and that it doesn't duplicate DR-9001, blocks action. The request relies on contract pricing, so its basis is the customer's contract and pricing exception.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (Strategic account, owner Sarah Chen). In the ERP it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), a different customer.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The submitted figures match: 15% is $37,500 off, leaving $212,500.
4. **The contract is active.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers NS-500. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **A pricing exception covers this product.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) allows up to 15% on NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the old 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which expired 2025-03-31). The request is at the 15% maximum, not over it, so eligibility is established. The exception itself says it gives no employee approval authority.
6. **The policy.** Pricing Policy 2027 applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01. Its approval levels: up to 5%, the Account Executive; above 5% up to 12%, the VP Sales; above 12%, the CRO. The 2026 matrix, where an Account Executive could approve up to 10% and the VP Sales up to 20%, no longer applies. Under it Michael Torres could have signed off; under the 2027 policy he can't.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%, well below 15%. Last September's DR-8104, which she approved herself at 15% under the 2025 rules, isn't a precedent under the 2027 policy.
8. **The approver.** The CRO, held by David Morgan according to the employee list and the org chart as of 2026-09-01. Since 2027-02-01 is still in the future, whoever holds the role on that date should be confirmed. The policy also requires a recorded approval for anything above 5%.
