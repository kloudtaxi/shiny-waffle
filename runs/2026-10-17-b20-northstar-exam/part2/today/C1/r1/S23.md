## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme qualifies for the 15%, but at that level the 2026 policy requires the VP Sales, Michael Torres, to approve it.

**The submitted date is wrong.** The CRM record for DR-9001 is dated **2026-09-23**, but the request you sent says **2025-09-23**. Sarah's email to Michael logging DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23. The difference changes the answer. Under the 2025 policy, an Enterprise Account Executive could approve up to 15%, so the request as sent would look self-approvable. I decided on the CRM date. Everything else matches CRM: CRM-2048, NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, Pending Approval.

1. **The request:** Sarah Chen asks for 15% off NS-500 for CRM-2048, citing "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". The basis is a contract term and pricing exception.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account. The billing name in the finance system (ERP) is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091) is a different company.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The amounts in the request are correct.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement, ACME-MFG-2025, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and grants no approval authority. The 15% request is within this limit.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less, so 15% is above her authority.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres according to the org chart (as of 2026-09-01) and the employee records. The policy also says approvals above 10% must be recorded in the CRM.

Last year's 15% request, DR-8104 (2025-09-16), was approved by Sarah herself. That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy permitted it. It doesn't carry over to 2026.
