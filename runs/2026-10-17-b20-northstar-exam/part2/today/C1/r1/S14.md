## Answer

Yes. Sarah Chen can approve this 8% discount herself, because it is within her own limit. But DR-9106 isn't in the CRM discount-request export I have, so that has to be confirmed before anyone acts on it.

1. **The request.** DR-9106 asks for 8% off the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (NS-EDGE) for account CRM-2048. On $85,000 list that is $6,800 off, leaving $78,200. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the justification "Competitive", and it is pending approval. The justification means it relies on standard pricing, not on contract terms. **The export (discount_requests.csv) has no DR-9106 row.** Its last rows are DR-7024 and DR-9001; DR-9001 is Sarah's separate 15% NS-500 request, which relies on the contract. I decided on DR-9106 as you gave it and made confirming it a blocking condition.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), a different account in Akron.
3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package. The $85,000 list value matches earlier NS-EDGE requests such as DR-7002.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It includes NS-EDGE as a product, but its only special pricing is up to 15% on the NS-500 (§3.2). Section 3.3 says the contract doesn't change internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only and says it applies to no other product. Nothing covers NS-EDGE, but this request doesn't need an exception because it uses standard pricing.
6. **Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix were in force on 2026-09-23. The 2027 policy, which cuts the account executive limit to 5%, only applies from 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101 and the September 2026 org chart). That role can approve discounts up to and including 10%, and 8% is under that.
8. **Approver.** The 10%-and-under band belongs to Enterprise Account Executives, so no VP Sales or CRO approval is needed.
