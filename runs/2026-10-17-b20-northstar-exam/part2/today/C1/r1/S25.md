## Answer

Yes, but not on Sarah's own authority. Acme qualifies for the 15% discount on NS-500, but under the 2026 policy Sarah can only approve up to 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, must approve it.

**What I checked**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 matches the CRM record exactly: Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), NS-500, 15.0%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval." The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on contract terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), trading as Acme Manufacturing. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a separate account.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The list value on the request matches.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-500 is a listed product, and §3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500. I found no termination or amendment.
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, at up to 15% of list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The CRO, David Morgan, approved it. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which expired on 2025-03-31. At 15%, the request is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **The policy:** Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply, both in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy (5% / 12% bands) doesn't apply because it only takes effect on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, which carries authority up to and including 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **The required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres (EMP-200; employee records and org chart as of 2026-09-01). The policy also requires the approval to be recorded in CRM.

**Why it isn't a straight approval:** Both the agreement (§3.3) and the exception say that contract eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority. The procedure also says a request can't be approved just because the contract allows the discount. Last September's DR-8104 (15%, approved by Sarah herself) doesn't change that; the 2026 policy governs this request.
