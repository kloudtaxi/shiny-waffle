## Answer

**No, CR-9207 can't be approved. The outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Acme Industrial Supply Co. can have at most $250,000, and the request is for $500,000. The parent guarantee cited in the request doesn't cover this company.

**The request.** I couldn't find CR-9207 in the knowledge base. The ERP credit-request file (credit_requests.csv) lists Sarah Chen's CR-9201, dated the same day, but that is for a different customer: Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), raising $250,000 to $400,000. So I decided on CR-9207 as you gave it, and confirming that it exists in Northstar ERP is a blocking condition. The ERP credit file does show C-1044's current limit as $50,000, which matches the request.

**The customer.** C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH (DUNS 09-552-1187; CRM account CRM-2091; **Standard** tier in CRM). It is a different company from Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing in Milwaukee (C-1001, CRM-2048, Strategic tier).

**Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated in 2026.

**Payment history.** The only invoices for C-1044 are INV-30001 (due 2025-02-25) and INV-30003 (due 2025-05-07). Neither was due in the 12 months before 2026-09-23 (from 2025-09-23 on), so there are no late invoices to count.

**Maximum limit.** The 2026 policy caps Standard accounts at $250,000. A parent guarantee raises the maximum only "for the customer the guarantee names." Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc., for $400,000, valid 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It names **Acme Mfg. Holdings** (Acme Manufacturing) and states "No other company is covered." Acme's support agreement (Schedule C) also says "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered." The guarantee isn't missing; the documents show it doesn't apply here, so asking for evidence isn't the right outcome. The maximum stays at $250,000, and $500,000 is over it.

**Authority.** This doesn't change the outcome, because a higher approver can't fix an ineligible request:
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she has no authority to approve credit limits.
- A $500,000 limit falls in the "over $100,000 up to $500,000" band, which needs Finance Manager approval. That is Priya Shah.
- No VP Sales concurrence is needed, because the account is Standard and the limit isn't above $500,000.

**What could work instead.** A request for up to $250,000 could go to Priya Shah for approval. A higher limit would need credit support that names Acme Industrial Supply Co. itself.
