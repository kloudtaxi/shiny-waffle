## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen had the authority to approve 15% on 2025-03-31, but Acme wasn't eligible for 15% on that date. The request is also not in the system of record.

**1. The request.** DR-9101 is not in Northstar CRM's discount request records (discount_requests.csv). The only Acme NS-500 requests at 15% are DR-8104 (dated 2025-09-16, already approved) and DR-9001 (dated 2026-09-23, still pending). I haven't substituted either one. I've decided on DR-9101 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists. Its basis is a contract term: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".

**2–3. Customer and product.** The customer is Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. The product is NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4–5. Contract and exception on 2025-03-31:**
- The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) only starts on 2025-04-01. It wasn't in force on the request date, so the term the request relies on doesn't apply.
- The 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also only starts on 2025-04-01. It replaces the 10% exception only for requests dated on or after that day.
- What applied on 2025-03-31 was the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10 under agreement ACME-SA-2023. It allows "up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500… for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." So on that date Acme could get at most **10%**, and 15% is over that limit.

**6–8. Authority.** The 2025 Pricing Policy was in force (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve "up to and including 15%" and requires VP Sales approval above that. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% was within her own authority. (The 2026 approval matrix, which caps her at 10%, doesn't apply to 2025.) But having the authority doesn't create eligibility: under the SOP, a request can't be approved just because someone could approve it. A more senior approver can't fix this either.

**9. Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** 15% is more than the 10% Acme was entitled to on 2025-03-31. Approving it would need new or amended terms, or a request dated 2025-04-01 or later, when the 15% exception applies.
