## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this, and the request can't go forward on the basis it gives. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** There is also a second problem: DR-9109 isn't in the CRM discount request records I can access.

**1. The request isn't on record.** The discount request export (discount_requests.csv) has no DR-9109. Acme's only pending request is DR-9001: NS-500, 15%, dated 2026-09-23, also by Sarah. Her email to Michael Torres that day talks about DR-9001 for the NS-500 and doesn't mention an NS-Edge request. I haven't treated DR-9001 as a substitute. The decision below is on DR-9109 as you gave it, and someone needs to confirm that DR-9109 actually exists.

**2. What the request relies on.** Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it rests on special contract terms, not standard pricing.

**3. Customer and product.**
- CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. It is the party to agreement ACME-MFG-2025 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing"). It is not CRM-2091, Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a separate account.
- NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. The submitted figures match: 12% is $10,200 off, for a net of $74,800.

**4. Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is active on 2026-09-23 (it runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31), and it lists NS-EDGE as a product supplied under it. However, its only special pricing term (§3.2) is "up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller." Being a product under the agreement doesn't make NS-EDGE eligible for that discount. Section 3.3 adds that contractual eligibility doesn't change internal approval authority.

**5. Pricing exception.** The current exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, applies to "NS-500 … only" and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, also covered only the NS-500 and expired on 2025-03-31. Nothing covers NS-EDGE, and special terms don't carry over from one product to another.

**6–8. Policy and authority.**
- Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply on the request date. Pricing Policy 2027 doesn't take effect until 2027-01-01.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% or less. 12% is over her limit.
- Discounts above 10% and up to 20% go to the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres.

**Decision.** Because the request relies on contract terms that don't cover this product, it needs commercial review. That review should either assess 12% on NS-EDGE as a standard, non-contract discount or put new terms in place. Even if it's cleared as a standard discount, 12% is over Sarah's limit and would need Michael Torres's approval.
