## Answer

**Answer: Acme qualifies for a $1,000,000 limit on 2026-09-23, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. It needs approval from Elena Novak (Director of Finance) and agreement ("concurrence") from Michael Torres (VP Sales). There's also a blocker: CR-9203 isn't in Northstar ERP, so nothing can be acted on until someone confirms it exists.**

**1. The request.** CR-9203 asks to raise Acme's limit from $250,000 to $1,000,000. It was submitted by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23 and relies on the parent guarantee. ERP's credit-request list has no CR-9203. The only pending Acme request is **CR-9201**, also from Sarah on the same day, for $250,000 → **$400,000**. Her email to Priya Shah describes that request too. I haven't treated CR-9201 as this request; the decision below is on CR-9203 as you gave it.

**2. The customer.** These are all the same company: ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing", same DUNS (04-812-7730) and the same Milwaukee address. CRM records the account as **Strategic**. ERP's current limit is $250,000, which matches the request.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**4. Payment history.** No invoice may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme has no problems:
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305: not due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.

**5. Maximum limit: $1,150,000.**
- The Strategic-account maximum is $750,000.
- The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) adds $400,000. It's given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. and names Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing as the only company covered. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it was in force on the request date.
- $1,000,000 is within the maximum, but only because of the guarantee. Without it the cap would be $750,000.

**6. Sarah's authority.** As an Enterprise Account Executive she has no credit-approval authority under the policy.

**7. Who must approve.** Limits over $500,000 need the Director of Finance, Elena Novak. Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence, Michael Torres. Neither of them submitted the request, so the rule that you can't approve your own request doesn't change anything.
