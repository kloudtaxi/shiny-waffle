## Answer

Acme qualifies for the higher limit, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. The outcome is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**: Priya Shah, the Finance Manager, must approve it. The amount also has to be sorted out first. You submitted $300,000, but the ERP request log (the system of record) has CR-9201 at **$400,000**, and Sarah's email to Priya also says $400,000. I decided on $400,000. The result would be the same at $300,000.

1. **The request.** CR-9201 is for ERP customer C-1001, raising the limit from $250,000 to $400,000 per the ERP. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 and it is "Pending Approval". It rests on Acme's own standing. Her email mentions Acme Group's parent guarantee only in case they need more room later, so the request doesn't depend on it.
2. **The customer.** These records are all the same company. The ERP name is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). In the CRM it is "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048), recorded as a **Strategic** account. Both records share the same address and registration number, and Sarah's account plan links them. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091) in Akron is a different company.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 is unpaid but wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
5. **The maximum.** Strategic accounts can go up to $750,000, so $400,000 is well within it without the guarantee. The guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) does name Acme Mfg. Holdings, for up to $400,000, from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It isn't needed here.
6. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. The 2026 policy gives that role no credit approval authority.
7. **Who must approve.** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval, which is Priya Shah. No concurrence is needed because the limit is below $500,000. Priya didn't submit the request, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't apply.
