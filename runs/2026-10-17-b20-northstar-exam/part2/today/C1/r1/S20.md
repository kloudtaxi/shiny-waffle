## Answer

No, Michael Torres can't approve this. Acme's agreement caps the NS-500 discount at 15%, so an 18% request goes beyond what Acme is entitled to. His seniority doesn't change that.

**There's also a records problem.** DR-9112 isn't in the Northstar CRM discount-request records. On 2026-09-23 the only Acme NS-500 request is **DR-9001**: 15%, $37,500 off a $250,000 list, net $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it and it's still Pending Approval. Her email to Michael that day also asks for 15% and cites DR-9001. I've decided DR-9112 as you gave it, not DR-9001. Confirming that DR-9112 actually exists in the CRM is a blocking condition.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9112, 18% off NS-500 ($45,000 off $250,000), dated 2026-09-23 and requested by Michael Torres. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special contract terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. The ERP bill-to name is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). Same customer under two names.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Clause 3.2 limits Acme to discounts of **up to 15%** on NS-500. Clause 3.3 says this doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a **maximum of 15%** off list. 18% is 3 points over that.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its approval matrix (APP-MATRIX-2026) apply to the request date. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Authority:** Michael is VP Sales. That role can approve discounts above 10% up to 20%, so he could normally sign off on 18%.
8. **Why that doesn't help:** Neither the agreement nor the SOP lets approval authority stretch a customer's contractual maximum. The discount sits within his authority but above what Acme is eligible for. Getting to 18% would need new or amended terms with Acme. A more senior approver can't fix it either.

He also appears as both requestor and approver here. That doesn't matter for a rejection, but it's worth flagging.
