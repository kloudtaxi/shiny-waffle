## Answer

**No, not at 18%. Acme Manufacturing's contract allows at most 15% on the NS-500.** The system of record also doesn't show an 18% request. The CRM's discount request log has DR-9001 at **15%**: $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, for a net of $212,500. The record you gave says 18%, $45,000 off and $205,000 net. Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres the same day also says Acme asked for "15% off list." Following the procedure, I decided on the logged 15%. On that figure the outcome is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**: Michael Torres, VP Sales, must approve.

1. **Request:** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, submitted by Sarah Chen. It relies on Acme's contract ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement") and is Pending Approval.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different account from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers the NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It allows up to **15%** and replaces the older 10% exception. 15% is within it; 18% would not be. On its own the 18% would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE: a more senior approver can't fix it, and it would need new or amended contract terms.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, with lower limits, only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and can approve up to 10%. Both the contract (section 3.3) and the exception say that what the customer is eligible for doesn't change who can approve it.
8. **Approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres according to the org chart dated 2026-09-01. The approval must be recorded in the CRM.

Before anyone acts on this, the 18% and the logged 15% need to be reconciled. I've made that a blocking condition. Sarah's own account plan said she only "believed" the rate was 15%, but the Schedule B exception now confirms it.
