## Answer

**Answer: BlueRiver Logistics qualifies for a $200,000 limit, but Michael Torres could not approve it himself. Priya Shah, the Finance Manager, would have had to approve it. There is also a bigger problem: CR-9206 does not appear in the credit-request records the knowledge base holds from Northstar ERP. The request has to be confirmed there before anything is acted on.**

1. **The request.** I couldn't find CR-9206 in credit_requests.csv. The only open request there is CR-9201, which is for Acme, so I haven't used it in place of this one. I judged CR-9206 as you gave it: customer C-1002, $100,000 to $200,000, dated 2025-09-23, submitted by michael.torres@northstar.example. The reason given is fleet expansion, so it rests on BlueRiver's own standing and no guarantee is involved. The current limit in the ERP credit file is $100,000, which matches.
2. **The customer.** ERP customer C-1002, "Blue River Logistics LLC", and CRM account CRM-2051, "BlueRiver Logistics", are the same company. They share the address (890 Harbor Way, St. Louis) and the DUNS number (07-331-9025). CRM puts it in the **Standard** tier.
3. **Policy.** The Northstar Credit Policy 2025 covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31, so it applies.
4. **Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or stay unpaid, more than 45 days after its due date. Three BlueRiver invoices fall in that window:
   - INV-61005 was due 2025-03-01 and paid early on 2025-02-26.
   - INV-61210 was due 2025-05-30 and paid early on 2025-05-28.
   - INV-61433 was due 2025-08-29 and paid on 2025-09-02, 4 days late. That is within the 45 days allowed.

   None breaks the rule.
5. **Maximum limit.** The maximum for a Standard account is $300,000. $200,000 is under it, so BlueRiver is **eligible**.
6. **Michael's authority.** He is VP Sales. The 2025 policy gives credit approval authority only to Accounts Receivable Specialists (up to $50,000), the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance. Sales roles have no authority to approve credit limits.
7. **Who must approve.** A new total limit above $50,000 and up to $750,000 needs the Finance Manager, who is Priya Shah. The 2025 policy requires no concurrence. The rule against approving your own request doesn't affect her, because Michael submitted it.

**Outcome: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Priya Shah must approve, and the decision is on hold until the request is confirmed in Northstar ERP.
