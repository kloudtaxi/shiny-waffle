## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and under the 2026 Pricing Policy she can approve discounts only up to 10%. Any discount above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. There is also a problem that blocks acting on it: **I can't find DR-9102 in the CRM discount request records.**

1. **The request isn't in the system of record.** The discount request export (discount_requests.csv) has no DR-9102. The only Acme request Sarah logged on 2026-09-23 is **DR-9001**, a different deal: NS-500 at 15%, citing "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". Her email to Michael Torres that day also mentions only DR-9001. I've judged DR-9102 as you gave it and not swapped in DR-9001. Someone needs to confirm DR-9102 exists before anything is acted on.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, Sarah Chen's account. In the ERP the same customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001).
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. The submitted figures add up: 25% is $21,250 off, for a net of $63,750.
4. **Contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) is active on the date, running 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and it lists NS-EDGE as a supplied product. But its only discount term (§3.2) is 15% on NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** The only exception in force, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers "NS-500 only". Nothing covers NS-EDGE. The request's justification is "Competitive", so it rests on standard pricing, not special terms. If anyone tried to rely on Acme's NS-500 terms for this product, it would need commercial review instead, because those terms don't carry over to other products.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), along with the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Authority:** Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales. Over 20% needs the CRO. At 25%, this needs the CRO.
8. **Approver:** David Morgan holds the CRO role, per the organization chart as of 2026-09-01. The policy requires approvals above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Decision:** Approve only with David Morgan's authorization as CRO, and only once DR-9102 is confirmed in the CRM.
