## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy her limit is 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve DR-9001.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 is in the CRM discount requests file exactly as you gave it. It's 15% off NS-500 for account CRM-2048, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 and it is Pending Approval. It relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement rather than standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings." They share the address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee) and ID 04-812-7730, and the agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing." This is not CRM-2091, Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a different company in Akron.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000, which matches the request.
4. **The contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 allows up to 15% on NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15%, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. The request is at the 15% maximum and does not exceed it.
6. **The policy:** Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy was written on 2026-09-15 but doesn't take effect until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **The required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager according to the employee list and the 2026-09-01 org chart.

**Why "same as last September" doesn't apply:** Sarah approved 15% herself on DR-8104 (2025-09-16). That was valid because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that to 10%, and the authority belongs to the role, not the person or account. Her email to Michael that morning ("I think their agreement covers it") is right about Acme's eligibility, but that doesn't change who has to approve. Under the 2026 policy, discounts above 10% also need the approval recorded in the CRM.
