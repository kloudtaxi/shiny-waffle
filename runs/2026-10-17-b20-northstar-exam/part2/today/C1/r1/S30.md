## Answer

**No. CR-9205 can't be approved.** BlueRiver Logistics fails the payment-history test in the 2026 credit policy. There is also a separate problem: CR-9205 doesn't appear in the ERP credit-request records.

**The request.** You gave me CR-9205: customer C-1002, $100,000 to $200,000, requested by Michael Torres on 2026-09-23. It relies on the customer's own standing, not on a guarantee. The knowledge base copy of the ERP credit requests (credit_requests.csv) has no CR-9205. The only pending request dated 2026-09-23 is CR-9201, which is Acme's, not BlueRiver's. I decided on the request as you gave it, and confirming that CR-9205 exists is a blocking condition. The current $100,000 limit does match the ERP credit record for C-1002.

**The customer.** In the ERP, C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC". In the CRM it is CRM-2051 "BlueRiver Logistics". Both records have the same address (890 Harbor Way, St. Louis) and the same registration number (07-331-9025), so they are the same company. Its CRM tier is **Standard**.

**The policy.** The 2026 credit policy applies, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid more than 30 days late, or be unpaid more than 30 days after its due date.
- **INV-62147** ($30,000, NS-Cloud 2026-Q2) was due 2026-05-30 and paid 2026-07-21, **52 days late**. That breaks the rule.
- INV-61688 was 2 days late, which is allowed. The other invoices in the window were paid on time.
- INV-30020 isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

**Maximum limit.** Standard accounts are capped at $250,000, so the $200,000 itself would be within the cap. No guarantee is in place or relied on for BlueRiver. The parent guarantee in the knowledge base names Acme.

**Authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales and has no authority to approve credit limits. A $200,000 limit falls in the over-$100,000 to $500,000 band, which needs Finance Manager approval (Priya Shah). VP Sales concurrence is only required for Strategic accounts above $500,000. Approval by a higher-level approver wouldn't help, though: the customer isn't eligible, whoever signs.
