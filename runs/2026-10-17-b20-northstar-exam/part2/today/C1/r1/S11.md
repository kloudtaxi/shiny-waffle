No, Sarah Chen could not have approved this herself. Under the 2025 pricing policy, a 22% discount needed approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. One problem comes first: **request DR-9103 does not appear in the Northstar CRM discount-request export**, so it has to be confirmed before anyone acts on this.

1. **The request:** I couldn't find DR-9103 in the CRM export (`discount_requests.csv`). Acme's records there are DR-8104 (2025-09-16, NS-500, 15%, approved) and DR-9001 (2026-09-23, NS-500, 15%, pending). As instructed, I decided on the request as you gave it and didn't swap in another record. Its stated reason is "Competitive", so it uses standard pricing and claims no contract terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (Strategic tier, account owner Sarah Chen). The legal entity is Acme Mfg. Holdings, DUNS 04-812-7730. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. 22% off that is $18,700, leaving $66,300 net, which matches the request.
4. **Contract:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It lists NS-EDGE as a product supplied, but its only discount term (§3.2, up to 15%) is for the NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** The current exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) covers "NS-500 only" and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." Nothing covers NS-EDGE, but the request doesn't rely on any special terms, so this doesn't block it.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025). The 2026 matrix, which sends discounts above 20% to the Chief Revenue Officer, doesn't apply to a 2025 request.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she could approve discounts up to and including 15%. 22% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** The policy says "Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval," and the VP Sales is Michael Torres. He approved a similar case, DR-7012 (NS-EDGE, 22%, Cedar Health, 2025-11-07).

**Answer:** The request is approvable, but only with Michael Torres's sign-off, not Sarah's. Before anyone acts on it, DR-9103 has to be confirmed as an actual CRM record.
