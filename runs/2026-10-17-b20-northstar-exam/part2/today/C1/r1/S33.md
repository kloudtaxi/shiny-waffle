**1. The request.** The knowledge base's copy of the ERP requests file (`credit_requests.csv`) has no CR-9203. The only pending Acme request in it is **CR-9201**: also C-1001 and Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, but for $250,000 → $400,000, with no guarantee mentioned. I haven't decided on CR-9201 instead. I've decided CR-9203 as you gave it, with "confirm it exists in ERP" as a blocking condition. The request relies on a guarantee: its justification says "Supported by the Acme Group parent guarantee."

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and the legal entity in the supply agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) are the same company. The DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address match, and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings is "doing business as" Acme Manufacturing. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044, a different company). The ERP current limit is $250,000, which matches the request.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**4. Payment history: clean.** Under the 2026 rule, no invoice due in the past 12 months may be paid or unpaid more than 30 days after its due date.
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid early, on 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid early, on 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.

**5. Maximum limit.** Acme is a Strategic account in CRM, so the maximum is **$750,000**. The requested $1,000,000 only works if a parent guarantee in force covers at least $250,000. The policy says a guarantee counts only "for the customer the guarantee names."
- No guarantee document exists in the knowledge base.
- The only mention is Sarah's email to Priya Shah (2026-09-23): "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it. Gina says it's still in place." That is hearsay from a contact at Acme, not the guarantee itself.
- The legal parent is **Acme Group Holdings, Inc.** (named in the Acme Schedule C). The guarantor's exact name, the amount, the dates and whether it names Acme Mfg. Holdings are all unknown.

**6. Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales and has no credit approval authority under the policy. She also can't approve her own request.

**7. Who would approve.** If the guarantee checks out, a $1,000,000 limit needs Director of Finance approval (over $500,000), which is **Elena Novak**. Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs VP Sales concurrence, which is **Michael Torres**. Neither of them submitted the request, so the self-approval rule doesn't move either role.

**8. Decision.** Ask for the guarantee before deciding. If it doesn't show up, or doesn't name Acme Mfg. Holdings for at least $250,000 on 2026-09-23, the answer becomes REJECT_OR_ESCALATE: $1,000,000 is above the $750,000 maximum, and no approver can fix that.
