## Answer

**SR-39340 is a Severity 1 ticket. Both targets were missed, and Cedar is owed a $320.00 service credit.** The customer reported it as P3, but that doesn't set the severity.

**Customer and scope.** The ticket is on CRM account CRM-2063, Cedar Health Systems. Its DUNS, 02-906-4418, matches the support terms (SUP-CHS-2025). The terms put Cedar on the Silver plan, cover NS-CLOUD, and started coverage on 2025-12-01. The fee is $4,000 a month. The ticket is in scope. The account tier is Standard and the account owner is Anthony Day.

**True severity: 1.** The description says NS-Cloud "won't load for anyone" and that none of the 14 facilities can see equipment status. That makes NS-Cloud unavailable to all users, which is Severity 1 under the severity guide. The engineer confirmed there was no workaround. Because the impact recorded at opening is Severity 1, Severity 1 applies from the open time.

**Schedule and clock.** Schedule v2.0 applies because the ticket was opened after 2026-04-01 CT. Severity 1 uses a 24×7 clock with two targets: initial response within 30 minutes and restoration within 4 hours. All times below are converted from EDT to CDT (one hour earlier):
- **Opened:** 06:05 CDT by portal. The clock starts at 06:05.
- **Pauses:** none are recorded.
- **Maintenance exclusions:** there is no maintenance notice for Cedar.
- **Customer-caused exclusion:** Northstar didn't assert it, so no root-cause finding is due.

**Measurement**
- **Response:** the engineer phoned Cedar at 08:20 CDT, 2 h 15 min after the clock started, against a 30-minute target. **Missed.**
- **Restoration:** service came back at 11:35 CDT, after 5 h 30 min. The target was 4 h and twice the target is 8 h, so this is a **band 1 miss**.

**Credit.** Cedar reported by portal and never telephoned the hotline, so the lower Severity 2 credit rates apply. That gives 3% for the response miss plus 5% for the band 1 restoration miss, 8% of $4,000 = **$320.00**. The monthly cap is 30%, or $1,200. Cedar has no other tickets opened in June 2026, so the full amount fits under the cap. Cedar's emailed claim names the ticket and arrived 2026-06-29 at 10:00 CDT. The claim window closed 2026-07-10 at 23:59 CDT, so the claim was on time.

**What Northstar owes.** The ticket record shows none of these engagements or notifications were done.
- Engage **Hannah Lindqvist** (Support Escalation Manager) as incident lead by 06:20 CDT on 2026-06-10.
- Notify **Anthony Day** (account owner) by 06:35 CDT.
- Notify **Marcus Adeyemi** (Director of Customer Support) by 08:05 CDT, when half the restoration target had passed without restoration.
- Notify **Michael Torres** (VP Sales) by 10:05 CDT, when the full restoration target had passed. No notice to the Chief Revenue Officer is needed because the account isn't Strategic.
- Marcus Adeyemi sends the written claim decision and Paul Brennan (Support Billing Manager) issues the $320.00 credit memo, both by **2026-07-29**. As of today (2026-10-17) neither is recorded, so both deadlines look missed.

**What the customer had to do**
- **Report a Severity 1 incident by phoning the hotline:** not done. This is why the lower credit rates apply.
- **Make a written claim on time:** done.
