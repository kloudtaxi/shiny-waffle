## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. The request also differs from Northstar CRM, which records it as a different product, so that needs fixing first.

**The CRM record doesn't match what you sent.** The `discount_requests.csv` record for DR-9001 is on the **NS-500 Industrial Controller** (list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500). The version you gave says NS-Cloud Operations Suite (list $120,000, discount $18,000, net $102,000). Customer, 15%, requestor, date, reason ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement") and status are the same in both. Sarah's email to Michael Torres the same day also describes DR-9001 as the NS-500 controller for Acme's third plant. I've decided on the CRM record and listed the product difference as a blocking condition.

**How I reached the decision (NS-500, 15%, 2026-09-23):**
1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings": same address and DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a separate company.
2. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500, NS-CLOUD and NS-EDGE.
3. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) allows up to 15% off list on **NS-500 only**, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10. 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
4. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy was published on 2026-09-15 but doesn't take effect until 2027-01-01.
5. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit. Both the agreement (§3.3) and the exception say Acme's eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
6. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales, which is **Michael Torres** (EMP-200). Approvals over 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**If the request really is for NS-CLOUD,** the answer changes to commercial review (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`). The 15% exception covers only NS-500, and nothing on file gives Acme special pricing on NS-CLOUD. VP Sales approval would not fix that.

**Decision: `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`.** Michael Torres must approve, and only once the product has been confirmed.
