## Answer

No. CR-9204 can't be approved. It asks for $1,300,000, which is above the most Acme can have even with the parent guarantee: $1,150,000. There is also a separate problem: I can't find CR-9204 in Northstar ERP's credit-request records.

**1. The request.** You quoted CR-9204 for customer C-1001, from $250,000 to $1,300,000, raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23 and relying on the Acme Group parent guarantee. The credit-request records in the knowledge base (credit_requests.csv) don't contain CR-9204. The only pending Acme request is **CR-9201**, also for C-1001 and dated 2026-09-23, but for **$400,000**. That matches Sarah's email to Priya Shah on the same day. I've decided on CR-9204 as you gave it, not on CR-9201, and made confirming it a blocking condition.

**2. The customer.** These are all the same company: ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (Strategic tier) and the company named in the guarantee. They share the same address and the same registration number, 04-812-7730. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044, Akron) is a different company.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**4. Payment history: no problem.**
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 was due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count yet.

**5. The maximum limit: exceeded.**
- A Strategic account can have up to $750,000.
- The guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It names Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing and is capped at **$400,000**. Under the policy it raises Acme's maximum by that amount, to **$1,150,000**.
- The requested $1,300,000 is $150,000 over that. The guarantee can't be stretched beyond its cap, and a higher approver can't fix this.

**6–7. Approval authority (for the record).** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she has no credit approval authority. A limit above $500,000 needs the Director of Finance, Elena Novak. Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs agreement from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Neither of them submitted the request, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't come into play.

**8. Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** At $1,150,000 or less, Acme would qualify, with approval from Elena Novak and agreement from Michael Torres.
