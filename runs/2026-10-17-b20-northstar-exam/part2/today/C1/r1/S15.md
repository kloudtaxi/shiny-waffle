I've checked the CRM records, contracts, policies and the employee list. I'm writing up the decision now.

1. **The request isn't in the system of record.** The CRM discount request log has no DR-9107. The only NS-500 request pending on 2026-09-23 is DR-9001, which is for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), at 15%, submitted by Sarah Chen. I've decided on DR-9107 exactly as you gave it and haven't used DR-9001 in its place. Someone needs to confirm DR-9107 exists before anyone acts on this.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics (Standard tier, St. Louis, account owner Kelly Floyd). The support terms name it as Blue River Logistics LLC, with the same DUNS number (07-331-9025).
3. **The basis doesn't fit the customer.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its NS-500 pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, up to 15%, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) are between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, trading as Acme Manufacturing. BlueRiver isn't a party to either. The only BlueRiver contract I found is its support terms (SUP-BRL-2025): a Silver support plan covering NS-CLOUD only, with no pricing terms. Special terms don't carry over to another customer, so nothing makes BlueRiver eligible for a contract discount on NS-500.
4. **The policy and his authority.** Pricing Policy 2026 applies on 2026-09-23. The 2027 policy, which would put 12% within VP Sales authority and anything above 12% with the CRO, only takes effect on 2027-01-01. Michael Torres is VP Sales. Under the 2026 policy, VP Sales covers discounts above 10% and up to 20%, so 12% is within his authority. Authority isn't the problem; the problem is that nothing makes BlueRiver eligible.
5. **Outcome: commercial review.** That means either re-submitting at standard pricing with a real reason, or putting BlueRiver-specific terms in place. If it's re-submitted at standard pricing, 12% is still within VP Sales authority, and the policy requires the approval to be recorded in CRM. Michael Torres is also the person who submitted the request. The policy documents don't address someone approving their own request, so I haven't treated that as a rule.
