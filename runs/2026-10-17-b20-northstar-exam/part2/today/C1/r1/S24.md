## Answer

Not by Sarah Chen herself. The discount is allowed under Acme's contract, but 15% is above her own approval limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it. Also, the request you were given says "Approved", but the CRM record shows it as **Pending Approval**. Nobody has approved it yet.

1. **The request.** The CRM record for DR-9001 matches your submission on account, product, 15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, requestor and date (2026-09-23). It cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. The only difference is the status field.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The address and DUNS number match (04-812-7730), and the agreement names "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing". It is not CRM-2091, Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a different company.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000, so 15% comes to $37,500.
4. **The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. I found no termination or amendment.
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to 15% off list, for NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% request is within the limit, so Acme is eligible. The older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) expired on 2025-03-31 and was replaced. Both the exception and section 3.3 of the agreement say this eligibility does not give any employee authority to approve.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to the whole of 2026. The 2027 policy, written on 2026-09-15, only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **The required approver.** Under the 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix, discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales. The org chart and employee list show Michael Torres in that role. The approval has to be recorded in Northstar CRM.

The 15% Sarah approved herself last September (DR-8104) was under the 2025 policy, when her limit was 15%. Under the 2026 policy she can't do that again.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Michael Torres needs to approve, and his approval must be recorded in CRM. Before anyone acts on it, the wrong "Approved" status in the submitted copy needs to be corrected.
