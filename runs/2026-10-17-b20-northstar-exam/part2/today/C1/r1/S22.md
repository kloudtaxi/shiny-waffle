## Answer

DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen herself. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to sign off. Before anything happens, someone also needs to sort out a mismatch: your submitted copy doesn't match what the CRM holds.

**The mismatch.** Your copy says 8.0%, a $20,000 discount and $230,000 net. The CRM record for DR-9001 says **15.0%, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net**. Sarah's own email to Michael Torres the same day also asks for "15% off list." I've decided on the CRM's 15%. At 8% Sarah could approve it herself, so which figure is right changes who approves.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, product NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen, status Pending Approval. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on special contract terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing," is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings, the ERP bill-to (C-1001). It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. 15% of that is $37,500, which matches the CRM.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Nothing shows it was terminated. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15% of list, and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception and "establishes commercial eligibility only." The requested 15% is at the limit, not over it.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force for all of 2026), with the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. The approval must be recorded in the CRM.

Sarah is relying on last September's 15% (DR-8104), which she approved herself. That was within her authority under the 2025 policy, which allowed account executives up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that to 10%, so last year's approval doesn't settle this one.
