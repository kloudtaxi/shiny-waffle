## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved an 8% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9110", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "8.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "20000", "net_value_usd": "230000", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

**Yes, on the merits Sarah Chen could have approved this herself, but DR-9110 isn't in the Northstar CRM discount-request records I can see, so that has to be confirmed before anyone acts on it.**

1. **The request isn't on record.** The CRM discount-request export has no DR-9110. The only Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048) NS-500 requests are DR-8104 (15%, 2025-09-16, approved) and DR-9001 (15%, 2025-09-23, pending). I haven't substituted either one. This decision is on DR-9110 as you gave it.
2. **Customer.** CRM-2048 Acme Manufacturing is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 Acme Mfg. Holdings. They share an address and DUNS number (04-812-7730), and Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The submitted figures add up: 8% off is $20,000, leaving $230,000.
4. **The justification cites the wrong agreement.** It says "Acme Master Supply Agreement", but that agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its 15% NS-500 exception both start on 2025-04-01. That's one day after the request date, so neither applied on 2025-03-31.
5. **The customer was still eligible.** On 2025-03-31 the 2023 exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023) applied. It allows up to 10% off NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so 8% is within it. The ACME-SA-2023 agreement document itself isn't in the knowledge base. Only the exception that refers to it is.
6. **Policy and authority.** Pricing Policy 2025 was in force (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Under it, Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so 8% is within her own limit. The 2026 approval matrix doesn't apply. Approval came from her role under the policy, not from the contract; the exception and the agreement both say they grant no approval authority.

**Decision: APPROVE**, on the condition that DR-9110 is confirmed in Northstar CRM. The CRM record should also be changed to cite EXC-ACME-NS500-10 / ACME-SA-2023 rather than the Master Supply Agreement. That doesn't change the outcome: 8% would also fall within her standard authority and within the later 15% exception.
