## Answer

**No. You can't offer Acme 18% on the NS-500 under DR-9001.** Acme's pricing exception allows at most 15% on this product, and a higher approver can't override that. Getting to 18% would need new or amended terms with the customer.

**How I got there**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 18% off a list value of $250,000 ($45,000 discount, $205,000 net) on the NS-500. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special contract terms.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing). Note that Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers only the NS-500, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a maximum of 15% off list. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and gives no Northstar employee any approval authority.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), with the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. The 2027 policy only applies to requests dated 2027-01-01 or later.
7. **Requestor's authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, which carries authority up to and including 10%.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres. He can't fix this request, though, because the limit it breaks is the customer's eligibility, not anyone's authority.

**Outcome:** 18% is above Acme's 15% maximum, so the result is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. At 15% or less, the request would instead need VP Sales (Michael Torres) to approve it, because it is above Sarah's 10% limit.

**One inconsistency:** Sarah's email of 2026-09-23 logging DR-9001 says Acme was "asking for 15% off list". The CRM record you gave says 18%. I decided on the CRM figure, since CRM is the system of record. Someone should check which figure is right, because a 15% request would be approvable with VP Sales sign-off. Also, I couldn't find the DR-9001 entry itself in the knowledge base's copy of CRM data (only the email mentions it), so I relied on the record as you gave it.
