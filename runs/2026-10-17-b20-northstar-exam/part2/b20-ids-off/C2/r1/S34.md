## Answer

I can't approve CR-9208 yet. The ERP has no request with that number. The request as you gave it would be eligible, but it needs Elena Novak (Director of Finance) to approve and VP Sales to concur. Michael Torres can't approve it himself, and it can't go ahead until someone confirms the request exists in the ERP.

**1. The request.** The knowledge base finds nothing for CR-9208. The only open Acme request in the ERP is **CR-9201**: same customer (C-1001), same date (2026-09-23), same reason (third-plant ramp-up), also "Pending Approval". But it asks for **$400,000**, not $650,000, and was submitted by **Sarah Chen**, not Michael Torres. I haven't treated CR-9201 as your request. I've decided on CR-9208 as you gave it. The request relies on Acme's own standing; it doesn't mention a guarantee.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, D-U-N-S 04-812-7730). Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company. CRM records Acme Manufacturing as a **Strategic** account.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid more than 30 days late, or is still unpaid more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes.
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24, on time.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22, on time.
- INV-62305: due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so not yet due when the request was made.

**5. Maximum limit.** The maximum for Strategic accounts is $750,000, so $650,000 is within it on Acme's own standing. Acme's parent also guarantees its debts: guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 from Acme Group Holdings, up to $400,000, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. That would raise the maximum to $1,150,000, but this request doesn't need it.

**6. Michael Torres's authority.** He is VP Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to Accounts Receivable Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager (up to $500,000) and the Director of Finance (above $500,000). VP Sales has no approval authority, so he can't approve this himself.

**7. Who must sign off.** The policy judges authority on the new total limit, $650,000:
- **Approval:** Director of Finance, held by **Elena Novak**.
- **Concurrence:** VP Sales, required for Strategic accounts above $500,000. **Michael Torres** is the only VP Sales on record. The policy has no rule stopping the person who made the request from concurring, so he can give it. It isn't recorded anywhere yet, and putting in the request doesn't count as concurring.
