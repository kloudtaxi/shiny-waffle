## Answer

Not yet. The request is based on another customer's pricing terms, so it needs commercial review before anyone approves it.

**1. The request.** The request is DR-9107. It's a 12% discount on NS-500 for CRM-2051, which is $30,000 off a $250,000 list price. Michael Torres submitted it on 2026-09-23. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing. DR-9107 doesn't appear in the knowledge base, so it needs confirming in the CRM.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics. Its legal name in its support terms is Blue River Logistics LLC. It has nothing to do with any Acme company.

**3–5. Contract and pricing exception.**
- No document called "Acme Master Supply Agreement" exists in the knowledge base.
- The closest match is agreement ACME-MFG-2025. Its Schedule B is the Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15): up to 15% off NS-500, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. But that exception's customer is **Acme Manufacturing** (CRM-2048), not BlueRiver.
- BlueRiver's only agreement is its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025). That's a Silver support plan covering the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. It contains no pricing terms and doesn't mention NS-500.
- So no BlueRiver contract term or pricing exception covers NS-500 on the request date. Special terms don't transfer from one customer to another.

**6–8. Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Its approval bands, also listed in the Approval Authority Matrix, are:
- up to 10%: Enterprise Account Executive
- over 10% and up to 20%: VP Sales
- over 20%: Chief Revenue Officer

Torres is VP Sales (EMP-200), so a 12% discount is within his own authority. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM. But having the authority doesn't make the customer eligible for special pricing: the Acme exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and grants no approval authority to anyone.

**9. Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The discount can't be approved on Acme's terms. To go ahead, either:
- Deal Desk or commercial review sets up proper pricing terms for BlueRiver, or
- the request is resubmitted on a standard pricing basis. At 12% it would then fall in the VP Sales band.

Torres would be approving his own request in that case. I found no rule in the knowledge base that forbids it, but it's worth checking.
