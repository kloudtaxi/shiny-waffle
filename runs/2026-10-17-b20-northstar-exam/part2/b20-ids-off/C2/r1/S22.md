## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve DR-9001 herself, but it can be approved if Michael Torres (VP Sales) signs off.** The discount is within what Acme's contract allows. There's also a mismatch between your submitted request and the CRM record that must be fixed first.

**The submitted figures don't match the CRM record (the system of record).** Your copy says 8.0% off ($20,000 discount, $230,000 net). CRM has DR-9001 at **15.0% off ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net)**. Everything else matches: account CRM-2048, product NS-500, list price $250,000, requestor sarah.chen@northstar.example, date 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". The procedure says to decide on the CRM value, so this decision uses 15%. If 8% is actually correct, Sarah could approve it herself, since it's within her 10% limit.

**How I got there:**
1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP system the same company is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (customer C-1001), with the same tax/DUNS number (04-812-7730).
2. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme can get up to 15% off NS-500, but "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
3. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers NS-500 only, runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and allows up to 15% off list. It replaced the older 10% exception, which ended 2025-03-31. It states it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and gives no Northstar employee approval authority. A 15% request is exactly at the cap, so Acme is eligible.
4. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
5. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit. The contract doesn't change that.
6. **Who must approve:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres (EMP-200). Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
