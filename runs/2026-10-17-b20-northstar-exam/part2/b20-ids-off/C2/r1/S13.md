## Answer

I can't approve it. The decision is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**. The contract terms the request cites belong to a different Acme company. Also, request DR-9105 itself isn't in the knowledge base.

**The request.** DR-9105 asks for 15% off NS-500 for Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091). Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on special terms. Looking up DR-9105 in Northstar CRM returned not_known, so I've decided on the request as you gave it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition below.

**The customer.** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is ERP C-1044 (Akron, OH). It is not Acme Manufacturing, which is CRM-2048 and ERP "Acme Mfg. Holdings" C-1001 (Milwaukee, WI). The two have different D-U-N-S numbers, and one document says outright: "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company."

**The contract and the exception.** Both belong to Acme Manufacturing, not to the customer on this request:
- The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It is with Acme Mfg. Holdings and allows up to 15% on NS-500.
- The pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) runs over the same dates, covers NS-500 only, and names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- Acme Manufacturing's support schedule (Schedule C) once covered Acme Industrial Supply, but that coverage is marked as ended.
- I found no contract or pricing exception for Acme Industrial Supply Co. on 2026-09-23. Special terms don't carry over from one customer to another, so this needs commercial review.

**Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less. 15% is above her authority.
- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
- He can't fix the missing eligibility, though. Even if a review sets up terms for this customer, the request would still need his approval.
- Both the agreement and the exception say they don't grant anyone approval authority.
