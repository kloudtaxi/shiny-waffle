## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve DR-9001 herself, but it can be approved if the VP Sales, Michael Torres, signs off.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on NS-500, but the discount is above the 10% Sarah is allowed to approve. One problem must be fixed first: the request you sent is marked "Approved", but the sales system shows it as "Pending Approval".

**What I checked**
1. **The request:** In the sales system's discount-request records, DR-9001 matches what you sent: account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, $250,000 list price, $37,500 off, $212,500 net, submitted by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23. The reason given is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on Acme's contract terms. The only difference is the status: the record says "Pending Approval".
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as Acme Mfg. Holdings, customer C-1001 in the billing (ERP) system. Both records show the same D-U-N-S company number, 04-812-7730. Sarah owns the account.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It covers NS-500 and allows discounts of up to 15% on it. It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, with a 15% maximum, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ended on 2025-03-31. The new exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and does not give any employee approval authority. **15% is exactly at the limit, so Acme is eligible.**
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), along with its Approval Authority Matrix. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager. Under the policy, any approval above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

- **Fix the status (this blocks the decision):** The request says "Approved", but no one with the authority has approved it. Revenue Operations should set it back to "Pending Approval", and confirm no order has gone out at the 15% price.
