## Answer

**Yes, on its merits, but it can't be acted on yet.** CR-9202 doesn't exist in Northstar ERP. The outcome is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**: Acme qualifies for $650,000, but Sarah Chen can't approve it. It needs Elena Novak (Director of Finance) to approve and Michael Torres (VP Sales) to concur. It's blocked until someone confirms CR-9202 exists in the ERP.

1. **The request.** The ERP has no CR-9202. The only pending Acme request is **CR-9201**: also customer C-1001, raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23 with the same reason, but for **$250,000 → $400,000**. I decided CR-9202 as you gave it and didn't substitute CR-9201. The request relies on Acme's own standing, not on a guarantee.
2. **The customer.** C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the ERP is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730, and the legal papers say it trades as Acme Manufacturing). Its CRM tier is **Strategic**.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 covers requests from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, and it fully replaces the 2025 policy.
4. **Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so the rule doesn't count it.
5. **Maximum limit.** Strategic accounts can go up to **$750,000**, so $650,000 fits without any guarantee. The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026: $400,000 from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. for Acme Mfg. Holdings, valid 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) exists and names this customer, but this decision doesn't use it.
6. **Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives her role no approval authority.
7. **Who must approve.** Authority depends on the new total limit, $650,000:
   - Anything over $500,000 needs the **Director of Finance**, Elena Novak.
   - Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need **VP Sales** concurrence, from Michael Torres.

Two things to watch, though neither blocks the decision. INV-62305 had no payment recorded as of today (2026-10-18), and it will break the 30-day rule if it's still unpaid on 2026-11-01. And that one invoice ($720,000) is larger than the requested limit.
