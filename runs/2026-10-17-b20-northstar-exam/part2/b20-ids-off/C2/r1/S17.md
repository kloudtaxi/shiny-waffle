## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this. The request asks for contract pricing on NS-EDGE, but no Acme contract term or pricing exception covers NS-EDGE. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**. Separately, 12% is above her own approval limit.

**The request isn't in the CRM records.** DR-9109 does not exist in the knowledge base. The closest record is **DR-9001**: same customer (CRM-2048), same requestor, same date (2026-09-23) and same justification, but for **NS-500 at 15%**. The request may have been entered wrongly, or it may be a separate request that was never recorded. I decided on DR-9109 as you gave it and didn't swap in DR-9001. Confirming the record is a blocking condition below.

1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (DUNS 04-812-7730). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a separate company.
2. **Product:** NS-EDGE, the NS-Edge Monitoring Package.
3. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-EDGE is one of the products it supplies, but its prices are "Northstar's current list prices… less any discount agreed in an Order." The only discount eligibility it states is "up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller."
4. **Pricing exceptions:** Neither one covers NS-EDGE.
   - EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product."
   - EXC-ACME-NS500-10 has been replaced by EXC-ACME-NS500-15 and is also NS-500 only.
   - Schedule C covers support for NS-EDGE, not pricing.

   The NS-500 terms don't carry over to NS-EDGE, so the special terms the request relies on don't exist for this product.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix.
6. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, with a limit of 10% or less. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Even if a commercial review sets up eligibility, 12% would still need his approval. The contract also says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

**Next steps:** Revenue Operations should confirm whether DR-9109 really exists. Deal Desk would then need to do a commercial review and, if appropriate, agree new or amended terms for NS-EDGE. After that, the request goes to Michael Torres.
