## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. The 15% is within what Acme's contract allows, but on 2027-02-01 she can only approve up to 5%. Anything over 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. **Outcome: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**, with one blocking problem: request DR-9113 isn't in the knowledge base.

1. **The request.** DR-9113 asks for 15% off NS-500, requested by Sarah Chen and dated 2027-02-01. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement. The ID couldn't be found under either "Northstar CRM" or "CRM", so the details above come from what you sent. Approval must wait until someone confirms the request exists in Northstar CRM with these values.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it's customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS 04-812-7730). The account owner is Sarah Chen. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091) is a different company. One oddity: looking up CRM-2048 by itself also returned a service ticket, SR-39385. This doesn't change the customer match.
3. **The product.** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on 2027-02-01. It covers NS-500 and allows discounts of up to 15% on it. It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers this product and date. It applies to NS-500 only, runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, allows a maximum of 15% off list price, and was approved by David Morgan. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests on or after 2025-04-01. It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." **15% is exactly at the limit, so Acme is eligible.**
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 is in effect from 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and fully replaces the 2026 policy.
   - Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 5%.
   - Over 5% up to 12% needs the VP Sales.
   - Over 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
   - Anything over 5% needs recorded approval evidence.

   The Approval Authority Matrix that let Account Executives approve up to 10% ended on 2026-12-31.
7. **Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 5%. 15% is over it.
8. **Required approver.** The over-12% band needs the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan (EMP-300). Her manager, Michael Torres (VP Sales), can only approve up to 12%, so his approval isn't enough.
9. **Decision.** Under the procedure, the contract permitting 15% doesn't give Sarah authority to approve it. Since eligibility is established but the discount exceeds her limit, the outcome is APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, once DR-9113 is confirmed in Northstar CRM.
