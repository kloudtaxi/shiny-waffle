## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, but 15% is above her own limit. Michael Torres, VP Sales, must approve it.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 matches the Northstar CRM record exactly: NS-500, 15.0%, $250,000 list price, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the request relies on contract terms.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In ERP the same customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, SKU NS-500.
4. **Active contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500. It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, covers the NS-500 only, and allows a maximum of 15% off list. 15% is within that maximum, so eligibility is established. The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), whose order window ended on 2025-03-31.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, which sets lower limits, only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres (EMP-200) holds that role, and he is Sarah's manager.

One more thing: approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM, with the approval evidence. Separately, the knowledge base also links the CRM-2048 number to a service ticket (SR-39385). It doesn't affect the decision, but someone should clean it up.
