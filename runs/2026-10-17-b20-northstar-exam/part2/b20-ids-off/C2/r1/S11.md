## Answer

No. Under the policy in force on 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could not have approved this 22% discount herself. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, would have to approve it. There's also a problem with the record: I couldn't find DR-9103 in Northstar CRM.

1. **The request isn't in the system of record.** I couldn't find DR-9103 under Northstar CRM. The CRM discount-request export has two other Acme requests from Sarah Chen, both for 15% on the NS-500 controller, not NS-EDGE:
   - DR-8104, dated 2025-09-16 and approved.
   - DR-9001, dated 2026-09-23 and pending.

   I haven't used either in place of DR-9103. I've assessed the request as you gave it, on the condition that someone confirms DR-9103 exists in CRM.
2. **Basis: standard pricing.** The justification given is "Competitive", not a contract term or pricing exception. So customer eligibility isn't in question, and the decision comes down to Sarah's authority. Acme has a Master Supply Agreement and NS-500 pricing exceptions, but this request doesn't rely on them, and none of them was shown to cover NS-EDGE.
3. **The customer is Acme Manufacturing,** CRM account CRM-2048. In ERP it's billed as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), and Sarah Chen is the account owner. "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (C-1044) is a different company.
4. **The product and figures check out.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. 22% of that is $18,700, leaving a net of $66,300, which matches the request.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix (AE limit 10%) only took effect on 2026-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
   - Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%.
   - Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval.
6. **Authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 15%. 22% is above it.
7. **Required approver:** the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres (Sarah's manager). The 2025 policy sets no higher band above 15%.
