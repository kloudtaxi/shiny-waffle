## Answer

**Answer: Yes, but not by Michael Torres on his own.** On the terms submitted, BlueRiver qualified for a $200,000 limit on 2025-09-23. The policy requires Finance Manager Priya Shah to approve it. Two blocking conditions apply first: CR-9206 is not in the knowledge base, and I could not confirm that ERP customer C-1002 is BlueRiver Logistics.

1. **The request.** CR-9206 asks to raise C-1002's limit from $100,000 to $200,000. Torres submitted it on 2025-09-23 because of fleet expansion. It relies on BlueRiver's own standing, not on a guarantee. I searched the knowledge base for CR-9206 and found no record of it. The nearest record is CR-9201, a different customer (C-1001) and a later date (2026-09-23). I did not use it. Per the procedure, I decided on CR-9206 as you gave it.
2. **The customer.** BlueRiver Logistics is a **Standard** tier account in the CRM (CRM-2051). The ERP shows C-1002 with a $100,000 limit, which matches the request. Its invoices are for NS-Cloud subscriptions, which fits the stated reason. But no record links C-1002 to BlueRiver or CRM-2051.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2025 applies, since it covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
4. **Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 45 days after its due date. C-1002 passes:
   - INV-61005: due 2025-03-01, paid 2025-02-26 (early)
   - INV-61210: due 2025-05-30, paid 2025-05-28 (early)
   - INV-61433: due 2025-08-29, paid 2025-09-02 (4 days late, well within 45)
5. **Maximum limit.** The maximum for Standard accounts is $300,000. The $200,000 requested is within it, so no guarantee is needed.
6. **Torres's authority.** Torres is VP Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $50,000), the Finance Manager (over $50,000 up to $750,000) and the Director of Finance (over $750,000). It also bars anyone from approving a request they submitted. He has no authority here.
7. **Who must approve.** A $200,000 total limit falls in the Finance Manager band. That role is held by **Priya Shah**, who did not submit the request. The 2025 policy requires no concurrence.
