## Answer

The true severity of SR-39881 is **Severity 2**, and both service levels were missed. Blue River filed its credit claim on time, so Northstar owes a **$200 service credit**. The written claim decision is due by 2026-09-27, but I couldn't find the escalation procedure's trigger rules, so I can't say who had to be engaged or notified during the incident, or by when.

**1. Customer.** CRM account CRM-2051, ERP customer C-1002 and DUNS 07-331-9025 all point to Blue River Logistics LLC (also recorded as "BlueRiver Logistics").

**2. Scope: in scope.** Blue River's support terms (SUP-BRL-2025) are on the Silver plan, cover NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD), and coverage started 2025-01-01. The fee is $2,500 a month.

**3. True severity: Severity 2 (High).** The ticket records that NS-Cloud shipment-exception alerts and route dashboards stopped updating at the St. Louis and Memphis depots, "2 of our 6". The other depots were fine, and dispatchers were phoning drivers to track exceptions. That matches three Severity 2 definitions in the severity guide:
- an alarming function unavailable for one or more sites;
- NS-Cloud unavailable to some, but not all, sites;
- a workaround that needs ongoing manual effort.

Severity 1 doesn't apply: operations didn't stop, and there was a workaround. The customer's own P2 happens to agree, but it doesn't set the severity.

**4. Schedule.** The ticket opened 2026-08-20 19:45 CDT, so schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01). For Severity 2 on Silver it sets an initial response of 2 business hours and a restoration of 12 business hours. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, except Northstar holidays. No holidays fall between 20 and 24 August 2026.

**5. Clock, pauses and exclusions.** The ticket opened on a Thursday after hours, so the clock started at the next business-hours start: Friday 2026-08-21 08:00 CDT.
- The automatic acknowledgment at 19:46 doesn't count as a response, because the response must come from a support engineer.
- The event log shows no "Awaiting Customer" pause or written stop-work request.
- No maintenance window applies: notice MN-2607 covered July.

**6. Measurement.**
- **Response: missed.** The target fell at 2026-08-21 10:00. The engineer responded at 10:20, 20 minutes late.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** Friday gives 10 business hours, and Monday 08:00–10:40 adds 2 h 40 min, for 12 h 40 min elapsed against a 12-hour target. That is over the target but not more than double it (24 hours).

**7. Credits.**
- The Severity 2 rates are 3% for the response miss and 5% for restoration band 1. That is 8% of $2,500, or **$200**.
- The monthly cap is 30%, or $750. The other Blue River ticket, SR-39561, was opened in July, so it doesn't count toward the August cap.
- The claim was received in writing by email on 2026-08-28 at 14:05 CDT and names the ticket. The window ran to 2026-09-23 23:59 CT (30 days after restoration on 08-24), so the claim was on time.

**8. What Northstar owes, and who must act.**
- **Claim decision:** Marcus Adeyemi, Director of Customer Support, must send a written decision by **2026-09-27 23:59 CDT** (the 30th calendar day after the claim arrived).
- **Credit memo:** Paul Brennan, Support Billing Manager, must issue it on the day the credit is approved, so no later than 2026-09-27.
- **Engagement and notification:** the escalation procedure says the Support Escalation Manager (Hannah Lindqvist) is notified "no later than that moment". But the knowledge base doesn't hold the triggering event, the other escalation roles, or their thresholds. I can't work out those deadlines, and I haven't guessed them.
- **Root-cause finding:** not required. That only applies if Northstar claims the customer caused the incident, and nothing in the record does.
- **Status:** today is 2026-10-18, so the decision and memo deadlines have passed. The record shows neither one.

**What the customer had to do:**

- A timely written claim was required, and it met that.
- It must provide information and access Northstar needs, but Northstar recorded no such request.
