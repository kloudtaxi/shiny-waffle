## Answer

I can't approve CR-9207 yet. The decision is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**. The request depends on an "Acme parent guarantee," but no guarantee in the knowledge base covers Acme Industrial Supply Co. Without one, $500,000 is double this customer's $250,000 maximum.

1. **The request.** I couldn't find CR-9207 in the ERP records in the knowledge base. The only pending request dated 2026-09-23 from Sarah Chen is CR-9201. That one is for a different customer, Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), raising its limit from $250k to $400k for a "third-plant ramp-up." I haven't used CR-9201 in its place. I decided on CR-9207 as you gave it, and confirming that it exists in Northstar ERP is a blocking condition. The request relies on a guarantee.
2. **The customer.** C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. (D-U-N-S 09-552-1187, Akron, OH; CRM-2091). Its CRM tier is **Standard**. Northstar's documents say it "is a different company" from ACME Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which is based in Milwaukee.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history.** The policy disqualifies a customer if any invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. C-1044 has two invoices, INV-30001 and INV-30003. Both fell due in early 2025, before that window (2025-09-23 to 2026-09-23), and neither was more than 30 days late anyway. Nothing disqualifies the customer here.
5. **The maximum limit.** The maximum for a Standard account is $250,000. A parent guarantee raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names. The only guarantee on file is GRT-ACME-2026: $400k from Acme Group Holdings, Inc., in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It covers only Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), not C-1044. So for this customer the maximum is $250,000, and $500,000 is over it.
6. **Requestor authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives that role no authority to approve credit limits.
7. **Required approver.** If the customer becomes eligible, a $500,000 limit falls in the band above $100k up to and including $500k, which needs the Finance Manager: Priya Shah. VP Sales concurrence applies only to Strategic accounts above $500k, so it doesn't apply here.

**What to do:** Sarah Chen needs to produce a guarantee that names Acme Industrial Supply Co. and is in force on 2026-09-23. If she can't, the request is over the customer's $250,000 maximum and should be rejected or resubmitted at $250,000 or less. She should also confirm whether she meant to cite GRT-ACME-2026, since it covers C-1001 (and her CR-9201) rather than this customer.
