## Answer

No, CR-9205 can't be approved. Blue River Logistics doesn't qualify for a higher limit because of a late payment, and getting a more senior approver wouldn't change that. Separately, I couldn't find CR-9205 in the knowledge base at all, so its existence needs to be confirmed.

1. **The request.** I searched for CR-9205 and found nothing. The ERP credit-request log only shows CR-9201 (another customer, C-1001) for that date. So I decided the request exactly as you gave it. Its stated reason, "Fleet expansion: larger quarterly NS-Cloud orders," relies on the customer's own standing, not on a guarantee from anyone else.
2. **The customer.** ERP customer C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC." In the CRM it's "BlueRiver Logistics" (CRM-2051). Both records have the same D-U-N-S number (07-331-9025) and the same address, so they're the same company. The ERP shows a current limit of $100,000, which matches the request.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31 and replaces the previous policy entirely.
4. **Payment history.** The policy says no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid more than 30 days after its due date. One invoice breaks that rule:
   - **INV-62147** ($30,000, NS-CLOUD 2026-Q2) was due 2026-05-30 and paid 2026-07-21, which is **52 days late**.
   - INV-61688 was paid 2 days late, which is within the rule. The other invoices in the 12-month window were paid on time.
5. **The maximum limit.** The CRM lists the account as Standard tier, so the maximum is $250,000. The requested $200,000 would fit under that, but the late payment rules the customer out anyway.
6. **Michael Torres's authority.** He's VP Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager (over $100,000 up to $500,000) and the Director of Finance (over $500,000). VP Sales only gives concurrence for Strategic accounts over $500,000. He can't approve this himself.
7. **Who would approve.** A $200,000 limit falls to the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. That doesn't matter here, since the customer isn't eligible.

**Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** The request can be looked at again once INV-62147 (due 2026-05-30) is more than 12 months in the past, or under a later policy.
