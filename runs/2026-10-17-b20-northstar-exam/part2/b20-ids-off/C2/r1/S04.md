## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen could not have approved this herself. The 15% discount needs approval from Michael Torres, VP Sales.** The request does not match the CRM on one point: you gave the date as 2025-09-23, but the CRM records DR-9001 as dated **2026-09-23**. That date also appears on the 2026-09-23 email where Sarah logged the request. I decided on the CRM date, and the difference changes the answer, so it is listed below as a condition.

1. **Request:** DR-9001, dated 2026-09-23 in the CRM, from Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive). It is for 15% off NS-500, which is $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, leaving $212,500. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on a contract term. Status: Pending Approval.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings, C-1001, with the same DUNS number (04-812-7730). It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on either date. It makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500, but says "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B to that agreement is exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers NS-500 only, sets a 15% maximum, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and does not grant anyone approval authority. The request asks for exactly 15%, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Under that policy, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That is Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager.
9. **Decision:** APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION. Acme is entitled to 15%, but that doesn't give Sarah authority to approve it.

**The date matters.** If the request really was dated 2025-09-23, the 2025 policy applies. That policy let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to and including 15%, so Sarah could have approved it herself. Someone needs to confirm the date before this decision is acted on.
