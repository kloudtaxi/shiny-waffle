## Answer

No. Michael Torres can't approve this, and neither can anyone more senior. The 18% is above the most Acme is entitled to under its contract, which is 15%. The issue is the customer's eligibility, not his authority.

**What the request relies on.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on special contract terms. The numbers in the request agree with each other: 18% of $250,000 is $45,000, leaving $205,000.

**What I found:**
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing in the CRM. In the ERP it is billed as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001).
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller." It also says "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers NS-500 only, over the same dates. Its "maximum eligible discount" is 15% of list price. It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10, and it states that it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix were in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The bands are: Enterprise Account Executive up to 10%, VP Sales over 10% up to 20%, Chief Revenue Officer over 20%.
- **Torres's authority:** He is VP Sales (EMP-200), so 18% is within his band. That doesn't change the outcome, because approval authority doesn't create eligibility the contract doesn't give.

**Decision:** Reject or escalate. The extra 3 points would need new or amended commercial terms, such as a revised Schedule B. Without that, the request would have to be resubmitted at 15% or less.

**The request ID isn't in the knowledge base.** I couldn't find DR-9112 anywhere. The only Acme NS-500 request I found is DR-9001, also dated 2026-09-23, which asked for 15%. That's a separate record, so I decided on DR-9112 as you gave it, and its existence in the CRM needs to be confirmed. Also, looking up CRM-2048 returned a service ticket (SR-39385) as well as ACME Manufacturing, so that ID is worth checking.
