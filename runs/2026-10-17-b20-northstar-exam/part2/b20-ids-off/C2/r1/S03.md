## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself, and as submitted it can't be approved at all until the product is sorted out. The system of record records DR-9001 for a different product from the one in your question.

**The request.** Northstar CRM's DR-9001 is for account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing; the ERP customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", C-1001). It asks for 15.0% and was requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". But CRM records it as:
- **product NS-500** (NS-500 Industrial Controller), not NS-CLOUD;
- **list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500**, not $120,000 / $18,000 / $102,000.

The company's rules say to decide on the system of record's values, so the decision below is for the NS-500 request. The mismatch is a blocking condition.

**Decision on the record (NS-500, 15%):**
1. **Contract:** the Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says the customer "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".
2. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, at a maximum of 15% of list price, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10. So 15% is within Acme's eligibility. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee".
3. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix apply.
4. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to and including 10%. A discount above 10% and up to 20% needs **VP Sales**, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200, Sarah's manager).
5. **Outcome:** **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is eligible for 15%, but Michael Torres must approve it, and the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**If the deal really is for NS-Cloud,** the answer is different. The MSA lists NS-Cloud only as a product supplied at "current list prices". The 15% term and the exception are limited to NS-500 and don't carry over to NS-Cloud. A 15% NS-Cloud request based on contract pricing would therefore be **REVIEW_REQUIRED** (commercial review), not an approval. That's why the product has to be confirmed before anything else happens.

One more thing: the CRM row has 2026-09-23 in a column labelled "expiration date". It looks like the request date, but I've noted it as something to confirm, not a blocker.
