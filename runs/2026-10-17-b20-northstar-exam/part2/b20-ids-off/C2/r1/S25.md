## Answer

**Not on Sarah's authority alone.** Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but 15% is above Sarah Chen's 10% limit, so the VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it. Outcome: **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**.

1. **The request:** The CRM record for DR-9001 matches what was submitted: account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off $250,000 list ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. It relies on contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (the agreement says it does business as Acme Manufacturing; DUNS 04-812-7730). Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044).
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500 and makes Acme "eligible for discounts of up to 15%." It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, at up to 15% of list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which expired 2025-03-31. It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The requested 15% is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, with the Approval Authority Matrix as its working reference. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager. Approvals over 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

The contract makes Acme eligible but doesn't give Sarah more authority. Nothing in the request differs from the system of record, so there are no conditions.
