## Answer

**No, it can't be approved.** $1,300,000 is above the most this customer can have, even after adding the parent guarantee. The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**, and a more senior approver can't change that. Separately, I couldn't find CR-9204 in the knowledge base's copy of the ERP records.

**1. The request.** The knowledge base has no CR-9204. The only ERP credit request for C-1001 on that date is **CR-9201**: Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, $250,000 → **$400,000**, "Third-plant ramp-up," Pending Approval. That's a different request with a different amount and reason, so I decided CR-9204 as you submitted it and didn't use CR-9201 in its place. Confirming CR-9204 exists is a blocking condition. The request relies on the parent guarantee.

**2. The customer.** C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the ERP is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (same DUNS, 04-812-7730). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091), which is a separate company. Its CRM tier is **Strategic**.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history: no problem.** The policy disqualifies a customer if any invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or is still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.

**5. The maximum limit: exceeded.**
- The Strategic-tier maximum is $750,000.
- The Parent Company Guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. and names Acme Mfg. Holdings. It runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it was in force on the request date. Its liability is capped at $400,000.
- Under the policy, an in-force parent guarantee raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount: $750,000 + $400,000 = **$1,150,000**.
- The request is **$150,000 over** that maximum.

**6–7. Who could approve.** This doesn't change the outcome. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives that role no credit approval authority. A limit above $500,000 would need the Director of Finance to approve and, for a Strategic account, the VP Sales (Michael Torres) to concur. I couldn't find who holds the Director of Finance role. Michael Torres is Sarah Chen's manager. The policy facts I found say nothing about whether that rules him out.

**Options:** resubmit at no more than $1,150,000, or get a larger guarantee. If CR-9201 ($400,000) is the request you actually meant, it needs its own decision.
