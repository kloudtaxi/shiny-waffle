However, I couldn't find request DR-9108 anywhere in the knowledge base, so someone needs to confirm it exists in Northstar CRM before the approval is acted on.

2. **The customer:** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. In the ERP it's customer C-1044, with the same DUNS number (09-552-1187). It is a different company from ACME Manufacturing (CRM-2048 / C-1001), which is the customer that holds the NS-500 pricing exceptions.
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, with a list price of $85,000. That matches the list value on the request.
4. **Contract and exception:** I found no contract or pricing exception for Acme Industrial Supply Co. covering NS-EDGE. The request doesn't rely on one, so that doesn't matter here.
5. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy, which drops the Account Executive limit to 5%, only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
6. **Authority:** "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%." The Approval Authority Matrix lists Enterprise Account Executive as ≤10%. Sarah Chen holds that title (EMP-101), so 8% is within her limit. Under this policy, written approval evidence is only required above 10%.
