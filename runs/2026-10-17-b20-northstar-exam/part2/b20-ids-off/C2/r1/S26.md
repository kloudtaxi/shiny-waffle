## Answer

**Yes, with authorization. Sarah Chen can't approve CR-9201 herself.** Acme qualifies for the $400,000 limit, but Priya Shah, the Finance Manager, must approve it.

**1. The request.** Northstar ERP records CR-9201 exactly as you submitted it. Customer C-1001, limit $250,000 → $400,000, raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23 for the third-plant ramp-up. Status is "Pending Approval." The ERP record cites no credit support. Sarah separately wrote that "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it," but Acme doesn't need it (see point 5).

**2. The customer.** C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the ERP and "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) in the CRM, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. Both records share D-U-N-S number 04-812-7730 and the same Milwaukee address. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044).

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes:
- INV-60412 ($85,000) was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, early.
- INV-62011 ($480,000) was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, early.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) is due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it falls outside the test.

**5. The maximum limit.** The CRM records Acme as a Strategic account, and the maximum for that tier is $750,000. $400,000 fits within that on Acme's own standing. The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) would also qualify: it names Acme Mfg. Holdings, is given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. for up to $400,000, and runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It would raise the maximum to $1,150,000, but the decision doesn't depend on it.

**6. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives that role no authority to approve credit limits.

**7. Who must approve.** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval. That role is held by Priya Shah. VP Sales concurrence applies only to Strategic limits above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.

**Worth a look before approving:** INV-62305 ($720,000) has no payment date on record. If it's still unpaid, it was 16 days overdue at today's date of 2026-10-18. That doesn't fail the policy test, but the open balance is above both the current and the requested limit.
