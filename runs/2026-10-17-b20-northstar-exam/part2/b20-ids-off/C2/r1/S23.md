## Answer

No, Sarah can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on NS-500, but under the 2026 policy anything above 10% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres, to approve it. Before anyone acts, the request date also has to be corrected.

**Request date doesn't match.** You submitted the date as **2025-09-23**, but the CRM record for DR-9001 says **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email to Michael Torres about DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23. I decided on the CRM date, as the procedure requires. The difference matters: under the 2025 policy, account executives could approve up to 15% themselves, so if the 2025 date were right, Sarah could have approved this on her own. All other figures in the submission match the CRM record.

1. **Request:** DR-9001 from Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive, EMP-101). 15% off NS-500, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. Status: Pending Approval.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It lists NS-500 and says Acme can get "discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, up to 15% of list price, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10. It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee". The requested 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200, Sarah's manager). Under the policy, the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
