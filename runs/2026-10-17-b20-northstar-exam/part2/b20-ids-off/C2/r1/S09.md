> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? ## Answer

I've checked the request, the contract, the pricing exceptions and the 2025 pricing policy. **No: Sarah Chen could not have approved this 15% discount on 2025-03-31.** The problem isn't her authority. The contract terms the request relies on weren't in effect until the next day.

**What I found**
- **The request isn't in the system of record.** DR-9101 doesn't exist in the CRM. A similar request, DR-9001, does exist: same account, product, 15% and amounts, also from Sarah Chen, but dated 2026-09-23. I haven't treated it as this one, so I've decided on DR-9101 as you gave it.
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which the ERP calls Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). Sarah Chen is the account owner.
- **Product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Contract:** The request cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025). That agreement lets Acme get up to 15% off NS-500, but it runs from **2025-04-01** to 2028-03-31. On 2025-03-31 it wasn't yet in force.
- **Pricing exception:** The 15% exception for NS-500 (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also starts on **2025-04-01**. It replaces an earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, only for requests dated on or after that day. So on 2025-03-31 the earlier exception still applied, under a 2023 agreement (ACME-SA-2023). The knowledge base doesn't record that exception's maximum discount or end date. Its name suggests 10%, but I haven't assumed that. If it was 10%, the outcome would become "reject or escalate", because a higher approver can't approve more than the customer is eligible for.
- **Policy and authority:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives like Sarah can approve discounts up to and including 15%; anything above 15% needs the VP Sales. So 15% was within her own authority. But having the authority isn't enough when the customer wasn't eligible for the discount on that date.

**Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The terms the request relies on didn't cover 2025-03-31, and terms can't be carried back to an earlier date. It needs commercial review: either the request is re-dated on or after 2025-04-01, or the earlier exception's terms are produced. The DR-9101 record also has to be confirmed first.
