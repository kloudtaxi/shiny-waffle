## Answer

**No, CR-9203 can't be approved yet. I need the guarantee document first (outcome: `REQUEST_EVIDENCE`).** Without the guarantee, the most Acme can have is $750,000, and $1,000,000 is above that.

1. **The request.** CR-9203 asks to raise Acme's limit from $250,000 to $1,000,000. Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive, Sales) submitted it on 2026-09-23, and it is Pending Approval. It rests on "the Acme Group parent guarantee", so it depends on a third party's credit support. I couldn't find CR-9203 in the knowledge base, so I'm deciding on the ERP record you gave.
2. **The customer.** ERP account C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings"), CRM account CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing", tier **Strategic**) and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. Its parent is recorded as **Acme Group Holdings, Inc.**
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history.** I found no Acme (C-1001) invoices among INV-30001 to INV-30020. The knowledge base lists 30 invoices, but I couldn't retrieve the other 10, so this check isn't finished.
5. **The maximum limit.** The maximum for a Strategic account is **$750,000**. A parent-company guarantee in force raises that by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names. Nothing establishes such a guarantee for Acme:
   - The only evidence is an informal note that "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it" and "Gina says it's still in place".
   - No guarantee document gives the guarantor's legal name, the company covered, the amount or the dates.
   - The note names "Acme Group", while the recorded parent is "Acme Group Holdings, Inc.". It isn't confirmed they are the same company.
   - Without the guarantee, $1,000,000 is $250,000 over the maximum. If it isn't produced, or doesn't cover enough, the request must be rejected or escalated. A more senior approver can't fix that.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She has none under the policy. Only Accounts Receivable Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager (up to $500,000) and the Director of Finance (above $500,000) may approve limits.
7. **Required approvers.** A limit above $500,000 needs the **Director of Finance**. Because Acme is Strategic, it also needs the **VP Sales** to agree. The knowledge base doesn't say who holds either role.
