## Answer

**SR-39207 has no breach and Northstar owes no service credit.** It is a Severity 2 ticket and met both targets. The restoration target was met with only 15 minutes to spare, and only because Memorial Day doesn't count on the clock.

**1. Customer.** The ticket's CRM account CRM-2063 is Cedar Health Systems, DUNS 02-906-4418. That DUNS belongs to Cedar Health Systems Inc. (ERP customer C-1003), the customer named in support terms SUP-CHS-2025.

**2. Scope: covered.** SUP-CHS-2025 covers NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD) on the Silver plan, at $4,000 a month. Coverage started 2025-12-01, before the ticket opened on 2026-05-22.

**3. True severity: Severity 2.** The ticket records that NS-Cloud is down at 2 of 14 facilities (Fishers and Carmel). The other 12 are working normally, and operations at both affected centres are continuing. The severity guide puts "NS-Cloud is unavailable to some, but not all, of the customer's users or sites" at Severity 2. Severity 1 needs operations stopped or every user affected, and neither applies. The customer's reported P2 happens to match.

**4. Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened 2026-05-22 16:20 −04:00, which is 15:20 CT on a Friday, so schedule v2.0 applies. For Severity 2 on the Silver plan, the targets are 2 business hours to respond and 12 business hours to restore. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, excluding Northstar holidays.

**5. Clock.** The clock started when the ticket opened, at 15:20 CT. No pauses, maintenance windows or claimed exclusions are recorded. Monday 2026-05-25 is Memorial Day, a listed Northstar holiday.

**7. Credits: $0.** The customer's portal claim arrived on 2026-06-01, within the 30-day window that ends 2026-06-25. With no missed target, though, no credit applies, so the 30% monthly cap doesn't matter.
