## Answer

No. Michael Torres has the authority for a 12% discount, but nothing makes BlueRiver eligible for the special pricing this request relies on, so it can't be approved as submitted. **Outcome: REVIEW_REQUIRED.**

**1. The request.** DR-9107: 12% off NS-500, $250,000 list, so $30,000 off and $220,000 net. Dated 2026-09-23 and submitted by michael.torres@northstar.example. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing. I couldn't find DR-9107 in the knowledge base, so the request is decided as you gave it.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, whose legal name is Blue River Logistics LLC (St. Louis, MO). The account owner is Kelly Floyd.

**3. The product.** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Active contract.** The only BlueRiver agreement on file is its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025): a Silver support plan, $2,500 a month, covering NS-Cloud only. It has no pricing or discount terms, and nothing in the knowledge base gives BlueRiver a supply or pricing agreement for NS-500.

**5. Pricing exception.** The agreement the request cites, ACME-MFG-2025, is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings (ACME Manufacturing, CRM-2048 / C-1001). It runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and allows up to 15% on NS-500, with the exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 attached as Schedule B. Those terms belong to Acme only and don't carry over to BlueRiver. Even for Acme, they set eligibility only: they say each discount still needs approval under Northstar's own policies. BlueRiver has no exception for NS-500.

**6. Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in force on the request date (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), along with its Approval Authority Matrix.

**7–8. Authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales (EMP-200). VP Sales approves discounts above 10% and up to 20%, so 12% is within his own limit. But authority is a separate question from eligibility, and having it doesn't fix the borrowed contract basis. The policy also requires contract-specific pricing exceptions to be reviewed.

**9. Decision.** Commercial review should decide whether BlueRiver gets terms of its own, or whether the request is resubmitted as a standard-pricing discount without the Acme reference. Torres is also both the requester and the approver; the knowledge base has no rule on self-approval, so I've only noted it.
