Anything above 15% needed VP Sales approval, a role held by Michael Torres, her manager. Separately, I couldn't find request DR-9103 itself in the knowledge base, so that has to be confirmed before anyone acts on it.

1. **The request.** No record of DR-9103 exists. Searching by name and as a CRM identifier both found nothing, so this decision is based on the request as you gave it. Its stated reason is "Competitive" and it cites no contract term or exception, so I treated it as standard pricing.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which the ERP lists as Acme Mfg. Holdings (customer C-1001). Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. The request's figures check out: 22% of $85,000 is $18,700, leaving $66,300.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-EDGE at list price, less any discount agreed in an order. Each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies. The contract only sets a discount ceiling (15%) for the NS-500, not for NS-EDGE.
5. **Pricing exceptions.** Both Acme exceptions (Strategic Account and Volume Pricing, the latter in contract Schedule B) cover only the NS-500. Nothing special applies to NS-EDGE, and since the request doesn't claim any special terms, it is judged under standard pricing.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in effect from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. The Approval Authority Matrix and its 10% / 20% / Chief Revenue Officer bands only started applying on 2026-01-01, so they don't apply here.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** Up to and including 15%. A 22% discount is beyond that.
8. **Required approver.** The 2025 policy says discounts over 15% need VP Sales approval and sets no higher band, so it's VP Sales: Michael Torres. The knowledge base doesn't give the dates he has held the role, so it's worth checking he held it on 2025-09-23.

**Decision:** Approve with authorization: Michael Torres, as VP Sales, has to approve it. Nothing should proceed until DR-9103 is confirmed to exist in Northstar CRM.
