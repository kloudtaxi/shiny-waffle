## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 by herself.** Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it. Acme is eligible for the 15%, but that's beyond her own approval limit.

**The date matters.** Your question says 2025-09-23, but Northstar CRM records DR-9001 on **2026-09-23**, and so does her email logging it. I've decided on the CRM date. Had the request really been dated 2025-09-23, the 2025 policy would have let her approve up to 15% herself.

- **Request:** DR-9001, 15% off NS-500. List price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. Raised by Sarah Chen, Enterprise Account Executive, citing contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. A service ticket for "ACME Mfg" carries that account number, and Sarah owns the account.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500, and it says each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers NS-500 only, up to 15% of list price, for the same dates. David Morgan approved it. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests from 2025-04-01. So 15% is exactly at Acme's limit. The exception also says it doesn't give any employee approval authority.
- **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers 2026. Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales. Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. The Approval Authority Matrix says the same.
- **Approver:** Michael Torres is VP Sales and Sarah's manager. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
