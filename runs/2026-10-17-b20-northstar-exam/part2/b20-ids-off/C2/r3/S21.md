> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2027-02-01? ## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on the NS-500, but under the 2027 pricing policy she can only approve up to 5%. A 15% discount needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. One thing must be fixed first: request DR-9113 isn't in the knowledge base, so it has to be confirmed in Northstar CRM before anyone acts on it.

1. **Request:** 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net) on the NS-500, dated 2027-02-01, from Sarah Chen. The figures add up. It relies on contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which is billed in the ERP as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). "Acme Industrial Supply Co." is a different company. The knowledge base also wrongly attaches CRM-2048 to a service ticket (SR-39385), which I ignored.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on the request date. It says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement, covering NS-500 only, up to 15%, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the older 10% exception and "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So 15% is exactly at Acme's maximum.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 (2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31) replaces the 2026 policy in full. The 2026 rules (Account Executives up to 10%, VP Sales up to 20%) and the 2026 approval matrix don't apply.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and they can approve discounts up to and including 5%. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Over 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. That's David Morgan (EMP-300), who also approved the exception. Sarah's manager, Michael Torres (VP Sales), can only approve up to 12%, so he isn't enough.

Two other points. Any discount over 5% needs the approval recorded as evidence. And who holds each role comes from undated employee records, while the request date is still in the future, so I've noted (but not made a blocker) that someone should confirm Morgan is still CRO on that date.
