I haven't used it in place of this request. I decided on the request as you gave it. The justification is "Competitive," so it relies on standard pricing, not on a contract or pricing exception.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in CRM. It is not Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048 / ERP C-1001). The account owner in CRM is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen. 3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), list price $85,000. 8% of that is $6,800, leaving $78,200, which matches the submitted figures.
4. **Contract and pricing exception:** Neither applies, because the request relies on standard pricing.
5. **The policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies on 2026-09-23 (it runs 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, which cuts the account executive limit to 5%, only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
6. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under the 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix, that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 8% is within her limit, so no VP Sales or Chief Revenue Officer approval is needed.
