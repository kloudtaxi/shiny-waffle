## Question (verbatim)

> Ticket SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data lost for six presses, reported P1): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge): true severity is 2, not P1. No service level was breached, no credit is owed, and Acme had nothing left to do. But the restoration target was met with no time to spare. I also couldn't find what the escalation procedure requires for a Severity 2 ticket.**

**1. Customer.** The ticket's account, CRM-2048, is Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. Its ERP customer number is C-1001 and its DUNS is 04-812-7730. Acme's parent company and Acme Industrial Supply Co. are not covered.

**2. Scope: covered.** Acme's support terms (Schedule C, SUP-ACME-C) list NS-Edge as a covered product on the Platinum plan. Coverage started 2025-04-01 00:00 CT, and the ticket was opened 2026-07-23 13:10 CDT.

**3. True severity: 2 (High).** The ticket records that vibration data was lost for presses 1–6 (of 14) at Milwaukee Plant 2. The presses kept running, and technicians checked them by hand every two hours. The severity guide puts this in Severity 2: a condition-monitoring function is unavailable for one or more machines, "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks". It isn't Severity 1, because production didn't stop.

**4. Schedule and targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies, because the ticket was opened after 2026-04-01 00:00 CT. Times are in Central Time; every ticket timestamp is already −05:00 (Central daylight time). For Severity 2 on Platinum:
- initial response within 2 hours;
- restoration within 12 hours;
- both clocks run 24×7.

**5–6. Clock and measurement**
| Event | Time (CDT) |
|---|---|
| Ticket opened (portal), clock starts | 2026-07-23 13:10 |
| Engineer responds, asks Acme for VPN credentials, status set to Awaiting Customer (pause starts) | 2026-07-23 15:00 |
| Credentials provided (Kelly Ballard, Acme IT), status set to In Progress (pause ends) | 2026-07-24 08:30 |
| Restored | 2026-07-24 18:40 |

- **Response: met.** It took 1 h 50 min against a 2-hour target. The response clock doesn't pause.
- **Restoration: met.** The pause counts because both its start and end are in the ticket, so 17 h 30 min comes off. Elapsed time is 1 h 50 min plus 10 h 10 min, which is exactly 12 h 00 min. A miss means "more than the target", so exactly 12 hours meets it. One more minute would have been a band-1 miss.
- No maintenance-window exclusion was recorded or needed.

**7. Credits: $0.** With no missed target, no credit applies, and the record shows no credit claim for this ticket.

**8. Obligations**
- **Northstar:** With no breach, there is no claim to decide and no credit memo to issue. The knowledge base holds only fragments of the escalation procedure: deadlines are the "latest compliant time", and one clause ends "…Escalation Manager no later than that moment". It doesn't give the Severity 2 rules for who to engage or notify, or when, so I haven't guessed them. I also found no rule on whether a root-cause finding is due. The current role holders, in case those steps apply, are:
  - Hannah Lindqvist, Support Escalation Manager
  - Owen Takahashi, Support Engineering Lead
  - Marcus Adeyemi, Director of Customer Support
- **Acme:** It had to provide the information and access Northstar needed, and it did when Kelly Ballard supplied the VPN credentials. The telephone-report rule applies only to Severity 1, so it doesn't apply here. No claim is needed.
