## Answer

**Answer: Yes, on the merits, but not by Sarah Chen alone, and not until the request record is sorted out.** Acme qualifies for a $650,000 limit. The policy requires Elena Novak, Director of Finance, to approve it, with Michael Torres, VP Sales, also signing off. One problem blocks action first: **CR-9202 isn't in the system of record.** Northstar ERP holds a different request for Acme, CR-9201, for **$400,000**. It has the same customer (C-1001), current limit ($250,000), requestor, date (2026-09-23) and justification, and is also "Pending Approval". As instructed, I decided on CR-9202 as you gave it, not on CR-9201.

1. **The request:** CR-9202 for customer C-1001, raising the limit from $250,000 to $650,000, dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. It rests on Acme's own standing; the justification is the third-plant ramp-up and doesn't mention a guarantee.
2. **The customer:** C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in ERP, CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" in CRM, and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. Legal documents say Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091). The CRM tier is **Strategic**.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** the rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid more than 30 days late, or be unpaid more than 30 days past due. Acme has no late invoices:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) falls due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so the rule doesn't apply to it.
5. **Maximum limit:** $750,000 for Strategic accounts, and $650,000 is within it. Acme Group Holdings has a parent guarantee for Acme Mfg. Holdings, but the request doesn't rely on it and doesn't need it.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** none. She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and the policy gives no Sales role approval authority. Authority is judged on the new total, $650,000.
7. **Required approvers:** limits over $500,000 need the Director of Finance (Elena Novak, EMP-402). Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need the VP Sales to agree (Michael Torres, EMP-200).
