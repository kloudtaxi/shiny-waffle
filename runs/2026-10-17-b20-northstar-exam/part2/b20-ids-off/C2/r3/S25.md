## Answer

**Not on Sarah's authority alone.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but Sarah can only approve up to 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 is in the system of record (discount_requests.csv) and matches what was submitted exactly: account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". The request relies on the "Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on contract terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings": same address and DUNS number (04-812-7730), and it trades as Acme Manufacturing. One oddity: looking up CRM-2048 also returns a service ticket record (SR-39385). That doesn't affect the decision.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It covers NS-500 and says customer eligibility is "up to 15%". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It covers NS-500 only, sets a maximum of 15% of list price, and was approved by David Morgan. It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which only covered orders up to 2025-03-31. The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The 15% requested equals the maximum, so the customer is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix were in force on the request date (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, which has lower limits, only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over that limit, and the customer's contract doesn't change her authority.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager.
9. **Decision:** **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** The policy says approvals above 10% need recorded approval evidence and must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
