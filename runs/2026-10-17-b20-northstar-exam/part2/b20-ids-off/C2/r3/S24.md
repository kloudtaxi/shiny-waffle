## Answer

**DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen alone.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on NS-500, but 15% is more than her own limit. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.

**One problem with the submission:** it says the status is "Approved", but the CRM record says **"Pending Approval"**. Nobody has approved this yet, so it must not be treated as approved. All other figures match the CRM record.

1. **The request (CRM record):** 15% off NS-500. List price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. Requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings). The two records share DUNS number 04-812-7730 and an address, and the contract says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500 and allows discounts of up to 15% on it. It also says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a maximum of 15% off list. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ran out on 2025-03-31. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The 15% request is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the Approval Authority Matrix that goes with it. The 2027 policy starts on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200). Sarah reports to him. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
