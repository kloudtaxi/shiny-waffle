## Answer

**Short answer: no, not as the request stands.** The 8% is within Sarah Chen's own authority. The problem is the reason given for it. The request says it is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", but that agreement hadn't started on 2025-03-31. The request also isn't in the system of record. So the decision is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**, not approval.

**1. The request.** I couldn't find DR-9110 in the knowledge base (lookup returned "not_known"). I've decided on the request as you submitted it: Acme Manufacturing, NS-500, 8% (US$20,000 off US$250,000, which matches), dated 2025-03-31, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example. It relies on a contract term, not standard pricing.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is billed as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), with the same D-U-N-S number, 04-812-7730. Sarah Chen is the account owner. One oddity: the CRM-2048 lookup also points to a service ticket, SR-39385. That looks like a data-quality issue.

**3. The product.** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. An active contract on 2025-03-31: not established.**
- The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from **2025-04-01** to 2028-03-31. Its 15% NS-500 term didn't exist yet on 2025-03-31. It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
- There is an older Acme agreement, ACME-SA-2023, but the knowledge base has no dates or terms for it.

**5. A pricing exception for this product on this date: not established.**
- EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement, maximum 15%, NS-500 only) also starts **2025-04-01**.
- The exception it replaces, EXC-ACME-NS500-10 (Volume Pricing Exception under ACME-SA-2023, status "Active"), may be the one that applied on 2025-03-31. But the knowledge base has no maximum discount or dates for it, and the request doesn't cite it.

**6–8. Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applies (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts "up to and including 15%"; only discounts above 15% need VP Sales. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 8% is within her own limit.

**9. Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** Her authority isn't the issue; the cited terms are. Special terms don't carry back to a date before they start, and the policy says contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed. Commercial review should either:
- move the request to on or after 2025-04-01, when the agreement and the 15% exception apply (8% would then fall within both the exception and her authority), or
- show which terms actually applied on 2025-03-31, most likely EXC-ACME-NS500-10, using its source document, or
- resubmit it on standard pricing.
