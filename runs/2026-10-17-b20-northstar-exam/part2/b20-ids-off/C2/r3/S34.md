Elena Novak (Director of Finance) has to approve it, with VP Sales concurrence. Two open issues block it.

**Open issues**
1. **CR-9208 isn't in the ERP.** The knowledge base has no CR-9208. The only credit request on file for C-1001 is **CR-9201**: Sarah Chen, $250,000 → $400,000, dated 2026-09-23, Pending Approval, with the same justification word for word. I've decided on CR-9208 as you gave it, not on CR-9201. Someone needs to confirm whether CR-9208 is a real separate request or a mis-keyed copy of CR-9201.
2. **Torres would be concurring on his own request.** For Strategic accounts above $500,000, the 2026 policy requires VP Sales concurrence. Torres is the VP Sales and also the requestor. The 2025 policy said "No one may approve or concur on a credit request they submitted." The 2026 policy replaces it in full, and I couldn't find that rule in it. Finance should confirm whether he can concur on his own request, or name someone independent.

**The checks**
- **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing is the trading name of Acme Mfg. Holdings. That's ERP C-1001 and CRM-2048, rated Strategic in the CRM. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044).
- **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
- **Payment history** (no invoice due in the prior 12 months paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days late): none fail.
  - INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
  - INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.
  - INV-62305 ($720,000): no payment recorded, but it isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
- **Maximum limit:** $750,000 for Strategic accounts, so $650,000 fits on Acme's own standing. The request doesn't rely on the parent guarantee. That guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc.) names Acme Mfg. Holdings for $400,000 from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, which would raise the maximum to $1,150,000.
- **Who approves:** Torres has no credit approval authority under the 2026 policy. Approval goes by the new total of $650,000. Anything above $500,000 needs Director of Finance approval, which is Elena Novak, plus VP Sales concurrence.
