## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen could not have approved DR-9101 on 2025-03-31. The problem isn't her authority. It's that Acme wasn't eligible for 15% on that date.

**The request.** DR-9101 asks for 15% off NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, billed in the ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings, C-1001). The list value is $250,000 and the discount is $37,500. Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive, raised it on 2025-03-31, citing the Acme Master Supply Agreement. **I couldn't find DR-9101 in the knowledge base**, so I've judged it as you gave it and made confirming it a blocking condition.

**Which contract terms applied on 2025-03-31:**
- **The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025)** allows up to 15% on NS-500. But it takes effect on 2025-04-01, one day after the request. Its 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also starts 2025-04-01, and it only replaces the old exception for requests dated 2025-04-01 or later. So the agreement the request relies on did not apply on the request date.
- **The terms that did apply** were the older volume pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023, status "Active"). It allows "up to **10%** off list price" on NS-500 "for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31". 2025-03-31 is the last day it covers.
- So Acme's ceiling on that date was **10%**, and 15% is over it.

**Authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31) lets Enterprise Account Executives approve "up to and including 15%". Only discounts over 15% need VP Sales. Sarah had the authority for 15%. That doesn't help, because her authority doesn't change what the customer is entitled to. A more senior approver can't fix this either. It needs new or amended terms, or the order dated under the new agreement.

**Note:** the request is dated one day before the new agreement starts. If the order was actually meant for 2025-04-01 or later, Acme would be eligible for 15% and Sarah could approve it herself under the 2025 policy. That would need a corrected or resubmitted request, so I've noted it in the record.
