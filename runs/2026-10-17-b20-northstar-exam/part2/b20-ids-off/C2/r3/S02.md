## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** The CRM record for DR-9001 shows 15%, not 18%, and 15% is the most Acme's contract allows on the NS-500. So the decision is made on the CRM's 15%: it can be approved, but only with sign-off from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**The request.** The CRM record for DR-9001 (account CRM-2048, NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen, status "Pending Approval") shows **15.0%**: $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, net $212,500. The figures you sent (18%, $45,000 off, net $205,000) don't match the CRM. I've decided on the CRM's 15% and logged the gap as a blocking condition. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so this relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.

**Customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730). It is not "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a different company.

**Contract and exception.**
- The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
- Schedule B to that agreement is pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers the NS-500 only, for the same dates, with a 15% maximum. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and does not give any employee approval authority.
- 15% is right at that limit, so it's allowed. 18% would go over it, and a more senior approver couldn't fix that. It would need new or amended contract terms.

**Policy and authority.**
- Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above that.
- The band from over 10% up to 20% needs **VP Sales** approval. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), her manager.
- Approvals above 10% have to be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Before anyone acts on this:**
- **Blocking:** Sarah Chen and Revenue Operations need to confirm the 15% / $37,500 / $212,500 in the CRM and correct the 18% figures you sent.
- **Not blocking:** The CRM row also has a 2026-09-23 date stored as an "expiration date". That's already past as of today (2026-10-18). Ask Revenue Operations whether the request or quote is still valid.
