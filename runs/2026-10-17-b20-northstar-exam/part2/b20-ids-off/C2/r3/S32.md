## Answer

**No, CR-9207 can't be approved as it stands.** It depends on the Acme parent guarantee, and the only guarantee on file covers a different Acme company. The decision is to ask for evidence of a guarantee that names Acme Industrial Supply Co. before deciding.

**1. The request.** C-1044, from $50,000 to $500,000, dated 2026-09-23, raised by Sarah Chen. It relies on a guarantee, not the customer's own standing. CR-9207 is not in the knowledge base. Sarah Chen has another request on record, CR-9201, but I haven't treated it as this one. Whether CR-9207 exists in the ERP needs confirming.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1044 and CRM-2091 are both Acme Industrial Supply Co. (same D-U-N-S number, 09-552-1187; Akron, OH; Net 45). Its CRM tier is **Standard**. A document states that Acme Industrial Supply Co. "is a different company" from ACME Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001).

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or be still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. The two C-1044 invoices I found were both due before that window:
- INV-30001 was due 2025-02-25 and paid 2025-03-04.
- INV-30003 was due 2025-05-07 and paid 2025-04-28.

I found no late invoices in the window. Some ERP invoice records didn't show up in search, so this check may not be complete.

**5. Maximum limit.** The Standard maximum is **$250,000**. A parent guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, but only "for the customer the guarantee names." The guarantee on file is GRT-ACME-2026, given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. for 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, capped at $400,000. It names **Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001)**, not C-1044.
- The only other source is a chat message saying "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it." That's an informal claim, not a guarantee.
- Without a guarantee, $500,000 is above the $250,000 maximum. A valid $400,000 guarantee naming this customer would raise the maximum to $650,000. That's why the outcome is to ask for evidence, not to reject.

**6. Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives her role no approval authority.

**7. Required approver.** Limits above $100,000 up to and including $500,000 need **Finance Manager** approval. VP Sales concurrence applies only to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed here. I couldn't find who holds the Finance Manager role.
