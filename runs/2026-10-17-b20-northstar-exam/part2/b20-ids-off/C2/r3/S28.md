## Answer

**Short answer: yes, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.** Acme qualifies for $1,000,000 under the 2026 policy once the parent guarantee is counted. The limit needs approval from Elena Novak (Director of Finance) and agreement ("concurrence") from Michael Torres (VP Sales). One thing has to happen first: I couldn't find CR-9203 in the knowledge base, so someone must confirm the request exists in Northstar ERP before anyone acts on it.

1. **The request:** I couldn't find CR-9203 anywhere in the knowledge base. The only request linked to Sarah Chen is CR-9201, and I haven't used it in its place. I've decided on the details you gave. The current limit of $250,000 is also unconfirmed, because no record in the knowledge base shows it.
2. **The customer:** ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which trades as Acme Manufacturing. In the CRM it's account CRM-2048, rated **Strategic**. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** the rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid early, on 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid early, on 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
5. **The maximum limit:** Strategic accounts can have up to $750,000. A parent-company guarantee in force raises that by the guaranteed amount, for the company the guarantee names.
   - The guarantee is GRT-ACME-2026, given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names its wholly owned subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings, which is this customer.
   - It covers up to $400,000 and is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31.
   - The maximum is therefore $1,150,000, which is above the $1,000,000 requested.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** she's an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives her role no authority to approve credit limits.
7. **Who must sign off:** limits over $500,000 need the Director of Finance, which is Elena Novak. Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need the VP Sales to agree, which is Michael Torres. Torres is Sarah Chen's manager, but I found no policy rule that stops him from giving that agreement.
