## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this, and nobody should approve it at 12% yet. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: the contract terms the request relies on don't cover NS-EDGE.

**What I checked**

1. **The request.** DR-9109 asks for 12% on NS-EDGE for $85,000 list, dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. The figures add up: $10,200 off leaves $74,800. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing. **DR-9109 is not in the knowledge base**, so I've decided on the request as you gave it and made confirming the record a blocking condition.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings, DUNS 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen is the account owner. One oddity: CRM-2048 is also attached to a service ticket, SR-39385. That looks like a tagging error and doesn't affect the decision.
3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package.
4. **Active contract: yes.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and lists NS-EDGE as a supplied product. But its prices are "Northstar's current list prices… less any discount agreed in an Order", and each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies." The only discount the agreement allows is up to 15% "on the NS-500 Industrial Controller."
5. **Pricing exception: none for this product.** Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, 15% maximum, effective to 2028-03-31) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 has been replaced and is also NS-500 only.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), with the Approval Authority Matrix as its working reference. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority.** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to 10%. 12% is over her limit.
8. **Approver for 12%.** The over-10% up to 20% band belongs to VP Sales, which is Michael Torres, her manager.

**Why it isn't APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION:** the 15% contract eligibility is for NS-500 and doesn't carry over to NS-EDGE. A VP signature can't fix a request that has no contract basis for this product. It needs commercial review, which the 2026 policy also requires for contract-specific pricing exceptions. That review could resubmit it as a standard-pricing request, which would then need Michael Torres's approval at 12%, or set up new terms for NS-EDGE. Even if the basis were valid, Sarah couldn't approve 12% herself.
