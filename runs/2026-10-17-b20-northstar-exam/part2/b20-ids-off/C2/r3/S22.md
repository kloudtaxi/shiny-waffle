## Answer

**Not on Sarah Chen's own authority.** DR-9001 can be approved only if Michael Torres, VP Sales, signs off. And the figures you sent don't match the CRM record, so that difference has to be resolved first.

**The CRM record differs from what you submitted.** CRM's DR-9001 row shows **15.0%** off a $250,000 list price: a $37,500 discount, leaving $212,500. Your version shows 8.0%, $20,000 and $230,000. Everything else matches: account CRM-2048, product NS-500, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, the justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", and status Pending Approval. I decided on the CRM figure of 15% and recorded the mismatch as a blocking condition. If 8% turns out to be correct, it's within Sarah's 10% limit and she could approve it herself.

**What I checked**
1. **Request:** it relies on a contract term, the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". It trades as Acme Manufacturing, with D-U-N-S 04-812-7730.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** the Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows up to 15% on NS-500, but says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers **NS-500 only**, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of **15% of list price**. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which applied to orders through 2025-03-31. At 15%, the request is at the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), with the Approval Authority Matrix as its working reference. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** as an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200), her manager. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is eligible for 15% under the exception, but Michael Torres must give the approval and it must be recorded in CRM.
