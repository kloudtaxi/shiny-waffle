## Answer

**Yes, Sarah Chen can approve this herself: 6% is within her own 10% limit. But DR-9111 isn't in the system of record, so confirm the request exists before anyone acts on it.**

1. **The request:** DR-9111, as you gave it. I couldn't find it under Northstar CRM or CRM, or by searching for its ID. The knowledge base has other requests (DR-7001 to DR-7019, DR-9001), but not this one. I decided on the request as given, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition. The reason given is "Competitive", so it relies on standard pricing, not on any special contract terms.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP the same customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), DUNS 04-812-7730. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product:** NS-CLOUD is NS-Cloud, the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. Acme was evaluating it and planning a pilot.
4. **Contract and pricing exceptions:** The request doesn't rely on either. Acme's known preferential pricing (about 15%, from the 2025 agreement) covers only the NS-500 controller. Pricing exceptions only cover the product and dates they name, so it gives no special eligibility on NS-Cloud. None is needed for a standard 6% discount.
5. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (PRICING-POLICY-2026) is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, so it covers 2026-09-23.
6. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). The policy says: "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%". 6% is within that. VP Sales approval only applies above 10% (up to 20%), and the Chief Revenue Officer above 20%. The extra "recorded approval evidence" rule also only applies above 10%.
7. **The arithmetic:** 6% of $120,000 is $7,200, leaving a net of $112,800. These match the figures you gave.

One thing to watch: the 2027 policy cuts Account Executive authority to 5%. It only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01, so it doesn't affect this one.
