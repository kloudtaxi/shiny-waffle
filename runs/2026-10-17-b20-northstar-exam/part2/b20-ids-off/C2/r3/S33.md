I can't approve CR-9203 yet. The request depends on the Acme Group parent guarantee, and I couldn't find any guarantee that names Acme Manufacturing. The outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**: the guarantee document has to be provided before the request can be decided.

**1. The request.** CR-9203, ERP customer C-1001, dated 2026-09-23 and submitted by Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive, Sales). It asks to raise the limit from $250,000 to $1,000,000. Its stated basis is the Acme Group parent guarantee. I couldn't find CR-9203 itself in the knowledge base, so the figures above come from the ERP record you supplied.

**2. The customer.** The ERP record for C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings". It is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730, and same address, 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee). The CRM tier is **Strategic**. Its parent is recorded as Acme Group Holdings, Inc.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or remain unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. I checked invoices INV-30001 to INV-30020 and none is billed to C-1001. The search said there were 30 matches but returned only those 20, so I can't confirm the payment history either way.

**5. The maximum limit.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. Under the 2026 policy, a parent guarantee in force raises that maximum by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names. $1,000,000 is above $750,000, so the request only works if a guarantee of at least $250,000 is in force for Acme Mfg. Holdings on 2026-09-23.
- The only evidence of a guarantee is one email line: "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it." That's an assumption, not the agreement. Nothing shows who signed it, which company it names, how much it covers, or when it's in force.
- The only legal document that mentions the parent is Schedule C (Support and Service Levels). It says "Coverage does not extend to the Customer's parent, Acme Group Holdings, Inc." That concerns service-level coverage, not credit, and doesn't establish a guarantee.

**6. The requestor's authority.** None. Sarah Chen is in Sales and holds no approval authority under the 2026 policy.

**7. Required approvers.** Authority applies to the new total limit of $1,000,000:
- Over $500,000 needs the Director of Finance: **Elena Novak**.
- Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence: **Michael Torres**, Sarah Chen's manager.

The 2026 policy facts I found don't repeat the 2025 rule that no one may approve or concur on their own request. Even if it applies, neither approver submitted this request.

**8. Decision.** The request relies on a guarantee that no available evidence establishes for this customer, so the outcome is REQUEST_EVIDENCE. Without the guarantee, $1,000,000 is over the $750,000 maximum. In that case the request would be rejected, and a higher approver couldn't change that.
