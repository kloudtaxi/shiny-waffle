## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve 15% on DR-9001, and a higher approver can't fix it either. The request relies on Acme's contract pricing, but the only 15% term Acme has covers the NS-500 controller, not NS-Cloud. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**.

1. **The request.** DR-9001: Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), NS-CLOUD, 15% off a $120,000 list price, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.
   - **Possible mismatch:** the email that logged DR-9001 in the CRM has the subject "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001". I couldn't read the CRM row itself, so whether DR-9001 is recorded as NS-500 or NS-CLOUD needs confirming. I decided on the request as you gave it.
2. **Customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same D-U-N-S number, 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product.** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000. The list value on the request matches.
4. **Active contract.** Yes. The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and lists NS-CLOUD as a supplied product. Its pricing is "Northstar's current list prices … less any discount agreed in an Order", and "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies". The only discount right it grants is up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
5. **Pricing exception.** None covers NS-CLOUD. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, maximum 15%) covers the "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product". Schedule C also says NS-Cloud "could be added only by a signed amendment to this Schedule", and I found no amendment.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), with the Approval Authority Matrix for Enterprise Discounts.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, which can approve up to 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver.** Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Under the standard 15% would need his sign-off anyway. That doesn't fix the missing contract cover.
9. **Decision.** Special terms don't carry over to another product, so the request needs commercial review. Two ways forward: a signed amendment or new exception that covers NS-CLOUD, or resubmitting it as a standard-pricing request that the VP Sales approves.
