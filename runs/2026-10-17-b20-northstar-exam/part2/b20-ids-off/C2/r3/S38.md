Those terms put the customer on the Silver plan at $2,500 a month. They cover NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD), and coverage began 2025-01-01, so the ticket is in scope.

**True severity: Severity 1 (Critical).** The ticket records that dispatch "can't see any shipments" and that there is "No workaround". The severity guide places "NS-Cloud unavailable to all of the customer's users" and a dispatch activity that cannot continue in Severity 1. This matches the P1 the customer reported.

**Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened 2026-07-11 at 23:20 Central (CDT, UTC−5), so Schedule v2.0 applies; it covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01. Severity 1 runs on a 24×7 clock, with a 30-minute initial response target and a 4-hour restoration target.

**When the clock started.** Maintenance notice MN-2607 covered NS-Cloud for this account, for a window from 2026-07-11 22:00 to 2026-07-12 02:00 CDT.
- **The window qualifies.** The notice went out Friday 2026-06-26. The business days after that, up to the window's start, are June 29 and 30, July 1 and 2, and July 6 to 10. July 3 was the observed Independence Day holiday. That is 9 business days, more than the 5 required.
- **The clock started at the window's end.** A ticket opened inside a qualifying window starts both clocks when the window ends, even if the maintenance caused the incident. The ticket says it did: "caused by the upgrade performed in window MN-2607". The clock therefore started at 2026-07-12T02:00-05:00.
- **No pauses or other exclusions apply.** None is recorded, and none would change the result.

**Measurement.**
