1. **The request:** DR-9105. Sarah Chen asked for 15% off NS-500 on 2026-09-23, citing "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". So it depends on contract terms, not standard pricing. **The knowledge base has no record of DR-9105**, so I decided on the request as you gave it, and confirming that it exists is a blocking condition.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. (Akron, OH; DUNS 09-552-1187; ERP C-1044). It is a separate company from ACME Manufacturing (Acme Mfg. Holdings, CRM-2048 / ERP C-1001, Milwaukee).
3. **Contract and exceptions:** The Master Supply Agreement is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings. Its Schedule C says outright: "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered". The two NS-500 pricing exceptions (Strategic Account and Volume Pricing) are also for ACME Manufacturing. I found no contract or exception for Acme Industrial Supply, and special terms don't carry over from one customer to another.
4. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her own limit is 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, which is Michael Torres.
5. **Decision:** Michael Torres signing off wouldn't fix this, because the customer has no established eligibility. It needs commercial review on standard terms, or new terms for this customer. If it goes ahead on standard pricing, 15% still needs Michael Torres's approval, because it's over her 10% limit.
