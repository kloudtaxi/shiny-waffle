**True severity: Severity 1, not the P3 Cedar reported.** The record says all users at all facilities were affected and no workaround existed. The severity guide defines Severity 1 as NS-Cloud being unavailable to all of a customer's users with no workaround. Northstar reclassified the ticket to Severity 1, but the record doesn't say when.

**Schedule and clock.** The ticket opened 2026-06-10 07:05 EDT, which is 06:05 CDT, so Schedule v2.0 applies. Severity 1 runs on a 24×7 clock, with a 30-minute response target and a 4-hour restoration target.

**Pauses and exclusions.** None are recorded: no Awaiting Customer status, no maintenance notice and no customer-caused claim.

**Measurement:**

- **Restoration:** due 10:05 CDT. Service was restored at 11:35 CDT (12:35 EDT), 5 h 30 min after opening. That is more than 4 h but no more than 8 h, so it's a band 1 miss.

**Credits:**
- Severity 1 rates only apply if the customer phoned the Support hotline before restoration. Cedar opened the ticket in the portal, and Northstar calling Cedar doesn't count, so the Severity 2 rates apply.
- Restoration band 1 is 5% of $4,000, which is **$200**.
- If the response was late, 3% (**$120**) is added, for **$320**.
- The monthly cap is 30%, or $1,200. Cedar's other ticket I found, SR-39207, opened in May, so it doesn't count toward June's cap.

- **Root cause:** no root-cause finding is due, because no exclusion was asserted.

**What Cedar had to do:**
- **Phone the hotline** at (312) 555-0187 for a Severity 1 incident: **not met**.
- **Submit a written claim on time:** **met**.
- Keep a technical contact reachable by phone until restoration. The record says nothing either way.
