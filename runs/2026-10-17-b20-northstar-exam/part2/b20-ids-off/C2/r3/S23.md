## Answer

I've checked the CRM record, the contract, the pricing exception and the 2026 pricing policy, and I'm writing up the decision now.

**Decision: approve with authorization.** Acme is entitled to a 15% discount on the NS-500, but 15% is more than Sarah Chen can approve herself. Michael Torres, the VP Sales, has to sign it off.

**The request.** In the CRM record (the system of record), DR-9001 is for account CRM-2048, product NS-500, a 15.0% discount on a $250,000 list price. That's a $37,500 discount and a net price of $212,500. It was raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example, is dated **2026-09-23** and is "Pending Approval". The reason given is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms rather than standard pricing.

**The date doesn't match.** The request you sent says 2025-09-23, but the CRM record and Sarah's covering email both say 2026-09-23. I decided on 2026-09-23 and listed the difference as a condition to fix. The date matters because a different year falls under a different pricing policy.

**Is Acme eligible for 15%?** Yes:
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings), with matching D-U-N-S number 04-812-7730.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers the NS-500 and says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) applies to the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% of list price. David Morgan approved it. It replaces the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 and states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
- The 15% requested is exactly the maximum, so it is within what Acme is entitled to.

**Who can approve it?**
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (it runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. She can't approve 15%, even though the contract allows it.
- Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

The figures in the request add up, and the amount, product, customer and requestor all match the CRM record. Note that under the 2027 policy a 15% discount would need the Chief Revenue Officer instead, so a request dated 2027 or later would have to be re-routed.
