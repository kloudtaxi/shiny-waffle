## Answer

**No, not yet. Ask for the Acme Master Supply Agreement before deciding (REQUEST_EVIDENCE).** DR-9001 says it relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," but the knowledge base has no such agreement and no pricing exception for Acme.

**What checks out**
- **The request:** The CRM record matches what you sent: DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval.
- **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (Strategic tier, Milwaukee). In the ERP it's the bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a different company.
- **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Under it and its Approval Authority Matrix, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%. Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, and over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.
- **The requestor's authority:** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is above her own limit.
- **The approver:** Michael Torres (EMP-200) is VP Sales and holds the 10–20% band.

**What's missing**
- **The contract:** No agreement called "Acme Master Supply Agreement" exists in the knowledge base. The only Acme agreement on file is ACME-MFG-2025, and the only part of it on file is Schedule C (Support and Service Levels). Schedule C covers support for NS-500 but says nothing about discounts or pricing.
- **A pricing exception:** None exists for Acme and NS-500 on 2026-09-23.

So nothing on file shows Acme is entitled to 15% on NS-500. Under the procedure, a request that relies on a contract term nobody can find goes back for the document. It doesn't get approved, and the VP Sales can't fix that alone.

**If the agreement turns up** and allows at least 15% on NS-500 on that date, the outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving and the approval recorded in Northstar CRM. The contract makes Acme eligible, but it doesn't raise Sarah's 10% limit. If the agreement caps the discount below 15%, the outcome would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.
