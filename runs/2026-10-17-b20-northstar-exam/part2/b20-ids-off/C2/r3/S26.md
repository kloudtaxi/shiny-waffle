I've finished checking. **Yes, CR-9201 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme qualifies for $400,000. A $400,000 limit needs approval from the Finance Manager, Priya Shah.

1. **The request:** The ERP record matches what you sent: CR-9201 for customer C-1001, raising the limit from $250,000 to $400,000, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval." In her message raising it, Sarah says "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it," so the request relies on the guarantee.
2. **The customer:** C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the ERP. It trades as Acme Manufacturing, which is CRM-2048, tier **Strategic**. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company and isn't counted here.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes.
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request, so the rule doesn't cover it.
5. **The maximum limit:** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. The parent guarantee (ref. GRT-ACME-2026) adds $400,000, making $1,150,000. That guarantee is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. to Northstar, runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and names Acme Mfg. Holdings, so it covers this customer. Acme would qualify for $400,000 even without it.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy's approval bands don't include her role, so she can't approve this.
7. **Who must approve:** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need the Finance Manager, who is Priya Shah. The VP Sales and Director of Finance are only needed above $500,000.

One thing to watch: INV-62305 ($720,000) was still unpaid on 2026-10-18, 16 days late. That's within the 30-day allowance, so it doesn't change the decision.
