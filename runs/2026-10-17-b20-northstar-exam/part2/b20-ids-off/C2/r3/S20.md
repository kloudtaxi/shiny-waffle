## Answer

No. Michael Torres has the authority to approve an 18% discount, but Acme isn't entitled to one. Acme's contract caps NS-500 discounts at 15%, so the request is above what the customer is eligible for, and no approver can fix that.

1. **The request.** DR-9112: 18% off a $250,000 list price on NS-500 ($45,000 discount, $205,000 net). Michael Torres submitted it on 2026-09-23 and cites "contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. The figures add up. However, I couldn't find DR-9112 in the knowledge base, so I'm deciding on the request as you submitted it.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (D-U-N-S 04-812-7730). It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091).
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller" and that "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) applies to NS-500 only and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its maximum is 15% of list price. It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated from 2025-04-01. It also states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and grants no approval authority.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply to requests from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **His authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales (EMP-200). That role can approve discounts above 10% and up to 20%, so 18% would be within his limit.
8. **Decision.** The 18% request is 3 points above Acme's 15% maximum. His authority doesn't change that, and neither would a higher approver. Two ways forward:
   - Change the request to 15% or less. At 15%, Michael could approve it himself as VP Sales.
   - Get Acme's terms amended through the Deal Desk, which owns the exception.
