## Answer

**Short answer: Probably yes, but not by Michael Torres alone.** On 2025-09-23 BlueRiver was eligible for a $200,000 limit. Under the 2025 credit policy, a limit that size needs the Finance Manager's approval, and that's Priya Shah. One thing has to be settled first: I couldn't find CR-9206 in the ERP credit-request records. Those records list CR-7001 to CR-7010 and CR-9201, but no CR-9206. So someone needs to confirm the request exists before anyone acts on this.

1. **The request.** I decided on the details you gave: C-1002, $100,000 to $200,000, dated 2025-09-23, from Michael Torres. It rests on BlueRiver's own standing ("Fleet expansion"), not on a guarantee. The ERP shows a current limit of $100,000, which matches.
2. **The customer.** ERP customer C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC". That's the same company as "BlueRiver Logistics" in the CRM (CRM-2051): both records have DUNS number 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. The CRM puts it in the Standard tier.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2025 applies, since it covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
4. **Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 45 days past its due date. Three C-1002 invoices fall in that window:
   - INV-61005 was due 2025-03-01 and paid early, on 2025-02-26.
   - INV-61210 was due 2025-05-30 and paid early, on 2025-05-28.
   - INV-61433 was due 2025-08-29 and paid 2025-09-02, 4 days late.

   None breaks the 45-day rule, so the customer is eligible.
5. **Maximum limit.** Standard accounts can go up to $300,000, so $200,000 is within the cap. No guarantee is involved.
6. **Who can approve.** Michael Torres is VP Sales, and the policy gives that role no credit-approval authority. The policy also says no one may approve a request they submitted.
7. **Required approver.** Limits above $50,000 and up to $750,000 need the Finance Manager (Priya Shah, EMP-401). No second sign-off is required in 2025.
