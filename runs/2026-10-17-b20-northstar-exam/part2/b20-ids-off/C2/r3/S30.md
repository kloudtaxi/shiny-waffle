## Answer

No, CR-9205 can't be approved. BlueRiver Logistics fails the 2026 credit policy's payment-history rule, so it isn't eligible for a higher limit. The amount and the approver aren't the problem, and a more senior approver can't fix this.

**1. Request.** CR-9205 is for ERP customer C-1002. It asks to raise the limit from $100,000 to $200,000 and is dated 2026-09-23. Michael Torres (VP Sales) submitted it, and its status is Pending Approval. The justification is the customer's own business growth ("Fleet expansion"), and it doesn't rely on a guarantee.

**2. Customer.** ERP C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC". CRM-2051 is "BlueRiver Logistics". They're the same company: both records have DUNS 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis, MO. In the CRM, the account is **Standard** tier.

**3. Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and requests dated on or after 2026-01-01.

**4. Payment history (where it fails).** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid more than 30 days after its due date. For this request, that means due dates from 2025-09-23 to 2026-09-23.

| INV-62391 | 2026-08-29 | 2026-08-28 | on time |

INV-30020 is due 2026-10-02 and INV-61433 was due 2025-08-29. Both fall outside the 12-month window.

**5. Maximum limit.** The Standard-tier maximum is $250,000, and no guarantee is on file. The $200,000 requested would be within that maximum, but that doesn't help because the payment-history rule fails.

**6–7. Authority (for the record only).** Approval is based on the new total of $200,000. That falls in the Finance Manager band (over $100,000, up to $500,000). Michael Torres is VP Sales and holds none of the policy's approval roles. The policy uses VP Sales only to concur on Strategic accounts over $500,000. So he couldn't approve this request alone in any case. I couldn't find who holds the Finance Manager role.

**8. Decision: `REJECT_OR_ESCALATE`.** The customer can be reconsidered once INV-62147 (due 2026-05-30) is more than 12 months old, as long as no other invoice is paid more than 30 days late in the meantime.
