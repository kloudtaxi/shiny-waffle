## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it's above her approval limit, so the VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it.

1. **The request (DR-9001):** The Northstar CRM record matches what you sent. It's 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23 and still Pending Approval. It cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings. Its legal name is Acme Mfg. Holdings, and it trades as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It lets Acme receive discounts of "up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, up to 15% of list price. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme qualifies. The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, which has lower limits, only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Her authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit. Her owning the account and having prepared the exception doesn't change that, because the matrix ties authority to the role, not the person or the account.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also her manager.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Under the policy, approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM, which I've added as a non-blocking condition.
