## Answer

I've finished checking: invoices, the guarantee, and who holds each approver role. Here is the result.

**No. The request can't be approved as submitted.** Even with the parent guarantee counted in full, $1,300,000 is above the most Acme can have under the 2026 policy, which is $1,150,000. Separately, CR-9204 doesn't exist in Northstar ERP.

1. **The request.** The ERP has no CR-9204. On 2026-09-23 it does hold **CR-9201**: also customer C-1001, also from Sarah Chen, also $250,000 current. But CR-9201 asks for **$400,000**, not $1,300,000, and its justification is the third-plant ramp-up, not the guarantee. I didn't substitute CR-9201; this decision is on CR-9204 as given. Someone needs to confirm whether CR-9204 exists at all.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (same D-U-N-S number, 04-812-7730, and a "doing business as" statement). Its CRM tier is **Strategic**. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044), which is a different company.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history is clean.**
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
5. **The maximum limit.**
   - The base maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000.
   - The Parent Company Guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names Acme Mfg. Holdings and runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it covers this customer on the request date.
   - It is capped at $400,000, so it raises the maximum to **$1,150,000**.
   - The request is $150,000 over that. A higher approver can't fix this.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales and has no credit approval authority under the policy.
7. **Approvers, if a request were within the limit.** A limit over $500,000 needs the Director of Finance (Elena Novak) to approve. Because Acme is Strategic, it also needs the VP Sales (Michael Torres) to concur. Michael Torres is Sarah Chen's manager.

**What could go forward:** a request at or below $1,150,000 that is actually entered in the ERP, approved by Elena Novak with Michael Torres concurring.
