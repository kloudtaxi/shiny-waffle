## Answer

**No, not on the evidence available.** Sarah had the authority to approve 8% on that date, but the agreement the request cites wasn't in force yet. So I can't decide this until the missing document turns up.

**What checks out**
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which is ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Sarah Chen is the account owner.
- **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Policy and authority:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force on 2025-03-31 (it runs 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Under it, "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%". Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so 8% is within her own limit. The 2026 approval matrix, which cuts that limit to 10%, doesn't apply to this date.

**What doesn't check out**
- **The cited agreement wasn't in force.** The request says "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) says "This Agreement is effective April 1, 2025". Its NS-500 exception, which allows up to 15% (EXC-ACME-NS500-15), also starts on 2025-04-01. The request is dated 2025-03-31, one day before either took effect.
- **The earlier exception can't be checked.** The previous exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, is under agreement ACME-SA-2023 and is marked "Active". It was only replaced for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01, so it probably covered 2025-03-31. But the knowledge base has neither its maximum discount nor its effective dates. The "-10" in the name suggests 10%, but I'm not treating that as established.
- **The request itself isn't in the records.** I couldn't find DR-9110 in the knowledge base. I've decided on the request as you gave it, but someone needs to confirm it exists in Northstar CRM.

**Outcome: REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The request relies on special terms that no available evidence shows were in force on 2025-03-31. If the earlier exception (or ACME-SA-2023) turns out to cover NS-500 on that date at 8% or more, Sarah can approve this herself, because 8% is within her limit. If the request was actually meant to fall on or after 2025-04-01, it would be covered by the 15% exception. In that case the date needs correcting in CRM, not just assuming.
