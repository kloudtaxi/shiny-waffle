## Answer

Yes, on the merits. But it would have needed Finance Manager approval (Priya Shah), because Michael Torres can't approve it himself. One problem blocks acting on it: **CR-9206 isn't in the ERP's credit-request records.** So I've decided the request as you gave it and made confirming that it exists a blocking condition.

**1. The request.** The ERP credit-request log has CR-7001 to CR-7010 and CR-9201, but no CR-9206. Looking up that ID returns "not_known". The request asks for a higher limit based on the customer's own standing ("Fleet expansion"). It doesn't mention a guarantee.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC". The CRM account CRM-2051 is "BlueRiver Logistics". Both have the same D-U-N-S number (07-331-9025) and address (890 Harbor Way, St. Louis, MO), so they are the same company. The CRM tier is **Standard**. The ERP shows a limit of $100,000 (last reviewed 2026-01-15), which matches the submitted current limit.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2025 applied (in effect 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 45 days after its due date. C-1002 invoices due in that window:
- INV-61005: due 2025-03-01, paid 2025-02-26 (on time)
- INV-61210: due 2025-05-30, paid 2025-05-28 (on time)
- INV-61433: due 2025-08-29, paid 2025-09-02 (4 days late, within the 45-day allowance)

Nothing breaks the rule, so the customer passes.

**5. Maximum limit.** The maximum for Standard accounts is $300,000. The requested $200,000 is within it, and no credit support is involved, so the customer is **eligible**.

**6. Michael Torres's authority.** He is VP Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $50,000), the Finance Manager (over $50,000 up to $750,000) and the Director of Finance (over $750,000). He has no authority, and as the person who submitted the request he couldn't approve it anyway.

**7. Required approver.** A $200,000 limit falls in the **Finance Manager** band. That role is held by **Priya Shah**, who didn't submit the request. The 2025 policy doesn't require any concurrence. One caveat: the employee records give job titles but no dates, so I couldn't confirm she held the role on 2025-09-23.
