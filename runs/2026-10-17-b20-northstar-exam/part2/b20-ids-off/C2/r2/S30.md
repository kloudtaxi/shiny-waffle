## Answer

**No. CR-9205 can't be approved. The outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**, because BlueRiver Logistics fails the payment-history rule. Sending it to a more senior approver doesn't change that. Also, I couldn't find CR-9205 in the knowledge base.

**1. The request.** The details below are as you supplied them: CR-9205, customer C-1002, raise $100,000 to $200,000, requested by Michael Torres on 2026-09-23. It relies on the customer's own standing, not a guarantee. The ERP credit-request records I could find include CR-9201 (Sarah Chen, C-1001, same date), but not CR-9205. I decided on the request as given, and confirming it exists in the ERP is a blocking condition.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC". CRM account CRM-2051 is "BlueRiver Logistics". They are the same company: same DUNS number (07-331-9025) and same address (890 Harbor Way, St. Louis, MO). The CRM lists it as a **Standard** account.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid more than 30 days after its due date. That covers invoices due from 2025-09-23 to 2026-09-23.

| Invoice | Due | Paid | Days late |

| INV-30014 | 2026-02-27 | 2026-02-25 | paid early |
| INV-61902 | 2026-03-01 | 2026-02-27 | paid early |
| **INV-62147** | **2026-05-30** | **2026-07-21** | **52 (fails)** |
| INV-62391 | 2026-08-29 | 2026-08-28 | paid early |

INV-30020 isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count. Earlier invoices fall outside the 12 months.

**5. Maximum limit.** The maximum for Standard accounts is $250,000, so $200,000 would have been within it. The only guarantee on record is Acme Group's guarantee for ACME Manufacturing, which doesn't cover BlueRiver. The late invoice alone makes the customer ineligible.

**6–7. Authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales. The policy gives that role no approval authority, so he can't approve this himself. Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need a Finance Manager's approval. I didn't look up who holds that role, because the decision doesn't depend on it.
