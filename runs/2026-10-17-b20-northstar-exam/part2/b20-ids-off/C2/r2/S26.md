## Answer

**Yes, CR-9201 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen. Under the 2026 credit policy it needs Finance Manager approval, and Priya Shah holds that role.** Acme is eligible for $400,000 on its own standing, so the outcome is `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`.

1. **The request:** The Northstar ERP record matches what you sent. CR-9201 is for customer C-1001, raising the limit from $250,000 to $400,000. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 and its status is "Pending Approval". The ERP justification is the third-plant ramp-up. In a separate message Sarah wrote "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it", so the request leans on a guarantee. I found that guarantee and it is valid (point 5). Acme qualifies even without it.
2. **The customer:** C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" in the ERP and "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) in the CRM. Legal documents say Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing, and the D-U-N-S number 04-812-7730 matches. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company. Acme Group Holdings, Inc. is the parent.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request can have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. The ERP has three invoices for C-1001:
   * INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, before it was due.
   * INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, before it was due.
   * INV-62305 is due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count yet.

   None were late.
5. **Maximum limit:** The CRM lists Acme as a Strategic account. The policy maximum for Strategic accounts is $750,000, so $400,000 fits on Acme's own standing. The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and covers up to $400,000. That would raise the maximum to $1,150,000, but it isn't needed here.
6. **Sarah's authority:** None. She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives credit approval authority only to Finance roles.
7. **Who must approve:** For a new total over $100,000 and up to $500,000, the policy requires Finance Manager approval. Priya Shah is Finance Manager. VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed.
