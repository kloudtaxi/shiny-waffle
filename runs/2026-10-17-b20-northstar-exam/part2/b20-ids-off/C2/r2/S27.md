## Answer

I'm done checking the records. The short answer is no, not yet: CR-9202 can't be approved on 2026-09-23 without two more sign-offs, and first someone has to confirm the request exists in the ERP.

**The request isn't in the ERP.** The knowledge base has no record called CR-9202. On the same day, the ERP has **CR-9201** for customer C-1001, also from Sarah Chen, with the same reason ("Third-plant ramp-up…") and the status "Pending Approval". But CR-9201 asks for **$400,000**, not $650,000. As the procedure requires, I decided CR-9202 as you gave it, at $650,000, and did not swap in CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9202 exists in the ERP is a blocking condition.

1. **Customer:** ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which does business as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730). Its tier in the CRM is Strategic. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044).
2. **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
3. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, on time.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, on time.
   - INV-62305 isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
4. **Maximum limit:** The maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000, and $650,000 is below it. The request rests on Acme's own standing, so the parent guarantee from Acme Group Holdings isn't needed.
5. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives that role no approval authority.
6. **Who must approve:** Any limit over $500,000 needs the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**. Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need agreement (concurrence) from the VP Sales, **Michael Torres**. I found no policy rule that stops Torres from concurring even though Sarah Chen reports to him.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is eligible for $650,000, but the request needs Elena Novak's approval and Michael Torres's concurrence, and nothing can proceed until CR-9202 is confirmed in the ERP.
