## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen couldn't have approved a 22% discount on her own authority.** On 2025-09-23 the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force. It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts "up to and including 15%" and says "Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval." Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. A 22% discount needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres (EMP-200), to approve it.

One thing must happen first. **I couldn't find DR-9103 in the knowledge base**, so the request as you gave it needs to be confirmed in Northstar CRM before anyone acts on it. CRM-2048 and the product do resolve correctly.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9103, 22% off $85,000 list ($18,700 off, $66,300 net; the figures add up). Requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2025-09-23. The justification is "Competitive" and no special terms are cited, so I treated it as a standard-pricing request.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, the same company as ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (D-U-N-S 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen owns the account. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091), which is a separate company.
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date, and it lists NS-EDGE as a supplied product. Pricing is "current list prices… less any discount agreed in an Order," and "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies." It sets no discount cap or entitlement for NS-EDGE.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers NS-EDGE. Acme's exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) applies to the NS-500 controller "only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." Its 15% ceiling therefore doesn't limit this request, and it can't be used to justify it either.
6. **Policy:** Pricing Policy 2025 (effective 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). The 2026 approval matrix (AE up to 10%, VP Sales 10–20%, CRO above 20%) only started on 2026-01-01, so it doesn't apply here.
7. **Authority:** 22% is above Sarah Chen's 15% limit.
8. **Approver:** VP Sales, which is Michael Torres.
