> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but under the 2026 pricing policy an Enterprise Account Executive can only approve up to 10%. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, must approve it.

1. **The request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing: $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, for a net of $212,500. The figures add up. It is dated 2026-09-23 and was raised by Sarah Chen, whose title is Enterprise Account Executive. It relies on the contract, citing "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." Her email of 2026-09-23 says she logged it in the CRM as DR-9001.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". The billing system calls the same company "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (customer C-1001). The DUNS number (04-812-7730) and Milwaukee address match. This is not Acme Industrial Supply Co., which is a separate company. Sarah Chen owns the account.
3. **The product.** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. It covers NS-500 and says customer "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It allows up to 15% off list, applies to "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only", and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which only covered orders up to 2025-03-31. 15% is within the 15% maximum, so Acme qualifies. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Its Approval Authority Matrix sets the same limits. The 2027 policy (5% for AEs, 12% for VP Sales) only starts on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
7. **Sarah's authority.** Enterprise Account Executives "may approve discounts up to and including 10%." 15% is above that. The matrix says authority attaches to the role, not to the person or the account.
8. **Required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres (EMP-200) holds that role and is Sarah's manager.
9. **Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme qualifies for 15%, but the amount is over Sarah's limit, so Michael Torres must approve.

Michael Torres's approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM, because the policy requires recorded approval for anything over 10%. A note in the account plan says the same 15% was "approved last September". That approval doesn't carry over, so it doesn't count for this request.
