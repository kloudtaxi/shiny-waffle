## Answer

**No, CR-9203 can't be approved yet. The decision is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The request depends on the Acme Group parent guarantee, and I couldn't find any guarantee document for Acme Manufacturing. Without it, $1,000,000 is above the most the 2026 policy allows for this customer.

1. **The request:** CR-9203 asks to raise C-1001's limit from $250,000 to $1,000,000. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23. Its stated support is "the Acme Group parent guarantee." The request record itself isn't in the knowledge base, so I couldn't check its figures against the system.
2. **The customer:** ERP C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings") and CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing") are the same company. They share the DUNS number 04-812-7730, and the supply agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. CRM records it as a Strategic account, and Sarah Chen is the account owner. Its parent is Acme Group Holdings, Inc. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history:** I found no invoices for C-1001. The invoices I could see (INV-30001 to INV-30020) belong to other customers, and the search said there were 30 results but would only show 20. So I can't apply the 30-day late-payment rule yet.
5. **Maximum limit:** The 2026 maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000. A parent guarantee in force raises that by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names. The only trace of a guarantee is an unsigned remark that "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it." I found no document, guarantor's legal name, named customer, amount or dates. Without a guarantee, $1,000,000 is $250,000 over the maximum.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives Sales no approval authority, and she can't approve her own request. The no-self-approval rule I found is in the 2025 policy; I didn't find a matching line in the 2026 one.
7. **Who must approve:** Under the 2026 policy, a limit over $500,000 needs the Director of Finance (Elena Novak). Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs the VP Sales to concur (Michael Torres, Sarah Chen's manager). That holds even if the guarantee turns up.

**What happens next:** Once a signed guarantee naming Acme Mfg. Holdings is produced, the decision depends on its amount. If it raises the maximum to at least $1,000,000 and the payment history passes, the outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION. If not, it would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.
