**No, not at 18%.** Acme's contract caps the NS-500 discount at 15%, and DR-9001 asks for 18%. The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. A more senior approver can't fix this, because the problem is what the contract allows, not who signs off. Acme would need new or amended pricing terms.

1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 18% off the NS-500. List is $250,000, the discount $45,000 and net $205,000. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 and cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms.
2. **Customer:** ACME Manufacturing. Sarah Chen owns the account. The contract names the customer as "Acme Mfg. Holdings".
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says every discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the contract) covers the NS-500 only, also from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its maximum eligible discount is **15% of list price**. It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 and "establishes commercial eligibility only." I found no amendment that raises the cap.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, with the Approval Authority Matrix as its working reference.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%, so 18% is beyond her limit either way.
8. **Approval band:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager. His approval still can't push the discount past the 15% the contract allows.

**Two things to check:**

- **Account ID:** In the knowledge base, CRM-2048 points to a service ticket (SR-39385), not to the Acme account.

**What to do:** Either change the request to 15% or less, which would then go to Michael Torres, or get the Deal Desk to amend Acme's terms before 18% can be considered.
