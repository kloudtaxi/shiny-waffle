## Answer

# SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data): Severity 2, no breach, no credit owed

**Customer and scope.** The ticket's CRM account CRM-2048 belongs to Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing (ERP customer C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730). Its Schedule C (SUP-ACME-C) gives it the Platinum plan. NS-Edge Monitoring Package is a covered product, and coverage started 2025-04-01. The ticket is in scope.

**True severity: Severity 2 (High), not the reported P1.** The ticket records vibration data lost on presses 1–6 of 14 at Milwaukee Plant 2. Maintenance techs were checking those presses by hand every two hours. The severity guide puts this in Severity 2: a condition-monitoring function is unavailable for one or more machines, "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks." It's also a Severity 1 condition with a workaround that needs ongoing manual effort. Severity 1 needs production to stop with no workaround, and the record doesn't show that.

**Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened 2026-07-23 13:10 CDT, which is already Central Time. Schedule v2.0 covers tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01. For Severity 2 on Platinum it sets a 2-hour response and 12-hour restoration on the 24×7 clock. Both clocks start at the open time. Because the clock is 24×7, nights and holidays aren't excluded.

- No maintenance-window exclusion and no customer-caused exclusion was raised.

**Credits.** None are owed because nothing was missed. The KB also shows no credit claim for SR-39644. The claim window (30 calendar days from restoration) closed 2026-08-23.

- No claim is needed. A claim would earn nothing, since no target was missed and the window has closed.
