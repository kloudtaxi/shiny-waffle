## Answer

**No, Sarah can't approve this.** The request relies on Acme's Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement's discount terms don't cover the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, so it needs commercial review (**REVIEW_REQUIRED**). Separately, I couldn't find request DR-9109 in the knowledge base, so its existence needs to be confirmed.

1. **The request.** I found no DR-9109 in the knowledge base. I've assessed it as you gave it: 12% off NS-EDGE, 2026-09-23, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example. Its basis is a contract term: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". The figures check out: 12% of $85,000 is $10,200, leaving $74,800.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730). Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044) is a different company.
3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000, matching the request.
4. **The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It supplies NS-EDGE, but at "current list prices, less any discount agreed in an Order", and each discount still needs internal approval. The only discount it allows is up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and says "It does not apply to any other Northstar product." Nothing covers NS-EDGE. The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 has been replaced and is also NS-500 only. An exception's terms don't carry over to other products.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix apply. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority.** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to and including 10%. 12% is over her limit, and a contract permitting a discount wouldn't give her more authority anyway.
8. **The approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres (EMP-200). Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM. His sign-off alone won't resolve this, though: the 12% first needs commercial review or new terms covering NS-EDGE.
