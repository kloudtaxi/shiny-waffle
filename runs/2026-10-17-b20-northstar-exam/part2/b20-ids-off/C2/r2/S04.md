> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23? ## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen could not approve this 15% discount on her own.** One correction first: Northstar CRM dates DR-9001 **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23, and the year changes the answer. The covering email ("Acme NS-500 — DR-9001") is also dated 2026-09-23. I decided on the CRM date.

**Request.** DR-9001 is for account CRM-2048: 15% off NS-500, list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. Sarah Chen requested it. It cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, and its status is Pending Approval.

**Customer and product.** The customer is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen owns the account. The agreement names the counterparty "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which the knowledge base treats as the same customer. One loose end: looking up CRM-2048 in the knowledge base returns a service ticket (SR-39385), not the account. The request still clearly concerns Acme, so this doesn't block the decision. The product is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**Contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows up to 15% on NS-500, but says "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

**Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, also from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% of list price. David Morgan approved it. It replaces the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) and states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." **15% is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.**

**Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to the whole of 2026 and fully replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01.
- Enterprise Account Executives, Sarah Chen's role, can approve up to 10%.
- Above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales.
- Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
- Anything above 10% needs approval evidence recorded in Northstar CRM.

The Approval Authority Matrix says the same, and adds that authority "attaches to the role, not to the individual or the account." The VP Sales is Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah Chen's manager.

**Why the date matters.** Under the 2025 policy, Account Executives could approve up to 15%. If the request really were dated 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved it herself. On the CRM date of 2026-09-23 she can't, so the request date needs confirming before anyone acts on this.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is eligible, but 15% is above Sarah Chen's 10% limit, so Michael Torres must approve.
