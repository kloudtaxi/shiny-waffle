## Answer

**DR-9001 can't be approved by Sarah Chen alone. It needs VP Sales approval from Michael Torres.** First, someone has to resolve why the request you were given doesn't match the CRM record.

**The figures don't match.** The CRM record for DR-9001 (discount_requests.csv) shows **15.0%** off a $250,000 list price: a $37,500 discount and $212,500 net. The submitted request says 8.0%, $20,000 and $230,000. Everything else matches: account CRM-2048, SKU NS-500, requestor sarah.chen@northstar.example, date 2026-09-23 and status Pending Approval. I decided on the CRM figure of 15%. If 8% turns out to be correct, the outcome changes: 8% is within Sarah's own 10% limit, so she could approve it herself.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request and basis:** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so the request relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings," which trades as Acme Manufacturing (DUNS 04-812-7730). It is not "Acme Industrial Supply Co.," which is a separate company.
3. **Product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers NS-500 and says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers NS-500 only and allows up to **15%** off list, in force 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. 15% is within that limit. The older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) covered orders through 2025-03-31 and has been replaced, so it doesn't apply. Schedule B also says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager.
9. **Decision:** `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`. Acme is eligible for 15% on NS-500, but the contract doesn't give Sarah the authority. Under the 2026 policy, any approval above 10% must also be recorded in Northstar CRM.
