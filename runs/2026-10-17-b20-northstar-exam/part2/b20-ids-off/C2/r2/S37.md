## Answer

## SR-39207 — Cedar Health Systems: no service level was breached, so Northstar owes no credit

**1. The customer.** The ticket's CRM account is CRM-2063, which belongs to Cedar Health Systems. Its DUNS number, 02-906-4418, matches ERP customer C-1003, Cedar Health Systems Inc. So the customer is identified by its numbers, not just its name.

**2. Scope: in.** Cedar's Support Terms (SUP-CHS-2025) cover NS-Cloud, on the Silver plan. Coverage started 2025-12-01, before this ticket was opened.

**3. True severity: Severity 2.** The customer reported it as P2. The ticket says "NS-Cloud is unavailable to all users at our Fishers and Carmel outpatient centers" and "Facility operations at both centers are continuing." The severity guide puts "NS-Cloud is unavailable to some, but not all, of the customer's users or sites" at Severity 2. Severity 1 would need NS-Cloud down for all users, or operations unable to continue, and neither applies.

**4. Schedule version and targets.** The ticket opened at 2026-05-22T16:20-04:00, which is 15:20 Central Time on a Friday. That falls under Schedule v2.0, which applies to tickets opened from 2026-04-01. For Severity 2 on Silver, the initial response target is 2 business hours and the restoration target is 12 business hours. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, excluding Northstar holidays.

**5. Clock, pauses and exclusions.** The clock started when the ticket opened, at 15:20 CT. Monday 2026-05-25 was Memorial Day, which is on Northstar's holiday calendar, so it counts as zero business hours. The ticket records no pauses and no maintenance exclusion applies. Neither would change the result, because both targets were met anyway.

**6. Measurement (all times CT):**
- **Initial response:** an engineer responded at 17:10 on Friday. That took 1 h 50 m against a 2 h target, so it was **met**.
- **Restoration:** service was restored at 17:05 on Tuesday 2026-05-26. Friday counts 2 h 40 m, the weekend and Memorial Day count nothing, and Tuesday counts 9 h 05 m. That totals **11 h 45 m** against a 12 h target, so it was **met**.
- Only the holiday keeps this inside the target. If Monday had been a working day, restoration would have taken 21 h 45 m and missed.

**7. Credits: none owed.** No target was missed, so the credit is $0. The customer did file a claim through the portal at 2026-06-01T08:30 CT. That was inside the 30-day window after restoration, which ran to 2026-06-25, so it was on time but earns no credit.

**8. Obligations**
- **Northstar:**
  - **Decide the claim.** The schedule says the Director of Customer Support decides every credit claim. That is **Marcus Adeyemi**, and the deadline is the 30th calendar day after receipt: **2026-07-01** (I've used 23:59 CT as the latest time). The decision should be to deny, because no target was missed. That date is now past, and the knowledge base doesn't record whether he decided it.
  - **No credit memo.** The Support Billing Manager, Paul Brennan, only issues one for an approved credit.
  - **No other escalation or notice is triggered.** No root-cause finding is set out for this case.
  - **Caveat:** the knowledge base holds only fragments of the Support Escalation Procedure. Its triggers aren't recorded, so I can't fully confirm that nothing else was owed.
- **Customer:**
  - **No phone report was needed.** The rule to phone the hotline applies only to Severity 1.
  - **The claim requirement was met:** in writing, naming the ticket, and on time.
  - **No information or access requests** from Northstar are recorded in the ticket.

**9. Outcome: NO_BREACH.**
