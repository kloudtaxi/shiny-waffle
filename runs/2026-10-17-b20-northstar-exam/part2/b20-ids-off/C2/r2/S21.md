## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but on 2027-02-01 her own approval limit is 5%. A discount above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. One more problem: I couldn't find request DR-9113 in the knowledge base, so it needs to be confirmed in the CRM before anyone acts on this.

1. **The request.** DR-9113: 15% off the NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing, requested by Sarah Chen, dated 2027-02-01. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement. The figures add up: 15% of $250,000 is $37,500, leaving $212,500 net. I found no record of DR-9113 itself, so everything below uses the details you gave me.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing (DUNS 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen is the account owner. Note that the CRM ID CRM-2048 is also attached to a service ticket, SR-39385, in the knowledge base. That's a data-quality issue, not a reason to stop.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller, SKU NS-500.
4. **Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is active on the request date. It says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception.** Schedule B to that agreement, exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, up to 15% off list. The requested 15% is exactly at that limit, so Acme is eligible. This exception replaced an older one, EXC-ACME-NS500-10 (up to 10%, orders through 2025-03-31), which no longer applies. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 (2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31) fully replaces the 2026 policy. Under the old rules an Enterprise Account Executive could approve up to 10%; that no longer applies.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 5%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver.** Above 5% and up to 12% needs the VP Sales; above 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. That is David Morgan (EMP-300), who also approved the exception.
9. **Decision.** Approve with authorization from David Morgan. Two things must happen first: confirm DR-9113 exists in the CRM, and complete the review of the contract-specific exception that the 2027 policy requires. Because the discount is over 5%, the approval must be recorded as evidence.
