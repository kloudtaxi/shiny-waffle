## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen's request CR-9207 to raise Acme Industrial Supply's credit limit from $50,000 to $500,000, citing the Acme parent guarantee, be approved on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

**No, CR-9207 can't be approved on what's on file. The outcome is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The request relies on "the Acme parent guarantee", but the only parent guarantee on file covers a different company.

**1. The request is missing from the system of record.** The ERP credit-request log has no CR-9207. Sarah Chen's only logged request from 2026-09-23 is **CR-9201**: Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), $250,000 → $400,000, also pending. I haven't treated CR-9201 as a stand-in. This decision covers CR-9207 as you gave it, and confirming that it exists is a blocking condition.

**2. The customer.** C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH (DUNS 09-552-1187, CRM-2091). Northstar CRM records it as a **Standard** tier account. A contract schedule says it is "a different company" from ACME Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001, Milwaukee).

**3. The guarantee doesn't cover this customer.** The only parent guarantee on file is GRT-ACME-2026:
- **Guarantor:** Acme Group Holdings, Inc.
- **Covers:** the payment obligations of its subsidiary **Acme Mfg. Holdings** only
- **Cap:** $400,000
- **In force:** 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31

Nothing on file shows a guarantee naming Acme Industrial Supply Co. Sarah's email says "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it", but that email is about CR-9201, not this customer. Under the 2026 policy, a guarantee only covers "the customer the guarantee names."

**4. Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**5. Payment history is clean.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request (2025-09-23 to 2026-09-23) may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Both of C-1044's invoices fall before that window:
- INV-30001: due 2025-02-25, paid 2025-03-04
- INV-30003: due 2025-05-07, paid 2025-04-28

Neither was over 30 days late anyway. The customer has had no invoices in the last 12 months.

**6. Maximum limit.** The Standard tier maximum is **$250,000**, and with no guarantee for this customer, that's the cap. $500,000 is twice that. If a valid guarantee naming C-1044 turns up, it raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, so it would need to be at least $250,000 for the request to qualify.

**7. Approval authority.** Authority goes by the new total of $500,000:
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, a role the policy gives no approval authority.
- Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need **Finance Manager** approval. That's Priya Shah.
- VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.

**What happens next:**
- **A guarantee naming Acme Industrial Supply Co. is produced** (covering 2026-09-23, at least $250,000): the request becomes approvable with Priya Shah's approval.
- **No such guarantee exists:** the request is over the $250,000 maximum and should be rejected or escalated. A more senior approver wouldn't change that.
