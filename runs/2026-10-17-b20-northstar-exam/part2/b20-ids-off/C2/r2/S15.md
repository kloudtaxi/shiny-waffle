## Answer

**No, Michael Torres can't approve this yet. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** He has the authority for a 12% discount. The problem is that the request relies on another customer's contract.

**Why**
- **The basis belongs to a different customer.** The request cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings (ACME Manufacturing, CRM-2048 / ERP C-1001). Its 15% NS-500 maximum applies only to Acme. BlueRiver isn't a party, and special terms don't transfer from one customer to another.
- **BlueRiver has no pricing terms of its own.** CRM-2051 "BlueRiver Logistics" is the same customer as ERP C-1002 "Blue River Logistics LLC" (same DUNS, 07-331-9025). The only BlueRiver agreement I found is its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025, Silver plan). Those cover NS-CLOUD only, set no discount, and don't mention NS-500. I found no contract or pricing exception for BlueRiver on NS-500 as of 2026-09-23.
- **Authority is not the issue.** Under Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the Approval Authority Matrix, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Torres is VP Sales (EMP-200), so 12% is within his limit. But having the authority doesn't make the customer eligible. This request needs commercial review first.
- **The request isn't in the knowledge base.** I couldn't find DR-9107 there, so it needs confirming in the system of record before anyone acts.

**Next steps:** Commercial review should decide whether BlueRiver gets new NS-500 terms. Otherwise the request can be resubmitted as standard pricing with an accurate justification. On a standard basis, 12% would fall within Torres's VP Sales authority.
