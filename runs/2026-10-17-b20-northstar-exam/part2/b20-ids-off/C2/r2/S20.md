## Answer

**No, Michael Torres can't approve this, and no other approver can either.** As VP Sales he has the authority for an 18% discount, but Acme is only eligible for up to 15% on NS-500. The decision is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**: the request needs new or amended contract terms, not a more senior sign-off.

**What I found:**

1. **Request.** DR-9112 for 18% off NS-500 ($250,000 list, $45,000 off), dated 2026-09-23. It relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement, so it depends on special terms. **I couldn't find DR-9112 in the knowledge base**, so it has to be confirmed in Northstar CRM before anything is acted on.
2. **Customer.** CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. In the ERP it's "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), DUNS 04-812-7730. The knowledge base also wrongly links CRM-2048 to a service ticket (SR-39385). That doesn't affect the decision.
3. **Product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500, but says Acme is eligible for discounts *"of up to 15%"* on it, and *"Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."*
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, for the same dates. Its maximum is **15% of list price**. It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests from 2025-04-01. It also says it gives eligibility only, not approval authority to any employee.
6. **Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, with lower limits, only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Michael's authority.** Under the 2026 policy, VP Sales approves discounts above 10% and up to 20%. Michael is VP Sales (EMP-200), so 18% is within his own limit.
8. **The real problem.** 18% is 3 points above the 15% Acme is eligible for. A higher approver can't fix that. Approving 18% would need a new or amended pricing exception, which the Deal Desk owns.

To go ahead, the request could be resubmitted at 15% or less, which Michael could approve himself under the 2026 policy. Or Acme's terms would need to be amended first.
