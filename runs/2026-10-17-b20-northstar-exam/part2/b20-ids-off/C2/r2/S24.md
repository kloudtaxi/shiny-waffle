## Answer

Not on Sarah Chen's own authority. DR-9001 can go ahead only if the VP Sales, Michael Torres, approves it. The submitted copy says "Approved", but the CRM still shows it as **Pending Approval**. No record of an approval exists, so that status must not be acted on.

**The request (CRM record DR-9001):** Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), product NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Dated 2026-09-23 and requested by Sarah Chen. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. Every figure matches the CRM except the status.

**Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The contract says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing, and both records share DUNS number 04-812-7730.

**Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers the NS-500. It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".

**Pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It allows up to 15% off list on the NS-500 only, so the requested 15% is within the limit.
- It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31.
- It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee".

**Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy starts on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%.
- Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales.
- Approvals above 10% need recorded evidence in Northstar CRM.

**Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so her limit is 10%. At 15%, the request is in the VP Sales band. That role is held by Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also her manager.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is entitled to the 15% under its contract, but that doesn't give Sarah Chen the authority to approve it. Michael Torres must approve it, and his approval must be recorded in the CRM.
