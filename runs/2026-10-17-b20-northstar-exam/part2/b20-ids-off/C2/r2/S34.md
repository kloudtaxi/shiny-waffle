## Answer

**CR-9208 can't be acted on yet, because Northstar ERP has no record of it.** On the merits the decision is **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**. Acme Manufacturing qualifies for $650,000, but Michael Torres can't approve it himself. The Director of Finance, Elena Novak, must approve it, and the VP Sales must concur.

**1. The request.** I couldn't find CR-9208 anywhere in the knowledge base. The only Acme request in the ERP extract is **CR-9201**, dated 2026-09-23 with the same justification. It differs in two ways: Sarah Chen submitted it, and it asks for **$400,000**, not $650,000. I haven't used CR-9201 in its place. I decided CR-9208 as you gave it, and confirming that it exists is a blocking condition below. The request doesn't mention a guarantee, so I treated it as based on Acme's own standing.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which trades as Acme Manufacturing. Its CRM account is CRM-2048, tiered Strategic. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044), which the records list as a separate company.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the previous policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, on time.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, on time.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

No invoices are late.

**5. Maximum limit.** The policy maximum for Strategic accounts is $750,000, so $650,000 is within it. The request doesn't need a guarantee. One exists anyway: guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 from Acme Group Holdings names Acme Mfg. Holdings. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and covers up to $400,000.

**6. Michael Torres's authority.** He is VP Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to these Finance roles:
- Accounts Receivable Specialists, up to $100,000
- Finance Manager, above $100,000 up to $500,000
- Director of Finance, above $500,000

So he has none.

**7. Required approvers.** Authority is based on the new total of $650,000.
- **Approval:** the Director of Finance, Elena Novak.
- **Concurrence:** the VP Sales, because Strategic accounts above $500,000 need it. Michael Torres holds that role, so he would be concurring on his own request. The policy doesn't forbid that, but I've noted it as a non-blocking condition so a different concurrer can be chosen if separation of duties is wanted.
