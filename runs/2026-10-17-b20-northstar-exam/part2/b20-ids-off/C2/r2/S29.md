> Can a request to raise Acme Manufacturing's credit limit to $1,300,000 (CR-9204, Sarah Chen), supported by the parent guarantee, be approved on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

**No, it can't be approved.** The requested $1,300,000 is above the most Acme Manufacturing can have, even counting the parent guarantee. The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**, and a more senior approver can't override it.

1. **The request.** CR-9204 asks to raise ERP customer C-1001 from $250,000 to $1,300,000, dated 2026-09-23. Sarah Chen raised it, and it relies on the Acme Group parent guarantee. I couldn't find CR-9204 in the knowledge base (only CR-9201 appears), so I decided on the figures you gave and added a condition to confirm the request exists.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings"), CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing") and D-U-N-S 04-812-7730 are the same company. CRM records it as a **Strategic** account.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history: clean.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
5. **The maximum limit: exceeded.**
   - The policy maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000.
   - The guarantee is GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and is capped at **$400,000**. It's in force for this customer, so it raises the maximum by that amount.
   - The maximum is therefore **$1,150,000**, which is **$150,000 below the $1,300,000 requested**.
6. **The requestor's authority: none.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives that role no credit approval authority.
7. **Who would approve, if it were eligible.** A limit above $500,000 needs the Director of Finance (Elena Novak). Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs VP Sales concurrence (Michael Torres). Being ineligible can't be fixed by a higher approver, though.

A revised request for $1,150,000 or less, still relying on the guarantee, could be approved with those two sign-offs.
