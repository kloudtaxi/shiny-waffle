## Answer

I can't approve DR-9001 yet. The request is justified by the "Acme Master Supply Agreement," and that agreement isn't anywhere in the knowledge base, so the outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**: get the agreement before deciding.

**Request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 (list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." That makes the basis a contract term, not standard pricing. The knowledge base only shows the ID in an email ("I've logged it in CRM as DR-9001"). The CRM export it holds (discount_requests.csv) is dated 2026-01-29, before the request, so I couldn't check the record itself.

**Customer.** CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing, which is ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (DUNS 04-812-7730). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044). Sarah Chen owns the account.

**Product.** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**Contract and pricing exception.**
- No "Master Supply Agreement" or similar pricing agreement for Acme exists in the knowledge base.
- The only Acme contract document is Schedule C (Support and Service Levels) to ACME-MFG-2025, in effect from 2025-04-01. It does cover NS-500, but only for support: a Platinum plan at $21,000 a month from 2026-01. It has no discount or pricing terms.
- No pricing exception names NS-500 for Acme on this date.
- An earlier request, DR-8104 (2025-09-16), used the same justification for the same 15% and was approved by Sarah Chen herself. A past approval doesn't prove the contract term exists. It also looks like a self-approval above the 10% threshold. I couldn't check this against the 2025 policy, so it's worth a look.

**Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), with the Approval Authority Matrix as its working reference. The 2027 policy starts 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.

**Authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to and including 10%. 15% falls in the >10% to ≤20% band, which needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Approvals above 10% also need recorded approval evidence in Northstar CRM. A contract allowing 15% would not give her that authority.

**What happens next:** get the agreement, then decide again. If it gives Acme at least 15% on NS-500 on 2026-09-23, the outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving. If it allows less than 15%, it would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. If it doesn't cover NS-500 or this date, it would be REVIEW_REQUIRED.
