## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this.** The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**. The request says the 15% comes from the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement's 15% term covers only the NS-500 controller. Nothing gives Acme a special discount on NS-Cloud.

**Request and parties**
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings), which signed the Master Supply Agreement. A different company, Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091), is a separate customer and is not involved.
- **Product:** NS-CLOUD (NS-Cloud Operations Suite) has a list price of $120,000, which matches the request. 15% is $18,000 off, for a net of $102,000.
- **Requestor:** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive and reports to Michael Torres, VP Sales.

**Is Acme eligible for 15% on NS-Cloud? No.**
- **The agreement (ACME-MFG-2025):** it runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It supplies NS-Cloud at Northstar's "current list prices, less any discount agreed in an Order". It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller", and "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".
- **The pricing exception (Schedule B, EXC-ACME-NS500-15):** it covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product".
- **The support schedule (Schedule C):** it says NS-Cloud "is not a covered product" and "could be added only by a signed amendment".

No document gives Acme an NS-Cloud discount, and special terms don't carry over from one product to another. So the 15% claim needs commercial review, or a signed amendment that adds NS-Cloud.

**Who could approve it?**
- **Policy in force:** the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy starts on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
- **Sarah's limit:** Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over her limit, even if Acme were eligible.
- **Required approver:** more than 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres (EMP-200). Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**The product on DR-9001 doesn't match.** The knowledge base has Sarah's email of 2026-09-23 to Michael Torres, "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001". It says Acme wants "the NS-500 controller", asks for 15% off list, and that she logged it "in CRM as DR-9001". The CRM record you gave says NS-CLOUD. I decided on the request as given (NS-CLOUD), but the product needs confirming first. If DR-9001 is actually for NS-500, Acme would be eligible for 15% under the exception, and the answer would become "approve with authorization" from Michael Torres. Sarah still couldn't approve it herself.
