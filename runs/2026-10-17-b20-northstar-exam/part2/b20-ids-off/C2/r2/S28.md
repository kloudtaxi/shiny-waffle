## Answer

**The customer qualifies for $1,000,000, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself, and CR-9203 can't be acted on yet because Northstar ERP has no record of it.** The decision is APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, held by a blocking condition until the request is confirmed in the ERP.

**1. The request.** I couldn't find CR-9203 anywhere in the knowledge base. The only pending ERP request for customer C-1001 is **CR-9201**: also by Sarah Chen, also dated 2026-09-23, but asking to go from $250,000 to **$400,000**, and justified by the third-plant ramp-up, not the guarantee. I haven't treated CR-9201 as the same request. This decision is on CR-9203 as you gave it: $250,000 to $1,000,000, relying on the parent guarantee.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which trades as Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730). Its tier in the CRM is **Strategic**. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044).

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. C-1001 passes:
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, early.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, early.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

**5. Maximum limit.** The maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000. A parent guarantee in force adds the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names. The guarantee (reference GRT-ACME-2026) is from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names Acme Mfg. Holdings, which is C-1001. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it covers the request date, and it is capped at $400,000. That makes the maximum $1,150,000, so $1,000,000 is within it.

**6. Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives her role no approval authority.

**7. Required approvers.** Authority is set by the new total limit of $1,000,000:
- Limits over $500,000 need the Director of Finance: **Elena Novak**.
- Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence: **Michael Torres**. He is Sarah Chen's manager, though I found no policy rule that stops him from concurring.
