## Answer

**DR-9001 can be approved at 15%, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.** The VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it. Acme is eligible for the discount. Eligibility doesn't give Sarah more authority.

1. **The request:** The CRM record for DR-9001 matches what was submitted exactly. It's account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off $250,000 list ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM's "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) and ERP's "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001) are the same company, with the same D-U-N-S number, 04-812-7730. This is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091), which the contract's Schedule C says is a different company.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It names NS-500 as up to 15% and says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** Schedule B, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, is for Acme Manufacturing and covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only". It runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a "Maximum eligible discount: 15% of list price". 15% is at the maximum, not above it, so Acme is eligible. The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee". It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which only covered orders through 2025-03-31.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix apply. The 2027 policy, which has tighter limits, only covers requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **The approver:** The band for more than 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales. Michael Torres (EMP-200) holds that role, and he is also Sarah's manager.

The policy also says approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM. I've added that as a non-blocking note in the record below.
