## Answer

No, it can't be approved yet. On the merits Acme qualifies for $1,000,000 if Elena Novak (Director of Finance) approves and Michael Torres (VP Sales) concurs. But CR-9203 isn't in Northstar ERP, so nothing can happen until the request is confirmed there.

**1. The request isn't in ERP.** The only pending request for customer C-1001 in `credit_requests.csv` is **CR-9201**. Sarah Chen submitted it the same day, 2026-09-23, to go from $250,000 to **$400,000** for the third-plant ramp-up. It doesn't mention the guarantee. I decided on CR-9203 as you gave it and did not use CR-9201 in its place. Confirming CR-9203 in ERP is a blocking condition.

**2. Same company in every system.** Acme Mfg. Holdings (ERP C-1001), Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, Strategic tier) and the company the guarantee names all share the same address (2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee). ERP and CRM also share the same DUNS number (04-812-7730). Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company.

**3. Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**4. Payment history is clean.** Two invoices fell due in the 12 months before 2026-09-23, and both were paid before their due dates:
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22

INV-62305 ($720,000) isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

**5. Maximum limit is $1,150,000.**
- Strategic accounts can go up to $750,000.
- Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 from Acme Group Holdings names only Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing. It caps the guarantor at $400,000 and runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it was in force on the request date.
- $750,000 + $400,000 = $1,150,000, so $1,000,000 is within the limit.

**6. Sarah Chen can't approve it.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the policy gives that role no credit approval authority.

**7. Who must sign.**
- Above $500,000 needs approval from the Director of Finance: **Elena Novak**.
- Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need concurrence from the VP Sales: **Michael Torres**.
- Neither of them submitted the request, so the rule that no one may approve their own request doesn't change anything.
