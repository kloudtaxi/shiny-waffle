**What I checked**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 is in the CRM discount request log (`discount_requests.csv`) with status "Pending Approval." Every figure you submitted matches that record: CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23. It relies on contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing," owned by Sarah Chen. In the ERP system the same company is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). The DUNS number 04-812-7730 and the Milwaukee address match. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044).
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. NS-500 is one of the products it covers. §3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500, as set out in Schedule B.
5. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) covers the NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15%, effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. The requested 15% is exactly at the limit, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) applies. The 2027 policy was published on 2026-09-15 but only takes effect on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts only up to 10%. 15% is over that limit. Both MSA §3.3 and Schedule B say the customer's eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
8. **Required approver:** The Approval Authority Matrix puts discounts above 10% and up to 20% with the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres, according to the employee list and the org chart as of 2026-09-01. The approval must be recorded in CRM.

**Why last year's precedent doesn't carry over:** Sarah's email says the 15% is "what we did for them last September." That deal, DR-8104 (2025-09-16), was the same deal, and she approved it herself. That was allowed under the 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered that limit to 10%, so she can't approve this one herself.

Nothing else needs to be fixed before Michael approves.
