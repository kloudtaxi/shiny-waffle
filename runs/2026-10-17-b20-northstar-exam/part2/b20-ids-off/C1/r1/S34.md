## Answer

**No, Michael Torres can't approve CR-9208 himself. Acme qualifies for $650,000, but under the 2026 Credit Policy the Director of Finance (Elena Novak) must approve it, and David Morgan must concur. There is also a blocking problem: CR-9208 isn't in Northstar ERP, so its existence has to be confirmed before anyone acts on it.**

**1. The request.** Northstar ERP's credit requests file has no CR-9208. The only Acme request (customer C-1001) there is **CR-9201**: Sarah Chen asked on 2026-09-23 to raise the limit from $250,000 to **$400,000**. It has the same justification as the request you gave me and is "Pending Approval." I have not treated CR-9201 as if it were CR-9208. I decided on CR-9208 as you gave it ($250,000 to $650,000, Michael Torres, 2026-09-23). Its justification is order volume and doesn't mention the parent guarantee, so I treated it as resting on Acme's own standing.

**2. The customer.** These are all one company, matched by address and DUNS number 04-812-7730:
- ERP C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings"
- CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", tier **Strategic**
- The company named in the guarantee

"Acme Industrial Supply Co." (C-1044) is a different company.

**3. The policy.** The Northstar Credit Policy 2026 covers requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or be unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme has no late invoices:
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) is still unpaid, but it isn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

**5. The maximum limit.** Strategic accounts can go up to $750,000, so $650,000 is within it. Acme's parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, $400,000, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) does cover this company and would raise the maximum to $1,150,000. It isn't needed for this amount.

**6. Michael Torres's authority.** As VP Sales he has no credit-approval authority under the policy. He also submitted the request, and the policy says no one may approve or concur on their own request.

**7. Who must sign off.**
- **Approval:** limits above $500,000 need the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**.
- **Concurrence:** Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need the VP Sales. That's Torres, but he submitted the request, so the concurrence passes to his manager, **David Morgan** (Chief Revenue Officer).
