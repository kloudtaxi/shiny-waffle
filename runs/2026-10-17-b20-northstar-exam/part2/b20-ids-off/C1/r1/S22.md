## Answer

Yes, DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen alone. Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it. Before anyone acts on it, the discount figure has to be cleared up: the request you sent says 8%, but the CRM record says 15%.

**The figures don't match.** Your copy shows 8% ($20,000 off, $230,000 net). The CRM record of DR-9001 in `discount_requests.csv` shows **15% ($37,500 off, $212,500 net)**, status Pending Approval. Sarah's email to Michael on 2026-09-23 also says Acme is "asking for 15% off list." I've decided on the CRM figure of 15%. At 8% Sarah could approve it herself, so which figure is right changes who signs.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, CRM-2048, NS-500, 15% (per CRM), dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. It relies on the customer's contract: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. In the ERP it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), the company that signed the agreement. It is a different customer from Acme Industrial (CRM-2091) and Acme Industrial Supply (C-1044).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. It covers NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's eligibility does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) allows up to **15%**, on NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The request is at that maximum, so Acme is eligible. The older 10% exception expired on 2025-03-31 and this one replaced it.
6. **Policy:** the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. The 2027 policy doesn't apply until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** as an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the **VP Sales**, who is **Michael Torres**. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in CRM.

**Why last year's approval doesn't carry over:** Sarah approved Acme's 15% herself last September (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered that limit to 10%.
