It relies on BlueRiver's own standing, not on a guarantee. The ERP's `credit_requests.csv` has no CR-9206 at all; it lists CR-9201 and CR-7001 to CR-7010. **2. The customer.** ERP C-1002 is "Blue River Logistics LLC" (DUNS 07-331-9025, 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis). It is the same company as CRM-2051 "BlueRiver Logistics": the DUNS number and address match. Its CRM account tier is **Standard**.

**3. The policy.** The request is dated 2025-09-23, so the **Northstar Credit Policy 2025** applies (effective 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).

**4. Payment history.** The 2025 rule: no invoice due in the previous 12 months may be paid more than 45 days late. Three C-1002 invoices fell due in that window, and all pass:
- INV-61005: due 2025-03-01, paid 2025-02-26 (early)
- INV-61210: due 2025-05-30, paid 2025-05-28 (early)
- INV-61433: due 2025-08-29, paid 2025-09-02 (4 days late, within the 45-day allowance)

A VP Sales has none. Separately, the policy says no one may approve their own request.

**7. Who had to approve.** A $200,000 limit falls in the Finance Manager band, which is Priya Shah. She isn't the requestor, so she may approve. The 2025 policy requires no second sign-off ("concurrence").

**8. Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION** by Priya Shah, Finance Manager, once the request is confirmed to exist.
