## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. The request is for 15%, and under the 2026 Pricing Policy an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it. It also can't go ahead yet, because the request you gave me doesn't match the CRM record.

**The CRM record is for a different product.** In `discount_requests.csv`, DR-9001 is for **NS-500** (NS-500 Industrial Controller) at $250,000 list, $37,500 discount and $212,500 net. It is not NS-Cloud at $120,000 list. Everything else matches: account CRM-2048, 15%, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", Pending Approval. Sarah's email to Michael Torres the same day also describes it as the NS-500 controller for Acme's third plant. I've decided on the CRM record (NS-500) and made the mismatch a blocking condition.

**Step by step:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, 15%, dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. It relies on the customer contract and its pricing exception, not on standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (DUNS 04-812-7730).
3. **Product:** NS-500 in the CRM record. The request you gave says NS-CLOUD.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) allows up to 15% on NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. A 15% request is within that limit, so the customer is eligible. The exception says it covers commercial eligibility only and gives no employee the authority to approve. Section 3.3 of the agreement says the same.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01. Last September's 15% approval (DR-8104) fell under the 2025 policy, where Account Executives could approve up to 15%. That precedent doesn't carry over.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Enterprise Account Executive, limit 10%. 15% is above it.
8. **Approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200). The approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**If the product really is NS-Cloud:** the NS-500 exception says it doesn't apply to any other product. Nothing gives Acme special terms on NS-Cloud. In that case the outcome would be REVIEW_REQUIRED (commercial review), not this approval.
