**2. Scope.** The ticket is in scope. Cedar's Silver plan covers NS-CLOUD, and coverage started on 2025-12-01, well before the ticket opened on 2026-05-22.

**3. True severity: Severity 2.** Cedar reported it as P2, but that doesn't set the severity. The severity comes from the recorded impact: NS-Cloud was down at 2 of Cedar's 14 facilities, operations at both continued, and the other 12 worked normally. NS-Cloud being unavailable to some but not all sites is Severity 2 under the severity guide. It doesn't reach Severity 1, which needs all users affected or operations stopped.

**4. Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened at 15:20 Central Time on Friday 2026-05-22, so schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened from 2026-04-01). Severity 2 on the Silver plan has a business-hours clock: response within 2 business hours and restoration within 12. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 Central, Monday to Friday. Monday 2026-05-25 was Memorial Day, a Northstar holiday, so it doesn't count.

**5. Clock.** The clock started at 15:20 Central on Friday, which is inside business hours. The ticket shows no pauses, there was no maintenance notice for Cedar, and Northstar didn't claim the incident was customer-caused.

**6. Measurement.** All times are converted from Eastern to Central.

- **Restoration: met.** Service came back at 18:05 Eastern on Tuesday 2026-05-26, which is 17:05 Central. The business time used was 160 minutes on Friday, none on Monday (holiday) and 545 minutes on Tuesday. That totals 11 h 45 min against a target that ran out at 17:20 Tuesday.

The Silver Severity 2 clock counts only business hours, and nights, the weekend and Memorial Day fall outside them.
