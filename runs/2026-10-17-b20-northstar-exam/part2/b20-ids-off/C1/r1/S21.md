**What the request relies on.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms rather than standard pricing.

**Findings:**
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. The contract is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing; the ERP customer is C-1001.
- **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on 2027-02-01. Clause 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500. Clause 3.3 says this eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15%, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. 15% is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) expired on 2025-03-31 and was replaced by this one.
- **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies, because it replaces the 2026 policy for requests dated from 2027-01-01. Its approval bands are:
  - Account Executives can approve up to 5%.
  - Over 5% and up to 12% needs VP Sales.
  - Over 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
- **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so her limit is 5% and 15% is beyond it. The Approval Authority Matrix only covers the 2026 policy, where 15% would have gone to VP Sales. It doesn't apply here.
- **Approver:** The Chief Revenue Officer is David Morgan (EMP-300), according to the employee records and the org chart as of 2026-09-01. Nothing in the knowledge base shows who will hold the role on 2027-02-01, so that should be checked (I haven't made it blocking).
