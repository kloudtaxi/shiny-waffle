## Answer

No, DR-9001 can't be approved yet. The request rests on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", and that agreement isn't anywhere in the knowledge base. The SOP says to ask for the document before deciding.

**Request:** The Northstar CRM record matches what you gave me. DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15% off $250,000 list, which is a $37,500 discount and $212,500 net. Requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval".

**Customer:** CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing" in the CRM. In the ERP the same company is billed as "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091) or the parent, Acme Group Holdings.

**Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The request's figures are consistent with that price.

**Contract and pricing exception:** I couldn't find either.
- The only Acme contract document in the knowledge base is Schedule C to ACME-MFG-2025, and it covers support only. It has no discount terms.
- The only mention of the 15% is in Sarah's own notes. Her Account Strategy says "I believe it's 15%… Worth confirming with Deal Desk", and her email to Michael Torres says "I think their agreement covers it."
- Last September's 15% (DR-8104, 2025-09-16) is not evidence of the contract. Sarah approved it herself, which the 2025 policy allowed: account executives could approve up to 15% that year.

**Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
- Account executives can approve up to 10%.
- Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% is above her authority. The VP Sales is Michael Torres.

Even if the agreement turns up and allows 15%, Sarah still can't approve this herself, because a contract doesn't change an employee's approval authority. If the document shows eligibility, the expected outcome would be approval by Michael Torres, with the approval recorded in the CRM. If it's missing, expired on 2026-09-23 or doesn't cover NS-500, the request goes to commercial review instead.
