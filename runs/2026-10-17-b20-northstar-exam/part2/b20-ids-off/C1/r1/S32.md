## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen's request CR-9207 to raise Acme Industrial Supply's credit limit from $50,000 to $500,000, citing the Acme parent guarantee, be approved on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

**No, CR-9207 can't be approved. The decision is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Acme Industrial Supply Co. can have a limit of at most $250,000, and the $500,000 requested is above that. The guarantee cited in the request doesn't cover this company.

1. **The request is not in the system of record.** Northstar ERP's `credit_requests.csv` has no CR-9207. Sarah Chen's only pending request dated 2026-09-23 is CR-9201, which is for a different customer: Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), from $250,000 to $400,000. I have decided CR-9207 as you gave it and made "confirm the request exists" a blocking condition. The current limit you gave ($50,000) matches what ERP shows for C-1044.

2. **The customer is a different company from the guaranteed one.** C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH (D-U-N-S 09-552-1187), CRM-2091, tier **Standard**. It is not Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing in Milwaukee (C-1001 / CRM-2048, Strategic).

3. **Policy in force:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

4. **Payment history passes.** The 2026 rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. C-1044 has only two invoices: INV-30001 (due 2025-02-25) and INV-30003 (due 2025-05-07). Both fall before the window (2025-09-23 to 2026-09-23), so it has no late invoices.

5. **The limit is above the maximum.**
   - The 2026 maximum for a Standard account is $250,000.
   - The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc., $400,000, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) covers only "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing… No other company is covered."
   - So the guarantee doesn't raise C-1044's maximum. $500,000 is $250,000 above it.
   - I didn't ask for more evidence because the guarantee was found and plainly excludes this customer.

6. **The requestor has no authority to approve.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (in Sales, reporting to Michael Torres), and the 2026 policy gives her role no credit approval authority. She also can't approve her own request.

7. **Approvers, for reference only.** A $500,000 limit falls in the band above $100,000 up to and including $500,000, which needs the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. No VP Sales concurrence is needed because the account is Standard, not Strategic. But a higher approver can't fix a limit that is above the customer's maximum.

The most this customer could receive under the 2026 policy is $250,000. That would still need Finance Manager approval, as a new request.
