## Answer

**No, CR-9205 can't be approved.** The decision is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE: BlueRiver Logistics paid an invoice too late in the past year, so it doesn't qualify for a higher limit. A more senior approver can't change that. Separately, CR-9205 isn't in the ERP's list of credit requests, which only holds CR-9201 and CR-7001 to CR-7010. I've judged the request as you gave it and added a blocking condition to confirm it exists.

1. **The request:** CR-9205, for ERP customer C-1002. It asks to raise the limit from $100,000 to $200,000, dated 2026-09-23, submitted by Michael Torres. It relies on the customer's own standing; no guarantee is mentioned. The $100,000 current limit matches the ERP credit record.
2. **The customer:** ERP C-1002 "Blue River Logistics LLC" and CRM-2051 "BlueRiver Logistics" are the same company. They share DUNS number 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. The CRM records it as a Standard account.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid more than 30 days after its due date, or be unpaid more than 30 days after it.
   - **INV-62147** ($30,000, NS-Cloud 2026-Q2) was due 2026-05-30 and paid 2026-07-21. That is **52 days late**, so it breaks the rule.
   - The other invoices due in that period were paid on time or at most 2 days late: INV-61688, INV-30014, INV-61902 and INV-62391.
   - INV-30020 ($494,700) is due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
5. **The maximum limit:** $250,000 for Standard accounts. $200,000 is within it, but the payment history alone makes the customer ineligible.
6. **Michael Torres's authority:** He is VP Sales. The policy gives VP Sales no approval authority for credit limits, only concurrence on Strategic accounts above $500,000, which doesn't apply here.
7. **Required approver:** For a $200,000 limit (above $100,000, up to $500,000), the Finance Manager must approve. That is Priya Shah, and she didn't submit the request, so the separation-of-duties rule doesn't come into play. This is recorded for completeness; it doesn't change the outcome.
