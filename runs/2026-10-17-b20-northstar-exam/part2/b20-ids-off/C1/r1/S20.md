## Answer

**No, Michael Torres can't approve this.** At 18%, the discount is above the most Acme Manufacturing is entitled to on the NS-500. His VP Sales authority covers 18%, but that doesn't help, because the problem is the customer's contract terms, not who approves.

**DR-9112 isn't in the CRM records.** The discount request records (discount_requests.csv) have no DR-9112. On 2026-09-23 the only Acme NS-500 request is **DR-9001**: 15%, $37,500, requested by Sarah Chen, status Pending Approval. Her email to Michael that morning also refers to DR-9001 at "15% off list". I haven't used DR-9001 in its place. The decision below is on DR-9112 as you gave it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** 18% off NS-500 ($250,000 list, $45,000 discount), requested by Michael Torres on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account. The legal entity is Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for "up to **15%**" on NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a 15% maximum. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). **The 18% requested is 3 points over that maximum.**
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy starts 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
7. **Authority:** Michael Torres is VP Sales (org chart as of 2026-09-01). Under the 2026 policy, discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, so the 18% is within his role's authority.
8. **Why that doesn't settle it:** the contract caps Acme at 15%, and a senior approver can't lift that cap. Going above 15% would need new or amended contract terms. Separately, Michael is both the requestor and the approver on DR-9112. If the record is kept and comes back at 15%, someone else should do the approval.
