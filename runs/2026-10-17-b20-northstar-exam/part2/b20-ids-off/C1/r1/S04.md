## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23? ## Answer

No. On the date in the system of record, Sarah Chen does not have the authority to approve this 15% discount herself. Acme is eligible for it under its contract, but the VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it.

**The dates don't match.** You gave the request date as 2025-09-23, but in Northstar CRM's `discount_requests.csv`, DR-9001 is dated **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email logging DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23, and it says Acme wants "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September". That earlier deal is a separate record, DR-8104, dated 2025-09-16, which Sarah approved herself. I've decided on the system of record's date and listed the difference as a blocking condition below.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on NS-500 for account CRM-2048. List price is $250,000, the discount is $37,500 and the net is $212,500. Sarah Chen requested it and it is Pending Approval. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings). Both share DUNS number 04-812-7730.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500. Section 3.3 says this eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15%, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It establishes commercial eligibility only and grants no approval authority to anyone. A 15% request is within the 15% maximum, so Acme is **eligible**.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and under the 2026 policy that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is **over her limit**.
8. **The required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for discounts over 10%.

If the request really were dated 2025-09-23, the 2025 policy would apply. That policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%, so she could have approved it then, as she did with DR-8104. But that isn't the date on record for DR-9001.

**Decision:** Approve with authorization. Acme is eligible for 15% under the contract, but Michael Torres must approve. Sarah can't approve it just because the contract allows 15%.
