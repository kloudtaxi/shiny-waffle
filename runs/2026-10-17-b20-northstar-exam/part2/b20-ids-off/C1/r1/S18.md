> Could Sarah Chen have approved an 8% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? ## Answer

**Yes, with conditions.** On 2025-03-31 an 8% NS-500 discount for Acme was within both Acme's eligibility and Sarah Chen's own approval authority. Two things need fixing before anyone acts on it: DR-9110 isn't in the system of record, and the request cites the wrong agreement.

**What I checked**
1. **The request.** DR-9110 does not appear in discount_requests.csv. The only Acme NS-500 requests there are DR-8104 (15%, 2025-09-16) and DR-9001 (15%, 2026-09-23). I decided on DR-9110 as you gave it. Its figures add up: 8% of $250,000 is $20,000, leaving a net of $230,000.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings." Both records show the same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address. The account is Strategic and Sarah Chen owns it.
3. **The product.** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract.** The request says it relies on the "Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025). That agreement doesn't start until **2025-04-01**, one day after the request, so it wasn't in force. Its 15% term (Schedule B, exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also starts 2025-04-01.
5. **The pricing exception.** A different exception does cover this product and date. EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023, allows **up to 10%** off NS-500 for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. 8% is within that, so Acme was eligible, but on that exception, not the agreement the request cites.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix doesn't apply.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under the 2025 policy that role can approve up to and including 15%. 8% is within that, so she can approve it herself.
8. **The approver.** No higher approver is needed. Above 15% would need the VP Sales.

**What has to happen first**
- Confirm DR-9110 exists in Northstar CRM.
- Correct the request's justification to cite EXC-ACME-NS500-10, so the record shows what eligibility actually rests on.
- Two smaller points that don't block approval:
  - The ACME-SA-2023 agreement itself isn't in the knowledge base. Only the exception document is, and it shows "Active."
  - That exception applies to orders placed by 2025-03-31, so the order date should be checked.
