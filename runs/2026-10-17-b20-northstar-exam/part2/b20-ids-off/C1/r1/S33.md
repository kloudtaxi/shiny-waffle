## Answer

**No, CR-9203 can't be approved yet. The outcome is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The $1,000,000 limit is only possible with the Acme Group parent guarantee, and nothing in the knowledge base shows that guarantee exists, who it covers or for how much. There is also a second problem: CR-9203 isn't in Northstar ERP at all.

**1. The request.** The ERP credit request list has no CR-9203. The only pending Acme request is **CR-9201**: also from Sarah Chen, also dated 2026-09-23, but asking for $250,000 → **$400,000** for a third-plant ramp-up, with no mention of a guarantee. I haven't treated CR-9201 as if it were CR-9203. I've decided on the request as you gave it, and confirming it exists in the ERP is a blocking condition.

**2. The customer.** These all refer to the same company (DUNS 04-812-7730):
- ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings"
- CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", tier **Strategic**
- the legal name in contract ACME-MFG-2025, Acme Mfg. Holdings doing business as Acme Manufacturing

The current limit of $250,000 matches the ERP.

**3. The policy** is the Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect for all of 2026.

**4. Payment history is clean.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid more than 30 days late or be unpaid more than 30 days past due.
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count. It still shows as unpaid in the ERP, though.

**5. The maximum limit.** Without a guarantee, a Strategic account can go up to **$750,000**, so $1,000,000 is above it. A parent guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer it names. The only mention of the guarantee is Sarah's email about CR-9201, which says it "should cover it. Gina says it's still in place." That's second-hand. There's no guarantee document, no amount, no dates, and nothing showing it names Acme Mfg. Holdings rather than another Acme company. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a separate company. Because the request relies on credit support that can't be located, the procedure says to ask for it before deciding.

**6–7. Who would approve, if the guarantee is produced.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and has no credit approval authority. A $1,000,000 limit needs:
- approval from the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**
- concurrence from the VP Sales, **Michael Torres**, because Acme is a Strategic account. He's Sarah's manager but didn't submit the request, so the separation-of-duties rule doesn't stop him.

If the guarantee doesn't cover at least $250,000 for Acme Mfg. Holdings, the request should be rejected. A more senior approver can't fix that.
