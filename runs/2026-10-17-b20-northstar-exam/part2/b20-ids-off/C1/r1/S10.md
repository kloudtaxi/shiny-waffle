## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** A 25% discount is above her 10% limit as an Enterprise Account Executive under the Pricing Policy 2026. It needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. There's also a blocking problem: **DR-9102 isn't in Northstar CRM's discount register**, so it has to be confirmed before anyone acts on it.

1. **The request isn't in the system of record.** The discount register (discount_requests.csv) has no DR-9102. The only pending Acme request from Sarah on 2026-09-23 is **DR-9001**, which is for NS-500 at 15% citing the Master Supply Agreement. That's a different product, discount and basis, so I haven't treated it as this request. I've assessed DR-9102 as you gave it.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing (Strategic tier; Sarah Chen owns the account). The contract names it as Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a separate account.
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. The submitted figures check out: 25% is $21,250 off, leaving $63,750.
4. **Basis is standard pricing.** The justification is "Competitive", not contract pricing. The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) is active from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and lists NS-EDGE as a supplied product. But its only discount term is up to 15% on NS-500. The pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers "NS-500 … only". So no special terms cover NS-EDGE, and the request doesn't claim any. If it were later recast as contract pricing, it would need commercial review, because Acme has no NS-EDGE terms.
5. **Policy:** the Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy (5% / 12% bands) only starts on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
6. **Authority:** Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. The VP Sales band covers above 10% up to 20%. **Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer**, which is David Morgan (EMP-300, per the org chart as of 2026-09-01). Michael Torres, VP Sales, can't approve 25%. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in CRM.
