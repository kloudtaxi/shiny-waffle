## Answer

Not on Sarah's own authority. Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy anything above 10% needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Also, the submitted request says "Approved", but the CRM record says "Pending Approval" with no approver, so it hasn't actually been approved yet.

1. **The request (CRM record):** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. The CRM status is "Pending Approval" with no approver or approval date. The submitted copy says "Approved", which doesn't match.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. Its ERP customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), the company named in the agreement as "doing business as Acme Manufacturing". Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091) is a different company.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers NS-500, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It covers this product and date, and 15% is exactly the maximum, so eligibility is established. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200, Sarah's manager). The policy also requires the approval to be recorded in CRM.

**Note:** Last year's precedent doesn't apply. DR-8104 (15%, September 2025) was approved by Sarah herself under the 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that limit to 10%.
