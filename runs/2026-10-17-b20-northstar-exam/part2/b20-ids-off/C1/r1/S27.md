## Answer

The customer qualifies for $650,000, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. It needs **Elena Novak (Director of Finance)** to approve and **Michael Torres (VP Sales)** to concur. There's also a problem to clear first: **CR-9202 is not in Northstar ERP's credit requests.** The only pending Acme request there is **CR-9201**: same customer (C-1001), same date (2026-09-23), same requestor and same justification, but **$250,000 → $400,000**. Sarah's email to Priya Shah that day also refers to CR-9201 at $400,000. I decided on CR-9202 as you gave it and did not swap in CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9202 exists is a blocking condition.

**1. Request and basis.** CR-9202 asks for $650,000 on Acme's own standing (`standard`). It doesn't cite a guarantee, and it doesn't need one.

**2. Customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are all the same company. CRM records it as a **Strategic** account. ERP shows the current limit as $250,000. This is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091).

**3. Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24, on time.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22, on time.
- INV-62305 ($720,000): due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
- No late invoices.

**5. Maximum limit.** The maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000, so $650,000 is within it on Acme's own standing. A parent guarantee does exist: GRT-ACME-2026 from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names Acme Mfg. Holdings, covers $400,000 and runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It isn't needed for this decision.

**6. Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. That role has no credit approval authority under the policy, and she can't approve her own request anyway.

**7. Required approvers.** Limits above $500,000 need Director of Finance approval: Elena Novak. Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence: Michael Torres. Neither of them submitted the request, so the separation-of-duties rule doesn't change who must sign.

**8. Outcome:** `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`, held by the blocking condition until CR-9202 is confirmed. If the real request is CR-9201 at $400,000, that's a separate decision: it would need only Finance Manager approval (Priya Shah).
