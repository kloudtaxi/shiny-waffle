## Answer

Yes, it can be approved, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to sign it off. Acme is entitled to the 15%, but under the 2026 policy that discount is above what an Account Executive can approve.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 is in the CRM discount log as "Pending Approval". It is for CRM-2048, product NS-500, a 15.0% discount, $250,000 list price, $37,500 off and $212,500 net, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example. It relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement, so the basis is a contract term and pricing exception. **The dates don't match:** the submission says 2025-09-23, but the CRM log, which is the system of record, says **2026-09-23**. Sarah's email to Michael logging DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23. I decided on the CRM date and listed the mismatch as a condition below. The date decides which policy applies.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", customer C-1001 (D-U-N-S 04-812-7730). This is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044).

**3. The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The figures in the request add up.

**4. The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers NS-500. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500, and Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." I found no record of it being ended or amended.

**5. The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It applies to NS-500 only, allows up to 15%, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10. **The 15% request is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.**

**6. The policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."

**8. Who must approve.** The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix puts discounts above 10% and up to 20% with the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres (EMP-200). Approvals above 10% must be recorded in the CRM.
