Their support terms (SUP-CHS-2025) are on the Silver plan, cover NS-CLOUD, and coverage started on 2025-12-01. The ticket is in scope.

**2. True severity: 1.** The ticket says "NS-Cloud won't load for anyone… none of our 14 facilities", and the engineer confirmed there was no workaround. The severity guide defines NS-Cloud being unavailable to all users, with no workaround, as Severity 1. Because the impact was recorded at opening, Severity 1 applies from the open time.

**3. Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened at 07:05 Eastern, which is 06:05 Central on 2026-06-10, so schedule version 2.0 applies. Severity 1 runs on a 24×7 clock, even on the Silver plan. The targets are a 30-minute first response and 4-hour restoration.

**4. Clock start, pauses and exclusions.** Both clocks started at 06:05 CT. The ticket records no pause. The only maintenance notice on record (MN-2607) is for a different customer's account (BlueRiver Logistics), and Northstar did not claim the customer caused the incident. So nothing is excluded.

**5. Measurement (Central time):**
- **First response: missed.** The engineer called at 08:20, 2 h 15 min after the clock started. The target ran out at 06:35.
- **Restoration: missed, band 1.** Service came back at 11:35, after 5 h 30 min. That is more than the 4-hour target but no more than double it (8 hours).

**6. Credit: $320.00.** Cedar reported by portal, not by phoning the hotline. Under that rule the lower Severity 2 credit rates apply, though the misses are still measured against the Severity 1 targets.
- The rates are 3% for the response miss and 5% for the restoration miss: 8% of the $4,000 June fee.
- The monthly cap is 30% ($1,200). No other credits exist for tickets Cedar opened in June, so the cap doesn't reduce this one.
- Cedar's written claim arrived on 2026-06-29 at 10:00 CT. The deadline was 2026-07-10 23:59 CT, so the claim was on time.
