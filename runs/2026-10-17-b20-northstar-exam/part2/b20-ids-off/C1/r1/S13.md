It is a separate company from Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing in Milwaukee (CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730).

**3. The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, with a list price of $250,000.

**4–5. Contract and pricing exception.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is signed with **Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing**, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its §3.2 and Schedule B (exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15) allow up to 15% off NS-500 for that customer only. The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 is also for Acme Manufacturing and expired on 2025-03-31. The Acme Group parent guarantee says "No other company is covered." Nothing I found gives Acme Industrial Supply Co. any special terms, and special terms don't transfer between companies.

The contract also says eligibility doesn't change internal approval authority (MSA §3.3).
