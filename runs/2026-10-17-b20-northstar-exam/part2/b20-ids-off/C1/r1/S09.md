## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? ## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen could not have approved this.** She had enough authority, but Acme was only eligible for 10% off NS-500 on 2025-03-31, not 15%. The outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. Also, there is no request DR-9101 in the Northstar CRM records in the knowledge base.

**The request.** The discount request file has no DR-9101. The closest records are DR-8104 (same terms, dated 2025-09-16, approved by Sarah herself) and DR-9001 (same terms, dated 2026-09-23, pending). I haven't used either one in place of DR-9101. I decided on the request as you gave it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.

**The customer.** CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing", a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. In ERP it is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001).

**The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) was signed in March 2025 but only takes effect on April 1, 2025. The contract the request cites was therefore not yet in force on 2025-03-31.

**The pricing exception.**
- **EXC-ACME-NS500-15** (Schedule B to the agreement, up to 15%) starts 2025-04-01, so it doesn't cover this date.
- **EXC-ACME-NS500-10** (under the older agreement ACME-SA-2023) covers NS-500 orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, up to **10%**. The request date is the last day it applies.
- The newer exception replaces the older one only for requests dated 2025-04-01 or later.

So eligibility on the request date tops out at 10%, and 15% exceeds it.

**The policy and authority.** Pricing Policy 2025 runs 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 15%; anything above 15% needs VP Sales. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 15% was within her own limit. The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix (10% limit for her role) doesn't apply to this date.

**Decision.** Her authority isn't the problem; the customer's eligibility is. A senior approver can't fix a discount above the customer's eligibility. It needs new or amended terms, or the request can be resubmitted at 10% or less, or dated on or after 2025-04-01 under the new exception.
