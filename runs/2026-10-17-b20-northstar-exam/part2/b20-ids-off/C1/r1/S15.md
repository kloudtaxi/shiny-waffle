## Answer

No, Michael Torres can't approve this as submitted, though not because he lacks authority. The request claims "contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", and that agreement is with Acme Manufacturing, not BlueRiver Logistics. The request also isn't in the system of record.

**1. The request.** DR-9107 isn't in `discount_requests.csv`, the discount request log I checked as the CRM system of record. The only pending NS-500 request on 2026-09-23 is DR-9001: Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), 15%, requested by Sarah Chen, with the same justification. I haven't substituted DR-9001. This decision is on DR-9107 as you gave it, and confirming it exists in the CRM is a blocking condition. If DR-9107 was meant to be DR-9001, it needs its own review.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, also recorded as Blue River Logistics LLC. It's a Standard-tier account owned by Kelly Floyd. It has no link to Acme.

**3. The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, with a list price of $250,000. The submitted values match: a $30,000 discount and a net of $220,000.

**4. An active contract.** BlueRiver's only agreement on file is its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025): a Silver support plan covering NS-CLOUD only, with no pricing terms. The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing.

**5. A pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% on NS-500, but only for Acme Manufacturing. No exception names BlueRiver. Special terms don't carry over to other customers, so the stated basis gives BlueRiver no eligibility.

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in effect, from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**7. The requestor's authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales. Under the 2026 policy, VP Sales approves discounts above 10% and up to 20%, so 12% is within his authority. But a customer's contract terms don't change who can approve, and authority alone doesn't establish that the customer is eligible.

**8. The required approver.** The 12% band is VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. He is also the requestor, so he'd be approving his own request. Nothing I found forbids that, but it's worth noting.

**9. Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The request relies on special terms, and no terms on file cover BlueRiver. It needs commercial review. If BlueRiver should get 12% at standard pricing, the request should be resubmitted with that basis. If BlueRiver has its own pricing agreement, that document needs to be provided.
