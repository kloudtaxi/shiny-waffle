## Answer

**Not at 18%.** Acme's contract allows at most 15% on the NS-500. The system of record has DR-9001 at 15%, and at 15% the request can go ahead, but only once Michael Torres (VP Sales) approves it. Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.

**The 18% figure is wrong.** The request you sent says 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). Northstar CRM (`discount_requests.csv`) has DR-9001 at **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), status Pending Approval. Sarah's email to Michael on 2026-09-23 also says Acme is "asking for 15% off list". As the procedure requires, I decided on the system of record's 15% and recorded the difference as a condition.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, product NS-500, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on special contract terms.
2. **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing is CRM-2048 in CRM and Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001) in ERP. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), a separate company.
3. **Product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers the NS-500, so it was active on the request date.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers the NS-500 only, allows up to **15%**, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and gives no employee approval authority.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres (EMP-200).

**Why Sarah can't approve it herself:** She approved the same 15% for Acme last September (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That was allowed then because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that limit to 10%. The contract allowing 15% doesn't change her own limit.

**If 18% really is wanted:** It's above the 15% maximum, so the outcome would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. A more senior approver can't fix that; it would need new or amended contract terms. Acme Manufacturing's account strategy note also guesses at "15%", which agrees with the exception.
