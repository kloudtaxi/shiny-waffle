## Answer

**No. CR-9204 can't be approved. The outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**, because $1,300,000 is more than the most Acme can be given, even with the parent guarantee counted. Separately, I couldn't find CR-9204 in the Northstar ERP extract (`credit_requests.csv`), so I decided on the request as you gave it.

1. **The request.** The ERP extract has no CR-9204. The only Acme request there is **CR-9201**: same customer (C-1001), same requestor and date, but $250,000 → **$400,000**, and justified by the third-plant ramp-up, not by the guarantee. I didn't substitute CR-9201. Sarah's email to Priya Shah only says the guarantee "should cover it" if Acme needs more headroom later. Before anything is done with CR-9204, someone needs to confirm it exists.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and the company named in the guarantee are the same company: same address and DUNS number, 04-812-7730. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 covers requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history** (the rule is nothing paid or left unpaid more than 30 days past due, looking back 12 months). There's no problem here:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) was due 2026-10-02, so it wasn't yet due on the request date. It's still unpaid in the extract. As of today (2026-10-18) that's 16 days late, which is within the 30-day limit.
5. **Maximum limit.** CRM lists Acme as a Strategic account, so the maximum is $750,000. The guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc.) names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is capped at $400,000. That raises the maximum to **$1,150,000**. The requested $1,300,000 is $150,000 over it.
6. **Requestor authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the policy gives that role no authority to approve credit limits.
7. **Approvers, for reference only.** Above $500,000 the policy needs the Director of Finance (Elena Novak). For a Strategic account it also needs the VP Sales to concur (Michael Torres). Sarah isn't either of them, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't change this. Getting their sign-off still wouldn't make Acme eligible for this amount.

A request for up to $1,150,000 would be within the maximum. It would need Elena Novak's approval and Michael Torres's concurrence.
