## Answer

No, Sarah Chen could not have approved this herself. Her limit is 15% and the request is for 22%, so it needs VP Sales approval. There's also a bigger problem: **DR-9103 isn't in Northstar CRM.**

**The request isn't in the system of record.** The discount requests export (discount_requests.csv) has no DR-9103, and the knowledge base has no record of that ID anywhere. Sarah's only requests for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048) are DR-8104 (NS-500, 15%, 2025-09-16, approved) and DR-9001 (NS-500, 15%, 2026-09-23, pending). Neither is for NS-EDGE at 22%. I haven't used either one in its place; the decision below is on the request as you gave it.

**The request as given:**
- **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. Note that "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091) is a different account.
- **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. The figures add up: 22% of $85,000 is $18,700, leaving $66,300.
- **Basis:** The justification is "Competitive", so this is standard pricing. It doesn't rely on any contract term.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2025-09-23, and it covers NS-EDGE. It doesn't give a discount on NS-EDGE, though. Acme's only pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, max 15%) covers the NS-500 only. That doesn't matter here because the request doesn't rely on special terms.
- **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applied on that date (it covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 15%; anything above 15% needs VP Sales. The 2026 matrix (≤10% / ≤20% / >20% for the CRO) doesn't apply yet.
- **Authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so 22% is beyond her limit. The VP Sales is Michael Torres. The org chart is only dated 2026-09-01, but he approved other above-15% requests in 2025 (DR-7008 on 2025-07-15 and DR-7012 on 2025-11-07), which suggests he held the role on 2025-09-23.

**Decision:** Approve, but only with Michael Torres's sign-off as VP Sales. Before anything happens, someone must confirm that DR-9103 actually exists in Northstar CRM. Until then, this decision can't be acted on.
