> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy she can only approve up to 10%. DR-9001 needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 is in Northstar CRM, and the record matches what you sent: CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on the customer's contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (DUNS 04-812-7730, the contract says "doing business as Acme Manufacturing"). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a different company.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Active contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15%, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It covers this product and date, and 15% is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible. It also says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) covered orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31 and has been replaced by the 15% one, so it doesn't apply.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and under that policy her role can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager.

**Why last September's approval doesn't apply now:** Sarah approved the same 15% discount herself in DR-8104 on 2025-09-16. That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered the limit to 10%. As the approval matrix puts it, "authority attaches to the role, not to the individual or the account."

Nothing else needs to happen before Michael approves. The policy does require that approvals above 10% are recorded in Northstar CRM.
