## Answer

**SR-39340 is a Severity 1 ticket, not P3. Northstar missed both the response and the restoration targets, and owes Cedar a $320.00 service credit.** The credit is paid at Severity 2 rates because Cedar never reported the outage by phone.

**1. Customer and scope.** The customer is Cedar Health Systems Inc.: CRM-2063, ERP C-1003, DUNS 02-906-4418. Its support terms (SUP-CHS-2025) put it on the Silver plan at $4,000 a month and cover NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD). Coverage started 2025-12-01. The ticket opened on 2026-06-10, so it is in scope.

**2. True severity: Severity 1.** The severity guide makes it Severity 1 when NS-Cloud is unavailable to all of the customer's users and there is no workaround. The ticket recorded "All facilities", and the engineer confirmed that all users were affected and no workaround existed. The impact never changed after opening, so Severity 1 applies from the open time. That means a round-the-clock clock (nights and weekends count) under schedule v2.0, which covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01.

**3. Measurement (all times in Central Time, as the schedule requires).**
- **Opened:** 07:05 Eastern by portal, which is 06:05 Central. No pauses or maintenance windows are recorded.
- **Initial response (target 30 minutes, due 06:35): missed.** The first engineer response came at 08:20 Central, 2 h 15 m after opening.
- **Restoration (target 4 hours, due 10:05): missed.** Service was restored at 11:35 Central, after 5 h 30 m. That is more than the target but no more than twice it, so it falls in the lower penalty band (band 1).

**4. Credit.**
- Severity 1 credit rates apply only if the customer phoned the Support hotline before restoration. Cedar opened the ticket by portal, and Northstar's call to Cedar doesn't count as a phone report. With no phone report recorded, Severity 2 rates apply, though the breaches are still measured against the Severity 1 targets.
- At Severity 2 rates: missed response 3% plus restoration band 1 5% = 8% of $4,000 = **$320.00**.
- The monthly cap is 30% of the fee, $1,200. No other Cedar ticket opened in June, so the cap isn't reached.
- Cedar's claim was timely. It arrived by email on 2026-06-29 at 10:00 Central, naming the ticket; the last day to claim was 2026-07-10.

**5. What Northstar owes.** The escalation duties follow the true severity, and the phone rule doesn't change them. The record doesn't say whether any of them were carried out.
- **06:20 Central:** Hannah Lindqvist (Support Escalation Manager) engaged as incident lead.
- **06:35:** Anthony Day (account owner in the CRM) notified.
- **08:05:** Marcus Adeyemi (Director of Customer Support) notified once 50% of the restoration target passed without restoration.
- **10:05:** Michael Torres (VP Sales) notified when the full target passed without restoration. Cedar's CRM tier is Standard, not Strategic, so the Chief Revenue Officer isn't required.
- **Claim decision:** Marcus Adeyemi must send a written decision no later than 2026-07-29, the 30th day after receipt. The schedule gives that day, not a time; I've used the end of the day (23:59 Central) in the record below.
- **Credit memo:** Paul Brennan (Support Billing Manager) issues it on the day the credit is approved, against the next support invoice. The approval date isn't recorded.
- **Root cause:** I found no root-cause requirement for this ticket.

**6. What the customer had to do.**

- **File a timely written credit claim: met.**
