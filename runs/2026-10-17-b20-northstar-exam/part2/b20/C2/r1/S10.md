**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** A 25% discount is above her limit, so the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan, has to approve it. There's also a bigger problem: I can't find request DR-9102 in the CRM discount-request records. That has to be confirmed before anyone acts on it.

1. **The request:** DR-9102, 25% off NS-EDGE ($85,000 list, $21,250 discount, $63,750 net), dated 2026-09-23, raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example. The justification is "Competitive", so it rests on standard pricing, not on contract terms. The only open Acme request on record for that date is DR-9001, a 15% discount on NS-500 that cites the Master Supply Agreement. That's a different request, so I haven't decided on it.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings). They share a DUNS number, 04-812-7730.
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003, Software), list price $85,000. That matches the submitted figures.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date, and it covers NS-Edge. But its 15% discount term applies only to the NS-500 controller. It gives no discount right on NS-Edge, and the request doesn't claim one.
5. **Pricing exceptions:** Both Acme exceptions on file (Strategic Account and Volume Pricing) are for NS-500 only. None covers NS-EDGE.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Her authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under that policy she can approve up to and including 10%, so 25% is over her limit.
8. **Who must approve:** Over 10% up to 20% goes to the VP Sales; over 20% goes to the Chief Revenue Officer. David Morgan holds the CRO role. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for anything over 10%.

**Decision: approve only with CRO authorization.** Because the request uses standard pricing, no contract eligibility needs to be established. If the request were changed to rely on the contract or an exception, it would need commercial review, because none of those terms cover NS-Edge.
