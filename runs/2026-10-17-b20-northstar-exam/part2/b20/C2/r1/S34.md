## Answer

I wouldn't act on CR-9208 yet, because the request isn't in the system of record. As submitted, it would need sign-off from two people besides Michael Torres: Elena Novak, Director of Finance, has to approve it, and David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer (CRO), has to concur.

**1. The request isn't in Northstar ERP.** The ERP credit request log has no CR-9208. Its only pending Acme request on 2026-09-23 is **CR-9201**, which has the same justification ("Third-plant ramp-up…") but a different requestor and amount: Sarah Chen, $250,000 → $400,000. I decided on CR-9208 as you gave it and did not swap in CR-9201. Someone needs to confirm whether CR-9208 exists, or whether it's really CR-9201 with a different requestor and amount, before anything is done.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. The legal documents say Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. CRM records the account tier as **Strategic**.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 was in force on 2026-09-23 (it runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It fully replaced the 2025 policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. None of INV-30001 to 30020 belongs to C-1001, so no late invoices were found. However, the knowledge base reports 30 invoice records and only those 20 could be pulled up. Acme's $480,000 order SO-7001 (completed 2026-06-10) also has no invoice I could find. Payment history should be checked against the full invoice ledger.

**5. Maximum limit.** The maximum for Strategic accounts is $750,000, and $650,000 is within it. The request doesn't rely on credit support. There is a $400,000 parent guarantee from Acme Group Holdings (GRT-ACME-2026) naming Acme Mfg. Holdings, but it isn't needed here.

**6. Michael Torres's authority.** He is VP Sales, which isn't one of the policy's approver roles, so he can't approve this himself. Under the policy, authority is judged on the new total limit of $650,000.

**7. Required approvers.**
- **Approval:** a limit over $500,000 needs the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**.
- **Concurrence:** a Strategic account over $500,000 also needs VP Sales to concur. The VP Sales is Michael Torres, but he submitted the request, and the 2026 policy says no one may approve or concur on a request they submitted. In that case the concurrence passes to the requestor's manager. The HR record (EMP-200 reports to EMP-300) makes that **David Morgan, CRO**.
- One org-chart entry reads the other way, as if David Morgan reports to Michael Torres. It looks like a misread, but if it were right, the concurrence would go to someone else. So Finance should confirm the reporting line.

**8. Decision.** The customer is eligible on the evidence available, and the limit needs the approvers the policy names. The outcome is `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`. Two checks block it until they're cleared: confirming the request exists, and confirming the payment history against the full invoice ledger.
