## Answer

I've checked the customer, the contract, the pricing exception and the 2026 policy. Here's the decision.

**No, Michael Torres can't approve this. The outcome is `REJECT_OR_ESCALATE`.** Under the 2026 policy, his VP Sales role covers discounts of 10–20%. But 18% is more than Acme is entitled to: its contract caps the NS-500 discount at 15%. A higher approver can't fix that; it needs new or amended terms. Separately, request DR-9112 isn't in the CRM records I have.

1. **The request.** DR-9112: 18% off NS-500, list $250,000, discount $45,000, net $205,000. Requested by Michael Torres on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms.
   - **DR-9112 isn't in the system of record.** The only CRM request for this account, product and date is **DR-9001**: 15%, $37,500 discount, requested by Sarah Chen, with the same justification. I haven't decided on DR-9001 in its place. Confirming DR-9112 is a blocking condition below.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The contract says Acme Mfg. Holdings is "doing business as Acme Manufacturing", and both records share DUNS 04-812-7730. The account is Strategic.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract: yes.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It makes Acme "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller", and "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception: yes, and it also caps at 15%.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025. It covers only NS-500, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and sets a "maximum eligible discount: 15% of list price." It was approved by David Morgan. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
   - **18% exceeds this cap by 3 points.**
   - A separate "Acme NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception" refers to an older agreement, ACME-SA-2023, and nothing in the knowledge base shows it was in force in 2026.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, is the policy that applies.
7. **His authority.** Account executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales, which is Michael Torres's role. Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
8. **Decision.** His authority isn't the problem; Acme's 15% eligibility is. A contract allowing a discount doesn't widen anyone's approval authority, and approval authority doesn't widen a customer's eligibility.

Michael Torres is also the requestor, so he would be approving his own request. That doesn't change this outcome, but it would matter for a resubmission.
