## Answer

**Yes, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.** Acme qualifies for a $400,000 limit on its own payment record, and the approval has to come from the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. The outcome is APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.

1. **The request:** CR-9201 in Northstar ERP matches what you sent: customer C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. The ERP justification is the third-plant ramp-up. In a message, Sarah also said "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it," but the limit doesn't need the guarantee, so I treated this as a standard request.
2. **The customer:** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (same DUNS 04-812-7730, and Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing). Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company, so its invoices don't count here.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** the rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 ($85,000): due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 ($480,000): due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000): due 2026-10-02, so it wasn't yet due on the request date. It is still unpaid.
5. **Maximum limit:** CRM records Acme as a Strategic account, and the maximum for Strategic accounts is $750,000. $400,000 is within that. Acme Group Holdings' parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, $400,000, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) does name Acme Mfg. Holdings, but it isn't needed.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** she is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives no credit approval authority to that role; the lowest band is Accounts Receivable Specialists, up to $100,000.
7. **Who must approve:** limits above $100,000 up to $500,000 need the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic limits above $500,000, so Michael Torres isn't needed.

Two points, neither blocking: invoice INV-62305 ($720,000) is now 16 days past due as of today and, if it stays unpaid, crosses the 30-day line on 2026-11-01. It is also larger than the new $400,000 limit.
