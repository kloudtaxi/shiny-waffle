## Answer

The answer is **not yet.** On the evidence, Acme is eligible for $650,000. But the request can't be acted on until two things are confirmed, and it would need sign-off from Elena Novak and Michael Torres.

**The main problem: CR-9202 isn't in the ERP's credit-request records.** The ERP has a different request for the same customer: **CR-9201**. It has the same requestor (Sarah Chen), date (2026-09-23), current limit ($250,000) and justification. It also shows "Pending Approval", but it asks for **$400,000**, not $650,000. I assessed CR-9202 as you submitted it and did not use CR-9201 in its place. Someone needs to confirm whether CR-9202 is a real, separate request or the wrong ID and amount for CR-9201.

**How the request checks out:**
- **Customer:** ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", the same company as "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730). The CRM puts it in the **Strategic** tier.
- **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It is in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.
- **Maximum limit:** $750,000 for Strategic accounts, so $650,000 fits on Acme's own standing. A parent-company guarantee exists (GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc., up to $400,000, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) and names this customer. The request doesn't depend on it, so I treated it as a standard request.
- **Payment history:** The policy rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. I checked the 20 invoices in the ERP list (INV-30001 to INV-30020), and none are billed to C-1001, so nothing breaks the rule. However, the ERP shows a completed $480,000 order for Acme (SO-7001, 2026-06-10). No invoice for it appears in what I found. If one exists and was more than 30 days late by 2026-09-23, Acme would not be eligible. Accounts Receivable should check this before anyone acts.
- **Who can approve:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales and has no credit-approval authority under the policy. The limit is judged on the new total of $650,000. Above $500,000 it needs **Director of Finance** approval, which is **Elena Novak**. Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs **VP Sales** concurrence, which is **Michael Torres** (Sarah's manager).
