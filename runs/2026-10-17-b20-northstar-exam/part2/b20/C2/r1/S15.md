Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing. **DR-9107 isn't in the system of record I can reach.** Neither the discount request records nor any CRM identifier contain it. The CRM does hold DR-9001, an Acme Manufacturing request (CRM-2048) for NS-500 on the same date, with the same list value and the exact same justification. I've decided on DR-9107 as you gave it, not on DR-9001.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, a Standard-tier account owned by Kelly Floyd. It appears in the support terms as "Blue River Logistics LLC". It is not an Acme company.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** BlueRiver's only agreement is its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025): Silver support for NS-Cloud, not NS-500, with no pricing terms. I found no BlueRiver supply agreement.
5. **Pricing exception.** The only NS-500 exception is EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15%, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31). Its customer is Acme Manufacturing, under agreement ACME-MFG-2025, so it doesn't cover BlueRiver. Special terms don't carry over to another customer.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The bands are: Enterprise Account Executives up to and including 10%, VP Sales above 10% up to 20%, and Chief Revenue Officer above 20%. Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.
7. **Authority.** 12% is in the VP Sales band, and Torres holds that role. But the policy's approval authority doesn't establish that the customer is eligible for special terms.

**Outcome: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.** The request needs commercial review. It could be re-based on standard pricing, where 12% would still need VP Sales approval and recorded approval evidence. Or BlueRiver could get its own NS-500 exception. Separately, someone needs to confirm DR-9107 exists in the CRM. Its justification matches Acme's DR-9001 word for word, which suggests it was copied from that request.
