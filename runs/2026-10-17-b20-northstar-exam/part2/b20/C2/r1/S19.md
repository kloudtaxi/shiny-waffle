## Answer

Yes. A 6% discount is within Sarah Chen's own approval authority. But DR-9111 isn't in the system of record, so the decision can't be acted on until someone confirms the request exists in Northstar CRM.

**1. The request.** No record in the knowledge base holds DR-9111. The CRM discount-request export has only one Acme request on 2026-09-23: DR-9001. That one is for NS-500 at 15%, justified as "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". DR-9001 is a different request, so I decided on DR-9111 as you gave it. Its justification is "Competitive", which means it rests on standard pricing, not on a contract term or exception. The figures add up: 6% of $120,000 is $7,200, leaving $112,800 net.

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing", Strategic, Midwest Enterprise) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings"). Both carry DUNS 04-812-7730, and Sarah Chen is the account owner.

**3. The product.** SKU NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. The account notes say Acme is evaluating NS-Cloud and planning a pilot on one line.

**4–5. Contract and exception.** The request doesn't rely on either. Acme's negotiated preferential pricing (15%) covers NS-500 and doesn't extend to NS-Cloud. That doesn't matter here, because this request rests on standard pricing.

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force, covering 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replacing the 2025 policy.

**7–8. Authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales (her manager is Michael Torres, VP Sales), and above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. At 6%, no higher approver is needed, and recorded approval evidence is only required above 10%.

**9. Decision.** Approve, with one blocking condition: confirm DR-9111 exists in Northstar CRM and matches the details given.
