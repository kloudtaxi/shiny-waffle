# SR-39802: out of scope (Acme Industrial Supply Co. does not have the Acme Platinum plan)

**Outcome: `OUT_OF_SCOPE`.** No service level could be breached and no credit is owed. Northstar's one documented duty is to tell the account owner, Zachary Brown, by **2026-08-10 18:00 CT**.

**1. The customer.** The ticket is linked to CRM account CRM-2091: **Acme Industrial Supply Co.**, 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH. Its ERP customer number is C-1044 and its DUNS number is 09-552-1187. This is not ACME Manufacturing (Acme Mfg. Holdings), which has CRM-2048, ERP C-1001 and DUNS 04-812-7730.

**2. Scope.** The claim of the "Acme Platinum support plan" comes only from the caller's own words on the ticket ("We have the Acme Platinum support plan"). The Platinum plan is in *Schedule C (Support and Service Levels) — Acme Manufacturing* (SUP-ACME-C, under agreement ACME-MFG-2025). That schedule covers Acme Mfg. Holdings for the NS-500 and NS-Edge products. It also says plainly: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered,"* and coverage *"does not extend to … any other affiliate."* The knowledge base has no support terms of Acme Industrial's own (its CRM tier is "Standard"). So no terms cover this customer and product, and the out-of-scope handling applies. The Service Level Schedule says out-of-scope tickets are handled "best effort", "no targets apply", "nothing can be breached", "no credit is owed", and the escalations don't apply.

**3. True severity: Severity 1 (Critical), for internal priority only.** The record says the NS-Edge gateways were offline, conveyor monitoring was dark, picking had halted and there was no workaround. A distribution business's operations had stopped, which meets the Severity 1 test. The customer also reported P1. For an out-of-scope ticket, severity is "classified only for internal priority" and sets no targets.

**4–6. Service levels.** The ticket was opened by phone at 2026-08-10 06:05 EDT (05:05 CT). The engineer first responded at 07:40 EDT, and service was restored at 14:20 EDT. Since no targets apply, these times are not measured against anything.

**7. Credits.** Amy Barrett emailed a credit claim at 2026-08-14 10:00 EDT ("Please apply the Severity 1 restoration credit under Acme's Platinum plan"). No credit is owed: the claim relies on a plan that belongs to a different company. The out-of-scope handling sets no formal duty to decide the claim or issue a credit memo, though it would be sensible to reply and explain why it is declined.

**8. What Northstar owes**
- **Notify the account owner:** Northstar Support must notify the account owner of CRM-2091, **Zachary Brown**, within 10 business hours of the open time. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, except Northstar holidays. The ticket opened before business hours on Monday 2026-08-10, so the clock runs from 08:00 CT. That makes the deadline **2026-08-10 18:00 CT (18:00:00-05:00)**.
- Nobody else needs to be engaged or escalated to.
- **Two gaps in the record:**
  - The holiday calendar says its list is complete, but I couldn't see which dates are on it. I assumed 2026-08-10 is not a Northstar holiday. If it is, the deadline moves later.
  - Nothing shows whether this notification was actually sent.

**What the customer must do:** none of the Schedule's customer obligations apply (the phone report for Severity 1, the timely claim). For the record, Acme Industrial did report by phone and did send a claim, but neither creates any right to a credit.
