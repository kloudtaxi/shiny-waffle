## Question (verbatim)

> Can a request to raise Acme Manufacturing's credit limit to $1,300,000 (CR-9204, Sarah Chen), supported by the parent guarantee, be approved on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

No, CR-9204 can't be approved. Even counting the parent guarantee, the most Acme can be given is $1,150,000, and the request is for $1,300,000. Sending it to a more senior approver doesn't change that.

**1. The request.** Northstar ERP shows CR-9204: customer C-1001, limit $250,000 → $1,300,000, raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. It relies on the parent guarantee. I couldn't find a separate record for CR-9204 in the knowledge base, so I've used the ERP record you supplied.

**2. The customer.** "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (ERP C-1001), "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) and "ACME Manufacturing" are the same company: same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and same address. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091). The CRM lists Acme Manufacturing as a **Strategic** account.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (CREDIT-POLICY-2026) applies. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme's record is clean:
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000): due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it wasn't due yet on 2026-09-23.

**5. Maximum limit (this is why it fails).**
- The policy maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000.
- A parent-company guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, for the customer it names.
- The guarantee is GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing", runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and caps the guarantor's liability at **$400,000**. So it covers this customer on the request date.
- That gives a maximum of $750,000 + $400,000 = **$1,150,000**. The $1,300,000 requested is **$150,000 over**.

**6. The requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager (over $100,000 up to $500,000) and the Director of Finance (over $500,000). She can't approve any amount herself.

**7. Approvers, if the limit were within the maximum.** A $1,300,000 limit would need Director of Finance approval plus VP Sales agreement (required for Strategic accounts over $500,000). Their approval wouldn't fix the over-maximum problem. The knowledge base doesn't say who holds either role.

**8. Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** If the request is resubmitted at $1,150,000 or less, it would still need the Director of Finance and the VP Sales.
