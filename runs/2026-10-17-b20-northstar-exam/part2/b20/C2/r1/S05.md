Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on a contract term, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "ACME Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (DUNS 04-812-7730).
3. **The product.** SKU NS-500.
4. **Active contract.** I found no Master Supply Agreement, and no other Acme pricing contract. The only Acme agreement on file is the Parent Company Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026, which is a credit guarantee and has nothing to do with pricing. All that supports the 15% is hearsay:
   - Sarah's email to Michael Torres says "their agreement covers it" and "which is what we did for them last September".
   - The account strategy note says "Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement" and "I believe it's 15%".
   
   None of these is the agreement itself, and none names a percentage, product scope or dates that are confirmed.
5. **Pricing exception.** None on record for NS-500 or Acme. The 2026 pricing policy also says "Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed."
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy, with lower limits, only applies to requests dated 2027-01-01 or later.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and they may approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over her limit, and a contract would not raise it.
8. **Required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval and recorded approval evidence. That role is held by Michael Torres (michael.torres@northstar.example).
9. **Decision.** The request relies on a contract term that no available document establishes, so the procedure says to ask for the document before deciding. If the agreement turns up and covers NS-500 at 15% or more on 2026-09-23, the expected outcome is APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION by Michael Torres. If it caps the discount below 15%, the outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. A past approval ("last September") doesn't establish that Acme is eligible now.
