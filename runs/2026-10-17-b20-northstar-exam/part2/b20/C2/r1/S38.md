## Question (verbatim)

> Ticket SR-39561 (BlueRiver Logistics, NS-Cloud down for all users): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39561: no service level was breached, so no credit is owed.** The customer claimed the Severity 1 restoration credit for a 5 h 45 min outage. That figure counts time inside a scheduled maintenance window that the schedule excludes. Once the window is excluded, both targets were met.

**1. Customer.** CRM account CRM-2051 (BlueRiver Logistics) has DUNS 07-331-9025. The same DUNS belongs to ERP customer C-1002, Blue River Logistics LLC, so all three identifiers point to one customer.

**2. Scope: covered.** Support Terms SUP-BRL-2025 put the customer on the Silver plan for NS-Cloud Operations Suite (NS-CLOUD). Coverage started on 2025-01-01, well before the ticket.

**3. True severity: Severity 1 (Critical).** The ticket record says "NS-Cloud has been down for all our users" since about 23:10, and "No workaround". That meets Severity 1 condition (b): NS-Cloud unavailable to all of the customer's users, with no workaround. It also matches the customer's reported P1.
- **Caveat:** the same source document also holds another BlueRiver ticket, SR-39881, a P2 at the St. Louis and Memphis depots. The line "The other depots are fine" appears to belong to SR-39881, but the knowledge base can't confirm which ticket it belongs to. If it actually described SR-39561, the ticket would be Severity 2. The result is still no breach: Severity 2 on Silver runs on business hours, and the whole incident fell on a weekend.

**4. Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened on 2026-07-11, so Schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01 CT). Severity 1 targets are a 30-minute response and 4-hour restoration, on a 24×7 clock. All timestamps are recorded at −05:00, which is Central Time in July, so no conversion is needed.

**5. Clock start, pauses and exclusions.**
- The ticket was opened by phone at 2026-07-11T23:20-05:00.
- Maintenance notice MN-2607 covered NS-Cloud from 2026-07-11 22:00 to 2026-07-12 02:00. It was sent on 2026-06-26, at least 8 business days before the window, well over the 5 required.
- Time inside a qualifying window counts on neither clock, so counting starts at 02:00.
- No pauses are recorded.

**6. Measurement.**

| Target | Result |
|---|---|
| Response (30 min) | **Met.** An engineer phoned at 23:35, inside the window, so no time counted. Even on raw time it was 15 minutes. |
| Restoration (4 h) | **Met.** Restored at 05:05, so 3 h 05 min counted. |

Without the exclusion, 5 h 45 min would have been a band-1 restoration miss. That band pays 10% of the $2,500 monthly fee, or $250. With the exclusion, nothing is owed.

**7–8. What Northstar owes**
- **Decide the claim:** reject it, since no target was missed. The deadline is 30 calendar days after receipt, which is 2026-08-13. The knowledge base names no role or holder for claim decisions, and doesn't record whether a decision was made. That date has already passed (today is 2026-10-18).
- **No credit memo.** Credit memos are issued only for approved credits. They would come from the Support Billing Manager, Paul Brennan.
- **Escalations:** the knowledge base has only fragments of the Escalation Procedure. It says escalation follows true severity and thresholds are based on the restoration target. It doesn't give the threshold percentages or which roles are engaged when. So I can't state who had to be engaged or by when, or whether a root-cause finding is required. The roles that exist are the Support Escalation Manager (Hannah Lindqvist), the Support Engineering Lead (Owen Takahashi) and the Director of Customer Support (Marcus Adeyemi).

**What the customer must do, and whether it did**
- **Report a Severity 1 by phone:** met. The ticket was opened by phone.
- **Keep a technical contact reachable by phone until restoration:** met. Northstar reached the night dispatch supervisor, and no failed call is recorded.
- **Claim in writing within 30 days of restoration** (by 2026-08-11): met. The emailed claim arrived on 2026-07-14, but it earns nothing because nothing was breached. The record doesn't say whether it went to support-credits@northstar.example.
