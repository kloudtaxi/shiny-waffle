## Answer

**No, CR-9203 can't be approved as it stands. The outcome is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** Acme qualifies for at most $750,000 on its own, and the parent guarantee the request depends on isn't documented anywhere in the knowledge base. There is also a second problem: CR-9203 itself isn't in the ERP.

1. **The request isn't in the system of record.** The ERP record for customer C-1001 shows only **CR-9201**: $250,000 → $400,000, by Sarah Chen, dated 2026-09-23, justified by the "Third-plant ramp-up," status Pending Approval. Sarah's own message says "I've raised CR-9201." I decided on CR-9203 as you gave it, not on CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9203 exists is a blocking condition.
2. **The customer.** "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048), "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (ERP C-1001) and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. Its tier is Strategic. Its parent is named as "Acme Group Holdings, Inc."
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 covers requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history is clean.** The policy rule: no invoice due in the prior 12 months may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid early, on 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 ($480,000) was due 2026-07-25 and paid early, on 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) is open, but it wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
5. **The maximum is the deciding issue.** The Strategic maximum is **$750,000**, and $1,000,000 is above it. The policy lets "a parent-company guarantee in force" raise the maximum by the guaranteed amount, but only "for the customer the guarantee names." The only evidence of a guarantee is a chat saying "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it" and "Gina says it's still in place." I found no guarantee document. That means nothing establishes which company it names, how much it covers, or when it is in force. The guarantor is also called "Acme Group" there, not Acme Group Holdings, Inc., so it isn't even clear it's the same parent.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives her no credit-approval authority.
7. **Approvers needed if the guarantee is produced.** Limits over $500,000 need Director of Finance approval, plus VP Sales concurrence because Acme is a Strategic account. The knowledge base doesn't say who holds either role.

If a signed guarantee naming Acme Mfg. Holdings, in force on 2026-09-23 and covering at least $250,000, turns up, the request becomes eligible. It would then need the Director of Finance's approval and the VP Sales's concurrence. If no guarantee is found, it should be rejected or escalated.
