## Answer

**Answer:** Acme can receive a $1,000,000 limit, but Sarah Chen can't approve it alone. It needs approval from the Director of Finance (Elena Novak) and concurrence from the VP Sales (Michael Torres). One thing blocks it first: there is no request CR-9203 in Northstar ERP. The only Acme request pending in ERP is **CR-9201**, which asks to raise the limit from $250,000 to **$400,000**. It has the same date and requestor, but a different reason ("Third-plant ramp-up"). I decided on CR-9203 as you gave it, and confirming that it exists is a blocking condition.

1. **The request:** CR-9203, ERP customer C-1001, from $250,000 to $1,000,000. It was raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23 and relies on the parent guarantee.
2. **The customer:** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. CRM records it as a **Strategic** account.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. I checked the ERP invoices INV-30001 to INV-30020, and none belongs to C-1001, so there are no late invoices. However, ERP shows order SO-7001 for C-1001 ($480,000, 2026-06-10) as "Completed", and I found no invoice for it. Finance should confirm the receivables record for C-1001.
5. **The maximum limit:** $1,150,000, which covers the $1,000,000 requested.
   - The 2026 policy caps Strategic accounts at $750,000.
   - A parent-company guarantee in force raises that cap by the guaranteed amount.
   - Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 is given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. for its subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings, which is this customer. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is capped at $400,000.
6. **The requestor's authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives that role no credit approval authority.
7. **Required approvers:** Limits above $500,000 need Director of Finance approval (Elena Novak). Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence (Michael Torres). Michael Torres is Sarah Chen's manager; I found no rule in the policy that stops him from concurring.
