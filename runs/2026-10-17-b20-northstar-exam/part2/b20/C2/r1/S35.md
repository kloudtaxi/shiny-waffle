## Answer

**Yes, but not by Sarah alone.** Acme qualifies, but Sarah Chen has no authority to approve credit limits. Priya Shah, the Finance Manager, has to approve it. There's also a figure to sort out first: the request you sent asks for **$300,000**, but the ERP record of CR-9201 says **$400,000**. I decided on the ERP figure. Acme also qualifies at $300,000, so the outcome is the same either way.

1. **The request:** ERP record CR-9201 is for customer C-1001. It raises the limit from $250,000 to $400,000, was submitted by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, and is "Pending Approval". It relies on Acme's own standing. The tier maximum covers it, so it doesn't need the parent guarantee.
2. **The customer:** ERP "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), CRM "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) and the legal name in the guarantee are one company. They share DUNS 04-812-7730. CRM lists the account as **Strategic**, and Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 ($85k) was due 2025-11-28 and paid early on 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 ($480k) was due 2026-07-25 and paid early on 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720k) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.
   - The C-1002 invoices belong to a different customer, Blue River Logistics, and don't count.
5. **The maximum:** $750,000 for Strategic accounts, so $400,000 is within it. The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings) names Acme Mfg. Holdings and runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It would raise the maximum by $400,000, but this request doesn't need it.
6. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to Accounts Receivable Specialists (up to $100k), the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance.
7. **Who must approve:** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.

Two things to flag:
- **INV-62305 ($720,000) is unpaid.** As of today (2026-10-18) it's 16 days past its due date. That's under the 30-day threshold, but the open balance alone is already above the requested $400,000 limit.
- **The guarantee only counts if it's cited.** If the request is meant to rely on it, the request should say so.
