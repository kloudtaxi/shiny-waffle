## Question (verbatim)

> Ticket SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data lost for six presses, reported P1): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing): true severity is 2, not P1. Neither target was missed, so no credit is owed and no escalation was triggered.** The restoration target was met with no time to spare: restoration came at exactly the deadline.

**Customer and scope.** The ticket carries CRM account CRM-2048. That account matches ERP C-1001 and DUNS 04-812-7730 (Acme Mfg. Holdings). It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which Schedule C says is not covered. Schedule C to ACME-MFG-2025 covers the NS-Edge Monitoring Package on the Platinum plan, with coverage from 2025-04-01. The ticket opened on 2026-07-23, so it is in scope.

**True severity: 2 (High).** The ticket records that vibration data for presses 1–6 (of 14) at Milwaukee Plant 2 was lost. It also records that "The presses are still running" and that technicians were doing manual vibration checks every two hours. Production didn't stop and a workaround existed, so it is not Severity 1. The severity guide's Severity 2 criterion (b) fits exactly: a condition-monitoring function is unavailable for one or more machines "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks". The reported P1 doesn't govern.

**Schedule and targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies, because the ticket opened after 2026-04-01 00:00 CT. Times below are US Central (UTC−05:00 in July). For Severity 2 on Platinum the targets are a 2-hour initial response and 12-hour restoration, on a 24×7 clock.

**One risk.** This result depends on the pause ending at 08:30. The event log says Kelly Ballard provided the credentials, but the only timestamp I can see is the 08:30 status change logged by Johnny Johnson. The log also says an engineer phoned Kelly Ballard, but I couldn't confirm that the call was on this ticket or when it happened. If the credentials were recorded as received any earlier than 08:30, restoration was missed (band 1). That would mean a 5% credit if Acme filed a timely claim, and the restoration notification below would have been due. It's worth checking the raw ticket log.

**What Northstar owes.** For Severity 2, the escalation procedure only requires action if a target lapses:
- **No initial response by the response deadline (15:10):** notify the Support Escalation Manager. This wasn't triggered, because the response came at 15:00.
- **Not restored by the restoration deadline (2026-07-24 18:40):** notify the Director of Customer Support and the account owner (Sarah Chen, per CRM). This wasn't triggered, because the ticket was restored at that moment.

There's no credit, claim decision or credit memo to handle. The documents I reviewed require no root-cause finding for this ticket.

**What Acme must do.**
- **Give Northstar the information and access it needs:** done. The VPN credentials were provided.
- **Telephone report:** not required, because that rule applies only to Severity 1. The portal report was fine.
- **Credit claim:** none is needed because nothing was breached. The claim window would have closed 30 calendar days after restoration anyway.
