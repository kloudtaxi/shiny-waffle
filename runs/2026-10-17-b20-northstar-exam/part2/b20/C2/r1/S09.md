> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? ## Answer

I've checked the records. Here's the answer.

**No. Sarah Chen could not have approved this. The problem is the customer's terms on that date, not her approval limit.**

1. **The request isn't in the CRM as given.** No request DR-9101 exists. The CRM has **DR-9001**, which matches on account, product, 15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, requestor and justification, but is dated **2026-09-23**. I haven't decided on DR-9001 instead. I decided on DR-9101 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists.
2. **Customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same address and DUNS 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract in force on 2025-03-31.** The request cites the "Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025). It allows up to 15% on NS-500, but **it only takes effect on 1 April 2025**, the day after the request date. The agreement in force on 31 March was **ACME-SA-2023**, which allows **up to 10%** on NS-500 for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.
5. **Pricing exception.** The 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) is also **effective from 2025-04-01**. It replaces the 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) only for requests dated 1 April 2025 or later. So on 31 March, Acme's maximum was 10%.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025).
7. **Her authority.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. The policy lets that role approve discounts up to and including 15%, so 15% was within her personal limit. Anything above 15% needs the VP Sales.
8. **Decision.** Her approval limit isn't the issue. 15% is above Acme's 10% maximum on that date. Neither Sarah nor a more senior approver can fix that. It needs new or amended terms, or a request dated on or after 2025-04-01, when the 15% terms apply. At 10% or less, she could have approved it herself. (Records I checked but didn't need for this outcome: the 2026 policy and approval matrix, which aren't in force on this date, and who holds the VP Sales role.)
