## Answer

**No, it can't be approved as submitted. The decision is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The special terms this request relies on belong to a different customer, and there's a record problem too.

**1. The request.** I couldn't find DR-9105 in the knowledge base. Northstar CRM does hold DR-9001, which has exactly the same figures: NS-500, 15.0%, list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, sarah.chen@northstar.example, 2026-09-23, same justification, "Pending Approval". But DR-9001 is on account **CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing)**, not CRM-2091. I haven't decided on DR-9001 in its place. I decided on DR-9105 as you gave it, and confirming it exists in the CRM is a blocking condition. It looks like either a mis-keyed account or a duplicate of DR-9001.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. In the ERP it is C-1044, in Akron, OH, with account owner Zachary Brown. It is a separate company from Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (CRM-2048 / ERP C-1001, Milwaukee).

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Active contract.** The "Acme Master Supply Agreement" (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It allows discounts of up to 15% on NS-500. However, it is between Northstar and **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. Acme Industrial Supply is not a party, and the Acme Manufacturing Schedule C says plainly: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered."* I found no contract for Acme Industrial Supply.

**5. Pricing exception.** The two Acme NS-500 exceptions in the knowledge base (the Strategic Account and Volume Pricing exceptions) are for Acme Manufacturing. Nothing in the knowledge base links them to Acme Industrial Supply.

**6. Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**7. Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.

**8. Required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager. Discounts over 10% also need recorded approval evidence.

**9. Decision.** The request relies on contract terms, and no contract or exception covers this customer for NS-500. Special terms don't carry over from another company, so this needs commercial review. VP Sales sign-off alone wouldn't settle it, because there are no terms that make Acme Industrial eligible.
