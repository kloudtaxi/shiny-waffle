## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. It can be approved only if VP Sales Michael Torres signs off, and only after the discount figure is sorted out.** The request you submitted says 8.0%, but the discount system's record of DR-9001 says 15.0%. I decided on the system's figure, 15%.

**1. The request.** The system record reads: "DR-9001, CRM-2048, NS-500, **15.0**, 250000, **37500**, **212500**, sarah.chen@northstar.example, 2026-09-23, Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement, Pending Approval." Your copy has 8.0%, a $20,000 discount and a $230,000 net price. Everything else matches. The request relies on the contract, not standard pricing.

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) and ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings) are the same company: "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing." The account is Strategic, and Sarah Chen owns it.

**3. The product.** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It says "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller," and also "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

**5. Pricing exception.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement, approved by David Morgan. It covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only," runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and allows at most "15% of list price." So 15% is within Acme's limit (and so would 8% be). The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 from 2025-04-01.

**6. Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.

**7. Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy she can approve discounts "up to and including 10%." 15% is above that, so she can't approve it.

**8. Required approver.** "Discounts greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval." That role is held by Michael Torres, her manager. The policy also says "Discounts greater than 10% require recorded approval evidence."

**9. Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme is eligible for 15% on this product on this date, but the amount is above Sarah Chen's own limit, so the VP Sales has to approve it. Before anyone acts, the 8% vs 15% difference must be resolved. If 8% is the real figure, the system record needs correcting. At 8% she could approve it herself, so the outcome would become APPROVE.

One more thing: she raised a change request, CR-9201, but the records don't say what it covers. It might be related to this difference.
