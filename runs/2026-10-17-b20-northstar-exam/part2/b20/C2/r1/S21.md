## Answer

I've checked the contract, the pricing exception, the 2027 policy and who holds each role. Here's the decision.

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme qualifies for a 15% discount on the NS-500, but 15% is more than her approval limit. Under the 2027 pricing policy it needs the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan, to approve. Before anyone acts on it, request DR-9113 has to be confirmed: it isn't in the knowledge base.

**How I got there**

1. **The request.** I couldn't find DR-9113 in the knowledge base. The only matching CRM record is DR-9001: same account, product, 15%, $250,000 list price, requestor and justification, but dated 2026-09-23, not 2027-02-01. I did not decide on DR-9001. I decided on DR-9113 as you gave it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition. The request relies on contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company: same DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings is "doing business as Acme Manufacturing". Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product.** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on 2027-02-01. It covers the NS-500 and says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller", but also that "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception.** Schedule B, exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% of list price. The requested 15% is within that maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies to requests dated 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. The 2026 approval matrix (APP-MATRIX-2026) expires 2026-12-31, so it doesn't apply.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and that role can approve discounts up to and including 5%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver.** The policy's bands are: up to 5% for Account Executives, over 5% up to 12% for the VP Sales, and over 12% for the Chief Revenue Officer. That role is held by David Morgan (EMP-300). Discounts over 5% also need recorded approval evidence, and contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.

**Before acting:**
- **Blocking:** confirm that DR-9113 exists in Northstar CRM with these details. If the request actually being approved is DR-9001, it's dated 2026-09-23, falls under a different policy and needs its own decision.
- **Not blocking:** the knowledge base has no dates on job titles. Check that David Morgan is still CRO on 2027-02-01, and that the agreement and the exception haven't been ended early (the agreement allows termination on 60 days' notice).
