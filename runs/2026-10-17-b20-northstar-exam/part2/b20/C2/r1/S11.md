2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". In the ERP system the same company is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), list price $85,000. The submitted figures check out: 22% of $85,000 is $18,700, leaving $66,300.
4. **Basis:** the justification is "Competitive", so the request relies on standard pricing, not special terms. No special terms would cover it anyway:
   - The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) was active on that date (2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31). It lists NS-Edge among the products supplied, but its 15% discount term applies only to the NS-500 controller.
   - The pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 also covers NS-500 only. It says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product".
5. **Policy in force:** the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). It says: "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%" and "Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval." The 2026 approval matrix only took effect on 2026-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** she is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so her limit is 15%. 22% is over it.
7. **Required approver:** VP Sales. That's Michael Torres (EMP-200), who is also Sarah's manager. His HR record has no dates, so I can't confirm he held the role on 2025-09-23.
