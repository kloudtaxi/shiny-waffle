## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this, and it can't go to a higher approver yet either. The outcome is `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.**

The request relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," but none of Acme's special terms gives a discount on the NS-Edge Monitoring Package. Two separate problems stand in the way.

**1. The request isn't in the CRM.** I couldn't find DR-9109 anywhere. The only Acme discount request on record (CRM-2048, dated 2026-09-23, same requestor and same justification) is **DR-9001: NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list price**. That's a different product, discount and value. I haven't used DR-9001 in its place. I decided on DR-9109 as you described it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition.

**2. No special terms cover this product:**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (DUNS 04-812-7730).
- **Product:** SKU NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), list price $85,000. That matches the list value in your request.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It lists NS-Edge among the products supplied. But its discount term is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller" only. For everything else, prices are list "less any discount agreed in an Order."
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The other exception I found, the NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception, is tied to the older agreement ACME-SA-2023 and is also NS-500 only.
- So the special terms don't carry over to NS-EDGE, and the request needs commercial review. Also, under the 2026 policy, contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.

**Authority, for when it gets past review:**
- **Policy:** the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the Approval Authority Matrix APP-MATRIX-2026.
- **Sarah Chen** is an Enterprise Account Executive. She can approve discounts up to and including 10%, so 12% is beyond her own authority.
- **Required approver:** discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres. Discounts above 10% also need recorded approval evidence.
- Even with special terms in place, the contract doesn't change her personal approval limit.
