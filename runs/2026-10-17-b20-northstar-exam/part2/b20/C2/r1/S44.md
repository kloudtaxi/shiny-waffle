## Answer

**SR-39690 result: BREACH_NO_CREDIT.** The true severity is **Severity 2**. Northstar met the response target but missed the restoration target by more than double. No credit is owed, because Cedar's credit claim arrived about 8 hours late.

**1. Customer and scope.** The ticket's customer is CRM account CRM-2063. That account carries DUNS 02-906-4418, which is also on ERP customer C-1003, Cedar Health Systems Inc. Its support terms (SUP-CHS-2025) put Cedar on the Silver plan at $4,000 a month and cover NS-EDGE. Coverage started 2025-12-01, so the ticket is in scope.

**2. True severity: Severity 2, not the P2 the customer reported.** Chiller alarms at the Central energy plant stopped forwarding, and Cedar's engineers were "doing hourly manual rounds" to cover the gap. The severity guide puts a missing alarm function in Severity 2 "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks". It isn't Severity 1, because a workaround was in place and operations continued.

**3. Schedule version and targets.** The ticket opened 2026-07-28 at 09:30 CT, so Schedule v2.0 applies (it covers tickets opened from 2026-04-01). For Severity 2 on Silver, the targets are 2 business hours to respond and 12 business hours to restore. The clock counts only 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, and none of 28–31 July is a holiday. All times below are converted from EDT (−04:00) to CDT (−05:00).

**4. Clock and measurement.** The clock started when the portal submission arrived, at 09:30 CT. The record shows no pauses, no "Awaiting Customer" time and no maintenance window.
- **Response: met.** The engineer responded at 11:05 CT, after 1 h 35 m. The deadline was 11:30 CT.
- **Restoration: missed, band 2.** Service was restored 2026-07-31 at 00:50 EDT, which is 2026-07-30 at 23:50 CT. That is 28.5 business hours (8.5 + 10 + 10). The 12-hour target ran out on 07-29 at 11:30 CT, and twice the target (24 hours) ran out on 07-30 at 13:30 CT.

**5. Credit: $0.** A band-2 restoration miss on Severity 2 earns 10%, which would have been $400. But v2.0 gives 30 calendar days from the restoration date, counted in CT. Restoration was on 07-30 CT, so the window ended 2026-08-29 at 23:59 CT. The claim came in by email on 2026-08-30 at 08:12 CT, and "a late claim earns no credit". The trap is the restoration date: in EDT it falls on 07-31, which would make the claim look on time. The older v1.0 rule of 60 days doesn't apply to this ticket. I couldn't find the monthly cap in the record, but with no credit owed it doesn't change anything.

**6. What Northstar owes**
- **Restoration-target notice:** tell Marcus Adeyemi (Director of Customer Support) and Anthony Day (Cedar's account owner in CRM) by **2026-07-29 11:30 CT**, if the ticket isn't restored by then. It wasn't. The record doesn't show whether these notices were sent. The procedure states this rule without naming a severity. I've applied it to Severity 2 because the Severity 1 and 3 rules are stated separately.
- **No response escalation:** the response was on time, so the Severity 2 notice for a late response doesn't apply.
- **Claim decision:** Marcus Adeyemi must send a written decision, declining the claim as late, by **2026-09-29** (30 calendar days after receipt). That date is already past, and the record doesn't show that he sent it.
- **No credit memo:** the claim fails, so there is nothing to issue.
- **Root cause:** I couldn't find any root-cause rule in the knowledge base.

**7. What the customer had to do**
- **Phone report:** this applies only to Severity 1, so it isn't required here.
- **Credit claim:** due by 2026-08-29 23:59 CT. **Not met**, because it arrived on 08-30.
- **Information and access:** Northstar recorded no requests to Cedar, so there was nothing to meet.
