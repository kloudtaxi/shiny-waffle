## Answer

**No, CR-9207 can't be approved as submitted.** The decision is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**. The request depends on an "Acme parent guarantee," and nothing in the records shows a guarantee that covers Acme Industrial Supply Co. The request also isn't in the system of record.

1. **The request isn't on file.** There is no CR-9207 in Northstar ERP. The only request Sarah Chen raised on 2026-09-23 is CR-9201, for a different customer (C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, $250,000 → $400,000). I haven't used that record in its place. I've assessed CR-9207 as you gave it, with confirming it exists as a blocking condition.
2. **The customer is a separate company.** C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091, Akron, OH). Its CRM tier is Standard and its account owner is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen. A support contract states that "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company" from Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The guarantee covers someone else.** The only signed guarantee is GRT-ACME-2026, given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It covers only its subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings (Acme Manufacturing, C-1001) and is capped at $400,000. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. The 2026 credit policy applies a guarantee only "for the customer the guarantee names." A chat note says "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it," but that isn't the agreement.
4. **Without a guarantee, the customer isn't eligible.** The 2026 policy caps Standard accounts at $250,000, and the request is for $500,000. That cap comes from a policy line the knowledge base matched only loosely, so it should be checked against the policy text. A guarantee would only help if it names Acme Industrial Supply Co. and adds at least $250,000.
5. **Payment history: no problem found, but not fully checked.** The one invoice I checked, INV-30001, was paid 7 days late (due 2025-02-25, paid 2025-03-04). It falls outside the 12-month window anyway. The customer's only other order (SO-5005) dates from March 2025, so its invoice was probably due before the window too. I haven't seen it or confirmed there are no other C-1044 invoices.
6. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales and has no approval authority under the policy. A $500,000 limit falls in the band above $100,000 up to and including $500,000, which needs the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. VP Sales concurrence applies only to Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.

If a guarantee naming Acme Industrial can't be produced, the request fails on the $250,000 Standard maximum. In that case the outcome becomes REJECT_OR_ESCALATE, and a more senior approver can't change that.
