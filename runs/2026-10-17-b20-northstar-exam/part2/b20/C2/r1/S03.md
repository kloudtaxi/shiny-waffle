## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. 15% is above her 10% limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) must approve it. But first, the request you sent doesn't match the CRM record for DR-9001.

**The CRM record shows a different product.** You submitted NS-CLOUD on a $120,000 list price ($18,000 off, $102,000 net). The CRM record for DR-9001 is for the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**: $250,000 list, $37,500 off, $212,500 net. The customer, 15% rate, requestor, date, justification and "Pending Approval" status all match. The covering email is also titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001". As the procedure requires, I decided on the CRM's version and recorded the difference as a blocking condition.

**What I checked**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS 04-812-7730, and the agreement says "doing business as Acme Manufacturing").
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It allows up to 15% on the NS-500 only, and says each discount still needs approval under internal policy.
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and allows a maximum of 15% of list on "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only". It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So 15% on the NS-500 is within what Acme is eligible for.
- **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date. The 2027 policy, written on 2026-09-15, only applies from 2027-01-01.
- **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, that role can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200). The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for discounts above 10%.

**If the request really is for NS-CLOUD**, the answer changes to **REVIEW_REQUIRED**. Neither the exception nor the agreement's 15% term covers NS-CLOUD, and special terms don't carry over to other products, so it would need commercial review. A higher approver wouldn't be enough.
