## Answer

**Yes, it can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme is entitled to a 15% discount on the NS-500. But 15% is more than an Enterprise Account Executive may approve, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) must approve it. The request date also needs correcting before anyone acts on the decision.

**Date mismatch.** The submitted request says **2025-09-23**. The CRM record of DR-9001 and the request email both say **2026-09-23**. I decided on 2026-09-23, the date in the system of record. The date matters because it decides which pricing policy applies: 2026-09-23 falls under the 2026 policy, while the submitted date would fall under the 2025 policy, which I didn't check. So correcting the date is a blocking condition.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500. List price is $250,000, the discount is $37,500 and the net price is $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it, and its status is Pending Approval. Its stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company. They share a DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, up to a maximum of 15%. David Morgan approved it. It states it gives "commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The 15% requested is at that maximum, not above it, so Acme is eligible.
6. **The policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role may approve discounts "up to and including 10%." She can't approve 15% herself, even though the contract allows it.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% "require VP Sales approval." Michael Torres is VP Sales, and Sarah reports to him. The policy also requires a recorded approval for any discount over 10%.
9. **Decision:** **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**, with Michael Torres as the approver.
