## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to a 15% discount on the NS-500, but 15% is above her own approval limit. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it. The request is DR-9001 in Northstar CRM, and what you submitted matches that record exactly.

**The request:** Acme Manufacturing (CRM account CRM-2048) asks for 15% off the NS-500 Industrial Controller. That's $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, for $212,500 net. Sarah submitted it on 2026-09-23, and it's still "Pending Approval". The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms rather than standard pricing.

**The customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company. The Master Supply Agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing, and both records share the same address and DUNS number (04-812-7730).

**Is Acme entitled to 15%? Yes.**
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers the NS-500 and says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement and was approved by David Morgan. It covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% of list price. The request is at that maximum, not over it. The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee".

**Who has to approve it?**
- **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, which runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy was written on 2026-09-15 but only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
- **Sarah's limit:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
- **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager.
- Acme's contract makes the customer eligible for 15%. It doesn't give Sarah any extra authority.

**Two things to note (neither stops the decision):**
- The 2026 policy says contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed, and that discounts above 10% need recorded approval evidence. Michael Torres's approval should be recorded, with the exception cited.
- If this slips into 2027, the 2027 policy would apply. Its limits are lower (Account Executives up to 5%, VP Sales up to 12%), so a 15% discount would then need approval from the Chief Revenue Officer instead.
