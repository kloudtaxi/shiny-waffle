## Answer

**No, Michael Torres can't approve this himself.** BlueRiver qualifies for $200,000, but the limit needs Finance Manager approval (Priya Shah). Two things must be settled before anyone acts on it, and the first is the bigger one: **CR-9205 isn't in the system of record.** The credit request records I can see show only one request from 2026-09-23, CR-9201 for a different customer (C-1001). So I decided on CR-9205 as you gave it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition.

1. **The request:** CR-9205 asks to raise ERP customer C-1002 from $100,000 to $200,000 on 2026-09-23, requested by Michael Torres. The reason given is fleet expansion. It doesn't mention a guarantee, so I judged it on the customer's own standing.
2. **The customer:** "BlueRiver Logistics" in the CRM (CRM-2051) and "Blue River Logistics LLC" in the ERP (C-1002) are the same company. Both records carry DUNS 07-331-9025 and 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. The CRM puts it in the Standard tier.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the previous policy.
4. **Payment history:** the rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
   - INV-30014 ($538,300) was due 2026-02-27 and paid 2026-02-25, two days early.
   - INV-30020 ($494,700) is due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count yet.
   - There are no late invoices, so the customer passes.
5. **The maximum:** Standard accounts can go up to $250,000, and $200,000 is under that. One caveat: the policy text behind the $250,000 figure was only a loose match in the knowledge base, so it should be checked against the policy itself.
6. **The requestor's authority:** Michael Torres is VP Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance. He has none, and authority is judged on the new total of $200,000.
7. **Who must approve:** limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need the Finance Manager, who is Priya Shah. VP Sales concurrence is only needed for Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it doesn't apply here.
