## Answer

**No, not on Sarah Chen's own authority.** The customer is entitled to 15% on NS-500, but under the 2026 policy she can only approve up to 10%. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, must approve it.

**Status mismatch:** The submission says the request is "Approved", but the CRM record shows **"Pending Approval"**, with the approver and approval-date fields empty. Nobody has approved it yet, so the "Approved" status should not be used.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request (CRM record):** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Dated 2026-09-23, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. The figures match the submission; only the status differs.
2. **Customer:** CRM's "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) is the same company as ERP's "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). The account name, DUNS number 04-812-7730 and address all match. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500", but also that "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, at a maximum of 15% of list price, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the earlier 10% exception and was approved by David Morgan. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee". The 15% request is within this limit.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, which has lower limits, only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role may approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, which must be recorded. Michael Torres (EMP-200) is VP Sales and Sarah Chen's manager.

Sarah Chen prepared the exception herself. That doesn't change anything, because David Morgan approved it, but it may be worth knowing.
