## Answer

**Yes, but Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on the NS-500 under its contract, but 15% is more than her own limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) must approve it.

**Checks**
1. **The request:** DR-9001 is in the CRM discount records and matches what was submitted on every field: CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, sarah.chen@northstar.example, dated 2026-09-23, "Pending Approval". It relies on special contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer:** the CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as the ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing). The DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address match.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. This matches the request.
4. **The contract:** the Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500 and says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15%" on it. It also says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and allows at most 15% off list. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The 15% requested is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) applies. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** she is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role may approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit. The contract doesn't change that.
8. **The required approver:** under the 2026 policy, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres (EMP-200) holds that role and is also Sarah's manager. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for anything above 10%, which his recorded approval provides.
