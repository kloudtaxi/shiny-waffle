## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this alone.** Northstar CRM records DR-9001 as dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23 as in the request you gave me. On the 2026 date, Acme is eligible for the 15% discount. But 15% is above Sarah's 10% limit under the Pricing Policy 2026, so VP Sales Michael Torres has to approve it.

**Why the date matters:** If the request really were dated 2025-09-23, the Pricing Policy 2025 would apply. That policy let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to and including 15%, so Sarah could have approved it herself. I've decided on the CRM date, but the two dates give different answers, so the date needs confirming before anyone acts.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah submitted it and it is Pending Approval. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it rests on special contract terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730).
3. **Product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is active on either date. It makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on NS-500, but says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% of list price. David Morgan approved it. It replaces EXC-ACME-NS500-10 and says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So 15% is within Acme's eligibility.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The Pricing Policy 2027, with lower limits, only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Under the 2026 policy, Enterprise Account Executives may approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to and including 20% need VP Sales approval. That is Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager. Discounts over 10% also need recorded approval evidence.

The contract and exception make Acme eligible for 15%, but they don't change who is allowed to approve it.
