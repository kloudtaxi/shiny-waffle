## Answer

**Short answer: not by Michael Torres alone.** BlueRiver Logistics qualifies for a $200,000 limit, but Priya Shah, the Finance Manager, has to approve it. Before anyone acts on it, someone must confirm that CR-9205 exists in Northstar ERP, because I couldn't find it in the knowledge base.

1. **The request.** I decided it as you gave it: $100,000 → $200,000, dated 2026-09-23, requested by michael.torres@northstar.example, to support larger NS-Cloud orders. It relies on the customer's own standing, not a guarantee. The ERP credit-request extract I can see has no CR-9205. The only request dated 2026-09-23 is CR-9201, which is a different customer (C-1001, Sarah Chen, $250k → $400k). So confirming the record exists is a blocking condition.
2. **The customer.** ERP customer C-1002 "Blue River Logistics LLC" and CRM account CRM-2051 "BlueRiver Logistics" are the same company. They share the DUNS number 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. CRM records it as a **Standard** account.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated in 2026.
4. **Payment history (passes).** The policy's rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or be unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
   - INV-30014 (SO-5018, $538,300) was due 2026-02-27 and paid 2026-02-25, two days early.
   - INV-30020 (SO-5030, $494,700) is due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so the rule doesn't apply to it.
   - These are the only invoices for this customer.
5. **The maximum limit (passes).** The policy caps Standard accounts at $250,000, and $200,000 is under that. No guarantee is involved; the only parent guarantee I found is for Acme. One caveat: the knowledge base only loosely matched the $250,000 figure to the policy text and gave it low confidence. I've noted that below but it doesn't hold up the decision.
6. **Who can approve.** Michael Torres is VP Sales. The 2026 policy gives that role no approval authority over credit limits. Its only Sales role is VP Sales concurrence for Strategic accounts above $500,000, which doesn't apply here.
7. **Required approver.** Under the policy, limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval, judged on the new total limit. That role is held by **Priya Shah**. No concurrence is needed, because this is a Standard account and the limit is under $500,000.
