## Answer

**Answer: Not on Sarah Chen's own authority.** Acme can get a 15% discount on NS-500, but the 2026 policy requires VP Sales approval for 15%. That approver is Michael Torres. Also, the request you were given says "Approved", but the CRM record says "Pending Approval".

1. **The request.** The CRM record for DR-9001 shows account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the reason "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". So it relies on contract terms. Every figure matches the copy you gave me except the status: CRM shows **"Pending Approval"** with no approver and no approval date.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company. They share a DUNS number (04-812-7730) and an address, and the agreement names "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing". This is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a different company.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows discounts of up to 15% on NS-500, but adds that "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a 15% maximum. It replaced the earlier 10% exception for requests from 2025-04-01. It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The requested 15% is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, which has tighter limits, only applies to requests dated 2027-01-01 or later, so it doesn't apply here.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is over her limit, and the contract doesn't change that.
8. **Required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres is VP Sales (and Sarah Chen's manager). The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for discounts over 10%.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Michael Torres must approve and record that approval. Until he does, DR-9001 must not be treated as approved, despite the "Approved" status on the copy you were given.
