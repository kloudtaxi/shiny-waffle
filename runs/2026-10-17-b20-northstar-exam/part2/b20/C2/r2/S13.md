## Answer

**No, it can't be approved as submitted. It goes to commercial review (REVIEW_REQUIRED).** The request relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement, and that agreement belongs to a different company.

**The request.** I couldn't find request DR-9105 anywhere in Northstar CRM. I decided on it as you gave it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition. The CRM does hold DR-9001, which has exactly the same details: NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, the same "Acme Master Supply Agreement" justification, Pending Approval. But DR-9001 is for account **CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing**, not CRM-2091. Either DR-9105 was keyed against the wrong account, or it duplicates DR-9001 under a different id. I did not decide on DR-9001 in its place.

**What I checked:**
- **Customer:** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron, OH (ERP customer C-1044). The account owner is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen. The records say outright that it "is a different company and is not covered" by Acme Manufacturing's terms.
- **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
- **Contract:** The only Master Supply Agreement in the knowledge base is between Northstar Industrial Systems and Acme Manufacturing (Acme Mfg. Holdings). I found no contract for Acme Industrial Supply.
- **Pricing exception:** Both Acme NS-500 exceptions I found (the "Strategic Account" one and the "Volume Pricing" one) are Acme Manufacturing documents. Nothing I found covers Acme Industrial Supply.
- **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies, in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
  - Enterprise Account Executives, Sarah Chen's role, can approve up to and including 10%.
  - Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager.
  - Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.

**Why this outcome:** Special terms don't carry over from one customer to another, so a discount justified by contract pricing has no basis here. Approval from Michael Torres wouldn't fix that, because his authority doesn't create eligibility. If commercial review turns this into a standard-pricing request, 15% would still need his approval.
