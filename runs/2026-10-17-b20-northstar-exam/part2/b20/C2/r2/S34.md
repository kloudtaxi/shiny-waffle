## Answer

**No, Michael Torres can't approve this himself. And CR-9208 can't be acted on yet, because it isn't in Northstar ERP.** Northstar ERP has no record CR-9208. Its only pending request for C-1001 on 2026-09-23 is **CR-9201**, and that one differs: Sarah Chen is the requestor and the new limit is **$400,000**. Its justification ("Third-plant ramp-up…") is the same. As required, I decided on CR-9208 as submitted rather than switching to CR-9201, and confirming the record is a blocking condition.

**Decision on the request as submitted: approve, but only with Director of Finance approval and VP Sales concurrence.**

1. **Customer:** ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings", the legal entity behind CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing". They match on DUNS 04-812-7730 and the same address. The CRM tier is **Strategic**.
2. **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
3. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. The invoice register (INV-30001 to INV-30020) has no invoices for C-1001, so none are late. The register also has no invoice for Acme's completed sales order SO-7001 ($480,000, 2026-06-10). That gap should be checked.
4. **Maximum limit:** $750,000 for Strategic accounts. $650,000 is within it on Acme's own standing, so the request doesn't need the guarantee. A parent guarantee from Acme Group Holdings does exist (GRT-ACME-2026). It covers Acme Mfg. Holdings for up to $400,000, from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31.
5. **Requestor's authority:** Michael Torres is VP Sales. Under the policy, approval authority sits with AR Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager (up to $500,000) and the Director of Finance (above $500,000). He has none.
6. **Required approvers for $650,000:** Elena Novak, Director of Finance, must approve. Because Acme is Strategic, the VP Sales must also concur, and that role is held by Torres himself. The policy has no rule I could find on whether a requestor may concur on his own request, so that needs confirming. The Acme NS-500 pricing exception approved by David Morgan grants no approval authority.
