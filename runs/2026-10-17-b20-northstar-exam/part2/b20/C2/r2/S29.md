## Answer

**No. The request has to be rejected or escalated because $1,300,000 is above the most Acme can be granted, even with the parent guarantee counted.** There's also a second problem: I can't find CR-9204 in the knowledge base at all.

**The request.** No record called CR-9204 exists. The only Acme credit request in Northstar ERP's request log is **CR-9201**: customer C-1001, raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, $250,000 → **$400,000**, for the third-plant ramp-up, status Pending Approval. Sarah Chen's own message also says "I've raised CR-9201". I haven't treated CR-9201 as this request. I decided on CR-9204 as you gave it, and confirming that it exists is a blocking condition.

**The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. The CRM tier is **Strategic**.

**Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**Maximum limit.** This is what decides it:
- The policy maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000.
- The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) adds its guaranteed amount. It was given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc., names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is limited to $400,000. So it does apply to this customer on the request date.
- That makes the maximum **$1,150,000**. The request is $150,000 over it. A higher approver can't override this.

**Payment history.** Not established. The invoices I checked belong to other customers, and I couldn't list every invoice to find Acme's. This doesn't change the outcome.

**Authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales and has no credit approval authority under the policy. A limit above $500,000 needs the Director of Finance to approve, plus VP Sales concurrence because Acme is a Strategic account. I couldn't find who holds either role.
