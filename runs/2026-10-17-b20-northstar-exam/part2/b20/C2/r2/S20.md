## Answer

No. Michael Torres can't approve the 18% discount on DR-9112, and neither can a more senior approver. Acme's contract terms allow at most 15% off NS-500, so 18% needs new or amended terms. There is also a problem with the record itself: I couldn't find DR-9112 in the knowledge base.

**The request record.** No record DR-9112 exists. The only request in the CRM for this account (CRM-2048), product (NS-500) and date (2026-09-23) is **DR-9001**. It has the same list value and justification, but it was raised by **Sarah Chen at 15.0%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). The submitted request says Michael Torres at 18.0% ($45,000 off). I haven't decided on DR-9001 in its place. The decision below is on DR-9112 as given, and confirming the record exists is a blocking condition.

**The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings": same DUNS number (04-812-7730), and it trades as Acme Manufacturing.

**The basis.** The request relies on contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").

**The contract and the exception.**
- The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is active from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers 2026-09-23. It makes Acme eligible for "discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says each discount is still subject to Northstar's internal approval.
- Its Schedule B, exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a maximum of **15% of list price**. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee".
- 18% is above that 15% ceiling.

**Policy and authority.**
- Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
- Under the 2026 policy, Account Executives can approve up to 10%, VP Sales approves above 10% up to 20%, and the Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) approves above 20%.
- Michael Torres is VP Sales, so 18% is within his authority. Authority isn't the obstacle; eligibility is. No approver can authorize a discount beyond what Acme's terms allow.

**What would let it go ahead:**
- Acme's terms are amended to allow more than 15%, or the request is changed to 15% or less.
- I couldn't read the Sales Discount SOP's section on self-approval, so whether Torres may approve a request he raised himself is still open. I've recorded this as a non-blocking note.
