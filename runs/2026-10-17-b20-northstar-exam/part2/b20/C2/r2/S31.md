Yes. Blue River qualified for $200,000, but **Michael Torres could not have approved it himself.** It needed sign-off from the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. There's also a catch: **I can't find CR-9206 anywhere in the knowledge base**, so it has to be confirmed in the ERP before anyone acts on this.

**The request.** Torres asked on 2025-09-23 to raise ERP customer C-1002 from $100,000 to $200,000. The reason given was fleet expansion, and the request relies on the customer's own standing, not a guarantee. The ERP's credit request register has CR-7001 to CR-7010 and CR-9201, but not CR-9206. As instructed, I decided on the request as you gave it and made confirming it a blocking condition. One possible lead: CR-9201 has the same day and month a year later (2026-09-23), but it's for a different customer (C-1001). I didn't treat it as this request.

**The customer.** "BlueRiver Logistics" in the CRM (CRM-2051) and "Blue River Logistics LLC" in the ERP (C-1002) are the same company. They share a DUNS number (07-331-9025) and an address (890 Harbor Way, St. Louis). The CRM lists it as a **Standard** account.

**Policy.** The Northstar Credit Policy 2025 applies, since it covers requests dated 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

**Payment history.** The 2025 rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid more than 45 days late, or be unpaid more than 45 days past due. Blue River's first invoice is INV-30014, issued 2026-01-13, so none fell due in that window. Nothing counts against it. If the real date turns out to be 2026-09-23, it still passes under the 2026 policy's 30-day rule: INV-30014 was paid two days early.

**Maximum limit.** The 2025 maximum for Standard accounts is $300,000, and no guarantee is involved. $200,000 is under the cap, so Blue River is eligible.

**Authority.** The 2025 policy gives approval authority to three roles only: AR Specialists up to $50,000, the Finance Manager above $50,000 up to $750,000, and the Director of Finance above $750,000. Torres is VP Sales, which holds none of these. He also submitted the request, and the policy says no one may approve a request they submitted.

**Required approver.** At $200,000 that's the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. No concurrence is needed in 2025. She didn't submit the request, so she's free to approve it. If the date is actually in 2026, the answer is the same: the Finance Manager band runs above $100,000 up to $500,000, and $200,000 is under the $250,000 Standard maximum.
