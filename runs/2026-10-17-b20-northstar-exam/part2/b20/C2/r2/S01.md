> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 on her own authority.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it is above her 10% limit, so the VP Sales, Michael Torres, must approve it.

1. **Request:** DR-9001 is in Northstar CRM exactly as you submitted it: account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, dated 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms rather than standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** The CRM account "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) and the ERP customer "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001) are the same company. They share a DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. It says Acme "is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers the NS-500 only, at a maximum of 15% of list price, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The 15% request is at the maximum, not above it.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy, which sets lower limits, doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is over that limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres holds that role, and Sarah reports to him. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for any discount above 10%.

The contract makes Acme eligible for the discount, but it doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it.
