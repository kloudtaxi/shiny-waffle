## Answer

**Not by Sarah Chen alone.** Acme qualifies for the higher limit, but Priya Shah (Finance Manager) has to approve it. There's also a mismatch to fix first: the submitted form asks for **$300,000**, but the credit-request record shows **$400,000**. I decided on the record's $400,000. Both amounts need the same approver.

**1. The request.** Record CR-9201: customer C-1001, current limit $250,000, requested $400,000, raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, reason "Third-plant ramp-up…", status Pending Approval. The record doesn't cite any guarantee. A chat message says "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it," but the request doesn't need it (see step 5).

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. The legal documents say Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091). The CRM lists Acme as a **Strategic** account.

**3. The policy.** The Northstar Credit Policy 2026 was in force on 2026-09-23. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or still be unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. None of the 20 ERP invoices I found (INV-30001 to INV-30020) is billed to C-1001, so no late invoices turned up. One caveat: sales order SO-7001 (C-1001, 2026-06-10, $480,000, status Completed) has no matching invoice. If it had been billed on Net 45 and left unpaid, it could break the rule. I've recorded this as a blocking condition.

**5. Maximum limit.** The maximum for a Strategic account is **$750,000**, so $400,000 is within it. The parent guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 also exists and is in force: Acme Group Holdings, Inc. guarantees Acme Mfg. Holdings up to $400,000, from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It isn't needed for this limit.

**6. Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives her no authority to approve credit limits.

**7. Required approver.** Authority is judged on the new total limit. Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need **Finance Manager** approval, and that role is held by **Priya Shah**. VP Sales concurrence and Director of Finance approval only apply above $500,000.
