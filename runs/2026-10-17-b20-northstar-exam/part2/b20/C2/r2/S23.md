## Answer

I've finished the checks. **DR-9001 can be approved, but not on Sarah Chen's own authority. Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but that is above the 10% Sarah can approve herself.

**1. The request.** The CRM record matches what was submitted on every field but one, the date. CRM has **2026-09-23**; the submission says 2025-09-23. The email where Sarah logged the request ("Acme NS-500 — DR-9001") is also dated 2026-09-23 09:14 CT. I decided on the CRM date. The request is 15% off NS-500, on $250,000 list, which gives $37,500 off and $212,500 net. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so its basis is a contract term or exception.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both records share the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee and DUNS number 04-812-7730. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).

**3. The product.** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller", and that "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It covers NS-500 only, allows a maximum of 15% off list, and was approved by David Morgan. It replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and does not give any employee approval authority. 15% is within the 15% maximum, so Acme is eligible.

**6. Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and under that policy she can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.

**8. Required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. The VP Sales is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. Any discount over 10% also needs the approval recorded.

**Before acting:** the submitted date should be corrected to match CRM. If 2025-09-23 were the real date, the 2025 pricing policy would apply instead, and I didn't check its approval limits.
