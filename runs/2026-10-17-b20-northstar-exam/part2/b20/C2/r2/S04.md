## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen could not approve this 15% discount herself.** Acme is entitled to the discount, but under the policy in force it needs VP Sales approval from Michael Torres.

**The date in your question doesn't match the CRM.** You gave 2025-09-23, but the CRM record for DR-9001 shows **2026-09-23**. I decided on the CRM date. This matters: under the 2025 Pricing Policy, Enterprise Account Executives could approve up to 15%, so on your date she could have approved it herself. On the recorded date she can't.

1. **The request:** DR-9001, NS-500, 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen raised it and its status is "Pending Approval." It relies on contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, which does business as Acme Manufacturing. The DUNS number and address match.
3. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows up to 15% on the NS-500, but says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
4. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers only the NS-500, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a 15% maximum. It replaced the earlier EXC-ACME-NS500-10. So 15% is exactly at the maximum and within eligibility. The exception says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
5. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, and anything above 10% needs recorded approval evidence.
6. **Her authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. 15% is over it.
7. **The approver:** VP Sales, held by Michael Torres (EMP-200).
