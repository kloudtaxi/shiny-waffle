## Answer

I can't give a final yes yet. On the facts, Acme qualifies for $650,000 and the request needs two sign-offs: Elena Novak (Director of Finance) and Michael Torres (VP Sales). But CR-9202 isn't in Northstar ERP, so it has to be confirmed there before anyone acts on this.

**The record problem.** The ERP credit-request log has no CR-9202. It has **CR-9201** for the same customer (C-1001), on the same date (2026-09-23), from the same requestor (Sarah Chen), with the same justification. That record asks to raise the limit from $250,000 to **$400,000**, not $650,000, and it's also Pending Approval. I decided on the request as you gave it and didn't substitute CR-9201. Someone needs to confirm whether CR-9202 is a real, separate request or a misstated CR-9201. If it's CR-9201, the decision changes: $400,000 needs only Finance Manager approval.

**Customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (same DUNS 04-812-7730; legal documents say Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing). In the CRM it's a **Strategic** account.

**Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes:
- INV-60412 ($85,000) was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 ($480,000) was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count.

**Maximum limit.** Strategic accounts can go up to $750,000, so $650,000 is within it on Acme's own standing. A parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. names Acme Mfg. Holdings for $400,000 from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It would raise the maximum to $1,150,000, but this request doesn't need it.

**Approval.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the policy gives her no credit-approval authority. Any limit above $500,000 needs Director of Finance approval, and for a Strategic account also VP Sales concurrence.

**Things to note:**
- As of today (2026-10-18), INV-62305 for $720,000 is 16 days past due and unpaid. That doesn't affect eligibility on the request date, but it becomes a problem for payment history after 2026-11-01.
- Michael Torres, who must concur, is Sarah Chen's manager. The policy doesn't say whether that matters.
