## Answer

No. Sarah Chen could not have approved this herself. She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the policy in force on 2025-09-23 limits her role to discounts of 15% or less. A 22% discount needs VP Sales approval, and that's Michael Torres. Note also that DR-9103 isn't in the knowledge base at all.

1. **The request.** I couldn't find DR-9103 anywhere in the knowledge base, so I decided on the request as you gave it. The only Acme discount request on file is DR-9001: NS-500, 15%, dated 2026-09-23, also by Sarah Chen. That's a different request and I did not use it. The justification "Competitive" points to standard pricing, not a contract term or exception.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings": same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and same address. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), list price $85,000. The submitted figures add up: 22% of $85,000 is $18,700, leaving a net of $66,300.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) was active on the request date (2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31). It covers NS-EDGE at list price "less any discount agreed in an Order". The only discount it sets is up to 15% on the NS-500, and each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies.
5. **Pricing exception.** None covers NS-EDGE. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is for "NS-500 only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product". The request doesn't rely on special terms anyway.
6. **Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applies (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). The 2026 policy and approval matrix only apply to requests dated from 2026-01-01.
7. **Her authority.** "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%." 22% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver.** "Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval." The HR and org records show Michael Torres as VP Sales, and he is Sarah's manager. Those records are undated, so I can't confirm he held the role on 2025-09-23.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** At standard pricing the discount can proceed, but only with VP Sales sign-off. It can't be acted on until someone confirms DR-9103 exists in Northstar CRM.
