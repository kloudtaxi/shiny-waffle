## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** Acme's contract terms cap the NS-500 discount at 15%, and the CRM record itself shows DR-9001 at **15%**, not 18%. The procedure says to decide on the system of record, so I've decided on 15%. At 15%, Acme is eligible, but the discount is above Sarah Chen's own limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

**The 18% figure.** The request you pasted says 18.0% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). The CRM export in the knowledge base says 15.0% for the same request ID, customer, product, requestor and date: $37,500 off $250,000, net $212,500. If 18% is really what's wanted, the outcome would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. It's above the 15% maximum, and a more senior approver can't fix that. It would need new or amended terms. I found no amendment raising the cap.

**How I got there:**
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The Master Supply Agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing", and the DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address match.
- **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 was active on 2026-09-23 (it runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31). It says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500" and that "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
- **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and caps the discount at 15% of list. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority". An older exception tied to agreement ACME-SA-2023 has been replaced.
- **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in force (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
- **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to and including 10%. Discounts above 10% up to 20% need VP Sales approval, and the policy requires recorded approval evidence for anything above 10%. The VP Sales is Michael Torres.
