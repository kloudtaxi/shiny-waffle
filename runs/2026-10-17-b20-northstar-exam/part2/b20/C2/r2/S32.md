## Answer

**No. CR-9207 can't be approved as submitted. The outcome is `REQUEST_EVIDENCE`.** The parent guarantee the request relies on covers a different company, and the request itself isn't in the credit request log.

**1. The request isn't in the system of record.** The credit request log has no CR-9207. The only request Sarah Chen has there dated 2026-09-23 is CR-9201, which is for a different customer: C-1001 Acme Mfg. Holdings, $250,000 → $400,000. I decided on CR-9207 as you gave it and haven't used CR-9201 in its place.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091, DUNS 09-552-1187, Akron, OH). Its CRM tier is **Standard** and its account owner is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen. A Schedule C document says that "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company" from Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001, CRM-2048, DUNS 04-812-7730, Milwaukee).

**3. The guarantee doesn't cover this customer.** GRT-ACME-2026 is given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is capped at $400,000. It guarantees only "its wholly owned subsidiary **Acme Mfg. Holdings**", which is C-1001. The policy says a guarantee applies only to "the customer the guarantee names." So nothing on file shows any guarantee for Acme Industrial Supply.

**4. Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
- **Maximum limit:** $250,000 for Standard accounts. The knowledge base only matched this figure loosely to the policy text (confidence 0.5), so it needs checking. If it holds, $500,000 is over the maximum unless a guarantee naming Acme Industrial raises it. Without one, the request would be rejected.
- **Payment history:** I found one invoice, INV-30001 (due 2025-02-25, paid 2025-03-04). It falls outside the 12 months before the request. I didn't check C-1044's other invoices, including the one for SO-5005, so payment history isn't fully checked.
- **Approval:** $500,000 is in the band "greater than $100,000 and up to and including $500,000", which needs Finance Manager approval. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and nothing shows her holding credit approval authority. She also can't approve her own request. I didn't look up who the Finance Manager is.

**What's needed:** someone has to confirm CR-9207 exists in ERP and supply a guarantee that names Acme Industrial Supply Co. Otherwise the request goes back as relying on another company's guarantee, and at $500,000 against a $250,000 Standard maximum it would be rejected.
