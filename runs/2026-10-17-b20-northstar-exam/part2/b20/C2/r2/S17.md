## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this.** The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: the request says it uses Acme Master Supply Agreement pricing, but nothing in that agreement gives Acme a discount on NS-EDGE. Separately, 12% is above her own limit of 10%.

**The request.** I couldn't find DR-9109 in the knowledge base. The only Acme discount request in the CRM export is DR-9001, which is for NS-500 at 15% (list $250,000), with the same date, requestor and justification. I haven't decided DR-9001 in its place. I've decided DR-9109 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists in Northstar CRM. The figures you gave add up: 12% of $85,000 is $10,200, leaving $74,800 net.

1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen owns the account.
2. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), list price $85,000, which matches the request.
3. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It does list NS-Edge as a supplied product, but its only discount right is "up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". For everything else, prices are "Northstar's current list prices… less any discount agreed in an Order".
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product". The older Acme NS-500 exceptions are also NS-500 only. Nothing covers NS-EDGE, and terms for one product don't carry over to another.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix (APP-MATRIX-2026).
6. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to and including 10%. 12% is over that.
7. **Who approves 12%:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence above 10%, and says contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed.

Even if the special terms were valid, Sarah still couldn't approve 12% herself. Before anyone can approve this, Deal Desk or commercial review needs to establish what discount Acme can get on NS-EDGE. Once that's settled, the VP Sales band would apply.
