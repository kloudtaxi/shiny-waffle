## Answer

Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. At 25%, the request needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. There's also a problem to clear first: I couldn't find request DR-9102 anywhere in the knowledge base.

**1. The request isn't on record.** Searching for DR-9102 found nothing. The only discount request recorded for account CRM-2048 on 2026-09-23 is DR-9001. That one is for a different product (NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list, "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"). I haven't used DR-9001 in its place. I've decided on DR-9102 as you gave it, with a blocking condition that someone confirms it exists in Northstar CRM.

**2. Customer and product match.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings": same address and DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the contract names one as doing business as the other. Sarah Chen is the account owner. SKU NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), with a list price of $85,000. That matches the request: 25% is $21,250 off, leaving $63,750.

**3. The request uses standard pricing.** The justification is "Competitive", not a contract term. That matters because Acme's special terms don't reach this product:
- The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It lists NS-EDGE as a supplied product, but its 15% discount eligibility applies only to the NS-500 controller.
- The pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers "NS-500 … only" and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." Even for NS-500 it would cap the discount at 15%.

**4. Who can approve.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (it runs 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. That role can approve up to and including 10%.
- Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales (Michael Torres).
- **Over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.** That's David Morgan.
- Anything over 10% needs recorded approval evidence.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** It can go ahead once David Morgan approves, but not until DR-9102 is confirmed in the CRM.
