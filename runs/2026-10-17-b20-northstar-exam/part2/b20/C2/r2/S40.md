## 1. Customer and scope
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2051, ERP customer C-1002, DUNS 07-331-9025. All three identifiers match the ticket and the support terms.
- **Support terms:** BlueRiver Logistics Support Terms (reference SUP-BRL-2025), Silver plan, $2,500 a month.
- **Coverage:** the terms cover NS-Cloud Operations Suite (NS-CLOUD) from 2025-01-01 00:00 CT. The ticket opened on 2026-08-20, so it is **in scope**.

## 2. True severity: Severity 2 (High)
- **What the ticket records:** alerts stopped at the St. Louis and Memphis depots. The other depots were fine. As a workaround, dispatchers were phoning drivers to track exceptions.
- **Why it isn't Severity 1:** dispatch continued through a workaround, and NS-Cloud was not down for all users.
- **Why it is Severity 2:** it meets test (b), an alarming function unavailable at one or more sites "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks". It also meets test (c), NS-Cloud unavailable to some but not all sites.
- The customer reported it as P2, which agrees.

- **Targets for Severity 2 on the Silver plan:** initial response within 2 business hours, restoration within 12 business hours.
- **Clock:** business hours only, 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday. The holiday calendar has no Northstar holiday between Aug 20 and Aug 24.
- **Clock start:** the ticket opened through the portal at 2026-08-20T19:45-05:00, a Thursday evening outside business hours. Both clocks therefore start at **2026-08-21T08:00-05:00**.
- **Pauses and exclusions:** none recorded. No "Awaiting Customer" status, no request to the customer, no maintenance window in August (notice MN-2607 covered July), and no claim that the customer caused the incident.

- **Claim:** received in writing by email on 2026-08-28T14:05-05:00. The window runs 30 calendar days after the restoration date (2026-08-24) and closes 2026-09-23 23:59 CT, so the claim was **on time**.
