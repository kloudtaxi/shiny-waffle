## Answer

**Yes, but only with authorization.** Acme can take a $1,000,000 limit under the parent guarantee. Sarah Chen can't approve it herself: it needs approval from Elena Novak (Director of Finance) and concurrence from Michael Torres (VP Sales). Before anyone acts, someone must confirm that CR-9203 exists, because I couldn't find it in Northstar ERP.

**The request.** I couldn't find a credit request CR-9203 anywhere in the knowledge base. The only pending ERP request for this customer (C-1001) is **CR-9201**: also raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, but for $250,000 → $400,000, justified by the third-plant ramp-up rather than the guarantee. I decided CR-9203 as you gave it and did not substitute CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9203 exists, and how it relates to CR-9201, is a blocking condition.

**The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing". Both records share the DUNS number 04-812-7730 and the Milwaukee address, and the legal documents say "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing". CRM lists it as a **Strategic** account.

**Policy.** The Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request can have been paid, or still be unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme passes:
- INV-60412 ($85k) was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24, early.
- INV-62011 ($480k) was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22, early.
- INV-62305 ($720k) was due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so the rule doesn't apply to it.

**Maximum limit.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc.) raises it by the $400,000 it guarantees, to **$1,150,000**. The guarantee names Acme Mfg. Holdings and is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, which covers the request date. $1,000,000 is within the maximum.

**Authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives credit approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $100k), the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance, so she has none. For limits over $500,000, the policy requires Director of Finance approval, plus VP Sales concurrence for Strategic accounts.
