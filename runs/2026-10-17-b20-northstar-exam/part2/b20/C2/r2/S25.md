## Answer

**DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on the NS-500, but 15% is above her 10% limit. Michael Torres (VP Sales) must approve it.

1. **The request:** The CRM record for DR-9001 matches what was submitted exactly. It is for account CRM-2048, product NS-500, a 15.0% discount on $250,000 list ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 and it is "Pending Approval". It rests on the Acme contract terms, citing "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company. They share a DUNS number (04-812-7730) and an address, and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings is "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. I found no record that it was terminated or amended. It makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500, but says "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% of list price. It was approved by David Morgan and replaced the older 10% exception for requests from 2025-04-01. The requested 15% is exactly at that maximum, so the request is within it. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority."
6. **The policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy, with lower limits, only starts on 2027-01-01, so it does not apply here.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under the 2026 policy she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **The required approver:** Under the 2026 policy, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres (EMP-200) holds that role, and Sarah Chen reports to him. Anything above 10% also needs the approval to be recorded.
