## Answer

**DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen alone. Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it, and the submitted figures have to be corrected first.**

**The submitted figures don't match the CRM record.** You submitted 8.0%, a $20,000 discount and $230,000 net. CRM shows DR-9001 at **15.0%, a $37,500 discount and $212,500 net**, on $250,000 list, dated 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. I decided on the CRM figure (15%). This matters: at 8% Sarah could approve the request herself, but at 15% she can't.

1. **Request:** DR-9001. The justification, "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", means it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing, Strategic tier, owner Sarah Chen) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings). They share an address and DUNS number (04-812-7730), and the agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500. It also says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It covers NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15% of list, and was approved by David Morgan. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and grants no approval authority. A 15% request is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), with the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. The 2027 policy doesn't apply until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above that, and the contract doesn't change it.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (her manager). Discounts over 10% also need recorded approval evidence.
