## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is eligible for 15% on the NS-500, but 15% is above her own approval limit. The Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan, has to approve it. First, though, someone must confirm that request DR-9113 exists: the knowledge base has no record of it.

1. **The request.** DR-9113 is not in the knowledge base. The only matching CRM record is **DR-9001**. It has the same account, product, 15% discount, amounts, requestor and justification, but it's dated **2026-09-23**, not 2027-02-01. I haven't treated DR-9001 as this request. I've decided on DR-9113 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists. The request relies on contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product.** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on 2027-02-01. It makes Acme eligible for "up to 15%" on the NS-500. It also says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025. It covers the NS-500 only, sets a maximum of 15% of list price, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority." The requested 15% is within the maximum, so eligibility is established.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 (PRICING-POLICY-2027) covers 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. The 2026 approval matrix expired on 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah Chen's limit.** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve discounts up to and including 5%. 15% is over that.
8. **Required approver.** Under the 2027 policy, discounts over 12% need the Chief Revenue Officer, which is David Morgan. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for any discount over 5%.
9. **Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme's contract and exception make it eligible, but that doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it.

Two things to note:
- The knowledge base doesn't say how long David Morgan has held the CRO title, so I've added a non-blocking check that he still holds it on 2027-02-01.
- He also approved the exception and signed the agreement. That doesn't stop him approving this request.
