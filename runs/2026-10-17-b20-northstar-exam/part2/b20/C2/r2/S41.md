## Question (verbatim)

> Ticket SR-39644 (Acme Manufacturing, NS-Edge vibration data lost for six presses, reported P1): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

I worked SR-39644 through the procedure. **No service level was breached and no credit is owed (`NO_BREACH`).** The true severity is Severity 2, not the P1 the customer reported.

**1. Customer.** CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730. That is Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. The ticket carries CRM-2048.

**2. Scope: in.** Acme's support terms (Schedule C to ACME-MFG-2025) cover the NS-Edge Monitoring Package on the Platinum plan, with coverage from 2025-04-01 00:00 CT. The ticket was opened on 2026-07-23, well after coverage started.

**3. True severity: 2 (High).** The impact on record is lost vibration data for presses 1–6 at Milwaukee Plant 2, covered by manual checks every two hours. The severity guide's Severity 2 includes "a … condition-monitoring function is unavailable … whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks." It is not Severity 1 because production didn't stop and there was a workaround.

**4. Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened 2026-07-23 13:10 CT, so schedule v2.0 applies (tickets opened on or after 2026-04-01). For Severity 2 on Platinum, the targets are a 2-hour initial response and a 12-hour restoration, on a 24×7 clock. Business hours and holidays don't affect it.

**5–6. Measurement.** All timestamps are already in CT (−05:00).
- **Initial response:** the engineer responded at 15:00, after 1 h 50 m against a 2 h target. **Met.**
- **Restoration clock:**
  - It started at 13:10, when the portal submission was received.
  - It paused from 15:00, when the engineer requested VPN credentials and set the ticket to "Awaiting Customer", until 07-24 08:30, when Johnny Johnson moved it back to In Progress after Kelly Ballard provided the credentials. That is 17 h 30 m paused.
  - Restoration was recorded at 07-24 18:40.
  - Counted time is 1 h 50 m plus 10 h 10 m, which is exactly 12 h 00 m. A miss needs more than the target, so this is **met**, with no margin.
- **Exclusions:** no maintenance window or other exclusion was recorded.

**7. Credits: none.** No target was missed, so no credit is owed. I found no credit claim for this ticket.

**8. Obligations.**
- **Northstar:** The Severity 2 escalation steps only apply if a target elapses unmet. The response came 10 minutes before its deadline, and restoration landed at the moment its target elapsed. Neither step was triggered, so nobody had to be engaged or notified. No claim decision or credit memo is due.
- **Customer:**
  - Acme had to give Northstar the information and access it needed. It did, by providing the VPN credentials.
  - No phone report was required, because that rule applies only to Severity 1. Reporting through the portal was fine.
  - No claim is needed.

**One caveat.** The result depends on the pause ending at 08:30. The record shows Kelly Ballard provided the credentials, but the only customer event with a timestamp is the 08:30 status change. If the credentials were actually recorded earlier, restoration would run past 12 hours. That would be a band 1 miss: a 5% credit, and notifications to the Director of Customer Support and the account owner, Sarah Chen. It's worth checking the ticket log to confirm.
