## Answer

**SR-39207 — Cedar Health Systems Inc.: no service level was breached and no credit is owed.** The ticket is Severity 2. Northstar's one remaining duty is to decline the customer's credit claim in writing by 2026-07-01. The customer met its own obligation.

**Customer and scope**
- **Customer:** The ticket's customer is Cedar Health Systems Inc. Its identifiers all match: CRM account CRM-2063, ERP customer C-1003 and DUNS 02-906-4418.
- **Coverage:** The support terms (SUP-CHS-2025) put the customer on the Silver plan. They cover NS-Cloud (NS-CLOUD), and coverage started 2025-12-01. The ticket was opened after that, so it is in scope.

**True severity: Severity 2.** The customer reported it as P2. The recorded impact is that NS-Cloud was unavailable at 2 of 14 facilities (the Fishers and Carmel outpatient centers), and "facility operations at both centers are continuing." That is not Severity 1, because operations didn't stop and not all users were affected. It meets Severity 2 criterion (c): NS-Cloud unavailable to some, but not all, sites.

**Schedule and targets.** The ticket was opened by portal at 2026-05-22 16:20 −04:00, which is 15:20 Central Time on a Friday. That falls under Schedule v2.0, which applies to tickets opened from 2026-04-01. For Severity 2 on Silver, the targets are an initial response within 2 business hours and restoration within 12 business hours. The clock runs 08:00–18:00 Central Time, Monday to Friday, excluding Northstar holidays. Monday 2026-05-25 (Memorial Day) is on Northstar's holiday calendar, so it doesn't count.

**Measurement.** No pauses or exclusions are recorded.

| Target | Measured (Central Time) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Response (2 business hours) | 15:20 → engineer response at 17:10 the same day = 1h50m | Met |
| Restoration (12 business hours) | Friday 15:20–18:00 (2h40m), plus Tuesday 08:00–17:05 (9h05m) = 11h45m | Met, with 15 minutes to spare |

**Credit: $0.** No target was missed. The customer's claim (portal, 2026-06-01 09:30 −04:00) arrived well within the 30-day window, which ran to 2026-06-25. It is a valid claim, but it gets nothing because there was no breach.

**What Northstar owes**
- **Decide the claim:** Marcus Adeyemi, Director of Customer Support, must send a written decision declining the claim. The deadline is the 30th calendar day after receipt, 2026-07-01. The record doesn't show whether that decision was sent.
- **Escalations:** None were triggered. A Severity 2 ticket requires notifying the Support Escalation Manager (Hannah Lindqvist) only if the response target passes with no response. It requires notifying the Director of Customer Support and the account owner (Anthony Day) only if the restoration target passes before restoration. Neither happened.
- **No credit memo and no root-cause finding are due.**

**What the customer must do**
- **Phone report:** Not required. Only Severity 1 incidents must be reported by phone, so the portal report was fine.
- **Claim:** Met, because the claim was submitted in writing through the portal and on time.
- **Ongoing:** The customer must give Northstar the information and access it needs. No request for either is recorded.
