## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the policy in force lets that role approve discounts only up to and including 10%. A 15% discount needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. There is also a product mismatch that has to be resolved before anyone acts on this.

**The record in Northstar CRM doesn't match the request as you gave it.** CRM row DR-9001 reads: `DR-9001, CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0, 250000, 37500, 212500, sarah.chen@northstar.example, 2026-09-23, Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement, Pending Approval`. The product is **NS-500 (Industrial Controller)**, not NS-CLOUD, and the amounts are $250,000 list, $37,500 discount and $212,500 net, not $120,000 / $18,000 / $102,000. The email about it is also titled "Acme NS-500 — DR-9001". I decided on the CRM's values and listed the difference as a blocking condition below.

**How I decided:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% on 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730).
3. **Product:** NS-500, according to the CRM.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows up to 15% on the NS-500 but says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers the NS-500 only, up to 15% of list price. The 15% requested is within that cap, so Acme is eligible. The exception says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The older 10% terms (ACME-SA-2023 and EXC-ACME-NS500-10) are expired or replaced.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** up to and including 10%, so 15% is above her limit.
8. **Who must approve:** discounts above 10% and up to and including 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. The policy also requires recorded approval evidence for anything above 10%.

**If the product really is NS-CLOUD,** the answer changes. The exception covers the NS-500 only and excludes every other product. The Master Supply Agreement lists NS-Cloud as a supplied product but gives it no discount. With no special terms covering NS-CLOUD, the outcome would be REVIEW_REQUIRED (commercial review), not approval.
