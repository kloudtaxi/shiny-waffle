## Answer

**No, DR-9001 can't be approved yet.** The request says the 15% is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", but that agreement isn't in the knowledge base. No other contract or pricing exception for NS-500 is there either. The procedure says to ask for the document before deciding, so the outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**.

1. **The request:** The CRM record for DR-9001 matches what you submitted: account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. In the ERP system it's customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS 04-812-7730). It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a different company.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **An active contract:** None found. The knowledge base has no "Master Supply Agreement" and no contract for Acme active on 2026-09-23. All it has is what people have said:
   - Sarah's email says "their agreement covers it" and that 15% "is what we did for them last September".
   - The account strategy says Acme "negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement" and "I believe it's 15%".
   
   Neither is the contract, and an earlier approval doesn't make Acme eligible now.
5. **A pricing exception:** None covers NS-500 for Acme on that date.
6. **The policy in effect:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (1 Jan – 31 Dec 2026). The 2027 policy, with lower limits, only applies to requests dated on or after 1 Jan 2027, so it doesn't apply here.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **The required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Discounts above 10% also need recorded approval evidence.

Once the agreement is produced and shows Acme can get at least 15% on NS-500 on 2026-09-23, this would likely become **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**, with Michael Torres approving. Even then, the contract alone can't approve it. If the agreement allows less than 15%, the request would be rejected or escalated instead.
