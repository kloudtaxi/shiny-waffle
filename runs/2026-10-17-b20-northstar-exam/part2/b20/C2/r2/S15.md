## Answer

No, not on the basis it gives. Torres has the authority for 12%, but the request relies on Acme's contract, which doesn't cover BlueRiver. It needs commercial review before anyone approves it. Separately, I couldn't find DR-9107 in the system of record.

**1. The request.** DR-9107: CRM-2051, NS-500, 12% ($250,000 list, $30,000 off, $220,000 net), requested by michael.torres@northstar.example on 2026-09-23. Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
- **The request record is missing.** The CRM discount-request records don't include DR-9107. The only NS-500 request on 2026-09-23 with this exact justification is **DR-9001**. That one is for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048) at 15%, requested by Sarah Chen. I haven't substituted it; this decision is on DR-9107 as you gave it.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, a Standard-tier account owned by Kelly Floyd. The support agreement's legal name for it is Blue River Logistics LLC.

**3. The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) is between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and allows up to 15% on NS-500. BlueRiver is not a party to it. BlueRiver's only agreement is its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025), a Silver support plan covering NS-CLOUD only, with no discount terms.

**5. The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15% on NS-500) names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. None names BlueRiver. Special terms don't carry over from one customer to another.

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on 2026-09-23.

**7–8. Authority.** Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. From over 10% up to 20% needs the VP Sales, and over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer. Torres is VP Sales, so 12% is within his authority. But authority doesn't create eligibility.

**9. The decision is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** BlueRiver needs a commercial review: either its own terms, or treatment as a standard-pricing request. Note that under standard pricing, a 12% discount would also need recorded approval evidence, because it's over 10%.
