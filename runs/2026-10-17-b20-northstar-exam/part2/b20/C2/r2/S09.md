## Answer

I've checked the request, the agreements and the 2025 policy, and I'm writing up the decision now.

**No. Sarah Chen could not have approved this as submitted.** She had enough authority. The problem is that Acme was only eligible for 10% on that date, and 15% is more than that.

1. **The request.** I couldn't find DR-9101 in the knowledge base. The closest record is DR-9001: same account, product, 15% and dollar amounts, but dated 2026-09-23. I haven't used that record in its place. I decided on DR-9101 as given, dated 2025-03-31, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition. The request relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it depends on special terms.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS, 04-812-7730).
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract on 2025-03-31.** The Master Supply Agreement the request cites (ACME-MFG-2025, up to 15% on NS-500) only took effect on **2025-04-01**, the day after the request. The agreement actually in force was **ACME-SA-2023**: "up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500… for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (15% maximum) is also effective from 2025-04-01. It replaces EXC-ACME-NS500-10 only "for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01," so the 10% terms still applied on 2025-03-31.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Authority.** Enterprise Account Executives "may approve discounts up to and including 15%," and Sarah Chen is one. So 15% was within her authority.
8. **Approver.** Above 15% the policy requires the VP Sales. That doesn't apply here, and the 2025 policy has no band covering a request above the customer's eligibility.
9. **Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Acme's eligibility on that date was 10%, and a higher approver can't fix that. A contract allowing a discount doesn't decide employee authority, and employee authority doesn't create contract eligibility. Two ways forward: lower the request to 10% or less, which she could approve herself, or date the order on or after 2025-04-01, when the 15% terms apply. Either way it would be a new or amended request.
