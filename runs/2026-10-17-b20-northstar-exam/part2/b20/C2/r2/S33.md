## Answer

**No, it can't be approved as submitted.** The request depends on a parent guarantee that I can't find anywhere in the knowledge base. Without it, $1,000,000 is above the most Acme can be given. The decision is to ask for the guarantee first. Separately, CR-9203 itself doesn't appear in the ERP records the knowledge base holds.

1. **The request.** The ERP credit-request records hold only one Acme request dated 2026-09-23: **CR-9201**, from Sarah Chen, raising the limit from $250,000 to $400,000. Its stated reason is the third-plant ramp-up, not the guarantee. CR-9203 isn't there. I've decided on CR-9203 as you gave it, not on CR-9201. Confirming that CR-9203 really exists is a blocking condition.
2. **The customer.** ERP C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings"), CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing") and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. Its tier is **Strategic**, and its parent is Acme Group Holdings, Inc.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history.** A customer is ineligible if any invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or is still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. I found no Acme invoice records, so I can't apply this test. The invoices are needed too.
5. **The maximum limit.** Strategic accounts can go up to **$750,000**. Under the policy, a parent guarantee in force raises that by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names.
   - The only sign of a guarantee is Sarah Chen's own message: "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it."
   - There is no guarantee document: no guarantor, no amount, no dates, and nothing saying which company it covers. The message also names "Acme Group" rather than the legal parent, Acme Group Holdings, Inc.
   - So $1,000,000 can't be shown to be within any limit. If the guarantee doesn't turn up, the request would be rejected or escalated, because it's $250,000 over the maximum.
6. **The requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives credit approval authority only to Finance roles, so she can't approve this herself.
7. **Who would have to approve.** A limit over $500,000 needs **Director of Finance** approval. Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs **VP Sales** agreement (the policy calls this concurrence). That's Michael Torres. I couldn't find who the Director of Finance is. Michael Torres is also Sarah Chen's manager; the policy as stored doesn't say this rules him out.
