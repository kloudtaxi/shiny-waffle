## Answer

**Yes, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme is eligible for a $400,000 limit. Sarah has no credit-approval authority, so the request needs approval from the Finance Manager, Priya Shah.

**1. The request.** CR-9201 is in the Northstar ERP credit-request log exactly as you submitted it: C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. Its justification is the third-plant ramp-up and doesn't cite any third-party support. In a separate email Sarah says "Acme Group's parent guarantee should cover it". The limit doesn't need the guarantee, so I decided it as a standard request.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing". They share DUNS 04-812-7730 and the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, and the guarantee names "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing". CRM lists the account tier as **Strategic**.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (CREDIT-POLICY-2026) applies to requests from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the earlier policy.

**4. Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. Acme's invoices:
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24, early.
- INV-62011 ($480,000, SO-7001): due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22, early.
- INV-62305 ($720,000, SO-7002): due 2026-10-02, after the request date, so the rule doesn't cover it.

No invoice breaks the rule.

**5. Maximum limit.** The Strategic maximum is $750,000, and $400,000 is well within it. The parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) is from Acme Group Holdings, names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is capped at $400,000. It's in force and would raise the maximum further, but this limit doesn't need it.

**6. Sarah Chen's authority.** She's an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The policy gives approval authority only to Accounts Receivable Specialists (up to $100,000), the Finance Manager and the Director of Finance. She has none.

**7. Who must approve.** Authority applies to the new total of $400,000. That falls in the band above $100,000 up to $500,000, which needs the **Finance Manager: Priya Shah** (EMP-401, Finance). Strategic accounts need VP Sales concurrence only above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.

**Two things to flag, neither blocking:**
- As of today (2026-10-18), INV-62305 for $720,000 is unpaid and 16 days past due. If it isn't paid by 2026-11-01 it will break the 30-day rule. It's also larger than the $400,000 limit requested.
- Sarah's email describes the guarantee as supporting the request. It exists and covers Acme, but the decision doesn't depend on it.
