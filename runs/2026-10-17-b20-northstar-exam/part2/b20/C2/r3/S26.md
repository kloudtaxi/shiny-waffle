## Answer

**No — Sarah Chen can't approve CR-9201 herself. The customer qualifies for $400,000, but the policy needs Finance Manager Priya Shah to approve it.** One check should be done first: Acme's $480,000 June order has no invoice on file.

**Request.** CR-9201 is in Northstar ERP exactly as you submitted it: C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, raised by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval. The ERP record gives order growth as the reason and cites no third-party support, so I treated it as a request on Acme's own standing.

**Customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. The guarantee document describes it as "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing." CRM records its tier as **Strategic**.

**Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It runs from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated from 2026-01-01.

**Payment history.** The rule: no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. The ERP invoice records (INV-30001 to INV-30020) contain no invoices for C-1001, so nothing breaks the rule. But sales order SO-7001 (C-1001, 2026-06-10, $480,000) is marked "Completed" with no matching invoice. On Net 45 terms it would have fallen due before the request date. Whether it was paid on time can't be checked, so I've made confirming it a blocking condition.

**Maximum limit.** Strategic accounts can go up to $750,000, so $400,000 fits on Acme's own standing. Separately, parent guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and covers up to $400,000. It was in force on the request date and would raise the maximum to $1,150,000. The decision doesn't depend on it.

**Requestor authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. The 2026 policy gives Sales staff no authority to approve credit limits.

**Required approvers.** The limit is judged on the new total of $400,000. That falls in the band above $100,000 and up to $500,000, which needs **Finance Manager** approval. Priya Shah holds that role. VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic limits above $500,000, so it isn't needed here.
