**Not on Sarah Chen's own authority.** I decided on the CRM record, which shows 15%, not the 8% in your submission. Acme is entitled to that discount, but it's above Sarah's 10% limit. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it, and the discount figure has to be sorted out before anyone acts.

**The submission doesn't match CRM.** The CRM record for DR-9001 is for the same account (CRM-2048), product (NS-500), date (2026-09-23), requestor and justification. But the amounts differ:

| | Discount | Discount value | Net value |

Sarah's email about the deal ("They're asking for 15% off list") also says 15%. I decided on 15%, so the 8% version needs to be explained or corrected first.

1. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". They share a DUNS number and address, and the contract describes Acme Mfg. Holdings as doing business as Acme Manufacturing.
2. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
3. **Contract:** the Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It allows up to 15% on the NS-500. It also says "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that contract. It covers the NS-500 only, at up to 15%, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So 15% is within Acme's eligibility.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to this request, since it was dated in 2026. The 2027 policy, with lower limits, only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
6. **Sarah's authority:** she is an Enterprise Account Executive, and under the 2026 policy that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above that.
7. **Who must approve:** discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres (EMP-200) holds that role, and the policy requires recorded approval evidence.

The contract makes Acme eligible for 15%, but that doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it.
