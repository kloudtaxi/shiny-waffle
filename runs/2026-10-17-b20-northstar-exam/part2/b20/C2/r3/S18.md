## Answer

**Yes, she could have, with conditions.** On 2025-03-31, 8% was within both Acme's eligibility for NS-500 and Sarah Chen's own approval limit. But the agreement the request cites wasn't in effect that day, and DR-9110 isn't in the knowledge base.

**1. The request.** I couldn't find DR-9110 anywhere in the knowledge base. The only Acme discount request on record is DR-9001: NS-500 at 15%, dated 2026-09-23, also by Sarah Chen. That's a different request, so I decided on DR-9110 as you gave it. Someone needs to confirm it exists in Northstar CRM before anyone acts on this. Its basis is special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS 04-812-7730; the agreement says one does business as the other). Sarah Chen is the account owner.

**3. The product** is NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The cited contract wasn't in effect.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it started the day after the request. The pricing exception attached to it, EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15%), also starts 2025-04-01. It replaces the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, only for requests dated on or after that day.

**5. An earlier agreement did cover this product and date.** Agreement ACME-SA-2023, through the Acme NS-500 Volume Pricing Exception, says Acme "may receive up to **10%** off list price on the NS-500 … for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31." Its status is Active. 8% is within that 10%. Two caveats:
- The knowledge base holds nothing about EXC-ACME-NS500-10 itself. I'm assuming it's the same as the Volume Pricing Exception, but that isn't confirmed.
- The cut-off falls exactly on the request date, so this only works if "to 2025-03-31" includes that day.

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).

**7. Her authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under that policy, "Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%." 8% is well inside that.

**8. Other approvers.** None needed. VP Sales approval only applies above 15%.

**9. Decision: APPROVE.** She has the authority, and the eligibility is established, just by the earlier agreement rather than the one cited. Before this is acted on:
- Confirm DR-9110 exists in the CRM with these figures.
- Correct the request's basis from the Master Supply Agreement (not yet in effect) to ACME-SA-2023 / EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
- Deal Desk should confirm the 10% term ran through 2025-03-31 inclusive. The 2025 policy also says "Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed."
