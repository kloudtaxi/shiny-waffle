2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings."
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), list price $85,000, which matches the request.
4. **Active contract:** the Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It supplies NS-Edge, but at "Northstar's current list prices, less any discount agreed in an Order." The only discount right it gives is "up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only… It does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older ACME-SA-2023 also covered only NS-500, at 10%, and ended 2025-03-31. Nothing on record covers NS-EDGE, and special terms don't carry over to other products.

**What happens next:** the CRM record needs confirming, and the request needs commercial review (Deal Desk owns pricing exceptions). As standard pricing, 12% would need Michael Torres's approval and a recorded approval.
