## Answer

**Can it be approved? Not yet.** CR-9208 isn't in Northstar ERP, so nothing can happen until someone confirms the request exists. On the facts as you gave them, Acme can have a $650,000 limit. But Michael Torres can't approve it himself: Elena Novak, Director of Finance, must approve it.

**The request isn't in the system of record.** The knowledge base has no ERP request CR-9208. The ERP's only pending request for this customer (C-1001) is **CR-9201**. That one has the same date (2026-09-23) and the same justification, but it differs in two ways:
- **Requestor:** Sarah Chen, not Michael Torres.
- **Amount:** $250,000 → $400,000, not $650,000.

I haven't treated CR-9201 as your request. I decided CR-9208 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists.

**How the decision was reached:**
1. **Customer:** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing". They share a DUNS number (04-812-7730) and an address, and the guarantee says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. The CRM tier is Strategic.
2. **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
3. **Payment history:** I checked every invoice in the ledger (INV-30001 to INV-30020). None is billed to C-1001, so no Acme invoice breaks the "paid or unpaid more than 30 days past due" rule. Acme does have one completed sales order (SO-7001, $480,000) with no invoice recorded. That gap is a non-blocking note.
4. **Maximum limit:** $750,000 for Strategic accounts, so $650,000 fits on Acme's own standing. A $400,000 parent guarantee from Acme Group Holdings (GRT-ACME-2026) covers Acme Mfg. Holdings for 2026–2027, but the request doesn't rely on it.
5. **Michael Torres's authority:** None. He is VP Sales, and the policy gives his role no approval band. It only gives VP Sales a concurrence role.
6. **Who must approve:** Limits above $500,000 need the Director of Finance (Elena Novak). For Strategic accounts they also need VP Sales concurrence, and the VP Sales is Michael Torres himself. The policy doesn't say whether a requestor may concur on his own request, so I've flagged that for confirmation without blocking on it.
