## Answer

**No.** On the date in the system of record, Sarah Chen can't approve this 15% discount herself. Acme is entitled to 15% on the NS-500, but the discount is above her own approval limit, so the VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it.

**The date doesn't match.** You gave 2025-09-23, but the CRM record for DR-9001 says **2026-09-23**, and so does the related email (sent 2026-09-23 09:14 CT). I decided on the CRM date. The date changes the answer:
- **On 2026-09-23** the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. Sarah can't approve 15% alone.
- **If the date really were 2025-09-23**, the Pricing Policy 2025 would apply. It let Enterprise Account Executives approve up to and including 15%, so she could have approved it herself.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah submitted it on the basis of "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms. Status: Pending Approval.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both records carry DUNS 04-812-7730.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on NS-500, but says "each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers NS-500 only, up to 15% of list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan approved it. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So 15% is within Acme's entitlement.
6. **Policy and authority:** Under the 2026 policy, Sarah (Enterprise Account Executive, EMP-101) can approve up to 10%. 15% falls in the VP Sales band. The approval matrix APP-MATRIX-2026 agrees and says authority attaches to the role, not to the person or the account. Discounts over 10% also need recorded approval evidence.
7. **Approver:** Michael Torres, VP Sales (EMP-200), who is Sarah's manager.
