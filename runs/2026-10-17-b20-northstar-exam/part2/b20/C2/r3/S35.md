## Answer

**CR-9201 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen alone.** The limit needs Finance Manager approval, which is Priya Shah. The amount also has to be settled first. The submission asks for $300,000, but the ERP record shows $400,000. Sarah's own email also says "from $250,000 to $400,000". I decided on the ERP figure of $400,000. The difference is a blocking condition below.

1. **Request:** In the ERP record, CR-9201 is for customer C-1001. It was raised by Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive) on 2026-09-23 to move the limit from $250,000 to $400,000, and its status is Pending Approval. Sarah's email relies on "Acme Group's parent guarantee", so the request relies on a guarantee rather than Acme's standing alone.
2. **Customer:** ERP C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The CRM account is CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", Strategic tier. They are the same company: same tax-registration number and address, and the guarantee says Acme Mfg. Holdings is "doing business as Acme Manufacturing". Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044) is a different company, and its invoices were not counted.
3. **Policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy.
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that no invoice due in the previous 12 months may have been paid, or still be unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date. None of the 20 invoice records I could retrieve (INV-30001 to INV-30020) belongs to C-1001, so no late invoices were found. However, I found no invoice at all for Acme's completed $480,000 order SO-7001. I've noted this below without blocking the decision.
5. **Maximum limit:** Strategic accounts can have up to $750,000, so $400,000 is within the limit even without the guarantee. The guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) is on file. Acme Group Holdings, Inc. gives it for Acme Mfg. Holdings, it runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and it covers up to $400,000. It was in force on the request date and raises the maximum to $1,150,000.
6. **Sarah's authority:** None. Only Accounts Receivable Specialists can approve on their own, and only up to $100,000. The authority test uses the new total limit.
7. **Required approver:** Limits above $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval, and Priya Shah is the Finance Manager. Director of Finance approval and VP Sales concurrence apply only above $500,000, so neither is needed. Even at the submitted $300,000, the same approver would be needed.
