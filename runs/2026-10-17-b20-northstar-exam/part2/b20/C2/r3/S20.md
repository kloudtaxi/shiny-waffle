## Answer

No. Michael Torres can't approve this request. The discount is within his authority, but it's above what Acme is entitled to under its contract.

1. **Request record:** I couldn't find DR-9112 in the knowledge base. The only Acme NS-500 request recorded for 2026-09-23 is DR-9001. That one was raised by Sarah Chen at 15% (net $212,500) with the same justification text. I haven't used DR-9001 in its place. The decision below is on DR-9112 as you gave it, and someone must confirm it exists in Northstar CRM before anything acts on it.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings". It's the counterparty on the Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025).
3. **Contract:** The agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers NS-500 and makes Acme eligible for discounts of "up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies".
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Its maximum is 15% of list price. It replaced the earlier EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated from 2025-04-01. It states it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and doesn't give any employee approval authority.
5. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31); the 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01. Its approval bands are:
   - Account Executives: up to 10%
   - VP Sales: over 10% up to 20%
   - Chief Revenue Officer (CRO): over 20%
6. **Authority:** Torres is VP Sales, so an 18% discount is within his band.
7. **Decision:** 18% is 3 points above Acme's 15% maximum. Under the SOP, authority doesn't make up for missing eligibility. Neither Torres nor anyone more senior can approve this under the current terms. Either the request is cut to 15% or less, or the Deal Desk (which owns Acme's pricing exception) negotiates amended terms. Separately, Torres would be approving his own request, which the governance team should look at.
