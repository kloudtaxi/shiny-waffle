## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this 15% discount herself.** Acme is eligible for it under its contract, but 15% is above her own limit. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 in Northstar CRM matches what you sent. It is for account CRM-2048, product NS-500, a 15% discount ($37,500 off $250,000 list, net $212,500), requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company. Both records carry DUNS 04-812-7730.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller (SKU NS-500).
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. It covers the NS-500 and says Acme is "eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller." It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies." I found no record that the agreement was ended early.
5. **The pricing exception:** Schedule B, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only". It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, allows up to 15% off list, and replaces the older 10% exception. The requested 15% is exactly at that ceiling, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, which has lower limits, only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under the 2026 policy, that role can approve discounts "up to and including 10%". 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** The policy says discounts "greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval". Michael Torres (EMP-200) is VP Sales and Sarah's manager. Discounts above 10% also need a recorded approval.
