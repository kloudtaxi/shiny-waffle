## Answer

**DR-9001 can't be approved on Sarah Chen's own authority.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it is above her 10% limit. Michael Torres, the VP Sales, has to approve it. Also, the request you sent says "Approved", but the CRM still shows it as **Pending Approval** with no approver or approval date. So it hasn't been approved yet.

1. **The request (CRM):** DR-9001 is for account CRM-2048. It asks for 15.0% off NS-500: $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". So it relies on special terms. Every figure matches the CRM except the status.
2. **The customer:** The CRM's "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) is the same company as the ERP's "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). The DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address match, and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows Acme up to 15% off NS-500, but says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers NS-500 only, runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and caps the discount at 15% of list. It replaced the older EXC-ACME-NS500-10. The requested 15% is exactly at the cap, so Acme is eligible. The exception also says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy doesn't start until 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, plus recorded approval evidence. Michael Torres is the VP Sales and Sarah's manager.
