## Answer

**DR-9001 can't be approved on Sarah Chen's own authority. It can go ahead once Michael Torres, VP Sales, approves it.** Acme is entitled to the 15% on NS-500, but Sarah can only approve up to 10%. The contract makes the discount allowed; it doesn't give her the authority to approve it.

1. **The request.** The CRM record matches what was submitted: CRM-2048, NS-500, 15.0%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, dated 2026-09-23, requested by sarah.chen@northstar.example, "Pending Approval". It relies on the customer's contract ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"), not standard pricing.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". They share DUNS number 04-812-7730 and address, and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings is "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".
3. **The product.** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000, which matches the request.
4. **Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers NS-500 and makes Acme eligible for discounts "of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller". It also says "Each discount remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only, at a maximum of 15% of list. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. David Morgan approved it, and it replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10 from 2025-04-01. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The 15% requested is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy (5% / 12% bands) only applies to requests dated from 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply here.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101). Under the 2026 policy that role may approve "up to and including 10%". 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver.** The 2026 policy says discounts "greater than 10% and up to and including 20% require VP Sales approval", and anything over 10% needs a recorded approval. Michael Torres (EMP-200) is VP Sales and is also Sarah's manager.

Nothing else needs to happen before approval. The submitted figures and date match the system of record.
