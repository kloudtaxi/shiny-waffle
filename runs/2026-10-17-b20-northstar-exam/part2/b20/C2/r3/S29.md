## Answer

**No, it can't be approved. Even with the guarantee, $1,300,000 is above the most Acme can be given.**

**First, CR-9204 isn't in the knowledge base.** The Northstar ERP request log has only one Acme request dated 2026-09-23: **CR-9201** (C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, Sarah Chen, "Third-plant ramp-up", Pending Approval). The record you pasted doesn't match it. Its ID, its $1.3M amount and its guarantee justification all differ, so I decided on CR-9204 as you gave it and didn't treat CR-9201 as a stand-in. Someone needs to confirm CR-9204 exists in the ERP.

1. **The customer:** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (DUNS 04-812-7730) and the guarantee's "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing" are the same company. Its CRM tier is **Strategic**.
2. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (CREDIT-POLICY-2026), in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
3. **The guarantee:** The Parent Company Guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026) is given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names Acme Mfg. Holdings, runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and caps the guarantor's liability at **$400,000**. So it covers this customer on the request date.
4. **The maximum:** The Strategic maximum is $750,000. Under the policy, a guarantee in force raises that "by the guaranteed amount", so the ceiling is **$750,000 + $400,000 = $1,150,000**. The $1,300,000 requested is **$150,000 over**. A higher approver can't override this.
5. **Payment history:** I couldn't fully check it. The tools only showed me 20 of the 30 invoices on record. The one I opened (INV-30001) belongs to a different customer (C-1044), and I found no invoice linked to C-1001. This doesn't change the outcome, but it would need checking before any future request.
6. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives her role no approval authority. A new total limit above $500,000 needs the Director of Finance to approve it, and because Acme is Strategic, Michael Torres (VP Sales) must also concur. I couldn't find who the Director of Finance is. None of this matters while the request is over the maximum.

To go forward, a new request would need to be at or below $1,150,000, or come with a larger guarantee. Within the cap, it would still need Director of Finance approval and VP Sales concurrence.
