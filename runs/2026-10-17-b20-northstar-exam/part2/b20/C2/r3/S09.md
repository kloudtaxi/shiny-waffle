## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen had the authority to approve 15%, but on 2025-03-31 Acme wasn't contractually eligible for it. The terms she cited took effect one day later. Separately, I couldn't find request DR-9101 in the knowledge base at all.

1. **The request isn't in the system of record.** The only Acme NS-500 discount request in the CRM is DR-9001, dated 2026-09-23. It has the same figures (15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requestor and justification. I decided on DR-9101 as you gave it, not on DR-9001. Confirming that DR-9101 really exists, and which date is right, is a blocking condition.

2. **Customer and product.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS 04-812-7730). The product is NS-500, the Industrial Controller.

3. **What the request relies on.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
   - **Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025):** offers up to 15% on NS-500, but it only takes effect on 2025-04-01. It wasn't in force on 2025-03-31.
   - **Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15:** also up to 15%, but also effective 2025-04-01. It replaces the earlier exception only for requests dated on or after that day.
   - **What was in force on 2025-03-31:** agreement ACME-SA-2023, with the volume pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10. It allows up to **10%** on NS-500 for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so its last day is the request date.

   15% is above the 10% Acme was eligible for that day.

4. **Sarah's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31) lets that role approve discounts up to and including 15%. Above 15% needs VP Sales.

5. **Decision: reject or escalate.** The obstacle is the customer's eligibility, not Sarah's authority, so a more senior approver can't fix it. Either the request is re-dated on or after 2025-04-01, when the new terms apply, or the discount is cut to 10% or less. Otherwise it needs new or amended terms.
