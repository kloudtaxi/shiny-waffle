> Ticket SR-39802 (Acme Industrial, Akron, NS-Edge gateways offline, claims the Acme Platinum plan): what is its true severity, was a service level breached, what does Northstar owe, who must be engaged or notified and by when, and what must the customer do?

## Answer

**SR-39802 is out of scope.** Acme Industrial has no support terms covering it, so no service level applies, nothing can be breached and no credit is owed.

**1. The customer.** The ticket's customer is Acme Industrial Supply Co.: CRM account CRM-2091, ERP customer C-1044, DUNS 09-552-1187, 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH. ACME Manufacturing (Acme Mfg. Holdings) is a different company, with CRM-2048, ERP C-1001 and DUNS 04-812-7730.

**2. Scope.** The "Acme Platinum support plan" comes only from the caller's own statement on the ticket ("We have the Acme Platinum support plan"). The only Acme Platinum terms on file are Schedule C (SUP-ACME-C), and they belong to Acme Mfg. Holdings. They do cover NS-Edge, but they say plainly: *"Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company and is not covered."* No other support terms in the knowledge base name Acme Industrial. The ticket is therefore out of scope.

**3. True severity.** It would be Severity 1 (Critical), but under the out-of-scope rules that rating is used only for internal priority. The reasons: the NS-Edge gateways were offline, conveyor monitoring was dark, picking had halted at Akron (a distribution activity that could not continue), and there was no workaround. That meets the Severity 1 test. It also matches the customer's reported P1, though the rating comes from the recorded impact, not from their P1.

**4. Breach.** None. The Escalation Procedure (SOP-SUPPORT-007) says that for out-of-scope tickets "no targets apply… nothing can be breached." For the record, these are the times logged (all CT):
- Opened: 2026-08-10 05:05 (06:05 EDT)
- Engineer response: 06:40
- Restored: 13:20

**5. What Northstar owes.**
- **No credit.** The rules say "no credit is owed." The customer emailed a claim on 2026-08-14 asking for the "Severity 1 restoration credit under Acme's Platinum plan." It should be declined because the ticket is out of scope. The rules set no deadline for answering a claim on an out-of-scope ticket.
- **No escalations.** The rules say "the escalations above do not apply." The ticket is handled best effort.
- **One notification.** Northstar Support must notify Zachary Brown, the account owner of CRM-2091, no later than 10 business hours after the open time.
  - Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT, Monday to Friday, excluding Northstar holidays. The ticket opened Monday 2026-08-10 at 05:05 CT, before business hours, so the count starts at 08:00 that day.
  - **The deadline is 2026-08-10T18:00-05:00.** This assumes 2026-08-10 is not a Northstar holiday. The calendar says its list is complete, but the dates themselves weren't returned, so I couldn't check.
  - The record shows no sign that this notification was sent.

**6. What the customer must do.** Nothing under a service level, because no terms apply.
- They phoned the ticket in, which is what the schedule's Severity 1 rule asks for.
- Their credit claim gives them nothing, because no credit applies.
- If Acme Industrial wants guaranteed service levels, it needs its own support terms with Northstar. Zachary Brown, as account owner, is the person to pursue that.
