**SR-39644 has true severity 2 (High), and no service level was breached. The outcome is `NO_BREACH`: Northstar owes no service credit, and no escalation was triggered.** The restoration result has no margin at all. The restoration time comes to exactly 12h00m against a 12-hour target.

Its support terms are Schedule C to ACME-MFG-2025, on the Platinum plan. They cover NS-Edge Monitoring Package, and coverage started 2025-04-01 00:00 CT. The ticket was opened through the portal at 2026-07-23 13:10 CDT, so it is in scope. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091) is a different company and isn't covered.

**2. True severity: 2, not the reported P1.**
- **Not Severity 1:** that needs a product failure that stops production with no workaround. Here a workaround exists: technicians check vibration on presses 1–6 by hand every two hours.
- **Severity 2, criterion (b):** a condition-monitoring function is unavailable for one or more machines, "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks."

**3. Rules and targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies, because the ticket was opened on or after 2026-04-01 00:00 CT. All times are Central Daylight Time (UTC−05:00). For Severity 2 on Platinum the targets are 2 hours to initial response and 12 hours to restoration, on a 24×7 clock.

**4. Measurement.** The clock starts when the portal submission is received, 2026-07-23 13:10. No maintenance window was found.
- **Initial response: met.** The engineer responded at 2026-07-23 15:00, after 1h50m.
- **Pause:** at 15:00 the engineer asked Acme for VPN credentials and set the ticket to "Awaiting Customer." Kelly Ballard (Acme IT Architecture) provided them. The ticket went back to "In Progress" at 2026-07-24 08:30. That pauses the restoration clock for 17h30m.
- **Restoration: met, exactly at target.** Restored at 2026-07-24 18:40. That is 29h30m elapsed, minus the 17h30m pause, which leaves 12h00m. A credit applies only when elapsed time is more than the target, so 12h00m is met.

The record has no separate timestamp for when the credentials were handed over. I used the 08:30 status change as the end of the pause. If they were actually provided earlier, restoration would go over 12 hours and become a band 1 breach. That would mean a 5% credit, if Acme files a timely claim.

- **Root cause:** I found no root-cause requirement for this ticket.
