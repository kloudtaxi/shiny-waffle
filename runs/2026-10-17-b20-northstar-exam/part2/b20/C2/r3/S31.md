## Answer

**Yes, with conditions.** On 2025-09-23 the customer qualified for $200,000, but Michael Torres could not approve it himself. Priya Shah, the Finance Manager, would have to approve it. One problem stops it from going ahead now: **CR-9206 is not in the knowledge base's copy of Northstar ERP.** The only CR-92xx request I found is CR-9201, which is a different request (C-1001, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, $250k → $400k). I decided on the request as you gave it, and checking that CR-9206 exists in ERP is a blocking condition.

**How I got there**
- **Customer:** ERP C-1002 "Blue River Logistics LLC" and CRM-2051 "BlueRiver Logistics" are the same company. They share DUNS 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. CRM records the account tier as **Standard**.
- **What the request relies on:** the customer's own standing ("Fleet expansion"). It doesn't cite a guarantee, and none in the knowledge base names Blue River.
- **Policy:** the request is dated 2025-09-23, so **Northstar Credit Policy 2025** applies (in effect 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
- **Payment history:** the 2025 rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 45 days after its due date. C-1002 has two invoices:
  - INV-30014 was due 2026-02-27 and paid 2026-02-25.
  - INV-30020 was due 2026-10-02 and is unpaid.

  Both fall after the request date, so neither counts against it. There are no late invoices in the review window.
- **Maximum limit:** $300,000 for Standard accounts. $200,000 is under it, so the customer is **eligible**.
- **Requestor's authority:** Torres is VP Sales. The 2025 policy gives credit approval authority only to AR Specialists (up to $50k), the Finance Manager (over $50k up to $750k) and the Director of Finance (over $750k). VP Sales is not on that list.
- **Who must approve:** a $200,000 limit falls in the **Finance Manager** band, held by **Priya Shah**. The 2025 policy requires no concurrence, and she didn't submit the request, so the rule against approving your own request doesn't stop her.
- **Current limit:** ERP shows C-1002 at $100,000, last reviewed 2026-01-15. That matches the submitted figure, but this record postdates the request.
