## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Under the 2026 pricing policy her limit is 10%, and the request is for 15%. The CRM record for DR-9001 also doesn't match what was submitted.

**The product doesn't match the CRM record.** In Northstar CRM, DR-9001 is for the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**, not NS-Cloud: "DR-9001,CRM-2048,NS-500,15.0,250000,37500,212500,sarah.chen@northstar.example,2026-09-23,Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement,Pending Approval". The submission says NS-CLOUD, a $120,000 list value, an $18,000 discount and $102,000 net. The CRM says NS-500, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount and $212,500 net. The customer, 15%, date, requestor and justification are the same. I've decided on the CRM's version and made the mismatch a blocking condition.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, dated 2026-09-23, raised by Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive). It relies on contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS, 04-812-7730).
3. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It allows "discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller" and says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only", up to a 15% maximum, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It "does not apply to any other Northstar product" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." 15% is within the maximum, so the customer is eligible on the NS-500.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%. Above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales.
6. **Approver:** The VP Sales is Michael Torres.

**If the request really is for NS-Cloud,** the answer changes to REVIEW_REQUIRED. Neither the 15% exception nor the agreement's discount clause covers NS-Cloud, and special terms don't carry over to other products. In that case it would go to commercial review, not to Michael Torres.
