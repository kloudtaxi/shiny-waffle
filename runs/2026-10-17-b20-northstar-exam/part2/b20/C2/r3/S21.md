## Answer

Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Under the 2027 Pricing Policy, a 15% discount needs approval from the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan. Acme is entitled to the 15%, but there's a blocking problem first: request **DR-9113 isn't in the system of record.**

1. **The request isn't on record.** No entity holds Northstar CRM `DR-9113`. The only Acme NS-500 request recorded is **DR-9001**, which has the same customer, product, 15%, $250,000 / $37,500 / $212,500, requestor and justification. But it's dated **2026-09-23**, not 2027-02-01. I haven't decided on DR-9001 instead. I decided on DR-9113 as you gave it, and its existence must be confirmed before anyone acts. If DR-9113 turns out to be DR-9001, the date difference matters: DR-9001 would fall under the 2026 policy instead.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS, 04-812-7730). It's a Strategic account, and Sarah Chen owns it.
3. **Product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's active on 2027-02-01. It allows up to 15% on the NS-500, but says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and caps the discount at 15% of list. The request is exactly 15%, so Acme is eligible. The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 applies (2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31; it fully replaces the 2026 policy).
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), and under the 2027 policy that role can approve discounts up to and including 5%. 15% is over her limit. (The 2026 policy allowed 10%, so she couldn't approve it alone then either.)
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 12% need the Chief Revenue Officer, who is David Morgan (EMP-300) in the HR and org records. Any discount over 5% also needs recorded approval evidence. The records don't give a date range for his role, so I've added a non-blocking check that he still holds it on 2027-02-01.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Once DR-9113 is confirmed, it can go ahead only with David Morgan's approval as CRO.
