**No. Michael Torres can't approve DR-9107 as submitted. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** His title gives him enough authority for a 12% discount, but the request depends on contract terms that belong to a different customer.

1. **The request.** I took DR-9107 as you gave it: BlueRiver Logistics (CRM-2051), NS-500, 12% off $250,000 list (a $30,000 discount, $220,000 net), requested by michael.torres@northstar.example on 2026-09-23. The stated justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special contract terms. **DR-9107 is not in the knowledge base.** The only BlueRiver discount request on record is DR-7018: 8% on NS-830-EQU, already approved in February 2026. So I couldn't check your figures against the system of record.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, a Standard-tier account owned by Kelly Floyd. Its legal name is Blue River Logistics LLC.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, in force 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) does allow up to 15% on NS-500, but its parties are Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, not BlueRiver. BlueRiver's only agreement is its Support Terms (SUP-BRL-2025), which cover NS-Cloud support. They contain no discount terms and don't cover NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception.** Both NS-500 exceptions on record (Strategic Account and Volume Pricing) are for Acme. I found nothing covering BlueRiver.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The Approval Authority Matrix (APP-MATRIX-2026) applies it.
7. **Authority.** Torres is VP Sales (EMP-200). Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, so 12% is within his band. Discounts above 10% also need recorded approval evidence.
8. **Decision.** One customer's special terms don't carry over to another. A contract that allows a discount isn't enough to approve it, and having the authority doesn't fix missing eligibility. The policy also says contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed. This needs commercial review. If BlueRiver gets its own terms, or the request is resubmitted on standard pricing, Torres's VP Sales band would cover 12%.
