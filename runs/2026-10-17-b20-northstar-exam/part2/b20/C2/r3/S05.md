## Answer

**No, it can't be approved yet.** DR-9001 rests on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," and I can't find that agreement, or any Acme pricing term, anywhere in the knowledge base. The outcome is **REQUEST_EVIDENCE**: get the agreement before deciding.

**What checks out:**
- **Request:** The CRM record matches what you sent: 15% off NS-500, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval.
- **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing, which is ERP customer C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings"), DUNS 04-812-7730. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044), which is a different company.
- **Product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy was created on 2026-09-15 but only covers requests dated from 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply here.
- **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and they can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over her limit.
- **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres (her manager, EMP-200). Discounts above 10% also need recorded approval evidence.

**Why it stops at eligibility:**
- **No contract found.** There is no Master Supply Agreement in the knowledge base. The only Acme agreements I found are:
  - Schedule C (support and service levels, ref. ACME-MFG-2025). It covers NS-500 support but sets no pricing.
  - A parent company guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026), which is a credit document.
- **No pricing exception** for NS-500 on the request date.
- **The 15% claims are second-hand.** The account strategy says Acme "negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing in the 2025 agreement" and "I believe it's 15%." Sarah's email says "their agreement covers it" and cites 15% given "last September." None of these are the contract itself.
- **Under the SOP,** a contract that permits a discount can't justify approval by itself, and an unlocated term means asking for the document first.

**Once the agreement is produced,** it needs to be shown to belong to Acme Manufacturing, cover NS-500, be active on 2026-09-23, and allow at least 15%. If it does, the likely outcome is APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION by Michael Torres, since 15% is over Sarah's limit either way. If it caps below 15% or doesn't cover NS-500, the outcome would be REJECT_OR_ESCALATE or REVIEW_REQUIRED.
