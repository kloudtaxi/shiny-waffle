## Answer

No, it can't be approved as submitted. The request needs **commercial review**: the contract it relies on belongs to a different customer.

**1. The request isn't in the CRM.** DR-9105 doesn't appear in Northstar CRM. The CRM does hold **DR-9001**, which has exactly the same figures: NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", Pending Approval. But DR-9001 is for account **CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing)**, not CRM-2091. I decided on DR-9105 as you gave it and did not use DR-9001 in its place. Someone needs to confirm which record is real and which account it belongs to.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. (ERP C-1044, Akron OH). A source document states plainly that "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company" from Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings.

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The contract.** The only "Acme Master Supply Agreement" is ACME-MFG-2025. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and allows up to 15% on NS-500. However, it's between Northstar and **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, signed by Edward Floyd and David Morgan. Acme Industrial Supply isn't a party to it. I found no contract for Acme Industrial Supply.

**5. Pricing exceptions.** The two NS-500 exceptions on file (Strategic Account and Volume Pricing) are for Acme Manufacturing. None covers Acme Industrial Supply.

**6–8. Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. A discount above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales approval, which is Michael Torres. Because there's no eligibility to approve, this authority question doesn't come into play yet.

**9. Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The request relies on special contract terms, but those terms cover a different customer, and they can't be transferred to another one. Approval from someone more senior wouldn't change that. Acme Industrial Supply would need its own terms through commercial review, or else the request should be checked against the right account. Also, Acme Industrial's account owner in the CRM is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen.
