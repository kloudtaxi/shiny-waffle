## Answer

**Answer: yes, with conditions.** Acme is eligible for a $1,000,000 limit under the parent guarantee. But Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Elena Novak (Director of Finance) must approve it and Michael Torres (VP Sales) must concur. First, though, someone has to confirm that CR-9203 exists: I couldn't find it in the knowledge base's copy of the Northstar ERP.

**1. The request isn't in the ERP.** The only pending Acme request on file is **CR-9201**: same customer (C-1001), requestor and date (2026-09-23), but asking for $250,000 → **$400,000**, justified by the "third-plant ramp-up", not the guarantee. I haven't decided on CR-9201. I've decided CR-9203 as you gave it, and confirming it exists is a blocking condition.

**2. Customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. CRM puts it in the Strategic tier.

**3. Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 (CREDIT-POLICY-2026) covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, so it applies.

**4. Payment history: clean.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request was paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date.
- INV-60412: $85,000, due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011: $480,000, due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305: $720,000, due 2026-10-02, so not yet due on the request date. It is still unpaid on the record today.

**5. Maximum limit: $1,150,000, so $1,000,000 fits.**
- The Strategic maximum is $750,000.
- A parent guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, for the customer it names.
- Guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 is given by Acme Group Holdings, Inc. and names its subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings (this customer).
- It runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is capped at $400,000.

**6. Requestor's authority: none.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives that role no approval authority.

**7. Who must sign off on the $1,000,000 total:**
- **Approval:** limits above $500,000 need the Director of Finance, Elena Novak.
- **Concurrence:** Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. He is Sarah Chen's manager. I found no policy rule that stops him from concurring.
