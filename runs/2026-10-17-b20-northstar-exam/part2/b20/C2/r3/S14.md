## Answer

**Yes, Sarah Chen can approve the 8% herself under the policy in force, but nothing should be acted on until someone confirms DR-9106 is a real CRM record. I couldn't find it.**

1. **The request.** I couldn't find DR-9106 anywhere in the knowledge base, which covers all 38 documents. The only Acme discount request on record is DR-9001: 15% on NS-500, also dated 2026-09-23. That's a different request, so I decided on DR-9106 as you gave it. Its justification is "Competitive", which doesn't rely on any contract term or exception, so the basis is standard pricing.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both have DUNS 04-812-7730, and Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product.** SKU NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003). Its list price is $85,000, which matches the submitted list value. 8% of $85,000 is $6,800, leaving $78,200, so the submitted figures check out.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-EDGE at list price "less any discount agreed in an Order" and doesn't set a discount maximum for NS-EDGE. The request doesn't rely on it anyway.
5. **Pricing exception.** Both Acme exceptions cover NS-500 only, not NS-EDGE. That doesn't matter here because the request doesn't rely on special terms.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy cuts the account executive limit to 5%, but it only applies to requests dated on or after 2027-01-01.
7. **Authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy, that role can approve discounts up to and including 10%. 8% is within that, and no recorded approval evidence is needed because that rule starts above 10%.
8. **Approver.** None beyond her. Above 10% up to 20% would need VP Sales, and above 20% the Chief Revenue Officer.
