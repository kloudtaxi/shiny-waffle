## Answer

No. CR-9207 can't be approved. Acme Industrial Supply is a Standard-tier customer, so its maximum is $250,000. The "Acme parent guarantee" doesn't cover this company, so nothing lifts the maximum to the $500,000 requested. A more senior approver can't fix that.

1. **The request:** CR-9207 isn't in the knowledge base. The credit-request log only has CR-9201, Sarah's request for a different customer (C-1001, $250,000 to $400,000, same date). So the request details, including the $50,000 current limit, come only from what was submitted. I decided it as given, with confirming it as a blocking condition. It relies on a guarantee.
2. **The customer:** ERP C-1044 is Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091, Akron OH, DUNS 09-552-1187), a Standard-tier account. The records say it is "a different company" from Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001, DUNS 04-812-7730).
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history:** no problem. The customer has two invoices, INV-30001 and INV-30003, both paid. Both fell due before 2025-09-23, so they're outside the 12-month window the policy checks.
5. **The maximum limit:** $250,000 for Standard accounts. A parent guarantee in force raises the maximum by the guaranteed amount, but only for the customer the guarantee names.
   - The only Acme parent guarantee on file is GRT-ACME-2026. Acme Group Holdings, Inc. gives it for "its wholly owned subsidiary Acme Mfg. Holdings", runs 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, and is capped at $400,000.
   - It doesn't cover Acme Industrial Supply. The guarantee Sarah cited was found, and it covers a different company, so the outcome is a rejection, not a request for evidence.
   - $500,000 is above the $250,000 maximum.
   - The knowledge base matched the $250,000 policy wording only loosely, so the figure should be checked against the policy text. It isn't blocking, because $500,000 is over any figure this tier is likely to have.
6. **Sarah's authority:** none. She's an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives approval authority only to Finance roles.
7. **Required approver:** for a $500,000 limit, the policy requires the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. That doesn't matter here, because the customer isn't eligible for this limit.
8. **Decision:** REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. A request at or below $250,000 based on the customer's own standing, or a guarantee that actually names Acme Industrial Supply, would need a new request.
