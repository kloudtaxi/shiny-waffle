Its support terms (SUP-CHS-2025) put it on the Silver plan. They cover NS-EDGE and coverage started on 2025-12-01. The ticket was opened through the portal at 2026-07-28 10:30 Eastern, which is 09:30 Central (CT), so it is in scope.

**True severity: 2.** The chiller alarms at the Central energy plant stopped forwarding, and Cedar Health's engineers covered it with "hourly manual rounds." The severity guide puts this under Severity 2(b): an alarming function is unavailable, "whether or not the customer covers it with manual checks." It is not Severity 1, because operations continued with a workaround.

**Targets.** Schedule v2.0 applies, because the ticket was opened after 2026-04-01 CT. For Silver, Severity 2 it gives 2 business hours for initial response and 12 business hours for restoration. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 CT on weekdays. The ticket shows no pauses and no maintenance window.

**Measurement (all times CT):**
- **Response: met.** An engineer commented on the ticket at 11:05, 1 h 35 min after opening.
- **Restoration: missed, band 2.** Restoration was recorded at 07-31 00:50 Eastern, which is 07-30 23:50 CT. Counting business hours: Tuesday 8.5 h, Wednesday 10 h and Thursday 10 h make 28.5 h. That is more than twice the target (24 h). The 12-hour target ran out on Wednesday 07-29 at 11:30.

**Credit: $0.** Band 2 for Severity 2 earns 10% of the $4,000 monthly fee, which would be $400. The claim window is 30 calendar days after the restoration date, counted in CT. That date is 07-30, so the last day was 08-29. Cedar Health's email claim arrived 08-30 at 08:12 CT, on day 31. A late claim earns no credit. Counted from the Eastern date (07-31) the claim would look on time, but the schedule counts in CT. I didn't find the monthly cap, but it doesn't matter when no credit is owed.

- Claim in writing within the window: not met, because the claim was one day late.
