## Answer

**No, CR-9204 can't be approved.** Even with the parent guarantee, Acme's maximum limit is $1,150,000, and the request is for $1,300,000. A separate problem: CR-9204 doesn't appear in Northstar ERP's credit request records. The only pending Acme request there is CR-9201 (from $250,000 to $400,000, same date, also from Sarah Chen). I've decided on CR-9204 as you gave it, not on CR-9201.

1. **The request.** You gave: C-1001, $250,000 to $1,300,000, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen, relying on the Acme Group parent guarantee. The ERP credit table does confirm the current limit of $250,000 for C-1001.
2. **The customer.** ERP's "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001) and CRM's "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) are the same company. Both records give the same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address. CRM records the account as **Strategic**.
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).
4. **Payment history** (the 2026 rule allows no invoice paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date):
   - INV-60412 was paid 4 days early (paid 2025-11-24, due 2025-11-28).
   - INV-62011 was paid 3 days early (paid 2026-07-22, due 2026-07-25).
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
   - So nothing was late; payment history is fine.
5. **Maximum limit.** The policy caps Strategic accounts at $750,000.
   - The guarantee is GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It names Acme Mfg. Holdings (doing business as Acme Manufacturing) and runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31, so it covers this customer on the request date.
   - It raises the maximum by $400,000, to **$1,150,000**.
   - $1,300,000 is $150,000 over that.
6. **Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, a role with no approval authority under the policy.
7. **Approvers, if the request were within the maximum.**
   - A limit over $500,000 needs Director of Finance approval: Elena Novak.
   - Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also needs VP Sales concurrence: Michael Torres.
   - Sarah can't approve her own request, but she isn't in an approving role anyway.
   - These approvers can't fix the maximum problem.

Sarah's 2026-09-23 email to Priya Shah only says the guarantee "should cover" future headroom. The guarantee itself caps cover at $400,000.
