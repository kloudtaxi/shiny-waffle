## Answer

Yes, but Sarah can't approve it herself. Acme is eligible for the 15% discount on the NS-500, but 15% is more than Sarah's own approval limit, so the VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it.

**What I checked**
- **The request:** The CRM record (discount_requests.csv) matches what was submitted except for one field. CRM shows DR-9001's status as **"Pending Approval"**, with no approver and no approval date. The submission says "Approved", but no approval is on record. I decided on the CRM value.
- **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same address, same DUNS number 04‑812‑7730).
- **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. The submitted figures check out: 15% is $37,500 off, leaving $212,500.
- **The contract:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date, 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 lets Acme get up to 15% off the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
- **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers the NS-500 only, up to 15% off list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. At 15%, the request is exactly at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.
- **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and its Approval Authority Matrix. Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Over 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales. Over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
- **Sarah's authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. She can't approve 15%, even though the contract allows it.
- **The approver:** The 15% band belongs to the VP Sales, Michael Torres (Sarah's manager).

Sarah approved the similar request last September herself (DR-8104, 2025-09-16), but that doesn't carry over. That was under the 2025 policy, which let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered that limit to 10%.
