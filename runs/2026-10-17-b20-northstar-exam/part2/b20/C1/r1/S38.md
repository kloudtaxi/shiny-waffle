**3. True severity: 1.** The ticket says NS-Cloud is down for all users, dispatch can't see shipments, and there is no workaround. That meets Severity 1 under both tests in the severity guide: dispatch operations stopped, and NS-Cloud is unavailable to all users. The customer's reported P1 doesn't set the severity; it just happens to agree.

**4. Schedule and targets.** The ticket opened 2026-07-11 23:20 CDT, so Schedule v2.0 applies. The Severity 1 targets are a 30-minute response and a 4-hour restoration on a 24×7 clock.

**5. Clock start and exclusions.**

- The notice needed at least 5 business days. Counting from 06-29 to 07-11, and leaving out the 07-03 holiday and weekends, it had 9. So the window qualifies.
- Because the ticket opened inside the window, both clocks start when the window ends: **2026-07-12 02:00 CDT**.
- The ticket records no pauses. The fact that the upgrade caused the outage doesn't matter; only time after the window counts.

**6. Measurement.**

- **Restoration: met.** Restored at 05:05, which is 3 h 05 min after clock start, against a 4 h target. The customer's "5 h 45 min" counts from 23:20 and wrongly includes the maintenance window.
