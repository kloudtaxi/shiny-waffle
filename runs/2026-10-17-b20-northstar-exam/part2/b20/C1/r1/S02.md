## Answer

**Not at 18%. Acme's terms allow at most 15% on the NS-500. The Northstar CRM record for DR-9001 actually shows 15%, and at 15% it can go ahead once Michael Torres (VP Sales) approves.**

**The figures don't match.** The request you sent says 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). The CRM record (discount_requests.csv) says DR-9001 is 15.0% of $250,000 list: $37,500 off, $212,500 net. The customer, product, date, requestor and status are the same in both. Under our procedure I decided on the CRM's 15%. Before anything is quoted, someone needs to confirm which figure is right.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 for Acme Manufacturing, NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, raised by Sarah Chen and pending approval. It relies on the contract: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both have DUNS 04-812-7730, and the agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers the NS-500, so it was active on the request date.
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers the NS-500 only, at up to 15% off list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It states that it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." I found no amendment allowing more than 15%.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated in 2026. The 2027 policy only starts on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and under the 2026 policy she can approve up to 10%. At 15% she needs approval. Under the 2025 policy she could approve up to 15% herself, which is how a similar Acme request (DR-8104, September 2025) was self-approved. That no longer applies.
8. **Who must approve:** the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix gives discounts above 10% and up to 20% to the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
9. **Decision:** approve at 15% with Michael Torres's approval. At 18%, the request would be over Acme's 15% maximum and the answer would be reject or escalate. A more senior approver can't fix that; it would need new or amended contract terms.
