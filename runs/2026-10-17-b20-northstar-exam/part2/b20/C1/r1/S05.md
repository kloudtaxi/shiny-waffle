## Answer

No, not yet. DR-9001 can't be approved or rejected until someone produces the Acme Master Supply Agreement. The request depends on that agreement, and it isn't in the knowledge base.

1. **The request:** The CRM record matches what you gave me: DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing" in CRM and "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001) in ERP. Both share DUNS 04-812-7730, so they are the same customer.
3. **The product:** NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** Schedule C (Support and Service Levels) points to a parent agreement, ACME-MFG-2025. But Schedule C only covers support, and the agreement itself isn't in the knowledge base. I can't confirm its terms or that it was active on 2026-09-23.
5. **Pricing exception:** I found no document setting out an NS-500 discount for Acme. What exists is hearsay:
   - The 2026 account strategy says "I believe it's 15%… Worth confirming with Deal Desk."
   - Sarah's email to Michael Torres says "I think their agreement covers it."
   - The 2025 request DR-8104 got 15%, but Sarah approved it herself under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. That says nothing about the contract.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy starts on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply.
7. **Sarah's authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit. Having contract terms wouldn't change that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres, per the org chart dated 2026-09-01.

**Decision: REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The procedure says that when a request relies on a contract term that can't be found, you ask for the document before deciding. Once the agreement shows Acme is entitled to 15% on NS-500 on that date, this becomes approval by Michael Torres. If it caps the discount below 15% or doesn't cover NS-500, the outcome changes.
