## Answer

I've checked the CRM log, the Acme agreement and pricing exceptions, and the pricing policies, so here is the decision.

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself, and it shouldn't go ahead as written.** The request depends on a contract term that doesn't cover this product, so it needs a commercial review first. There's also a record problem: DR-9109 isn't in the CRM discount request log.

**1. The request isn't in the system of record.** The CRM discount request log (discount_requests.csv) has no DR-9109. The only pending Acme request is DR-9001, also dated 2026-09-23 by Sarah Chen, but it's for the NS-500 at 15%. I haven't treated DR-9001 as the same request. This decision covers DR-9109 as you gave it, on condition that someone confirms it exists in CRM.

**2. Customer and product.** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing. NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000, which matches the request.

**3. The contract is active but doesn't give a discount on this product.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It does list NS-EDGE as a product Northstar supplies. However, its discount clause (§3.2) offers up to 15% only on the NS-500, through Schedule B. For any other product, prices are list price less whatever discount an order agrees (§3.1).

**4. No pricing exception covers NS-EDGE.**
- EXC-ACME-NS500-15 applies to the NS-500 only and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product."
- EXC-ACME-NS500-10, the earlier one, was also NS-500 only, ran until 2025-03-31 and has been replaced.
- The request's stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", but no contract discount exists for NS-EDGE. Exceptions don't carry over to other products.

**5. Approval authority.** The Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives like Sarah Chen can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. So 12% is beyond Sarah's authority whatever the basis. The Pricing Policy 2027 was published on 2026-09-15 but only takes effect on 2027-01-01, so it doesn't apply here.

**Outcome: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** Commercial review needs to decide whether Acme gets a discount on NS-EDGE, by new or amended terms or by treating this as a standard request. Even then, a 12% discount would still need Michael Torres's approval under the 2026 policy.
