## Answer

**Answer: Yes, but not by Michael Torres himself.** On 2025-09-23 BlueRiver qualified for a $200,000 limit. The approval had to come from the Finance Manager, Priya Shah. One problem comes first: CR-9206 isn't in Northstar ERP's credit-request records, so someone needs to confirm the request exists before acting on this.

**1. The request.** I couldn't find CR-9206 in the ERP credit-request file, which lists CR-9201 and CR-7001 to CR-7010 only. I decided on the request as you gave it. It relies on BlueRiver's own standing. No guarantee is cited.

**2. The customer.** ERP's C-1002 "Blue River Logistics LLC" and CRM's CRM-2051 "BlueRiver Logistics" are the same company. They share DUNS 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. The ERP limit is $100,000, which matches the request.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2025 was in force from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** Under the policy, no invoice due in the previous 12 months may be paid, or still unpaid, more than 45 days after its due date. Three invoices fell due in that window:
- INV-61005 was due 2025-03-01 and paid 2025-02-26.
- INV-61210 was due 2025-05-30 and paid 2025-05-28.
- INV-61433 was due 2025-08-29 and paid 2025-09-02, which is 4 days late and within the 45-day allowance.

None were late under the rule.

**5. The maximum limit.** CRM lists BlueRiver as a Standard account, so its maximum is $300,000. $200,000 is within it, so BlueRiver is eligible.

**6. The requestor's authority.** Michael Torres is VP Sales. The credit policy gives that role no approval authority, and the policy also bars anyone from approving a request they submitted.

**7. Who must approve.** Limits above $50,000 and up to $750,000 need Finance Manager approval. That is Priya Shah, who reports to Elena Novak, the Director of Finance. The 2025 policy requires no concurrence.
