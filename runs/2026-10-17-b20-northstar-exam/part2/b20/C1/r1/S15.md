## Answer

No, not as submitted. Michael Torres has the authority to approve a 12% discount, but the request rests on a contract that belongs to a different customer. It needs commercial review before anyone approves it. There's also a second problem: DR-9107 isn't in the CRM records I can access.

1. **The request:** DR-9107, for account CRM-2051, NS-500, 12% ($30,000 off $250,000 list), raised by Michael Torres on 2026-09-23. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing. **DR-9107 doesn't appear in the CRM discount requests file (discount_requests.csv).** The last record there is DR-9001, which is Sarah Chen's request for Acme (CRM-2048) at 15% on the same date. I'm deciding on DR-9107 as you gave it and not substituting DR-9001.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics, an account on the Standard tier. Its contract name is Blue River Logistics LLC, St. Louis.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **BlueRiver's contracts:** The only one on file is its support agreement (SUP-BRL-2025): a Silver support plan for NS-CLOUD at $2,500 a month. It has no pricing terms and doesn't cover NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers BlueRiver. The Acme agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allow up to 15% on NS-500, but only for Acme Manufacturing. Special terms don't carry over to another customer.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies, along with the 2026 approval matrix.
7. **Authority:** Michael Torres is VP Sales, which covers discounts above 10% and up to 20%. So 12% is within his own authority. That doesn't fix the missing eligibility, though. The SOP says having authority doesn't make a customer eligible for contract pricing.
8. **Required approver:** For 12%, that's the VP Sales role, which is Michael Torres himself. He would be both requesting and approving. The SOP doesn't forbid that, but it's worth noting.

**Decision: commercial review required.** BlueRiver has no contract term or pricing exception for NS-500, and Acme's terms can't be used for it. Two ways forward: resubmit the request on standard pricing, or put BlueRiver-specific terms in place. If it's resubmitted on standard pricing, 12% would be within Torres's authority under the 2026 policy.
