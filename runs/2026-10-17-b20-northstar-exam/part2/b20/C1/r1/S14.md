## Answer

Yes. Sarah Chen can approve 8% on her own authority, but DR-9106 has to be confirmed in the system of record before anyone acts on it.

**The problem with the request ID:** DR-9106 isn't in the system of record. The discount requests export (discount_requests.csv) has no such ID. The only Acme request dated 2026-09-23 is DR-9001, which is a different request: 15% on the NS-500 controller, citing the Master Supply Agreement. I decided on DR-9106 as you gave it, not on DR-9001, and added a blocking condition to confirm it exists.

1. **The request:** DR-9106 asks for 8% on NS-EDGE for CRM-2048. That is $6,800 off a list value of $85,000, for $78,200 net. It is dated 2026-09-23 and was requested by Sarah Chen. The justification is "Competitive," so it relies on standard pricing, not on any contract term or exception.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730).
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package. The list value matches other NS-EDGE requests ($85,000).
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date, and it covers NS-EDGE. Its special pricing (section 3.2) applies only to the NS-500.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers NS-EDGE. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is for the NS-500 only. That doesn't matter here, because the request doesn't rely on special terms.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in effect from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2027 policy, which cuts the account executive limit to 5%, doesn't start until 2027-01-01.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (employee list and org chart). Under the 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix she can approve up to and including 10%. 8% is within that.
8. **Approver:** The band for ≤10% is Enterprise Account Executive, so no VP Sales or CRO sign-off is needed.

**Decision: APPROVE**, once DR-9106 is confirmed in the system of record.
