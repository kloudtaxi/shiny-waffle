## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. The CRM record for DR-9001 also doesn't match what you sent: it isn't for the NS-Cloud Operations Suite.

**The product doesn't match.** In Northstar CRM (`discount_requests.csv`), DR-9001 is for **NS-500** (the NS-500 Industrial Controller), not NS-CLOUD:
`DR-9001,CRM-2048,NS-500,15.0,250000,37500,212500,sarah.chen@northstar.example,2026-09-23,Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement,Pending Approval`
The dollar figures differ too. CRM has a list value of $250,000, a discount of $37,500 and a net of $212,500. Your version has $120,000, $18,000 and $102,000. Sarah's email of 2026-09-23 ("Acme NS-500 — DR-9001") also says Acme wants "the NS-500 controller for the third plant." So I've decided on the CRM values, and confirming the product is a blocking condition.

**How the decision was reached (on the CRM record)**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% on NS-500, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen (Enterprise Account Executive). It relies on Acme's contract pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address match.
3. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers **NS-500 only**, up to 15% off list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. 15% is within that limit, so Acme is eligible. The exception says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." MSA §3.3 says the same.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales.
6. **Approver:** 15% is above Sarah's 10% limit, so **Michael Torres (VP Sales)** must approve. Last September's 15% approval (DR-8104) fell under the 2025 policy, when Account Executives could approve up to 15%. That limit no longer applies.

**If the request really is for NS-CLOUD,** the answer changes to commercial review (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`). The MSA includes NS-CLOUD as a product it supplies, but no pricing exception covers it. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product," and the MSA only grants the 15% for NS-500. So the request would need new commercial terms, not just a more senior approver.
