## Answer

**DR-9001 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme is entitled to the 15%, but under the 2026 policy a 15% discount needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. One thing must be fixed first: the request you submitted is dated 2025-09-23, but the CRM record is dated 2026-09-23. I decided on the CRM date, and the date decides who can approve.

**1. The request.** The CRM record DR-9001 is for account CRM-2048, product NS-500, a 15.0% discount ($250,000 list, $37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen raised it, it is Pending Approval, and the justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". So it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing. Every other field matches what you submitted; only the date differs. Her email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also confirms the 2026 date.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". In the ERP system it is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), with the same DUNS number and address. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a different company.

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 says Acme is eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."

**5. The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers the NS-500 only, with a 15% maximum, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% requested equals the maximum, so Acme is eligible. This exception replaced the older 10% one (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It also states it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."

**6. The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit.

**8. Who must approve.** The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix puts discounts over 10% and up to 20% with the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in the CRM.

**About the date.** Under the 2025 policy, an Enterprise Account Executive could approve up to 15% herself. That is how last September's identical request, DR-8104, was self-approved. Precedent doesn't carry over: authority belongs to the role under whichever policy is in force on the request date. The submitted date needs correcting before anyone acts on this.
