There's also a problem with the amount: the submitted request asks for $300,000, but the credit request record (credit_requests.csv) and Sarah's 2026-09-23 email to Priya both say **$400,000**. It relies on Acme's own standing. Sarah's email mentions Acme Group's parent guarantee only as cover for "more headroom later", so the request doesn't depend on it.
2. **The customer:** "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (ERP C-1001), "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091) is a different company.
3. **The policy:** Northstar Credit Policy 2026, which covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
4. **Payment history:** The rule is that nothing due in the prior 12 months can have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes:
   - INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
   - INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
   - INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, after the request date.
5. **The maximum limit:** The CRM records Acme as a Strategic account, so the maximum is $750,000. $400,000 is within it, and so is $300,000. The guarantee isn't needed. For reference, it does name Acme Mfg. Holdings, it's for $400,000, and it runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31.
6. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she has no credit approval authority. The policy also bars anyone from approving their own request.
7. **The required approver:** Limits over $100,000 and up to $500,000 need Finance Manager approval, and that's Priya Shah. The VP Sales concurrence only applies to Strategic accounts going above $500,000, so it isn't needed. Either amount falls in the same band, so the outcome is the same.
