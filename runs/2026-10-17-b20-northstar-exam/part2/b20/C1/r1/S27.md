## Answer

Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme qualifies for $650,000 on its own standing, but the limit needs the Director of Finance to approve it and the VP Sales to concur. There's also a bigger problem: CR-9202 isn't in the system of record, so nothing can be acted on until someone confirms the request exists.

**1. The request.** The only Acme (C-1001) credit request in `credit_requests.csv` is **CR-9201**. It was also raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23 with the same justification, but it asks for **$400,000**, not $650,000. There is no CR-9202. I haven't used CR-9201 in its place; I've decided CR-9202 as you gave it, with confirming it as a blocking condition. The request doesn't mention a guarantee, so I've treated it as relying on Acme's own standing. Sarah's email about CR-9201 does say Acme Group's parent guarantee "should cover" more headroom later.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings", CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. CRM lists it as a **Strategic** account. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091, Standard tier, Akron), which is a different customer.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies, since it covers requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may have been paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days past its due date. Acme passes:
- INV-60412: due 2025-11-28, paid 2025-11-24 (on time).
- INV-62011: due 2026-07-25, paid 2026-07-22 (on time).
- INV-62305 ($720,000): due 2026-10-02, so it wasn't due yet on the request date.

**5. The maximum limit.** The 2026 maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000, so $650,000 is within it without any guarantee. For reference, guarantee GRT-ACME-2026 from Acme Group Holdings names Acme Mfg. Holdings, covers $400,000 and runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. It would raise the maximum to $1,150,000, but this request doesn't need it.

**6. The requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, which is not a credit-approval role. Separately, the separation-of-duties rule means she can't approve her own request.

**7. Required approvers.**
- **Approval:** $650,000 is above $500,000, so the Director of Finance must approve. That is **Elena Novak**.
- **Concurrence:** Strategic accounts above $500,000 also need the VP Sales to concur. That is **Michael Torres**, Sarah's manager. He isn't the requestor, so he can concur.

**Worth noting:**
- As of today (2026-10-18), INV-62305 ($720,000) is still unpaid, 16 days past due. That doesn't change eligibility as of the request date. But if it's still unpaid on 2026-11-01, it will be more than 30 days late and would fail the payment rule for later requests.
- That one invoice is already larger than the $650,000 limit being requested.
