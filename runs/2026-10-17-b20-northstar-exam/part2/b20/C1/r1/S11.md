## Answer

I've checked the policy, contract and CRM records. Here's the answer.

**No. Sarah Chen could not have approved this herself.** Under the policy in force on that date, 22% needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Also, I couldn't find DR-9103 in the CRM's discount request records, so its existence needs confirming first.

1. **The request:** DR-9103 asks for 22% off NS-EDGE for account CRM-2048, dated 2025-09-23, with the reason "Competitive." That means it's based on standard pricing, not a contract term. The figures add up: 22% of $85,000 is $18,700, leaving $66,300. But the CRM discount request records (`discount_requests.csv`) don't list DR-9103. They hold Acme requests DR-8104 (NS-500, 15%, 2025-09-16) and DR-9001 (NS-500, 15%, 2026-09-23), neither of which is this request. I decided on DR-9103 as you gave it.
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (same DUNS number, 04-812-7730). Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000, which matches the request.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers NS-EDGE. Its only discount term (§3.2, up to 15%) applies to NS-500. §3.3 says the customer's contract eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers NS-500 only, so nothing covers NS-EDGE. That doesn't matter here because the request is on standard pricing.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in effect 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix doesn't apply to this date.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, who can approve up to and including 15%. 22% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Under the 2025 policy, anything over 15% needs VP Sales approval, which is Michael Torres. For comparison, DR-7012 (22% on NS-EDGE, Nov 2025) was approved by him.
