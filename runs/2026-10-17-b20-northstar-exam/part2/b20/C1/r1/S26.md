## Answer

**Yes, CR-9201 can be approved, but not by Sarah Chen.** Acme qualifies for $400,000 on its own record. That limit needs approval from Priya Shah, the Finance Manager. No other sign-off is required.

**1. The request.** The ERP record matches what you submitted: CR-9201, customer C-1001, $250,000 to $400,000, raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It rests on Acme's own standing. Sarah's email to Priya mentions the Acme Group parent guarantee only as cover "if they need more headroom later", so this request doesn't depend on it.

**2. The customer.** The ERP's "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), the CRM's "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048, Strategic tier) and the guarantee's "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing" are the same company. They share the DUNS number 04-812-7730 and the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091), which is a separate company.

**3. The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies. It covers requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history.** Acme had two invoices due in the 12 months before the request, and both were paid early:
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.

A third invoice, INV-62305 for $720,000, falls due on 2026-10-02, after the request date, so it doesn't count against the payment rule. There are no late invoices.

**5. The maximum limit.** The policy allows up to $750,000 for Strategic accounts, so $400,000 is well within it. The guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, $400,000, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) names this company, but it isn't needed for this request.

**6. Sarah's authority.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. That role isn't in any of the policy's approval bands, so she has no authority to approve credit limits.

**7. Who must approve.** A limit above $100,000 and up to $500,000 needs Finance Manager approval, and Priya Shah holds that role. VP Sales concurrence is only required for Strategic accounts above $500,000, so it doesn't apply. Priya didn't submit the request, so the rule against approving your own request isn't triggered.
