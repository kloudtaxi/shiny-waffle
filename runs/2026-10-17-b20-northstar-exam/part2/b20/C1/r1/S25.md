## Answer

**Yes, but not by Sarah Chen alone.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount under its contract, but 15% is more than an Account Executive can approve under the 2026 policy. Michael Torres, VP Sales, must approve it.

1. **The request:** The CRM record for DR-9001 matches what was submitted. Account CRM-2048, product NS-500, 15.0% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, status "Pending Approval". It relies on the contract: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The address and DUNS number (04-812-7730) match. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. That matches the request.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-500. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15%, effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% request is at the cap, so Acme is eligible. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, issued 2026-09-15, only applies to requests dated 2027 or later.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, who can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **The required approver:** Under the Approval Authority Matrix, discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales: Michael Torres. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in CRM.

Sarah approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme herself last year (DR-8104, 2025-09-16, under the 2025 policy). That doesn't matter here: the 2026 policy governs this request.
