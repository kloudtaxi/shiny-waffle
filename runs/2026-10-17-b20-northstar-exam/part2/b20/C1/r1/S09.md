## Answer

No. On 31 March 2025, Sarah Chen could not have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme. She had the authority, but Acme wasn't entitled to that much. Before relying on any of this, note that **DR-9101 isn't in Northstar CRM**. I decided the request as you gave it, without swapping in a similar record.

**Main reason: the agreement the request cites wasn't in force yet.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." That agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028. Its 15% NS-500 terms (§3.2, Schedule B, EXC-ACME-NS500-15) also start on 1 April 2025. On 31 March 2025 neither applied.

**What did apply that day:** the earlier NS-500 pricing exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023. It caps the discount at **10%** for orders from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2025, so 31 March was its last day. 15% is above that cap. Higher-level sign-off can't fix this; it needs new or amended commercial terms.

**Her authority wasn't the problem.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. The 2025 Pricing Policy (in force 1 January to 31 December 2025) lets that role approve up to and including 15%, so 15% was within her limit. The SOP is clear, though, that authority doesn't create eligibility, and a discount can't be approved on contract terms alone.

**Similar records that do exist in CRM:**
- **DR-9001**: identical figures, dated 23 September 2026, status Pending Approval.
- **DR-8104**: also identical, dated 16 September 2025, approved by Sarah Chen.

They may be what you mean, but I didn't decide either one in place of DR-9101.
