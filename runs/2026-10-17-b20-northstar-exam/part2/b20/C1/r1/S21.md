## Answer

Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to a 15% discount on the NS-500, but under the 2027 pricing policy an Account Executive can only approve up to 5%. The CRO, David Morgan, has to approve it. Before anyone acts on it, request DR-9113 also has to be confirmed in CRM, because I couldn't find it there.

**1. The request.** DR-9113 isn't in Northstar CRM. The discount requests table has no row with that id, and looking up the id finds nothing. Every value you submitted matches a different record, **DR-9001**: Acme, NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list, sent by Sarah on 2026-09-23 and still Pending Approval. Only the id and date differ. I haven't decided on DR-9001. This decision is for DR-9113 as you gave it, dated 2027-02-01, which is in the future (today is 2026-10-18). The request says it relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the basis is contract terms.

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) and ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings) are the same company: same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and same address. The Master Supply Agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing". Sarah owns the account.

**3. The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.

**4. Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it's in force on 2027-02-01. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and caps the discount at 15%. 15% is within that cap, so Acme is eligible. The exception also says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The 2023 exception has been replaced by this one.

**6. Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2027 covers requests dated from 2027-01-01 and fully replaces the 2026 policy. The 2026 approval matrix, which let Account Executives approve up to 10%, doesn't apply.

**7. Sarah's authority.** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, so under the 2027 policy she can approve up to 5%. 15% is over her limit.

**8. Required approver.** Anything over 12% needs the Chief Revenue Officer, which is David Morgan according to the employee list and the org chart. The 2027 policy also requires recorded approval evidence for any discount over 5%.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Before it can be acted on:

- **Not blocking:** the org chart only goes up to 2026-09-01, so check that David Morgan is still CRO on 2027-02-01.
