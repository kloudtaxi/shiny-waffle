## Answer

**Yes, Sarah Chen could have approved this herself. My decision is APPROVE, but nobody should act on it until DR-9110 is confirmed to exist in Northstar CRM.**

**Problem 1: the request isn't in the CRM records.** The CRM's discount request list has no DR-9110. The only Acme NS-500 requests it holds are DR-8104 (15%, 2025-09-16, approved) and DR-9001 (15%, 2026-09-23, pending). I haven't used either of those in place of this one. I decided on the request as you gave it and made confirming it a blocking condition.

**The checks:**
1. **Request:** 8% off NS-500, list $250,000, net $230,000. Dated 2025-03-31, raised by Sarah Chen. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". Both records share the same address and DUNS number (04-812-7730).
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Contract on 2025-03-31:** The agreement the request cites, ACME-MFG-2025, is the wrong one. It was signed on 2025-03-20 and 22 but only takes effect on **April 1, 2025**, one day after the request. What was in force that day was ACME-SA-2023, marked "Active".
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-10 (under ACME-SA-2023) allows up to **10%** off NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 to **2025-03-31**, so it covers this date and product. The 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) only starts on 2025-04-01 and doesn't apply. 8% is within 10%, so Acme was eligible.
6. **Policy in force:** the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). The 2026 approval matrix, which caps Account Executives at 10%, does not apply to this date.
7. **Sarah's authority:** she is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2025 policy, that role "may approve discounts up to and including 15%", so 8% is within her own limit.
8. **Approver:** no one else is needed. Above 15% would need the VP Sales.

The contract made Acme eligible for the discount; Sarah's authority to approve it comes from the 2025 policy. As both the agreement (§3.3) and the exception say, the contract doesn't grant approval authority.

**Problem 2: the justification cites the wrong document.** On that date, Acme's eligibility came from the 2023 exception, not the Master Supply Agreement. I've made correcting the recorded basis a non-blocking condition, because the discount is within both the 10% cap and Sarah's 15% limit either way.
