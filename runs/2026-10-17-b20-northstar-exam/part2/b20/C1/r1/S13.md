**2. The customer.** CRM-2091 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** (ERP C-1044, Akron, OH; account owner Zachary Brown). The documents say directly that it "is a different company" from Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings (CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, Milwaukee).

Special terms don't carry over to another company, so the cited contract pricing doesn't cover this customer.

**9. Decision.** The request depends on special terms that don't cover this customer, so it needs commercial review. A more senior approver can't fix that.
