## Answer

I recommend rejecting CR-9205 (REJECT_OR_ESCALATE). BlueRiver doesn't qualify for a higher limit because it paid an invoice too late within the last 12 months. Two other problems: CR-9205 isn't in Northstar ERP, and Michael Torres can't approve any credit limit himself.

**Is the request in the system?** ERP's credit request file (credit_requests.csv) has no CR-9205. The only pending request dated 2026-09-23 is CR-9201, which is for Acme (C-1001), so I haven't treated it as this one. I decided on the request as you gave it. ERP does confirm the other figures: C-1002 has a current limit of $100,000 (last reviewed 2026-01-15). The request relies on BlueRiver's own standing, not on a guarantee or other third-party support.

**The customer.** ERP customer C-1002, "Blue River Logistics LLC", and CRM account CRM-2051, "BlueRiver Logistics", are the same company. They share DUNS 07-331-9025 and the address 890 Harbor Way, St. Louis. CRM lists it as a **Standard** account.

**The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies, since it covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

**Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid, or left unpaid, more than 30 days after its due date.
- **INV-62147** (NS-Cloud subscription for 2026-Q2, $30,000) was due 2026-05-30 and paid 2026-07-21. That is **52 days late**, which breaks the rule.
- The other invoices due in that window (INV-61688, INV-30014, INV-61902, INV-62391) were paid on time or within 2 days. INV-30020 isn't due until 2026-10-02.

**Maximum limit.** The maximum for a Standard account is $250,000, and no guarantee covers BlueRiver. The $200,000 requested would be within the maximum, so the payment history alone is what blocks it.

**Who can approve.** Under the policy, only Finance roles approve credit limits. Michael Torres is VP Sales, so he has no approval authority. A $200,000 limit falls in the over-$100,000 to $500,000 band, which needs the Finance Manager: Priya Shah. No VP Sales concurrence is needed, because BlueRiver isn't Strategic and the limit isn't over $500,000. Even so, a higher approver can't override the payment-history problem.
