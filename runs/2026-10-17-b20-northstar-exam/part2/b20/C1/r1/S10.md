1. **The request:** DR-9102 asks for 25% off the NS-EDGE package for Acme, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. The justification is "Competitive", so it rests on standard pricing, not a contract term. The `discount_requests.csv` export has no DR-9102. Sarah's only open Acme request that day is DR-9001, which is 15% off NS-500 under contract pricing. That's a different request, so I haven't used it. I've decided on DR-9102 as you gave it, with a blocking condition to confirm it exists in the CRM.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing". In the ERP it's customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which trades as Acme Manufacturing. It is not Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), a separate account.
3. **The product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. The submitted figures check out: 25% is $21,250 off, leaving $63,750 net.
4. **Contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-EDGE as a supplied product but gives no NS-EDGE discount. Its 15% strategic pricing applies to NS-500 only.
5. **Pricing exception:** The only current exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, says "NS-500 only… does not apply to any other Northstar product". Nothing covers NS-EDGE. Since the request doesn't rely on special terms, this doesn't block it, but no contract support for the 25% should be assumed.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy, published 2026-09-15, only takes effect on 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, which carries authority up to 10%. 25% is beyond that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 20% need the Chief Revenue Officer, which is David Morgan according to the org chart and employee records. Michael Torres, VP Sales, can only approve discounts above 10% up to 20%, so his sign-off isn't enough.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** The discount can go ahead on standard pricing if David Morgan approves it and the approval is recorded in the CRM, as the policy requires for anything over 10%. Nothing may be acted on until the CRM confirms DR-9102 exists.
