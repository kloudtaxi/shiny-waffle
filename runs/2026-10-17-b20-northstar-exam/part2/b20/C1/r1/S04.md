# DR-9001: Acme Manufacturing, 15% off NS-500

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is entitled to the 15% discount under its agreement, but on the date the CRM records for this request, she can only approve up to 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.

**The date doesn't match the CRM.** You gave 2025-09-23, but Northstar CRM records DR-9001 as dated **2026-09-23**. Sarah's own email to Michael logging DR-9001 is also dated 2026-09-23. I've decided on the CRM date. This matters: if the date really were 2025-09-23, the 2025 policy would apply. That policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%, so Sarah could have approved it herself. You may be thinking of **DR-8104**: the same account, product and 15%, dated 2025-09-16, which Sarah approved herself under the 2025 policy. That's the "what we did for them last September" in her email. DR-8104 doesn't carry over to DR-9001.

## How I got there
1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off a list value of $250,000 ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen raised it and its status is "Pending Approval". It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the basis is a contract term or exception.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The two systems share the same DUNS number, 04-812-7730, and the agreement names "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing".
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 allows up to 15% on NS-500. Section 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, up to 15%, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ended 2025-03-31. **15% is at the maximum, so Acme is eligible.** The exception "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy was issued on 2026-09-15 but doesn't take effect until 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Required approver:** The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix puts discounts over 10% and up to 20% with VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
