## Answer

**No, it can't be approved as it stands. My answer is REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The $1,000,000 limit is above what Acme can have on its own record, and it depends on an Acme Group parent guarantee that I can't find anywhere in the knowledge base. There is a second problem: CR-9203 itself isn't in the ERP.

**1. The request isn't in the ERP.** The credit requests file (`credit_requests.csv`) has no CR-9203. The only Acme (C-1001) request is **CR-9201**: Sarah Chen, dated 2026-09-23, $250,000 → **$400,000**, pending approval, for the third-plant ramp-up, with no guarantee cited. Sarah's email to Priya Shah confirms CR-9201 was for $400,000. She mentions the guarantee only as possible "headroom later," adding "Gina says it's still in place." I have not treated CR-9201 as this request. I've assessed CR-9203 as you gave it, and confirming it exists in the ERP is a blocking condition.

**2. The customer.** ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" is the same company as CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (DUNS 04-812-7730). Its tier in the CRM is **Strategic**. The current limit in the ERP credit file is $250,000, which matches the request.

**3. Policy.** The Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31).

**4. Payment history: clean.** Late means paid, or still unpaid, more than 30 days after the due date, for invoices due in the 12 months before the request.
- INV-60412 was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) was due 2026-10-02. That is after the request date, so it doesn't count.

**5. Maximum limit: this is where it fails.** The policy caps Strategic accounts at $750,000. A guarantee raises the cap only by its guaranteed amount, and only if it is in force and names this customer. To reach $1,000,000, Acme needs a guarantee of at least $250,000 naming Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001) and in force on 2026-09-23.
- No guarantee document exists in the knowledge base.
- The only mentions are Sarah's email and Gina's verbal comment. That doesn't establish the amount, who it names, or the dates.
- The parent is Acme Group Holdings, Inc. A guarantee only counts if it names C-1001.

**6–7. Who would approve, if eligibility is established.**
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. She has no credit approval authority.
- Above $500,000, the policy requires approval from the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**.
- Because Acme is a Strategic account, it also requires concurrence from the VP Sales, **Michael Torres**.
- Neither of them submitted the request, so the rule that no one may approve their own request doesn't change anything.
- If no guarantee can be shown, the most Acme can get is $750,000. That needs the same two people.
