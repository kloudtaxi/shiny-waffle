## Answer

**No. Michael Torres cannot approve this.** As VP Sales he has the authority for an 18% discount, but Acme isn't entitled to 18% on the NS-500. Its contract and pricing exception cap the discount at 15%, and no approver can raise that cap. Separately, request DR-9112 isn't in the system of record.

**Step by step**

1. **The request.** The CRM discount-request log has no DR-9112. The only Acme NS-500 request dated 2026-09-23 is **DR-9001**: 15%, $37,500 off, net $212,500, raised by Sarah Chen, Pending Approval. Sarah's email to Michael that morning also says DR-9001 at 15%. I decided on DR-9112 as you gave it and did not substitute DR-9001. Its stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" and ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company (shared DUNS 04-812-7730; the agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing").
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000.
4. **Active contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for **up to 15%** on the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers the NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a **15% maximum**. It replaced the older 10% exception and states that it gives no employee approval authority. 18% is above this maximum, so the request goes beyond what Acme is entitled to.
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (calendar 2026).
7. **Requestor's authority.** VP Sales may approve discounts above 10% and up to 20%, so 18% is within Michael's limit. That doesn't help here, because having the authority doesn't create eligibility the contract doesn't give.
8. **Approver by band.** The 18% band belongs to VP Sales, which Michael holds. But the problem is commercial: even the CRO can't approve past the exception's cap. Allowing 18% would need new or amended terms, and only David Morgan (CRO), who approved the current exception, can approve them.
9. **Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Two ways forward: reduce the request to 15% or less, which is within both Acme's entitlement and Michael's authority, or get the exception amended.
