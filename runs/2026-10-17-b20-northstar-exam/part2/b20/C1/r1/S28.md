Elena Novak (Director of Finance) must approve and Michael Torres (VP Sales) must concur. Before anyone acts, there's a blocking problem: **CR-9203 isn't in Northstar ERP.** The only pending Acme request in the ERP export (credit_requests.csv) is **CR-9201**: C-1001, $250,000 → $400,000, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, justified by the third-plant ramp-up and not by a guarantee. **2. Customer:** "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (ERP C-1001), "Acme Manufacturing" (CRM-2048) and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044 / CRM-2091) is a different company.

**4. Payment history (no invoice paid or unpaid more than 30 days past due, for invoices due in the 12 months before the request):**

**6. Requestor's authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales. She has no credit approval authority.

**7. Who must approve:**

- Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence, from Michael Torres.
- Sarah submitted the request, so she can't approve or concur, but she holds neither role, so no one has to be replaced.
