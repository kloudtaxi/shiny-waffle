## Answer

I recommend **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**: the request can't be approved. CR-9207 asks for $500,000, but Acme Industrial Supply Co. can have at most $250,000. The parent guarantee Sarah Chen cites doesn't cover this company, and a more senior approver can't fix that.

**What I checked**

1. **The request.** CR-9207 for customer C-1044, from $50,000 to $500,000, dated 2026-09-23, submitted by Sarah Chen. It relies on a guarantee. **CR-9207 isn't in the ERP request log** (`credit_requests.csv`). The only Acme request Sarah Chen filed that day is CR-9201, for a different company: C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings, $250,000 to $400,000. I decided on CR-9207 as you gave it and didn't swap in CR-9201. The $50,000 current limit does match the ERP credit table for C-1044.
2. **The customer.** C-1044 in the ERP is Acme Industrial Supply Co. of Akron, OH (CRM-2091). The CRM records it as a **Standard** account owned by Zachary Brown. It is a separate company from Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing (C-1001 / CRM-2048, Milwaukee, Strategic). The support Schedule C says outright that "Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different company."
3. **The policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated in 2026.
4. **Payment history.** The rule is that no invoice due in the 12 months before the request may be paid or left unpaid more than 30 days past due. C-1044 has no invoices due in that window. Its last two were INV-30001 (due 2025-02-25, paid 2025-03-04) and INV-30003 (due 2025-05-07, paid 2025-04-28). Its payment history is clean.
5. **The maximum limit.** Standard accounts can go up to **$250,000**. The guarantee is GRT-ACME-2026, from Acme Group Holdings, Inc. It covers only **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, and says "No other company is covered." It is capped at $400,000 and runs from 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. So it doesn't raise this customer's maximum. Sarah's email says the guarantee is "still in place," but that refers to Acme Manufacturing's credit. The $500,000 request is $250,000 over the maximum.
6. **The requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive in Sales, and the policy gives her role no authority to approve credit. She also can't approve a request she submitted herself.
7. **The approver.** A $500,000 limit falls in the "over $100,000 up to $500,000" band, which needs the **Finance Manager, Priya Shah**. No Director of Finance approval is needed, and no VP Sales sign-off, because the account is Standard and the limit isn't over $500,000. This only matters if a request within the $250,000 maximum comes back.

If extra credit for Acme Manufacturing (C-1001) is the real goal, that's CR-9201, which needs its own decision.
