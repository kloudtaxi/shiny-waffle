## Answer

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, but nothing can happen until CR-9208 is confirmed to exist.** Acme is eligible for $650,000. Michael Torres can't approve it himself: the Director of Finance must approve, and his own manager must give the Sales concurrence in his place.

**1. The request isn't in the system of record.** Northstar ERP's credit request records have no CR-9208. The only pending request for customer C-1001 is CR-9201, raised by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23, from $250,000 to $400,000, with the same justification. I haven't substituted CR-9201. I decided CR-9208 as you gave it, and confirming it exists in the ERP is a blocking condition. The request relies on Acme's own standing, not on a guarantee.

**2. The customer.** ERP customer C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings"), CRM account CRM-2048 ("Acme Manufacturing") and DUNS 04-812-7730 are the same company. CRM records it as a **Strategic** account. The ERP shows a current limit of $250,000, which matches the request.

**3. Policy.** Northstar Credit Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31.

**4. Payment history: passes.** Of C-1001's invoices due in the 12 months before 2026-09-23:
- INV-60412 ($85,000) was due 2025-11-28 and paid 2025-11-24.
- INV-62011 ($480,000) was due 2026-07-25 and paid 2026-07-22.
- INV-62305 ($720,000) wasn't due until 2026-10-02, so it doesn't count.

None was paid or left unpaid more than 30 days past due.

**5. Maximum limit: passes.** The maximum for a Strategic account is $750,000, so $650,000 fits on Acme's own standing. Acme Group Holdings' parent guarantee (GRT-ACME-2026, $400,000, in force 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31) names this company and would raise the maximum further, but the request doesn't need it.

**6. Michael Torres's authority.** As VP Sales he holds no credit approval authority under the policy. He also submitted this request, so he can't approve or concur on it.

**7. Who must sign off.**
- **Approval:** limits over $500,000 need the Director of Finance, **Elena Novak**.
- **Concurrence:** Strategic accounts over $500,000 also need VP Sales concurrence. Michael Torres holds that role but is the requestor, so it passes to his manager, **David Morgan** (Chief Revenue Officer).

**Something to keep in mind:** invoice INV-62305 for $720,000 was due 2026-10-02 and is still unpaid. Today (2026-10-18) it is 16 days overdue. That's within the policy's 30-day threshold, but it would make Acme ineligible if still unpaid after 2026-11-01. It is also far above the current $250,000 limit.
