**How I got there**

Its justification is "Competitive," so it uses standard pricing, not any contract term.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2051 is BlueRiver Logistics (in the CRM account file). The same customer also appears as "Blue River Logistics LLC" (DUNS 07-331-9025).
3. **The product.** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000. The figures you sent add up: 18% of $120,000 is $21,600, leaving a net of $98,400.
4. **Contract and exception.** BlueRiver's only agreement on file is a Silver support plan (SUP-BRL-2025), which covers support, not pricing. I found no pricing exception for BlueRiver. None is needed, because the request doesn't rely on special terms.

7. **Points to note.**

   - The policy and the matrix both require approvals above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM, and that can't happen until the request exists there.
