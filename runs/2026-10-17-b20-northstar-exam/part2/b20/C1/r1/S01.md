## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it is above her 10% limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

**Outcome: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 matches the CRM record (discount_requests.csv) on every field. It's a 15% discount on NS-500, list price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 and it is "Pending Approval". It is justified as "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing) is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 (Acme Mfg. Holdings). They share DUNS number 04-812-7730 and the address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000. This matches the request.
4. **Active contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers NS-500. It was active on the request date. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The 15% request is at that maximum, not over it, so Acme is eligible. The older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) has been replaced and expired on 2025-03-31. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). The 2027 policy was published on 2026-09-15, but it only applies from 2027-01-01.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is beyond that.
8. **Required approver:** The Approval Authority Matrix (2026) puts discounts above 10% and up to 20% with the VP Sales. Michael Torres holds that role on the org chart dated 2026-09-01, and Sarah reports to him. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

The customer's contract entitles them to the discount, but it doesn't give Sarah the authority to approve it. Nothing else needs to happen before this decision can be acted on.
