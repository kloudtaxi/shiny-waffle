## Answer

**Yes. On 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved this herself.** One problem first: the knowledge base records DR-9001 with a request date of **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23. That date changes the answer (see the last section).

**Checks for 2025-09-23**
1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500 (list price $250,000, discount $37,500). The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** The CRM account is "Acme Manufacturing," and Sarah Chen owns it. The ERP customer is C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings." The graph has no direct link between these two records, so the match rests on the name and on the Acme exception documents, which use both.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement is in the graph by name only, with no terms or dates. The eligibility comes from the pricing exception below.
5. **The pricing exception:** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It allows up to **15% off list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31**, and David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% and ended 2025-03-31. 15% is within the maximum, so the customer is eligible.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. The 2025 policy lets that role approve discounts **up to and including 15%**, and VP Sales approval is only needed above 15%. So 15% is within her own authority.
8. **Approver:** No one else is needed. The exception covers the customer's eligibility, not her authority; her authority comes from the policy.

**If the request date is really 2026-09-23:** The exception still covers it, but the 2026 policy limits Enterprise Account Executives to **10%**. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need **VP Sales** approval, and that is **Michael Torres**, Sarah's manager. The outcome would then be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, not APPROVE. Please confirm which date is correct.
