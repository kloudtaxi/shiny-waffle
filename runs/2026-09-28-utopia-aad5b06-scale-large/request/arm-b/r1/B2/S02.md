## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** The request is higher than the discount Acme is allowed on the NS-500, so it should be rejected or escalated. Sending it to a more senior approver doesn't fix that. It needs new or amended commercial terms.

**How I got there**

1. **The request:** DR-9001, 18% off NS-500 for CRM account CRM-2048. List price is $250,000, the discount is $45,000 and the net price is $205,000. Sarah Chen requested it on 2026-09-23. It cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM calls them "Acme Manufacturing" and Sarah Chen owns the account. In ERP they are "Acme Mfg. Holdings", customer C-1001, Milwaukee WI. I found no record in the knowledge base that links CRM-2048 itself to either name.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The knowledge base has a "Master Supply Agreement" entry, but it has no dates, status or terms. I couldn't confirm directly that the agreement itself was active on 2026-09-23. That doesn't change the outcome, because the pricing exception below sets the limit either way.
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. It covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller and caps the discount at **15% of list price**. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% until 2025-03-31. David Morgan approved the current exception on 2025-03-20. **18% is 3 points over the 15% cap.**
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applied on the request date. Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, and anything over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
7. **Requestor's authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% and she can't approve 18% herself.
8. **Approver:** By discount size, 18% falls in the VP Sales band. The VP Sales is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. His sign-off still wouldn't make the 18% valid, because the exception's cap stays at 15%, and the exception itself says it gives no Northstar employee approval authority.

**Options**
- Resubmit at **15% or less**. The exception covers that, but it's still above Sarah's 10% limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would need to approve it.
- To give 18%, the Acme exception would first need to be amended through commercial review.
