## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself.** Acme qualifies for the 15%, but her own approval limit under the 2026 policy is 10%. Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve it.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 for account CRM-2048 on 2026-09-23. That's $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, leaving $212,500. Sarah Chen submitted it and cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** In the CRM the account is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen owns it. In the ERP the matching record appears to be Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which has the same NS-500 discount terms. The knowledge base doesn't link the two records directly, so I'm treating them as the same customer based on those shared terms. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (C-1044, owned by Zachary Brown) is a different customer.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement exists in the knowledge base, but none of its terms or dates are recorded. Eligibility here comes from the pricing exception below.
5. **The pricing exception:** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20. It allows up to 15% off list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller, and replaces the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31). It covers this product and date, and 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception also says it gives no Northstar employee any approval authority.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 is in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It lowered the account-executive limit from 15% under the 2025 policy to 10%. Under the 2025 policy Sarah could have approved 15% herself; under the 2026 policy she can't.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **The required approver:** The 2026 policy sends discounts above 10% and up to 20% to the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, Sarah's manager since 2026-09-01. The Chief Revenue Officer only comes in above 20%.
