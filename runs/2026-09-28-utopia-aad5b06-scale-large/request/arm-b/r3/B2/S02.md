## Answer

I've checked the exception, the policy and the approval chain. Here's the decision.

**No, we can't offer 18%.** Acme's pricing exception for the NS-500 caps the discount at 15%, so DR-9001 has to be rejected or escalated for new commercial terms. A more senior approver can't approve past that cap.

1. **Request:** DR-9001 is for 18% off NS-500, which is $45,000 off $250,000 list, for $205,000 net. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"), not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** Acme is called "Acme Manufacturing" in the CRM, where Sarah Chen is the account owner. In the ERP it's "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001, Milwaukee, WI). The knowledge graph has no record for the CRM account ID CRM-2048, so I linked the two by name and account owner, not by ID.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement appears in the graph but has no terms or dates, so I can't show it was active on 2026-09-23. The discount limit that applies comes from the pricing exception below.
5. **Pricing exception:** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date, and it sets the maximum discount at **15% of list**. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% and expired 2025-03-31. David Morgan approved the new exception on 2025-03-20. The exception states that it sets which discounts Acme can get but gives no Northstar employee approval authority.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date. It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve up to 10%, requires VP Sales for over 10% up to 20%, and requires the Chief Revenue Officer above 20%.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. 18% is beyond it.
8. **Approver band:** 18% falls in the VP Sales band, which is Michael Torres (Sarah reports to him). But that only matters for a discount Acme is eligible for, and 18% is above the 15% cap.
9. **Decision:** REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. The request goes 3 points past the exception's maximum. The options are:
   - Change the request to 15% or less. Since that's still over Sarah's 10% limit, Michael Torres (VP Sales) would then have to approve it.
   - Get Acme's exception or contract amended to allow a higher discount.

   The CRM justification cites the Master Supply Agreement. If anyone thinks it allows more than 15% on the NS-500, they should produce that document. Nothing in the knowledge base shows that it does.
