## Answer

**Yes, if the request was made on 2025-09-23.** The 2025 policy let Sarah Chen approve 15% herself, and Acme's pricing exception allowed 15% on NS-500 that day. But the CRM record in the knowledge base dates DR-9001 **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23. That date matters: under the 2026 policy she could approve only up to 10%, and 15% would need VP Sales approval.

**How I got there (for 2025-09-23):**
1. **The request:** DR-9001, 15% off NS-500 (list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500), requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** the CRM account is Acme Manufacturing, which Sarah owns. The ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings." The graph doesn't link the two names or tie account CRM-2048 to either one, so treating them as the same customer is my assumption.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** the "Master Supply Agreement" is in the graph but has no terms or dates, so I can't show it was active on the request date. Acme's earlier NS-500 terms (a 10% maximum under exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10) ran 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so they had expired by then.
5. **Pricing exception:** the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" applies only to NS-500. It sets a 15% maximum from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10. David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20. It covers this product and date, and 15% is exactly at its maximum. The exception grants no approval authority to employees.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Sarah's authority:** she is an Enterprise Account Executive, and the 2025 policy lets that role approve "up to and including 15%." VP Sales approval is needed only above 15%. The graph gives no dates for her role, so I assumed she held it in September 2025.
8. **Approver needed:** none beyond Sarah.

**If the recorded 2026-09-23 date is right:** the 2026 policy limits Enterprise Account Executives to 10% and requires VP Sales approval for more than 10% up to 20%. The exception still covers 15%, so the outcome would be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`, with Michael Torres (VP Sales) as approver. Sarah could not approve it herself.

**Before relying on this:** confirm the request date in the CRM, and ideally that CRM-2048 is the same customer as C-1001.
