## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme is eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, but her own limit under the 2026 pricing policy is 10%. The request needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 Industrial Controller: $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23 and it is Pending Approval. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** The CRM account is "Acme Manufacturing", owned by Sarah Chen. The ERP record for the same eligibility is customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings".
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The knowledge base mentions a "Master Supply Agreement" but holds none of its terms or dates. So I can't show that a contract was active on 2026-09-23 from the contract itself.
5. **The pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception (acme_pricing_exception.md) sets Acme's maximum NS-500 discount at 15% of list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31.
   - David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20.
   - It replaces the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ran from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.
   - It covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller, and the request date falls inside its dates.
   - 15% is exactly the maximum, so eligibility is established.
   - The document says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 replaces the 2025 policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. Its approval bands:
   - up to and including 10%: Enterprise Account Executive
   - over 10% up to and including 20%: VP Sales
   - over 20%: Chief Revenue Officer
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. At 15%, the request is beyond it.
8. **The required approver:** 15% falls in the VP Sales band. Michael Torres holds that role, and Sarah has reported to him since 2026-09-01.
9. **Decision:** APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION. The customer's terms allow 15%, but that doesn't give Sarah authority to approve it. Michael Torres must approve it.

One gap: the request cites the Master Supply Agreement, but the eligibility above comes from the pricing exception, not the agreement. If you want the contract confirmed as active on the request date, ask for the Master Supply Agreement itself. The exception alone establishes eligibility for this product and date.
