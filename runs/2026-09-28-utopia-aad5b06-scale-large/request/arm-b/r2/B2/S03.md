## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself, and it shouldn't go to anyone for sign-off yet. The only Acme discount terms on record cover a different product, so the request needs commercial review first.

**The request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off $120,000 list ($18,000 off, $102,000 net) on NS-CLOUD. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it depends on special terms rather than standard pricing.

**Customer.** Sarah Chen owns the CRM account "Acme Manufacturing." In the ERP system the customer is C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings." I'm treating these as the same company based on the names; I found no field that links CRM-2048 to C-1001 directly.

**Is there a discount for this product?** No.
- **The agreement:** "Master Supply Agreement" exists in the knowledge base as a name only. None of its terms are recorded, and it isn't linked to Acme, so I can't confirm it covers NS-Cloud.
- **Acme's only pricing exception:** the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception," valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It allows up to 15% but "applies only to" the NS-500 Industrial Controller and doesn't apply to any other Northstar product. It replaced the earlier EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10%.
- **The 15% figure:** it matches the NS-500 terms exactly, so the request looks like it applies the NS-500 discount to NS-Cloud. Exceptions only cover the products and dates they name.
- **NS-Cloud Operations Suite:** nothing in the knowledge base links it to any contract, exception or discount.

**Could she approve it anyway?**
- **Her limit:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date. It lets Enterprise Account Executives, which is Sarah Chen's role, approve up to 10%. (The 15% limit was in the 2025 policy, which the 2026 policy replaced.)
- **Who would approve 15%:** discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah Chen's manager.
- **Why he can't approve it now:** a higher approver can't create eligibility the customer doesn't have. Even if the contract did allow 15%, that wouldn't change Sarah Chen's own limit.

**Next step:** send DR-9001 for commercial review. Ask Sarah Chen for the Acme Master Supply Agreement and the clause she's relying on for NS-Cloud pricing. If a term covering NS-Cloud turns up, bring the request back; it would then need Michael Torres's approval as VP Sales. If there's no such term, Acme needs new or amended terms before 15% can apply. As standard pricing, she could approve up to 10% herself.
