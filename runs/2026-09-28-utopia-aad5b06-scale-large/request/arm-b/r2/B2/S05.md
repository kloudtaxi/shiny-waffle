**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 exists in the knowledge base, but nothing links it to a customer. Three Acme records could be it:

- **Acme Industrial Supply Co.:** ERP customer C-1044, owned by Zachary Brown. It also has a "15% off list" request recorded, and Gina confirmed an NS-500 controller on 2026-09-23.

**5. Pricing exception.** None found for NS-500 on 2026-09-23.
