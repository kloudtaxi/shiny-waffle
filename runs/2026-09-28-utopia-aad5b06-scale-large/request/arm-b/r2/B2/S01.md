## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but it's above her personal limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 Industrial Controller. That's $37,500 off a $250,000 list price, for a net of $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**2. The customer.** In the CRM the account is "Acme Manufacturing," and Sarah Chen is the account owner. In the ERP the customer is C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings." The document that sets the current pricing exception names both. The knowledge base has no facts on account ID CRM-2048 itself, so the link from that ID to Acme is taken from the request.

**3. The product.** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Contract and 5. pricing exception.**
- The "Master Supply Agreement" exists in the knowledge base only as a name, with no dates or terms. So I couldn't confirm a separate active contract.
- The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" does cover this request:
  - It applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
  - It allows up to 15% off list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, which includes 2026-09-23.
  - It replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% and ran from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.
  - The exception says it grants eligibility, not approval authority to any employee.
- The requested 15% is exactly at the limit, so Acme is eligible.

**6. Policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31 and replaces the 2025 policy.

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. The 2026 policy lets that role approve up to 10%, so 15% is above her limit. (The 2025 policy allowed 15%, but it no longer applies.)

**8. Required approver.** Under the 2026 policy, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Michael Torres is VP Sales, and Sarah Chen reports to him.

**9. Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Acme can get the 15%, but only with Michael Torres's approval. The customer's eligibility doesn't change who is allowed to approve.
