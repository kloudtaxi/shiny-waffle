## Answer

**No, not at 18%. The decision is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** Acme is only eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500 on 2026-09-23. Having a more senior person sign off can't fix that: it needs new or amended commercial terms.

**Findings, step by step**

1. **Request:** DR-9001 is for 18% off the NS-500. List price is $250,000, the discount is $45,000 and the net is $205,000. Sarah Chen requested it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** In the CRM, Acme Manufacturing (account CRM-2048) is owned by Sarah Chen. In the ERP, the customer is C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings." These are treated as the same customer: the Acme records share the same documents and eligibility terms.
3. **Product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** The knowledge base has an entity called "Master Supply Agreement," but it has no terms, dates or links to anything. I can't confirm from it that a contract was active on 2026-09-23, or what discount it allows.
5. **Pricing exception:** Acme's eligibility for the NS-500 comes from the **Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception**. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. It applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller and sets a **maximum of 15% off list**, valid from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers the request date. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. No corrections or withdrawals to either exception have been recorded.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. Above 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. 18% is above it.
8. **Required approver:** 18% falls in the VP Sales band. Michael Torres holds that role, and Sarah Chen reports to him.
9. **Decision:** The 18% is 3 points above Acme's 15% maximum. Having Michael Torres approve it would satisfy the approval rules, but it would not make Acme eligible. The exception itself says it gives no Northstar employee approval authority.

**Options**
- **Offer 15% or less.** Acme is eligible for that, but Michael Torres (VP Sales) still has to approve it, because anything over 10% is above Sarah Chen's limit.
- **Find the Master Supply Agreement terms.** If the MSA really allows 18% on the NS-500, the request would need to be reassessed with that document. The knowledge base doesn't show it.
- **Pursue an amended exception** above 15% through commercial review.
