- **The dates don't match.** You gave 2025‑09‑23, but the CRM record in the knowledge base has **2026‑09‑23**. I assessed your date, but this should be checked, because it changes which policy applies.

**2. The customer.** Sarah Chen is the account owner of Acme Manufacturing. Nothing in the knowledge base links CRM‑2048 to that account. The ERP also has similar names ("Acme Mfg. **3. The product.** NS‑500 is the NS‑500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The contract.** The Acme Manufacturing term for NS‑500 ("may receive up to … off list price") ran from **2023‑04‑01 to 2025‑03‑31**. It had expired by 2025‑09‑23, and the maximum percentage isn't recorded. I found no active agreement for the request date.

**5. A pricing exception.** An "Acme NS‑500 Strategic Account Exception" exists:
- It applies only to NS‑500, replaces an earlier exception called EXC‑ACME‑NS500‑10, and was approved on 2025‑03‑20 by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer).
- It sets which discounts the customer can get. It explicitly gives no Northstar employee the right to approve them.
- Its maximum discount and end date are not in the knowledge base, and the request doesn't cite it. So I can't tell whether it covers 15% on this date.

**6. The policy.** There is a Pricing Policy 2025 and a 2026 policy that replaces it, but neither has an effective date recorded. For 2025‑09‑23 the 2025 policy is the likely one.
- The 2025 policy says Enterprise Account Executives may approve some discounts and that others need VP Sales approval.
- The percentage limits for each band are not in the knowledge base.

**7. Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, but her limit is unknown, so I can't say whether 15% is within it. Even if the customer qualifies, that doesn't give her the authority.

**8. The approver.** VP Sales is Michael Torres. I can't confirm that 15% falls in his band.

- a current MSA or amendment covering NS‑500 on the request date, or the Strategic Account Exception document with its maximum and dates;
- the 2025 (and 2026) policy approval bands and effective dates;
- the CRM‑2048 to Acme customer link;
- confirmation of the correct request date.
