2. **The customer:** In the CRM the account is "Acme Manufacturing", and Sarah is the account owner. In the ERP it is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (customer C-1001). The knowledge base doesn't link the CRM id CRM-2048 to either record, so the match is by name.
3. **The product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. No pricing terms are linked to it.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23.
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception allows up to 15% off from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller and says it does not apply to any other Northstar product. The knowledge base has no Acme term for NS-Cloud. The 15% in the request matches the NS-500 maximum, which suggests the NS-500 terms are being applied to a product they don't cover.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy, which allowed AEs up to 15%.

**Decision:** The terms relied on don't cover this product. Getting VP sign-off wouldn't fix that, because under the SOP special terms don't carry over to other products. Next steps:

- If Sarah believes the Master Supply Agreement has its own NS-Cloud pricing clause, she should send the document. The knowledge base has no such term.
