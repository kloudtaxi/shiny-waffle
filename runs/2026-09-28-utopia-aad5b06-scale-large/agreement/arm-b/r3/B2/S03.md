## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001.** The contract pricing the request relies on only covers a different product, and 15% is above her own limit anyway. The request needs commercial review.

1. **Request:** DR-9001, dated 2026-09-23 and submitted by Sarah Chen, asks for 15% off $120,000 list (net $102,000). Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing" / "ACME Manufacturing", and Sarah Chen owns the account. The ERP customer C-1001 is "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The Acme contract records use both names.
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD, the NS-Cloud Operations Suite. The knowledge base has no contract terms, exceptions or pricing linked to this product.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covered 2026-09-23.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers NS-Cloud. The only Acme exception is the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception", approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20:
   - It sets a maximum of 15% off list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, replacing EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10%.
   - It "applies only to" the NS-500 Industrial Controller and does not apply to other Northstar products.
   - It establishes that Acme is commercially eligible for the discount, but gives no Northstar employee authority to approve it.

   The 15% matches the NS-500 terms, but those terms can't be carried over to NS-Cloud.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), which replaced the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2026 policy she can approve up to and including 10%, so 15% is above her limit. The 2025 policy would have let her approve 15%, but it no longer applies.
8. **Required approver, if eligibility were established:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. The VP Sales is Michael Torres, who has been Sarah's manager since 2026-09-01.

**Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** Commercial review needs to decide whether Acme has, or should get, contract or exception terms that cover NS-Cloud. If they do, the request would still need Michael Torres's approval as VP Sales, since Sarah's limit is 10%. The Acme contract terms alone can't justify approving it.
