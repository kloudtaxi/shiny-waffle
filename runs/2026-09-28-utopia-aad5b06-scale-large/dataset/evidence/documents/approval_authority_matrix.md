# Approval Authority Matrix — Enterprise Discounts (2026)

Applies to discount requests governed by the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026.

| Role | Discount authority |
|---|---|
| Enterprise Account Executive | ≤10% |
| VP Sales | >10% and ≤20% |
| Chief Revenue Officer | >20% |

Notes:

- Authority attaches to the role, not to the individual or the account.
- Discounts are measured against current list price.
- Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
