> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

All three IDs refer to the same customer: **Acme Manufacturing**, whose legal and billing name is **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. It is based at 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209, and has DUNS number 04-812-7730.

| ID | System | Record |
|---|---|---|
| **CRM-2048** | CRM | "Acme Manufacturing", a Strategic account in the Midwest Enterprise segment, owned by Sarah Chen, DUNS 04-812-7730 |
| **C-1001** | ERP | "Acme Mfg. Holdings", Industrial Manufacturing, same Milwaukee address and DUNS, payment terms Net 45 |
| **ACME-MFG-2025** | Legal contract | Master Supply Agreement between Northstar and "Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing" at the same Milwaukee address, running 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 |

Sarah Chen's 2026 account plan also states the link directly: "CRM account: CRM-2048 … ERP bill-to: Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001)."

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.** Only the "Acme" name is shared:
- **IDs:** CRM-2091 in the CRM and C-1044 in the ERP.
- **Location and industry:** 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310. Its industry is Industrial Distribution, not Industrial Manufacturing.
- **DUNS:** 09-552-1187, compared with 04-812-7730 for Acme Manufacturing.
- **Account:** a Standard, Midwest Mid-Market account owned by Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen.
- **Support records:** it shows up as "Acme Industrial (CRM-2091)" in service request SR-40418 for its Akron site.

This matters for pricing. The master agreement ACME-MFG-2025 and its NS-500 pricing exceptions (EXC-ACME-NS500-15, the 15% discount that replaced the earlier 10% exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10) belong to Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings. None of the documents I found say they cover Acme Industrial Supply Co.
