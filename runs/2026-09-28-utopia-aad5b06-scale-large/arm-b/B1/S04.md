## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

Yes. On 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen had the authority to approve a 15% discount on NS-500 for Acme Manufacturing on her own.

**Why:**
1. **Authority rules in force (Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31):** Enterprise Account Executives could approve discounts up to and including 15%. Only discounts above 15% needed VP Sales approval. 15% is exactly at her limit.
2. **Her role:** Both the employee list and the org chart show her as an Enterprise Account Executive reporting to Michael Torres, VP Sales. Neither document is from 2025: the org chart is dated 2026-09-01 and the employee list has no date. I found nothing suggesting she had a different role in 2025.
3. **Customer's eligibility:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% off list, for NS-500 only, over the same period. It replaced the older 10% exception from 2025-04-01. The exception itself states that it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So the approval power comes from the 2025 policy, not from the exception.
4. **Records:** The discount log has a matching request, DR-8104: Acme (CRM-2048), NS-500, 15%, requested by Sarah Chen on 2025-09-16 and marked Approved. The approver column is cut off in the search results, so I couldn't confirm who signed it.

**This does not carry over to 2026.** The 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix cut Enterprise Account Executive authority to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% now need VP Sales approval, and approvals above 10% must be recorded in the CRM. Her current request, DR-9001 (15%, dated 2026-09-23, status Pending), therefore needs Michael Torres to approve it. In her email about it she says Acme wants "what we did for them last September," but the Acme agreement only makes the customer eligible for 15%. It doesn't give her the authority to approve it. If you meant the 2026-09-23 request rather than 2025, the answer is no.
