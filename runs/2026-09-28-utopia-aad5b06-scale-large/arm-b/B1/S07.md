## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing can get up to 15% off list on the NS-500 because its current supply contract, signed in 2025, says so. That is only a ceiling on what it can be offered: each discount still needs internal approval.

**Why it qualifies**

1. **Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025.** This is the contract between Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, which trades as Acme Manufacturing. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Section 3.2 says: "Customer is eligible for discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."
2. **Schedule B, pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15.** This covers Acme Manufacturing and the NS-500 only, with a maximum discount of 15% of list price over the same dates. David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20; Sarah Chen prepared it. Morgan signed the agreement for Northstar on 2025-03-20 and Edward Floyd, Acme's VP Procurement, signed on 2025-03-22.
3. **It replaces the earlier 10% deal.** The 2023 exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, allowed up to 10% on orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. The new exception says it replaces that one for requests dated 2025-04-01 or later. The knowledge graph still only records the old 10% entitlement, which has now expired, so rely on the documents rather than the graph for the current figure.
4. **It has been used before.** Discount request DR-8104 (account CRM-2048, dated 2025-09-16) was for 15% off $250,000 on the NS-500, justified as "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." It was approved.

**Limits**

- **It doesn't approve anything by itself.** The exception "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." Section 3.3 of the agreement says each discount "remains subject to approval under Northstar's internal policies."
- **The current request is still pending.** Sarah Chen emailed Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 about DR-9001: 15% off a $250,000 NS-500 order for Acme's third plant. It is listed as pending, so it needs approval like any other discount.
- **It covers the NS-500 only.** Other products Acme buys under the agreement, NS-Cloud and NS-Edge, are not included.
- **It applies to Acme Manufacturing only (CRM-2048).** Acme Industrial (CRM-2091) is a separate account and isn't covered.
