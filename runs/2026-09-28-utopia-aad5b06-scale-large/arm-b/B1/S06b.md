## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **Why it's 10%:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023, allows Acme up to 10% off NS-500 list price on orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. 2024-10-01 falls inside that period, and the document lists its status as Active.
- **Why it isn't 15%:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 raises the maximum to 15%, but only from 2025-04-01. It replaces the 10% exception just for requests dated on or after that day, so it didn't apply on 2024-10-01. Discount requests that cite 15% "per Acme Master Supply Agreement" refer to the later 2025 agreement (ACME-MFG-2025).

I didn't find any amendment or withdrawal of the 10% exception.
