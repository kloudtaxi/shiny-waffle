## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum NS-500 discount was **10% off list price**.

- **Source:** `acme_pricing_exception_2023.md` says: *"Acme Manufacturing may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."* This is exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, and 2024-10-01 falls inside that period.
- **Later exception doesn't change this:** A newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (`acme_pricing_exception.md`, approved by CRO David Morgan on 2025-03-20) replaces EXC-ACME-NS500-10 only for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. The knowledge base doesn't record the newer exception's percentage.
- **No corrections:** Neither record has been corrected or withdrawn.

One caveat: the 10% cap comes from a pricing exception document, not from the text of a contract. The only contract the knowledge base mentions is a "Master Supply Agreement," and it has no recorded terms, so there's nothing that sets a different contract figure.
