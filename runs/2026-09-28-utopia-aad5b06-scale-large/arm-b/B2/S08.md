## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

I can only partly answer this. The knowledge base shows who C-1001 is, but it has nothing that ties CRM-2048 or ACME-MFG-2025 to it. It does clearly show that Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.

**What the records show**
- **C-1001** is the ERP customer ID for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. Its details: Industrial Manufacturing, billing address 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209, DUNS 04-812-7730, payment terms Net 45.
- **CRM-2048** exists as a name only. It has no facts or links, so nothing ties it to C-1001 or to any Acme record. Don't confuse it with **C-2048**, which is a different ERP customer (Flores and Sons Co., Minneapolis).
- **ACME-MFG-2025** isn't in the knowledge base at all.
- **Acme Manufacturing** (also written "ACME Manufacturing") is the account in the sales and pricing records. Its account owner is Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive. It is covered by the NS-500 pricing exception, valid 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. Some of the same source documents mention both "Acme Manufacturing" and "Acme Mfg. Holdings", which suggests they're the same customer under a trading name and a legal name. But the graph doesn't formally link them, so that's a likely reading, not a confirmed match.

**Is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer? No.** Every identifying detail is different:

| | Acme Mfg. Holdings / Acme Manufacturing | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| ERP ID | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| DUNS | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |
| Industry | Industrial Manufacturing | Industrial Distribution |
| Bill-to | Milwaukee, WI 53209 | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310 |
| Account owner | Sarah Chen | Zachary Brown |

The only thing they share is Net 45 payment terms, which many customers have. Because the DUNS numbers differ, they are separate legal entities. So the NS-500 exception for Acme Manufacturing shouldn't be applied to Acme Industrial Supply Co.

To confirm that CRM-2048 and ACME-MFG-2025 belong to Acme, you'd need a CRM-to-ERP cross-reference or the record where those IDs are defined. Neither is in the knowledge base now.
