## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

I think so. Under the pricing policy in force on 2025-09-23, a 15% discount was within Sarah Chen's own approval limit. Two things I couldn't confirm are below, along with a possible mix-up of the year.

**Why she had the authority on that date**
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and she owns the Acme Manufacturing account.
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 covered requests up to 2025-12-31. It says: *"Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts up to and including 15%,"* and *"Discounts greater than 15% require VP Sales approval."* 15% is exactly at her limit, so it wouldn't have needed VP Sales.
- The Pricing Policy 2026 only replaced the 2025 policy "for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01," so it doesn't apply to this date.

**Acme's discount agreements for the NS-500**
- The old exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) capped Acme at **10%** off list on the NS-500, but only for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. It didn't apply in September 2025.
- A newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception," approved by CRO David Morgan on 2025-03-20, replaced it for requests from 2025-04-01. That document says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So Sarah's authority to approve still came from the pricing policy, not this exception.
- **Not confirmed:** the records don't give the discount percentage in the new exception. I can't tell whether Acme was allowed as much as 15% on the NS-500 at that time. Her authority to approve is clear, but Acme's eligibility for that amount isn't.

**Possible year mix-up**
The only matching request on record is **DR-9001**. Sarah requested it on **2026-09-23**, not 2025: 15% off $250,000 list, which is $37,500 off for a net of $212,500. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," and its status is still "Pending Approval." Around the same date, Sarah emailed that Gina at Acme had confirmed they want the NS-500 for a third plant. If this is the request you mean, the 2026 policy applies instead. It hands the limits to a separate Approval Authority Matrix, and that matrix's limits aren't in the records. So I can't say whether Sarah could approve DR-9001 herself.
