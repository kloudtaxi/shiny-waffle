> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

I can't confirm the 15% from the knowledge base. No stored fact says Acme gets 15% off the NS-500. What's recorded is a 10% exception that has expired, and a newer exception whose discount rate isn't recorded.

**The old 10% exception (expired)**
- *acme_pricing_exception_2023.md*: "Acme Manufacturing may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."
- The graph records it as valid only for that period, so it no longer applies today.

**The newer "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" (acme_pricing_exception.md)**
- **Who approved it:** "David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, 2025-03-20." The org chart and employee list confirm he is CRO.
- **What it replaces:** it "Supersedes EXC-ACME-NS500-10 for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01." The "-10" in that ID is probably the old 10% exception.
- **What it covers:** it "applies only to the product identified above" (the NS-500 Industrial Controller) and "does not apply to any other Northstar product."
- **What it doesn't do:** it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." So even if Acme qualifies, a salesperson can't approve the discount on their own authority.
- **The rate and dates** of this exception aren't recorded. If 15% comes from anywhere, it's most likely this document, but I can't show that.

**Deal background**
- Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive, owns the Acme account (crm_accounts.csv).
- In an email to Michael dated 2026-09-23, Sarah wrote: "Gina at Acme confirmed this morning they want to move ahead with the NS-500 controller for the third plant."

In short, the knowledge base supports this much: a newer, CRO-approved exception covering only the NS-500 replaced the expired 10% deal from April 2025. It doesn't show that the new rate is 15%, or whether the exception is still in effect. Before quoting 15%, check the rate and dates in acme_pricing_exception.md itself, and get approval through the normal channel, since the exception gives no one authority to approve it.
