## Answer

3. **No Acme exception covers this deal.** The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller, not to NS-Cloud Operations Suite. They also don't confirm that 15% falls within the VP Sales limit rather than needing someone higher, such as Finance (the Director of Finance is Elena Novak).
