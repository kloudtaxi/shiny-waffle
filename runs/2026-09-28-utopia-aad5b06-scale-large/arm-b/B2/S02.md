## Answer

I can't confirm that 18% is allowed, and the records point to "not without further approval."

1. **The request doesn't say 18%.** DR-9001 (dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen, the Acme account owner) asks for **15%** on the NS-500: $250,000 list, $37,500 off, $212,500 net. Its status is **Pending Approval**, so no discount on it has been approved. 18% would be a new, larger request.

2. **The contract discount it relies on had ended.** The request cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." But the Acme NS-500 discount allowance in the records ran from 2023-04-01 to **2025-03-31**, so it no longer applied on 2026-09-23.

3. **The special Acme exception doesn't let anyone sign off on its own.**
   - The "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception" was approved on 2025-03-20 by David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer. It replaces an earlier exception coded EXC-ACME-NS500-10.
   - It covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller and makes Acme *eligible* for special pricing.
   - It states that it gives **no Northstar employee authority to approve** the discount. The request still needs normal sign-off.

4. **Normal sign-off rules:** discounts are measured against the current list price. Account executives can approve some discounts themselves; larger ones need the VP Sales.

**What I couldn't find:** the actual percentage limits. That includes the cap in the agreement, the rate the exception allows (the "-10" in the old code may mean 10%, but that's a guess), and the account executive and VP Sales thresholds. So I can't say whether 18% falls within any allowed limit. I also found a note that someone named Gina "confirmed" something about the NS-500 on 2026-09-23, but the records don't say what.

**Recommendation:** don't offer 18% on the strength of DR-9001. Check the percentages in the approval rules document ("Approval Authority Matrix — Enterprise Discounts") and the exception. Then either change DR-9001 to 18% or file a new request and send it to the right approver, probably at least the VP Sales.
