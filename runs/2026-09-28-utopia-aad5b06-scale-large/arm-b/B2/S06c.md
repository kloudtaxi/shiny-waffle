## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2022-10-01?

## Answer

I found no contractual NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2022-10-01.

The only contractual NS-500 discount on record comes from the pricing exception document `acme_pricing_exception_2023.md`. It says: *"Acme Manufacturing may receive up to 10% off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) for orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31."*

That 10% cap started six months after 2022-10-01, so it doesn't cover that date. I found no earlier Acme contract or exception for the NS-500. The discount-related records I found are about who can approve discounts, not what a customer is contractually entitled to. The records also mention a later "Strategic Account Exception", approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, that replaced the 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It came after 2022 as well.

If an earlier contract exists, it isn't in the knowledge base.
