## Answer

I've checked the contract, the pricing exception, the 2026 policy and the org chart. Here's the decision.

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is eligible for the 15% discount, but 15% is above her own limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". They share the same DUNS number (04-812-7730) and address, and the contract names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller, which the contract lists in Section 2.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** Yes. EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers the NS-500 only, up to 15% off list, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the older 10% exception for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. 15% is within that maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception also says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, both covering 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2026 policy fully replaces the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to 10% under the 2026 policy.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres (employee EMP-200, Sarah's manager). Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Don't use the 2025 approval as a precedent.** Sarah approved an identical Acme request herself (DR-8104, 15%, 2025-09-16). That was allowed then because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy lowered that limit to 10%.
