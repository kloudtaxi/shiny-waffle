## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** Acme Manufacturing's contract caps its NS-500 discount at 15%. Approval from a more senior person can't fix that; going above 15% needs new or amended contract terms.

**The numbers don't match.** You quote DR-9001 at 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). But our discount request records show DR-9001 at 15% ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also says Acme is "asking for 15% off list." I decided on the 18% you gave me. Someone should confirm which figure is correct in CRM.

**How I got there:**
1. **The request:** DR-9001 on 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen, for NS-500 at a $250,000 list value. It relies on the contract: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing. In the finance system it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for "up to 15%" on the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15%, over the same dates as the contract. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ended 2025-03-31). It sets what Acme is eligible for, not who can approve.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to this date, along with the Approval Authority Matrix for 2026.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive and can approve up to 10%.
8. **Who would normally approve:** For discounts above 10% and up to 20%, it's the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
9. **Decision:** 18% is more than the 15% Acme is eligible for, so VP Sales approval alone isn't enough.

If the request is really 15%, Acme is eligible for it. It's still above Sarah's 10% limit, though, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would need to approve it and record the approval in CRM.
