## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001.** The request relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement's 15% discount covers only the NS-500 controller, not NS-Cloud. The request needs commercial review. It can't simply be passed up to a more senior approver.

**Discrepancy to fix first:** The request you gave lists **NS-CLOUD** at $120,000 list. The copy of DR-9001 in our discount log (`discount_requests.csv`) lists **NS-500** at $250,000 list ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Everything else matches: same account, 15%, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, same justification, pending. I've assessed the request as you gave it (NS-CLOUD), and the CRM record should be corrected.

1. **The request:** DR-9001, 15% off NS-CLOUD ($18,000 off $120,000, leaving $102,000). Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23, and it relies on the contract ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement").
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. Its billing account in the finance system is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which trades as Acme Manufacturing. CRM-2091 (Acme Industrial Supply Co.) is a separate customer.
3. **The product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, which lists at $120,000.
4. **The contract:** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Its product list includes NS-CLOUD. But its only pricing term (§3.2) gives up to 15% **on the NS-500** only. For everything else (§3.1), prices are list less whatever discount an order agrees. §3.3 also says the customer's pricing rights don't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **The pricing exception:** The only one in force, EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement), covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." I found no exception or amendment for NS-CLOUD. Sarah's own 2026 account plan only says Acme is evaluating NS-Cloud; it mentions no agreed cloud pricing.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%, no longer applies.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit, so she couldn't approve this herself even if the product were covered.
8. **Required approver:** Under the 2026 approval matrix, a discount above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres. That only matters once there are terms that make Acme eligible for this discount on NS-CLOUD.

**Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The NS-500 terms don't carry over to NS-Cloud, so the 15% needs commercial review. That means new or amended terms for NS-CLOUD, or treating it as a standard discount. If it's then justified as a standard discount, Michael Torres would have to approve it as VP Sales.

If the CRM record is right and the product is really NS-500, the answer changes. Acme is eligible for up to 15% under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, but the discount is still above Sarah's 10% limit. The outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving.
