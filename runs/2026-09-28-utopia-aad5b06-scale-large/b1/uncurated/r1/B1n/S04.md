> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23? ## Answer

**Yes. On 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved this herself.** One caution first: in the knowledge base, DR-9001 is dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23. If 2026 is the real date, she could not approve it alone (see the end).

**How the 2025-09-23 date works out:**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500. The list price is $250,000, the discount is $37,500 and the net price is $212,500. Sarah Chen submitted it, and the stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." So it relies on contract terms.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, whose account owner is Sarah Chen. It matches ERP customer Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Don't confuse it with a different account, CRM-2091, Acme Industrial Supply Co.
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2025-09-23 and covers NS-500. Section 3.3 says the contract does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **The pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to 15% off NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended 2025-03-31. 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible. The exception also says it gives no employee any approval authority.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (valid 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Her authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (employee EMP-101). Under the 2025 policy, that role can approve discounts up to and including 15%. So she is covered in her own right, not because the contract allows the discount.
8. **Required approver:** Only discounts above 15% need the VP Sales, so no one else is needed.

**Outcome: APPROVE.** Both conditions are met: Acme is eligible, and 15% is within her 2025 limit. The knowledge base also has an identical earlier request, DR-8104 (dated 2025-09-16, 15%, NS-500, CRM-2048), marked Approved.

**If the actual date is 2026-09-23 (as the knowledge base records it):** Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply instead. Account Executives can approve only up to 10%, and discounts above 10% up to 20% need the VP Sales. Acme would still be eligible, but the outcome would be **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**, with Michael Torres (VP Sales) approving. The approval would also have to be recorded in Northstar CRM. Please confirm which date is right.
