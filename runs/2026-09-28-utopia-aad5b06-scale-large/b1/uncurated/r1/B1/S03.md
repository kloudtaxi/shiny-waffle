## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this, and it shouldn't go to a higher approver either. The Acme contract terms cover the NS-500 controller only, not NS-Cloud, so the request needs commercial review first.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-CLOUD for Acme (account CRM-2048), dated 2026-09-23 and submitted by Sarah Chen. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
- **The records don't match what you sent.** The CRM export in the knowledge base lists DR-9001 as **NS-500**, list value $250,000, discount $37,500. Your copy says NS-CLOUD at $120,000. Someone needs to confirm which product the request is actually for.

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). It is not "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091) or "Acme Industrial Supply Co."

**3. The product.** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000.

**4. Active contract.** Yes. Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It lists NS-CLOUD as a product Northstar will supply, but its only discount term (§3.2) is "up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller" under Schedule B.

**5. Pricing exception.** None covers NS-CLOUD.
- EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) covers "NS-500 … only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product."
- The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 also covered NS-500 only, and it ended 2025-03-31.
- Sarah's own 2026 account plan calls the preferential pricing NS-500-only and says NS-Cloud is still under evaluation.

**6. Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, with the Approval Authority Matrix (APP-MATRIX-2026).

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit. The 15% limit she had in 2025 no longer applies. Also, the agreement's own §3.3 says contract eligibility doesn't change internal approval authority.

**8. Who would approve it.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. That would matter only after the customer is shown to qualify for 15% on NS-CLOUD.

**9. Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The discount rests on special terms that don't cover this product. Terms for one product don't carry over to another. The request needs commercial review, or an amendment adding NS-CLOUD terms, before anyone can approve it. If the request turns out to be for NS-500, the 15% would be allowed under the contract and would go to the VP Sales for approval (APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION).
