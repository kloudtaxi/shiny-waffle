No, not at 18%. The request asks for more than Acme's contract allows, so it needs to be rejected or sent back for new terms. A more senior approver can't fix this.

**Contract check**
1. **Request.** The request DR-9001 is for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048). The product is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, the date is 2026-09-23 and Sarah Chen submitted it. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so its basis is a contract term, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer.** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. This is a different company from "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091).
3. **Contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date, and it covers the NS-500. Section 3.3 says the contract doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
4. **Pricing exception.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers the NS-500 only, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a **maximum of 15% off list**. It replaced the earlier 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which expired 2025-03-31. I found no amendment allowing 18%.
   - The knowledge graph's record of the 15% limit was withdrawn in a data clean-up on 2026-09-29. The exception document itself still says 15%, and I relied on the document.
5. **Result.** 18% is 3 points above what Acme is entitled to (about $7,500 on $250,000 list).

**Who could approve (for reference)**
- The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to this date.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and can approve up to 10%.
- A discount above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres.
- However, his approval can't grant a discount the customer isn't eligible for.

**Mismatch in the request itself.** The knowledge base's copy of the CRM record shows DR-9001 at **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also says Acme asked for 15%. Only the record you pasted says 18%, so it's worth confirming which figure is correct.

**What you can do:**
- **At 15%:** Acme is eligible. This would be approved subject to sign-off by Michael Torres, because 15% is above Sarah's 10% limit.
- **At 18%:** Acme's terms would need to be amended first, with a new or changed exception from the Deal Desk. Only then could it go to Michael Torres for approval.
