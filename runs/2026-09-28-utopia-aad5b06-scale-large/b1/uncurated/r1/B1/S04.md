## Answer

**Yes, on 2025-09-23 Sarah Chen could have approved this 15% discount herself.** But there's a date problem: the CRM export in the knowledge base records DR-9001 as dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23. On that date she could not approve it alone.

**Decision for 2025-09-23, the date you gave:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, 15%, $37,500 off $250,000 list. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is ACME Manufacturing. In the ERP the customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active and covers NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to 15% on NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the earlier 10% exception, which expired 2025-03-31. 15% is within the limit, so eligibility is established. The exception grants no approval authority to anyone.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2025 policy that role can approve up to and including 15%, so 15% is within her own authority.
8. **Required approver:** only discounts above 15% need VP Sales, so no one else has to sign off.

**If the date is 2026-09-23, as the CRM export shows:** the Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix apply instead. Enterprise Account Executives can approve only up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Eligibility is still established, so the outcome would be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`, approved by Michael Torres.
