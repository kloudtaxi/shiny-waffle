## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy anything above 10% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres, to approve it.

**Request and customer.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 Industrial Controller: $37,500 off $250,000 list, for $212,500 net. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"). CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah owns the account.

**Is Acme entitled to 15%? Yes.**
- **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500, as set out in Schedule B.
- **Pricing exception:** Schedule B is exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. The request is for the right product, within the dates, and at the maximum, not over it.
- **Graph note:** Some structured records of the 15% maximum and the contract term were withdrawn from the knowledge graph on 2026-09-29. Nothing replaced them, and the signed contract and exception still say 15% and 2025–2028. I relied on those documents directly.

**Does Sarah have the authority? No.**
- **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covered the request date. It fully replaced the 2025 policy for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01.
- **Sarah's limit:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, who can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is over that.
- **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager.
- **Contract terms don't change this:** Section 3.3 of the agreement says Acme's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority. The exception says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."

**Why this differs from last year.** In her email to Michael on 2026-09-23, Sarah notes that Acme got 15% "last September." That was DR-8104 on 2025-09-16, under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that limit to 10%, so the earlier deal isn't a precedent for her approving this one.

**Next step:** Michael Torres approves DR-9001. The policy requires a written record of approvals above 10%, so his approval needs to be recorded in Northstar CRM.
