## Answer

No, DR-9001 can't be approved yet. The request depends on the "Acme Master Supply Agreement", and nothing in the knowledge base shows that agreement or its terms. The SOP says that when a request relies on a contract term that can't be found, you ask for the document before deciding.

**What the records show**

1. **Request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on NS-500 ($250,000 list price, $37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the reason "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". It is still pending, so the basis is a contract term, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing, is the same company as ERP customer C-1001, Acme Mfg. Holdings. Both are at 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, with the same DUNS number (04-812-7730). Don't confuse it with a different company, Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044, Akron).
3. **Product:** NS-500.
4. **Active contract:** No contract record for C-1001 was found. The "Acme Master Supply Agreement" exists in the graph only as a name, with no linked facts. The version of the knowledge base from before the latest changes doesn't have it either.
5. **Pricing exception:** None was found for NS-500 or any other product for this customer.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It fully replaces the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres (Approval Authority Matrix 2026, org chart as of 2026-09-01).

**Why earlier evidence doesn't settle it**
- The claim that the agreement covers 15% rests on belief, not the document. Sarah's email of 2026-09-23 says "I think their agreement covers it". Her own account plan says "I believe it's 15%… Worth confirming with Deal Desk."
- The earlier 15% request, DR-8104 (2025-09-16), used the same justification, but it was dated under the 2025 policy. That doesn't show the terms still apply in 2026, and special terms don't carry over.
- Even if the agreement is produced, Michael Torres would still have to sign off, because a contract allowing a discount doesn't widen Sarah's own authority.

**Next step:** Ask Sarah Chen or Deal Desk for the signed Acme Master Supply Agreement. Once it shows an NS-500 discount of at least 15% in effect on 2026-09-23, the request would become approve-with-authorization, signed by Michael Torres.
