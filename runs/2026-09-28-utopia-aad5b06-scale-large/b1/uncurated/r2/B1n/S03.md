## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this, and neither can a higher approver on the terms as they stand. Acme has no special pricing that covers NS-Cloud, so the request needs commercial review. **Outcome: REVIEW_REQUIRED.**

**The records don't match.** In the knowledge base's copy of the CRM discount log (`discount_requests.csv`), DR-9001 (CRM-2048, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, 15%, "Pending Approval") is for **NS-500** at a list value of **$250,000**. Your request is for **NS-CLOUD** at $120,000. I've decided it as you gave it, but the CRM record should be reconciled first.

**How I got there**

1. **The request.** DR-9001: 15% off NS-CLOUD for Acme, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing (Strategic tier), is the same customer as ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which trades as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The product.** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 is active (2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31). NS-CLOUD is one of the products Northstar supplies under it. But its only discount term (§3.2) is up to 15% on the **NS-500 controller**, per Schedule B. Nothing in it grants a discount on NS-CLOUD. §3.1 only says prices are list less any discount agreed in an order.
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, up to 15%) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only". It says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product". The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 is also NS-500 only and has expired. **No exception covers NS-CLOUD.**
6. **Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix 2026 are both in force for 2026.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% or less. 15% is above it.
8. **Approver band.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres. That doesn't solve the problem, though: the request depends on contract terms that don't cover this product, and a contract permitting a discount doesn't justify approval on its own.

**Next steps:** Commercial or Deal Desk review of NS-CLOUD pricing for Acme. That means either new or amended terms covering NS-CLOUD, or resubmitting as a standard competitive discount. As a standard discount, 15% would still need Michael Torres's approval (VP Sales), since it's above Sarah Chen's 10% limit. If the correct record is actually NS-500, as the CRM log shows, the Acme exception would cover it. That request would then need Michael Torres's approval (APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION).
