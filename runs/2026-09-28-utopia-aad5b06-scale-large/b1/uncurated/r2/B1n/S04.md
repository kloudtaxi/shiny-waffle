## Answer

**Yes, if the date really is 2025-09-23.** Sarah Chen could have approved this herself. The 2025 policy lets an Enterprise Account Executive approve discounts up to and including 15%, and Acme's pricing exception allows exactly 15% on the NS-500.

**The date doesn't match the CRM record.** In the knowledge base's CRM request log (`discount_requests.csv`), DR-9001 is dated **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23, and is still "Pending Approval". On 2026-09-23 the answer changes: the 2026 policy caps an Account Executive at 10%, so this 15% request would need the VP Sales, Michael Torres, to approve it. The contract eligibility would still hold. Please confirm the real request date before anyone acts on this. The decision below uses the date you gave.

**How I checked (as of 2025-09-23):**
1. **The request:** DR-9001, 15% off $250,000 list ($37,500 discount, $212,500 net), requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, owned by Sarah Chen. It's billed in the finance (ERP) system as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **The product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active, and it covers the NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception from that start date. The request is at the maximum, not over it, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it grants no approval authority.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is up to and including 15%. The request is within it. The approval rests on her own authority under the policy, not on the contract alone.
8. **Required approver:** Only discounts above 15% need the VP Sales, so nobody else has to sign off.
