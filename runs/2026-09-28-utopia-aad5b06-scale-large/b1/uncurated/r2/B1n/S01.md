## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on the NS-500, but it is above her own limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it.

**How I got there**

1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off the NS-500 for account CRM-2048 on 2026-09-23. On a $250,000 list price that is $37,500 off, for a net of $212,500. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, owned by Sarah Chen. The ERP bill-to is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), and the agreement is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. It is not the separate account Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23 and lists the NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The request is at the maximum, not above it, so Acme is eligible. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It also says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix were in force on 2026-09-23. They fully replace the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit under the 2026 policy is 10%. 15% is over that.
8. **Required approver:** The band for over 10% up to 20% is VP Sales, held by Michael Torres (EMP-200).

**Why the earlier approval doesn't count here:** Sarah's email to Michael says it's "what we did for them last September." That was DR-8104 on 2025-09-16, approved under the 2025 policy, when Enterprise Account Executives could approve up to 15%. That limit dropped to 10% on 2026-01-01, so the old approval doesn't carry over.

**Decision:** Approve with authorization. Michael Torres needs to approve it and record the approval in Northstar CRM, which is required for anything above 10%.
