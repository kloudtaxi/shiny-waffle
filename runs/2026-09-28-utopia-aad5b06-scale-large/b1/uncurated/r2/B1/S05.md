**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, who can approve up to and including 10%. 15% is above her limit.

**8. The required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres.

**9. Why last year's approval isn't a precedent.** The 15% approved in September 2025 (DR-8104) fell under the 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%. That policy was fully replaced for requests from 2026-01-01.

**10. Next steps.**

- If it shows that, the outcome becomes APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving.
- If it allows less than 15%, the outcome is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.
- If it doesn't cover NS-500 or this date, the outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.
- Whatever the contract says, Sarah can't approve 15% herself.
