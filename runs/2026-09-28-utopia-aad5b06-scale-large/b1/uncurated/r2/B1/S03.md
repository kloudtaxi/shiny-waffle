## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this.** The request can't be approved as it stands: it needs commercial review (**REVIEW_REQUIRED**). The Acme contract discount it relies on covers only the NS-500 controller, not NS-Cloud. Even if a 15% discount on NS-Cloud were justified, it would also be above Sarah's own approval limit.

**How I got there**

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off the NS-Cloud Operations Suite (NS-CLOUD) for Acme Manufacturing on 2026-09-23. Sarah Chen submitted it, and its stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". So it relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP system it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), per the 2026 account strategy. Sarah is the account owner.
3. **The product:** NS-CLOUD, the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, lists at $120,000.
4. **Contract:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It lists NS-CLOUD as a product Northstar supplies. But its discount term (§3.2) gives Acme up to 15% **on the NS-500 Industrial Controller only**, through the pricing exception in Schedule B. Nothing in the agreement gives a discount on NS-CLOUD. §3.3 also says the contract doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% and runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It says it covers the NS-500 only and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." It replaced the older 10% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which also covered only the NS-500. I found no exception for Acme on NS-CLOUD.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix both apply from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve discounts of up to 10%. 15% is above that.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres. But a higher approver can't fix a missing contract basis, and the contract doesn't make Sarah's limit any higher.

**Next step:** Send it to commercial review. They can either treat it as a standard-pricing discount with its own justification, which would then need Michael Torres's approval as VP Sales, or put a new or amended pricing exception for NS-CLOUD in place.

If NS-500 is actually correct, the answer changes to APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION: Acme is eligible for 15% under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, but Michael Torres still has to approve because Sarah's limit is 10%.
