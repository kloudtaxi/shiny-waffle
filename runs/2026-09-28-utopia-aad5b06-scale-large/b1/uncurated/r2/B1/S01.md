## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** Acme is eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500, but under the 2026 policy Sarah can only approve discounts up to 10%. A 15% discount needs approval from the VP Sales, **Michael Torres**.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 is a 15% discount on NS-500 for Acme ($250,000 list, $37,500 off, $212,500 net), dated 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on a contract term, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** The CRM account is CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing". The billing customer in the finance system (ERP) is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). The agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing, so they are the same customer.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500 under Schedule B. Section 3.3 says this eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) covers the NS-500 only, sets the maximum at 15% of list price, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which expired on 2025-03-31. 15% is within the maximum, so eligibility is established. The exception also states that it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix both apply from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (employee list and org chart), and that role can approve up to 10%. Her 15% self-approval of an identical request in 2025 (DR-8104) is not a precedent: the 2025 policy let account executives approve up to 15%, but the 2026 policy replaced it in full.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200). The matrix also requires approvals above 10% to be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**One data point to note:** On 2026-09-29, a batch of curation corrections withdrew several knowledge-graph facts, including the exception's 15% maximum and the agreement's term. The source documents themselves haven't changed and still say 15% and 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so I relied on them.
