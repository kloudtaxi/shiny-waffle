**No. We can't offer 18%, and a higher approver can't fix that.** The request relies on the Acme contract terms, and those terms cap the NS-500 discount at **15%**. 18% is above what the customer is eligible for, so it needs new or amended terms, not just a sign-off.

**The request doesn't match our records.** You quoted DR-9001 at 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). The copy of Northstar CRM in the knowledge base shows DR-9001 at **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), with the same account, product, requestor, date and justification. Someone should confirm which figure is correct. I've assessed the 18% you asked about.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 from Sarah Chen, dated 2026-09-23. It's for 18% off NS-500 at $250,000 list. It relies on contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same company as ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date and covers NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) covers NS-500 only, with a **maximum of 15% off list**, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). It sets the customer's eligibility only and gives no employee any approval authority. **18% exceeds that 15% cap.**
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix were in force on 2026-09-23.
7. **Requestor's authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10% on her own.
8. **Required approver:** the band for more than 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. But approval authority only matters once the customer is eligible, and at 18% they aren't.

**What can happen next:**
- **Stay at 15% or less:** Acme is eligible for that, but it's still above Sarah's 10% limit. Michael Torres (VP Sales) would need to approve it and record the approval in CRM. That would be approval with authorization.
- **Keep 18%:** this needs the Acme exception or agreement amended through commercial review with the Deal Desk. The current exception was approved by David Morgan (CRO).
