## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001, and under the SOP the request can't be approved as submitted either. Outcome: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.** The request cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but nothing in that agreement gives a discount on NS-CLOUD.

**Walking through the SOP (SOP-SALES-014):**

1. **Request:** DR-9001, 15% on NS-CLOUD ($120,000 list, $18,000 off, $102,000 net), from sarah.chen@northstar.example, dated 2026-09-23. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD, the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, $120,000 list price.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. NS-CLOUD is on its product list (§2), but that only means Northstar supplies it. The agreement's only discount term (§3.2) is up to 15% on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, via Schedule B. §3.3 also says the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers NS-CLOUD. Schedule B (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) is for "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 was also NS-500 only, and it expired 2025-03-31. Sarah's own 2026 account plan says Acme is still only *evaluating* NS-Cloud.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and Approval Authority Matrix APP-MATRIX-2026, both valid 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101), so she can approve up to 10%. The 15% ceiling for Account Executives was in the 2025 policy, which the 2026 policy replaced. 15% is above her limit whatever product it's for.
8. **Required approver:** More than 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200). But a higher approver doesn't fix the missing eligibility.
9. **Decision:** The Acme deal terms don't carry over to NS-CLOUD. Deal Desk or Commercial needs to review it. The options are to treat it as a standard-pricing request (15% would then need Michael Torres as VP Sales) or to set up a new or amended NS-CLOUD exception.

**The CRM record doesn't match the knowledge base.** The CRM export in the knowledge base (discount_requests.csv) lists DR-9001 as **NS-500**, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount, with the same date, requestor and reason. You gave me NS-CLOUD and $120,000. Please check which is correct before recording anything. If it's really NS-500, Schedule B does cover 15% on that date. The outcome would then be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`: Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to approve it, not Sarah.
