> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23? ## Answer

**Yes. If the request date really is 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved this discount herself.** But the CRM log dates DR-9001 **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23, and a different pricing policy applies in 2026.

**The date problem.** In `discount_requests.csv`, DR-9001 (CRM-2048, NS-500, 15%, sarah.chen@) is dated **2026-09-23** and is still "Pending Approval". The 2025-dated request for the same deal is **DR-8104** (2025-09-16), which was already approved. The decision below uses the date you gave. Please confirm the date against the CRM, because it changes the answer.

**Assessment for 2025-09-23:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, 15% off $250,000 list (net $212,500). It relies on contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as the ERP bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen owns the account.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active. It lists NS-500. §3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off NS-500 under Schedule B. §3.3 says that eligibility does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers NS-500 only, with a maximum of 15%, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. The 15% request is at the maximum, not above it, so Acme is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
7. **Authority:** Under that policy, Enterprise Account Executives may approve discounts "up to and including 15%". Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (per the employee list and org chart), so 15% is within her own authority. Her authority comes from the policy, not from the contract.
8. **Required approver:** No higher approver is needed. The policy only requires VP Sales for discounts above 15%.

**If the date is 2026-09-23 (as the CRM shows):** Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. There, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve only up to 10%, and 10–20% needs the VP Sales, **Michael Torres**. Acme's contract and exception would still make it eligible for 15%. The outcome would be **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**: Sarah Chen could not approve it herself, and Michael Torres would have to.
