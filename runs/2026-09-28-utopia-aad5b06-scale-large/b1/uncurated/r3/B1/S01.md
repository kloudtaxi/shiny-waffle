## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount on the NS-500, but under the 2026 policy that discount needs sign-off from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 is for CRM account CRM-2048, SKU NS-500, 15% off a $250,000 list price ($37,500 off, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen entered it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: the justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is "Acme Manufacturing", and Sarah Chen owns the account. In the ERP system the customer is "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). The agreement confirms these are the same company: it's made with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing, at the same Milwaukee address. This isn't "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091), which is a different customer.

**3. The product** is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Active contract: yes.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers the NS-500. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500 under Schedule B.

**5. Pricing exception: yes, for this product.** Schedule B is exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It covers the NS-500 only, allows up to 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. The 15% requested equals the maximum, so Acme qualifies.

**6. Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and the matching 2026 approval matrix. The 2025 policy, which let account executives approve up to 15%, has been replaced.

**7. Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit. Neither the contract nor the exception changes this: MSA §3.3 says the customer's eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority, and the exception says it gives no employee approval authority.

**8. Required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, which is Michael Torres (EMP-200).

Two things to note:
- **Precedent:** Sarah's email says Acme got 15% "last September" (DR-8104, 2025-09-16). That was dated under the 2025 policy, when account executives could approve up to 15%. It doesn't carry over to 2026.
- **Knowledge-base records:** Some summary records about the exception's 15% cap, the agreement's dates and reporting lines have been withdrawn. The documents they came from are no longer in the knowledge base. My conclusion rests on the original agreement, exception, policy, matrix and employee list, which are still there.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Michael Torres needs to approve, and because the discount is over 10%, the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
