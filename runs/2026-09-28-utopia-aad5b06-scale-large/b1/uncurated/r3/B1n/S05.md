3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, list price $250,000. It is not NS-500-EQU or NS-500-SOF.
4. **Active contract:** I found none. The "Acme Master Supply Agreement" exists in the graph only as a name, with no facts attached. No contract document with terms or dates turned up in any search.
5. **Pricing exception:** I found none for NS-500. Everything written about it is second-hand and hedged:

   - Her email to Michael says "I think their agreement covers it."

7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10%. So 15% is beyond what she can approve.
8. **Required approver:** Under the Approval Authority Matrix 2026, discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres. Anything over 10% also needs the approval recorded in CRM.

**Next step:** Get the signed Acme agreement or pricing exception from Sarah or Deal Desk. It needs to name NS-500, a maximum discount of at least 15%, and dates that cover 2026-09-23. If it does, the outcome becomes APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving. If it's expired, doesn't include NS-500, or caps the discount below 15%, the request goes to commercial review or gets rejected.
