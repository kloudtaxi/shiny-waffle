## Answer

No. We can't offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500. Acme's pricing exception for the NS-500 caps the discount at 15% of list. Having a senior person sign off doesn't change that. Going above 15% would need new or amended terms with Acme.

**First, the requested amount doesn't match our records.** The request you pasted says 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). The DR-9001 row in our discount request records says 15% ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), still Pending Approval. Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also says Acme asked for "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." Someone should confirm which figure is right. The answer below is for 18%, as you asked.

1. **Request:** DR-9001, account CRM-2048, NS-500, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: the justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as the billing account "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), doing business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen owns the account. It is a different customer from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers the NS-500. Section 3.3 says the contract doesn't change our internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, allows a maximum of 15% off list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). I found no amendment raising the 15% cap. **18% is above what Acme is eligible for.**
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 approval table, both in effect all of 2026.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to 10%. The 15% limit she had under the 2025 policy no longer applies.
8. **Approver band:** 18% sits in the "over 10% and up to 20%" band, which needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres. His approval can't fix a request that goes beyond Acme's eligibility, though.

**Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE** at 18%. Either:
- bring the request down to 15% or less. It would then be within Acme's eligibility but above Sarah's 10% authority, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would need to approve it and the approval must be recorded in the CRM; or
- negotiate new or amended terms with Acme, through the Deal Desk and Legal, before any discount above 15%.
