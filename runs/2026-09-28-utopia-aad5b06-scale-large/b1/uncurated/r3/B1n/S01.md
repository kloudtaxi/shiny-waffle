> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but under the 2026 policy she can only approve up to 10%. The request is valid; it needs sign-off from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 for account CRM-2048. List price is $250,000, the discount is $37,500 and the net is $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on a contract term: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. In the ERP it is billed to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). The Master Supply Agreement is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing, at the same Milwaukee address. So it's the same customer. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a separate account.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-500 is one of the products it covers. Section 3.3 says Acme's contract pricing "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025) covers NS-500 only. It allows up to 15% off list, is valid from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and was approved by CRO David Morgan. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ended on 2025-03-31. The requested 15% is at the maximum, not over it, so Acme is eligible. The exception says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, both effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The 2026 policy fully replaces the 2025 one.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (employee record and org chart), so her limit is 10%. The 15% request exceeds it.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales. That is Michael Torres (EMP-200), Sarah's manager. Because the discount is over 10%, the approval must also be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Why last year's approval doesn't apply:** In her 2026-09-23 email, Sarah says 15% is "what we did for them last September." That was DR-8104, which she approved herself on 2025-09-16. She could do that because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy cut that limit to 10%. Also, the contract making Acme eligible doesn't give her authority to approve it.
