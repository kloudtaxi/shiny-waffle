1. **Request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-500. The list value is $250,000 and the discount is $37,500. Sarah Chen raised it, citing contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.

3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active and covers NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) allows up to 15% on NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which expired 2025-03-31. The requested 15% is exactly at the maximum, so the customer is eligible.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, in force for all of 2025. An Enterprise Account Executive may approve discounts up to and including 15%. Anything above 15% needs the VP Sales.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101, reporting to Michael Torres). 15% is within her limit.
8. **Result:** The customer is eligible and the discount is within her authority, so she can approve it. This matches DR-8104, an identical request she made on 2025-09-16, which is recorded as Approved.

- The outcome would then be `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`, with **Michael Torres (VP Sales)** as the approver. Sarah Chen could not approve it herself.
