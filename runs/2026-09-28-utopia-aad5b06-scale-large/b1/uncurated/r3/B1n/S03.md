## Answer

**No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself, and it shouldn't go to a higher approver yet either. It needs commercial review (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`)**, because Acme's special pricing doesn't cover NS-Cloud.

**One thing to check first:** the record you gave doesn't match the knowledge base's copy of Northstar CRM (`discount_requests.csv`). There, DR-9001 is for **NS-500** (list $250,000, 15%, discount $37,500, net $212,500), not NS-CLOUD at $120,000. Everything else matches: account CRM-2048, Sarah Chen, 2026-09-23, same justification, Pending Approval. I've decided on the record you gave (NS-CLOUD), but the CRM record needs to be corrected either way.

**How I got there**
1. **The request:** DR-9001, 15% off NS-CLOUD (list $120,000, discount $18,000), dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In ERP it's Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **The product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-CLOUD is one of its listed products. But its only discount term (§3.2) gives up to 15% on the **NS-500 only**, through Schedule B. §3.3 says the customer's eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (15% maximum, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 is also NS-500 only and ended 2025-03-31. I found nothing covering NS-CLOUD. Sarah's own 2026 account plan says Acme is only "evaluating NS-Cloud," and her note about the 15% refers to NS-500 pricing.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, both in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** As an Enterprise Account Executive, she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit. (The 15% limit she had under the 2025 policy no longer applies.)
8. **Approver:** Anything over 10% and up to 20% goes to the VP Sales, Michael Torres. But a higher approver doesn't fix a product the special terms don't cover.
9. **Decision:** The request relies on contract terms, and none of them covers this product. That's `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. The NS-500 terms don't carry over to NS-CLOUD. To go ahead, you'd need either new or amended terms for NS-CLOUD, or to resubmit as standard pricing. Under standard pricing, anything up to 10% is within Sarah's authority, and 10–20% needs Michael Torres's approval.

If the CRM record is right and the request is actually for NS-500, the answer changes to `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`. 15% is within the exception's maximum, but still above Sarah's 10%, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) would need to approve it.
