## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this, and nobody should approve it yet. The 15% discount relies on the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement only gives the 15% on the NS-500 controller, not on NS-Cloud. The request needs commercial review first. Separately, 15% is above Sarah's own 10% limit.

**One thing to check first:** the knowledge base's own copy of DR-9001 (discount_requests.csv) doesn't match what you sent. It shows DR-9001 as **NS-500**, list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500, with the same requestor, date, justification and "Pending" status. The version you sent says NS-CLOUD, list $120,000, discount $18,000, net $102,000. I decided the request as you gave it (NS-CLOUD). Please confirm which product is actually being ordered, because the answer changes (see the end).

**How I got there**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, 15% on NS-CLOUD, dated 2026-09-23, requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on contract terms ("Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"), not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** The account CRM-2048, "Acme Manufacturing", is the same customer as ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which trades as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah owns the account. Don't confuse it with Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091), which is a different customer.
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, list price $120,000.
4. **Active contract:** Yes. ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and lists NS-CLOUD as a product it covers.
5. **Pricing exception:** None for NS-CLOUD. Section 3.2 of the agreement gives up to 15% "on the NS-500 Industrial Controller" only, as set out in Schedule B. Schedule B (exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15) says: "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only… does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) was also NS-500 only, and ended 2025-03-31. I found no amendment or exception that covers NS-CLOUD. Having NS-CLOUD on the agreement's product list doesn't give it a discount.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% or less.
8. **Who would approve:** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres. Section 3.3 of the agreement also says the customer's contract terms don't change Northstar's internal approval authority.

**If the product is actually NS-500** (as the CRM record shows), Acme is eligible for up to 15%, so the request qualifies. It would then be approvable only with Michael Torres's sign-off as VP Sales, not by Sarah.
