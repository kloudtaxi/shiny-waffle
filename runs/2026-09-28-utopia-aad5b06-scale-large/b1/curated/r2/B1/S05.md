Both records share DUNS number 04-812-7730 and the Milwaukee address, and the account plan links them. It is a different customer from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091 / C-1044, Akron).

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less. At 15%, the request is above her authority.

**9. Decision: REQUEST_EVIDENCE.** The SOP says that when a request relies on a contract term that can't be located, you ask for the document before deciding. Even if the contract turns out to allow 15%, that wouldn't let Sarah approve it herself. It would still need Michael Torres's approval (APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION). If the contract doesn't cover the NS-500, or doesn't cover this date, the request goes to commercial review. If it caps the discount below 15%, the request must be rejected or escalated for new or amended terms.

**Next step:** ask Sarah Chen or Deal Desk for the signed agreement, and any amendment, showing the NS-500 pricing term, its maximum discount and its effective dates.
