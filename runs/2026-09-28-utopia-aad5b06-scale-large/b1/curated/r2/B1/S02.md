## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** Acme's contract caps its NS-500 discount at 15%, and 18% is above that. A more senior sign-off can't fix this. It would need new or amended contract terms.

**The request.** DR-9001: Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), NS-500, 18% off $250,000 list, dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. It relies on "contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the basis is the contract terms.

**The numbers don't match the stored record.** The copy of the CRM request table in our records shows DR-9001 at **15.0%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net), not 18% ($45,000 / $205,000). Sarah's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also says Acme "are asking for 15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." Someone should confirm which figure is actually on the request.

**What I checked:**
1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is not the separate account "Acme Industrial Supply Co." (CRM-2091).
2. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
3. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for "up to **15%**" on the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this eligibility does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a maximum of 15% of list. It replaces the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). I found no amendment that raises the cap.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix. Account executives can approve up to 10%, the VP Sales from over 10% up to 20%, and the Chief Revenue Officer above 20%.
6. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. Under the matrix, 18% would need VP Sales Michael Torres. But authority doesn't matter here, because the discount is above what the contract allows.

**What to do:** Send it back. Either offer Acme 15% or less, or get Legal and the Deal Desk to agree amended terms. If the request really is 15% (as the stored record and the email suggest), Acme is eligible for it. It would then go through as "approve with authorization": still above Sarah's 10% limit, so Michael Torres (VP Sales) must approve it and the approval must be recorded in CRM.
