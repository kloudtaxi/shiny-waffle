## Answer

**Yes. If the request is dated 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved the 15% discount herself.** But the CRM in the knowledge base dates DR-9001 **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23. On the 2026 date she could not approve it on her own.

**The date problem.** The copy of DR-9001 in `discount_requests.csv` shows `request_date` 2026-09-23. Every other field matches what you sent, and the status is "Pending Approval." A nearly identical request, DR-8104, was dated 2025-09-16 and is marked approved. The approver's name is cut off in the search excerpt; it starts with "s" and may be Sarah Chen. That's probably the "15%… we approved for them last September" that Sarah mentions in her 2026 account plan. Please confirm the real date before acting on this.

**For 2025-09-23, the date you asked about:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, CRM-2048, NS-500, 15%, $250,000 list price (net $212,500), requested by Sarah Chen. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the ERP customer "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001), which trades as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the date. It lists NS-500 as a supplied product.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that agreement. It covers **NS-500 only**, allows up to **15%**, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaces the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. So 15% is within Acme's allowed maximum.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). Enterprise Account Executives may approve up to **and including 15%**. Above 15% needs the VP Sales.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive (employees.csv and org chart). 15% is within her own limit, so no one else has to sign off. The exception itself says it gives no one approval authority; her authority comes from the 2025 policy.
8. **Outcome:** APPROVE.

One caveat: the org chart is dated 2026-09-01 and the employee list has no date. So her title in 2025 is inferred, not directly shown for that date.

**If the date is really 2026-09-23 (as the CRM shows):** The customer can still get 15%, because the agreement and exception run to 2028. But the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix cap Enterprise Account Executives at **10%**. A 15% discount falls in the over-10%-to-20% band, which needs the **VP Sales, Michael Torres** (Sarah Chen's manager). The outcome would then be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, not APPROVE.
