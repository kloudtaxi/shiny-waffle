## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself.** The customer is eligible for the 15% discount, but it is above her own approval limit. DR-9001 needs sign-off from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off list on an NS-500, dated 2026-09-23. It was raised by Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive. The list value is $250,000, the discount $37,500 and the net $212,500. The stated basis is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on a contract term.

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). The 2026 account strategy links the two IDs, and the agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing".

**3. The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. It covers the NS-500. Clause 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500. Clause 3.3 says that eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."

**5. The pricing exception.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only. It sets a maximum of 15% of list and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception from 2025-04-01. It "establishes commercial eligibility only" and gives no Northstar employee approval authority. The request asks for exactly 15%, so it is within the limit.

**6. The policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) and fully replaces the 2025 policy.

**7. Sarah's authority.** Under the 2026 policy, an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%. So 15% is above her limit. Under the 2025 policy she could approve up to 15%. That is probably why a similar request last September (DR-8104, 15% on the NS-500) was approved, and why her email to Michael assumes the same will happen again. That limit no longer applies.

**8. The required approver.** The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix gives discounts above 10% and up to 20% to the VP Sales. The employee records and the organization chart show that is Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager. Approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Decision:** Acme is eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500, and 15% does not exceed that. Under the SOP, the contract permitting the discount does not let Sarah approve it. Michael Torres, VP Sales, must approve it and record the approval in CRM.
