> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23? ## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to up to 15% on the NS-500, so the discount is allowed. But under the 2026 policy she can only approve up to 10%. **Outcome: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** Michael Torres, VP Sales, has to approve DR-9001.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 is 15% off a $250,000 list price: a $37,500 discount, $212,500 net. Sarah Chen raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is "ACME Manufacturing." In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which trades as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah's 2026 account plan links the two records, and the contract names both.

**3. The product.** SKU NS-500, the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Active contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Clause 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500, "as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B." Clause 3.3 says this "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is Schedule B to that contract. It covers the NS-500 only, sets a maximum of 15% of list price, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. It says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and gives no employee authority to approve. The request is for exactly 15%, so it is within eligibility, not above it.

**6. Policy in force.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix 2026 both apply from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. The bands are:
- up to 10%: Enterprise Account Executive
- over 10% and up to 20%: VP Sales
- over 20%: Chief Revenue Officer

**7. Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. At 15% the request is over her limit.

**8. Required approver.** 15% falls in the VP Sales band. Michael Torres holds that role, and Sarah reports to him (reporting lines as of 2026-09-01). Under the matrix, approvals above 10% must be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Why her earlier approval doesn't carry over:** Sarah approved an identical 15% NS-500 request for Acme herself on 2025-09-16 (DR-8104). That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy fully replaced it and cut that limit to 10%. The contract doesn't change who can approve, so her 2025 approval is no precedent now.
