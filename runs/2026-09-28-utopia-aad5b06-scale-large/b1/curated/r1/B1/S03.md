## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve this. The outcome is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The request relies on Acme's contract terms, but those terms cover only the NS-500, not NS-Cloud. Even if they did cover it, 15% is above Sarah's own limit.

**Check before acting: the request details don't match the CRM record.** In the knowledge base's copy of the Northstar CRM export (`discount_requests.csv`), DR-9001 is for **NS-500**, list $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500. You gave it as **NS-CLOUD**, list $120,000. I decided on the request as you gave it (NS-CLOUD). If the correct product is NS-500, the answer changes (see the end).

**Steps**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, 15% off $120,000 list ($18,000 discount), dated 2026-09-23, from sarah.chen@northstar.example. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the basis is special contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** The CRM account is Acme Manufacturing, owned by Sarah Chen. It is the same company as Acme Mfg. Holdings (ERP customer C-1001), which trades as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-CLOUD (NS-Cloud Operations Suite).
4. **Contract:** Active. The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and lists NS-CLOUD among the products supplied. However, its only discount term (§3.2) gives up to 15% **on the NS-500 Industrial Controller**, via Schedule B. It gives no discount for NS-CLOUD. §3.3 adds that the contract doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** None covers NS-CLOUD. The only current one, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, is for the "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only" and "does not apply to any other Northstar product". The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 was also NS-500 only and expired on 2025-03-31.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the Approval Authority Matrix 2026, both in force for all of 2026.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres (Sarah's manager).

**Decision:** The contract discount for NS-500 can't be carried over to NS-CLOUD, and a higher approver can't fix that. The request needs commercial review. Two routes:
- Resubmit it on a standard basis (e.g. competitive). At 15% it would still need Michael Torres (VP Sales) to approve. For comparison, the CRM export shows DR-7819, a 15% NS-CLOUD discount for another account, approved by Torres in May 2026.
- Or negotiate new terms that cover NS-CLOUD.

**If the product really is NS-500** (as the CRM export shows): Acme is eligible for up to 15% under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, so 15% is within the limit. The outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres (VP Sales) approving, because it's still above Sarah's 10% limit.
