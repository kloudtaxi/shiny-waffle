## Answer

**Yes.** If the request is dated 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved the 15% discount herself. One caveat first: the knowledge base's copy of the CRM record dates DR-9001 **2026-09-23**, not 2025-09-23, and the date decides which policy applies.

**How I checked (per SOP-SALES-014)**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 is for 15% off NS-500. List price is $250,000, the discount is $37,500 and the net is $212,500. The requestor is Sarah Chen. The basis is special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as the ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which trades as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different company from CRM-2091 "Acme Industrial Supply Co."
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller, with a list price of $250,000. That matches the request.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and covers NS-500, so it was active on the date. Its clause 3.3 says contractual eligibility does not change internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement) allows up to 15% off NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ended 2025-03-31). 15% is exactly the maximum, so Acme is eligible for this discount.
6. **Policy on 2025-09-23:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applies. Enterprise Account Executives may approve up to and including 15%; above 15% needs the VP Sales.
7. **Her authority:** Sarah Chen (EMP-101) is an Enterprise Account Executive and the account owner for CRM-2048. 15% is within her limit.
8. **Required approver:** No one else is needed.

Approval here rests on her own authority under the policy, not just on the contract allowing 15%. That matches the SOP rule that a contract term alone doesn't justify approval. An identical request, DR-8104 on 2025-09-16, is recorded as approved, which fits this reading.

**About the date:** If DR-9001 is really dated 2026-09-23, the result changes. Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix limit Enterprise Account Executives to 10%. Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Acme would still be eligible for 15%, but Sarah could not approve it herself. The outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres as approver. The record is still "Pending Approval," so please confirm the correct date in the CRM before closing it.
