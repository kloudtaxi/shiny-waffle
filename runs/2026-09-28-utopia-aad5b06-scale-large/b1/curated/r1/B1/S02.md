## Answer

**No, not at 18%.** Acme's agreement lets it get at most 15% off the NS-500, so 18% goes beyond what the customer is entitled to. A more senior sign-off can't fix that. It would need new or amended contract terms.

**Your record doesn't match ours.** The CRM export in the knowledge base has DR-9001 at **15.0%**: $37,500 off, net $212,500, status "Pending Approval." Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres on 2026-09-23 also says Acme is "asking for 15% off list, which is what we did for them last September." Your version says 18% ($45,000 off, net $205,000). Please confirm which figure is right. I decided on 18% as you asked; the 15% case is at the end.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001, NS-500, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so the request relies on the contract's special terms.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP bill-to Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). The agreement names it as Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date and covers the NS-500. Its section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 applies to the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, with a **maximum of 15% off list**. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), and I found no later amendment. **18% is above that maximum.**
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix, both covering 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. Under the 2025 policy that limit was 15%, which is presumably how the 15% deal (DR-8104) was approved last September. That limit no longer applies.
8. **Required approver:** 18% falls in the over-10%-up-to-20% band, which is the VP Sales role, held by Michael Torres. Even his approval wouldn't cover the 3 points above what Acme is entitled to.

**If the right figure is 15%:** it's within Acme's entitlement but above Sarah's 10% limit. The outcome would be approval with sign-off from Michael Torres as VP Sales, and it must be recorded in CRM.
