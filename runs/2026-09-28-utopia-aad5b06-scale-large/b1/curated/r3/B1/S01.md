## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001 herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% on the NS-500, but under the 2026 policy she can only approve up to 10%. **Michael Torres, VP Sales, must approve it.**

**How I reached this**

1. **The request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% ($37,500) off a $250,000 list value on the NS-500, requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23. It relies on contract pricing under the Acme Master Supply Agreement.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), per the 2026 account strategy document. The agreement confirms these are the same company.
3. **The product.** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Section 3.2 makes Acme eligible for up to 15% on the NS-500. Section 3.3 says this eligibility "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority."
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) covers the NS-500 only, with a 15% maximum, and runs to 2028-03-31. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. The request is exactly at the 15% limit, so Acme is eligible. The exception itself says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee."
6. **The policy.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and its Approval Authority Matrix apply to requests from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. They fully replace the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **The approver.** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, currently Michael Torres (Sarah's manager). Because this approval is above 10%, it must also be recorded in Northstar CRM.

**Two things to watch for**
- Sarah approved an identical 15% NS-500 discount for Acme herself in September 2025 (DR-8104). That was allowed then, because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. It doesn't carry over to 2026.
- Sarah's 2026 account plan says she only "believes" the Acme rate is 15%. That doesn't matter here, because the signed agreement and the exception both confirm it.
