## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this. The request needs commercial review because the contract terms it relies on don't cover the NS-Cloud Operations Suite.

**1. The request.** DR-9001 asks for 15% off NS-CLOUD ($120,000 list, $18,000 off, $102,000 net). The requestor is Sarah Chen and the date is 2026-09-23. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on special contract terms, not standard pricing.
- **The records don't match.** The knowledge base's copy of DR-9001 (discount_requests.csv) shows product **NS-500**, $250,000 list, $37,500 discount. The record you gave shows NS-CLOUD. The product needs to be confirmed in the CRM system. I've decided on the product as you gave it.

**2. The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings". The agreement names Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing.

**3. The product.** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite, listed at $120,000.

**4. The contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. NS-CLOUD is on its list of supplied products (§2). But the only discount term (§3.2) is up to 15% on the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**. For everything else, §3.1 sets list price less any discount agreed in an order. The agreement gives no discount eligibility for NS-CLOUD.

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B) covers "NS-500 Industrial Controller (NS-500) only". It says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 was also NS-500 only, and it expired on 2025-03-31. No exception for NS-CLOUD exists. Sarah Chen's own 2026 account plan only mentions NS-500 pricing, and she wasn't sure of the rate ("I believe it's 15%… worth confirming with Deal Desk").

**6–8. Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix were in force on the request date.
- Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. 15% is above it.
- Discounts over 10% up to 20% fall in the VP Sales band. That role is held by Michael Torres.
- The 2025 policy's 15% limit for account executives no longer applies.
- §3.3 of the agreement says the customer's eligibility doesn't change internal approval authority.

**9. Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The discount depends on contract terms that don't cover this product. Even if Michael Torres approved it, the discount still wouldn't be covered: the NS-500 terms don't carry over to NS-CLOUD. It needs commercial review (Deal Desk), and new or amended terms if a 15% NS-CLOUD discount is wanted. Without those, anything above 10% would be a standard-pricing request needing VP Sales approval.

If the knowledge base's version is correct and the request is actually for NS-500, the answer changes to APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION. 15% is within the exception's 15% maximum, but Michael Torres (VP Sales) would still have to approve it.
