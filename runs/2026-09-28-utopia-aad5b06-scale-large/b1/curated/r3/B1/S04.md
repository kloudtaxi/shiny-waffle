## Answer

**Yes. On 2025-09-23, Sarah Chen could have approved the 15% discount herself. But the CRM record for DR-9001 is dated 2026-09-23, not 2025-09-23, and under the 2026 policy the answer is no.**

**How the 2025-09-23 answer was reached**
1. **Request basis:** The request relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). The Master Supply Agreement says Acme Mfg. Holdings does business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active and it covers the NS-500. Section 3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility does not change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to the agreement) allows up to 15% off list, for the NS-500 only, from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. So 15% is within Acme's eligibility.
6. **Policy in force:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, covering 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Enterprise Account Executives may approve up to and including 15%. Above 15% needs VP Sales.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive (EMP-101) and owns the account. 15% is at her limit, so it is within her authority.
8. **Outcome:** APPROVE. Eligibility comes from the exception and authority comes from the policy. The contract alone is not the reason.

**The date conflict**
- **What the CRM file shows:** `discount_requests.csv` lists DR-9001 with a request date of 2026-09-23, status Pending Approval.
- **Under the 2026 rules:** The 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix limit Account Executives to 10%. Anything above 10% up to 20% needs VP Sales, which is Michael Torres. The exception still covers 15% in 2026. So the result would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION: Torres approves, not Sarah. Please confirm which date is correct before recording a decision.
