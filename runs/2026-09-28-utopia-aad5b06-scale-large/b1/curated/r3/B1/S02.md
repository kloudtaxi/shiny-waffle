No. At 18%, DR-9001 goes over Acme's contract ceiling. Acme is eligible for at most 15% off the NS-500, so the outcome is **REJECT_OR_ESCALATE**. Getting a more senior approver to sign off won't fix this. 18% would need new or amended contract terms.

**The records disagree on the requested discount.** The request you pasted says 18% ($45,000 off, $205,000 net). The copy of DR-9001 in the knowledge base (`discount_requests.csv`) says **15%** ($37,500 off, $212,500 net, still Pending Approval). Sarah's email to Michael Torres the same morning also says Acme is "asking for 15% off list". Someone needs to confirm which figure is correct. I decided on the 18% you asked about.

**How I got there:**
1. **Request:** DR-9001 for NS-500, $250,000 at list, dated 2026-09-23, from Sarah Chen. It relies on "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on special contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same company as ERP bill-to "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (C-1001). The contract names Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing.
3. **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on 2026-09-23. Clause 3.2 caps NS-500 discounts at **15%**. Clause 3.3 says the contract doesn't change who inside the company can approve discounts.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and sets a **maximum of 15% off list**. It replaced the older 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) from 2025-04-01. I found no amendment or correction that raises the cap. **18% is 3 points over it.**
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 approval matrix: sales reps (Enterprise Account Executives) can approve up to 10%. Over 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales. Over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. She can't approve either 18% or 15% herself. (Last September's 15% under DR-8104 fell under the 2025 policy, which allowed reps up to 15%. That no longer applies.)
8. **Approver band:** 18% falls in the VP Sales band (Michael Torres). But he can't approve a discount the contract doesn't allow.

**Next steps:**
- If Acme really needs 18%, the contract terms have to change first: an amended or new exception approved commercially. The original exception was approved by the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan.
- If the correct figure is 15% (as the stored CRM record and Sarah's email say), it's within Acme's cap. It would then be **APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION**, with Michael Torres (VP Sales) approving because it's above Sarah's 10% limit.
