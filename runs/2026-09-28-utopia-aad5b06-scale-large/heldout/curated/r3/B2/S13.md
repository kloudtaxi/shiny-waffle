- **Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings** is ERP customer C-1001. Its account owner is Sarah Chen.

The graph shows no link between the two. CRM-2091 isn't linked to either one, so I'm relying on your wording that this request is for Acme Industrial Supply.

**3. Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4–5. Contract and exception:** Both exist, but both are for Acme Manufacturing / Acme Mfg. Holdings:
- The **Master Supply Agreement** runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The graph doesn't link it to Acme Industrial Supply Co.
- The **Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception** allows up to 15% off NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% until 2025-03-31. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20.

- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval, and that's Michael Torres.
- Even with eligibility established, this request would need his sign-off. Neither the contract nor the exception gives any employee approval authority.

**9. Decision:** REVIEW_REQUIRED. A VP Sales approval can't make up for missing eligibility. There are two ways forward:
- **If the CRM account was chosen by mistake** and the deal is really for Acme Manufacturing (Sarah Chen's own account), correct the request. It would then be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION by Michael Torres.
