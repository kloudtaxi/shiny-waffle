## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen could not have approved DR-9103 herself.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force on 2025-09-23. It lets an Enterprise Account Executive approve discounts up to and including 15%. The request is for 22%, so it needs VP Sales approval (Michael Torres).

**What I checked**

1. **Request:** DR-9103 asks for 22% off NS-EDGE (list $85,000, discount $18,700, net $66,300). Sarah Chen raised it on 2025-09-23 with the justification "Competitive". It doesn't cite any contract term or pricing exception, so I treated it as standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** The CRM account is Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), and Sarah Chen is its account owner. The ERP customer appears to be C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (Milwaukee, WI; Net 45). The knowledge base doesn't state that link directly. I matched them because both carry the same Acme NS-500 discount terms.
3. **Product:** NS-EDGE, the NS-Edge Monitoring Package.
4. **Contract:** A Master Supply Agreement is effective from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. Nothing I found gives it any discount term for NS-EDGE.
5. **Pricing exception:** The Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and allows up to 15%. It applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller and does not extend to other products, so it doesn't cover NS-EDGE. It also says it gives no Northstar employee approval authority. The request doesn't rely on it anyway.
6. **Policy:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. The 2026 policy, which lowers the Account Executive limit to 10% and adds a CRO band above 20%, doesn't apply to a 2025 request.
7. **Authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 15%. At 22%, she is over it.
8. **Approver:** Under the 2025 policy, anything above 15% needs VP Sales, and Michael Torres holds that role. The 2025 policy as recorded has no higher band.

**Caveats**
- Michael Torres's VP Sales role has no start or end date on record. His reporting line (Sarah Chen reports to him) is only recorded from 2026-09-01. Confirm he was VP Sales in September 2025.
- If the "Competitive" justification actually depends on a special Acme term for NS-EDGE, none is on record. The request would then go to commercial review instead.
