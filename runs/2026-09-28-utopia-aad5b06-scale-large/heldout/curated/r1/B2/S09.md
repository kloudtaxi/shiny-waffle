> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? ## Answer

**No.** Sarah Chen could not have approved 15% on 2025-03-31. Her authority was high enough, but Acme's eligibility on that date was only 10%. The agreement the request cites didn't start until the next day.

**1. Request:** DR-9101 for NS-500, requested by Sarah Chen on 2025-03-31. It asks for 15% ($37,500 off a $250,000 list price). The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on contract terms rather than standard pricing.

**2. Customer:** The CRM account is Acme Manufacturing, owned by Sarah Chen. The ERP customer is C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings". I found no record linking the ID CRM-2048 to either one. I'm treating them as the same customer because both have the same NS-500 eligibility on the same dates.

**3. Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Contract on the request date:** The Master Supply Agreement runs **from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028**. It was not in effect on 2025-03-31, the date the request relies on it.

**5. Exception covering NS-500 on the request date:** EXC-ACME-NS500-10 covered 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so it still applied on the request date. It sets the NS-500 maximum at **10% of list**. The newer Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception raises the maximum to 15%, but only from 2025-04-01. It covers only the NS-500. It also states that it doesn't give any employee approval authority.

**6. Policy in effect:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025, valid 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

**7. Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2025 policy, that role can approve discounts **up to and including 15%**, so she had enough authority for 15%.

**8. Required approver:** VP Sales approval applies only above 15%, so no higher approver was needed for authority reasons.

**9. Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** 15% is above Acme's 10% maximum on 2025-03-31. A more senior approver can't fix that; it needs new or amended terms. The simple fix is timing. From 2025-04-01, the MSA and the new exception allow 15%, and the 2025 policy lets an Enterprise Account Executive approve 15%. So the same request dated on or after that day would be within Sarah's own authority, and she could approve it herself. Under the 2026 policy, her limit falls to 10%, and VP Sales approval would be needed for 15%.
