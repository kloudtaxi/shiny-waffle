## Answer

No. Sarah Chen could not have approved DR-9103 herself. 22% is above her 15% limit, and the 2025 policy puts any discount over 15% with the VP Sales. The request can go ahead only if the VP Sales approves it, most likely Michael Torres.

**The request.** DR-9103 is a 22% discount on NS-EDGE (NS-Edge Monitoring Package) for CRM account CRM-2048, Acme Manufacturing. It is dated 2025-09-23, and the list price of $85,000 would drop to $66,300. The justification is "Competitive" and cites no contract or exception, so I treated it as a standard-pricing request.

**The customer.** In the CRM it is Acme Manufacturing, owned by Sarah Chen. In the ERP it is customer C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings", in Milwaukee, WI, on Net 45 terms.

**Special terms.** Acme has one pricing exception, the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception". It allows up to 15% from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 and replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10%. It applies only to the NS-500 Industrial Controller, so it doesn't cover NS-EDGE, and it says it gives no Northstar employee approval authority. I found no active Acme contract that covers NS-EDGE. That doesn't affect this request, which doesn't rely on special terms. If it had relied on the NS-500 exception, the outcome would be commercial review, because the exception doesn't carry over to other products.

**The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 15%, and anything above 15% needs VP Sales approval. The 2026 policy (EAE up to 10%, VP Sales above 10% and up to 20%, Chief Revenue Officer above 20%) doesn't apply to a 2025 request.

**Authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit was 15%. At 22%, the discount is 7 points above it.

**Approver.** The records list Michael Torres as VP Sales. That role has no dates attached, and his record as Sarah Chen's manager only starts on 2026-09-01. Please confirm he held the role on 2025-09-23. The records also show no approval band above VP Sales in the 2025 policy. If the policy document has a higher tier that the records missed, it could change who has to sign off.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** The request uses standard pricing, so there are no special terms to establish. It can proceed only with VP Sales approval.
