No, Sarah can't approve this herself. DR-9102 is a standard-pricing request, and under the 2026 policy a 25% discount has to be approved by the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan.

**1. The request.** DR-9102 is Sarah Chen's request of 2026-09-23 for 25% off the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (NS-EDGE) for CRM account CRM-2048. That is $21,250 off a list price of $85,000, for a net of $63,750. The only justification given is "Competitive", and it points to no contract term or exception, so I've treated it as standard pricing.

**2. Customer.** Sarah is the account owner for Acme Manufacturing in the CRM. The ERP customer appears to be C-1001, "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (Milwaukee, WI, Net 45). The graph has no direct link between the two records. They seem connected only because the same source document mentions both, so this match is likely but not confirmed.

**3–5. Contract and exception.** Acme does have a pricing exception, the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception", approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20:
- It allows up to 15% off list from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, replacing an earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10).
- It covers **only the NS-500 Industrial Controller** and states that it does not apply to other products.
- It sets eligibility only. It gives no employee any approval authority.

So nothing gives Acme special terms on NS-EDGE. This request doesn't rely on special terms anyway. If it were meant to rely on the NS-500 exception, the outcome would change to REVIEW_REQUIRED, because the exception doesn't carry over to other products. Even on NS-500, 25% would be above its 15% limit.

**6–8. Policy and authority.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date (it runs 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and replaced the 2025 policy). Its approval bands are:
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to and including 10%. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive.
- Over 10% up to 20% needs VP Sales approval.
- **Over 20% needs Chief Revenue Officer approval.** The CRO is David Morgan.

At 25%, the request is outside Sarah's authority and in the CRO band. The 2025 policy's 15% limit for Enterprise Account Executives no longer applies.

**Decision: APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION.** The request can go ahead only if David Morgan approves it as CRO. Sarah can't approve it, and neither can her manager, Michael Torres, unless he is the CRO.
