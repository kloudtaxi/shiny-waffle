## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen had the authority to approve 15% on 2025-03-31, but Acme was only entitled to 10% on NS-500 that day. The request cites a contract that didn't start until the next day.

**Checks for DR-9101:**
1. **Request:** 15% off NS-500 for CRM account CRM-2048 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount), requested by Sarah Chen on 2025-03-31. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it depends on special contract terms, not standard pricing.
2. **Customer:** The knowledge base has Sarah Chen as account owner of Acme Manufacturing. The ERP name is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001). CRM-2048 exists but isn't linked to anything, so I'm relying on your statement that it is the Acme account.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs **from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028**. It was not yet in force on 2025-03-31.
5. **Pricing exception:** On 2025-03-31 the exception in force was EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ran from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31 and **capped NS-500 at 10% off list**. The exception that allows 15% (the "Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception," approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20) replaced it but **only starts on 2025-04-01**. The request is one day early.
6. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force for all of 2025.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2025 policy she can approve up to and including 15%, so the amount is within her limit. VP Sales approval only applies above 15%.
8. **Required approver:** None. The problem is the customer's entitlement, not Sarah's authority, and a more senior approver can't fix that.

**Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** The discount is 5 points above what Acme was entitled to on that date. Approving it because the new agreement allows 15% would break the SOP rule that a request can't be approved just because a contract permits it. It would also apply the new terms before their start date. There are two fixes:
- **Re-date the request (or the order) to 2025-04-01 or later.** Under the new exception it would then be within both Acme's entitlement and Sarah's own authority.
- **Keep the 2025-03-31 date and cap the discount at 10%.**
