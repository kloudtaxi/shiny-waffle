## Answer

**No, not as submitted. This needs commercial review (REVIEW_REQUIRED).** The request cites "Acme Master Supply Agreement" pricing, but the Acme agreement that allows 15% on NS-500 belongs to a different customer from Acme Industrial Supply.

**Findings**

1. **The request:** DR-9105 is for 15% off NS-500. That is $37,500 off a $250,000 list price. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special terms (the Master Supply Agreement), not standard pricing.
2. **The customer:** there are two separate "Acme" customers in the ERP records:
   - **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** is ERP C-1044, in Akron, OH, with DUNS 09-552-1187. It is an industrial distributor and its account owner is Zachary Brown.
   - **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, also called "Acme Manufacturing", is ERP C-1001, in Milwaukee, WI, with DUNS 04-812-7730. Its account owner is Sarah Chen.
   
   The CRM account ID on the request, CRM-2091, isn't linked to either customer in the knowledge base.
3. **The product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **The contract:** a Master Supply Agreement was in effect from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. No link exists between it and Acme Industrial Supply Co.
5. **The pricing exception:** the Acme NS-500 Strategic Account Exception covers NS-500 only and allows up to 15% from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10%. David Morgan approved it. The only customer it gives the 15% to is **Acme Mfg. Holdings**. No link exists between it and Acme Industrial Supply Co. Special terms don't carry over to another customer, even one with a similar name.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 was in force on the request date.
7. **Authority:** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive and can approve up to 10%. 15% is above her limit.
8. **Required approver:** discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. Even so, his approval can't create eligibility the customer doesn't have.

**Next steps**
- **Check which customer CRM-2091 really is.** If the request was meant for Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001) and was just logged under the wrong name, the 15% would be within the exception. Michael Torres would still need to approve it (APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION).
- **If it really is Acme Industrial Supply Co.**, commercial review is needed. That means either new or amended terms for this customer, or reworking the request under standard pricing. Under standard pricing Sarah can approve up to 10%, and anything above that goes to Michael Torres.
