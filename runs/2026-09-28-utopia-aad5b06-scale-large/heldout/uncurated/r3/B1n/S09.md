## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen could not have approved DR-9101 on 2025-03-31. She had the authority, but on that date Acme was only eligible for 10% off the NS-500, not 15%.

**The request.** DR-9101 asks for 15% off the NS-500 for CRM account CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing): $250,000 list, $37,500 discount. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2025-03-31. It relies on special terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**Customer and product.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, and Sarah Chen owns the account. The customer is billed in the ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091) is a different account. The SKU is NS-500, the Industrial Controller.

**Contract on 2025-03-31.** The Master Supply Agreement the request cites (ACME-MFG-2025) was signed in March 2025 but runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. On the request date it was not yet in effect. The only earlier agreement on record is ACME-SA-2023, which is named in the 2023 pricing exception. That agreement document itself isn't in the knowledge base.

**Pricing exception on 2025-03-31.**
- **EXC-ACME-NS500-10** covers the NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so it applies on this date. Its maximum is 10%.
- **EXC-ACME-NS500-15** is Schedule B of the new agreement and allows up to 15%. It starts on 2025-04-01 and replaces the 10% exception only for requests dated on or after that day.

The request is one day early. The 15% term did not exist yet, and 15% is above the 10% maximum that did apply.

**Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in effect. Under it, Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to and including 15%. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so authority is not the obstacle. That doesn't help: under the discount procedure (SOP), a contract's eligibility doesn't change an employee's authority, and authority doesn't create eligibility. A more senior approver would not fix this either.

**Decision.** This is REJECT_OR_ESCALATE. The discount is above what Acme was eligible for on the request date. The fix is on the pricing side, not the approval side:
- redate the request to 2025-04-01 or later, when the 15% exception applies and Sarah could approve it herself under the 2025 policy; or
- reduce it to 10% or less; or
- get amended terms for 2025-03-31.
