## Answer

**No, it can't be approved as submitted. The decision is REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The request cites the Acme Master Supply Agreement, but that agreement and its NS-500 exception belong to a different customer. CRM-2091 is Acme Industrial Supply Co., not Acme Manufacturing.

1. **The request.** DR-9105 asks for 15% off NS-500, on a $250,000 list value (a $37,500 discount). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23. The reason given is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," so it relies on special contract terms. DR-9105 doesn't appear in the knowledge base's discount request records, so I used the request exactly as you provided it.
2. **The customer.** CRM-2091 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.**, a Mid-Market industrial distributor at 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH. Its ERP record is C-1044, its DUNS number is 09-552-1187, and its account owner is Zachary Brown.
   - This is a separate company from **Acme Manufacturing**. That customer is CRM-2048, billed in ERP as Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), based in Milwaukee, WI, with DUNS 04-812-7730. Sarah Chen owns that account.
   - The names are similar, but the address, DUNS number, ERP ID and owner are all different.
3. **The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.
4. **Active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) is with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. I found no contract of any kind for Acme Industrial Supply Co. The only document about that account is a service ticket (SR-40418), which says "no change to commercial terms requested."
5. **Pricing exception.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% on NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, but it names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. Nothing covers Acme Industrial Supply, and special terms don't carry over to another customer.
6. **Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and Approval Authority Matrix APP-MATRIX-2026, both running 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
7. **Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less. 15% is above her limit. The 15% limit Account Executives had under the 2025 policy no longer applies.
8. **Required approver.** Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need the VP Sales, who is Michael Torres. A higher approver can't fix a missing eligibility basis, though. The agreement itself says in §3.3 that contract eligibility doesn't change internal approval authority.

**Two things to check:**
- Sarah logged a nearly identical request the same day for **Acme Manufacturing**: DR-9001, on CRM-2048, still pending. Her email to Michael Torres about the third-plant NS-500 order refers to DR-9001. DR-9105 may have been filed against the wrong account by mistake, so confirm which customer the order is actually for.
- If the order really is for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), the decision would be different. That customer is eligible for 15% under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, so the likely outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with sign-off from VP Sales Michael Torres.
