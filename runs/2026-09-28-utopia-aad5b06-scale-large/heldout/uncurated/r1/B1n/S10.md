1. **Customer:** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing in Milwaukee, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. Its legal entity is Acme Mfg. Holdings. It is not Acme Industrial Supply Co. in Akron (CRM-2091), a separate account.
2. **Product:** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, a software product listed at $85,000.
3. **Contract:** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It covers NS-EDGE as a product, but gives no special discount on it. 6. **Sarah Chen's authority:** She is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. The 25% request is above it.
7. **Required approver:** The Chief Revenue Officer. The September 2026 org chart and the employee records both show David Morgan in that role. Because the discount is above 10%, the approval must be recorded in Northstar CRM.
