## Answer

No. DR-9105 can't be approved as submitted. It needs **commercial review**, because the contract it relies on belongs to a different company.

**1. The request.** DR-9105 asks for 15% off NS-500 for CRM-2091 ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount). Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23. Its justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special contract terms. DR-9105 does not appear in the knowledge base's copy of the discount request log.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2091 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.**: 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron OH, DUNS 09-552-1187, ERP customer C-1044. Its account owner is Zachary Brown, not Sarah Chen. It is a separate legal entity from **Acme Manufacturing**, which is:
- CRM-2048, with ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings"
- based at 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee WI, DUNS 04-812-7730
- owned by Sarah Chen

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Contract.** The Acme Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) is with Acme Mfg. Holdings (Acme Manufacturing), Milwaukee. I found no contract with Acme Industrial Supply Co.

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% on NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It is Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025 and names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. I found no exception for CRM-2091 or C-1044. The only Acme Industrial record I found is a support ticket (SR-40418 on NS-EDGE), which says no change to commercial terms was requested.

**6–8. Policy and authority.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix were in force. Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less. A discount above 10% and up to 20% needs the VP Sales, Michael Torres. Even with valid terms, 15% would be beyond her own authority.

**9. Decision: REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The special terms cited in the request cover Acme Manufacturing, not Acme Industrial Supply Co. Special terms don't carry over to another company. Neither a similar name nor having the same sales rep changes that. A higher approver can't fix the eligibility problem; it needs commercial review.

On the same date Sarah logged DR-9001, an identical request for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), and emailed Michael Torres about it. DR-9105 may therefore have been logged against the wrong account. If the deal is really for CRM-2048, it would need re-submitting there. Acme Manufacturing is eligible for up to 15% under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, so the likely outcome would be APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION, with Michael Torres approving.
