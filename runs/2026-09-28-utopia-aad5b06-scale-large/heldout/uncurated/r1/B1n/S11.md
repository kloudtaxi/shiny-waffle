## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen could not have approved this herself. It needs VP Sales approval, and Michael Torres held that role.** Under the 2025 policy she could approve up to 15%, and 22% is over that. Nothing blocks the discount on the customer side, so it can go ahead once the VP Sales signs off.

**How I got there**

1. **The request.** DR-9103, dated 2025-09-23. It asks for 22% off the NS-Edge Monitoring Package for account CRM-2048, which is $18,700 off an $85,000 list price. Sarah Chen submitted it. The justification is "Competitive," so it relies on standard pricing, not a contract term or exception.
2. **The customer.** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing. In the ERP it is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product.** SKU NS-EDGE, list price $85,000.
4. **Contract.** The Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 was active on the request date (2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) and covers NS-EDGE. But it sets no discount for NS-EDGE. Its only special pricing is up to 15% on the NS-500 controller. Section 3.3 also says the customer's contract terms don't change Northstar's internal approval authority.
5. **Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers the NS-500 only, and says it applies to no other product. So no exception covers NS-EDGE. That doesn't matter here, because the request doesn't rely on one.
6. **Policy in force.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 (1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025). The 2026 policy and approval matrix only apply to requests dated from 2026-01-01.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. The 2025 policy lets that role approve up to and including 15%, so 22% is beyond her limit.
8. **Required approver.** The 2025 policy says anything over 15% needs VP Sales approval. It has no separate tier for the Chief Revenue Officer. The only org chart on file is dated 2026-09-01 and lists Michael Torres as VP Sales. The CRM log backs this up for September 2025: he approved other over-15% requests then, for example DR-7431 (22%, approved 2025-09-16).
