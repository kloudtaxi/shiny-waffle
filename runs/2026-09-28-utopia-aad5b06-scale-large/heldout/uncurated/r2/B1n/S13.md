## Answer

I've checked the customer records, contracts and 2026 policy. No, it can't be approved as recorded. The outcome is **REVIEW_REQUIRED**, because the Acme agreement it cites belongs to a different customer.

**1. The request.** DR-9105 asks for 15% off NS-500 for account CRM-2091. It is dated 2026-09-23 and was raised by Sarah Chen. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it depends on special contract terms rather than standard pricing.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2091 is **Acme Industrial Supply Co.** It's a mid-market distributor in Akron, OH, with ERP customer C-1044 and DUNS 09-552-1187. Its account owner is Zachary Brown. It is not **Acme Manufacturing** (CRM-2048; ERP C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings"; Milwaukee, WI; DUNS 04-812-7730), whose account owner is Sarah Chen. The two companies have different DUNS numbers and addresses, so they are separate legal entities.

**3. The product.** NS-500 Industrial Controller.

**4. Contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on the request date. But it was signed with Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing. I found no contract with Acme Industrial Supply Co.

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 allows up to 15% on NS-500 from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It is Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025 and names Acme Manufacturing as the customer. It doesn't cover CRM-2091, and special terms don't carry over to another customer. The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 expired on 2025-03-31.

**6. Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix were in force.

**7. Requestor authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10% or less. 15% is above her authority.

**8. Required approver.** The band for over 10% up to 20% belongs to VP Sales, held by Michael Torres. That only matters once eligibility is established. A higher approver can't make up for eligibility the customer doesn't have. Also, the contract itself (section 3.3) says contract eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.

**Also worth checking:** I couldn't find DR-9105 in the discount request log. The log does have **DR-9001**: the same 15% NS-500 request on the same date by Sarah Chen, but for **CRM-2048 (Acme Manufacturing)**. Sarah's 2026-09-23 email to Michael Torres also mentions DR-9001 for Acme's third plant. DR-9105 may have been entered against the wrong Acme account. If the deal is really for Acme Manufacturing, the 15% would be allowed under EXC-ACME-NS500-15, but it would still need Michael Torres (VP Sales) to approve it. As recorded against CRM-2091, it needs commercial review.
