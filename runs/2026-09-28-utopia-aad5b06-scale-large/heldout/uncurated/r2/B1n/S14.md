**How I got there**

3. **The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package (PROD-003), with a list price of $85,000. That matches the request.
4. **The contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date, and it covers NS-EDGE. Section 3.3 says the contract doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority. The request doesn't rely on it anyway.
5. **The pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (up to 15%) covers the NS-500 controller only and doesn't apply to NS-EDGE. The older EXC-ACME-NS500-10 expired on 2025-03-31. Neither matters here, because the request is on standard terms.
6. **The policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, and the 2026 Approval Authority Matrix apply. The 2025 policy's 15% limit no longer applies.
7. **Her authority.** The employee list and org chart show Sarah Chen (EMP-101) as an Enterprise Account Executive, whose limit is 10%.
