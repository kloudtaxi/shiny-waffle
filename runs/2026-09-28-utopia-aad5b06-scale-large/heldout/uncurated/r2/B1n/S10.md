## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. DR-9102 asks for 25% on NS-EDGE, and under the 2026 policy an Enterprise Account Executive can approve up to 10%. Anything over 20% must be approved by the Chief Revenue Officer, David Morgan.

**1. The request.** DR-9102 was entered in Northstar CRM for account CRM-2048. It asks for 25.0% off NS-EDGE: $21,250 off a list value of $85,000, for a net of $63,750. Sarah Chen requested it on 2026-09-23, and it is Pending Approval. The justification is "Competitive". The request doesn't cite a contract or a pricing exception, so I treated it as standard pricing.

**2. The customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen. In the ERP (the billing system) the customer is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. Two other accounts have similar names and are not this customer: Acme Industrial Supply Co. / Acme Industrial (CRM-2091, Akron).

**3. The product.** NS-EDGE is the NS-Edge Monitoring Package, list price $85,000. That matches the list value in the request.

**4. Contract.** Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was active on the request date. It lists NS-EDGE among the products Northstar supplies. Its only discount term, §3.2, gives up to 15% on the NS-500 alone. §3.3 says the customer's pricing eligibility doesn't change Northstar's internal approval authority.

**5. Pricing exception.** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 applies "only" to the NS-500, and the earlier EXC-ACME-NS500-10 expired on 2025-03-31. No exception covers NS-EDGE, so no special discount entitlement applies here. That's consistent with a request based on standard pricing.

**6. Policy.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) applies, along with Approval Authority Matrix APP-MATRIX-2026.

**7. Requestor's authority.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10% or less. She is not authorized for 25%.

**8. Required approver.** Discounts over 20% go to the Chief Revenue Officer. That's David Morgan, according to the org chart as of 2026-09-01 and the employee records.

**9. Decision: `APPROVE_WITH_AUTHORIZATION`.** Nothing establishes a special discount entitlement for this product, and none is needed for a standard-pricing request. The discount is above Sarah Chen's authority, so David Morgan must approve it. The policy requires his approval to be recorded in Northstar CRM because the discount is over 10%.
