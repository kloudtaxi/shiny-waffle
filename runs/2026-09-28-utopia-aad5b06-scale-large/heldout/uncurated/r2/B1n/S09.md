## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-03-31? The request, as recorded in Northstar CRM: {"request_id": "DR-9101", "account_id": "CRM-2048", "product_sku": "NS-500", "requested_discount_pct": "15.0", "list_value_usd": "250000", "discount_value_usd": "37500", "net_value_usd": "212500", "requested_by": "sarah.chen@northstar.example", "request_date": "2025-03-31", "justification": "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", "status": "Pending Approval"}

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen couldn't have approved DR-9101 on 2025-03-31. She had the personal authority for 15% that day, but Acme wasn't contractually eligible for 15% until the next day.

**1. Request.** DR-9101 asks for 15% off NS-500 for account CRM-2048. List value is $250,000, the discount is $37,500 and the net is $212,500. Sarah Chen raised it on 2025-03-31. The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so the request relies on contract terms, not standard pricing.

**2. Customer.** CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, whose account owner is Sarah Chen. Its ERP name is Acme Mfg. Holdings (C-1001), which does business as Acme Manufacturing. It is a different customer from Acme Industrial Supply Co. (CRM-2091).

**3. Product.** The SKU is NS-500, the Industrial Controller.

**4. Active contract on 2025-03-31.** The agreement the request cites, ACME-MFG-2025, only takes effect on **2025-04-01** and runs to 2028-03-31. So it wasn't in force on the request date. The agreement that did apply was the older ACME-SA-2023, which is referenced in the 2023 exception.

**5. Pricing exception for NS-500 on 2025-03-31.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10 applied that day. It allows **up to 10%** on NS-500 for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so the request falls on its last day. The 15% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) only starts on 2025-04-01. It replaces the 10% exception for requests dated on or after that day. On the request date, Acme's maximum was 10%.

**6. Policy in force.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 applied (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). The 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix don't apply yet.

**7. Sarah Chen's authority.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive. Under the 2025 policy, that role can approve discounts up to and including 15%. So 15% was within her own authority.

**8. Required approver.** Under the 2025 policy, discounts of 15% or less need no one above her. Only discounts over 15% go to the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

**9. Decision: REJECT_OR_ESCALATE.** 15% is more than the 10% Acme was entitled to on 2025-03-31. Her authority, or any higher approver's, doesn't change that. Contractual eligibility and employee authority are separate checks, and both have to pass. Options:
- Reduce the discount to 10% or less. That fits the 2023 exception, and Sarah could approve it herself.
- Re-date the order to 2025-04-01 or later, when the new agreement and the 15% exception take effect.
- Get new or amended terms.

I couldn't find the ACME-SA-2023 agreement itself in the knowledge base. Its terms come only from what the 2023 exception says about it. This doesn't change the outcome: nothing on file allows more than 10% on that date.
