# Parent Company Guarantee

**Guarantee reference:** GRT-ACME-2026-NEW

This Guarantee is given by **Acme Group Holdings, Inc.** ("Guarantor"), Milwaukee, Wisconsin, in favour of
**Northstar Industrial Systems** ("Northstar").

## 1. Guaranteed party

The Guarantor guarantees the payment obligations to Northstar of its wholly owned subsidiary
**Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as **Acme Manufacturing** ("Customer").

## 2. Amount

The Guarantor's liability under this Guarantee is limited to **$600,000** in aggregate.

## 3. Term

This Guarantee is effective from 2026-09-01 to 2027-12-31.
