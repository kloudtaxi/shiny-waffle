# Supplemental Parent Guarantee

**Supplement reference:** GRT-ACME-2026-SUP2

This Supplement is given by **Acme Group Holdings, Inc.** ("Guarantor") in favour of
**Northstar Industrial Systems** ("Northstar") for the payment obligations of
**Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as **Acme Manufacturing**.

## 1. Additional guarantee amount

The Guarantor agrees to provide an additional **$200,000** of aggregate guarantee support for
Acme Mfg. Holdings, increasing the available guarantee support to **$600,000**.

## 2. Term

This Supplement is effective from 2026-07-08 through 2027-12-31.
