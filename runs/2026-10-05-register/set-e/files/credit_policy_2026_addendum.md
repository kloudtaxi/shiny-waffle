# Northstar Credit Policy 2026 — Strategic Concurrence Addendum

This Finance addendum updates the concurrence threshold in the 2026 credit policy.

## Strategic account concurrence

For Strategic accounts, VP Sales concurrence is required only for credit limits
**greater than $750,000**.

All other approval authority and eligibility provisions of the Northstar Credit Policy 2026 remain unchanged.
