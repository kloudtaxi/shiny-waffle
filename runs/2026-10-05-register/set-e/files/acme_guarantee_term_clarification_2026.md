# Guarantee Term Clarification

This clarification confirms that the **$400,000 aggregate liability limit** under the
Parent Company Guarantee for **Acme Mfg. Holdings**, doing business as Acme Manufacturing,
remains unchanged.

The clarification does not add a guaranteed party, increase the aggregate liability,
or alter the effective period of the guarantee.
