## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's contractual maximum discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **15% off list price**.

**Where this comes from:**
- **Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025, §3.2:** Acme can get discounts of up to 15% on the NS-500, as set out in Schedule B. The agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. The only early-termination clause covers material breach on 60 days' notice, and nothing I found shows it was used.
- **Schedule B, exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15:** sets the maximum at 15% of list, for the NS-500 only, effective 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. Deal Desk owns it, and CRO David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20.
- **Older exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10:** allowed up to 10% for orders from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. It no longer applies: EXC-ACME-NS500-15 replaced it for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01, and its term ended anyway.

**Things to keep in mind:**
- The 15% only makes Acme eligible for that discount. It doesn't approve any request. MSA §3.3 says each discount still needs approval under Northstar's internal policies, and Schedule B says it gives no Northstar employee approval authority.
- That matters for the request filed that day. Sarah Chen logged DR-9001 on 2026-09-23 for 15% on a $250,000 NS-500 order, which comes to $37,500 off and a net of $212,500. It's within the contractual maximum, but it still needs internal approval, and the record I saw shows it as pending.
- The scope is the NS-500 only. The 15% doesn't cover NS-Cloud, NS-Edge or any other Northstar product.
