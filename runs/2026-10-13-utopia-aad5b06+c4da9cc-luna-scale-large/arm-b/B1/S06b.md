> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 Industrial Controller was **10% off list price**.

- **What set the limit:** The cap came from the pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, part of agreement ACME-SA-2023. It allowed up to 10% off list on NS-500 orders placed from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so 2024-10-01 is inside that window.
- **The 15% limit came later:** A newer exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to agreement ACME-MFG-2025), raised the cap to 15%. It only took effect on 2025-04-01, runs to 2028-03-31, and replaces the 10% exception from that date. It didn't apply on 2024-10-01.
- **No later changes:** The knowledge base has no record of the 10% exception being corrected or withdrawn.
