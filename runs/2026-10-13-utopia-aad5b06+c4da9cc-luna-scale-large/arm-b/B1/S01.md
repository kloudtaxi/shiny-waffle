2. **The exception doesn't give anyone approval rights.** The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only. It does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." The sales discount procedure (SOP-SALES-014) says the same thing: being allowed a discount under a contract doesn't change who can approve it, and a request can't be approved just because the contract permits it.
3. **The 2026 policy applies.** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and fully replaces the 2025 policy. Under it:
   - Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts up to 10%.
   - VP Sales approves discounts above 10% and up to 20%.
   - The Chief Revenue Officer approves anything above 20%.
4. **Sarah's role.** The org chart dated 2026-09-01 lists her as an Enterprise Account Executive reporting to Michael Torres, VP Sales. Her limit is 10%, so a 15% discount has to go to the VP Sales.

**Watch out for the 2025 precedent.** On 2025-09-16, Sarah approved her own request for the same deal: 15% on NS-500 for Acme (request DR-8104).
