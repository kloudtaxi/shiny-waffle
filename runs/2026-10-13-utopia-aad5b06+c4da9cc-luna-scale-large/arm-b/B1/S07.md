## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing can get up to 15% off list on the NS-500 because its signed contract with Northstar says so. That makes Acme eligible for the discount, but it does not approve any particular request.

**Evidence**

1. **Master Supply Agreement ACME-MFG-2025**, signed by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) on 2025-03-20 and by Edward Floyd (Acme, VP Procurement) on 2025-03-22.
   - The parties are Northstar and Acme Mfg. Holdings, doing business as Acme Manufacturing.
   - It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31.
   - §3.2 says the customer "is eligible for discounts of up to **15%** on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, as set out in the pricing exception attached as Schedule B."

2. **Pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 (Schedule B to that agreement)**
   - It sets the maximum discount at 15% of list price, for the NS-500 only. It "does not apply to any other Northstar product."
   - It covers the same dates as the agreement, 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31.
   - It was prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20.
   - It replaces the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10, for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. That earlier exception, under agreement ACME-SA-2023, allowed only 10% off the NS-500 from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31.

3. **Customer identity:** The CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" (a Strategic account owned by Sarah Chen) and the ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" are the same company. Their address and DUNS number (04-812-7730) match, and the agreement names both.

4. **Past use:** Discount request DR-8104 gave 15% off the NS-500 on 2025-09-16, on a $250,000 list price ($37,500 discount). The stated reason was "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**How this applies to the current request (DR-9001, dated 2026-09-23)**
- The request is for 15% on a $250,000 NS-500 order for Acme's third plant. It falls inside the agreement's term and the exception's product scope, so the eligibility holds.
- Approval is a separate step. The exception says it "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." §3.3 of the agreement says each discount still has to be approved under Northstar's internal policies.
- Approval rules have changed since DR-8104:
  - Under the 2025 pricing policy, Account Executives could approve up to 15%.
  - Under the 2026 policy and approval matrix, Account Executives can approve only up to 10%. Anything above 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales approval.
  - So DR-9001 needs VP Sales sign-off. It can't be self-approved the way last September's request apparently was.
- Sarah Chen's email to Michael Torres says the agreement covers the 15%. That is right on eligibility, but on its own it isn't enough to approve the request.
