## Answer

Not on 2026-09-23 as it stands. Sarah can't approve the 15% herself, Michael Torres (VP Sales) has to sign it off, and he can't do that until someone produces the Acme agreement the request relies on.

**1. The 2026 policy applies, not the 2025 one.** The request is dated 2026-09-23, so the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 governs it. That policy replaces the 2025 policy in full for requests dated on or after 2026-01-01. Under the 2026 policy:
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%.
- Discounts over 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval.
- Discounts over 20% need the Chief Revenue Officer.

The 2026 Approval Authority Matrix says the same thing and adds that "authority attaches to the role, not to the individual or the account." Approvals above 10% must be recorded in the CRM with evidence.

**2. Last September's approval isn't a precedent.** Sarah approved the same 15% NS-500 discount herself in 2025 (DR-8104). That was allowed because the 2025 policy let Account Executives approve up to 15%. That authority ended on 2025-12-31.

**3. Sarah's role and the approver.** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, so her limit is 10%. At 15%, DR-9001 needs Michael Torres, who is VP Sales and her manager. Her 2026-09-23 email already went to him, and the request currently shows as Pending.

**4. The contract argument doesn't change who approves.** The request's justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." The discount procedure says:
- Having a contractual right to a discount doesn't change an employee's approval authority.
- A request must not be approved just because the customer's contract allows the discount.
- A pricing exception only covers the product and dates it names.
- If a request relies on a contract term that can't be found, ask for the document before deciding.

I couldn't find the Acme Master Supply Agreement or any NS-500 pricing exception in the knowledge base. Sarah herself is unsure. Her email says "I think their agreement covers it," and her account plan says "I believe it's 15%… Worth confirming with Deal Desk."
