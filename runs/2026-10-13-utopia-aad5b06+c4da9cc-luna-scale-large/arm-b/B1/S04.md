## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

**Yes.** Sarah Chen had the authority to approve this discount herself on 2025-09-23.

1. **Her role:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive who reports to Michael Torres (VP Sales), and she owns the Acme Manufacturing account. One caveat: my source for her title is the org chart dated 2026-09-01. I didn't find a dated record of her title in September 2025, so I'm assuming it was the same then.
2. **The 2025 policy applies:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 covers 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve discounts **up to and including 15%**, and only discounts above 15% need VP Sales approval. A 15% discount is exactly at her limit.
3. **Acme was eligible for 15%:** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025) and its pricing exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) run from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. They allow up to 15% off list price on the NS-500 only. The exception was approved by David Morgan, the Chief Revenue Officer. It replaced the earlier 10% exception from 2023.
4. **The exception doesn't give anyone approval authority.** Both the exception and section 3.3 of the agreement say the approval still has to fit internal policy. Here the authority comes from the 2025 policy, so that's satisfied.

The CRM has a matching earlier case: request DR-8104, a 15% NS-500 discount on Acme's account ($250,000 list value), submitted by Sarah on 2025-09-16 and marked Approved.

**The same request in 2026 would need VP Sales approval.** From 2026-01-01, the 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix cap Enterprise Account Executives at 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales sign-off. Request DR-9001, the same 15% NS-500 discount for Acme submitted by Sarah on **2026-09-23**, is still pending, which fits that rule. If you meant that 2026 request, the answer is no: Sarah can't approve it, and Michael Torres would have to.
