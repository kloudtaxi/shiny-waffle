## Answer

All three IDs point to the same customer: **Acme Manufacturing**, whose legal name is Acme Mfg. Holdings, based in Milwaukee, WI. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**

**How the three IDs connect**

| ID | System | Record |
|---|---|---|
| CRM-2048 | CRM account | Acme Manufacturing: Strategic tier, Midwest Enterprise segment, owner Sarah Chen |
| C-1001 | ERP customer | Acme Mfg. Holdings, payment terms Net 45 |
| ACME-MFG-2025 | Contract | Master Supply Agreement with Acme Mfg. Holdings "doing business as Acme Manufacturing", in effect 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 |

They match on three points:
- **Address:** all three records give 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209.
- **DUNS number:** the CRM and ERP records both show 04-812-7730.
- **Cross-reference:** Sarah Chen's 2026 account strategy lists CRM-2048 and ERP bill-to C-1001 together for the same account.

The NS-500 pricing exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is filed as Schedule B to ACME-MFG-2025. That puts it under this customer too.

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is separate**

| | Acme Manufacturing | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| CRM ID | CRM-2048 | CRM-2091 |
| ERP ID | C-1001 | C-1044 |
