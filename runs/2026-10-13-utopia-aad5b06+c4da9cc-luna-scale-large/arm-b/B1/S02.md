## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No, not on the basis given.** Nothing in the knowledge base supports 18% for Acme on the NS-500 on 2026-09-23. Also, DR-9001 isn't an 18% request.

**1. DR-9001 asks for 15%, not 18%.** The CRM record shows 15% off a $250,000 list price: a $37,500 discount and a $212,500 net. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement," and it is still Pending Approval. Her email that day to Michael Torres also says Acme asked for 15%.

**2. Acme's contract caps the NS-500 at 15%.** The Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, in force 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) points to a pricing exception for this, EXC-ACME-NS500-15. That exception allows "up to 15%" on the NS-500 only. It replaced the earlier 10% exception, and I found no amendment raising the cap. So 18% can't be justified as "contract pricing." It would be a new discount outside the contract, and the knowledge base has no reason or approval on record for it.

**3. Who would have to approve it.** The 2026 Pricing Policy and Approval Authority Matrix set the limits:
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve up to 10%.
- Anything over 10% and up to 20% needs VP Sales approval.
- Anything over 20% needs the Chief Revenue Officer.

That means Sarah can't approve either 15% or 18% herself; both need VP Sales. The contract (section 3.3), the exception document and the sales discount procedure all say that what a customer is entitled to under its contract doesn't change who is allowed to approve the discount.

**Why last year's precedent doesn't carry over:** Sarah's note says "what we did for them last September." That was DR-8104 on 2025-09-16, which fell under the 2025 policy, where account executives could approve up to 15%. The 2026 policy fully replaced that one for requests dated from 2026-01-01.

**Bottom line:** Approve DR-9001 at 15% only with VP Sales sign-off. If Acme actually wants 18%, that needs its own business case and VP Sales approval as a discount outside the contract.
