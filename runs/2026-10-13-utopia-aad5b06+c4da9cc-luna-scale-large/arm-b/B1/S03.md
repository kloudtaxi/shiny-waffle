## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve it. A 15% discount on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite needs **Michael Torres (VP Sales)** to approve it.

1. **Policy on the date:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 applies to requests dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy completely.
2. **Her limit:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive who reports to Michael Torres. Under the 2026 policy and the Approval Authority Matrix, that role can approve discounts up to 10%. Discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. The matrix says authority goes with the role, not the person or the account.
3. **The contract doesn't change this:** Acme's Master Supply Agreement (ACME-MFG-2025, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31) covers NS-Cloud. But clause 3.3 says Acme's contract pricing "does not modify Northstar's internal approval authority." The sales discount procedure (SOP-SALES-014) also says a request can't be approved just because the customer's contract allows the discount.
4. **No pricing exception covers NS-Cloud:** The 15% strategic account pricing (clause 3.2 and exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15) applies to the NS-500 Industrial Controller only. The exception says it "does not apply to any other Northstar product." I found no exception for NS-Cloud. So Acme has no special pricing on NS-Cloud, and 15% would be an ordinary discount that still needs VP Sales approval.

**Possible mix-up:** The only Acme request from Sarah dated 2026-09-23 is DR-9001, and it's for the **NS-500**, not NS-Cloud. It's 15% off a $250,000 list price and still pending. Her email to Mike points to last September's 15% approval (DR-8104). That was allowed under the 2025 policy, when account executives could approve up to 15%, but it doesn't carry over to 2026. Whichever product is meant, the answer is the same: 15% is over her 10% limit, so it goes to Michael Torres. Before he approves an NS-Cloud discount, someone should check that it's actually the product being requested.
