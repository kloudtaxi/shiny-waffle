## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

All three identifiers point to **Acme Manufacturing**. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**

**The three identifiers**
- **CRM-2048** is Acme Manufacturing's CRM account ID. That record lists the account as Strategic / Enterprise tier in the Midwest segment, with Sarah Chen as account owner.
- **C-1001** is its ERP customer ID. Sales orders SO-7001 and SO-7002 are billed under it. The bill-to address is 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209, on Net 45 terms.
- **ACME-MFG-2025** isn't stored as an attribute of Acme Manufacturing, so this link is less certain than the other two. It comes from the pricing-exception document, where Schedule B points to "ACME-MFG-2025". The same document names Acme Manufacturing as the exception's customer. The exception covers the NS-500 Industrial Controller, was prepared by Sarah Chen, was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, and replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10. It's most likely a contract or agreement reference for Acme Manufacturing.

Acme Manufacturing also goes by "Acme Mfg. Holdings" and "ACME Manufacturing", with DUNS 04-812-7730.

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is not the same customer**

| | Acme Manufacturing | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| CRM account | CRM-2048 | CRM-2091 |
| ERP customer | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| DUNS | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |
| Location | Milwaukee, WI | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310 |
| Industry | Industrial Manufacturing | Industrial Distribution |
| Tier | Strategic / Enterprise | Standard / Mid-Market |
| Account owner | Sarah Chen | Zachary Brown |

The only things they share are the "Acme" name, the Midwest segment and Net 45 terms. Every identifier differs, so they shouldn't be merged.

Watch for the short name "Acme Industrial": it belongs to the Akron company (CRM-2091), not Acme Manufacturing. Service request SR-40418, in Akron, OH, and deal records DR-7028, DR-7264 and DR-7560 are filed under it. Records labelled "Acme Industrial" shouldn't be counted toward Acme Manufacturing.
