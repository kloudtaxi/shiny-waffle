**Why it can't go through:**

1. **It's above her limit.** Under the Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31), an Enterprise Account Executive can approve discounts of up to 10%. 15% is over that, so someone more senior has to approve it. The policy says higher discounts need the VP Sales or the Chief Revenue Officer. Her VP Sales is her manager, Michael Torres. The records don't show which percentage goes to which of those two roles, so I can't say for sure whether Torres alone is enough. Approvals above 10% also have to be recorded in Northstar CRM, and the policy requires recorded approval evidence. No approval is recorded for DR-9001.

2. **The contract reason doesn't change who must approve.** The discount playbook says the contract making a customer eligible "does not change employee authority." A contract can make 15% allowed, but it doesn't give Sarah the right to approve it.

3. **There's no proof the contract was active.** The playbook says a requested discount must not be approved unless an active customer contract allows it. Requests that rely on a contract term also have to be checked for an active contract and backed by a document. Here's what the records show:
   - The "Acme Master Supply Agreement" is named in the request but has nothing else recorded: no dates, terms or status.
   - Acme did negotiate 15% preferential NS-500 pricing under a "2025 agreement," and the Deal Desk was asked to confirm it. Nothing shows that confirmation came back or that the agreement was still active on 2026-09-23.
   - A CRM note says Acme asked for "15% off list," and that their NS-500 controller (Gina) confirmed on 2026-09-23. That shows what the customer wants, not that a contract allows it.

**Next steps:** Get the MSA and confirm it was active and covered NS-500 at 15% on 2026-09-23 (the Deal Desk confirmation would do). Then route the request to Michael Torres (VP Sales), or to the CRO if the authority matrix calls for it, and record the approval in Northstar CRM.
