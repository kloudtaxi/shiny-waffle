## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing is eligible for up to 15% off the NS-500 list price because of a signed pricing exception. Their contract and the 2025 deal Acme points to are not the real basis.

**Why Acme qualifies**
- The exception is **EXC-ACME-NS500-15**. It covers Acme Manufacturing (legal name Acme Mfg. Holdings, CRM-2048 / ERP C-1001) and the NS-500 Industrial Controller only.
- The most it allows is **15% of list price**.
- It runs from **2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31**, so it is in force today (2026-10-13).
- It **repeals the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10**. That one allowed up to 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31 under agreement ACME-SA-2023. Its own record still says "Active", but the newer exception replaced it.
- Sarah Chen prepared the 15% exception and **David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20**.

**The current request**
- **DR-9001**, dated 2026-09-23 and submitted by Sarah Chen, asks for 15% on an NS-500 at a $250,000 list price.
- That is a $37,500 discount and a net price of $212,500, which is within the exception's limit.
- The status is **Pending Approval**.

**Caveats before approving**
1. **The stated reasons point to the wrong document.** DR-9001 cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement". Sarah's email to Michael says 15% is "what we did for them last September, and I think their agreement covers it." The knowledge base has no record that the agreement grants 15%. The discount-request process says the requested discount is allowed by the customer contract, and a discount must not be approved if it rests on a term that isn't in the contract. The evidence on file should name EXC-ACME-NS500-15.
2. **The exception doesn't approve the deal.** It says Acme is commercially eligible but explicitly gives no approval authority. DR-9001 still needs sign-off at the right level. The policy says discounts need VP Sales or Chief Revenue Officer approval, plus recorded approval evidence. The knowledge base doesn't say which discount levels need which approver.
3. **It covers the NS-500 only.** The exception doesn't apply to other Northstar products, such as the NS-Cloud pilot in Acme's account plan.

**Sources:** the EXC-ACME-NS500-15 exception document, the earlier EXC-ACME-NS500-10 record, the discount-request log (DR-9001), Sarah Chen's email to Michael (`email_sarah_to_michael.md`), and the product catalog ($250,000 NS-500 list price).
