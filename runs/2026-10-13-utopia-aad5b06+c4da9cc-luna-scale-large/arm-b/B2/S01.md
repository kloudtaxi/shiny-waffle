> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.** Acme is allowed to receive a 15% discount, but the approval has to come from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

1. **Who can approve what in 2026.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 covers discount requests and was in effect on 2026-09-23 (it runs 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31). It sets three approval levels:
   - Enterprise Account Executive: up to 10%
   - VP Sales: more than 10%, up to 20%
   - Chief Revenue Officer: more than 20%
2. **Sarah's role.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, so she can approve up to 10%. A 15% discount needs the VP Sales.
3. **Acme's exception.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It allows up to 15% off list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was valid on 2026-09-23. It replaces the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). Sarah prepared it and David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. The exception says it makes Acme eligible for the discount but gives no one the authority to approve it. So 15% is allowed, but the normal approval rules still apply.
4. **Who should approve.** Michael Torres is VP Sales and Sarah's direct manager, so he's the right approver. The 2026 policy also requires the discount to be worked out from the current list price, and the approval to be recorded with evidence.

The 10% / 10–20% / over 20% figures come from what the knowledge base extracted from the 2026 policy document, not the full text. They match how the policy itself lists Enterprise Account Executive, VP Sales and CRO approval.
