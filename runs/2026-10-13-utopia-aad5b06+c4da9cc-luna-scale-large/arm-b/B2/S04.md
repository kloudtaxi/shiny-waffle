> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

No. Under the rules on record, Sarah Chen could not have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing herself on 2025-09-23. She could prepare and submit it, but Michael Torres (VP Sales) or someone above him would have had to sign off.

**Her role:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and the account owner for Acme Manufacturing. She reports to Michael Torres, the VP Sales.

**The rules in force on that date:** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 ran from 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31. The 2026 policy that replaced it only took effect on 2026-01-01. Under the approval limits on record:
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts of **up to 10%**.
- Discounts **above 10% and up to 20%** need **VP Sales** approval.

A 15% discount is in the VP Sales range, so it was beyond Sarah's own approval limit.

**The Acme exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-15) doesn't change this:**
- It does allow up to 15% off list price on the NS-500 Industrial Controller for Acme Manufacturing. It took effect 2025-04-01 and runs to 2028-03-31, so it covered September 2025. It also replaced an earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10).
- Sarah *prepared* it, but David Morgan *approved* it on 2025-03-20.
- The exception says Acme can receive the discount, but it states that it does not give anyone the authority to approve it. So a 15% request still needed someone with authority above 10%.

**Caveats:**
- The records show Sarah's title with no dates. I'm assuming she was still an Enterprise Account Executive in September 2025, and nothing suggests she held a higher role.
- The policy also requires recorded approval evidence. I didn't find a record of an actual 2025-09-23 approval, so I can't say who, if anyone, approved one that day.
