## Question (verbatim)

> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No.** Acme Manufacturing can get at most 15% off list on the NS-500 on 2026-09-23, so 18% is over the limit.

- **Acme's limit is 15%.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme and the NS-500 Industrial Controller and caps the discount at "15% of list price." It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, and it replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which ran 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31). I found no record of it being corrected or withdrawn.
- **Policy doesn't allow going above the contract.** The discount request procedure says a discount must not be approved unless the customer's contract permits it. The Acme exception also says it only sets what Acme is eligible for and does not give anyone authority to approve.
- **DR-9001 is for 15%, not 18%.** As recorded, it asks for 15% on a $250,000 list price: a $37,500 discount and a $212,500 net price. Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23, citing "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." It is still "Pending Approval." On the same date, Acme asked for "15% off list." At 18%, the discount would be $45,000 and the net price $205,000, which is $7,500 more than the contract allows.

The 15% request fits the contract, but it still needs approval from the right person, with that approval on record. Discount policy says larger discounts need VP Sales or Chief Revenue Officer sign-off. The knowledge base doesn't include the percentage cut-offs for those levels, so I can't tell you who has to approve the 15%.

To go to 18%, Acme would need a new or amended exception first. A one-off approval on the request wouldn't be enough under current policy.
