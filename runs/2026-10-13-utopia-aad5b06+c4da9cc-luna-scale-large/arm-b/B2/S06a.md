On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 was **15% off list price**.

One caveat: the exception says it sets what Acme is commercially eligible for but does not grant approval authority. Even a 15% deal still has to go through the normal discount approval process.
