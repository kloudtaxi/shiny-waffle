## Answer

**Short answer: Not on her own authority.** Acme is entitled to the 15%, but Sarah Chen can't sign it off herself. The VP Sales, Michael Torres, has to approve it. There's also a date problem you should check first.

**The date doesn't match.** You give 2025-09-23, but the knowledge base records DR-9001 with `request_date` **2026-09-23**. Every other field matches. The year decides which pricing policy applies, so please confirm it in Northstar CRM. On the recorded date (2026), the answer is clear. On your date (2025), part of it can't be confirmed (see the last point below).

1. **Request:** DR-9001, 15% off NS-500 (list $250,000, discount $37,500), status Pending Approval. It relies on the contract: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **Customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is the same customer as ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (Milwaukee, WI). Sarah Chen owns the account. Don't confuse it with "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091), a different account.
3. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000.
4. **Contract:** The Master Supply Agreement runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2028, so it's active on either date.
5. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers this product, allows up to 15% of list, and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10), which ran 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. David Morgan (CRO) approved it on 2025-03-20. The 15% request is exactly at the maximum, so **Acme is eligible**. The exception document says it "does not grant approval authority."
6. **Policy:** On 2026-09-23 it's Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), which replaced the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** She's an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role's limit is **≤10%**. 15% is over her limit.
8. **Who must approve:** Discounts above 10% up to 20% need the **VP Sales**, which is **Michael Torres**, her manager. The 2026 policy also requires recorded approval evidence.
9. **If the date really is 2025-09-23:** Acme would still be eligible, and the 2025 policy also sends some discounts to the VP Sales. But the knowledge base doesn't record the 2025 policy's limit for Account Executives, so I can't confirm whether 15% was within her authority then. The 10%/20% bands are only on record for 2026.
