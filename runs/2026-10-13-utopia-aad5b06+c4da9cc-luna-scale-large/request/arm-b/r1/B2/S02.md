## Answer

**No, we can't offer 18%.** It is above the most Acme is contractually eligible for on the NS-500, which is 15%. A more senior approver can't fix that. Going above 15% would need new or amended commercial terms.

**What the request is based on.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement", so it relies on special terms, not standard pricing.

**Before you act, check the request itself.** The knowledge base records DR-9001 at **15.0%** (discount $37,500, net $212,500). Your version says 18.0% (discount $45,000, net $205,000). The CRM record may have been changed, or one of the two versions is wrong. I assessed the 18% you gave me. The 15% version would get a different answer (see below).

**What I checked:**
1. **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing is CRM account CRM-2048 and ERP customer C-1001 (also called "Acme Mfg. Holdings"). It is a Strategic/Enterprise account owned by Sarah Chen.
2. **Product:** NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000.
3. **Contract:** A Master Supply Agreement exists between Northstar and Acme, and Northstar supplies the NS-500 under it. The knowledge base has no start or end dates for it, so I couldn't confirm it was active on 2026-09-23.
4. **Pricing exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme on the NS-500, runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, and so covers 2026-09-23. It allows **at most 15% off list**. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10). Sarah Chen prepared it and David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. The exception states that it sets commercial eligibility but does not give anyone approval authority.
5. **Policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. It replaced the 2025 policy.
6. **Requestor's authority:** Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive, and AEs can approve up to 10%. Either 18% or 15% is beyond what she can approve herself.
7. **Higher approvers:** The policy sends larger discounts to VP Sales (Michael Torres, Sarah's manager) or the Chief Revenue Officer. The knowledge base doesn't capture the percentage cut-offs between those two, so I can't say which one an 18% discount would need. It doesn't change the outcome, because no approver can go past the 15% maximum.

**If the request is actually 15%:** it would fall within the exception. It would then be approved with authorization from whichever approver the 2026 policy names for 15%, not by Sarah alone.
