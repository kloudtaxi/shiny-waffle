## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve DR-9001, and the request shouldn't go ahead as filed. **Outcome: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.**

**Why**

1. **The request relies on special terms.** The justification is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement" for 15% off NS-CLOUD: $120,000 list, $18,000 discount, $102,000 net, dated 2026-09-23.
2. **The customer is the same entity in both systems.** CRM account CRM-2048 "Acme Manufacturing" is ERP customer C-1001 "Acme Mfg. Holdings" (Milwaukee, WI). It is a Strategic account and Sarah Chen is the account owner.
3. **The product is the right one.** NS-CLOUD is the NS-Cloud Operations Suite (PROD-002), list price $120,000, so the figures in the request match.
4. **There is an active contract.** The Master Supply Agreement runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. It covers supplying NS-Cloud, but I found no discount term for NS-Cloud in it.
5. **The pricing exception doesn't cover this product.** The only active Acme exception is EXC-ACME-NS500-15: up to 15% off list, valid 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20 and prepared by Sarah Chen. It applies only to the **NS-500 Industrial Controller**, and the exception says it does not apply to other Northstar products. It replaced EXC-ACME-NS500-10, the earlier 10% NS-500 exception (2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31). The 15% almost certainly comes from the NS-500 exception, and exceptions only apply to the product and dates they name.
6. **The policy in force is Northstar Pricing Policy 2026** (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31), which replaced the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah Chen's authority is too low.** As an Enterprise Account Executive she can approve up to 10%. 15% is above that, so she can't approve it whatever the customer's eligibility. The exception itself states it does not grant approval authority.
8. **The approver would be VP Sales.** Under the 2026 policy, discounts above 10% and up to 20% need VP Sales approval. That is Michael Torres, Sarah's manager. Even so, approving it would only settle authority, not whether Acme is entitled to the discount.

**Next steps:** The request needs commercial review. Either a new pricing exception or an amendment covering NS-Cloud for Acme is put in place (and then approved by VP Sales), or the request is cut to ≤10% on standard terms. Sarah could approve ≤10% herself, but only as a standard-pricing request, not on the strength of the contract. If someone thinks a specific Master Supply Agreement clause gives an NS-Cloud discount, they should provide that clause; nothing I found in the knowledge base shows one.
