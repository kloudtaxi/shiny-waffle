## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve this herself. Acme is entitled to the 15% discount, but it's above her personal limit of 10%. The discount needs approval from the VP Sales, Michael Torres.

1. **The request:** DR-9001 asks for 15% off an NS-500 for Acme ($250,000 list, $37,500 discount, $212,500 net). Sarah Chen, an Enterprise Account Executive, raised it on 2026-09-23. It relies on special contract terms: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."
2. **The customer:** CRM account CRM-2048 is Acme Manufacturing, which the ERP holds as customer C-1001 ("Acme Mfg. Holdings"). Sarah owns the account. A separate account, Acme Industrial (CRM-2091), is a different customer and isn't relevant here.
3. **The product:** SKU NS-500 is the NS-500 Industrial Controller (PROD-001), list price $250,000.
4. **The contract:** A Master Supply Agreement between Northstar Industrial Systems and Acme exists, but I couldn't find its start and end dates. An older 10% term (2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, linked to agreement ACME-SA-2023) had expired by the request date.
5. **The pricing exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 is for Acme Manufacturing and the NS-500. It allows up to 15% off list and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers the request date. It replaces the earlier 10% exception and doesn't apply to other products. David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) approved it on 2025-03-20. The exception itself says it establishes the customer's eligibility but does not give anyone approval authority. So Acme is eligible, and the 15% requested is exactly at the maximum.
6. **The policy:** Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) applies. It replaced the 2025 policy.
7. **Sarah's authority:** An Enterprise Account Executive can approve discounts up to 10%. 15% is over her limit.
8. **The required approver:** Discounts above 10% and up to 20% go to the VP Sales. That's Michael Torres, who is also Sarah's manager. The policy also requires the approval to be recorded.

**Caveats:**
- The approval bands come from a document that says the 2026 policy governed discount requests. The policy's own text, as the knowledge base records it, names VP Sales and CRO approval but not the percentage thresholds.
- The request cites the Master Supply Agreement, but eligibility actually rests on the pricing exception. The exception is enough on its own, so I didn't ask for more documents. Still, the justification should probably cite EXC-ACME-NS500-15.
