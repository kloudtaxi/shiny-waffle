## Question (verbatim)

> Could Sarah Chen have approved a 15% NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing on 2025-09-23?

## Answer

**No. She was eligible to request 15%, but she couldn't approve it herself.**

1. **Acme was eligible for 15%.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 sets a maximum of 15% off list for the NS-500 Industrial Controller for Acme, valid from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10, 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31). Sarah Chen prepared it and David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20. But the exception says outright that it gives eligibility only and **does not grant approval authority**.

2. **Sarah's title is Enterprise Account Executive.** She owns the Acme account and reports to Michael Torres, VP Sales.

3. **Her approval limit didn't cover 15%.** On 2025-09-23 the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 was in force (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve some discounts and requires VP Sales approval for larger ones. The approval limits recorded in the knowledge base are:
   - Enterprise Account Executive: up to 10%
   - VP Sales: over 10% and up to 20%

   A 15% discount falls in the VP Sales band, so Michael Torres would have had to approve it. The policy also requires a record of the approval.

**Caveats:**
- The 10% and 20% limits come from a separate approval-authority document. The 2025 policy entry itself doesn't show its percentages, so I can't confirm the two documents use the same limits for 2025.
- The 15% request on file has Acme ready to move ahead with the NS-500 on **2026-09-23**, not 2025. If you meant 2026, the 2026 pricing policy applies instead. It has a similar split between Account Executives, VP Sales and the Chief Revenue Officer, but its percentages aren't recorded, so I can't confirm where 15% falls under it.

None of the corrections or withdrawals recorded in the knowledge base affect these facts.
