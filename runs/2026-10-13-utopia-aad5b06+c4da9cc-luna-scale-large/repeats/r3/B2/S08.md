> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

**Answer:** All three IDs refer to one customer, **Acme Manufacturing** (also recorded as "ACME Manufacturing", "Acme Mfg. Holdings" and "Acme"). **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**

**How each ID maps to Acme Manufacturing:**
- **CRM-2048** is Acme Manufacturing's CRM account ID. The account is in the Midwest segment, tagged Strategic / Enterprise tier, and owned by Sarah Chen.
- **C-1001** is its ERP customer ID. The bill-to address is 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209, with DUNS 04-812-7730 and Net 45 terms. Sales orders SO-7001 and SO-7002 are booked under C-1001.
- **ACME-MFG-2025** is less direct. The knowledge base doesn't link this ID to the customer record itself. It only shows it tied to "Schedule B" of a pricing exception. That document is one of the sources for the Acme Manufacturing record, and it names Acme Manufacturing as the customer of that exception. So it's most likely Acme Manufacturing's 2025 contract or agreement reference, but that's an inference, not a recorded link.

**Why Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate customer.** None of its identifiers match:

| | Acme Manufacturing | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| CRM account | CRM-2048 | CRM-2091 |
| ERP customer ID | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| DUNS | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |
| Location | Milwaukee, WI | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH 44310 |
| Industry | Industrial Manufacturing | Industrial Distribution |
| Tier | Strategic / Enterprise | Standard / Mid-Market |
| Account owner | Sarah Chen | Zachary Brown |

The only things they share are the "Acme" name, the Midwest segment and Net 45 payment terms. The graph shows no connection between the two.

The graph also has a separate record called "Acme Industrial" that lists CRM-2091 as an alternate name. It's linked to service request SR-40418 and to DR-7028, DR-7264 and DR-7560. That record looks like a duplicate of Acme Industrial Supply Co. that was never merged, so SR-40418 and those three DR records likely belong to Acme Industrial Supply, not Acme Manufacturing. It may be worth merging the two records.
