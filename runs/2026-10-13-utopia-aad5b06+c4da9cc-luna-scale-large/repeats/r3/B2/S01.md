## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

**No, Sarah Chen can't approve it herself.** Acme is eligible for a 15% discount, but approving that size of discount is outside her authority.

**What allows a 15% discount**
- Exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-15** covers Acme Manufacturing on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It allows up to 15% off list price from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers 2026-09-23. It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10).
- That exception was approved by David Morgan (Chief Revenue Officer) on 2025-03-20. It says outright that it makes Acme eligible but **does not give anyone approval authority**.

**Why Sarah can't sign off**
- Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive. She is Acme's account owner and reports to Michael Torres (VP Sales).
- The records give Enterprise Account Executives discount authority of **10% or less**. 15% is above that.
- The 2026 pricing policy (in effect 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31, replacing the 2025 policy) applies on that date. It lets Account Executives approve some discounts, but larger ones need VP Sales or CRO approval, plus recorded approval evidence.
- One caveat: I couldn't find the exact percentage limits written into the 2026 policy. The 10% figure comes from a separate record that doesn't say which year it applies to. I found nothing raising the limit to 15% or more.

**The request itself**
- It's **DR-9001**, submitted by Sarah on 2026-09-23. It asks for 15% off an NS-500 listed at $250,000, which is a $37,500 discount and a net price of $212,500. It cites "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement" and is still marked **Pending Approval**.

The 15% is allowed under Acme's contract terms, but someone above Sarah has to approve it. That's at least Michael Torres (VP Sales), and possibly David Morgan (CRO) if the 2026 policy puts 15% in the CRO's band.
