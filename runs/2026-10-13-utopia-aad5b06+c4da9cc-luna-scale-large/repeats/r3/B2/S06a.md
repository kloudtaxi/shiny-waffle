## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 was **15% of list price**.

- **What applies:** Pricing exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-15** covers Acme Manufacturing and the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It allows a discount of up to 15% of list price. It took effect on 2025-04-01 and runs until 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. Sarah Chen prepared it and David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20.
- **What it replaced:** It repeals the earlier exception, **EXC-ACME-NS500-10**, which allowed up to 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31. That 10% limit no longer applied by September 2026.
- **Two limits:** The exception sets what Acme is eligible for. It doesn't give anyone the authority to approve that discount. It also doesn't cover other Northstar products, only the NS-500.

On the same day, 2026-09-23, Acme said it wanted to move ahead with the NS-500 controller and asked for 15% off list. That request matches the contractual maximum exactly. I checked the knowledge base for corrections or withdrawn records, and nothing changes this exception.
