> Can Sarah Chen approve 15% for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No. Sarah Chen can't approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on the NS-Cloud Operations Suite on 2026-09-23.

1. **The discount is above her limit.** Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive and the account owner for Acme. The knowledge base gives that role a discount limit of "≤10%", and 15% is over it.
2. **The 2026 pricing policy applies.** It covers 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31 and replaced the 2025 policy. It allows Enterprise Account Executives to approve discounts only up to their limit. Larger discounts need approval from the VP Sales or the Chief Revenue Officer, plus recorded approval evidence.
3. **Acme's 15% exception doesn't cover this product.** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31. It lets Acme receive up to 15% off list price, but only on the NS-500 Industrial Controller, and it says it doesn't apply to other Northstar products. The NS-Cloud Operations Suite is a separate software product with a $120,000 list price.
4. **Even on the NS-500, the exception wouldn't let her approve it.** It establishes that Acme qualifies for the discount but says it "does not grant approval authority". Sarah prepared it; David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20.

**Next step:** the request would need to be escalated. Sarah reports to Michael Torres, the VP Sales, so he is the natural next approver. The policy text in the knowledge base doesn't give the exact percentage that separates VP Sales approval from CRO approval. Check the 2026 policy to confirm whether 15% on NS-Cloud also needs the CRO.

Also, Acme's 2026-09-23 note says it wants to "move ahead with" the NS-500 controller and is asking for 15% off list. If that request was really for the NS-500 rather than NS-Cloud, Acme would qualify for 15% under the exception. Sarah still couldn't approve it herself; it would need sign-off at the VP Sales level or higher.
