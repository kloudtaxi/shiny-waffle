> Can we offer Acme Manufacturing 18% on the NS-500 (DR-9001 at 18%) on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No, 18% isn't supported. Acme's contract pricing on the NS-500 tops out at 15% for 2026-09-23.

**What applies on that date**
- **The 15% exception:** Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15 covers Acme Manufacturing on the NS-500 Industrial Controller. It sets a **maximum eligible discount of 15% of list price** and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20 and Sarah Chen prepared it.
- **The old 10% exception:** EXC-ACME-NS500-10 (2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31) gave Acme 10%. The 15% exception replaced it, so it no longer applies.
- **Limits of the 15% exception:** It sets what Acme is eligible for. It explicitly **does not grant approval authority**, so even 15% still has to go through normal approval.

**What DR-9001 asks for**
- The request is for **15%, not 18%**: 15% off a $250,000 list price is a $37,500 discount, leaving a net of $212,500.
- Sarah Chen submitted it on 2026-09-23 with the justification "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement." Its status is **Pending Approval**. Her email says Acme asked for "15% off list, which is what we did for them last September."

**What 18% would mean**
- 18% would be a $45,000 discount and a net of $205,000. That's 3 points above Acme's contract cap, so the agreement can't be used to justify it.
- Getting there would need a new or revised exception, or a separate approval under the pricing policy. A 2026 policy replaced the Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 on 2026-01-01 and sets who can approve discounts, but I couldn't find its actual thresholds or approvers. I can't tell you who could sign off on 18%.

DR-9001 at 15% is within Acme's entitlement once it's approved. To offer 18%, you'd need to confirm the approval route under the 2026 pricing policy first.
