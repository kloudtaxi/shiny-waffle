## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2022-10-01?

## Answer

I can't find any contractual NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing that was in effect on 2022-10-01, so I can't give a maximum for that date. Searching the knowledge base as of that date returns nothing for Acme. The earliest discount terms on record start six months later:

| Period | Terms | Maximum NS-500 discount |
|---|---|---|
| 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31 | Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under supply agreement ACME-SA-2023 | 10% |
| 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31 | Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, which replaces the 10% one. Approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20 and tied to the Master Supply Agreement | 15% of list price |

Neither document covers October 2022, and the NS-500 product record has no discount terms dated before April 2023 either. If Acme had a contract in 2022, it hasn't been added to the knowledge base.

Two related points:
- The 2025 exception sets which discounts Acme is eligible for. It doesn't give anyone the authority to approve them.
- One record still lists the old 10% exception as "Active," even though the 15% one replaced it. That entry looks out of date.

If you meant a date when terms were in force, it's 10% for April 2023 to March 2025, and 15% for April 2025 onward, which includes today.
