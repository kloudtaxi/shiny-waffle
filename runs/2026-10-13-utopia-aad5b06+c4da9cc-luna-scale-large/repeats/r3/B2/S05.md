## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen's 15% NS-500 discount for Acme (DR-9001) be approved on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

I can't confirm this can be approved, and the records point toward no.

**What DR-9001 says**
- Requested by Sarah Chen on 2026-09-23 for Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048), product NS-500.
- 15% off a $250,000 list price: a $37,500 discount, leaving $212,500.
- The reason given is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement".
- Status is still Pending Approval.

**Why it doesn't clear**
1. **The contract isn't on record.** The pricing procedure says to check that the customer has an active contract. A discount that depends on a contract term must not be approved unless the contract actually allows it. The "Acme Master Supply Agreement" exists in the records only as a name, with no dates, terms or link to Acme. The only other trace of Acme's 15% NS-500 price is a note that it was agreed in a "2025 agreement" and that the Deal Desk was confirming it. Nothing shows that agreement was still in force on 2026-09-23.
2. **Sarah may not be allowed to approve it herself.** She is an Enterprise Account Executive reporting to Michael Torres. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026, in force from 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, replaced the 2025 policy. It lets Enterprise Account Executives approve some discounts, but larger ones need the VP Sales or the Chief Revenue Officer. I couldn't find the percentage or dollar limits, so I can't tell where 15% / $37,500 falls. The policy also says a contract making the customer eligible doesn't widen the employee's own approval limit.
3. **Two more policy conditions.** Contract-specific pricing exceptions must be reviewed, and every discount needs a recorded approval. DR-9001 has neither.

**Why last year's approval doesn't settle it:** Sarah approved an identical request herself (DR-8104: 15%, $250,000 list, same reason) on 2025-09-16. That was under the 2025 policy, and the 2026 policy has replaced it.

**What would let it go ahead:** a copy of the Acme Master Supply Agreement showing it was active on 2026-09-23 and allows 15% on NS-500, plus the 2026 approval limits for Enterprise Account Executives. If 15% is over her limit, it needs the VP Sales or CRO to sign off. Either way, a contract-pricing review and a recorded approval are still needed.
