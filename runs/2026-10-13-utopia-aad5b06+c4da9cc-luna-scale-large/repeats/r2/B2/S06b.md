## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2024-10-01?

## Answer

On 2024-10-01, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 was **10%**.

- **What applied on that date:** exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which falls under agreement ACME-SA-2023. It allows Acme up to 10% off from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so 2024-10-01 is inside that window.
- **What replaced it later:** a newer exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, raises the maximum to 15% of list price and repeals the 10% one. It was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, takes effect on 2025-04-01 and expires on 2028-03-31. Because it starts after 2024-10-01, it doesn't change the answer for that date.
- **Not a contractual limit:** the knowledge base also records Acme "asking for" 15% off list. That's their request, not something the contract gives them.

The newer exception also says it sets which discount Acme is eligible for. It doesn't give anyone the authority to approve that discount.
