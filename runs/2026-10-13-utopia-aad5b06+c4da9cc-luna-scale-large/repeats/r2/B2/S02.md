## Answer

**No.** 18% is above the most Acme Manufacturing can get on the NS-500 under its current contract terms.

**Why:**
- **The exception caps it at 15%.** Acme's NS-500 exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15, sets the "maximum eligible discount" at **15% of list price**. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it was in force on 2026-09-23. David Morgan approved it on 2025-03-20, and it replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10).
- **Pricing policy:** a discount request "must not be approved" unless the customer's contract allows it. The exception gives Acme the right to a discount up to 15%, but it says it "does not grant approval authority." So even 15% still needs approval from the right person, with that approval recorded.
- **Escalation can't fix it.** The policy has approval levels (Enterprise AE, VP Sales, Chief Revenue Officer), but they don't raise the contract cap. I couldn't find the exact percentage limits for each level in the knowledge base.

**DR-9001 doesn't match your question.** The record shows **15%**, not 18%:
- Account CRM-2048 (Acme), NS-500, requested 2026-09-23 by Sarah Chen
- List price $250,000, discount $37,500, net $212,500
- Status: **Pending Approval**
- Justification: "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement"

This also matches Acme's own note that they're "asking for 15% off list."

**What I'd do:** offer 15% (net $212,500) and get the approval recorded on DR-9001. To go to 18%, Acme's contract would first need a new or amended exception, and I found no sign of one.
