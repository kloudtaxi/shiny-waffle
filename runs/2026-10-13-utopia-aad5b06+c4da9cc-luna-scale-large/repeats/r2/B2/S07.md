## Question (verbatim)

> Why is Acme Manufacturing eligible for a 15% discount on the NS-500, and what is the evidence?

## Answer

Acme Manufacturing can get up to 15% off list on the NS-500 Industrial Controller because of a signed customer pricing exception, **EXC-ACME-NS500-15**. That exception replaced an older 10% one that has expired.

**What the exception says**
- **Exception ID:** EXC-ACME-NS500-15
- **Customer:** Acme Manufacturing (CRM-2048, ERP C-1001, Milwaukee, WI, Strategic/Enterprise tier)
- **Product:** NS-500 Industrial Controller only. It does not apply to any other Northstar product.
- **Maximum discount:** 15% of list price
- **Valid:** 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it is still in effect today (2026-10-13)
- **Approved by:** David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, on 2025-03-20
- **Prepared by:** Sarah Chen, the account owner
- **Replaces:** EXC-ACME-NS500-10, which allowed 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31 under agreement ACME-SA-2023

**Supporting evidence**
- In a note dated 2026-09-23, Acme said it wants to move ahead with the NS-500 controller and is asking for 15% off list. The note says "their agreement covers" that 15%, and Sarah logged it. This matches the exception's limit.
- The account record backs up the commercial reason: Acme has about $125M in revenue and plans to roll out the NS-500 at a third plant, which should bring more controller orders.

**Things to watch**
1. **The exception doesn't approve the discount by itself.** It establishes that Acme can receive the discount, but says outright that it does not grant approval authority. A 15% quote still needs sign-off from whoever can approve that level under the current pricing policy. The Northstar Pricing Policy 2025 ran for calendar year 2025 and was replaced from 2026-01-01. I couldn't find the actual approval thresholds in the knowledge base.
2. **15% is a ceiling, and it only covers the NS-500.** Other products can't be bundled in at that rate.
3. **The old 10% exception is still listed as "Active" in its original document,** but it has been formally repealed by the 15% exception and its dates ended on 2025-03-31. Don't use it.
4. **There is a separate customer called "Acme Industrial" (CRM-2091) with its own discount requests.** It is not Acme Manufacturing, so check you're working on the right account.
