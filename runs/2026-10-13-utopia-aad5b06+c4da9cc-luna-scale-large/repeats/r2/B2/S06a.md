## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

On 2026-09-23, Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual discount on the NS-500 was **15% of list price**.

That figure comes from exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-15**:
- **Customer and product:** Acme Manufacturing, NS-500 Industrial Controller.
- **Validity:** effective 2025-04-01 and expires 2028-03-31, so 2026-09-23 falls inside it.
- **What it replaced:** it repeals the earlier exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-10. That one allowed up to 10% from 2023-04-01 to 2025-03-31, so it no longer applied.
- **Who signed off:** prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20.
- **Later changes:** nothing on record corrects or withdraws it.

Also on record:
- The exception sets what Acme is commercially eligible for. It says it does not itself give anyone authority to approve a discount, so a deal at 15% may still need sign-off under the usual approval process.
- On 2026-09-23, Acme said it wanted to move ahead with the NS-500 controller and asked for 15% off list. That request is exactly at the contractual maximum.
