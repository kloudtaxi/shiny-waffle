Larger discounts need VP Sales approval, and the policy also has a tier that needs Chief Revenue Officer approval. Any discount also needs recorded approval evidence and must be calculated against the current list price. The NS-Cloud list price is $120,000.

2. **Acme's exception doesn't cover NS-Cloud:** Acme does have a 15% exception, EXC-ACME-NS500-15. It runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, was prepared by Sarah Chen and approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20. But it covers only the NS-500 Industrial Controller and doesn't apply to other Northstar products. The exception also states that it only makes the customer eligible for the discount and doesn't give anyone authority to approve it. So even for the NS-500, Sarah couldn't approve 15% herself.

3. **What the request is for:** On 2026-09-23, Acme said it wanted to move ahead with the NS-500 controller and asked for 15% off list.
