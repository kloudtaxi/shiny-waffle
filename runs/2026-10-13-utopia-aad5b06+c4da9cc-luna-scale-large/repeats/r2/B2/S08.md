## Question (verbatim)

> Which customer do CRM-2048, C-1001 and ACME-MFG-2025 refer to — and is Acme Industrial Supply Co. the same customer?

## Answer

All three IDs point to **Acme Manufacturing**. **Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a different customer.**

**The three IDs**
- **CRM-2048** is Acme Manufacturing's CRM account ID. That account is Strategic/Enterprise tier, in the Midwest segment, and owned by Sarah Chen.
- **C-1001** is Acme Manufacturing's ERP customer ID. It bills to 2200 N Industrial Pkwy, Milwaukee, WI 53209, on Net 45 terms. Sales orders SO-7001 and SO-7002 use this ID.
- **ACME-MFG-2025** links to Acme Manufacturing less directly. The knowledge base doesn't tie it straight to the customer record. It only connects it to "Schedule B" of a pricing exception, which comes from a document naming Acme Manufacturing as the customer. That exception covers the NS-500 Industrial Controller, was approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, was prepared by Sarah Chen, and replaces the earlier exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10. So ACME-MFG-2025 is most likely the contract or pricing reference for that exception.

Acme Manufacturing is also recorded under the names "ACME Manufacturing", "Acme Mfg. Holdings" and "Acme". Its DUNS number is 04-812-7730.

**Acme Industrial Supply Co. is a separate company.** Its records don't match Acme Manufacturing's on any key field:

| | Acme Manufacturing | Acme Industrial Supply Co. |
|---|---|---|
| CRM account | CRM-2048 | CRM-2091 |
| ERP customer ID | C-1001 | C-1044 |
| DUNS | 04-812-7730 | 09-552-1187 |
| Bill-to | Milwaukee, WI | 17 Tallmadge Ave, Akron, OH |
| Industry | Industrial Manufacturing | Industrial Distribution |
| Tier | Strategic / Enterprise | Standard / Mid-Market |
| Account owner | Sarah Chen | Zachary Brown |

They share the Midwest segment and Net 45 terms, but that isn't enough to call them the same company.

The knowledge base also has a separate entry called "Acme Industrial" that is an alias for CRM-2091. It belongs to Acme Industrial Supply Co., not Acme Manufacturing. It appears on service request SR-40418 and on records DR-7028, DR-7264 and DR-7560. Those service and support records should be counted under Acme Industrial Supply Co.
