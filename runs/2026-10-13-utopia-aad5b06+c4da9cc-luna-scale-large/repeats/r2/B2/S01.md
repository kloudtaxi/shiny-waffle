## Question (verbatim)

> Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme Manufacturing on an NS-500 Industrial Controller on 2026-09-23?

## Answer

No, Sarah Chen can't approve it herself. Acme is allowed to receive a 15% discount on the NS-500, but approving one that size is above her level and needs the VP Sales.

**Acme is allowed the discount**
- Pricing exception **EXC-ACME-NS500-15** covers Acme Manufacturing for the **NS-500 Industrial Controller only**. It allows up to **15% off list price** and runs from 2025-04-01 to 2028-03-31, so it covers 2026-09-23.
- It replaced the earlier 10% exception (EXC-ACME-NS500-10) for requests dated on or after 2025-04-01. David Morgan, Chief Revenue Officer, approved it on 2025-03-20.
- The exception says it "establishes commercial eligibility only" and "does not grant approval authority to any Northstar employee." Sarah Chen wrote it up, but that doesn't give her any power to approve the discount.

**Who can approve it (pricing_policy_2026.md, in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, replacing the 2025 policy)**
- Enterprise Account Executives can approve discounts **up to and including 10%**.
- Discounts **over 10% and up to 20%** need **VP Sales approval**.
- Discounts over 20% need Chief Revenue Officer approval.
- Any discount over 10% also needs recorded approval evidence.

Sarah Chen is an Enterprise Account Executive and owns the Acme account, so she can only approve up to 10%. A 15% discount needs VP Sales sign-off. Her manager, **Michael Torres**, is the VP Sales, so he's the one to approve it. The approval evidence also has to be recorded. She can still prepare and submit the request.
