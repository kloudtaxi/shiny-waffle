## Answer

**What DR-9001 is:** a 15% discount on NS-500 for Acme (CRM-2048). List value is $250,000, so the discount is $37,500 and the net price is $212,500. Sarah requested it, and its status on 2026-09-23 was "Pending Approval". The stated reason is "Contract pricing per Acme Master Supply Agreement."

**Why she can't approve it:**
1. **Her limit is 10%.** The Northstar Pricing Policy 2026 (in effect 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31) sets who can approve discounts. Sarah is an Enterprise Account Executive, and that role can approve up to 10%.
2. **Above 10% needs a more senior approver.** The policy names the VP Sales (Michael Torres, Sarah's manager) and the Chief Revenue Officer (David Morgan). It also requires recorded evidence of approval, and approvals above 10% have to be logged in Northstar CRM. The Approval Authority Matrix decides which of the two is needed, but the knowledge base doesn't record its percentage bands. So I can't say whether 15% needs Torres alone or the CRO as well.
3. **The contract doesn't raise her limit.** The policy says contract eligibility "does not change employee authority." A contract can make a customer eligible for a price, but it doesn't let a 10% approver sign off on 15%.

**The contract reason can't be confirmed either:**
- The policy says a discount request must be checked against an active customer contract and the contract term it relies on. The requested discount may only be approved if the contract permits it.
- "Acme Master Supply Agreement" exists in the knowledge base by name only, with no dates, terms or discount level. The only related record is a "2025 agreement" in which Acme negotiated preferential NS-500 pricing, which Deal Desk was to confirm. Nothing shows that agreement was active on 2026-09-23 or that it allows 15%.

**Don't treat DR-8104 as a precedent.** It's an earlier 15% NS-500 request for the same Acme account with the same reason. Sarah requested and approved it herself on 2025-09-16. That was under the 2025 pricing policy, which the 2026 policy replaced. Under the 2026 rules she couldn't approve it alone. The record of her approving it may be worth raising with Sales leadership or Deal Desk.

**Next steps:**
1. Send DR-9001 to Michael Torres (VP Sales), or to David Morgan (CRO) if the Approval Authority Matrix requires it.
2. Have Deal Desk confirm that the Acme Master Supply Agreement was active on 2026-09-23 and allows 15% on NS-500.
3. Record the approval in Northstar CRM.
