## Question (verbatim)

> What is Acme Manufacturing's maximum contractual NS-500 discount on 2022-10-01?

## Answer

I couldn't find a contractual NS-500 discount for Acme Manufacturing that was in effect on 2022-10-01. When I filter the knowledge base to that date, nothing comes back for Acme, and the earliest discount on record starts six months later.

These are the only two NS-500 discount terms for Acme on record:

| Period | Maximum NS-500 discount | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 2023-04-01 → 2025-03-31 | **10%** | Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-10, under agreement ACME-SA-2023 |
| 2025-04-01 → 2028-03-31 | **15% of list price** | Exception EXC-ACME-NS500-15, approved by David Morgan on 2025-03-20, prepared by Sarah Chen. It replaces the 10% exception and matches the current Master Supply Agreement's dates. |

The knowledge base doesn't say whether Acme simply had no special NS-500 pricing in October 2022 or whether an earlier contract just never made it into the documents. Either way, the answer isn't 10% or 15%, because neither had started by then. One loose end: the 10% exception is still marked "Active," even though the 15% one replaces it. That doesn't change the 2022 answer.

If you need to know what Acme was actually charged in 2022, the source to check is any pre-2023 contract with Acme outside the knowledge base, or Northstar's standard pricing policy for that time.
