Behind the panel · Sovera OWM lab

Northstar is a synthetic company, built to answer one question.

Where does knowledge end and organizational understanding begin?

Everything above is a front. Behind it sits a complete fictional organization: people, reporting lines, customers, contracts, pricing policies and a pending discount request. Its records are scattered across CRM, ERP, documents and email, deliberately messy and sometimes contradictory.

We load that evidence into a knowledge foundation, then check whether an AI system can do what a good employee does: work out what the evidence means here, and what should happen next.

  1. Evidence17 artifacts from CRM, ERP, Drive, Service and email.
  2. Knowledge foundationUtopia / OG-RAG extracts entities, facts and provenance.
  3. Organizational World ModelAdds meaning: roles, authority, policy in force, exceptions.
  4. DecisionA governed outcome, with its reasons and its evidence.

The central case · DR-9001

Can Sarah Chen approve a 15% discount for Acme on 2026-09-23?

The contract says Acme can have 15%. A system that stops there answers yes. It is wrong.

The right answer needs facts that no single document states together: Sarah's role, the 2026 policy, the authority bands, the reporting line, and the pricing exception with its dates and product scope.

Eligibility is not authority.

Run it

Rebuild the whole company from one seed.

  1. Clone the lab.
    git clone https://github.com/kloudtaxi/shiny-waffle.git
  2. Generate the dataset. The same seed gives byte-identical files.
    uv sync && uv run northstar build
  3. Load dataset/evidence/ into Utopia as the Northstar knowledge base. Load the scenario-5 variant as a separate one.
    dataset/evidence-variants/missing-contract-evidence/
  4. Ask each scenario question, then record answers in dataset/evaluation/.

Keep the answer key out. Never load dataset/answer-key/, or this page, into the system you're testing.
